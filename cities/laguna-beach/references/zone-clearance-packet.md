# Zone Clearance packet completion

Use this alongside `planning-routing.md` and the current official form. The pinned
July 2026 Zone Clearance PDF has **43 physical PDF pages**, although its printed
application pagination ends at 25. Page references below are physical PDF pages.
Source: https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/home/showpublisheddocument/26718/639216211418770000
SHA-256: `ec6fee57c42ae574c76451e766a8e94cfae0756d46d4abc79678a925ed7996ed`.

The portal's Application and Plans categories are upload containers, not a completeness
checklist. Filling the 20 mapped contact/location/description targets does not complete
the packet. Read the applicable exhibits, including pages without form widgets.

| Component | Source pages | Completion evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Location, contacts and scope | 1–2 | Reconcile address/APN, every applicable contact and scope with the plans. Record an absent representative as not applicable rather than inventing one. |
| Title sheet and project summary | 3, 22–23 | Scope, sheet index, zoning, constraints, parcel/legal description, contacts, and existing/proposed summary. Required standards and conformance need actual sources. |
| Survey, site plan and cross-sections for built-in pool | 3–6, 21, 24–26 | Exhibit C pool row identifies title, survey, site plan and cross-sections. Read the detailed checklist; a diagram does not replace the professional survey. |
| Grading and drainage | 6 | Evaluate the stated new-project and greater-than-20-cubic-yard triggers. Include the preliminary plan and cut/fill quantities where applicable. Water volume alone is a lower bound, not an excavation estimate. |
| Landscape and other site-specific sheets | 6–8, 21, 30–31 | Resolve applicability from project facts and current rules; document each exclusion. |
| Owner affidavit | 7, 32–33 | Page 33 has no PDF widget. The actual form requires a wet/scanned or authenticated electronic signature; a typed name is expressly insufficient. |
| Completeness statement and hazardous-site certification | 40–43 | Pages 41 and 43 contain signature lines without widgets. The hazardous-site statement requires a sourced list determination; do not infer it from an empty field or fictional example. |
| Conditional studies/deposit agreement | 7–19, 34–39 | Check water quality, habitat, historic, coastal/bluff, geotechnical, flood, traffic and parking triggers. A non-trigger must be supported, not assumed. |

Exhibit C places the built-in pool row under Design Review. Do **not** use that older
matrix heading to override the later operative ordinance's pool height/equipment
routing analysis. Use the current law corpus and `planning-routing.md` for the route;
use the matrix as a document checklist with its date and this discrepancy visible.

Before calling a packet ready, create a component register: requirement, cited source,
applicability with reason, file/page satisfying it, and remaining action. Reconcile
non-widget declarations separately from `fill-form.py`'s widget-based review report.
Do not manufacture signatures, professional seals, property findings, or City action.

For the authorized fictional video, authored synthetic facts and visibly simulated
signature exhibits are appropriate. Label them in every exported artifact. A complete
rehearsal packet proves that the fictional workflow can be demonstrated; it never proves
that the real parcel is eligible or that a real City application is complete.
