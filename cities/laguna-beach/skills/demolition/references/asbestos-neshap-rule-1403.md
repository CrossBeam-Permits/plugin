# AQMD Asbestos Notification & Survey

Source: South Coast Air Quality Management District (SCAQMD) Rule 1403 -- "Asbestos Emissions from Demolition/Renovation Activities"; Bay Area Air Quality Management District (BAAQMD) Regulation 11 Rule 2 -- "Asbestos Demolition, Renovation and Manufacturing"; federal backstop: 40 CFR Part 61 Subpart M (NESHAP for asbestos).

These regional rules implement the federal asbestos NESHAP in California. The two largest AQMDs (SCAQMD and BAAQMD) cover most of the state's population. Other AQMDs (San Joaquin Valley APCD, Sacramento Metro AQMD, Mojave Desert AQMD, San Luis Obispo APCD, San Diego APCD, etc.) have their own near-equivalent rules; structure of this checklist applies generally.

---

## Why This Matters

Asbestos was used in California building materials -- vinyl floor tile, mastic, drywall joint compound, popcorn ceilings, pipe insulation, transite siding, plaster, roofing felt, fireproofing -- well into the 1980s. **Disturbing asbestos-containing material (ACM) without proper survey and abatement releases respirable fibers and is a federal/state air-quality violation.**

> **Practical risk threshold:** pre-1980 structures have a very high probability of containing ACM. Cal/OSHA presumes ACM in pre-1981 floor tile, plaster, and thermal-system insulation unless tested otherwise (Title 8 §1529).
>
> **Regulatory threshold:** none. SCAQMD Rule 1403 and BAAQMD Reg 11-2 both require a survey for EVERY demolition regardless of building age.

## SCAQMD Rule 1403 (LA, Orange, Riverside, San Bernardino counties)

### Jurisdiction
South Coast AQMD: Los Angeles County (except Antelope Valley), Orange County, the western/urbanized portions of Riverside County, and the southwestern portion of San Bernardino County.

### Survey Requirement (Rule 1403 §d)
An asbestos survey shall be performed prior to any demolition or renovation activity. The survey must be conducted by a **Certified Asbestos Consultant (CAC)** or a **Certified Site Surveillance Technician (CSST)** working under a CAC's supervision (Cal/OSHA Title 8 §341.15 certification).

The survey identifies, samples, and quantifies all suspect ACM in the structure.

> **No age-based exemption.** Rule 1403 §j (Exemptions) contains no exception for newer buildings -- every facility being demolished requires a survey.

### Notification Requirement (Rule 1403 §e)
Written/electronic notification submitted to South Coast AQMD:
- **Minimum 10 working days** before demolition or renovation starts (a 14-calendar-day operational window).
- For ordered demolition / emergency demo: notification "as early as possible" but no later than the next working day.

Since November 2016, all notifications must be submitted via the SCAQMD web application (Rule 1403 Notification Web App).

### Notification Content
Site address, facility description, year built, square footage, type of operation, contractor info, asbestos consultant info, dates, amount of friable + Category I + Category II nonfriable ACM, abatement contractor, waste disposal facility.

### On-Site Documentation
Copies of the notification AND the survey report must be kept at the worksite during the project. (Rule 1403 §h)

### Procedure Designations
Rule 1403 categorizes work into procedures (Procedure 1–5) based on the type and amount of ACM disturbed. The procedure dictates abatement methods, containment, and air monitoring.

### Fees
SCAQMD fee schedule (Rule 301); a survey-only or "no-ACM" notification still incurs a base notification fee.

## BAAQMD Regulation 11 Rule 2 (9 Bay Area counties)

### Jurisdiction
BAAQMD covers all of Alameda, Contra Costa, Marin, San Francisco, San Mateo, Santa Clara, and Napa counties; the southern half of Sonoma; and the southwestern portion of Solano.

### Survey Requirement
Same general structure as SCAQMD -- a pre-demolition survey by a Cal/OSHA-certified consultant is required. (Reg 11-2-301)

### Notification Requirement (Reg 11-2-401.3)
- **At least 10 working days prior** to commencement of demolition.
- **72-hour exception** for residential buildings of ≤4 dwelling units, with payment of an additional fee.
- Emergency demolition: as early as possible, no later than the first working day after the emergency.
- Notification period does not start until a complete notification is submitted.

### Trigger Threshold for Renovation (Reg 11-2-303)
For renovations (not demolitions), notification is required where the removal exceeds:
- 100 sq ft of surface area ACM, OR
- 100 linear ft of pipe/duct ACM, OR
- 35 cubic feet aggregate ACM.

**For demolitions: notification is required regardless of ACM quantity.**

### Fees
BAAQMD fee schedule per Reg 3-2 + Reg 11-2.

## Federal Backstop -- 40 CFR Part 61 Subpart M

The state/AQMD rules implement (and often exceed) the federal Asbestos NESHAP at 40 CFR Part 61 Subpart M:
- §61.145 -- demolition/renovation standards
- §61.149 -- waste disposal
- §61.150 -- standard for waste disposal

If a California jurisdiction lacks an AQMD rule, the federal NESHAP applies directly.

## Plan-Review Checklist

| # | Item | Pass Criteria | Citation |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | Asbestos survey on file | Signed survey by CAC or CSST submitted with permit application | SCAQMD 1403 §d / BAAQMD 11-2-301 |
| A2 | Survey covers entire structure | Floor plans annotated with sample locations; all suspect materials addressed | SCAQMD 1403 §d |
| A3 | AQMD notification ≥10 working days | SCAQMD web-app confirmation OR BAAQMD notification + J-number | SCAQMD 1403 §e / BAAQMD 11-2-401.3 |
| A4 | Notification copy + survey copy on-site during demo | Plan note: "Copies of Rule 1403 notification and survey shall be maintained on-site at all times during demolition" | SCAQMD 1403 §h |
| A5 | Abatement contractor identified (if ACM present) | DOSH-registered asbestos abatement contractor named on notification | Cal/OSHA T8 §341.6 |
| A6 | Waste-disposal facility identified | AQMD-approved Class II or Class III landfill or registered ACM facility on notification | 40 CFR 61.150 |

## Common Failure Patterns

1. **No survey at all** -- contractor submits demo permit application, then asserts "the building is too new for asbestos." Reject; no age exemption exists.
2. **Survey by an uncertified inspector** -- a general home inspector or Phase I ESA consultant is not a CAC. Reject; require CAC/CSST.
3. **Notification submitted same day as permit application** -- 10-working-day clock has not run; permit may issue but demolition cannot start until the clock expires. Plan note required.
4. **Friable ACM identified, no abatement plan** -- ACM must be abated by a DOSH-registered contractor before general demolition begins; survey + abatement plan + abatement notification are separate from the demo notification.
5. **Renovation notification used for demolition** -- BAAQMD distinguishes the two; demolition notification is broader (every demo) and must use the demolition form/category.

## How to Cite in Corrections Output

> **FAIL -- Item 8 (AQMD asbestos survey + notification):** No pre-demolition asbestos survey by a Certified Asbestos Consultant or Certified Site Surveillance Technician is included in the submittal. SCAQMD Rule 1403 §d (or BAAQMD Reg 11-2-301) requires a survey for every demolition regardless of building age. Provide signed survey report and South Coast AQMD Rule 1403 notification confirmation (submitted ≥10 working days before demolition start) before permit issuance.
