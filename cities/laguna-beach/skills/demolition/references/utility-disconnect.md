# Utility Disconnect Verification

CBC §3303.6 ("Service utility connections shall be discontinued and capped") is the state-level mandate. The operational reality in California is that **every utility must be physically disconnected and that disconnect must be documented in the permit file before the building official can issue the demolition permit.**

> **This is a permit-issuance prerequisite, not a final-inspection item.** Verify all four primary services (gas / electric / water / sewer) plus any secondary services (data, fuel oil, propane) in the file before the permit prints.

---

## Why "Before Permit Issuance"?

A demolition cannot legally or safely commence with live utilities. Severing a live gas service mid-demolition has killed people in California (the 2010 San Bruno pipeline explosion is the canonical reminder that gas-line work is unforgiving). Cutting a live electrical service drop can drop high-voltage conductors onto the work zone. Active water/sewer creates contamination and erosion. Active data/fiber severs telecom for the surrounding block.

Cities therefore treat utility-disconnect confirmations as a hard prerequisite -- collected at application or held-for-correction, never inspected-in-the-field after the fact.

## The Four Primary Services

### Natural Gas

**Provider in most of California:** PG&E (Northern + Central CA), SoCalGas (most of Southern CA), San Diego Gas & Electric (San Diego County + S Orange), Long Beach Gas (city-owned), SMGT (Southwest Gas, parts of Mojave/Inyo/Mono).

**Procedure:**
1. Owner/contractor calls gas company; schedules service shutoff and meter removal.
2. Gas company locks off service at street, pulls meter, caps line.
3. Gas company issues **written shutoff confirmation** (PG&E Form / SoCalGas "Service Termination Notice" / similar) with date, address, and account number.
4. Confirmation goes in the permit file.

**Cycle time:** 5–15 business days; SoCalGas demolition coordinations can run 3+ weeks at peak.

> **Common failure:** contractor uses "blue-tag" interim shutoff at the meter without removal. That is not a permanent disconnect. Require meter removal documentation.

### Electric Service

**Provider:** PG&E, SCE (Southern California Edison), SDG&E, LADWP (City of LA), SMUD (Sacramento Municipal), Riverside Public Utilities, Pasadena Water & Power, Anaheim Public Utilities, Burbank Water and Power, Glendale Water & Power, Modesto Irrigation District, Imperial Irrigation District, and various municipal utilities.

**Procedure:**
1. Service drop removed at the riser/weatherhead OR underground service de-energized at the transformer.
2. Meter pulled and meter socket locked.
3. Utility issues **written de-energization notice / service-removal record**.
4. Confirmation in permit file.

**Cycle time:** 7–21 business days; SCE service drops typically 10–14 days.

> **Common failure:** contractor pulls own meter with main breaker off but never coordinates with the utility, leaving the service drop energized at the weatherhead. Require utility-issued documentation, not contractor self-certification.

### Water

**Provider:** Municipal water department, water district (Metropolitan Water District retail members, irrigation districts, mutual water companies). Hundreds of California water purveyors.

**Procedure:**
1. Curb-stop shutoff turned off.
2. Meter pulled (most jurisdictions) OR service abandoned at main with cap installed.
3. Backflow assembly removed if present.
4. Utility issues **shutoff confirmation** showing date and account.
5. Confirmation in permit file.

**Cycle time:** 1–10 business days; usually fastest of the four services.

> **Common failure:** abandonment at the meter without cap at the main. For full-structure demo where the lot is being redeveloped, a cap at the main is generally required so the new structure can re-tap cleanly. Confirm city policy.

### Sewer

**Provider:** Municipal sewer department or sanitation district. In most cases the city's public-works department.

**Procedure:**
1. Lateral excavated at the property line / cleanout.
2. Capped with approved fitting (typically a concrete plug or rubber-cap-and-clamp per local spec).
3. **Sewer cap inspection** by the city before backfill -- inspection card sign-off OR a written sewer-cap permit close-out.
4. Confirmation in permit file.

**Cycle time:** 3–10 business days; requires a separate sewer cap permit in many cities.

> **Common failure:** lateral not capped, just plugged with rags or temporarily blocked. Surface flooding and surcharging into adjacent sewers can result. Insist on the sewer-cap inspection card.

## Secondary Services (Check Where Present)

| Service | Disconnect Method | Documentation |
|---|---|---|
| Telephone / DSL / cable / fiber | Provider removes drop and ONT | Letter or work order from AT&T / Charter / Frontier / Cox / Comcast / Sonic |
| Propane | Tank pumped + removed by licensed propane company | DOT-stamped tank-removal receipt; CSLB C-36 contractor if piping work |
| Fuel oil (older properties) | Tank purged, possibly removed | Tank-removal permit + UST closure if regulated |
| Solar PV | System de-energized, modules removed; utility net-metering account closed | Utility decommissioning + DRE/CSLB C-46 contractor |
| Wells (private water) | Well destruction per DWR Bulletin 74-81/74-90 by C-57 contractor | County DEH well-destruction permit + report |
| Septic tanks | Pumped, top crushed, backfilled per local DEH | County DEH septic abandonment permit |

| # | Item | Pass Criteria | Citation |
|---|---|---|---|
| U1 | Gas disconnect on file | Utility-issued shutoff notice with date + meter removal | CBC 3303.6 |
| U2 | Electric disconnect on file | Utility-issued de-energization / service-removal record with date | CBC 3303.6 |
| U3 | Water shutoff on file | Utility-issued shutoff confirmation with date | CBC 3303.6 |
| U4 | Sewer cap permit / inspection card | Sewer-cap permit issued and capped, inspected, signed off (or scheduled before demo start) | CBC 3303.6; local code |
| U5 | Telecom / data services confirmed (if applicable) | Provider work order or drop-removal confirmation | CBC 3303.6 |
| U6 | Propane / fuel oil tanks (if present) | Tank-removal receipts and any required UST closure documentation | CBC 3303.6 |
| U7 | Solar / PV decommissioned (if present) | Utility net-metering decommissioning letter + system removal documentation | CBC 3303.6 |
| U8 | Wells / septic (if present) | County DEH well-destruction OR septic-abandonment permits and reports | DWR 74-81; local DEH |

## Common Failure Patterns

1. **Contractor self-certification with no utility documentation.** Reject; require utility-issued paperwork. Self-certifications carry no weight if a live service is later found.
2. **"Meter pulled" but service drop still energized.** Pulling the meter does NOT de-energize the service drop or weatherhead. Require utility involvement.
3. **Sewer "capped" by contractor without inspection card.** The city or sewer district inspection is non-negotiable.
4. **Old solar PV system with abandoned utility net-metering account.** The PV system can backfeed even with the main service pulled. Require dedicated PV decommissioning.
5. **Forgotten propane tank behind the structure.** Old propane tanks for water heating are common in pre-1960 SoCal foothill properties. Site inspection / Phase I ESA flags them.
6. **Permit issued contingent on "disconnects to follow."** Don't. Hold the permit at the counter until all four primary services are documented.

---

## How to Cite in Corrections Output

> **FAIL -- Item 10 (Utility disconnect confirmations):** No utility-issued disconnect documentation is in the permit file for [gas / electric / water / sewer]. CBC §3303.6 requires that "service utility connections shall be discontinued and capped" before demolition. Provide: (1) gas-company written shutoff confirmation with meter-removal date; (2) electric utility de-energization / service-removal record; (3) water shutoff confirmation; (4) sewer-cap permit and inspection card. Permit cannot issue until all four are on file.
