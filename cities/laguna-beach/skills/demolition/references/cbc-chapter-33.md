# CBC Chapter 33 -- Safeguards During Construction (Demolition Focus)

Source: 2025 California Building Code (Title 24, Part 2), Chapter 33. Cross-reference: California Fire Code (Title 24, Part 9), Chapter 33 -- "Fire Safety During Construction and Demolition" -- for fire-specific provisions.

This file walks through the sections of CBC Chapter 33 that govern **full-structure demolition**. For interior non-structural demolition, use the LTIA skill instead.

---

## SECTION 3301 -- General

### 3301.1 Scope
Governs safety during construction AND demolition, plus protection of adjacent public and private property. Fire safety also pulls from CFC Chapter 33.

### 3301.4 Maintenance of exits, structural elements, fire protection
Required exits, existing structural elements, fire protection devices, and sanitary safeguards shall be maintained throughout alteration / addition / demolition activity, **except** where (1) the element is itself being demolished with substitute provisions made, or (2) the existing building is unoccupied.

> **For full demolition of a vacant standalone structure:** Exception 2 generally applies and the maintenance-of-egress provisions relax. For demo of a structure attached to or sharing systems with an occupied building (party wall, shared standpipe, shared egress): Exception 2 does NOT apply -- maintain everything.

### 3301.5 Removal of waste materials
Waste shall be removed in a manner that prevents injury or damage to persons, adjacent properties, and public rights-of-way.

## SECTION 3302 -- Owner's Responsibility for Fire Protection

### 3302.1 Site Safety Plan -- PERMIT PREREQUISITE
A written site safety plan must be submitted **and approved before a building permit is issued**. It establishes the fire-prevention program for all phases of demolition.

### 3302.1.1 Required components (all 13)
1. Site safety director name + contact
2. Training documentation (site safety director + fire-watch personnel)
3. Emergency reporting procedures
4. Fire-department vehicle access routes
5. Locations of fire protection equipment (extinguishers, standpipes, FDC, hydrants)
6. Smoking and cooking policies + designated areas + signage
7. Temporary heating equipment locations + safety considerations
8. Hot-work permit plan
9. Combustible-waste-material control plan
10. Hazardous-material storage/use locations
11. Site security + fire-watch provisions
12. Plan-change procedures
13. Other site-specific items per CFC

### 3302.2 Site safety director
Owner designates a single accountable person. Has authority to enforce Chapter 33 on-site.

### 3302.3 Daily fire-safety inspection
Daily on-site documented inspection covering hot-work clearance, temporary heating clearance, combustible-debris removal, temporary wiring, hazmat storage, fire apparatus access (≥20 ft clear), fire hydrant visibility, FDC accessibility, standpipe service, extinguisher availability, and fire-watch records. **Documentation kept on-site until C of O issues.**

## SECTION 3303 -- Demolition (CORE SECTION)

### 3303.1 Construction documents
Construction documents AND a schedule for demolition shall be submitted where required by the building official. Work shall not begin until both are approved. **In practice every California city requires both for full-structure demolition.**

### 3303.2 Pedestrian protection -- PRE-DEMO REQUIREMENT
Demolition shall not commence until pedestrian protection per Section 3306 / Table 3306.1 is in place.

### 3303.3 Means of egress
A horizontal exit shall not be destroyed until a substitute means of egress has been provided and approved. (Applies to demolition adjacent to or attached to occupied buildings.)

### 3303.4 Vacant lot
After demolition, the vacant lot shall be filled and maintained to existing grade or per local jurisdiction ordinance. **Plan note required.**

### 3303.5 Water accumulation
Provisions to prevent water accumulation or damage to foundations on the premises or on adjacent property. Pair with 3304.1.5 storm-water management for sites <1 acre.

### 3303.6 Utility connections -- THE BIG ONE
"Service utility connections shall be discontinued and capped in accordance with the approved rules and the requirements of the applicable governing authority."

This is the state-level mandate that every utility (gas, electric, water, sewer, and any data/fuel-oil services) must be properly disconnected and capped before demolition commences. The detailed disconnect protocols come from the utility companies' tariff rules. See `utility-disconnect.md` for the practical checklist.

### 3303.7 Fire safety during demolition
Pull in CFC Chapter 33 -- hot-work controls, combustible-waste removal, fire-watch.

## SECTION 3304 -- Site Work (Demolition-Adjacent)

### 3304.1.5 Storm-water drainage during construction (CA amendment)
Sites disturbing less than 1 acre AND not part of a larger common plan ≥1 acre shall manage storm-water drainage per CALGreen Chapter 4 Division 4.1. (Larger sites trigger state SWPPP separately.)

## SECTION 3306 -- Protection of Pedestrians

### 3306.1 Table 3306.1 -- Protection required
| Height of construction | Distance to lot line | Protection required |
|---|---|---|
| ≤8 ft | <5 ft | Construction railings |
| ≤8 ft | ≥5 ft | None |
| >8 ft | <5 ft | Barrier + covered walkway |
| >8 ft | ≥5 ft, but <¼ of construction height | Barrier + covered walkway |
| >8 ft | ≥¼ but <½ of construction height | Barrier only |
| >8 ft | >½ of construction height | None |

### 3306.2 Walkways
Required in front of every demolition site unless governing authority allows sidewalk closure. Minimum 4 ft wide, durable surface, accessible per CBC Chapter 11A or 11B, designed for ≥150 psf live load.

### 3306.3 Directional barricades
Where the walkway extends into the street, a directional barricade directs vehicles away from pedestrians.

### 3306.4 Construction railings
Minimum 42 inches high.

### 3306.5 Barriers
Minimum 8 ft high, full length of construction site, on the side of walkway nearest the demo.

### 3306.6 Barrier design
Either designed per Chapter 16 loads OR constructed per the prescriptive specification (2×4 plates, 3/4-inch boards or rated structural panels, specific stud spacing).

### 3306.7 Covered walkways
8-ft clear height, lit at all times, designed for ≥150 psf (or 75 psf for light-frame construction ≤2 stories), or use the prescriptive 2×6 / 4×6 / 4×12 framing alternative.

### 3306.8 Repair / removal
Pedestrian protection maintained throughout demo; removed immediately on completion with public property restored.

### 3306.9 Adjacent to excavations
Every excavation within 5 ft of a street lot line shall be enclosed by a barrier ≥6 ft high.

## SECTION 3307 -- Protection of Adjacent Property

### 3307.1 Protection required + 10-DAY EXCAVATION NOTICE
Adjacent public and private property shall be protected. Protection covers footings, foundations, party walls, chimneys, skylights, roofs. Water-runoff and erosion control during demo.

> **Written notice to adjacent property owners required at least 10 days before excavation starts.** This is statutory (also see Civil Code §832).

### 3307.2 Excavation retention systems
Where shoring is used, it shall be designed by a registered design professional, monitored for movement, and removed only when adequate replacement support is provided.

---

## SECTION 3308 -- Temporary Use of Streets, Alleys, Public Property
Where demo equipment/materials are staged on public property, follow local right-of-way permit requirements. Cannot block fire hydrants, FDCs, alarm boxes, catch basins, manholes, or be within 20 ft of street intersections.

---

## SECTION 3309 -- Fire Extinguishers
Minimum one portable fire extinguisher (ordinary-hazard size, per Section 906): (1) at each stairway on all floor levels where combustibles have accumulated; (2) in every storage / construction shed; (3) additional where special hazards (flammable liquids) exist.

---

## SECTION 3310 -- Means of Egress

### 3310.1 Temporary stairways
Where construction exceeds 40 ft above lowest fire-dept access level, a temporary or permanent stairway must be provided, extended to within one floor of the highest point of construction with secured decking.

### 3310.2 Maintenance of means of egress
Means of egress -- including accessible means of egress -- maintained at all times during demolition. Exception: existing egress need not be maintained where approved temporary egress is provided.

---

## SECTION 3311 -- Standpipes

### 3311.1 Where required
Buildings required to have standpipes by Section 905.3.1 must have at least one standpipe in service for construction; installed before construction exceeds 40 ft above lowest fire-dept access; extended to within one floor of highest secured deck.

### 3311.2 Buildings being demolished -- KEY DEMO PROVISION
Where an existing standpipe is in a building being demolished, it shall be maintained operable for fire-department use. The standpipe shall be demolished with the building but **shall not be demolished more than one floor below the floor being demolished.**

---

## SECTION 3312 -- Automatic Sprinkler System
Not typically applicable to full demolition (sprinklers usually decommissioned with utility shutoff). For partial demo of a sprinklered structure, ensure protection of remaining occupied portions.

---

## SECTION 3313 -- Water Supply for Fire Protection
Applies primarily during construction phase; for pure demolition the standpipe water supply (3311) is the relevant provision.

---

## SECTION 3314 -- Fire Watch During Construction

### 3314.1 Fire watch during construction / demolition
A fire watch shall be provided during nonworking hours for:
- Construction or demolition exceeding 40 ft in height above lowest adjacent grade at any point along building perimeter, OR
- New multistory construction with aggregate area >50,000 sf per story, OR
- As required by the fire code official.

---

## Contractor Licensing (Reference)

CSLB Class **C-21 (Building Moving / Demolition)** is required for standalone demolition projects in California (Business & Professions Code §7058; 16 CCR §832.21). Class **B (General Building)** is acceptable when demolition is incidental to a broader construction scope on the same permit.

Verify CSLB license number on permit application; check status at cslb.ca.gov before issuance.
