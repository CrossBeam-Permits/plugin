# Egress Requirements

Source: 2025 California Residential Code (Title 24, Part 2.5), Sections R318-R321.

---

## Emergency Escape and Rescue Openings (CRC R319)

### Where Required (R319.1)
An emergency escape and rescue opening (egress window or door) is required in:
1. **Every sleeping room** (bedroom)
2. **Basements** (unless no habitable space and <=200 SF)
3. **Habitable attics**
4. **The room to which a sleeping loft is open**

Openings must open directly to a public way, or to a yard or court that opens to a public way.

### Additions Specifically (R319.6)
When a dwelling addition contains sleeping rooms:
- An emergency escape and rescue opening is required in **each new sleeping room**
- New basements in additions require an egress opening

**Exceptions to R319.6:**
1. Egress not required in new basement if it contains a sleeping room with its own egress opening
2. Egress not required in new basement if existing basement (accessed from new) has an egress opening
3. Operable window complying with R319.7.1 accepted as egress (reduced dimensions for existing buildings)

### Minimum Dimensions (R319.2)

| Dimension | Requirement |
|---|---|
| **Net clear opening area** | Min 5.7 SF |
| **Net clear opening area (grade floor)** | Min 5.0 SF |
| **Min net clear height** | 24" |
| **Min net clear width** | 20" |
| **Max sill height above floor** | 44" |

**Critical:** Both height AND width minimums must be met simultaneously with the 5.7 SF area requirement. A window that is 24" x 34" = 5.67 SF would FAIL (does not meet 5.7 SF even though individual dimensions are met).

### Common Compliant Window Sizes

| Window Type | Typical Size | Net Clear Opening | Compliant? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Single-hung 3'0" x 4'0" | 20" x 34" = 4.72 SF | No — area too small |
| Single-hung 3'0" x 5'0" | 20" x 46" = 6.39 SF | Yes |
| Casement 2'4" x 4'0" | 24" x 44" = 7.33 SF | Yes |
| Slider 4'0" x 3'0" | 22" x 32" = 4.89 SF | No — area too small |
| Slider 5'0" x 4'0" | 28" x 44" = 8.56 SF | Yes |

*Actual net clear opening varies by manufacturer. Verify with specific product data.*

### Operational Requirements (R319.1.1)
- Must be operable from inside without keys, tools, or special knowledge
- Window opening control devices (per ASTM F2090) are permitted
- Control devices must be at max 70" above finished floor
- Release mechanism must be operable at all times
- Security bars: must have approved release device (interior and exterior if required by jurisdiction)

### Egress Under Decks/Porches (R319.2.4)
Egress openings under decks, porches, or cantilevers must:
- Be fully openable
- Provide a path min 36" high x 36" wide to a yard or court

## Area Wells (CRC R319.4)

When the egress opening bottom is below grade (basement windows):

### Minimum Size (R319.4.1)
- Horizontal area: min 9 SF
- Min projection from wall: 36"
- Min width: 36"
- Must allow full opening of the egress window

### Ladder/Steps Required (R319.4.2)
When area well depth >44":
- Permanently affixed ladder or steps required
- Must not obstruct the window in the open position
- **Ladder:** min 12" inside width, project min 3" from wall, rungs max 18" OC
- **Steps:** min 12" inside width, min 5" tread depth, max 18" riser height

### Drainage (R319.4.3)
Area wells must drain — connect to foundation drainage system.
Exception: Not required on well-drained soils (Group I per Table R401.4.1(2)).

### Covers (R319.4.4)
Window well covers, bars, or screens:
- Must still meet minimum net clear opening size
- Must be releasable from inside without keys, tools, or excessive force

## Means of Egress (CRC R318)

### General Requirements
- Every dwelling must have at least one exit
- Exit must lead directly to a public way or to a yard or court providing access to a public way

### Exit Door (R318.2)
- Min one egress door per dwelling unit
- Side-hinged, swinging
- **Min clear width:** 32" (when open 90 degrees)
- **Min height:** 80" (6'-8")
- Egress door must be readily openable from egress side without keys, special knowledge, or effort

### Hallways (R318.3)
- Min width: 36" (3 ft)
- Must provide a continuous, unobstructed path to an exit

### Stairways (R318.7)

| Dimension | Requirement |
|---|---|
| **Min width** | 36" above handrail, 31.5" at/below handrail |
| **Max riser height** | 7-3/4" (8-1/4" for spiral stairs) |
| **Min tread depth** | 10" (measured nose to nose) |
| **Max riser variation** | 3/8" between any two risers |
| **Max tread variation** | 3/8" between any two treads |
| **Min headroom** | 6'-8" measured vertically from tread nosing |
| **Nosing** | 3/4" to 1-1/4" projection beyond tread below |

### Winders (R318.7.2)
- Min 10" tread at 12" from narrow side
- Min 6" at narrowest point
- Not to be used as part of the required exit unless specifically designed

### Spiral Stairways (R318.7.3)
- Min 26" clear width
- Min 7-1/2" tread depth at 12" from narrow edge
- Max 9-1/2" riser height
- Min headroom: 6'-6"
- Only permitted as means of egress from an area <=400 SF

### Landings (R318.7.4)
- Required at top and bottom of each stairway
- Min length in direction of travel: 36" (or depth of door swing if door swings over landing)
- Floor or landing not more than 1-1/2" below top of threshold at exterior doors
- Landing not more than 7-3/4" below interior door threshold

## Handrails (CRC R320)

### Where Required
- At least one handrail on stairways with 4 or more risers
- Both sides for stairs wider than 44"

### Dimensions
- Height: 34" to 38" measured vertically from stair nosing
- Graspable profile: circular cross-section 1-1/4" to 2" outside diameter, or shaped to provide equivalent graspability
- Min 1-1/2" clearance between handrail and wall
- Continuous for full length of stairs (exceptions for newel posts)
- Returns: must return to wall or terminate in newel post

## Guards and Window Fall Protection (CRC R321)

### Guards (R321.1)
Required at open sides of:
- Walking surfaces (including stairs, ramps, landings) more than 30" above grade or floor below
- Min height: **36"** at porches, balconies, etc.
- Min height: **42"** at open sides of stairs
- Openings in guards: must not allow passage of a 4" sphere (except triangle at tread/riser/guard intersection: 6" sphere)

### Window Fall Protection (R321.3)
Operable windows where:
- Sill height less than 24" above finished floor, AND
- More than 72" above grade on exterior

Must have:
- Window opening control device, OR
- Fall prevention device complying with ASTM F2090

**Exception:** Emergency escape windows (egress) — fall prevention devices must be releasable.

## Replacement Windows for Egress (R319.5)

When replacing a window serving as an egress opening:
- Exempt from R319.2 dimensions and R319.4.4 requirements IF:
  1. Replacement is manufacturer's largest standard size that fits existing frame/rough opening
  2. Not part of a change of occupancy

### Existing Emergency Escape Openings (R319.7.1)
For existing buildings where change of occupancy triggers egress:
- Existing operable window: min 4 SF net clear opening, 22" height, 20" width
- Replacement window: must meet 4 SF minimum and be largest standard size fitting existing frame

## Summary for Additions

### Every Addition Must Address:
1. **Bedroom egress:** Each new sleeping room needs an egress window meeting 5.7 SF / 24" / 20" / 44"
2. **Basement egress:** New basements need at least one egress opening
3. **Exit path:** Continuous path from addition through dwelling to exit
4. **Stair compliance:** Any new stairs must meet current dimensions (7-3/4" max riser, 10" min tread)
5. **Guards:** All elevated surfaces >30" above grade need guards
6. **Window fall protection:** Low sill windows >72" above exterior grade need protection devices

### Common Plan Review Failures
- Bedroom window that meets area but not minimum height or width
- Window sill >44" above floor (casement windows sometimes have high sills)
- Stair risers exceeding 7-3/4" (very common in older homes being matched)
- Missing guards at new second-story balconies or elevated landings
- Egress window blocked by deck framing or overhang
