# Energy Code Envelope Requirements

Source: 2025 California Energy Code (Title 24, Part 6), Section 150.2 (Additions) and Section 150.1 (Prescriptive Standards).

---

## Addition Size Tiers

The Energy Code has different requirements based on addition size. Identify the applicable tier first:

| Tier | Addition Size | Key Provisions |
|---|---|---|
| Minimal | <=300 SF | Exempt from roofing product requirements (150.1(c)11) |
| Small | <=400 SF | Max fenestration: greater of 75 SF or 30% addition CFA |
| Medium | 401-700 SF | Max fenestration: greater of 120 SF or 25% addition CFA; QII not required |
| Full | >700 SF | Full prescriptive per 150.1(c) with addition-specific modifications |
| Ventilation exempt | <=1,000 SF | Exempt from whole-dwelling mechanical ventilation |
| Full ventilation | >1,000 SF | Whole-dwelling ventilation required based on entire dwelling CFA |

**PV systems are NOT required for additions** (Exception 6 to Section 150.2(a)).

## Wall Insulation

### Additions >700 SF (Section 150.2(a)1A)
Must meet Section 150.1(c) requirements with these modifications:
- Extensions of existing wood-framed walls may retain existing dimensions
- Install cavity insulation: R-15 in 2x4 framing, R-21 in 2x6 framing
- When existing siding is NOT being removed: cavity insulation only, continuous insulation not required

### Additions <=700 SF (Section 150.2(a)1B)
Same wall insulation rules as >700 SF:
- R-15 in 2x4 framing, R-21 in 2x6 framing for extensions of existing walls
- When existing siding is NOT being removed: continuous insulation not required

### Prescriptive U-Factors (Table 150.1-A, New Construction Standard)

| Climate Zones | Framed Walls Max U-Factor |
|---|---|
| CZ 1-5, 8-16 | U-0.048 |
| CZ 6-7 | U-0.065 |

**Achieving U-0.048:** Typically requires R-21 cavity + R-4 continuous insulation, or R-15 cavity + R-4 CI.

**Achieving U-0.065:** R-15 or R-21 cavity insulation alone is sufficient.

### Mass/Masonry Walls
- Above grade interior insulation: U-0.077 (R-13) all CZs except CZ 16 (U-0.059, R-17)
- Above grade exterior insulation: U-0.125 (R-8) all CZs except CZ 16 (U-0.077, R-13)
- Below grade interior: U-0.077 (R-13)
- Below grade exterior: U-0.200 (R-5) most CZs; CZ 14-15: U-0.100 (R-10); CZ 16: U-0.053 (R-19)

## Floor Insulation (Table 150.1-A)

| Floor Type | All Climate Zones (except noted) |
|---|---|
| Raised floors (wood framed) | R-19 (U-0.037) |
| Concrete raised floors | Varies by CZ: U-0.092 to U-0.269 |
| Slab perimeter | NR (no requirement) except CZ 16: R-7 |

**Exception:** Raised-floor insulation may be omitted if foundation walls are insulated to wall minimums, vapor retarder covers crawl space floor, vents have auto louvers, and RA4.5.1 is met.

|---|---|---|
| CZ 1, 3, 5 | 0.27 | NR |
| CZ 2, 4 | 0.27 | 0.23 |
| CZ 6-7 | 0.30 | 0.23 |

| CZ 11-14 | 0.27 | 0.23 |
| CZ 15 | 0.30 | 0.203 |
| CZ 16 | 0.27 | NR |

### Fenestration Area Limits for Additions

| Addition Size | Max Total Fenestration | Max West-Facing |
|---|---|---|
| >700 SF | Greater of 175 SF or 20% addition CFA | Greater of 70 SF or per Table 150.1-A (5% CFA in CZ 2, 4, 6-15) |
| 401-700 SF | Greater of 120 SF or 25% addition CFA | <=60 SF in CZ 2, 4, 6-15 |
| <=400 SF | Greater of 75 SF or 30% addition CFA | <=60 SF in CZ 2, 4, 6-15 |

**CZ 15 special:** Alterations adding fenestration must have max SHGC of 0.23.

### Doors
- Swinging doors separating conditioned from unconditioned: max U-factor 0.20
- Exception: Fire-rated garage-to-house doors exempt from U-factor requirement

## Cool Roof Requirements

### New Construction (Section 150.1(c)11)
| Roof Type | Climate Zones | Min Aged Solar Reflectance | Min Thermal Emittance | OR Min SRI |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Steep-slope | CZ 10-15 | 0.20 | 0.75 | 16 |
| Low-slope | CZ 13, 15 | 0.63 | 0.75 | 75 |

### Additions
- Additions >300 SF: must comply with 150.1(c)11 (Exception 1 to 150.2(a))
- Additions <=300 SF: exempt from roofing product requirements

### Exemptions
- Building integrated PV panels exempt
- Roof constructions >=25 lb/ft2 exempt

## Quality Insulation Installation (QII)

| Addition Size | QII Required? |
|---|---|
| >700 SF | Yes (Table 150.1-A) |
| <=700 SF | No (Section 150.2(a)1B, modification v) |

When QII is required: insulation installation must be field verified per Reference Appendix RA3.5.

For additions >700 SF extending existing walls, these QII exceptions apply:
- Existing window and door headers not required to be insulated
- Air sealing not required when existing air barrier is not being removed

## Lighting (Section 150.0(k))

All luminaires in the addition must meet high-efficacy requirements:
- JA8-compliant light sources (LED or equivalent)
- Applies to all permanently installed luminaires
- No exceptions for additions

---

## Water Heater for Additions (Section 150.2(a)1D)

When an additional water heater is installed as part of the addition, one of these is required:
1. **Heat pump water heater (HPWH):** with communication interface meeting 110.12(a) or ANSI/CTA-2045-B port; storage tank NOT outdoors; placed on insulated surface (R-10 min)
2. **NEEA Tier 3+ HPWH:** same as above but meets NEEA Advanced specification
3. **Electric point-of-use:** only for additions <=500 SF
4. **Executive Director-approved equivalent**

**No gas water heaters** for new installations in additions.

## Compliance Paths

### Prescriptive (Section 150.2(a)1)
Meet all requirements listed above for the applicable addition size tier. Most straightforward for plan review.

### Performance (Section 150.2(a)2)
Energy budget expressed in long-term system cost (LSC). Two options:
- **Addition alone:** The addition meets the energy budgets of Section 150.1(b)
- **Existing + alteration + addition:** Combined analysis using existing conditions plus proposed improvements

Performance approach allows trade-offs between components (e.g., better windows offset slightly less insulation).

## CF-1R Compliance Documentation

Every addition must have a CF-1R (Certificate of Compliance) demonstrating energy code compliance. This is typically prepared by the Title 24 energy consultant and submitted with the permit application. The CF-1R specifies:
- Climate zone
- Prescriptive or performance path
- Insulation values
- Fenestration specifications
- HVAC system specifications
- Compliance result (pass/fail)
