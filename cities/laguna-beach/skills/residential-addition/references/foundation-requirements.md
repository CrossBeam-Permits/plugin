# Foundation Requirements

Source: 2025 California Residential Code (Title 24, Part 2.5), Chapter 4.

---

## Soil Requirements (CRC R401.4)

### When a Soils Report Is Required
A soils test is required where quantifiable data indicates the likely presence of:
- Expansive soils
- Compressible soils
- Shifting soils
- Other questionable soil characteristics

The building official determines whether to require a soils test. The test must be performed by an approved agency using an approved method.

### California-Specific Soil Provisions (CRC R401.4.1.1)
- Cities/counties must enact ordinances requiring preliminary soil reports for subdivisions
- If preliminary report shows critically expansive soils, lot-by-lot soil investigation required
- Soil investigation must be prepared by a CA registered civil engineer
- Corrective action must be incorporated as a building permit condition

### Presumptive Load-Bearing Values (Table R401.4.1(1))

In lieu of a geotechnical evaluation, the following values may be assumed:

| Soil Type | Bearing Capacity (psf) |
|---|---|
| Crystalline bedrock | 12,000 |
| Sedimentary and foliated rock | 4,000 |
| Sandy gravel and/or gravel (GW, GP) | 3,000 |
| Sand, silty sand, clayey sand (SW, SP, SM, SC, GM, GC) | 2,000 |
| Clay, sandy clay, silty clay, silt (CL, ML, MH, CH) | 1,500 |

**Note:** Where the building official determines in-place soils with bearing capacity <1,500 psf are likely present, a soils investigation is required.

### Soil Classifications (Table R401.4.1(2))

| Soil Group | Drainage | Frost Heave | Expansion |
|---|---|---|---|
| Group I (GW, GP, SW, SP, GM, SM) | Good | Low-Medium | Low |
| Group II (GC, SC, ML, CL) | Medium | Medium-High | Low-Medium |
| Group III (CH, MH) | Poor | Medium-High | High |
| Group IV (OL, OH, Pt) | Poor-Unsuitable | Medium | Medium-High |

### Seismic Design Category
Where SDC is C or greater AND soil testing is performed, the geotechnical report must include:
- Site class determination
- Short-period spectral response acceleration (SDS)
- Seismic design category assignment per Table R301.2.2.1.1

## Concrete Requirements (CRC R402.2)

### Minimum Compressive Strength (Table R402.2)

| Location | Negligible Weathering | Moderate Weathering | Severe Weathering |
|---|---|---|---|
| Foundations not exposed to weather | 2,500 psi | 2,500 psi | 2,500 psi |
| Interior slabs on grade | 2,500 psi | 2,500 psi | 2,500 psi |
| Walls/foundations exposed to weather | 2,500 psi | 3,000 psi | 3,000 psi |
| Porches, steps, garage floors | 2,500 psi | 3,000 psi | 3,500 psi |

**Most of Southern California:** Negligible weathering = 2,500 psi minimum for all locations.

**Air entrainment:** Required for moderate and severe weathering concrete (5-7% air content).

## Footing Dimensions (CRC R403.1, Table R403.1(1))

### Minimum Footing Sizes

| Number of Stories | Min Width | Min Depth Below Grade | Min Thickness |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 story | 12" | 12" | 6" |
| 2 story | 15" | 18" | 7" |
| 3 story | 18" | 24" | 8" |

**California practice:** Most jurisdictions require deeper footings than the IRC minimums shown above. Typical California requirements:
- 1 story: 12" wide x 18" deep
- 2 story: 15" wide x 24" deep
- 3 story: 18" deep x 30" deep (or per engineer)

**Note:** Local frost line depth may require deeper footings. Check Table R301.2 for local requirements.

### Interior Bearing Wall Footings
- Width: same as exterior for same loading
- Depth: may be reduced if fully protected from frost
- Must bear on undisturbed soil or properly compacted fill

## Foundation Reinforcement (CRC R403.1.3)

### Minimum Reinforcement
- Concrete footings: min one #4 rebar continuous, positioned within top 12" of footing
- Splices: min 15" overlap for #4 bar (40 bar-diameters)
- Corner bars: extend min 24" past corner in each direction
- Rebar chairs or supports to maintain position during pour

### Stem Wall Reinforcement
- Stem walls >4 ft: typically require vertical and horizontal reinforcement per engineering
- Short stem walls (typ. 18-24"): one #4 horizontal bar within 12" of top

## Anchor Bolts (CRC R403.1.6)

### Requirements
- **Size:** Min 1/2" diameter
- **Embedment:** Min 7" into concrete or grouted masonry
- **Spacing:** Max 6 ft on center
- **End distance:** Within 12" of each end of sill plate, and within 12" of each side of each splice in the sill plate
- **Washer:** Standard cut washer on each bolt at plate
- **Nut:** Tightened to plate

### Anchor Bolt Placement
- Bolts must be in the center third of the sill plate width
- Min 7 bolt-diameters from each end of the plate piece
- In SDC D0-D2: square plate washers (min 3" x 3" x 3/16") may be required

### Alternative: Approved Connectors
- Proprietary foundation-to-plate connectors may be used per manufacturer's evaluation report
- Must be equivalent to code-prescribed anchorage

## Connection to Existing Foundation (CRC R403.1.5)

### Doweling Into Existing Foundation
When a new addition foundation ties into an existing foundation:
1. **Drill holes** in existing foundation at specified spacing (typically 12-16" OC)
2. **Epoxy anchors:** Use approved epoxy adhesive system for doweling rebar into existing concrete
3. **Dowel size:** Match rebar size of new foundation (typically #4)
4. **Embedment depth:** Per manufacturer's specifications for the epoxy system (typically 4-6" for #4 bar)
5. **Clean holes:** Holes must be clean and dry before epoxy injection
6. **Overlap with new rebar:** Min 15" lap splice (40 bar-diameters for #4)

### Stepped Footings (CRC R403.1.5)
- Where footings step at different elevations (common at sloped sites or connection to existing):
  - Max slope of step: 2 horizontal to 1 vertical (2:1)
  - Step height must not exceed 3/4 of the distance between steps
  - Reinforcement must be continuous through steps
  - Min overlap at step: 2 ft on each side

### Matching Existing Footing Depth
- New footing depth should match existing footing depth where they connect
- If existing footing is deeper, new footing may step down to match
- If existing footing is shallower than current code: consult building official (existing may be grandfathered)

## Slab-on-Grade (CRC R506)

### Requirements for Addition Slabs
- **Minimum thickness:** 3-1/2" (CRC R506.1)
- **Fill:** Max 8" of compacted fill below slab (or per soils report)
- **Vapor retarder:** Min 6-mil polyethylene under slab in contact with earth, unless approved alternative
- **Base course:** Min 4" of clean aggregate (1/2" to 3/4" minus) between earth and vapor retarder
- **Control joints:** Max 10 ft OC in each direction (ACI 302.1R)

### Slab Reinforcement (Common Practice)
- #3 bars at 24" OC each way, or 6x6 W1.4xW1.4 WWR on chairs
- Thickened edge at perimeter if slab serves as footing (turned-down slab)
- Thickened slab under bearing walls within the addition

## Site Drainage (CRC R401.3)

### Grading Requirements
- Surface drainage must be diverted to storm sewer or approved collection point
- Grade must fall min 6" within first 10 ft from foundation walls
- Exception: Where barriers prevent 6" fall, drains or swales must be constructed
- Impervious surfaces within 10 ft of foundation: min 2% slope away from building

---

## Crawl Space Requirements (CRC R408)

If the addition has a crawl space:
- **Access:** Min 18" x 24" opening (CRC R408.4)
- **Ventilation:** Cross ventilation required; NFA = 1/150 of underfloor area
- **Vapor retarder:** When required, min 6-mil polyethylene lapped 6" at joints, extended up foundation walls 6"
- **Height:** Min 18" from bottom of floor joists to grade in crawl space
- **Drainage:** Crawl space floor graded to drain away from footings
