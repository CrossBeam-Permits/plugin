# Fire Separation Requirements

Source: 2025 California Residential Code (Title 24, Part 2.5), Section R302.

---

## Exterior Wall Fire Separation (CRC R302.1)

Fire separation distance is measured from the building face to the closest lot line, the centerline of a street or alley, or an imaginary line between buildings on the same lot.

### Without Sprinklers — Table R302.1(1)

| Element | Fire Separation Distance | Requirement |
|---|---|---|
| **Walls** | 0 ft | 1-hour fire-resistance rated (both sides) |
| **Walls** | >=5 ft | No rating required |
| **Projections** | <2 ft | Not allowed |
| **Projections** | 2 ft to <5 ft | 1-hour on underside (or heavy timber, or FRT wood) |
| **Projections** | >=5 ft | No rating required |
| **Openings** | <3 ft | Not allowed |
| **Openings** | 3 ft | 25% max of wall area |
| **Openings** | >=5 ft | Unlimited |
| **Penetrations** | <3 ft | Must comply with R302.4 |
| **Penetrations** | >=3 ft | No protection required |

### With Sprinklers — Table R302.1(2)

When dwelling is sprinklered per R309:

| Element | Fire Separation Distance | Requirement |
|---|---|---|
| **Walls** | 0 ft | 1-hour rated (outside exposure only) |
| **Walls** | >=3 ft | No rating required |
| **Projections** | <2 ft | Not allowed |
| **Projections** | 2 ft | 1-hour on underside |
| **Projections** | >=3 ft | No rating required |
| **Openings** | <3 ft | Not allowed |
| **Openings** | >=3 ft | Unlimited |

**Subdivision exception:** In sprinklered subdivisions where the adjoining lot has >=6 ft open setback, fire separation for walls can be 0 ft with unlimited openings.

### Exceptions to R302.1
1. Walls perpendicular to the fire separation line
2. Walls between dwelling units on the same lot and their accessory buildings
3. Detached sheds/playhouses exempt from permits: no wall protection required
4. Detached garages <=2 ft from lot line: roof eave projections up to 4" permitted
5. Foundation vents complying with code

### Eave Overhang Fire Protection
- Projection 2 ft to <5 ft from lot line: 1-hour underside rating
- Rating may be reduced to 0 hours if:
  - Fireblocking from wall top plate to roof sheathing underside (eave), OR
  - No vent openings in overhang or gable wall (rake)

## Garage-to-House Separation (CRC R302.6, Table R302.6)

### Separation Requirements (Table R302.6)

| Separation | Material |
|---|---|
| Garage walls shared with dwelling | Min 1/2" gypsum board on garage side |
| Garage ceiling below habitable space | Min 5/8" Type X gypsum board |
| Structural members supporting dwelling/garage separation | Min 1/2" gypsum board or equivalent |

### Door Requirements (R302.6)
Openings between garage and dwelling:
- **No opening directly into a sleeping room**
- Door must be: solid wood >=1-3/8" thick, OR solid/honeycomb core steel >=1-3/8" thick, OR 20-minute fire-rated assembly
- Self-closing or automatic-closing device required
- Raised threshold not required (but floor must be sloped for drainage)

### Duct Penetrations (R302.6)
- Ducts penetrating garage-to-dwelling walls/ceilings: min No. 26 gage sheet steel or approved material
- No duct openings (supply or return registers) in the garage

### Garage Floor
- Noncombustible material (concrete)
- Sloped toward main vehicle entry or drain

## Dwelling Unit Separation — Duplexes (CRC R302.3)

### Fire-Resistance Rating (R302.3.2)
- Without sprinklers: 1-hour fire-resistance rating
- With sprinklers (R309): 1/2-hour fire-resistance rating
- Testing per ASTM E119, UL 263, or CBC 703.2.2

### Continuity (R302.3.3)
- Assemblies must provide continuous fire-resistance rating between units
- Horizontal assemblies: extend to and be tight against exterior walls or vertical separations
- Vertical assemblies: extend from foundation to roof sheathing (or ceiling with conditions)

### Vertical Assembly Alternative (R302.3.3.2)
Vertical assembly may terminate at ceiling if:
- Ceiling: min 5/8" Type X gypsum board
- Attic draft stop above and along vertical assembly
- Structural framing supporting ceiling: min 1/2" gypsum board

### Supporting Construction (R302.3.4)
Vertical and horizontal assemblies must be supported by construction with equal or greater fire-resistance rating.

### Vertically Stacked Units Without Sprinklers (R302.3.5)
When one unit is above the other and no sprinklers:
1. Assemblies must limit smoke transfer
2. Notification appliance connected to other unit's smoke alarms in each unit

### Shared Accessory Rooms (R302.3.6)
Shared utility rooms, storage rooms between duplex units:

| Separation | Material |
|---|---|
| From dwelling units and attics | Min 1/2" gypsum on accessory room side |
| From habitable rooms above/below | Min 5/8" Type X gypsum |
| Supporting structures | Min 1/2" gypsum |

- No opening from shared room directly to sleeping room
- Doors: solid wood >=1-3/8", solid steel, or 20-minute fire-rated, self-closing

## Fireblocking (CRC R302.11)

### Where Required
Fireblocking must be installed in concealed spaces of stud walls, partitions, and furred spaces at:

1. **Ceiling and floor levels** — block vertical spread of fire in stud cavities
2. **All interconnections** between vertical and horizontal concealed spaces (soffits, drop ceilings, cove ceilings)
3. **Top and bottom of stair stringers** at floor and ceiling levels
4. **Openings around vents, pipes, ducts** at ceiling and floor levels
5. **Chimneys and fireplaces** — fireblocking at each floor level and ceiling
6. **Cornices** at the top plate level at the intersection with exterior walls

### Acceptable Materials
- 2-inch nominal lumber (actual 1-1/2")
- Two thicknesses of 1-inch nominal lumber with broken lap joints
- 23/32-inch wood structural panels with joints backed by 23/32" panels
- 1/2-inch gypsum board
- 1/4-inch cement-based millboard
- Batts or blankets of mineral/glass fiber (unfaced, no paper)

### Exterior Wall-Ceiling Intersection
Fireblocking is critical at the intersection of exterior walls and ceiling framing, especially at soffits and eave returns.

## Draftstopping (CRC R302.12)

### Floor-Ceiling Assemblies (R302.12.1)
Draftstopping required in floor-ceiling assemblies where the floor below has usable space above:
- Must divide the concealed space into areas <=1,000 SF
- Located above/below supporting walls and partitions
- Located above dwelling unit separation walls (duplex)

### Attic Spaces (R302.12.2)
Draftstopping required in attic spaces of dwelling unit separations and above the assembly separating dwelling units.

### Acceptable Materials
- 1/2-inch gypsum board
- 3/8-inch wood structural panels
- 3/8-inch particleboard
- 1-inch nominal lumber
- Other approved materials adequate to prevent free passage of flame and products of combustion

---

## Penetration Firestopping (CRC R302.4)

### Through Penetrations (R302.4.1)
Through penetrations of fire-resistance-rated assemblies must:
- Be installed as tested in the approved fire-resistance-rated assembly, OR
- Be protected by an approved penetration firestop system (ASTM E814 or UL 1479)
- F rating >= required fire-resistance rating of the assembly

### Membrane Penetrations (R302.4.2)
Membrane penetrations (penetrating only one side):
- Steel electrical boxes: max 16 sq in, min 24" separation between boxes on opposite sides
- Other: approved listed firestop caulk, putty, or wrap

### Practical Applications in Additions
- Plumbing and electrical penetrations through garage-to-house walls
- HVAC duct penetrations through rated assemblies
- Any pipe, conduit, or cable penetrating a rated wall or floor

## Fire Sprinklers

### When Required (CRC R309)
Fire sprinklers are required for:
- New residential construction (varies by local adoption)
- Additions that create conditions triggering sprinkler requirements
- Attached garages with habitable space above (R317.5.1)

### Addition Exception (R317.5.1)
Automatic residential fire sprinkler system NOT required when additions or alterations are made to existing carports/garages that do not already have sprinklers.

### Impact on Fire Separation
When sprinklers are installed throughout:
- Exterior wall fire-resistance requirements may be reduced (Table R302.1(2))
- Dwelling unit separation may be reduced to 1/2-hour (R302.3.2)
- Additional openings may be permitted in fire-separation-distance walls
