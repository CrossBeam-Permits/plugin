# Laguna Beach WUI Code — Fire-Resistive Construction Requirements

Source: the City source document (not included in this plugin) (LBMC Ch. 14.70, Ord. 1725, 12/18/2025)

This file extracts the WUI code amendments most relevant to roofing, exterior walls, and construction in the VHFHSZ.

---

## Scope (§14.70.015, amending 101.2)

Applies to: building materials, systems, and assemblies used in **exterior design and construction** of:
- New buildings
- Additions
- Alterations
- Repairs

Located within the wildland-urban interface (WUI) area in Laguna Beach.

**Exception**: With building official approval, minor repairs or replacement of exterior materials may use same materials as existing.

---

## Roof Assembly (§14.70.020, amending 504.2)

**ALL roofs and ALL re-roofs shall have a roof assembly that complies with CLASS A fire classification** when tested per ASTM E108 or UL 790.

**Exception**: With building official approval, small sections may be re-roofed with same existing material.

**CRITICAL FOR PLAN REVIEW**: This means every re-roof in Laguna Beach's WUI area (essentially the whole city) must be Class A. This is MORE restrictive than state code which allows Class A, B, or C depending on FHSZ.

---

## Vent Size Restriction (§14.70.025, adding 504.10.4)

Each individual ventilation opening into:
- Enclosed attics
- Enclosed eave soffit spaces
- Enclosed rafter spaces (where ceilings applied directly to underside of roof rafters)
- Underfloor spaces

**Must NOT exceed 144 square inches** in area.

Openings >144 sq in must be protected by minimum **1-3/8" thick solid core wooden door or equivalent**.

---

## Exterior Wall Coverings (§14.70.030, amending 504.5.2)

Exterior wall covering or wall assembly (including insulation) must comply with ONE of:
1. **Noncombustible material**
2. **Ignition-resistant building material** labeled for exterior use
3. **Fire-retardant-treated wood** labeled for exterior use per CBC §2303.2
4. **Fire-retardant-treated wood shingles/shakes** qualified as "Class B" roof covering per CBC §1505.6 — acceptable as wall covering when installed over solid sheathing

---

## Vinyl Windows (§14.70.035, adding 504.8.3)

Vinyl window frames must have:
- **Welded corners**
- **Metal reinforcing** in the interlock area

---

## Skylights (§14.70.040, adding 504.8.4)

Skylights and sloped glazing must:
- Comply with Section 504.8
- **Must be of GLASS**
- **Plastic skylights and plastic sloped glazing are NOT allowed**

---

## Defensible Space (§14.70.060, adding 604.1.1)

All existing habitable buildings in VHFHSZ must maintain defensible space per the **Defensible Space Guideline for Existing Structures in the VHFHSZ** (dated August 2021, as amended by City Council resolution).

**Exceptions**:
1. New construction or major remodels in Fuel Modification areas → must comply with Section 613 (fuel modification plan)
2. Existing structures built under Fuel Modification Guidelines (Resolution 89.104) → maintain per approved plan

---

## Fuel Modification for New Construction/Major Remodel (§14.70.070, adding 613)

All new buildings or major remodels in Fuel Modification designation areas must:
1. Submit preliminary fuel modification plans to fire code official with tentative map
2. Submit final fuel modification plans before building permit
3. Plans must meet criteria in the 2019 LB Fire Dept Landscape/Fuel Modification Standards
4. Plans may be altered with fire code official prior approval
5. All elements must be maintained per approved plan

## Summary: WUI Requirements Affecting Re-Roofs

| Requirement | Code Section | Detail |
|------------|-------------|--------|
| **Class A roof** | §14.70.020 (504.2) | ALL roofs/re-roofs must be Class A per ASTM E108/UL 790 |
| **Vent restrictions** | §14.70.025 (504.10.4) | Max 144 sq in per opening |
| **No plastic skylights** | §14.70.040 (504.8.4) | Glass only |
| **Exterior walls** | §14.70.030 (504.5.2) | Noncombustible or ignition-resistant |
| **Vinyl windows** | §14.70.035 (504.8.3) | Welded corners + metal reinforcing |
| **Minor repair exception** | §14.70.015 | Same material OK with building official approval |
