## Part B — Ord. 1713 rules that differ from or add to state law

### Approval paths — 25.17.010(d)

- **(d)(1) CDP.** Any ADU or JADU in the coastal zone except an application that "only seeks to convert existing livable space in a legally established primary dwelling unit" into an ADU or JADU ((d)(1)(C)(ii)). Processed as de minimis, minor (25.07.013) or regular (25.07.012) development ((d)(1)(C)(iii)). The City "may elect to hold public hearings" ((d)(1)(A)). Appeals under Chapters 25.07 and 25.05 ((d)(1)(C)(iv)).
- **(d)(2) Building permit only** (no CDP and meets (f)):
  - (A) converted on a single-family lot, with up to 150 sf added to an accessory structure for ingress and egress;
  - (B) one new detached ADU on a single-family lot, side and rear setbacks at least 4 ft, total floor area 800 sf or less, height within (f)(2). This is the guaranteed 800 sf path;
  - (C) converted ADUs in non-livable space of existing multifamily structures, at least one and up to 25% of existing units;
  - (D) up to two detached ADUs on a multifamily lot, 4 ft side and rear setbacks, height within (f)(2).
- **(d)(3) ADU permit plus building permit:** no CDP and not a (d)(2) scenario. Meets (f) and (g). The ADU-permit fee is set by Council resolution ((d)(3)(B)); amount from the fee tool.

### Process — 25.17.010(e)

- Non-CDP ADUs: ministerial, no discretionary review, no hearing ((e)(2)(A)); 60-day rule ((e)(2)(B), state floor).
- Delay allowed only when filed with an application to create a new single-family or multifamily dwelling ((e)(2)(B)(ii)).
- Garage demolition for an ADU reviewed and issued with the ADU ((e)(2)(D)).
- CDP ADUs: processed under Chapter 25.07; the Permit Streamlining Act applies "to the extent required by state law" ((e)(1)). The 66329 rules are state floor (state skill).

### Coastal requirements — 25.17.020

Applies to every ADU and JADU in the coastal zone that is not exempt under 25.17.010(d)(1)(C)(ii) (25.17.020(a)).

- (b) Must not conflict with any coastal resource protection policy in the certified LCP or the Coastal Act. More restrictive standards may be imposed to protect "hazardous areas, environmentally sensitive areas, wetlands, public views, and public trails and accessways."
- (c)(1) Setbacks: underlying-zone front setback; 25.50.004(D) additional front and rear building setback; 25 ft from the top of an oceanfront bluff; 25 ft from a watercourse centerline, and 25 ft from top of bank for a USGS blue-line stream.
- (c)(2) Replacement parking: garage, carport or covered parking demolished or converted is reviewed with the ADU; existing spaces replaced on site 1:1, capped at the dwelling's required minimum; may be uncovered, tandem, or in a setback (not a bluff or watercourse setback) at least 3 ft from all property lines; entirely on private property and not in a required turnaround.
- (c)(3) Pedestrian access: "An all-weather firefighter access path must be provided from the right-of-way to the front door of the ADU or JADU. The access must be a minimum of 3 feet wide and non-combustible. Stairs must be provided for any slope greater than 10 percent."
- (c)(4) Vehicular access: same access as the primary dwelling unless Fire and Public Works find secondary access will not degrade safety or traffic; new access to streets or alleys "is subject to discretionary design review approval and potentially other entitlements" (Design Review, 25.05.040(B)(1)(k)).
- (c)(5) Outdoor living space attached to an ADU meets the ADU's height and setbacks; roof decks prohibited ("a deck above the uppermost story of a building, with no access from interior living space at the same floor level").
- (d)(1) "No ADU or JADU may cause a violation of any provision in the certified LCP."
- (d)(2) ADUs do not count toward the major-remodel calculation, "except as necessary to comply with section 30253 of the Coastal Act limiting alterations of existing bluff top, beachfront, and coastal canyons".

### Drafting points to flag

- (d)(1)(C)(iii) cites "LBMC section 25.07.008" for de minimis development. The de minimis waiver is 25.07.009.
- (d)(1)(C)(ii) cites "LBMC § 25.07.008(1)(1)(a)". The certified section is 25.07.008(A)(1)(a).
- (d)(2)(D) says "a lot with a proposed multifamily dwelling". State law covers existing multifamily lots too (state skill). Read it with the state floor.
- **(f) and (g) apply to (d)(2) and (d)(3) ADUs only. A CDP ADU is a (d)(1) ADU.** It is held to the certified LCP, including certified Chapter 25.17, plus Ord. 1713 25.17.020 (Part D).

## Part C — Certified LCP Chapter 25.17 (Ord. 1687/1691)

Use this text for the LCP-conformity finding on an ADU CDP. It is not the local permit rule for applications filed since 3/12/2025.

### Guaranteed allowance — 25.17.040(D)

"The following types of ADUs and JADUs require compliance with this subsection D and other development standards do not apply. Coastal resource protection policies continue to apply."

- (D)(1) One attached or detached ADU up to 850 sf, or 1,000 sf with more than one bedroom, with 4 ft side and rear setbacks.
- (D)(2) Converted ADU or JADU within a primary dwelling or accessory structure; up to 75 sf expansion for any use, plus up to 150 sf for ingress and egress at an accessory structure. A converted JADU must be inside existing habitable space.
- (D)(3) Multifamily lots: converted or detached ADUs, not both.
- (D)(4) Heights: 16 ft detached; 18 ft near major transit (plus 2 ft to match roof pitch); 18 ft on a multistory multifamily lot; attached 25 ft or the zone limit, whichever is lower.

### Non-guaranteed ADUs — 25.17.040(E)

| Item | Rule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Size | 150 sf minimum; JADU 500 sf maximum; detached 850 / 1,000 sf; attached the same or 50% of the primary, whichever is less | (E)(1) |
| Height | Limits in (D)(4); "ADUs above the height limitations shall be subject to design review in accordance with Section 25.05.040"; measured to the lowest floor or grade, including chimneys, vents, mechanical enclosures and stairways | (E)(2)(a)-(b) |
| Setbacks | 4 ft side and rear; 25 ft from oceanfront bluff top; 25 ft from watercourse centerline; greater setbacks to protect coastal resources; 25.50.004(D) applies | (E)(3) |

| Lighting | Hooded, fully shielded, aimed down; no glare | (E)(5)(b) |
| Outdoor living space | Meets ADU height, setbacks and design standards; limited to 20% of the ADU floor area; "Elevated decks more than 3′ above adjacent existing grade shall require design review. Roof decks shall be prohibited." | (E)(5)(c) |
| Parking | One space unless exempt (12 exemptions); in the coastal zone, demolished or converted parking replaced 1:1 up to the required minimum | (E)(6) |

### Other certified provisions

- 25.17.030(A)(3): ADUs not counted toward the major-remodel calculation, except as needed for Coastal Act section 30253 (bluff top, beachfront, coastal canyons).
- 25.17.050: independent exterior access; both addresses visible; historic-property ADUs meet the Secretary of the Interior's Standards; new vehicular access subject to design review.
- 25.17.070 and 25.17.080: fee refunds for deed-restricted affordable ADUs (10-year minimum) and ADA-compliant ADUs.
- 25.17.090: Coastal Act not superseded, "except that the City shall not be required to hold public hearings for coastal development permit applications for ADUs."
- 25.17.100: no public notice or hearing unless the ADU needs a discretionary permit such as a CDP; then that permit's notice and appeal rules apply.
- 25.17.110: code enforcement amnesty (5-year enforcement delay on request, until January 1, 2030, where not needed for health and safety).

## Part D — Which text binds which ADU, and where they differ

**The text settles which binds.**
- **ADU that needs a CDP (a (d)(1) ADU): the certified LCP binds.** "When an ADU or JADU requires a
  coastal development permit under the City's certified Local Coastal Program ("LCP"), including this
  chapter, the ADU or JADU must comply with the requirements of the City's certified LCP"
  (25.17.010(d)(1)(B), Ord. 1713). "No ADU or JADU may cause a violation of any provision in the
  certified LCP" (25.17.020(d)(1)). The CDP needs a finding of conformity with the certified LCP
  (25.07.012(G)(1)), and the certified Chapter 25.17 (Part C) is part of it. Ord. 1713 25.17.020 also
  applies by its own terms (25.17.020(a)). Ord. 1713 (f) and (g) do not: (f) covers ADUs "approved
  under subsections (d)(2) or (d)(3)" and (g) "only ... ADUs that require an ADU permit under
  subsection (d)(3)". So for a CDP ADU apply the right-hand column below plus every 25.17.020 row; a
  left-hand limit outside 25.17.020 does not bind it (if Planning applies one anyway, FLAG it).
- **ADU with no CDP:** Ord. 1713 only (left-hand column). The certified text does not apply.
- **Still open:** a certified limit that falls below the state floor (Gov. Code 66329(a) keeps the
  Coastal Act in force, but a limit that does not carry out the Coastal Act is a state-law question;
  FLAG, do not FAIL), and how the City reads the two texts where both bind a CDP ADU and differ
  (meet both). Confirm with Planning at pre-application.

| Topic | Ord. 1713 | Certified Chapter 25.17 |
|---|---|---|
| Firefighter access path | Required for coastal non-exempt ADUs: 3 ft, all-weather, noncombustible, stairs over 10% slope (25.17.020(c)(3)) | Not in the certified text |
| Attached ADU stories | Not more than two stories ((f)(2)(D)) | No story limit stated |

**Over-height ADU: which governs, and why.** Both texts take an over-height ADU off the ministerial
track; they differ only in the vehicle.
- **No CDP:** Ord. 1713 alone. Over the (f)(2) peak height, the route is redesign or a conditional use
  permit under (j). "The Planning Commission shall review all Conditional Use Permit applications"
  (25.05.030(E)).
- **CDP ADU:** the certified LCP binds ((d)(1)(B)), so its height limit and its "design review"
  requirement apply. The local permit for departing from a standard is still (j): (d)(1)(B) is one of
  "subsections (a) through (i)", so an ADU over the certified limit does not conform to it, and (j)
  is the only provision in the current zoning text that allows it. Our reading: the CUP governs the
  route, and the certified design review is held in the same hearing, not as a separate DRB
  approval. Once a CUP is in the project, the Planning Commission hears the whole project: it takes
  "projects for which the planning commission is the primary discretionary review authority, such as
  approval of a conditional use permit" (25.05.040(F)(1)(ii)), and the CDP goes to "the same" body
  (25.07.012(D)). So say: CUP, Design Review and CDP for the ADU and every other piece, at one
  Planning Commission hearing.
- **State floor first:** a detached ADU at or under 16 ft (18 ft near major transit, plus up to 2 ft
  to match the house roof pitch; 18 ft on a multistory multifamily lot) or an attached ADU within 25 ft
  or the zone limit cannot be sent to a CUP for height (Gov. Code 66321(b)(4); state skill). The CUP
  route is only for height above what state law guarantees. Never call an over-height ADU "allowed
  by right".
