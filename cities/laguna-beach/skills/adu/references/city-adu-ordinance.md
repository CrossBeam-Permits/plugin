# Laguna Beach ADU/JADU Development Standards — Quick Reference

Source: Laguna Beach Municipal Code Chapter 25.17 (Ord. 1687, 2023; Ord. 1691, 2023)
Scraped from: https://ecode360.com/42899740

---

## Zones Where ADUs Are Permitted

ADUs are permitted on any lot with an existing or proposed dwelling in a residential zone. ADUs are also permitted in nonresidential zones on lots with legal nonconforming dwellings per §25.17.040(A)(2).

---

## ADU Types Allowed

| Type | Description | Section |
|------|-------------|---------|
| Attached ADU | Physical expansion of primary dwelling, attached to it | 25.17.020 |
| Detached ADU | Independent structure entirely separate from primary dwelling | 25.17.020 |
| Converted ADU | Within walls of primary dwelling or existing detached structure (including garage conversions) | 25.17.020 |
| JADU | Up to 500 sq ft, entirely within single-family residence, separate entrance required | 25.17.020 |

---

## Number of Units Allowed (Section 25.17.040(C)-(D))

- **Single-family lots**: 1 ADU (attached, detached, or converted) + 1 JADU
- **Multifamily lots — converted**: Minimum 1 ADU from livable/non-livable space; additional conversions up to 25% of existing units (from non-livable space: storage, boiler rooms, attics, basements, garages)
- **Multifamily lots — detached**: Up to 2 detached ADUs (18 ft max height, 4 ft side/rear setbacks)
- **Multifamily lots**: Either converted OR detached ADUs, not both

---

## Size Limits (Section 25.17.040(D)(1)-(2), (E)(1))

### Guaranteed Allowance (Section D — fewer standards apply)

| Unit Type | Max Size |
|-----------|----------|
| Attached or detached ADU (studio/1-bed) | 850 sq ft |
| Attached or detached ADU (2+ bedrooms) | 1,000 sq ft |

### Non-Guaranteed (Section E — all standards apply)

| Unit Type | Min Size | Max Size |
|-----------|----------|----------|
| Any ADU or JADU | 150 sq ft | -- |
| JADU | 150 sq ft | 500 sq ft |
| Detached ADU (studio/1-bed) | 150 sq ft | 850 sq ft |
| Detached ADU (2+ bed) | 150 sq ft | 1,000 sq ft |
| Detached ADU (ADA compliant) | 150 sq ft | 1,000 sq ft |
| Attached ADU (studio/1-bed) | 150 sq ft | 850 sq ft or 50% of primary dwelling, whichever is less |
| Attached ADU (2+ bed) | 150 sq ft | 1,000 sq ft or 50% of primary dwelling, whichever is less |
| Attached ADU (ADA compliant) | 150 sq ft | 1,000 sq ft or 50% of primary dwelling, whichever is less |

### Converted ADU Expansion Allowances (Section D(2))

- Up to **75 sq ft** expansion beyond existing structure dimensions (any use)
- Accessory structures: additional **150 sq ft** expansion for ingress/egress

---

## Height Limits (Section 25.17.040(D)(4))

| Scenario | Max Height |
|----------|------------|
| Detached ADU on single-family or multifamily lot | **16 ft** |
| Detached ADU within 1/2 mile walking distance of major transit stop or high-quality transit corridor | **18 ft** (+ 2 ft additional to match primary dwelling roof pitch) |
| Detached ADU on multifamily, multistory lot | **18 ft** |
| Detached ADU on multifamily lot (guaranteed allowance) | **18 ft** |
| Attached ADU | **25 ft** or zoning code height limit for primary dwelling, whichever is lower |

Height measured from finished roof surface to lowest finished floor or natural/finished grade, whichever is more restrictive. Includes chimneys, vents, mechanical enclosures, stairways (Section 25.17.040(E)(2)(b)).

ADUs above these height limits require **design review** per Section 25.05.040.

---

## Setback Requirements (Section 25.17.040(D)(1), (E)(3))

| Setback Type | Requirement |
|-------------|-------------|
| Interior side yard | **4 ft** minimum |
| Rear yard | **4 ft** minimum |
| Oceanfront bluff | **25 ft** from top of bluff |
| Watercourse | **25 ft** from centerline |
| Converted ADU (existing nonconforming structure) | May maintain existing nonconforming setbacks; expansions beyond existing footprint must meet standard setbacks |

Greater setbacks may be imposed for coastal resource protection (hazardous areas, ESHA, wetlands, public views, trails) per certified LCP. Section 25.50.004(D) additional building setbacks apply.

---

## Lot Coverage

No ADU-specific lot coverage provisions in Chapter 25.17. Standard zoning district lot coverage rules apply per Section 25.17.030(D) (unless the guaranteed allowance applies, in which case only Section D standards govern).

---

## Parking Requirements (Section 25.17.040(E)(6))

### Required Parking
- **1 parking space per ADU** (unless exempt)
- May be tandem on driveway
- Minimum **3 ft from side property lines** (larger setbacks may apply per Fire/Building)
- Not within required on-site turnaround area

### Replacement Parking (Coastal Zone)
When garage/carport is demolished or converted for ADU: replace existing parking **1:1 ratio** on-site, but not more than minimum required off-street spaces for primary structure. Replacement may be uncovered, tandem, or in front/side setback on private property.

### Parking Exemptions — No parking required when:
1. JADU (always exempt)
2. Converted ADU from existing habitable space or accessory structure (replacement still required if existing parking is displaced)
3. ADU deed-restricted as affordable housing
4. ADA-compliant ADU
5. ADU within **1/2 mile walking distance of public transit** or within Downtown Specific Plan area
6. ADU on lot within **100 ft of free on-street parking** with adequate supply and no emergency access degradation
7. ADU in structure on California Register of Historic Resources or City historic register
8. ADU on property within locked-gate community
9. On-street parking permits required but not offered to ADU occupant
10. Car-share vehicle within **1 block** of ADU
11. ADU proposed as part of existing primary structure
12. ADU submitted with application for new primary structure on same lot

---

## Owner-Occupancy Requirements (Section 25.17.050(I))

- **ADUs**: Not required.
- **JADUs**: Required. Owner must permanently reside in either the primary dwelling or the JADU, with satisfactory documentation.

---

## Rental and Sale Restrictions (Section 25.17.050(K)-(L))

- **Minimum rental term**: 31 consecutive calendar days (no short-term/vacation rentals)
- **No separate sale**: ADU cannot be sold separately from primary residence unless specifically permitted by state law

---

## Design Standards (Section 25.17.040(E)(5))

| Standard | Requirement |
|----------|-------------|
| Architectural match | Exterior materials, finish, color scheme, roof design, and pitch must match primary dwelling — applies to ADUs **above 12 ft** in height that are **visible from any public or private roadway** (excluding alleys) |
| Lighting | Outdoor lighting must be hooded, fully shielded, aimed downward; no light trespass/glare |
| Outdoor living space | Covered porches/decks/patios limited to **20% of ADU floor area**; must meet height, setback, and design standards |
| Elevated decks | Decks more than **3 ft above adjacent grade** require design review |
| Roof decks | **Prohibited** |

---

## Utility Connections (Section 25.17.060)

- All ADUs/JADUs must connect to public water, electric, and sewer
- **Guaranteed allowance ADUs** (Section D): Not considered new residential uses for utility connection/fee purposes (no new connection fee or capacity charge), unless built with a new primary dwelling
- **Non-guaranteed ADUs**: City may require new/separate utility connection; connection fee must be proportionate to ADU burden (based on sq ft or DFU values)
- **Onsite water treatment**: Requires percolation test within last 5 years (or recertified within 10 years); local health officer approval may be required

---

## Impact Fees (Section 25.17.060(B))

| ADU Size | Impact Fees |
|----------|-------------|
| Less than 750 sq ft | **No impact fees** |
| 750 sq ft or more | Proportionate to primary dwelling square footage |

---

## Additional Standards (Section 25.17.050)

| Topic | Requirement |
|-------|-------------|
| Access | ADU/JADU must have independent exterior access; interior access to primary allowed |
| Addresses | Both primary and ADU addresses must be visible from public right-of-way |
| JADU deed restriction | Required: prohibition on separate sale, size/attribute restrictions, runs with land |
| Fire sprinklers | Not required if not required for primary dwelling; JADU not considered separate residential unit |
| Kitchen — ADU | Full kitchen required |
| Kitchen — JADU | Efficiency kitchen (cooking facility, appliances, food prep counter, storage cabinets) |
| JADU bathroom | If no separate bathroom, must have separate entrance from main entrance + interior entry to main living area |
| Historic resources | ADU projects on historic properties must comply with Secretary of Interior Standards (§25.17.050(E)) |
| Nonconformities | Conversion of legal nonconforming structures need not correct nonconforming zoning; must meet current building/electrical/fire code |
| Passageway | Not required |
| Vehicular access | Must use same vehicular access as primary; secondary access requires Fire/Public Works finding of no safety/traffic degradation; new vehicular access to streets/alleys requires design review |
| Space between buildings | Minimum per Building and Fire Code (Section 25.17.040(E)(4)) |

## Laguna Beach-Specific Provisions (Differences from State Law)

1. **Coastal Zone overlay**: ADUs not exempted under Chapter 25.07 require a Coastal Development Permit (see `city-adu-faq.md` for CDP categories and fees). City is not required to hold public hearings for CDP applications for ADUs. (Section 25.17.090)
2. **Bluff and watercourse setbacks**: 25 ft from oceanfront bluff top and 25 ft from watercourse centerline — these are Laguna Beach-specific coastal resource protections not found in state ADU law. (Section 25.17.040(E)(3)(b)-(c))
3. **Replacement parking in Coastal Zone**: Existing parking must be replaced 1:1 when garage/carport is demolished or converted — more restrictive than state law which generally does not require replacement parking. (Section 25.17.040(E)(6)(a))
4. **Design review triggers**: ADUs above height limits require design review; elevated decks >3 ft above grade require design review; new vehicular access requires design review. (Sections 25.17.040(E)(2)(a), (E)(5)(c), 25.17.050(M))
5. **Architectural matching**: Required for ADUs above 12 ft visible from roadways — state law does not impose this level of design standard for ministerially-approved ADUs. (Section 25.17.040(E)(5)(a))
6. **Roof decks prohibited**: Explicit prohibition not found in state ADU law. (Section 25.17.040(E)(5)(c))
7. **Outdoor living space cap**: 20% of ADU floor area for covered exterior spaces — state law has no such limit. (Section 25.17.040(E)(5)(c))
8. **Public transit definition**: Explicitly includes Laguna Beach Transit and Trolley for parking exemption purposes. (Section 25.17.020)
9. **Downtown Specific Plan area**: Parking exemption applies within DSP area — city-specific geographic exemption. (Section 25.17.040(E)(6)(c)(v))
10. **Code enforcement amnesty**: Until January 1, 2030, owners of pre-2020 ADUs or ADUs built under formerly noncompliant ordinances may request 5-year delay in enforcement if corrections are not necessary for health/safety. (Section 25.17.110). Pre-2018 unpermitted ADUs may also qualify for code enforcement amnesty under §25.17.030(C)(5).
11. **Affordable housing incentive**: All city building/planning/zoning/impact fees refunded after final for deed-restricted affordable ADUs (10-year minimum deed restriction). (Section 25.17.070)
12. **ADA/accessible housing incentive**: All city building/planning/zoning fees refunded after final for ADA-compliant ADUs. (Section 25.17.080)

---

## Processing (Section 25.17.030)

- **Ministerial review**: 60-day approval/denial deadline (deemed approved if exceeded)
- **Exception**: If ADU/JADU submitted with new primary dwelling application, ADU follows primary dwelling timeline (no public hearing for ADU itself)
- **Deviations from objective standards**: Subject to design review, variance, CDP, and other discretionary entitlements
- **Denial**: City must provide full comment set listing deficiencies and remedies
