# Laguna Beach ADU — Coastal Development Permit Requirements

Source: the City source document (not included in this plugin) (LBMC Ch. 25.07, ecode360.com/42898801)

> **City overlay — local delta only.** This file covers Laguna Beach-specific CDP procedures, fees, notice requirements, and the habitable area conversion exemption. For state-level Coastal Act policies, CDP exemption rules, required findings framework, and Commission appeal procedures, see `../../coastal-development/references/california-baseline.md`.

Nearly all of Laguna Beach is within the coastal zone, so most ADUs will need to navigate this chapter.

> **ADU-only CDPs follow state procedure first.** For a CDP that covers only an ADU, Gov. Code 66329(a) and (c) control the procedure: decision within 60 days of a complete application, concurrent with the ADU application, no required public hearing, and no appeal to the Coastal Commission under PRC 30603, even in the appealable area (City ADU & JADU Application, footnote 4; state skill the California ADU (Accessory Dwelling Unit) rules (Permit Process — Ministerial Review, Timelines, and Deemed Approval), which come from the CrossBeam connector (see "California Code Compliance" in `../SKILL.md`)). Ord. 1713 lets the City elect to hold a hearing (25.17.010(d)(1)(A)). The Chapter 25.07 notice, hearing and Commission-appeal rules below apply in full to a CDP that also covers other development. Fees: use the fee tool (`estimate_fees`); no amounts are quoted here.

## CDP Requirement (S 25.07.004)

A coastal development permit is required for **all proposed development** within the coastal zone except for development specifically exempted under SS 25.07.008 and 25.07.010.

---

## Exemptions (S 25.07.008)

### ADU-Specific Exemption (S 25.07.008(A)(1)(a))

Improvements to single-family dwellings are exempt **unless** they fall into certain categories. The first disqualifying category is:

> Guest houses and self-contained accessory dwelling units (**excluding conversions of habitable area within the walls of the primary dwelling unit**; where habitable area means an area that meets the requirements of the California Building Code (CBC) for sleeping, living, cooking, or dining purposes, excluding enclosed places (e.g., closets, bath or toilet rooms, hallways, laundries, pantries, storage spaces, utility rooms, etc.))

**Translation for ADU review:**
- **New construction ADU** (detached or attached addition) = NOT exempt, CDP required
- **Garage conversion ADU** = NOT exempt (garage is not habitable area), CDP required
- **Conversion of existing livable space** (e.g., converting a bedroom/den into an ADU) = EXEMPT from CDP
- **JADU** (always within existing walls of primary dwelling) = EXEMPT from CDP

### Other Exemption Disqualifiers (S 25.07.008(A)(1)(b)-(g))

Even a conversion ADU loses its exemption if it falls within the sensitive-area or threshold exceptions. The 10% floor-area, 10% height and detached-structure items in (A)(1)(c) apply **only** between the sea and the first public road paralleling the sea, or within 300 ft of the inland extent of any beach or of the mean high tide line where there is no beach. The location items in (A)(1)(b) (beach, wetland, stream, seaward of mean high tide, within 50 ft of a bluff edge, ESA, highly scenic area) apply anywhere. These disqualifiers (b)-(g) implement the state-level exception classes in 14 CCR §13250(b) — see `../../coastal-development/references/cdp-when-required.md` for the full list. Key triggers: sensitive locations (beach, wetland, bluff edge, ESHA), 10% floor area/height thresholds between sea and first public road, water wells/septic, and landform alteration near sensitive areas.

### Record of Exemptions (S 25.07.008(B))

The Community Development Director maintains a record of all exempt development (name, location, description). Ord. 1732 removed the requirement that the record be available on the City's website. For exemptions in Coastal Commission appealable areas, the Director must notify the Coastal Commission Executive Director within **7 calendar days**.

### Appeal of Exemption Determination (S 25.07.008(C))

For appealable development that is exempted:
- Development **cannot commence** until the appeal period expires
- A qualified appellant may appeal to the Coastal Commission within **10 working days** of the Commission's receipt of the record of exemption
- Grounds for appeal: allegation that the development is not actually exempt

## De Minimis Waiver (S 25.07.009)

The Director of Community Development may issue a written waiver from CDP requirements for de minimis development.

### Eligibility Requirements (S 25.07.009(B))

ALL of the following must be satisfied:
1. Development is in the coastal zone, is **not appealable**, not in an appeal area, not in Coastal Commission permit jurisdiction, and no local public hearing is required
2. Consistent with certified LCP with **no potential for any adverse effect** (individually or cumulatively) on coastal resources — must be in writing with factual evidence
3. Site does **not** involve a violation

### Eligible Project Types (S 25.07.009(C))

- Projects requiring no conditions of approval for LCP consistency, where all applicable LCP policies are objective
- Projects in areas where similar projects have been approved routinely without conditions or opposition

### Ineligible Projects (S 25.07.009(D))

De minimis waiver **cannot** be used for:
1. Projects with questions of LCP conformity or potential coastal resource/public access impacts
2. Projects with known opposition, probable controversy, or outstanding LCP violations
3. Divisions of land (including condominiums)
4. Projects where the Coastal Commission Executive Director is making a violation/appealability determination

**If the Executive Director determines a waiver was issued despite these restrictions, the waiver is invalidated.**

### Notice Requirements (S 25.07.009(E))

- **Mailed notice** (first class) at least **10 calendar days** before decision to:
  - All property owners within **300 ft**
  - All residents within **100 ft**
  - Persons who requested notification for that project/site/coastal decisions
  - Coastal Commission
- **Posted on property** in a conspicuous place, easily readable, as close to the development site as possible, for at least **10 calendar days** before the director's decision
- **Posted on City website**

### Notice Content (S 25.07.009(E)(3))

Must include: project description/location, coastal zone statement, filing date, applicant name, application number, effective date, comment submission procedure, and statement about public comment period.

### City Council Report (S 25.07.009(F))

Director must report proposed waivers to the City Council at its next available public hearing. Waiver does not take effect until after the report. **If a majority of the City Council objects, the waiver is voided** and the application proceeds through the full CDP process.

### Notice of Final Action (S 25.07.009(G))

Within **7 calendar days** of issuing the waiver, notify the Coastal Commission and anyone who requested notification in writing.

## Minor Development / Administrative CDP (S 25.07.013)

The Director may waive the public hearing requirement for minor developments.

### Minor Development Criteria (S 25.07.013(A))

ALL of the following must be satisfied:
1. Consistent with the certified LCP
2. Requires **no discretionary approvals** other than a CDP
3. Has **no adverse effect** (individually or cumulatively) on coastal resources or public access

### Procedure (S 25.07.013(B))

1. **Notice** mailed/delivered per S 25.07.014 requirements (see Notice section below), plus additional statements:
   - Public hearing will be held upon written request received within **15 working days** from date of notice
   - Whether the project is appealable to the Coastal Commission
   - Final date to submit a hearing request
   - Date the CDP will be granted if no hearing is requested
   - Project description and why it qualifies for waived hearing
   - Statement that failure to request a hearing may result in loss of ability to appeal to Coastal Commission
2. **Published notice** at least **10 days** before end of public comment period (City website or newspaper of general circulation)
3. If **no request for hearing** is received within 15 working days, the permit is **deemed approved**
4. If a hearing is requested, the matter is scheduled per standard CDP procedures

### Notice of Final Action (S 25.07.013(B)(3))

For appealable projects: notice mailed first class to applicant, owner/agent, persons who requested notification, and Coastal Commission on or before the **7th calendar day** following end of local appeal period.

## Standard CDP / Full Public Hearing (S 25.07.012)

Required when the project does not qualify for exemption, de minimis waiver, or minor development process.

### Category Determination (S 25.07.012(A))

At application submittal, the Director determines whether development is:
1. In Coastal Commission original jurisdiction (applicant must go to Commission directly)
2. Appealable to the Coastal Commission and requires a CDP
3. Non-appealable and requires a CDP
4. Exempt or categorically excluded

### Approval Authority (S 25.07.012(D))

- CDP reviewed concurrently with any other required permits
- Approving body for the CDP is the same as for the underlying zoning/other permits
- If only a CDP is required, the Director of Community Development may approve or deny
- Director sets public hearing date with notice per S 25.07.014

### Review Criteria (S 25.07.012(F))

CDP applications are reviewed against 9 criteria in S 25.07.012(F) covering access, marine/ESHA resources, recreation, scenic resources, landforms, visual compatibility, archaeological resources, utilities, and public services. These implement the Coastal Act Chapter 3 resource protection policies — see `../../coastal-development/references/resource-protection-*.md` for the underlying state policies.

### Required Findings for Approval (S 25.07.012(G))

A CDP may be approved or conditionally approved only after findings of: **(1)** LCP conformity, **(2)** conformity with public access/recreation policies of Coastal Act Chapter 3 (for development between sea and first public road), and **(3)** no significant CEQA impacts. These implement PRC §30604 — see `../../coastal-development/references/cdp-process.md` for the state-level findings framework.

### Conditions (S 25.07.012(H))

The approving body may impose conditions necessary to enable the required findings. When conditions involve public access and/or open space or conservation easements, the Coastal Commission Executive Director must be notified.

### Effective Date (S 25.07.012(I))

- **Appealable projects**: CDP is not effective until the Coastal Commission receives notice of final action AND the 10 working day appeal period expires with no valid appeal (or all appeals are resolved)
- **Non-appealable projects**: CDP is not effective until the Coastal Commission receives notice of final action
- **Lapse**: CDP approval lapses and becomes void **2 years** after the effective date if not executed or if construction is not commenced and diligently prosecuted
- **Extensions**: One 2-year extension, then one final 1-year extension (must be requested in writing before expiration)

## Notice Requirements (S 25.07.014)

### Exempt Development (S 25.07.014(A))

No notice or hearing required. City maintains a record (applicant name, location, description) available to the Coastal Commission or any interested person.

### Appealable Development (S 25.07.014(B))

Within **10 calendar days** of accepting the application (or at least 10 days before the first public hearing), first class mail to:
- Each applicant
- All persons who requested notice
- All property owners within **100 ft**
- All residents within **100 ft**
- Coastal Commission

Notice must include: coastal zone statement, filing date, applicant name, application number, project description/location, hearing date/time/place, hearing procedures, and local + Coastal Commission appeal system (including fees).

### Non-Appealable Development (S 25.07.014(C))

At least **10 calendar days** before the hearing, first class mail to:
- Any person who filed a written request
- All property owners within **300 ft**
- All residents within **100 ft**
- Coastal Commission

If heard by the Planning Commission, notice must also be published in a newspaper of general circulation.

### Notice of Action (S 25.07.014(D))

On or before the **7th calendar day** following the decision, notice mailed first class to applicant, owner/agent, all persons who requested notification, and Coastal Commission. Includes findings, conditions, and appeal procedures. Serves as notice of final action if no appeal is filed.

For appealable development, the local government's decision becomes effective **10 working days** after Coastal Commission receipt of the notice of final action, unless an appeal is filed.

## Appeals (S 25.07.016)

### General Rule

Development under an approved CDP **cannot commence** until all appeal periods expire or, if appealed, until all appeals (including Coastal Commission) are exhausted.

### Local Appeals

For appealable development, local appeals must be exhausted before filing with the Coastal Commission, **except**:
- Appellant was denied initial local appeal by ordinance restricting who may appeal
- Notice/hearing procedures did not comply with this chapter
- City charges an appeal fee
- Two Coastal Commission members appeal the project

### Coastal Commission Appeals (S 25.07.016(B))

Commission appeals follow the state-level procedures — see `../../coastal-development/references/cdp-appeals.md`. Key local note: **10 working days** from Commission receipt of notice of final action to file. Grounds: non-conformity with certified LCP or Coastal Act Chapter 3 access policies. **Not available for an ADU-only CDP** (Gov. Code 66329(c)).

---

## Appealable Development Definition (S 25.07.006)

Laguna Beach's appealable development definition (S 25.07.006) mirrors the state-level geographic triggers in PRC §30603(a) — see `../../coastal-development/references/appealable-areas.md` for the full definition. Key Laguna Beach note: because most of the city is in the Coastal Zone and near the ocean, many ADU sites are in appealable areas. The appealable area still matters for notice and for de minimis eligibility (25.07.009(B)). It does not open a Commission appeal of an ADU-only CDP (Gov. Code 66329(c)).

---

## Emergency CDP (S 25.07.020)

Not typically applicable to ADUs, but for reference: the Director may grant temporary emergency authorization for remedial work. Emergency CDPs are valid for **60 days** and the applicant must subsequently file a regular CDP application. The Director **cannot** grant emergency CDPs for development in Coastal Commission jurisdiction or for appealable development.
