# Laguna Beach — Fireworks / Temporary Use: Banned

**Fireworks are prohibited in Laguna Beach — storage, use, sale, and possession of all fireworks (including "Safe & Sane") are banned. Public displays by a state-licensed pyrotechnician and theatrical/film effects (CFC §5608) require an LBFD Fire Code Official permit issued directly by the Laguna Beach Fire Department. Contact LBFD Fire Prevention.**
