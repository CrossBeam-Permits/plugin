## Applicability

This standard detail covers simple freestanding concrete masonry unit (CMU) block walls. NOT suitable for:
- Sites with slope steeper than 10%
- Adverse soil conditions (expansive soil, liquefaction, flood hazard)
- Any deviation from the standard detail → requires registered design professional

**No modifications** to any portion of these details are permitted when using the standard detail.

## Permit Triggers

| Condition | Permit Required? |
|-----------|-----------------|
| Block wall **>30" height** from adjacent grade | **YES** |
| Block wall **any height** within **3 feet** of property line | **YES** |
| Wood or metal fence (not CMU) | OTC review with survey waiver if within 3' of property line |

---

## General Notes (Critical for Plan Review)

1. Finish grade must be equal elevation on each side (max 6" offset, no surcharge loads)
2. Block wall >30" from adjacent grade → building permit required
3. Block wall any height within 3' of property lines → building permit required
4. Running bond pattern — head joints offset min 1/4 unit length
5. Clean-outs for all lifts >4 ft
6. Expansion joints at max 30 ft spacing
7. **No wall/fence within 36" of fire hydrant; no wall/fence/foundation within 12" of water meter**
8. **Zoning/Planning Division must approve plans BEFORE permit issued** (height, location, community design standards)
9. **Property corners must be monumented by licensed surveyor BEFORE construction**
10. **Licensed surveyor must certify location of ALL new fences within 3' of property line BEFORE footing inspection/concrete pour**
11. No projections in public right of way (including stone cap or trim)
12. Freestanding only — no retaining, no part of building or other structure
13. No railing/guard/screen where wall + attachment exceeds 6'-0" above lowest adjacent grade. This limits use of the standard detail; it is not a zoning height rule. Zoning permits code-required guards and railings in any yard (LBMC 25.50.012(B)(2)); a taller assembly needs an engineered design.

## Material Specifications

| Material | Specification |
|----------|--------------|
| **Concrete blocks** | f'm = 1,500 psi min, normal weight, Grade N, ASTM C90. **Solid grouted** — both footing alternates are labelled `6" MIN. CMU (SOLID GROUTED)` on the June 2026 sheet (the April 2020 sheet said only `6" MIN. CMU`) |
| **Grout** | 2,000 psi min or 1 part cement, 3 parts sand, 2 parts pea gravel (3/8" max) |
| **Mortar** | 1,800 psi min, Type S or 1 part cement, 3 parts sand, 1/2 part lime |
| **Concrete (footing)** | 2,500 psi min or 1 part cement, 2.5 parts sand, 3.5 parts gravel (3/4" max), 7 gal water/90 lb sack |
| **Reinforcing steel** | Deformed bar, free from loose rust, ASTM A615 Grade 40 or 60 |

---

## Required Inspections

1. **Footing inspection**: Footing trenches with steel in place, BEFORE placing concrete
2. **Reinforcing steel inspection**: Steel in wall, BEFORE grouting. Mid-height steel and grouting inspection required when 6" slump stone block is used.
3. **Final inspection**: Wall completed and site clean

---

## Survey Waiver Process

For walls/fences **within 3 feet of property line**, Laguna Beach requires either:
- A licensed surveyor certifies the fence location before footing inspection, OR
- A **Request to Waive Survey** form (standard-plans/waive-survey-walls-fences.pdf)

The survey waiver is available for wood or metal fences (OTC eligible). CMU block walls within 3' of property line always require surveyor certification.
