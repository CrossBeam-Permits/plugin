# Health Equipment — Sinks, Equipment Installation, Spacing, Certification

This reference covers equipment-related construction requirements from the CRFC that affect facility layout and plumbing plans.

---

## 1. Warewashing Sinks (HSC 114099-114103)

### Three-Compartment Sink (Required)

| Specification | Value | Code Section |
|---------------|-------|-------------|
| Number of compartments | 3 (minimum) | HSC 114099(a) |
| Compartment size | Large enough to immerse largest equipment/utensils | HSC 114099(b) |
| Minimum dimensions (industry standard) | 18"W x 18"L x 12"D per compartment | HSC 114099(b) |
| Drainboards | 2 integral metal drainboards required | HSC 114099(a), 114103(a) |
| Drainboard size | Each at least equal to area of largest compartment | HSC 114103(d) |
| Drainboard position | Soiled at entry side, clean at exit side | HSC 114103(a) |
| Drainboard drainage | Sloped and drained to approved liquid waste receptor | HSC 114103(e) |
| Hot water temperature | Minimum 120 deg F | HSC 114192(a) |
| Wash water temperature | Minimum 100 deg F | HSC 114099.2(b) |

### Hot Water Sanitizing (HSC 114099.4-114099.6)

| Method | Temperature | Duration | Code Section |
|--------|-------------|----------|-------------|
| Manual immersion | 171 deg F minimum | 30 seconds minimum | HSC 114099.6(a) |
| Mechanical (dishwasher) | 160 deg F surface temp (per irreversible indicator) | Per machine cycle | HSC 114099.7(a) |
| Hot water sanitize compartment | 171 deg F with integral heating device and rack/basket | Continuous | HSC 114099.4 |

### Mechanical Warewasher (Dishwasher)

| Requirement | Code Section |
|-------------|-------------|
| 2 metal drainboards adjacent (may share with adjacent manual sink) | HSC 114103(b) |
| All drainboards large enough for soiled and cleaned items during operating hours | HSC 114103(d) |

### Sink Use Restrictions

| Rule | Code Section |
|------|-------------|
| Warewashing sinks NOT for handwashing (exception: pre-1996 with no dedicated handwash in prep area) | HSC 114125(a) |
| Food prep, handwash, and warewash sinks NOT for mop water, maintenance tools, or maintenance materials | HSC 114123 |

### Grandfathered 2-Compartment Sink

Facilities built before January 1, 1996 with a 2-compartment sink: may continue using it until major remodel (HSC 114099(a) exception).

## 2. Food Prep Sink (HSC 114163)

| Specification | Value | Code Section |
|---------------|-------|-------------|
| Minimum dimensions | 18"W x 18"L x 12"D | HSC 114163(a)(1) |
| Maximum compartments | 2 | HSC 114163(a)(1) |
| Drainboard | Integral drainboard or adjacent table, minimum 18"W x 18"L | HSC 114163(a)(1) |
| Location | In food prep area, exclusively for food prep | HSC 114163(a)(2) |
| Accessibility | Accessible at all times during operation | HSC 114163(a)(2) |
| Water supply | Hot and cold running water through mixing valve | HSC 114163(a)(3) |
| Drainage | Indirect drain to floor sink | Per plumbing code |

### Splashguard Near Handwash Sink

| Condition | Requirement | Code Section |
|-----------|-------------|-------------|
| Handwash sink within 24" of food prep or warewash sink | Metal splashguard minimum 6" high, extending back to front of drainboard, with rounded corners | HSC 113953(b)(2) |
| Distance >=24" between sinks | No splashguard required | HSC 113953(b)(2) |

## 3. Equipment Design and Certification (HSC 114130)

### Certification Requirements

| Requirement | Code Section |
|-------------|-------------|
| All new/replacement food-related equipment: ANSI-accredited certification for sanitation (NSF, UL, Intertek ETL, CSA) | HSC 114130(b) |
| All new/replacement electrical appliances: meet UL standards via ANSI certification | HSC 114130(d) |
| Food-contact surfaces: safe, durable, corrosion-resistant, nonabsorbent, smooth, easily cleanable | HSC 114130.1 |
| Multiuse food-contact surfaces: smooth, free of breaks/seams/cracks, smooth welds, accessible for cleaning | HSC 114130.3 |
| Wood prohibited as food-contact surface (exceptions: hard maple cutting boards, rolling pins, salad bowls, chopsticks, cedar grilling planks, confectionery paddles at 230+ deg F) | HSC 114132(a) |

## 4. Equipment Location and Installation (HSC 114161-114172)

### Prohibited Locations (HSC 114161)

Equipment and food cabinets NOT permitted in: locker rooms, toilet rooms, refuse rooms, mechanical rooms, under unshielded sewer lines, under leaking water lines, under open stairwells.

### Equipment Spacing and Sealing

| Configuration | Requirement | Code Section |
|---------------|-------------|-------------|
| Fixed equipment — sides, behind, above | Spaced to allow access for cleaning | HSC 114169(a)(1) |
| Fixed equipment to adjoining equipment/walls/ceilings | Gap no more than 1/32" (1 mm) | HSC 114169(a)(2) |
| Fixed equipment exposed to spillage/seepage | Sealed to adjoining equipment or walls | HSC 114169(a)(3) |
| **Floor-mounted fixed equipment** | **Sealed to floor** OR elevated on legs with **minimum 6" clearance** | HSC 114169(b) |
| **Table-mounted fixed equipment** | **Sealed to table** OR elevated on legs with **minimum 4" clearance** | HSC 114169(d) |

### Other Installation Requirements

| Requirement | Code Section |
|-------------|-------------|
| Liquid waste drain lines: NOT through ice machine or ice storage bin | HSC 114171 |
| Pressurized cylinders: securely fastened to rigid structure | HSC 114172 |
| Clean equipment/utensils/linens/single-use articles: stored minimum 6" above floor | HSC 114178(a) |
| Electrical power at all times for exhaust, lighting, water heaters, refrigeration, and all approved appliances | HSC 114182 |

## 5. Indirect Waste Connections (HSC 114193)

Equipment producing liquid waste must connect via indirect waste pipe discharging through air gap into floor sink:

| Equipment | Connection Type | Code Section |
|-----------|----------------|-------------|
| Steam tables | Indirect to floor sink via air gap | HSC 114193(a) |
| Ice machines | Indirect to floor sink via air gap | HSC 114193(a) |
| Food prep sinks | Indirect to floor sink via air gap | HSC 114193(a) |
| Warewashing sinks | Indirect to floor sink via air gap (or direct downstream from floor drain) | HSC 114193(a), (d) |
| Display cases | Indirect to floor sink via air gap | HSC 114193(a) |
| Walk-in refrigeration | Indirect to floor sink via air gap | HSC 114193(a) |
| Reach-in refrigeration | Indirect to floor sink OR functioning evaporator | HSC 114193(b) |

### Air Gap Specifications

| Specification | Value | Code Section |
|---------------|-------|-------------|
| Minimum air gap | Twice the diameter of the water supply inlet | HSC 114193.1 |
| Absolute minimum | Never less than 1 inch | HSC 114193.1 |
