# Health Construction — Floors, Walls, Ceilings, Lighting, Ventilation, Enclosure

This reference covers the physical facility construction requirements from the California Retail Food Code (CRFC) that a health department plan reviewer checks on every restaurant TI submission.

---

## 1. Facility Enclosure (HSC 114266)

| Requirement | Code Section |
|-------------|-------------|
| Permanent food facility must be fully enclosed with permanent floors, walls, and overhead structure | HSC 114266(a) |
| Open windows, folding doors, nonfixed storefronts allowed ONLY with approved IPM and food safety risk mitigation plan (see `outdoor-dining.md`) | HSC 114266(d) — added by AB 592 |
| Must be fully enclosed during non-operation hours | HSC 114266(d)(1)(A) |
| Must have ability to operate while fully enclosed | HSC 114266(d)(1)(B) |
| No gaps exceeding 1/4 inch in enclosure | CRFC general requirement |
| Private homes/living quarters NOT used for food facility operations | HSC 114285(a) |
| Living/sleeping quarters on premises: separated by complete partitioning, no doors/openings | HSC 114286(b) |

### Small Facility Exemptions

| Size | Exemption | Code Section |
|------|-----------|-------------|
| Display area <=25 sq ft | Minimal compliance set | HSC 114289(c)(1) |
| Display area >25 sq ft but <300 sq ft, prepackaged non-PHF only | Exempt from most requirements but must meet HSC 114266 (enclosure) | HSC 114289(a), (c)(2) |

## 2. Floor Requirements (HSC 114268-114269)

### Where Approved Flooring Is Required

Food prep, food storage, utensil washing, refuse storage, janitorial, toilet rooms, handwashing areas (HSC 114268(a)).

### Floor Surface Standard

**Smooth, durable, nonabsorbent, easily cleanable** (HSC 114268(a)).

### Floor Coving

| Dimension | Value | Code Section |
|-----------|-------|-------------|
| Coving radius at floor-wall juncture | 3/8" minimum | HSC 114268(b) |
| Coving height up wall | 4" minimum | HSC 114268(b) |
| Exception | Areas storing only unopened bottles/cans/cartons/sacks | HSC 114268(b) |

### Floor Drains and Slopes

| Requirement | Value | Code Section |
|-------------|-------|-------------|
| Floor drains required in water-flushed and pressure-spray cleaning areas (new construction/extensive remodel) | — | HSC 114269(a) |
| Floor slope to drains | 1:50 | HSC 114269(a) |
| Floor sinks required for equipment liquid waste discharge (new construction/extensive remodel) | — | HSC 114269(b) |

### Floor Materials and Duckboards

Mats and duckboards must be removable and easily cleanable (HSC 114272).

---

## 3. Wall Requirements (HSC 114271)

### Wall Surface Standard

**Durable, smooth, nonabsorbent, easily cleanable** (HSC 114271(a)).

### Where Wall Requirements Apply

Same areas as floors: food prep, food storage, utensil washing, refuse storage, janitorial, toilet rooms, handwashing areas.

### Wall Exemptions (HSC 114271(b))

| Area | Exempt? |
|------|---------|
| Bar areas (except near sinks/food prep) | Yes |
| Areas storing only unopened containers | Yes |
| Dining/sales areas | Yes |
| Offices | Yes |
| Consumer-only restrooms | Partial — must still be nonabsorbent and washable |
| Dressing/locker areas | Yes |

### Conduits and Attachments

| Requirement | Code Section |
|-------------|-------------|
| Conduits: install within walls where practicable; if exposed, mount to facilitate cleaning | HSC 114271(d) |
| Acoustical paneling: allowed only if installed not less than 6 ft above floor | HSC 114271(c) |

---

## 4. Ceiling Requirements (HSC 114271)

Same standard as walls: **durable, smooth, nonabsorbent, easily cleanable** (HSC 114271(a)).

Same exemption areas as walls (HSC 114271(b)).

## 5. Lighting Requirements (HSC 114252)

### Minimum Lighting Levels

| Area | Minimum | Measurement Point | Code Section |
|------|---------|-------------------|-------------|
| Walk-in refrigeration | 10 foot-candles | 30" above floor | HSC 114252(a) |
| Dry food storage | 10 foot-candles | 30" above floor | HSC 114252(a) |
| Alcoholic beverage prep surfaces | 10 foot-candles | — | HSC 114252(a) |
| Inside reach-in/under-counter refrigerators | 10 foot-candles | — | HSC 114252(a) |
| Consumer self-service areas | 20 foot-candles | — | HSC 114252(b)(1) |
| Fresh produce/prepackaged food sales | 20 foot-candles | — | HSC 114252(b)(1) |
| Server stations with food prep | 20 foot-candles | — | HSC 114252(b)(2) |
| Handwashing areas | 20 foot-candles | 30" above floor | HSC 114252(b)(3) |
| Warewashing areas | 20 foot-candles | 30" above floor | HSC 114252(b)(3) |
| Equipment/utensil storage | 20 foot-candles | 30" above floor | HSC 114252(b)(3) |
| Toilet rooms | 20 foot-candles | 30" above floor | HSC 114252(b)(3) |
| All areas during cleaning | 20 foot-candles | — | HSC 114252(b)(4) |
| **Food employee work surfaces** (knives, slicers, grinders, saws) | **50 foot-candles** | — | HSC 114252(c) |

### Light Fixture Requirements

| Requirement | Code Section |
|-------------|-------------|
| Light bulbs: shielded, coated, or shatter-resistant in areas with nonprepackaged ready-to-eat food, clean equipment, or unwrapped single-use articles | HSC 114252.1(a) |
| Heat lamps: shield surrounding and extending beyond bulb (only face exposed), or approved shatter-resistant coating | HSC 114252.1(c) |

## 6. Ventilation — General (HSC 114149)

| Requirement | Code Section |
|-------------|-------------|
| All areas: sufficient ventilation for proper food storage and employee comfort | HSC 114149(a) |
| Toilet rooms: vented to outside via openable screened window, air shaft, or light-switch-activated exhaust fan | HSC 114149(b) |
| Mechanical exhaust ventilation over ALL cooking equipment — must effectively remove odors, smoke, steam, grease, heat, vapors. Installed per California Mechanical Code | HSC 114149.1(a) |
| Exemption from hood: enforcement agency determines equipment produces no toxic gases, smoke, grease, vapors, or heat per manufacturer specs | HSC 114149.1(c) |
| Makeup air: at the rate of air exhausted (1:1 ratio) | HSC 114149.1(d) |
| HVAC designed so intake/exhaust do not contaminate food or create air currents affecting PHF temperatures | HSC 114149.3 |

### Hood Construction Requirements (HSC 114149.2)

| Requirement | Code Section |
|-------------|-------------|
| Installed to allow thorough cleaning of all surfaces (hood, filters, piping, lights, troughs, hangers, flanges, ducts) | HSC 114149.2(a) |
| Designed to prevent grease/condensation from draining onto food or equipment | HSC 114149.2(b) |
| Filters/grease extractors: readily removable for cleaning | HSC 114149.2(c) |
| Joints/seams: substantially tight; solder only for sealing | HSC 114149.2(d) |
| Ducts: all seams completely tight, sufficient clean-outs, properly sloped, cleaned regularly | HSC 114149.2(f) |

## 7. Vermin and Animal Exclusion (HSC 114259)

| Requirement | Code Section |
|-------------|-------------|
| Facility constructed/equipped/maintained to prevent entrance and harborage of animals, birds, rodents, insects | HSC 114259 |

### Passthrough Window Service Openings (HSC 114259.2)

| Parameter | Value | Code Section |
|-----------|-------|-------------|
| Maximum opening size | 216 sq in each | HSC 114259.2 |
| Maximum opening with air curtain | 432 sq in | HSC 114259.2 |
| Minimum spacing between openings | 18 inches apart | HSC 114259.2 |
| Window type | Self-closing solid or screened | HSC 114259.2 |
| Minimum screen mesh | 16 mesh per square inch | HSC 114259.2 |
| Counter surface | Smooth, easily cleanable | HSC 114259.2 |

## 8. Refuse and Outdoor Areas (HSC 114245)

| Requirement | Code Section |
|-------------|-------------|
| Indoor refuse storage area: must meet floor, wall, ceiling, and vermin exclusion requirements | HSC 114245.3 |
| Outdoor refuse area: nonabsorbent material (concrete/asphalt), easily cleanable, durable, sloped to drain | HSC 114245.4 |
