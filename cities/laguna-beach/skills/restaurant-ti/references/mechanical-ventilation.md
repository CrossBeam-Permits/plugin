# Mechanical Ventilation — Type I/II Hoods, Exhaust, Makeup Air, Ducts

This reference covers California Mechanical Code requirements for commercial kitchen ventilation in restaurant TI projects.

---

## 1. When Hoods Are Required

### Type I Hood (CMC 508) — Grease-Producing Equipment

Required for: deep-fat fryers, broilers, grills, hot-top ranges, ovens, barbecues, rotisseries, and any equipment producing grease-laden vapors.

**Exception**: Not required for appliances listed per UL 197 with grease discharge <=2.9E-09 oz/in3 at 500 CFM.

### Type II Hood (CMC 519) — Heat/Steam-Producing Equipment

Required above: equipment and dishwashers generating steam, heat, or combustion products where grease/smoke is NOT present (e.g., high-temp dishwashers, steam kettles).

## 6. Makeup Air (CMC 511.3)

| Requirement | Value | Code Section |
|-------------|-------|-------------|
| Maximum negative pressure in commercial kitchen | 0.02 inch water column | CMC 511.3 |
| Internal makeup air | Shall not exceed 10% of exhaust airflow | CMC 511.3 |
| Air balance | Design plans must include outdoor air balance schedule/diagram | CMC 511.3.1 |

**Key principle**: Makeup air must be provided so the kitchen does not create excessive negative pressure. The makeup air rate must match the exhaust rate (HSC 114149.1(d) and CMC 511.3).

## 7. Fire Suppression for Hoods (CMC 513)

### System Requirements

| Requirement | Code Section |
|-------------|-------------|
| Fire-extinguishing equipment required for grease removal devices, hood exhaust plenums, and exhaust duct systems | CMC 513 |
| Must comply with UL 300 (wet chemical systems) | CMC 513 |
| ALL existing dry and wet chemical systems must comply with UL 300 | CMC 513 |
| Must have both automatic AND manual actuation | CMC 513 |
| Upon actuation: all fuel and electric power to cooking equipment shall automatically shut off | CMC 513 |
| Carbon dioxide extinguishers prohibited | CMC 513 |
| Class K portable fire extinguishers required for combustible cooking media | CMC 513 |

### Equipment Shutoff Detail

When the fire suppression system activates:
- All gas supply to cooking equipment under the hood: **auto shutoff**
- All electrical power to cooking equipment under the hood: **auto shutoff**
- Exhaust fan: must **continue to operate** after activation

## 8. Fan Interlock (CMC 511.2.3, NFPA 96 8.2)

| Requirement | Code Section |
|-------------|-------------|
| Exhaust fan activates when any appliance under hood turns on | CMC 511.2.3 |
| Exhaust fan must start on suppression activation if ducting protected or flow needed for agent distribution | NFPA 96 8.2 |
