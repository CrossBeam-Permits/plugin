# Building Occupancy, Egress, and Accessibility

This reference covers CBC requirements for restaurant A-2 occupancy classification, means of egress, assembly provisions, fire protection triggers, and ADA accessibility.

---

## 1. Occupancy Classification (CBC 303)

### A-2 Definition (CBC 303.3)

Group A-2 includes: banquet halls, casinos (gaming areas), nightclubs, restaurants/cafeterias/similar dining facilities (including associated commercial kitchens), taverns and bars.

### When A-2 Does NOT Apply

| Condition | Classification | Code Section |
|-----------|---------------|-------------|
| Occupant load <50 persons | Group B | CBC 303.1.1 |
| Room <750 sq ft, accessory to another occupancy | Group B | CBC 303.1.2 |

---

## 2. Occupant Load Calculation (CBC Table 1004.5)

### Load Factors for Restaurant Spaces

| Space Type | Factor | Calculation Method |
|------------|--------|-------------------|
| Assembly with tables and chairs (dining) | 15 net sq ft per occupant | Net (usable floor area) |
| Assembly concentrated (chairs only, no tables) | 7 net sq ft per occupant | Net |
| Assembly standing space (bar area) | 5 net sq ft per occupant | Net |
| Commercial kitchen | 200 gross sq ft per occupant | Gross (wall to wall) |
| Fixed seating booths | 1 person per 24 inches of seat length | Count |

### Occupant Load Rules

| Rule | Code Section |
|------|-------------|
| Occupant load must be posted in any assembly/dining/drinking room with 50+ occupants | CBC 1004.9 |
| Occupant load can be increased if all code requirements met, but cannot exceed 1 person per 7 sq ft | CBC 1004.5.1 |
| Calculate at 50% male / 50% female for fixture counts; round fractions up | CPC 422.1.1 |

## 3. Number of Exits (CBC Table 1006.2.1)

### Group A Minimum Exits

| Occupant Load | Minimum Exits Required |
|---------------|----------------------|
| 1-49 | 1 exit (common path max 75 ft) |
| 50-500 | 2 exits |
| 501-1,000 | 3 exits |
| >1,000 | 4 exits |

---

## 4. Exit Access Travel Distance (CBC Table 1017.2)

| Sprinkler Status | Maximum Travel Distance (Group A) |
|------------------|----------------------------------|
| Without sprinkler | 200 feet |
| With sprinkler | 250 feet |

---

## 5. Egress Sizing (CBC 1005)

| Component | Factor |
|-----------|--------|
| Stairways | 0.3 inch per occupant (0.2 if sprinklered with voice/alarm) |
| Other egress components (corridors, doors) | 0.2 inch per occupant (0.15 if sprinklered with voice/alarm) |

Minimum door width: 32 inches clear (36 inches nominal).

---

## 6. Assembly-Specific Egress (CBC 1030)

### Main Exit (CBC 1030.2)

Assembly with >300 occupants must have a main exit that:
- Accommodates not less than **one-half** the total occupant load
- Fronts on a street or 20-ft wide unoccupied open space

### Assembly 100-300 Occupants (CBC 1030.3.1)

At least one exit must be:
- Directly to an exit, OR
- Through a non-main lobby, OR
- Via a 1-hour rated corridor

### Aisle Accessways at Tables (CBC 1030.13.1.1)

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Minimum width | 12 inches plus 1/2 inch for each foot beyond 12 ft from aisle |
| Maximum travel along aisle accessway | 30 feet to choice of two egress paths |

### Seat Fastening (CBC 1030.15)

Seats at tables without ramped/tiered floors are NOT required to be fastened to floor.

---

## 7. Sprinkler Requirements for A-2 (CBC 903.2.1.2)

Automatic sprinkler system required for Group A-2 when ANY of:

| Trigger | Threshold |
|---------|-----------|
| Fire area size | Exceeds 5,000 sq ft |
| Occupant load | 100 or more |
| Floor location | Located on floor other than level of exit discharge |
| Multiple fire areas | Structure >5,000 sq ft with multiple A-2 fire areas separated by <4-hour fire walls |

---

## 8. Fire Alarm Requirements for Group A (CBC 907.2.1)

| Trigger | Requirement |
|---------|-------------|
| Assembly occupant load >=300, or >100 above/below exit discharge | Manual fire alarm system |
| Exception | Manual boxes not required if sprinklered throughout with notification on waterflow |
| Assembly occupant load >=1,000 | Emergency voice/alarm communication system |

---

## 9. ADA / Accessibility (CBC Chapter 11B)

### Dining Surfaces (CBC 11B-226)

| Requirement | Value | Code Section |
|-------------|-------|-------------|
| Accessible dining surfaces | Minimum 5% of seating/standing spaces | CBC 11B-226.1 |
| Distribution | Dispersed throughout the space | CBC 11B-226.2 |
| Counters >34" high | Must have a portion at least 60 inches long complying with 11B-902.3 | CBC 11B-226.3 |

### General Accessibility

| Requirement | Code Section |
|-------------|-------------|
| Accessible route to all dining areas | CBC 11B-206 |
| Accessible restrooms | CBC 11B-213 |
| Accessible entrance | CBC 11B-206.4 |
