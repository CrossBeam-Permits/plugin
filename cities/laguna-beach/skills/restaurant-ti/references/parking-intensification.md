# Laguna Beach — Parking & Intensification of Use for Restaurant TI

**Operative code:** LBMC Chapter 25.52 (Off-Street Parking); Chapter 25.40 (Downtown Specific Plan); Sec. 25.07.006 (intensification-of-use definition).

---

## Two Parking Regimes — Pick the Right One

| Location | Restaurant ratio | Source |
|----------|------------------|--------|
| **Citywide (default)** | **1 space per 100 sf gross floor area** (including outdoor seating) OR **1 per 3 seats**, whichever is greater | Ch. 25.52 |
| **Downtown Specific Plan area** | **3 spaces per 1,000 sf** for non-residential uses (substantially more lenient) | Ch. 25.40, 2022 CCC-certified update |

**The DSP ratio is an incentive.** The 2022 DSP update intentionally lowered downtown parking demand to encourage downtown business, including food service. Confirm the parcel is inside the DSP boundary on the city zoning map before applying 3 per 1,000 sf.

**Outdoor seating counts.** Under Ch. 25.52, outdoor seating area is part of the gross floor area used for parking calculations — whether a sidewalk café, private patio, or parking-lot conversion. The "outdoor dining doesn't count for parking" interpretation is wrong in Laguna.

---

## Intensification-of-Use Test

This is the most consequential parking analysis for restaurant TIs. Per Sec. 25.07.006:

> A change in use is "intensification of use" when the new use requires more off-street parking than the prior legal use.

If intensification fires, two independent entitlements are triggered:

1. **Coastal Development Permit** — under Ch. 25.07, intensification is "development" requiring CDP regardless of building scope.
2. **Conditional Use Permit** — per Sec. 25.05.030, intensification is a CUP trigger even in zones where the new use is otherwise by-right.

### How to Run the Test

| Step | Detail |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Identify prior legal use of the tenant space (lease history, certificate of occupancy, business license records) |
| 2 | Apply the relevant Ch. 25.52 ratio to the prior use to get prior-use demand |
| 3 | Apply the relevant ratio to the proposed restaurant to get proposed demand |
| 4 | If proposed > prior → intensification of use → CDP + CUP triggers |
| 5 | If proposed = prior or <= prior → no intensification (rare for retail→restaurant conversion) |

### Common Prior-Use Ratios

| Prior use | Typical Ch. 25.52 ratio |
|-----------|-------------------------|
| Retail | 1 space per 250 sf |
| Office | 1 space per 300 sf |
| Personal service (salon, etc.) | 1 space per 200 sf |
| Bank | 1 space per 300 sf |
| Warehouse / storage | 1 space per 1,000 sf |
| Vacant (no prior legal use) | Use the most recent legal use; if none, planning director determination |

### Worked Example

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Tenant space | 2,000 sf indoor + 500 sf proposed patio |
| Prior use | Retail clothing store, 2,000 sf |
| Prior demand (citywide) | 2,000 / 250 = **8 spaces** |
| Proposed restaurant GFA (incl. outdoor) | 2,500 sf |
| Proposed demand (citywide, 1 per 100 sf) | **25 spaces** |
| Intensification? | **YES — 25 > 8** |
| CDP trigger | YES |
| CUP trigger | YES |
| Parking shortfall (assuming 8 stalls available on-site) | 17 spaces |
| Remedy | In-lieu fee, parking study, shared parking agreement, or PC variance |

## In-Lieu Fee Program

Citywide, the in-lieu fee program allows a restaurant to satisfy its parking shortfall by paying a per-stall fee into the city parking fund. Check the current per-stall fee on the city fee schedule before quoting numbers. The fee is a one-time payment at permit issuance.

In the DSP area, the in-lieu program is more aggressively used because downtown parking is harder to provide on-site. The DSP's lower ratio also reduces the underlying demand.

## Parking Demand Study

Where the standard ratio overstates real demand (e.g., shared-use scenario with adjacent office building that has off-peak hours opposite to restaurant peak), a parking demand study may support a reduction. Requires planning director acceptance; typically follows ULI or ITE methodology. PC may grant a parking variance under Sec. 25.05.025.

## Other Parking Items

| Item | Standard |

| EV-ready spaces | Per CALGreen Sec. 5.106.5.3 — applies to non-residential alterations meeting threshold |

| Loading | Per Ch. 25.52 — loading-space requirements for non-residential by GFA |
| Tandem | Generally not counted; check specific subsection |
| Compact | Limited to specified percentage |

## Output Findings

- **PASS** — Correct ratio applied (DSP vs citywide); prior-vs-proposed analysis complete; intensification finding documented; CDP/CUP triggered as appropriate; ADA and EV-ready spaces shown.
- **FAIL** — Restaurant ratio mis-applied (e.g., DSP ratio used on a non-DSP parcel); outdoor seating omitted from GFA; intensification ignored.
- **FLAG** — Prior legal use ambiguous; lease/CofO records incomplete; in-lieu fee accepted but funding source not documented.
- **N/A** — Identical use replacement (e.g., restaurant-for-restaurant with same seat count, same operation).
