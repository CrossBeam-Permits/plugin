# Health Food Safety — Layout Requirements for Food Handling and Storage

This reference covers CRFC requirements related to food handling and storage that affect facility layout and plan review. Operational requirements (temps, handling procedures) are included only where they create construction or layout requirements.

---

## 1. Food Preparation Areas (HSC 113984)

| Requirement | Code Section |
|-------------|-------------|
| Adequate and suitable counter space for all food prep operations | HSC 113984(a) |
| Food prep within fully enclosed food facility (limited food prep in approved food compartment) | HSC 113984(b) |
| Overhead protection above all food prep, food display, warewashing, and food storage areas | HSC 113984(e) |

### Consumer Access Through Food Prep Area (HSC 113984.1)

When consumers must pass through or adjacent to food prep areas:

| Requirement | Value | Code Section |
|-------------|-------|-------------|
| Ready-to-eat food prep separated from consumer path | At least 3 feet | HSC 113984.1 |
| Consumer access route barrier | Rail or wall at least 3 feet high, or clearly delineated path | HSC 113984.1 |

---

## 2. Food Storage (HSC 114047-114049)

### General Storage Requirements

| Requirement | Code Section |
|-------------|-------------|
| Food stored in clean, dry area, not exposed to splash/dust/vermin | HSC 114047(b) |
| Food stored at least 6 inches above floor | HSC 114047(b) |

### Prohibited Storage Locations (HSC 114049)

Food NOT stored in:
- Locker rooms
- Toilet rooms
- Dressing rooms
- Refuse rooms
- Mechanical rooms
- Under sewer lines
- Under leaking water lines
- Under open stairwells

---

## 3. Restroom Path Requirements (HSC 114276)

| Requirement | Code Section |
|-------------|-------------|
| Consumer path to toilets must NOT pass through food prep, food storage, or utensil washing areas | HSC 114276(b)(2) |
| Consumer toilets required when onsite food consumption | HSC 114276(b)(1) |
| Consumer toilets required when facility >20,000 sq ft (built after 7/1/1984) | HSC 114276(b)(1) |

---

## 4. Equipment and Utensil Storage (HSC 114178)

| Requirement | Code Section |
|-------------|-------------|
| Clean equipment, utensils, linens, and single-use articles stored at least 6 inches above floor | HSC 114178(a) |

---

## 5. Equipment Location Restrictions (HSC 114161)

Equipment and food cabinets NOT permitted in:
- Locker rooms
- Toilet rooms
- Refuse rooms
- Mechanical rooms
- Under unshielded sewer lines
- Under leaking water lines
- Under open stairwells
