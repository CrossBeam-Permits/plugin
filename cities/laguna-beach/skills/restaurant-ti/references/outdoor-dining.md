# AB 592 — Outdoor Dining and Open-Front Restaurant Operations

AB 592 (Gabriel, Chapter 469, Statutes of 2025), effective January 1, 2026, makes permanent several COVID-era outdoor dining provisions and creates a new pathway for open-front restaurant operations.

---

## 1. Open-Front Restaurant Operations (HSC 114266(d))

### What Is Now Allowed

Restaurants may operate using **open windows, folding doors, or nonfixed storefronts** during hours of operation, notwithstanding the general requirement that permanent food facilities be fully enclosed (HSC 114266(a)).

### Restaurant Definition for This Section

Same meaning as "bona fide public eating place" in BPC 23038 — broader than AB 671's definition, does NOT exclude fast food (HSC 114266(d)(1)).

### Requirements

| Requirement | Detail | Code Section |
|-------------|--------|-------------|
| IPM and food safety plan | Must be developed and submitted to enforcement agency for approval BEFORE operation | HSC 114266(d)(1) |
| Full enclosure during non-operation | Facility must be fully enclosed when not operating | HSC 114266(d)(1)(A) |
| Ability to operate enclosed | Must have ability to operate while fully enclosed (backup capability) | HSC 114266(d)(1)(B) |
| Plan documented in writing | Available on request to enforcement officers | HSC 114266(d)(2) |
| Annual review | Review and update annually or on facility/operation change | HSC 114266(d)(4) |

### IPM Plan Required Components (HSC 114266(d)(3))

| Component | Description | Code Section |
|-----------|-------------|-------------|
| Risk assessment | Comprehensive evaluation of facility layout, operations, storage, surrounding environment for vermin risk | HSC 114266(d)(3)(A) |
| Control procedures | Preventive and responsive measures for vermin (trash/compost control), contamination (dust/debris), cleanliness | HSC 114266(d)(3)(B) |
| Monitoring procedures | Regular inspection/documentation of vermin activity, problem areas, control effectiveness; frequency appropriate to risk | HSC 114266(d)(3)(C) |
| Employee training | All employees upon hire and annually on pest prevention, restaurant procedures, individual responsibilities | HSC 114266(d)(3)(D) |
| Record keeping | Dates/times of self-closure events; dates of all pest control visits, exclusion/treatment descriptions, copies of receipts — kept onsite for 12 months | HSC 114266(d)(3)(E) |

### Self-Closure Mandate (HSC 114266(d)(5))

Restaurant must **self-close upon observation of vermin activity** inside the facility (including droppings or markings). Must remain closed and not operate **until all vermin are eliminated**.

### Enforcement Agency Approval Standard (HSC 114266(d)(6))

| Principle | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Shall not unreasonably withhold approval | Agency must make every reasonable effort to find conditions/measures allowing open-front operation |
| Denial standard | Only if agency demonstrates, based on substantial evidence in the record, that specific unique circumstances exist such that no reasonable conditions can mitigate a significant public health/safety risk |

### Enforcement Authority (HSC 114266(d)(7))

Agency retains authority to suspend or revoke IPM plan approval if:
- Approved plan is not followed
- Vermin observed during inspection
- Complaints of vermin presence are verified

## 2. Satellite Food Service (HSC 114067)

### Definition

Food service operated from a satellite location by a fully enclosed permanent food facility. Restricted to limited food preparation.

### Requirements for Satellite Food Service

| Requirement | Code Section |
|-------------|-------------|
| Must be operated by a fully enclosed permanent food facility | HSC 114067(b) |
| Before conducting: submit written standard operating procedures to enforcement agency | HSC 114067(c) |
| All food prep within food compartment or fully enclosed facility | HSC 114067(d) |
| Overhead protection above all food handling areas | HSC 114067(e) |
| Approved handwashing and warewashing facilities (permanent or self-contained) for nonprepackaged food | HSC 114067(f) |
| During non-operation: food/utensils stored in enclosed satellite, approved compartments, or permanent facility | HSC 114067(h) |
| Same permitholder for both permanent facility and satellite | HSC 114067(j) |

### Written Operating Procedures Must Cover (HSC 114067(c))

1. All food products handled and dispensed
2. Proposed food prep and handling procedures
3. Cleaning schedules for utensils, equipment, structures; refuse disposal
4. Food transport procedures and contamination prevention
5. How potentially hazardous foods are temperature-maintained per HSC 113996

### COVID-Era Waiver — Now Permanent (HSC 114067(k))

Permitted food facilities may prepare and serve food as temporary satellite food service **without a separate permit** and **without submitting written operating procedures** — but must maintain procedures onsite for review. Previously expiring July 1, 2026; now permanent.

## 3. Parking Relief (Gov. Code 65907)

### What Changed

When outdoor dining expansion interferes with, reduces, eliminates, or impacts required parking:
- Local jurisdiction that has NOT adopted its own ordinance must reduce required parking by the number of spaces needed for the outdoor dining area
- **Previously temporary** (expired July 1, 2026) — **now permanent**

### Practical Impact

Restaurants adding outdoor dining in parking areas do not need to provide replacement parking, unless the local jurisdiction has adopted its own ordinance addressing this.

### COVID-19 Temporary Catering Authorizations

| Parameter | Value | Code Section |

| Sunset | Entire section repealed January 1, 2029 | BPC 25750.5(e) |

## 5. Interaction Between AB 592 and AB 671

When a restaurant TI involves open-front design elements (folding doors, NanaWall):
- The TI permit follows **AB 671's fast-track** process (QPC pathway, 20-day timeline)
- The open-front operations require an **IPM plan under AB 592** (separate health department approval)
- Both processes run in parallel — different agencies, different timelines
