# Health Plumbing & Sanitation — Handwashing, Water, Waste, Restrooms, Janitorial

This reference covers plumbing and sanitation construction requirements from the CRFC that affect restroom, handwashing, and plumbing layouts.

---

## 1. Handwashing Stations (HSC 113953)

### Location Requirements

| Requirement | Code Section |
|-------------|-------------|
| Handwashing facilities within or adjacent to toilet rooms | HSC 113953(a) |
| Count per local building/plumbing codes | HSC 113953(a) |
| Facilities built/extensively remodeled after 1/1/1996 handling nonprepackaged food: dedicated handwash sinks in food prep areas AND warewashing areas not adjacent to food prep | HSC 113953(b)(1) |
| Sufficient in number, conveniently located, accessible at all times | HSC 113953(b)(1) |

### Handwash Sink Specifications

| Specification | Value | Code Section |
|---------------|-------|-------------|
| Water temperature | Warm water via mixing valve or combination faucet | HSC 113953(c) |
| If not readily adjustable | Minimum 100 deg F, maximum 108 deg F | HSC 113953(c) |
| Minimum flow duration | 15 seconds under pressure | HSC 113953(c) |

### Handwash Sink Accessories (HSC 113953.2)

At each handwashing sink, provide dispensers for:
- (a) Handwashing cleanser (soap)
- (b) Single-use towels OR heated-air hand drying device

### Splashguard Between Sinks

| Condition | Requirement | Code Section |
|-----------|-------------|-------------|
| Handwash sink within 24" of warewash or food prep sink | Metal splashguard minimum 6" high, extending from back edge to front edge of drainboard, with rounded corners | HSC 113953(b)(2) |
| Distance between sinks >=24" | No splashguard required | HSC 113953(b)(2) |

## 2. Water Supply (HSC 114190-114195)

### General Requirements

| Requirement | Code Section |
|-------------|-------------|
| All plumbing installed per local plumbing ordinances | HSC 114190 |
| Pressurized potable hot and cold water supply required | HSC 114192(a) |
| Water under pressure permanently plumbed to all fixtures and equipment requiring water | HSC 114192.1(a) |
| Water source capacity: sufficient for peak demand | HSC 114195 |
| Hot water generation: sufficient for peak demand throughout facility | HSC 114195 |

### Hot Water Temperatures

| Application | Temperature | Code Section |
|-------------|-------------|-------------|
| General (at faucet) | 120 deg F minimum | HSC 114192(a) |
| Handwashing (if not adjustable) | 100-108 deg F | HSC 113953(c) |
| Warewash (wash compartment) | 100 deg F minimum | HSC 114099.2(b) |
| Sanitize (hot water method) | 171 deg F minimum for 30 seconds | HSC 114099.6(a) |
| Warm water for handwash only | Allowed if water supply used ONLY for handwashing | HSC 114192(d) |

## 3. Liquid Waste and Drainage (HSC 114197-114201)

| Requirement | Code Section |
|-------------|-------------|
| Liquid waste through approved plumbing to public sewer or approved private system | HSC 114197 |
| Equipment compartments with moisture: sloped to outlet for complete drainage | HSC 114199 |
| Grease trap/interceptor: NOT in food/utensil handling area unless approved by enforcement agency | HSC 114201(a) |
| Grease traps: easily accessible for servicing | HSC 114201(b) |

---

## 4. Toilet Facilities (HSC 114276)

### Employee Toilets

| Requirement | Code Section |
|-------------|-------------|
| Employee toilet facilities required, clean, good repair | HSC 114276(a) |
| Count per local building/plumbing code | HSC 114276(a) |
| Employee toilets in amusement parks/stadiums/food courts: within 200 ft travel distance (300 ft for pre-existing) | HSC 114250.1(a) |

### Consumer Toilets

| Requirement | Code Section |
|-------------|-------------|
| Required when: onsite food consumption, OR facility >20,000 sq ft built after 7/1/1984 | HSC 114276(b)(1) |
| Consumer path to toilets: must NOT pass through food prep, food storage, or utensil washing areas | HSC 114276(b)(2) |
| Buildings >20,000 sq ft: at least 1 separate men's and 1 separate women's toilet | HSC 114276(b)(3) |
| Toilet room doors: self-closing, prevent passage of flies/dust/odors | HSC 114276(c)(1) |
| Pre-2004 with onsite dining: must provide toilets or post sign stating none provided | HSC 114276(f) |

---

## 5. Janitorial Facilities (HSC 114279-114281)

| Requirement | Code Section |
|-------------|-------------|
| Minimum 1 curbed cleaning facility or janitorial sink with hot and cold water and drain | HSC 114279(a) |
| Separate room/area/cabinet for cleaning equipment and supplies storage | HSC 114281 |
| Must be separated from food prep, food storage, and warewashing areas | HSC 114281 |

---

## 6. Refuse Areas (HSC 114245)

| Area | Requirement | Code Section |
|------|-------------|-------------|
| Indoor refuse storage | Must meet floor, wall, ceiling, and vermin exclusion requirements | HSC 114245.3 |
| Outdoor refuse area | Nonabsorbent material (concrete/asphalt), easily cleanable, durable, sloped to drain | HSC 114245.4 |

---

## 7. No Smoking (HSC 113978)

"No smoking" sign must be posted in food prep, food storage, and warewashing areas.
