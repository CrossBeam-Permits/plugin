# Laguna Beach — Construction Hours & Noise

**Operative code:** LBMC Sec. 7.25.080 (Construction Activity Noise Regulations); Sec. 7.25 (Noise — CBD1/CBD2 receiver-side zones).

---

## Allowable Construction Hours (Sec. 7.25.080)

| Day | Permitted hours |
|-----|----------------|
| **Monday – Friday (weekdays)** | **7:30 AM – 6:00 PM** |
| **Saturday** | **PROHIBITED** (weekend day per §7.25.080(B)) |
| **Sunday** | **PROHIBITED** |
| **Federal Holidays** | **PROHIBITED** |

Verbatim §7.25.080(A)–(B): noise-generating construction allowed on weekdays only, 7:30 a.m. – 6:00 p.m.; on any weekend day or federal holiday "No person ... shall operate any tool, equipment or machine in a manner which produces loud noise."

**There is no Saturday construction window.** Some neighboring cities (Costa Mesa, Newport Beach) allow Saturday 8a–5p; Laguna does not. Several recon agents and historical handouts assumed a Sat window — that assumption is wrong.

**Exceptions §7.25.080(D):** emergency authorization, public-works exception, demonstrable compliance with §7.25.040 dBA limits, or the Sawdust Festival / Art-A-Fair / Laguna Art Museum art-org exception.

---

## Downtown Summer Moratorium

The city has a **summer construction moratorium in the downtown area, typically Memorial Day through Labor Day**, for street-facing construction work. This is to preserve the visitor/tourism experience. Confirm scope and dates with planning at intake. Workarounds:

- Interior-only work may continue (with door closed, no exterior noise generation)
- Pre-moratorium phase: complete all exterior demolition, façade work, hood termination, sign installation before Memorial Day
- Post-moratorium phase: resume after Labor Day

Mark the schedule constraint on the construction-management plan if downtown.

---

## CBD1 / CBD2 Receiver-Side Noise Zones (Sec. 7.25)

Laguna's noise ordinance imposes receiver-side dBA limits in CBD1/CBD2 zones (commercial business districts) for ongoing operational noise (post-construction). Relevant to restaurant TI plan review:

- **Hood exhaust fans** — must meet receiver-side dBA at the property line (and at adjacent residential receivers if applicable)
- **Rooftop HVAC** — same
- **Outdoor sound (music, speakers, patio noise)** — typically governed by CUP conditions of approval, not Sec. 7.25 alone
- **Trash compactors, refrigeration condensers** — must meet receiver-side dBA, often requires sound enclosure

Acoustic analysis is recommended for any plan set that adds mechanical equipment near a residential receiver (within ~50 ft typically). For Mixed-Use buildings with residential above the restaurant, vibration isolation is also relevant.

## Enforcement

LBFD/Building Inspector enforces construction hours during inspection. Code Enforcement and PD handle complaints. CUP review-on-complaint clause (Sec. 25.05.030(G)) allows revocation/modification if a restaurant repeatedly violates noise conditions.

---

## Output Findings

- **PASS** — Sheet G-1 carries the correct construction-hours and Sunday/holiday-prohibited notes; mechanical equipment meets receiver-side dBA where applicable; downtown moratorium acknowledged if downtown.
- **FAIL** — Hours note missing or incorrect; mechanical equipment sound rating not provided; downtown project lacks moratorium acknowledgment.
- **FLAG** — Acoustic analysis recommended but not provided; mechanical adjacent to residential receiver and no sound data on submittal.
- **N/A** — No new mechanical and no exterior construction (rare).
