# Energy Efficiency — Lighting Power Density, HVAC, Alterations

This reference covers California Energy Code (Title 24 Part 6) requirements relevant to restaurant TI projects.

---

## 1. Lighting Power Density (LPD)

### Alteration Trigger

When >=10% of luminaires in a space are altered, replaced, or added, the new lighting must comply with Section 140.6 LPD limits (Section 141.0).

### Complete Building Method (Table 140.6-B)

| Building Type | Allowed LPD (W/sq ft) |
|---------------|----------------------|
| Restaurant | 0.65 |

### Area Category Method (Table 140.6-C)

Use this when different restaurant spaces have different lighting needs:

| Space Type | General LPD (W/sq ft) | Decorative LPD (W/sq ft) | Total (W/sq ft) |
|------------|----------------------|--------------------------|-----------------|
| **Kitchen/Food Preparation** | 0.95 | — | 0.95 |
| Dining — Bar/Lounge, Fine Dining | 0.45 | 0.35 | 0.80 |
| Dining — Cafeteria/Fast Food | 0.45 | — | 0.45 |
| Dining — Family, Leisure | 0.40 | — | 0.40 |
| Corridor | 0.40 | — | 0.40 |
| Restroom | 0.65 | 0.35 | 1.00 |
| Lobby, Main Entry | 0.70 | 0.25 | 0.95 |
| Lounge, Breakroom, Waiting | 0.55 | 0.25 | 0.80 |
| Storage (general) | 0.50 | — | 0.50 |
| Outdoor dining | 0.004-0.075 | — | Varies by lighting zone |

**Judgment call**: Restaurants often mix space types. Use the Area Category Method for mixed spaces; Complete Building Method only when the entire building is the restaurant.

## 2. HVAC Efficiency for Alterations (Section 141.0)

| Trigger | Requirement | Code Section |
|---------|-------------|-------------|
| New or replacement HVAC equipment | Must meet Section 140.4 efficiency requirements | 141.0 |
| New or replacement ductwork | Must meet Section 140.4 insulation and sealing requirements | 141.0 |
| Kitchen exhaust makeup air | Must not exceed 10% of exhaust if internal; see CMC 511.3 | CMC 511.3 |

---

## 3. Commercial Kitchen Metering (Section 130.5-B)

| Requirement | Code Section |
|-------------|-------------|
| Commercial kitchens with load >=25 kVA must be separately metered as load centers | Table 130.5-B |

---

## 4. Fenestration (Windows) for Alterations (Section 141.0-A)

When fenestration (windows, storefronts) is altered in a restaurant TI:

| Requirement | Code Section |
|-------------|-------------|
| Altered fenestration must meet Table 141.0-A U-factor requirements for climate zone | 141.0 |
| Applies to storefront glazing, folding glass walls (NanaWall), replacement windows | 141.0 |

---

## 5. Occupancy Change (Section 141.0)

When occupancy classification changes (e.g., retail to restaurant), energy code requirements apply to the **new** occupancy. The new restaurant must meet the LPD and HVAC requirements for restaurant/A-2 use, not the previous use.
