# Laguna Beach — Outdoor Dining / Sidewalk Cafe

**Operative code:** the zone's use table (Downtown Specific Plan Table 5-1: "Outdoor Dining" is DA, Director administrative approval, in the CBD districts); LBMC Ch. 11.50 (Revocable Encroachment Permits); Ch. 25.52 (parking); Ch. 25.07 (CDP); Sec. 25.05.035 (Temporary Use Permits) only for a temporary or seasonal program.
**Operative state law:** AB 592 (2024) — permanent outdoor dining authorization; preempts over-restrictive local ROW rules.

**Program status (2026-05-12):** The city does NOT yet have a permanent outdoor-dining ordinance. Per LA Times reporting on 2025-11-29 ("Laguna Beach looking to develop long-term outdoor dining program," `latimes.com/socal/daily-pilot/news/story/2025-11-29/...`), the City is still actively developing the long-term program. Pandemic-era TUPs have been extended via successive council actions. The code already gives the permanent route: the use approval from the zone's use table (Director administrative approval in the Downtown Specific Plan) plus a Revocable Encroachment Permit for seating in the public right-of-way (Ch. 11.50; the City's application list names the REP for a sidewalk café). Lead with that route. A TUP fits only a temporary or seasonal program (25.05.035). Watch for a permanent outdoor-dining ordinance in 2026–2027.

## Two Parallel Paths

### Path A — Public Right-of-Way (sidewalk café)

Use this path when seating is in the public sidewalk fronting the restaurant.

| Requirement | Detail |
|-------------|--------|
| Use approval | The zone's use table. Downtown Specific Plan: "Outdoor Dining" is DA in CBD-1, CBD-2, CBD VC, CBD Office, Arts and CBD CB, "subject to approval by the Director of Community Development" (DSP Table 5-1; Ch. 5 "Administrative Approval (DA)"). Elsewhere, the zone list (for example C-N lists restaurants "with or without outdoor seating"; take-out seating "only as authorized under the conditional use permit") |
| Revocable Encroachment Permit (Ch. 11.50) | For seating, railings, planters or platforms on the public sidewalk. "A discretionary approval by the Laguna Beach design review approval authority" after a noticed public hearing (11.50.010, 11.50.035); ADR (B)(3)(a)(viii) when not tied to a DRB or Planning Commission project, hearing not waivable; recorded covenant, liability insurance, removal on demand (11.50.015-11.50.070). 11.50.030 speaks of minor structures in unimproved right-of-way; the City's application list still names the REP for a sidewalk café, so lead with it and confirm the filing at intake |
| Public Works right-of-way permit | For any street work (curb, sidewalk, utility connection) tied to the dining area (Ch. 11.16) |
| Temporary Use Permit (Sec. 25.05.035) | Only for a temporary or seasonal program; not the default route |
| Minimum unobstructed sidewalk width | **3 ft** (ADA path-of-travel must be preserved at all times) |
| No blocking of building entrances | Including adjacent tenants' entries |
| Furniture | Restaurant furniture only — no fixed structures in ROW; tables and chairs must be removable for street cleaning, emergency access |
| Heaters / umbrellas | Permitted with conditions; gas heaters subject to LBFD review |
| Hours | Per the use approval, CUP or TUP conditions |
| AB 592 interaction | State law preempts the city from over-restricting permanent outdoor dining, but ROW safety and ADA conditions still apply locally |
| Indemnity / insurance | Required by the REP (11.50.070) |

**Liquor service in ROW.** ABC license must specifically authorize the outdoor expansion area. Coordinate with ABC; PC may add CUP conditions for ROW liquor service.

### Path B — Private Property (patios, parking-lot conversions, decks)

Use this path when seating is on the restaurant parcel itself (interior courtyard, patio, deck, parking-lot conversion).

| Requirement | Detail |
|-------------|--------|
| Use permit per zone list | E.g. C-1 permits outdoor tables for a non-alcohol full-service restaurant operated inside a building (25.20.004); alcohol, entertainment or take-out seating needs a CUP (25.20.006). See `conditional-use-permit.md` |
| Planning route | Match the physical work to current Sec. 25.05.040 closed lists; street visibility alone is not a trigger — see `planning-design-review-routing.md` |
| Coastal Development Permit | Test intensification by required parking under the governing plan (Sec. 25.07.006): under Ch. 25.52 outdoor seating counts toward food-service floor area, so added seating usually raises required parking and points to a CDP; under the Downtown Specific Plan's single ratio, compute it. See `coastal-development-permit.md` |
| Revised parking calculation | **Outdoor seating counts toward GFA** for parking under Ch. 25.52. See `parking-intensification.md`. |
| Building permit | Required for any structures (railings, roofs, foundations, decks, heaters with gas lines) |
| Fire / LBFD | Hood smoke recirculation, deck fire-resistive construction, exit access through outdoor area — coordinate per LBFD |
| OCHCA approval | Outdoor food prep / service area must meet CRFC overhead protection (12" beyond food/equipment), surfaces, vermin exclusion |

## Parking-Lot Conversions ("Parklet-on-private")

Converting on-site parking stalls into outdoor seating is a common Laguna scope. Implications:

1. **Loses parking** — recompute Ch. 25.52 ratio with new GFA (including new outdoor seating); shortfall needs in-lieu certificates (special parking district only, 25.52.006(E)) or a parking-relief CUP (Ch. 25.52).
2. **Adds GFA** — outdoor seating counts.
3. **Intensifies use when required parking goes up** — additional CDP trigger (Sec. 25.07.006).
4. **May need a CUP** — where the zone list requires one for the restaurant or its outdoor seating, or parking relief is sought.

In practice: a parking-lot conversion matches the commercial parking-area Design Review category at §25.05.040(B)(1)(l), and commonly also needs CDP + CUP + Building + LBFD + OCHCA review plus recalculated parking.

## AB 592 Notes

AB 592 (effective 2024) made outdoor dining authorization permanent and limits cities from imposing over-restrictive requirements. Key points:

- Cities may still apply uniformly applicable health, safety, and accessibility standards.
- Cities may still require building/fire/health plan review.
- Cities may NOT impose moratoria solely targeted at outdoor dining or require restaurants to revert to pre-2020 footprints.
- ABC license expansions associated with COVID-era authorizations may be made permanent per BPC Sec. 25750.5.

The state baseline in the California Restaurant Tenant Improvement rules (AB 592 — Outdoor Dining and Open-Front Restaurant Operations), which come from the CrossBeam connector (see "California Code Compliance" in `../SKILL.md`) (the California Restaurant Tenant Improvement rules (AB 592 — Outdoor Dining and Open-Front Restaurant Operations), which come from the CrossBeam connector (see "California Code Compliance" in `../SKILL.md`) in the state skill) carries the substantive AB 592 rules. ## Output Findings

- **PASS** — Correct path (ROW vs private) identified, all required permits in application, parking recomputed, OCHCA / LBFD coordination documented.
- **FAIL** — Sidewalk dining proposed without the use approval and Revocable Encroachment Permit (ROW), or without the zone-required CUP (private); parking not recomputed; CDP not addressed.
- **FLAG** — Outdoor dining location ambiguous (ROW vs property line); ABC license expansion status unclear; AB 592 vs local ROW conditions conflict.
- **N/A** — No outdoor dining in scope.
