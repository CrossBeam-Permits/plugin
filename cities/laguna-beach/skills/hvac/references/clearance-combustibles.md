# Clearance to Combustibles & Furnace Installation

Source: 2025 California Mechanical Code (Title 24, Part 4), Sections 303 and 904.

## General Clearance Rule (CMC 303.1)

Listed equipment and appliances shall be installed in accordance with the **conditions of the listing, the manufacturer's installation instructions, and the CMC**. Clearances from combustible materials shall be those specified in the listing or on the rating plate.

> These authorities apply together; manufacturer instructions do not displace listing conditions or CMC requirements.

## Closet and Alcove Installations (CMC 303.2)

- Clearance reductions shall **NOT be allowed** for appliances listed for closet or alcove installation
- Central heating furnaces **NOT listed** for closet or alcove installation shall be installed in a room or space having a volume **not less than 12 times the total volume of the furnace**

## Clearance Reduction Methods (CMC 303.10)

Section 303.10 requires appliance and vent-connector clearances that avoid hazards and follow the applicable Chapter 8, Chapter 9, or NFPA 211 provisions. Where the CMC does not provide a clearance, apply the listing and manufacturer's installation instructions.

Use Section and Table 303.10.1 only for a permitted **clearance reduction**:
- Listed equipment and appliances must comply with both the listing and Table 303.10.1.
- For unlisted equipment and appliances, the manufacturer must permit the reduction, the CMC must not otherwise provide it, and Table 303.10.1 applies.

## Furnace-Specific Requirements

### Bedroom and Bathroom Installations (CMC 904.2)

A central heating furnace or low-pressure boiler installed in a bedroom or bathroom shall use one of these configurations:

- A closet in the bedroom or bathroom with a listed, gasketed door assembly and listed self-closing device; a compliant threshold and bottom door seal; combustion air obtained from outdoors; and exclusive use of the closet for the furnace or boiler; or
- A direct-vent central heating furnace or low-pressure boiler.

### Floor Construction (CMC 904.4)

A central heating furnace or boiler shall be installed according to the manufacturer's instructions using one of these methods:

- A floor of noncombustible construction with noncombustible flooring and finish and no combustible material against its underside; or
- A fire-resistive slab or arch with no combustible material against its underside.

An appliance listed for installation on a combustible floor, or an installation on a floor protected in an approved manner, is excepted from those two methods.

### Crawl Space Installation (CMC 904.4.1.1-904.4.1.2)

Furnaces installed in crawl spaces shall be supported on:
- A concrete slab minimum **3 in. above adjoining ground**; OR
- Suspended from floor framing above per manufacturer's instructions, minimum **6 in. above ground**

### Furnace with Cooling Coil (CMC 904.8(1))

A furnace with an attached cooling coil shall have a blower capable of overcoming the resistance of the cooling coil and duct system friction losses.

### Return Air Requirements (CMC 604.4)

Where a central heating furnace is installed so supply ducts carry air to areas outside the space containing the furnace, the return air shall also be handled by a duct sealed to the furnace casing and terminating outside the furnace space.

### Attic Installation (CMC 904.10)

Furnaces may be installed in an attic or furred space more than **5 ft in height**, provided:
- Required listings are met
- Furnace and duct clearances are observed
- Horizontal furnaces may be installed in attics with proper clearances

## Supply Duct Clearance Near Furnace

### Listed Furnaces (CMC 603.13.2)

Supply air ducts connecting to listed central heating furnaces shall have the same minimum clearance to combustibles as required for the furnace supply plenum for a distance of **not less than 3 ft** from the supply plenum.

### Unlisted Furnaces (CMC 603.13.3)

Supply air ducts connecting to unlisted furnaces with temperature limit controls (max 250 deg F):
- Minimum **6 in. clearance** to combustibles for a distance of **not less than 6 ft** from the supply plenum

## Framing Requirements for Attic Furnaces

For furnaces less than 350 lbs on existing conventional roof framing:

| Requirement | Detail |
|------------|--------|
| Weight limit | Must be less than 350 lbs (engineering required if heavier) |
| Truss framing | Engineering design required for trusses |
| Location | Close to at least 2 adjacent walls |
| Modified joists | If 1 ceiling joist is cut for access, adjacent joists shall be doubled |
| Vertical clearance | 30 in. min from top of ceiling framing to underside of roof framing |

> Source: Costa Mesa standard plan reference; this reflects common California practice based on CBC structural requirements.

## Quick Reference — Clearance Summary

| Situation | Clearance | Code |
|-----------|----------|------|
| General rule | Per the listing/rating plate, manufacturer's instructions, and CMC | CMC 303.1 |
| Non-listed furnace room volume | >= 12x furnace volume | CMC 303.2 |
| Supply duct (listed furnace) | Same as furnace plenum for 3 ft | CMC 603.13.2 |
| Supply duct (unlisted furnace) | 6 in. for 6 ft from plenum | CMC 603.13.3 |
| Single-wall vent pipe | Per Table 802.7.3.3 for the appliance and vent material | CMC 802.7.3.3 |
| Type B vent | Per its listing and the manufacturer's installation instructions | CMC 802.1–802.1.1 |
| Bedroom or bathroom furnace/boiler | Comply with the §904.2 closet configuration or use a direct-vent unit | CMC 904.2 |
| Furnace floor | Use a §904.4 installation method or an applicable listed/approved exception | CMC 904.4 |
| Crawl space support | 3 in. above grade (slab) or 6 in. (suspended) | CMC 904.4.1.1-904.4.1.2 |

**Vent-clearance source boundary:** Single-wall metal-pipe clearance varies by appliance and vent material under Table 802.7.3.3; Type B vent clearance follows the product listing and manufacturer's instructions. Do not apply the former universal 6-inch single-wall or 1-inch Type B values.
