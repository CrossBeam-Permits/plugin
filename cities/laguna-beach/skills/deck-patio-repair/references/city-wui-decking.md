# Laguna Beach WUI Code — Fire-Resistive Deck & Exterior Construction Requirements

Source: the City source document (not included in this plugin) (LBMC Ch. 14.70, Ord. 1725, 12/18/2025), the City source document (not included in this plugin) (LBMC Ch. 14.50), and California Wildland-Urban Interface Code (IWUIC) Section 504.7.3

---

## Scope — WUI Code Applies to Deck Repairs and Alterations

Per Section 14.70.015 (amending IWUIC 101.2), the WUI code applies to building materials, systems, and assemblies used in **exterior design and construction** of:
- New buildings
- Additions
- Alterations
- **Repairs**

Located within the wildland-urban interface (WUI) area in Laguna Beach.

**Exception**: With building official approval, minor repairs or replacement of exterior materials may use same materials as existing.

**What this means for decks**: Any deck repair, alteration, or new construction in the VHFHSZ must comply with WUI fire-resistive requirements unless the building official grants the minor repair exception.

---

## Deck Walking Surface Materials (IWUIC 504.7.3)

In VHFHSZ areas, the walking surface material of decks, porches, balconies, and stairs must be constructed with one of the following:

### Option 1 — Fire-Tested Material (ASTM E2632 + ASTM E2726)
Material must pass both tests:
- **ASTM E2632**: Peak heat release rate <=25 kW/ft2 (269 kW/m2), no sustained flaming/glowing at end of 40-min observation, no burning falling particles
- **ASTM E2726**: Tested with Class A roof test brand, no sustained flaming/glowing at end of 40-min observation, no burning falling particles
- Must also comply with ASTM E84 or UL 723 per Section 503.2.4

### Option 2 — Ignition-Resistant Building Material
Per Section 503.2.4 performance requirements.

### Option 3 — SFM Standard 12-7A-4 + Section 503.2.4
Must comply with both the SFM fire test standard and flame spread requirements.

### Option 4 — Fire-Retardant-Treated Wood
Labeled for exterior use, complying with CBC Section 2303.2.

### Option 5 — Noncombustible Material
Concrete, stone, brick, metal grating, etc.

### Option 6 — SFM Standard 12-7A-4A Material
With attached exterior wall covering that is noncombustible or ignition-resistant.
**Exception**: Wall may be any compliant material if decking achieves Class B flame spread per ASTM E84 or UL 723.

### Option 7 — ASTM E2632 Performance Material
Peak heat release <=25 kW/ft2, with attached wall noncombustible or ignition-resistant.
**Exception**: Same as Option 6.

### Practical Guidance

| Material | Qualifies? | Notes |
|----------|-----------|-------|
| Standard pressure-treated lumber (ACQ/CA) | **NO** — unless manufacturer provides ASTM E2632/E2726 test data | Most common PT lumber does NOT meet IWUIC 504.7.3 |
| Fire-retardant-treated wood (FRTW) | **YES** (Option 4) | Must be labeled for exterior use per CBC 2303.2 |
| Composite decking (Trex, TimberTech, etc.) | **Maybe** | Must have ASTM E2632/E2726 test reports; many premium composite products qualify |
| Concrete / stone / tile | **YES** (Option 5) | Noncombustible |
| Aluminum decking | **YES** (Option 5) | Noncombustible |
| Ipe / tropical hardwood | **NO** — unless tested | Natural hardwoods do not automatically qualify despite high density |

**CRITICAL FOR PLAN REVIEW**: Always require documentation (test reports, manufacturer certification, or material labeling) showing the proposed decking material meets one of the seven options above.

## Deck-to-Wall Flashing (IWUIC 504.7.3.1)

A minimum **6-inch metal flashing**, applied vertically on the exterior wall, is required at ALL deck-to-wall intersections.

This is **in addition to** the CRC R507.9.1.5 ledger flashing requirements (which specify min 2" vertical extension). The IWUIC requirement is more restrictive at 6 inches.

---

## Exterior Wall Coverings Adjacent to Decks (Ch. 14.70.030)

Per Section 14.70.030 (amending IWUIC 504.5.2), the exterior wall covering or assembly at walls adjacent to decks must comply with one of:

1. **Noncombustible material**
2. **Ignition-resistant building material** labeled for exterior use
3. **Fire-retardant-treated wood** labeled for exterior use per CBC Section 2303.2
4. **Fire-retardant-treated wood shingles/shakes** qualified as Class B roof covering per CBC 1505.6, installed over solid sheathing

**Why this matters for deck permits**: When a deck is attached to the house, the wall section adjacent to the deck must meet these WUI requirements. If existing siding is combustible (e.g., untreated wood siding), it may need to be replaced where it meets the deck — especially if the project scope triggers WUI compliance beyond the minor repair exception.

## Underfloor Ventilation Under Elevated Decks (Ch. 14.70.025)

Per Section 14.70.025 (adding IWUIC 504.10.4):

Each individual ventilation opening into **underfloor spaces** must NOT exceed **144 square inches** in area.

Openings exceeding 144 sq in must be protected by a minimum **1-3/8" thick solid core wooden door or equivalent**.

**Application to decks**: Elevated decks with enclosed underfloor framing spaces (skirted decks, decks built over crawl spaces) are subject to this vent size restriction. Open-frame decks without skirting or enclosure generally do not have underfloor vent openings subject to this provision.

---

## Balcony and Projection Provisions (Ch. 14.50.116)

Per Section 14.50.116 (amending CBC 705.2.3.1):

Balconies and similar projections of combustible construction (other than fire-retardant-treated wood) must be fire-resistance rated per Table 601 for floor construction, or Type IV construction. Aggregate length limited to 50% of building perimeter per floor.

### Exceptions:

| Exception | Condition |
|-----------|-----------|
| **Type I/II buildings, <=3 stories** | Fire-retardant-treated wood permitted for balconies, porches, decks, exterior stairways NOT used as required exits |
| **Type III/IV/V buildings** | Type V construction permitted but must comply with WUI Chapter 5 (Section 504) — meaning full WUI decking material requirements apply |
| **Sprinkler extension** | Where sprinkler protection extends to balcony areas, aggregate length limit is removed |

**Applicability**: This primarily affects multi-family and commercial buildings, not single-family residential decks (which are governed by CRC). However, duplexes and townhouses in Laguna Beach may trigger these provisions.

## Stainless Steel Fastener Requirement (Coastal Proximity)

Per CRC R507.2.3 and Table R507.2.3 (state code): within 300 ft of salt water, ALL fasteners and connectors must be **stainless steel**.

Laguna Beach is a narrow coastal city — the vast majority of properties are within 300 ft of the ocean or tidal lagoons. **For practical purposes, assume stainless steel is required for ALL deck projects in Laguna Beach** unless the property is verified to be more than 300 ft from salt water.

### Affected Hardware:
- Nails (framing, decking attachment)
- Screws (decking, ledger, guards)
- Bolts, nuts, washers (ledger connection, post bases)
- Metal connectors (joist hangers, post-to-beam, post-to-footing)
- Guard post hardware
- Lateral load hold-down devices

Standard hot-dipped galvanized hardware is NOT acceptable within 300 ft of salt water.
