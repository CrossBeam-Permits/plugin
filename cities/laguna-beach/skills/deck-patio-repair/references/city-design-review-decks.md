# Laguna Beach — Current Deck and Patio Planning Route

**Law as of:** 2026-10-10

**Authority:** LBMC §25.05.040 as replaced by Urgency Ordinance No. 1732

## Closed-list classification

| Scope | Current route |
|---|---|
| Deck, slab, terrace, or patio no more than 3 ft above adjacent legal ground elevation | Zone Clearance, §25.05.040(B)(2)(m) |
| Railing | Zone Clearance, §25.05.040(B)(2)(r) |
| Deck, slab, terrace, or patio more than 3 ft above adjacent legal ground elevation | Administrative Design Review, §25.05.040(B)(3)(a)(iii) |
| Deck work that constitutes or accompanies a major remodel | Design Review, §25.05.040(B)(1)(a) |
| New floor area, a story or a raised roof over 15 ft built with the deck | Design Review, §25.05.040(B)(1)(c), for that piece. The deck itself stays under its own item (specific over general); a deck surface or guard more than 15 ft up is UNSETTLED |
| Development in an ESA | Design Review, §25.05.040(B)(1)(e), unless a licensed professional determines, subject to peer review, that it does not impact the ESA |
| Deck on top of a structure, including an ADU roof deck | Design Review (City Planning intake ruling, September 2026; untested since Ord. 1732). Ordinance reading: ADR (iii) |
| In-kind repair or like-for-like replacement (same size, height, location) | Zone Clearance, §25.05.040(B)(2)(c); it governs over the elevated-deck ADR item (ZPC-2026-1362, ZPC-2026-1825) |

**Who hears Design Review:** the Design Review Board for single-family or two-family developments; the Planning Commission for 3+ units, the Downtown Specific Plan area, nonresidential and other projects (§25.05.040(F)(1)(i)-(ii)). The Director may refer any (B)(2) or (B)(3) deck up (§25.05.040(A)(2)(d)).

The Title 25 ESA definition (§25.08.010, Ord. 1732) expressly includes slopes of 30% or greater, for Design Review. Chapter 25.07 keeps its own habitat-only ESA definition (§25.07.006, "For the purposes of this chapter"), so a 30% slope alone does not defeat a CDP exemption. A CDP may still be required under Chapter 25.07 for other reasons.

The elevated-deck ADR category cannot use the public-hearing waiver. Mailed hearing notice is due at least 14 calendar days before hearing; if the project must be staked, staking/certification uses the same minimum. Appeals must be filed within 14 calendar days.

## Repealed rules not to use

- Do not cite former `(B)(2)(h)`, `(B)(2)(l)`, or `(B)(2)(m)` for the old patio/deck/railing matrix.
- Do not use former `(B)(2)(w)` as a categorical WUI-hardening exemption; in current law `(B)(2)(w)` governs certain above-ground utility structures.
- Do not route by the former aggregate-addition/new-upper-story `(B)(1)(j)` language; current `(B)(1)(j)` governs pool/spa height.
- Do not omit the independent timing rules: §25.05.065(D) requires 21/14-day mailed notice for Design Review/ADR, the staking provisions use the same respective minimums when staking applies, and §25.05.065(E) separately requires posting at least 10 days before hearing.

## ADU-attached decks

Urgency Ord. 1713, LBMC 25.17.020(c)(5): covered porches, decks, patios and other outdoor living space attached to a coastal-zone ADU must meet the ADU's height and setbacks, and roof decks (a deck above the uppermost story with no access from interior living space at the same level) are prohibited. For an ADU that needs a CDP the certified Ord. 1687/1691 text still binds (25.17.010(d)(1)(B), Ord. 1713), so its cap applies: outdoor living space "limited to 20% maximum floor area of the ADU" (certified 25.17.040(E)(5)(c)). An ADU with no CDP has no 20% cap. Elevated decks still route by the closed lists above.

## Coastal screen

Repair/maintenance without addition, enlargement, or expansion may qualify for the §25.07.008(A)(3) CDP exemption. New or enlarged work, work near a bluff or a habitat ESA (§25.07.006; slope alone is not an ESA under Ch. 25.07), and work in an appealable area require the full Chapter 25.07 screen. Do not infer a CDP exemption from Zone Clearance.
