# Energy Storage System (ESS) Requirements

Source: 2025 California Residential Code (Title 24, Part 2.5), Section R330; 2025 California Fire Code (Title 24, Part 9), Section 1207; 2025 California Electrical Code (Title 24, Part 3), Article 706.

---

## Equipment Listings (CRC R330.2)

ESS shall be listed and labeled per **UL 9540**.

**Exception**: Where approved, repurposed unlisted battery systems from electric vehicles are allowed to be installed **outdoors** or in **detached sheds** located not less than **5 ft (1524 mm)** from exterior walls, property lines, and public ways.

---

## Installation Requirements (CRC R330.3)

- Installed per manufacturer instructions and listing
- **Spacing**: Individual units separated by not less than **3 ft (914 mm)** from each other
  - Exception: Smaller separation permitted where documented adequate by large-scale fire testing per CFC 1207.1.7

---

## Permitted Locations (CRC R330.4)

Unless the habitable-space exception below applies, ESS shall be installed only in:

| Location | Notes |
|----------|-------|
| **Detached garages and detached accessory structures** | -- |
| **Attached garages** | Separated from dwelling per R302.6 |
| **Outdoors / exterior side of exterior walls** | Not less than **3 ft (914 mm) from doors and windows** directly entering dwelling |
| **Enclosed utility closets, basements, storage/utility spaces** within dwelling | Finished or noncombustible walls and ceilings required. Unfinished wood-frame: provide **5/8" Type X gypsum wallboard** |

### Locations NOT Permitted
- **Sleeping rooms**
- Closets or spaces **opening directly into sleeping rooms**

### Habitable-Space Exception (CRC R330.1 Exception 1)

ESS listed and labeled for use in habitable spaces in accordance with **UL 9540** may be installed through the R330.1 Exception 1 path when installed in accordance with the listing, the manufacturer's instructions, and the California Electrical Code. This exception does not permit installation in sleeping rooms or closets or spaces opening directly into sleeping rooms, which remain prohibited by R330.4.

---

## Maximum Aggregate Ratings (CRC R330.5 / Table R330.5)

| Location | Max Aggregate kWh | Installation Requirements |
|----------|-------------------|--------------------------|
| Utility closets, basements, storage/utility spaces in dwellings | **40** | -- |
| Attached garages | **80** | -- |
| Exterior walls within 3 ft of dwellings (noncombustible wall) | **200** | Exterior walls/eaves constructed with noncombustible surface* |
| Exterior walls within 3 ft (no noncombustible) | **100** | -- |
| On/within 3 ft of exterior walls, combustible | **100** | -- |
| Detached garages/accessory structures | **200** | -- |
| Detached garages/accessory structures (>=10 ft from property lines and dwellings) | **600** | Min 10 ft from property lines and dwellings |
| Outdoors on ground (<10 ft from property lines/dwellings) | **200** | Min 3 ft from property lines and dwellings |
| Outdoors on ground (>=10 ft from property lines/dwellings) | **600** | Min 10 ft from property lines and dwellings |

*Noncombustible wall surface shall extend: min 5 ft horizontally from ESS edge, min 1 ft below bottom edge, min 8 ft above ESS or to noncombustible eave (whichever less).*

### Individual Unit Limits
- Individual ESS units: max **20 kWh** each
- Total aggregate on property: max **600 kWh**
- Installations exceeding limits: per CFC 1207.1 through 1207.9

---

## Electrical Requirements (CRC R330.6)

- Installed per **California Electrical Code (Article 706)**
- Inverters listed per **UL 1741** or provided as part of UL 9540 listing
- Grid-connected systems: inverters listed for **utility interaction**

---

## Fire Detection (CRC R330.7)

### Smoke Alarms
Rooms and areas where ESS installed (dwelling units, basements, attached garages) shall have **smoke alarms per R310**.

### Heat Detectors
A **listed heat detector**, interconnected to smoke alarms, shall be installed in locations where smoke alarms **cannot be installed based on their listing** (e.g., garages).

**Note**: Attached garages with ESS require a listed heat detector interconnected with dwelling smoke alarms.

---

## Impact Protection (CRC R330.8)

### When Required
ESS installed in locations subject to **vehicle damage**:

### Normal Driving Path (R330.8.1)
Space between garage vehicle opening and interior face of back wall, to 48" above finished floor, width of garage door opening.

Impact protection required for ESS:
- In the normal driving path
- On back wall within **36" left or right** of driving path
- On side wall within **24" from back wall** and **36" of driving path**

**Exception**: Impact protection is not required only where both conditions are met: the clear height of the garage vehicle opening is **7 ft 6 in. or less**, and the ESS is installed **not less than 36 in. above the finished floor**.

A back-wall or side-wall location or mounting height alone does not satisfy the exception.

### Impact Protection Methods (R330.8.3)

Use one of the R330.8.3 impact-protection options: compliant bollards, a compliant wheel-barrier option under R330.8.3 Item 2, or another approved method designed for the specified impact load under Item 3.

#### Bollard Options

| Option | Construction | Spacing and ESS Clearance |
|--------|--------------|---------------------------|
| Embedded steel pipe | Minimum 48-in.-long by 3-in.-diameter Schedule 80 steel pipe; embedded in a concrete pier at least 12 in. deep and 6 in. in diameter; at least 36 in. exposed; pipe filled with concrete | Maximum 5-ft interval; at least 6 in. from the ESS |
| Surface-mounted steel pipe | Minimum 36-in.-high by 3-in.-diameter Schedule 80 steel pipe fully welded to a steel plate at least 8 in. long by 1/4 in. thick; bolted to the concrete floor with 4-1/2-in.-long concrete anchors embedded at least 3 in. | Maximum 60-in. spacing; at least 6 in. from the ESS |
| Premanufactured steel pipe | Concrete-filled bollard anchored according to the manufacturer's installation instructions | Maximum 60-in. spacing; at least 6 in. from the ESS |

The former single 38-in.-high hybrid bollard recipe is not an R330.8.3 option; select one complete option above without combining its dimensions with another.

**Parking and encroachment source boundary:** CRC R330 and the reviewed CFC 1207 corpus do not establish the former 10-ft-by-20-ft or 20-ft-by-20-ft required-parking-space bollard prohibition or a universal 10-in. ESS encroachment allowance. Apply either rule only after recording the named jurisdiction or authority, current document and edition or effective date, page/section/detail locator, geographic scope, project-type scope, and confirmation that the source remains current.

## Mechanical Ventilation (CRC R330.9)

ESS that produce **hydrogen or other flammable gases** during charging shall be provided with **mechanical ventilation** per California Mechanical Code.

**Note**: Lithium-ion batteries do not typically produce hydrogen during normal operation. Lead-acid and nickel-cadmium batteries may require ventilation.

---

## Electric Vehicle Use (CRC R330.10)

Temporary use of owner/occupant electric vehicle to power dwelling while parked in attached/detached garage or outdoors:
- Comply with **vehicle manufacturer instructions**
- Comply with **California Electrical Code**

---

## Labeling and Documentation (CRC R330.11)

1. Copy of manufacturer's **installation, operation, maintenance, and decommissioning instructions** provided to owner or placed at conspicuous location near ESS
2. **Label on installed system** with contact information for qualified maintenance and service providers

---

## ESS Exceeding Table R330.5 Limits (CRC R330.5)

ESS with capacities exceeding Table R330.5 values or Table 1207.11.4 (CFC) shall comply with **CFC Section 1207.1 through 1207.9**.

---

## Toxic Gas Prohibition (CRC R330.12)

ESS that have potential to release **toxic or highly toxic gas** during charging, discharging, and normal use shall **NOT be installed** within Group R-3 or R-4 occupancies.
