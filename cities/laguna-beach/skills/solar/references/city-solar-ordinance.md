# Laguna Beach Solar — Municipal Code Ch. 14.84

Source: the City source document (not included in this plugin) (ecode360.com/42894680)
Ordinance: Ord. 1607 § 1, 2015

---

## Key Provisions

### Applicability (§14.84.030)
- Covers ALL small residential rooftop solar energy systems in the city
- Systems legally established before this chapter → not subject unless materially modified
- Routine maintenance and like-kind replacements → NO permit required

### Definition: Small Residential Rooftop Solar (§14.84.020)
ALL four conditions must be met:
1. Not larger than **10 kW AC nameplate** or **30 kW thermal**
2. Conforms to all state fire, structural, electrical codes as adopted by Laguna Beach
3. Installed on a **single- or duplex-family dwelling**
4. Does not exceed **maximum legal building height**

### System Requirements (§14.84.040)
- Must meet all applicable health/safety standards (city + state)
- Water heating systems: certified by accredited listing agency per CA Plumbing/Mechanical Code
- Electricity systems: meet CEC, IEEE, UL standards, and PUC rules

### Permit Fees (§14.84.060)
- Determined by city council resolution
- **Must not exceed 10% of original system cost, max $1,000**
- Fee per Gov. Code §§ 65850.55, 66015 and H&S Code § 17951
- **Fee may be WAIVED** if Community Development Director determines no significant aesthetic or view impacts

### Expedited Permit Review (§14.84.070)
- **Administrative, nondiscretionary** review process
- Applications meeting checklist/standard plan: **building permit issued within 3 business days**
- Review limited to health and safety requirements only
- Building official may require use permit only if **specific, adverse impact** on public health/safety (based on substantial evidence)
- Use permit denial requires written findings + basis for rejecting feasible alternatives
- Conditions must mitigate at lowest possible cost; city shall use best efforts to ensure the selected method, condition, or mitigation meets Civil Code § 714(d)(1)(A) and (B) (Solar Rights Act)
- **NOT subject to HOA approval** (Civil Code § 4080)

### Inspection Requirements (§14.84.070(g)-(i))
- **Only ONE inspection required** for expedited-eligible systems
- Inspection scheduled within **2 business days** of request
- Consolidated inspections encouraged
- If system fails inspection → subsequent inspection authorized (but not bound by expedited timeline)

### Electronic Submittal (§14.84.050)
- All documents available on city website
- Electronic submittal (email/web/fax) must be available
- Electronic signatures accepted

### Board of Appeals
- Board of appeals = **Planning Commission** (not separate board)
- Appeals from building official decisions go to Planning Commission
