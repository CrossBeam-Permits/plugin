# Gas Water Heater Venting & Combustion Air

## 1. Appliance Venting Categories

Gas appliances are classified into four venting categories based on flue gas pressure and condensation behavior. The examples below are illustrative; determine the actual category from the appliance listing and manufacturer documentation.

| Category | Flue Pressure | Condensation | Common Example — Verify Listing |
|----------|--------------|--------------|----------------------------------|
| I | Negative (natural draft) | Non-condensing | Standard atmospheric tank WH (40-gal, 50-gal with draft hood) |
| II | Negative | Condensing | Rare in residential |
| III | Positive | Non-condensing | Some power-vent tank WH |
| IV | Positive | Condensing | Some condensing tankless and tank water heaters |

**Key rule:** Table 509.4 permits several venting-system paths for Category I appliances, including Type B gas vent, a chimney, single-wall metal pipe where allowed, a listed chimney-lining system, or a listed special gas vent. Categories II-IV use venting specified or furnished by the listed-appliance manufacturer (CPC 509.4.1). Listed direct-vent appliances follow their manufacturer instructions; through-the-wall terminations also comply with CPC 509.2.6.1 and 509.8.1.

## 2. Vent Types

### Type B Gas Vent (Double-Wall)
- Listed to UL 441 (CPC 509.1)
- **Permitted for:** Listed Category I appliances and other appliances identified in Table 509.4; this is a common residential tank-water-heater path, but not the only permitted path
- Double-wall construction: aluminum inner liner, galvanized steel outer
- Clearance to combustibles: as listed (typically 1 inch)
- Screws/rivets shall not penetrate the inner wall except at the transition from draft hood/flue collar (CPC 509.6(4))

### Single-Wall Metal Connector
- Galvanized sheet steel, min 0.0304 inch thick (CPC 509.7)
- **Single-wall metal pipe shall NOT be used as a vent in dwellings and residential occupancies** (CPC 509.7.3)
- May only be used as a **vent connector** (the horizontal run from appliance to vertical vent) in conditioned spaces
- Cannot pass through attics, inside walls, concealed spaces, or floors (CPC 509.7.3.2)
- Clearance to combustibles: 6 inches for listed appliances with draft hoods (Table 509.7.3.4(1))
- Cannot be used outdoors where 99% winter design temp is below 32F (CPC 509.7.1)

### Key Distinction
- **Vent connector** = horizontal run from appliance draft hood to vertical vent/chimney
- **Vent** = the vertical portion terminating through the roof
- Single-wall is allowed for connectors in conditioned space but is prohibited as the vent in dwellings; select the vertical vent from the other applicable Table 509.4 paths

## 3. Vent Connector and Vent Sizing

### Primary Sizing Routes

- Size a connector serving one appliance with a single draft hood or a Category I fan-assisted combustion system under the applicable Section 510 tables or an engineering method (CPC 509.10.2)
- Where two or more appliances connect to a common vent or chimney, size each connector under Section 510 or an engineering method (CPC 509.10.2.2)
- For a qualifying single-story natural-draft Category I venting system, CPC 509.6.2.1 permits Section 510 sizing, the exact effective-area alternatives in 509.6.2.1(3) or (4), or engineering methods. Those effective-area alternatives are complete methods for their stated configurations, not universal connector prerequisites
- Fan-assisted systems and mixed fan-assisted/draft-hood systems use Section 510 or other engineering methods (CPC 509.6.2.1(2))

### Section 510 Boundaries

- Where a Section 510 table determines a vent size smaller than the outlet or flue collar, use that smaller size only when every condition and exception in CPC 510.1.1 is met; do not apply a partial downsizing shortcut
- For a single-appliance configuration, connector upsizing is limited by CPC 510.1.12; for a multiple-appliance configuration, use CPC 510.2.21
- Corrugated connectors shall not be smaller than the listed categorized vent diameter, flue collar diameter, or draft hood outlet diameter (CPC 510.1.11)
- For an appliance with multiple draft-hood outlets or flue collars, follow the manufacturer manifold instructions or an engineering method. CPC 509.10.2.1 provides a separate alternative effective-area method that includes the 1-foot-rise condition

### Additional Termination Rules
- **Type B/L vent:** minimum 5 feet vertical above highest connected draft hood or flue collar (CPC 509.6.1(2))
- **Chimneys:** at least 3 feet above roof penetration AND 2 feet above any building surface within 10 feet horizontal (CPC 509.5.4)
- **Chimney height:** at least 5 feet above highest connected draft hood outlet (CPC 509.5.4.2)
- **Vents >12 inches or <8 feet from wall:** terminate at least 2 feet above roof penetration AND 2 feet above any building within 10 feet horizontal (CPC 509.6.1(1)(b))
- Vent must terminate at least 3 feet above any forced air inlet within 10 feet (CPC 509.6.1(7))
- All gas vents must terminate with a listed cap or roof assembly (CPC 509.6.1(6))

## 5. Critical Connector and Vent Rules

### No Reduction in Direction of Flow
- Vent connectors sized per tables shall not reduce in the direction of flow unless specific downsizing rules are met (CPC 510.1.1)

### Connector Slope
- Minimum 1/4 inch per foot upward slope toward the vent or chimney (CPC 509.10.7)
- No dips or sags permitted
- Exception: connectors attached to mechanical draft systems per manufacturer instructions

### Maximum Connector Length
- **Single-wall connector:** max horizontal length = 75% of chimney/vent height (CPC 509.10.8.1)
- **Type B double-wall connector:** max horizontal length = 100% of chimney/vent height (CPC 509.10.8.2)

### Connector Maximum Horizontal Length by Diameter (Table 510.2)
| Connector Diameter (in) | Max Horizontal Length (ft) |
|------------------------|--------------------------|
| 3 | 4.5 |
| 4 | 6 |
| 5 | 7.5 |
| 6 | 9 |
| 7 | 10.5 |
| 8 | 12 |

### Elbows and Joints
- Table lateral lengths already include two 90-degree elbows; each additional elbow up to 45 deg reduces max capacity by 5%, each 45-90 deg elbow by 10% (CPC 510.1.2)
- Joints: min 3 sheet-metal screws equally spaced, or listed vent material per manufacturer (CPC 509.10.5)

## 6. Combustion Air Requirements (CPC 506.0 / CMC 701.0)

Combustion air rules appear in CPC 506 and CMC 701. They largely parallel each other under different section numbers; CPC 506 governs the water-heater installation path.

### General Rule (CPC 506.1 / CMC 701.1)
- Combustion air shall be provided in **all spaces containing Category I appliances** (most standard tank water heaters)
- Other appliances (not Category I) shall provide combustion air per manufacturer's instructions (CPC 506.1.1 / CMC 701.1.1)
- Draft hood shall be in the **same room/space** as the appliance (CPC 506.1.2 / CMC 701.2)

### Indoor-Air Method Boundary
Indoor combustion air is permitted when the required volume is established under the standard method or the known-air-infiltration-rate method. Where the infiltration rate is known to be less than 0.40 ACH, CPC 506.2.2 / CMC 701.4.2 requires the known-rate calculation; 0.40 ACH is not a minimum eligibility threshold. If the applicable indoor-air method is not met, introduce outdoor air under CPC 506.4-506.7.3 / CMC 701.6-701.9.3. Do not assume that leakage through a garage door or other envelope component supplies adequate combustion air without the required volume or calculation.

---

### Combustion Air Option Summary

| Option | Method | Sizing | Key Requirement |
|--------|--------|--------|-----------------|
| **1. Single Opening** | 1 opening in upper 12 in of exterior wall | Min 1 sq in / 3,000 BTU/h | Opening >= sum of vent connector areas; 1" sides/back, 6" front clearance (CPC 506.4.2 / CMC 701.6.2) |
| **2. Two Exterior Openings** | 2 openings in exterior wall (upper + lower 12 in) | Min 1 sq in / 4,000 BTU/h each (direct) | Both openings direct to outdoors (CPC 506.4.1 / CMC 701.6.1) |
| **3. All Indoor Air** | Standard or known-infiltration method | Standard method: min 50 cu ft / 1,000 BTU/h; known-rate method: calculate under 506.2.2 / 701.4.2 | Known infiltration below 0.40 ACH requires the known-rate method (CPC 506.2 / CMC 701.4) |
| **4. Openings to Indoor Space** | Openings from enclosed space (upper + lower 12 in) | Min 1 sq in / 1,000 BTU/h, not less than 100 sq in | Connected space must have adequate volume (CPC 506.3 / CMC 701.5) |
| **5. Crawl Space & Attic** | Openings to ventilated attic and crawl space | Min 1 sq in / 4,000 BTU/h each | Crawl space supplies the lower opening in this configuration (CPC Figure 506.4.1(1) / CMC Figure 701.6.1(1)) |
| **6. Vertical Ducts to Attic** | 2 openings ducted to ventilated attic | Min 1 sq in / 4,000 BTU/h each | Configure the upper and lower openings under CPC 506.4.1 / CMC 701.6.1 and the applicable figure |

### Horizontal Ducts (CPC 506.4.1 / CMC 701.6.1)
When 2 horizontal ducts are used, sizing increases to **1 sq in per 2,000 BTU/h** each (double the vertical/direct rate).

### Indoor Air Source (CPC 506.2 / CMC 701.4)
- Indoor air may be established by the standard volume method or the known-air-infiltration-rate method
- Where infiltration is known to be less than **0.40 ACH**, use the known-rate calculation in CPC 506.2.2 / CMC 701.4.2; the equations do not permit using a rate above 0.60 ACH
- Under the standard method, minimum required air volume is **50 cu ft per 1,000 BTU/h** (CPC 506.2.1 / CMC 701.4.1)
- Communicating rooms (openings without doors) count toward volume

### Openings (CPC 506.4 / CMC 701.6)
- Minimum dimension of any air opening: **3 inches** (CPC 506.4 / CMC 701.6)
- Where screens are used, the mesh openings shall not be smaller than **1/4 inch** (CPC 506.8.1 / CMC 701.10.1)
- Metal louvers: assume **75% free area**; wood louvers: assume **25% free area** (CPC 506.8 / CMC 701.10)
- Nonmotorized louvers and grilles shall be in a **fixed, open position** (CPC 506.8 / CMC 701.10)
- Motorized louvers shall be interlocked with appliance, fully open before ignition (CPC 506.8.2 / CMC 701.10.2)

### Combustion Air Ducts (CPC 506.9 / CMC 701.11)
- Shall be **galvanized metal** or material of equivalent performance (CPC 506.9(1) / CMC 701.11(1))
- Exterior openings shall be no less than **12 inches above finished grade** (CPC 506.9(6) / CMC 701.11(8))
- Each duct shall serve only **one enclosure** or appliance space (CPC 506.9(3) / CMC 701.11(3))
- Same duct shall NOT serve both upper and lower openings (CPC 506.9(4) / CMC 701.11(4))
- Screens shall NOT be on ducts terminating in attic (CPC 506.9(5) / CMC 701.11(5))
- Remaining space around chimney shall NOT be used for combustion air (CPC 506.9(8) / CMC 701.11(7))
- Combustion-air ducts or plenums shall not be routed where fire dampers are required, and manually operated dampers shall not be installed in combustion-air openings. With prior approval, power-actuated movable louvers may be used when electrically interlocked to prevent fuel delivery unless fully open (CMC 701.12)

**Unsupported-recipe boundary:** Do not restore a numeric attic-sleeve or concealed-vent shield-plate recipe without the named jurisdiction, agency, manufacturer, or standards publisher; current document title and edition/effective date; exact page, table, section, or detail; geographic and project-type scope; and confirmation that the source remains current. Without all five, use the applicable nonnumeric code or approved-design route.

### Attic and Crawl Space Sources (CPC 506.4.1 / CMC 701.6.1)
- Ventilated attics and crawl spaces are considered **equivalent to outdoors** for these opening configurations (CPC 506.4.1 / CMC 701.6.1)
- In the crawl-space/attic configuration, the crawl space supplies the lower opening and the attic receives the upper opening (CPC Figure 506.4.1(1) / CMC Figure 701.6.1(1))

## 6A. Vent Location & Protection (CPC 509.6 / CMC 802.6)

### General (CPC 509.3 / CMC 802.3)
- Venting system shall convey **all flue and vent gases to outdoors**
- Type B and Type B-W gas vents shall comply with UL 441, and Type L gas vents shall comply with UL 641; listed chimneys and vents shall be installed under the applicable code and manufacturer instructions (CPC 509.1-509.1.1 / CMC 802.1-802.1.1)
- Determine the appliance category and fan-assisted status from its listing and manufacturer documentation, then use the applicable CPC 509/510 or CMC 802/803 route

### Protection in Insulated Assemblies (CPC 509.6.1.1)
- Install an approved steel shield at least **26 gauge** between the vent and insulation; extend it at least **2 inches above the insulation** and secure it to the structure under the manufacturer instructions

### Screw Restriction (CPC 509.6(4) / CMC 802.6(4))
- Screws into inner wall of double-wall vent **shall NOT be allowed** except at draft hood outlet or to single-wall connector

### Vent Shall Not Terminate Through (CPC 509.3.5 / CMC 802.3.5)
- Fabricated ducts or plenums

### Vent Termination Requirements (CPC 509.6.1 / CMC 802.6.1)

| Requirement | Specification |
|-------------|---------------|
| **Type B/L vent min height** | 5 ft vertical above draft hood or flue collar (CPC 509.6.1(2) / CMC 802.6.1(2)) |
| **Roof penetration (vents <=12 in)** | Per CPC Table 509.6.1 / CMC Table 802.6.1 by roof slope (min 1 ft for flat to 6:12) |
| **Roof penetration (vents >12 in)** | Min 2 ft above the roof and 2 ft above any building portion within 10 ft horizontally (CPC 509.6.1(1)(b) / CMC 802.6.1(1)(b)) |
| **Forced air inlet clearance** | Min 3 ft above any forced air inlet within 10 ft (CPC 509.6.1(7) / CMC 802.6.1(7)) |
| **Roof flashing** | Vent extends through the roof flashing, roof jack, or roof thimble (CPC 509.6.1(6) / CMC 802.6.1(6)) |
| **Listed cap** | All gas vents shall terminate with a listed cap or listed roof assembly (CPC 509.6.1(6) / CMC 802.6.1(6)) |

## 7. Direct-Vent Appliances (Sealed Combustion)

Direct-vent (sealed combustion) appliances draw combustion air from outdoors and exhaust directly outdoors through a concentric or two-pipe system. CPC 506.1 exempts them from its combustion-air provision, but applicable direct-vent termination and wall-penetration provisions still apply.

### Key Rules (CPC 509.2.6)
- Install listed direct-vent appliances under the manufacturer instructions
- **Combustion air provisions (Section 506) do not apply** to direct-vent appliances (CPC 506.1 Exception)
- Through-the-wall terminations comply with CPC 509.2.6.1 and Table 509.8.1; permanently seal annular spaces under CPC 509.8.2

### Through-the-Wall Termination Clearances (Table 509.8.1)
| Location | Direct Vent | Non-Direct Vent |
|----------|------------|----------------|
| Above grade/deck/porch | 12 in | 12 in |
| To openable window/door (<=10k BTU/hr) | 6 in | 4 ft below/to side, 1 ft above |
| To openable window/door (>10k to <=50k BTU/hr) | 9 in | 4 ft below/to side, 1 ft above |
| To openable window/door (>50k to <=150k) | 12 in | 4 ft below/to side, 1 ft above |
| To openable window/door (>150k) | Manufacturer instructions, but not less than the non-direct-vent row-B clearance | 4 ft below/to side, 1 ft above |
| To mechanical air inlet | 10 ft horiz or 3 ft above | 10 ft horiz or 3 ft above |
| Above paved public sidewalk/driveway or other nuisance/hazard area | 7 ft and not above public walkways or nuisance/hazard areas | 7 ft and not above public walkways or nuisance/hazard areas |
| Under veranda/porch/deck | 12 in (2 sides open) | 12 in (2 sides open) |

Wall penetrations for direct-vent pipes must be permanently sealed (CPC 509.8.2).

## 8. Common Residential Scenarios

### 40-Gallon Tank in Garage (if listed Category I, ~40,000 BTU/hr)
- **Venting:** Select an allowed Table 509.4 system. If Type B is used, determine diameter from the appliance outlet/listing and the applicable Section 510 sizing table; gallon capacity alone does not establish a 4-inch vent
- **Termination:** For a qualifying Type B vent at least 8 feet from a wall on a flat-to-6:12 roof, Table 509.6.1 gives 1 foot above the roof; Section 509.6.1(2) also requires at least 5 feet above the highest connected draft hood or flue collar
- **Combustion air:** Document the applicable CPC 506.2 indoor-air method or provide another CPC 506 method; do not assume garage-door leakage is adequate
- **Elevation:** Burner/ignition 18 inches above floor unless FVIR-listed (CPC 507.13)
- **Connector slope:** 1/4 in/ft upward; **Seismic:** two straps, upper/lower 1/3 (CPC 507.2)

### 50-Gallon Tank in Interior Closet (~50,000 BTU/hr)
- **Venting:** Select a Table 509.4 venting-system path and size it from the appliance outlet/listing and Section 510; Type B is one common path
- **Combustion air (two-opening):** Each opening min 100 sq in (506.3 minimum), one high one low
- **Combustion air (single-opening):** Min 16.7 sq in (50k/3000), at top of closet, direct to outdoors
- **Clearances:** 30 in working space (CPC 507.25); draft hood in same room (CPC 506.1.2)

### Tankless Water Heater (Category IV, ~199,000 BTU/hr)
- **Venting:** Per manufacturer -- typically PVC/CPVC or stainless steel concentric vent
- **Direct-vent models:** Sealed combustion, no combustion air openings needed in room
- **Power-vent models (indoor air):** Need combustion air per 506.0
- **Termination:** Through-the-wall per Table 509.8.1 or through roof per manufacturer
- **Condensate drain:** Required for Category IV (CPC 509.9)
- **Wall termination:** For a ~199,000-BTU/h direct-vent appliance, follow manufacturer instructions and provide no less than the non-direct-vent row-B clearances in Table 509.8.1; do not apply the 9- or 12-inch window-clearance rows for lower input ranges
- **Category IV wall termination:** For a vent through an outside wall that discharges perpendicular to the adjacent wall, provide 10 feet horizontally to an operable opening in an adjacent building, except where the terminal is at least 2 feet above or 25 feet below that opening (CPC 509.8.3)
