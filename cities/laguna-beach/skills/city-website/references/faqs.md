## Can I develop an unimproved property?

Verify whether a property is designated a building site using City Maps (GIS).
Locate the property, then read its data pop-ups for **"building site
designation"** — immediately below it will show "yes" or "no". "Yes" means the
property has the potential to be developed; "no" means it may not be developed.

GIS: https://lagunabeach.maps.arcgis.com/apps/webappviewer/index.html?id=75a3aa3236c7475bb5e81925d130a763

## Can my ADU be used as a short-term rental?

**No.** ADUs are intended to create new housing opportunities for the community.
Short-term rental means renting a lodging unit for **thirty consecutive days or
less**.

## Can my project be reviewed over-the-counter?

⚠️ This answer is about **Planning** over-the-counter review. It is a different
process from the **Building** division's over-the-counter plan review — see
building-permits.md for that one. Don't merge the two lists.

The City FAQ's project examples predate Ord. 1732. Current law requires a Zone
Clearance for the listed Design Review exemptions and no longer contains the
former window-neighbor-consent split. Re-roofs, fences, equipment, and windows
must be routed against current §25.05.040(B) and §25.05.045 even when the counter
can review them without an appointment. Interior modifications with no exterior
modification take Zone Clearance under §25.05.040(B)(2)(d).

Bring **two hard copies**, and per project type:

- **Window replacements** — floor plans identifying which windows are being replaced, all room uses labeled, and a window schedule with the type and size of each. The source FAQ's neighbor-consent shortcut is superseded as a statutory routing rule; use the current closed lists and include elevations for new/expanded openings.
- **New fencing and free-standing walls** — a site plan showing property lines, setbacks, footprint, length, height, materials, and an elevation detail. Height limits are in LBMC § 25.50.012 (generally between 3 and 6 feet), https://ecode360.com/42901720. **Chain link and similar metal fences in residential areas require design review** and are not eligible.
- **Re-roof** — a printed color and material board with manufacturer name, color, and a product photo. **The City generally does not allow white, light tan, or light grey colors, or highly reflective roof finishes.** The planner may request a physical sample.
- **Air conditioning** — complete the air conditioning unit application packet, https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/home/showdocument?id=8866

## Does Laguna Beach require a business license?

**Yes — a business may not be established without first receiving a business
license from the City.** Planning staff at the Community Development front
counter will confirm whether your intended location is zoned for that use, and
advise on approvals needed before you start (for example a Conditional Use
Permit) or zoning deficiencies to address, such as parking.

Apply at the cashier desk at City Hall or online:
https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/do-business-here/business-license/starting-a-business-in-laguna-beach

*(Note: on the City's page the "apply online" link and the "starting a business"
information link point to the same URL. If a resident is looking for a payment
portal specifically, tell them to confirm at the counter.)*

## Does my project require Design Review?

Site improvements and development subject to design review are described in
**LBMC § 25.05.040** — https://ecode360.com/42898558 — which has the
comprehensive list. Requirements may vary with a property's prior entitlement
history. Certain minor development qualifies for streamlined **Administrative
Design Review**; everything else goes to the **Design Review Board or Planning
Commission**.

Common projects generally exempt from design review:
https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/home/showdocument?id=13590

## How do I check the status of my project?

Planning applications generally start with a Zoning Plan Check submittal.
Applications are reviewed **in the order received, with a 30-day turnaround**.
**Contact your assigned planner if you haven't had an update by the end of the
30-day review period.** The planner determines whether the application is
incomplete with corrections required, or complete. Complete applications may
require entitlement review and approval at a noticed public hearing before
construction plans can be submitted for a building permit.

## How do I find the legally permitted size of my home?

It requires review of all building records on file — permits or plans note the
existing or proposed square footage. **If the records show a smaller home than
what exists on the property, the difference is considered unpermitted and must be
legalized or removed in coordination with the City.** Use the "Property
Information" tab at the bottom of the left-hand menu in the City's mapping
software to view City files.

## How do I get a copy of my property's prior-approved plans?

Construction plans are public record but, **due to copyright protection, may only
be inspected at the Community Development public counter**; digital copies are
viewable there during normal business hours.

To obtain a copy you must submit a completed **Plan Duplication Request Form and
Duplication Request Affidavit** (https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/home/showdocument?id=5152).
The City then requests authorization by certified letter from the current or
original property owner and all registered professionals associated with the
plans; **those parties have 30 days to respond**. Once all authorizations are
received the City provides a flash drive with the plans, mailed or picked up at
the counter. Plan retrieval is billed by the City; it is not in Master Fee
Schedule document 26193, and the fee estimate does not model it. Do not quote an
amount. Send the resident to the Planning Division — see `../../city-reference/references/contacts-hours.md`.
Contact staff before requesting — plans are not available for all properties.

## How do I locate property information?

Search the property in the City's mapping software
(https://gis.lagunabeachcity.net/Html5Viewer/index.html?viewer=LagunaBeachPublicGIS),
click the parcel, and you get building site designation, special setback and
height requirements, environmental constraints, and access to planning and
building files. Click "Layer" at the bottom and select "Zoning" for the zoning
district. **Architectural and structural plans, where they exist, can only be
accessed at the City Hall planning counter.**

City Council proceedings: https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/live-here/city-council/meetings-agendas-and-minutes
— find the meeting date and open the document dropdown. For records **before
November 2009**, use the City Clerk's records search:
https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/government/departments/city-clerk/records-search

## How do I submit a planning application?

New planning applications generally start with a **Zoning Plan Check** submittal;
projects limited to interior improvements may go straight to a building permit
submittal.

**Planning applications are accepted by appointment only** and are typically
reviewed within 30 days. **Applications missing required information will not be
accepted, and partially complete applications will not be accepted.** The
appointment portal carries the application forms and submittal requirements —
note that the residential and commercial planning applications have **different**
requirements. Portal:
https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/government/departments/community-development/planning-zoning-copy

⚠️ See planning-applications.md for a conflict between this answer ("by
appointment only") and the design review process page ("accepted only through the
online public permitting portal").

## My Zoning Plan Check application was deemed complete. When will I receive a date for my public hearing?

Once the application is deemed complete, the assigned planner prepares a draft
staff report and resolution analyzing the project for the decision-making
authority. **You will be contacted to fulfill final scheduling requirements once
management staff have approved the staff report and resolution.** (The City gives
no fixed number of days here; the design review process page says complete
applications are tentatively scheduled roughly 45 days out — see
planning-applications.md.)

## Code Enforcement FAQ

Full answers are reproduced in code-enforcement.md. All are
`[source: https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/government/departments/community-development/code-enforcement/frequently-asked-questions, as of 2026-06-18]`:

- **Are leaf blowers allowed?** No — prohibited at any time within city limits, with four narrow exceptions.
- **Who handles overgrown vegetation?** Right-of-way or signage obstruction goes to Code Enforcement; view blockage goes through View Preservation/Restoration, a Hedge Height Claim, or an approved landscape plan; branches between private properties are a civil matter.
- **What does Code Enforcement not handle?** Vegetation between private properties, lawn furniture and umbrellas, portable BBQs and fireplaces, EZ-Up canopies (unless used as a carport), potted plants, flags (unless a new flagpole is structurally installed), and small portable play equipment (unless over 120 sq ft and higher than 12 feet).
- **Are tenant/landlord disputes code enforcement matters?** Typically no — civil, unless building safety or unpermitted construction is involved.
- **How do I confirm a property has active permits, or report unpermitted construction?** Building Division, (949) 497-0715.
- **How do I file a service request or complaint?** In person at 505 Forest Ave., online via Ask Laguna 24/7, or (949) 497-0301.
