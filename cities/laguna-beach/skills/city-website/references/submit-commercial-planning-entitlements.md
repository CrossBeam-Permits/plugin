# Submit: Commercial Planning Entitlements — EnerGov type 116

**The commercial half of the umbrella.** Exactly like Residential Planning
Entitlements (114): there is **no** application type for Design Review, Coastal
Development Permit, Variance, or Conditional Use Permit on the commercial side
either — they are all filed through this one, and the City opens the `DR-`,
`CDP-`, and `ZPC-` child records itself under a single `PR-` project. Same
wizard, same two required attachments, zero questions. What is different is the
**file set: 21 attachment slots**, and several of them carry City-written
conditions that read as requirements. It is **not** a building permit.
[source: https://lagunabeachca-energovweb.tylerhost.net/apps/SelfService#/search, as of 2026-08-10]

**Apply:** https://lagunabeachca-energovweb.tylerhost.net/apps/SelfService#/plan/apply/116/0/0 (portal account required)
**Catalog name, verbatim:** "Commercial Planning Entitlements" — category PLANNING ENTITLEMENTS, module PLANS.
**Catalog description, verbatim:** "Use this to apply for Commercial Planning Entitlements. ATTENTION: THIS IS NOT A BUILDING PERMIT."
Internally the City runs this as **Zoning Plan Check - Commercial** — the `ZPC-` number.

Unless noted otherwise, everything below is the City's own configuration of this
application, read from the portal. The apply URL is login-gated in a browser;
the mirrored pull is the City source document (not included in this plugin).
[source: https://lagunabeachca-energovweb.tylerhost.net/apps/SelfService#/plan/apply/116/0/0, as of 2026-08-10]

## Before you start

- **If your project needs zoning review but no discretionary entitlement**, the City's instruction on this form is to submit a **Zone Clearance Application** instead — For residential projects that is the Zoning Plan Check work class of type 114. No commercial Zone Clearance work class has been confirmed online, so for a commercial project book an appointment or confirm with Planning. See `how-to-submit.md`.
- **⚠️ The City also routes planning submittals to an appointment.** The ADU form tells non-ADU planning projects to "schedule an appointment for submittal" instead of filing online, while 116 sits live and applyable in the catalog. Both readings were live on 2026-08-10; booking an appointment is never wrong and is the safe move. See `how-to-submit.md`, Channel C. [source: https://lagunabeachca-energovweb.tylerhost.net/apps/SelfService#/plan/apply/182/0/0, as of 2026-08-10]
- **Identify every entitlement you are requesting and get its form.** Applications and handouts: https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/government/departments/community-development/planning-zoning/applications-handouts
- **Oceanfront or oceanfront bluff site?** Read the Bluff Edge Determination condition under "Optional attachment slots" before you scope the submittal — the City's own wording makes it mandatory for a large class of projects.
- **Ordering a title report?** The City requires it dated **within one year of the application date**, so do not reuse an old one.
- **This is the first of two filings.** The building permit is a separate, later **Commercial Permit (184)** — see `submit-commercial-permit.md`.
- **A portal account.** See `how-to-submit.md` → "Before any of this".

[source: https://lagunabeachca-energovweb.tylerhost.net/apps/SelfService#/plan/apply/116/0/0, as of 2026-08-10]

## The wizard, step by step

**1 — Location.** "ATTENTION: This application is for Commercial Planning
Entitlements only." Click **+**, enter the address, select it from the results;
if not found, **Enter Manually**. Address is required. (The City's field prompt
still says "for the primary residence" — copy-paste from the residential form.)

**2 — Basic info.** Description is required: a "detailed scope of work", a
"comprehensive explanation of the work you plan to do" that **matches your
plans**. Square footage is required. **Valuation is optional.**

**3 — Contacts.** Click **+** to search by person or business name, then **Add**;
if not found, **Enter Manually**. The City lists: **Applicant** and **Owner**
(required), plus **Contractor**, **Engineer**, and **Architect** "if applicable".

**4 — More info.** No questions. The City's note, verbatim: "Note: Applications
and handouts can be on found the City's website" — linking the applications and
handouts page above. (The word order is the City's.)

**5 — Attachments.** 21 slots. See below.

**6 — Signature and submit.** An electronic signature is required.

[source: https://lagunabeachca-energovweb.tylerhost.net/apps/SelfService#/plan/apply/116/0/0, as of 2026-08-10]

## The questions you will face

**None.** Zero custom questions on this type. Everything the City learns comes
from your description and your uploads — which is why the attachment set below
is the real content of this application.
[source: https://lagunabeachca-energovweb.tylerhost.net/apps/SelfService#/plan/apply/116/0/0, as of 2026-08-10]

## Required attachments

Two, both PDF, one file each:

| Attachment | The City's description |
|---|---|
| Commercial Planning Application | Commercial Planning Application |
| Plans | Project Plans for Reviews |

The City's written instruction, condensed: the application must identify the
specific entitlement or approval requested; complete and submit the applicable
form for **each** requested entitlement; consult the Laguna Beach Municipal Code
to determine your project's requirements; and submit **all required application
materials — plans, technical studies, etc. — as identified in the applicable
application forms**. An incomplete application draws a correction request before
the plans are reviewed.
[source: https://lagunabeachca-energovweb.tylerhost.net/apps/SelfService#/plan/apply/116/0/0, as of 2026-08-10]

## Optional attachment slots worth knowing

19 optional slots. Six carry a City-written condition — filed as optional,
described as required whenever the condition applies. **The City's wording,
verbatim:**

| Slot | The City's condition, verbatim |
|---|---|
| Bluff Edge Determination | "Applications for new development (including accessory structures), major remodels, and additions on oceanfront or oceanfront bluff properties are required to submit a bluff edge determination." |
| Planning Commission Concept Review Application | "Required if a concept review hearing is recommended by Planning staff." |
| Revocable Encroachment Permit Application | "Required when a project proposes private encroachments into an otherwise unimproved, unused public right-of-way, and is subject to the requirements of Chapter 11.50 of the Laguna Beach Municipal Code." |
| Sidewalk Vendor Permit Application | "Applications for a roaming or stationary sidewalk vendor permit are required to submit a completed Sidewalk Vendor Permit Application. Sidewalk vending is regulated by Chapter 5.27 of the Laguna Beach Municipal Code." |
| Tentative Map | "If the project includes a new condominium or subdivision, submit a Tentative Map." |
| Title Report | "Must be dated within one year of the application date." |

The rest, alphabetically, with the City's description where it gave one:

- **Digital Photographs** — "Includes interior and exterior project site photos,"; PDF/JPEG/TIF; unlimited files
- **Hazardous Waste Form** — "Complete and upload the Hazardous Waste & Substances Form"
- **Letter of Intent** — "Describe the project/proposal and reason for the request."
- **Lot Line Adjustment**, **Road Extension**, **Short-Term Lodging Application**, **Sign Plans**, **Tenant Mix Table**, **Variance Justification** — no description given
- **Miscellaneous** — "Project Miscellaneous Files"; the only slot taking PNG; unlimited files
- **Reports** — "Project Reports Files"; unlimited files
- **Technical Studies** — unlimited files
- **Temporary Uses** — "Provide a full description of the temporary activities as well as information relevant to the mitigation of event impacts, such as parking and noise."

All are PDF-only except Digital Photographs and Miscellaneous, and all are
single-file except Digital Photographs, Miscellaneous, Reports, and Technical
Studies.
[source: https://lagunabeachca-energovweb.tylerhost.net/apps/SelfService#/plan/apply/116/0/0, as of 2026-08-10]

## Contacts and licences

- Required: **Applicant, Owner**. Optional: Architect, Contractor, Engineer.
- **Nothing on this type is licence-validated**, and no contractor licence is required to file it — this is a planning filing. The licence gate attaches to the building permit and to issuance, and every business working in the city needs a Laguna Beach business licence. [source: https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/government/departments/community-development/building-permits, as of 2026-07-08]

## After you submit

The City's confirmation message, verbatim:

> Thank you for your submittal. A planner will review your application to ensure
> all required information has been submitted, and contact you in 2 - 3 business
> days for payment.

Identical to the residential type: a **completeness screen by a planner** first,
payment invoiced after it passes, then substantive review. No in-person-issuance
warning — nothing is issued here. If the project goes to hearing, see the Design
Review Board and Planning Commission timing in `planning-applications.md`.
[source: https://lagunabeachca-energovweb.tylerhost.net/apps/SelfService#/plan/apply/116/0/0, as of 2026-08-10]
