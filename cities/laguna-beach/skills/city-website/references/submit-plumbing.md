# Submit: Plumbing Permit — EnerGov type 188

The stand-alone plumbing permit: **repipes, fixture work, sewer and water
service, gas piping, backflow devices, drains, and — the big one — water
heaters.**

**There is no water-heater type in the Laguna Beach catalog.** A water heater
files here. The City's own count sheet on this form carries three explicit
fields for it: **"Water Heater", "Water Heater New Vent", and "Water Heater,
Tankless"** — so a water-heater replacement is unambiguously a Plumbing Permit,
even though nothing in the catalog says the word.
[source: https://lagunabeachca-energovweb.tylerhost.net/apps/SelfService#/permit/apply/188/0/0, as of 2026-08-10]

If plumbing work is part of a larger remodel or addition, it rides along on the
**Residential Permit (183)** instead — see `submit-residential-permit.md`.

**Apply:** https://lagunabeachca-energovweb.tylerhost.net/apps/SelfService#/permit/apply/188/0/0 (portal account required)
**Catalog name, verbatim:** "Plumbing Permit" — category MECHANICAL, ELECTRICAL, AND PLUMBING, module PERMITS.
**Catalog description, verbatim:** "This application is for a Plumbing Building Permit. \*PLEASE BE ADVISED THAT THE FINAL PERMIT ISSUANCE IS CONDUCTED IN PERSON AT THE BUILDING COUNTER."

Unless noted otherwise, everything below is the City's own configuration of this
application, read from the portal. The apply URL is login-gated in a browser;
the mirrored pull is the City source document (not included in this plugin).
[source: https://lagunabeachca-energovweb.tylerhost.net/apps/SelfService#/permit/apply/188/0/0, as of 2026-08-10]

## Before you start

- **A signed permit application.** The only required attachment on this type is "Permit Application", described by the City simply as "Building Application". Sign it before you upload.
- **A portal account.** See `how-to-submit.md` → "Before any of this".
- ⚠️ *Our guidance, not a City statement:* **a heat-pump water heater that needs a new circuit realistically also needs an Electrical Permit (187).** The portal gives no triage for this and the card copy will not tell you. If the swap adds a dedicated circuit or a panel change, plan on a second application — see `submit-electrical.md`.
- ⚠️ *Our guidance, not a portal requirement:* the City publishes a **"Smoke Detector & Carbon Monoxide Self Certification"** form on the Building Applications & Forms page, and it is commonly triggered on dwelling permits — water heaters included. It is not one of this type's upload slots, so ask the Building Division whether your job needs it rather than assuming. [source: https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/government/departments/community-development/building-permits/applications-forms, as of 2026-08-10]
- *Also ours:* the City publishes **two** paper forms that could answer to "Building Application" — the *Building Application* and an *Electrical, Mechanical, and Plumbing Permit Application* — and the portal does not say which one this slot wants. If in doubt, call the Building Division, (949) 497-0715. [source: https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/government/departments/community-development/building-permits/applications-forms, as of 2026-08-10]

[source: https://lagunabeachca-energovweb.tylerhost.net/apps/SelfService#/permit/apply/188/0/0, as of 2026-08-10]

## The wizard, step by step

**1 — Location.** "ATTENTION: This application is for a Plumbing Permit only."
Click **+**, type the address of the primary residence into the search field,
select it once results populate; if it is not found, click **Enter Manually**.
Address is required.

**2 — Basic info.** Description is required, and the City asks for a "detailed
scope of work" — a "comprehensive explanation of the work you plan to do" that
**matches your plans**. Note the City's field warning, verbatim: "\*\* Square
Footage is for office use only. Do Not use." Square footage and valuation are
not required on this type.

**3 — Contacts.** Click **+** to search by person or business name, select the
result and click **Add**; if not found, **Enter Manually**. The City lists:
**Applicant** and **Owner** (required), plus **Contractor** "(If applicable)".

**4 — More info.** "Please enter the number of items each for Plumbing," plus a
pointer to the City's [applications and forms page](https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/government/departments/community-development/building-permits/applications-forms).

**5 — Attachments.** Signed application; project plans if applicable. See below.

**6 — Signature and submit.** An electronic signature is required.

[source: https://lagunabeachca-energovweb.tylerhost.net/apps/SelfService#/permit/apply/188/0/0, as of 2026-08-10]

## The questions you will face (38 fields, 1 group)

This is **not** a questionnaire about the project — it is the fixture count sheet
that drives the itemized plumbing fees. One group, all optional, all numeric, in
the City's order:

| Group | Fields | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Plumbing | 38 | kitchen sink, dishwasher, garbage disposal, shower/tub, bathroom sink, toilet/water closet/urinal, service sink, water piping per fixture, hose bibb, lawn sprinkler system, vent fixture, building sewer, sewer cap and connection, sewage disposal system, cesspool, vacuum breakers, water softener/treatment, **Water Heater**, **Water Heater New Vent**, **Water Heater, Tankless**, water service, backflow devices (2″ or smaller / over 2″), roof drain, drain piping, rainwater system per drain, floor sink, floor drain, drinking fountains, grease interceptor/trap, gas piping per outlet, temporary gas service, gas pressure regulator, gas service/meter release, clothes washer, misc not listed |

A straight tank swap is typically one entry under **Water Heater**; a tankless
conversion is **Water Heater, Tankless**; if the install requires a new vent, the
City meters that separately as **Water Heater New Vent**. Count only what you are
actually installing and leave the rest blank — the City bills plumbing work as
separate itemized fees, so these numbers become money.
[source: https://lagunabeachca-energovweb.tylerhost.net/apps/SelfService#/permit/apply/188/0/0, as of 2026-08-10]
[source: https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/government/departments/community-development/building-permits, as of 2026-07-08]

## Required attachments

One, PDF, one file:

| Attachment | The City's description |
|---|---|
| Permit Application | Building Application |

The City's written instruction on this step reads differently from the
configuration: it says "Applicants must submit the following: Project plans (if
applicable)" — but **Plans is an optional slot on this type and Permit
Application is the only required one.** The condensed rest of the instruction:
submit all required documents to avoid delays — an incomplete application draws
a correction request *before* the plans are reviewed at all.
[source: https://lagunabeachca-energovweb.tylerhost.net/apps/SelfService#/permit/apply/188/0/0, as of 2026-08-10]

## Optional attachment slots worth knowing

Eight more categories exist on this type, all PDF unless noted, unlimited files
unless noted:

- **Plans** — "Project Plans for Reviews", one file. The City's written instruction asks for project plans "if applicable"; this is where they go. A like-for-like water-heater swap often has none; a repipe or a sewer job does.
- **Specification Sheets** — "Project Specification Sheets". The water-heater or backflow-device cut sheet, which is the fastest way to settle a reviewer's question about the model.
- **Energy Calculations** and **Energy Calculation Addendum** — Title 24 compliance; expected when the scope triggers it.
- **Plan Check Responses** (.doc/.docx/.pdf/.jpg/.jpeg) — where you upload your answers to a correction letter on a resubmittal.
- **Revision Form** — for a change after the permit exists. The slot's description is now a link to the Building Division's Applications & Forms page, https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/government/departments/community-development/building-permits/applications-forms, which is where the portal points you for the form. [source: https://lagunabeachca-energovweb.tylerhost.net/apps/SelfService#/permit/apply/188/0/0, attachment setup read through the portal's public API, as of 2026-10-26]
- **Mechanical Equipment Guide** — the slot exists on this type too, but the "REQUIRED FOR ALL A/C PERMITS BY PLANNING" instruction is written only on the Mechanical Permit (186). See `submit-mechanical.md`.
- **Miscellaneous** (.pdf/.tif/.tiff/.jpeg/.jpg/.png) — everything else.

[source: https://lagunabeachca-energovweb.tylerhost.net/apps/SelfService#/permit/apply/188/0/0, as of 2026-08-10]

## Contacts and licences

- Required: **Applicant, Owner**. Optional: Agent, Architect, Contractor, Contractor – Prior, Designer, Engineer, Other, Owner - Prior, Owner/Builder, Subcontractor, Tenant.
- **Contractor and Subcontractor entries are licence-validated** by the portal.
- Permits issue only to an owner-builder or a **City-licensed** contractor, and every business working in the city needs a Laguna Beach business licence. [source: https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/government/departments/community-development/building-permits, as of 2026-07-08]

## After you submit

The City's confirmation message, verbatim:

> Thank you for your Plumbing Permit application. A permit technician will
> review your application to ensure all required information has been submitted,
> and contact you in 2 - 3 business days for payment.

So submitting does not start plan check — a technician screens it for
completeness first, then invoices. **And the permit itself is not issued
online.** This type carries the City's warning, verbatim: "\*PLEASE BE ADVISED
THAT THE FINAL PERMIT ISSUANCE IS CONDUCTED IN PERSON AT THE BUILDING COUNTER."
Plan on a counter trip to 505 Forest Ave at the end.
[source: https://lagunabeachca-energovweb.tylerhost.net/apps/SelfService#/permit/apply/188/0/0, as of 2026-08-10]
