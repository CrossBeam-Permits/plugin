# Submit: Residential Planning Entitlements — EnerGov type 114

**This is the umbrella type, and it is the single most important thing to know
about planning in Laguna Beach.** There is **no** application type for Design
Review, Coastal Development Permit, Variance, or Conditional Use Permit. All of
them are filed through this one application, and the City then opens the `DR-`,
`CDP-`, and `ZPC-` child records itself under a single `PR-` project. A resident
searching the catalog for "design review" or "coastal development permit" finds
nothing and concludes the City does not take it online. It does — it takes it
here. And it is **not** a building permit; the card says so in capitals.
[source: https://lagunabeachca-energovweb.tylerhost.net/apps/SelfService#/search, as of 2026-08-10]

**Apply:** https://lagunabeachca-energovweb.tylerhost.net/apps/SelfService#/plan/apply/114/0/0 (portal account required)
**Catalog name, verbatim:** "Residential Planning Entitlements" — category PLANNING ENTITLEMENTS, module PLANS.
**Catalog description, verbatim:** "Use this to apply for Residential Planning Entitlements. ATTENTION: THIS IS NOT A BUILDING PERMIT."
Internally the City runs this as **Zoning Plan Check - Residential** — that is where the `ZPC-` number comes from.

Unless noted otherwise, everything below is the City's own configuration of this
application, read from the portal. The apply URL is login-gated in a browser;
the mirrored pull is the City source document (not included in this plugin).
[source: https://lagunabeachca-energovweb.tylerhost.net/apps/SelfService#/plan/apply/114/0/0, as of 2026-08-10]

## ⚠️ The City gives two different answers for how to file this

Both were live on 2026-08-10. Present both; do not silently pick one.

- **Reading 1 — file it online.** Type 114 is a live, applyable online type, and public records show the `ZPC-` → `DR-` → `CDP-` child pattern it produces. [source: https://lagunabeachca-energovweb.tylerhost.net/apps/SelfService#/applicationassistant?sectionName=All&moduleId=3&categoryName=PLANNING%20ENTITLEMENTS&showTemplates=false, as of 2026-08-10]
- **Reading 2 — book an appointment.** The ADU form's first step reads, verbatim: "Projects that don't involve an ADU and/or a JADU should schedule an appointment for submittal at the following link: Book an Appointment." Taken at face value that routes every non-ADU planning submittal to the appointment booker. [source: https://lagunabeachca-energovweb.tylerhost.net/apps/SelfService#/plan/apply/182/0/0, as of 2026-08-10]

**Safe move:** file type 114 *and* book an appointment — booking one is never
wrong for a discretionary planning submittal. Confirm with Planning,
(949) 497-0713. See `how-to-submit.md`, Channel C.

## Before you start

- **If your project needs zoning review but no discretionary entitlement**, the City's instruction on this very form is to submit a **Zone Clearance Application** instead. Zone Clearance is filed online through this same type (114), as the Zoning Plan Check work class. See `how-to-submit.md`.
- **Work out which entitlements you are asking for, first.** The City's rule, condensed from its own attachment instructions: the application must identify the specific entitlement or approval requested; complete and submit the applicable form for **each** one; consult the Laguna Beach Municipal Code to determine what your project needs.
- **Get the forms** from the City's planning applications and handouts page: https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/government/departments/community-development/planning-zoning/applications-handouts. The direct link to each form (Design Review, Coastal Development Permit, Variance, Zone Clearance and the rest) is in the application links table of `planning-applications.md`; link the specific form, not just the page.
- **Budget for staking.** The City's administrative design review workflow includes a **staking deposit**, a **certification of staking date**, and later **stakes removed / staking deposit refunded** — real applicant obligations that appear nowhere in the application card copy. [source: https://lagunabeachca-energovweb.tylerhost.net/apps/SelfService#/search, as of 2026-08-10]
- **This is the first of two filings.** The building permit is a separate, later **Residential Permit (183)** — see `submit-residential-permit.md`. For an ADU, use type 182 instead — see `submit-adu.md`.
- **A portal account.** See `how-to-submit.md` → "Before any of this".

[source: https://lagunabeachca-energovweb.tylerhost.net/apps/SelfService#/plan/apply/114/0/0, as of 2026-08-10]

## The wizard, step by step

**1 — Location.** "ATTENTION: This application is for Residential Planning
Entitlements only." Click **+**, enter the address of the primary residence,
select it from the results; if not found, **Enter Manually**. Address is required.

**2 — Basic info.** Description is required: a "detailed scope of work", a
"comprehensive explanation of the work you plan to do" that **matches your
plans**. Square footage is required. **Valuation is optional** — the City says so
explicitly.

**3 — Contacts.** Click **+** to search by person or business name, then **Add**;
if not found, **Enter Manually**. The City lists: **Applicant** and **Owner**
(required), plus **Contractor**, **Engineer**, and **Architect** "if applicable".

**4 — More info.** No questions. The City's note here, verbatim: "Note: Please be
sure to re view and submit all required documents to avoid delays in processing
your application." (The typo is the City's.)

**5 — Attachments.** See below.

**6 — Signature and submit.** An electronic signature is required.

[source: https://lagunabeachca-energovweb.tylerhost.net/apps/SelfService#/plan/apply/114/0/0, as of 2026-08-10]

## The questions you will face

**None.** This type has zero custom questions — no questionnaire, no fixture
counts, no checkbox declarations. Everything the City learns about the project
comes from your description and your uploads, which is exactly why the
description and the entitlement-specific forms carry so much weight here.
[source: https://lagunabeachca-energovweb.tylerhost.net/apps/SelfService#/plan/apply/114/0/0, as of 2026-08-10]

## Required attachments

Two, both PDF, one file each:

| Attachment | The City's description |
|---|---|
| Application | *(the City gives no description on this slot)* |
| Plans | Project Plans for Reviews |

"Application" here means the application form(s) for the entitlements you are
requesting — the City's instruction is to submit "All application forms that
apply, based on the specific entitlements or approvals requested" plus "All
required application materials (plans, technical studies, etc.) as identified in
the applicable application forms". One PDF slot, one file: combine them.
Incomplete applications draw a correction request before the plans are reviewed.
[source: https://lagunabeachca-energovweb.tylerhost.net/apps/SelfService#/plan/apply/114/0/0, as of 2026-08-10]

## Optional attachment slots worth knowing

Three, and they are where everything that will not fit the two required slots
goes:

- **Miscellaneous** — "Project Miscellaneous Files"; PDF, TIF/TIFF, JPEG/JPG, PNG; unlimited files. The only slot on this type that accepts images.
- **Reports** — "Project Reports Files"; PDF, unlimited files.
- **Technical Studies** — PDF, unlimited files. The City's attachment instruction names "technical studies" as required material when an entitlement form calls for them, so this optional slot regularly carries required content.

[source: https://lagunabeachca-energovweb.tylerhost.net/apps/SelfService#/plan/apply/114/0/0, as of 2026-08-10]

## Contacts and licences

- Required: **Applicant, Owner**. Optional: Architect, Contractor, Engineer.
- **Nothing on this type is licence-validated**, and no contractor licence is required to file it — this is a planning filing. The licence gate attaches to the building permit and to issuance. [source: https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/government/departments/community-development/building-permits, as of 2026-07-08]

[source: https://lagunabeachca-energovweb.tylerhost.net/apps/SelfService#/plan/apply/114/0/0, as of 2026-08-10]

## After you submit

The City's confirmation message, verbatim:

> Thank you for your submittal. A planner will review your application to ensure
> all required information has been submitted, and contact you in 2 - 3 business
> days for payment.

So submitting starts a **completeness screen by a planner**, not the substantive
review; payment is invoiced after that screen passes. This type carries **no**
in-person-issuance warning — nothing is issued here.
[source: https://lagunabeachca-energovweb.tylerhost.net/apps/SelfService#/plan/apply/114/0/0, as of 2026-08-10]

What follows, if the project goes to design review: the **Design Review Board
meets the 2nd and 4th Thursday of the month at 5:00 p.m.** in Council Chambers,
there is a **two-week neighbour-notification period** before a hearing can be
set, and appeals to City Council are due **within ten business days** of the
Board's decision.
[source: https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/government/departments/community-development/building-permits/how-are-building-permits-processed, as of 2026-06-18]
⚠️ The City publishes a **14-day** appeal period on its design-review-process
page — see the appeal-period discrepancy flagged in `planning-applications.md`
and confirm the deadline with your assigned planner.
