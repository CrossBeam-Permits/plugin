# Submit: ADUs and JADUs — EnerGov type 182

**This is a PLAN, not a building permit — and it is the FIRST of two filings.**
The card says so in the City's own capitals: "ATTENTION: THIS IS NOT A BUILDING
PERMIT." A resident who files only this ends up with zoning clearance and no
authority to build. This is the single most likely place for a Laguna ADU
applicant to get stuck.

**Apply:** https://lagunabeachca-energovweb.tylerhost.net/apps/SelfService#/plan/apply/182/0/0 (portal account required)
**Catalog name, verbatim:** "Accessory Dwelling Units (ADUs) and Junior Accessory Dwelling Units (JADUs)" — category PLANNING ENTITLEMENTS, module PLANS.

Unless noted otherwise, everything below is the City's own configuration of this
application, read from the portal. The apply URL is login-gated in a browser;
the mirrored pull is the City source document (not included in this plugin).
[source: https://lagunabeachca-energovweb.tylerhost.net/apps/SelfService#/plan/apply/182/0/0, as of 2026-08-10]

## The two-record sequence — say this first, every time

1. **Plan 182** — this application. Planning review of the ADU/JADU against
   state law, the Laguna Beach Municipal Code, and the City's certified Local
   Coastal Program.
2. **Permit 183** — a *separate* **Residential Permit** filed afterward, which is
   what actually authorizes construction:
   https://lagunabeachca-energovweb.tylerhost.net/apps/SelfService#/permit/apply/183/0/0

The portal states the disclaimer but gives no "what next" guidance, so the
second filing is on the applicant to know about. See `submit-residential-permit.md`.
[source: https://lagunabeachca-energovweb.tylerhost.net/apps/SelfService#/plan/apply/182/0/0, as of 2026-08-10]

## Before you start

- **This form is for ADU/JADU projects only.** The City's first instruction, verbatim: "Projects that don't involve an ADU and/or a JADU should schedule an appointment for submittal at the following link: Book an Appointment" — https://lbwaitlist.cxmflow.com/OABS/Appointment/Index/2c052fc7-571f-4b76-9790-7e91f103c408
- **Read the City's two ADU documents before filing** — they are linked from inside the form itself:
  - ADU/JADU Frequently Asked Questions: https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/home/showpublisheddocument/20356/638651045595800000
  - ADU/JADU Application Review and Process Flowchart: https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/home/showpublisheddocument/20358/638651045605800000
- **Get the ADU or JADU application form** from the City's planning applications and handouts page: https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/government/departments/community-development/planning-zoning/applications-handouts
- **If your project needs zoning review but no discretionary entitlement**, the City's instruction is to submit a **Zone Clearance Application** instead — filed online as the Zoning Plan Check work class of Residential Planning Entitlements (114). See `how-to-submit.md`.
- **A portal account.** See `how-to-submit.md` → "Before any of this".

## The wizard, step by step

**1 — Location.** Click **+**, enter the address **of the primary residence**,
select it from the results; if not found, **Enter Manually**. Address is required.
The ADU-only warning and the appointment link sit on this step.

**2 — Basic info.** Description is required: "a comprehensive explanation of the
work you plan to do", and it must **match your plans**. Square footage is
required. **Valuation is optional** on this type — the City says so explicitly.

**3 — Contacts.** Click **+** to search by person or business name, then **Add**;
if not found, **Enter Manually**. The City lists: **Applicant** and **Owner**
(required), plus **Contractor**, **Engineer**, and **Architect** "if applicable".
No contractor licence is validated on this type — it is a planning filing.

**4 — More info.** No questions. The step carries the City's note that ADUs and
JADUs are regulated by state law, the LBMC, and the certified Local Coastal
Program, with the FAQ and flowchart links above.

**5 — Attachments.** See below. PDF only.

**6 — Signature and submit.** An electronic signature is required.

[source: https://lagunabeachca-energovweb.tylerhost.net/apps/SelfService#/plan/apply/182/0/0, as of 2026-08-10]

## Required attachments

Two, both PDF, one file each:

| Attachment | The City's description |
|---|---|
| ADU or JADU Application | ADU or JADU Application |
| Plans | Project Plans for Reviews |

The City's written instruction on this step is the general Planning Division
rule, condensed: the application must **identify the specific entitlement or
approval requested**; complete and submit the applicable form for each
entitlement requested; consult the Laguna Beach Municipal Code to work out what
your project needs; and submit **all required application materials — plans,
technical studies, etc. — as identified in the applicable application forms**.
Incomplete applications draw a correction request before the plans are reviewed.
[source: https://lagunabeachca-energovweb.tylerhost.net/apps/SelfService#/plan/apply/182/0/0, as of 2026-08-10]

## Optional attachment slots worth knowing

Four, all PDF, unlimited files — they mirror the City's plan-check divisions, so
what you upload here routes to the reviewer who needs it:

- **Planning** — Planning Division plan check files
- **Public Works Plans** — Public Works plan check files
- **Water Quality Plans** — water quality plan check files
- **Landscape Plans** — landscape plan check files

A detached ADU that adds impervious area or touches the right-of-way will
realistically need the Public Works and Water Quality sets.
[source: https://lagunabeachca-energovweb.tylerhost.net/apps/SelfService#/plan/apply/182/0/0, as of 2026-08-10]

## Contacts and licences

- Required: **Applicant, Owner**. Optional: Architect, Contractor, Engineer.
- Nothing on this type is licence-validated, and no contractor licence is required to file it — the licence gate attaches to the building permit (183) and to issuance. [source: https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/government/departments/community-development/building-permits, as of 2026-07-08]

## After you submit

The City's confirmation message, verbatim:

> Thank you for your ADU/JADU submittal. A planner will review your application
> to ensure all required information has been submitted, and contact you in
> 2 - 3 business days for payment.

So submitting starts a **completeness screen by a planner**, not the substantive
review — payment is invoiced after that screen passes. This type carries **no**
in-person-issuance warning, because nothing is issued here: the output is a
planning approval, and the building permit is the separate second filing.
[source: https://lagunabeachca-energovweb.tylerhost.net/apps/SelfService#/plan/apply/182/0/0, as of 2026-08-10]
