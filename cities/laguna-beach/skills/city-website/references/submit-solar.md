# Submit: SolarAPP+ Permit — EnerGov type 124

Residential rooftop photovoltaic that has **already cleared SolarAPP+**. This is
the only solar type in the Laguna Beach catalog, and it is the second half of a
two-step channel — see `how-to-submit.md`, Channel B.

**Apply:** https://lagunabeachca-energovweb.tylerhost.net/apps/SelfService#/permit/apply/124/0/0 (portal account required)
**Catalog name, verbatim:** "SolarAPP+ Permit (MUST USE SolarAPP+ FIRST BEFORE PROCEEDING)" — category SOLAR, module PERMITS.

Unless noted otherwise, everything below is the City's own configuration of this
application, read from the portal. The apply URL is login-gated in a browser;
the mirrored pull is the City source document (not included in this plugin).
[source: https://lagunabeachca-energovweb.tylerhost.net/apps/SelfService#/permit/apply/124/0/0, as of 2026-08-10]

## Before you start

- **A SolarAPP+ approval must already exist.** The first wizard step reads, verbatim: "DO NOT PROCEED WITHOUT SolarAPP+ APPROVAL FIRST." The type name repeats it in capitals. There is no path through this form without one.
- **The installer must be a registered SolarAPP+ contractor.** The City's own link for becoming one: https://help.solar-app.org/article/15-permit-application (now redirects to `help.gosolarapp.org`). Start at https://gosolarapp.org/.
- **You will need the SolarAPP+ approval number** — the form asks for it as a required field and the City wants it in the scope description too.
- **A portal account.** See `how-to-submit.md` → "Before any of this". Registration is Tyler Identity; use one email consistently.
- **Scope check — these are NOT covered by this permit and need separate permits:** generators; ground-mounted PV; service upgrades outside SolarAPP+ scope; main panel upgrades; new service meter installations; and a **reroof done as part of the solar install**. The City lists all six on the form.

[source: https://lagunabeachca-energovweb.tylerhost.net/apps/SelfService#/permit/apply/124/0/0, as of 2026-08-10]

## The wizard, step by step

**1 — Location.** Add the property. The City's instruction here is the SolarAPP+
gate quoted above, not an address tip. Address is required.

**2 — Basic info.** Description is required, and the City is specific about what
it wants in it, verbatim: "Description should include if this is a new or altered
system and the size, in kilowatts DD, of the solar array, the total kWh for EES
(if applicable) and the SolarAPP+ approval number." Square footage and valuation
are also required fields on this type.

**3 — Contacts.** Click **+** to search an existing contact by person or business
name, then **Add**; if not found, click **Enter Manually**. The City names three
required contacts: **Applicant, Contractor** ("re-enter applicant name if
applicant is the contractor"), and **Owner**. The Contractor entry is
licence-validated by the portal.

**4 — More info.** "Must acknowledge all the statements below." This is the
acknowledgement wall — see the next section.

**5 — Attachments.** "All documents to be in PDF format."

**6 — Signature and submit.** An electronic signature is required.

[source: https://lagunabeachca-energovweb.tylerhost.net/apps/SelfService#/permit/apply/124/0/0, as of 2026-08-10]

## The questions you will face (31 fields, 4 groups)

| Group | What it asks |
|---|---|
| Solar Info | **Solar App Approval ID** — required. The portal's own format hint is `SA12Ab34cD-1a-B2-3cD4-Z`. |
| ACKNOWLEDGEMENT | Seven checkboxes, each acknowledging a statement: Solar Local Codes, Solar Safe Means of Access, Solar Class A Rating, Solar Minimum Access, Solar Overcurrent Protection, Solar Conduit, Solar Approval Documentation, and Separate Permits Required. |
| LICENSED CONTRACTOR'S DECLARATION | **License Class** and **License Number**, both required, under an affirmation. |
| WORKERS' COMPENSATION DECLARATION | **Carrier**, **Policy Number**, and **Expiration Date**, all required, plus certificate-of-consent and performance-of-work acknowledgements. |

The acknowledgements are where a resident should slow down: "Separate Permits
Required" is the one that catches solar jobs bundled with a reroof or a panel
upgrade. [source: https://lagunabeachca-energovweb.tylerhost.net/apps/SelfService#/permit/apply/124/0/0, as of 2026-08-10]

## Required attachments

Both are required, PDF only, one file each:

| Attachment | The City's description |
|---|---|
| SolarAPP Approval Document | SolarAPP+ Approval Documents |
| SolarAPP Specification Sheets | SolarAPP+ Specification Sheets |

The written instruction on the attachments step says it slightly differently —
"Approved SolarAPP+ Inspection Checklist" and "SolarAPP+ Specification Sheets" —
so bring the approved SolarAPP+ output packet in full and you satisfy both
readings. [source: https://lagunabeachca-energovweb.tylerhost.net/apps/SelfService#/permit/apply/124/0/0, as of 2026-08-10]

## Optional attachment slots worth knowing

Eight more categories exist on this type, all PDF unless noted, unlimited files:
Equipment or Component Data Sheets; Miscellaneous (also accepts .tif/.tiff/.jpeg/.jpg/.png);
Plan Check Responses (.doc/.docx/.pdf/.jpg/.jpeg); Revision Form (its description
links the Building Division's Applications & Forms page, https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/government/departments/community-development/building-permits/applications-forms, as of 2026-10-26); Single Line
Diagram; Site Plan or Roof Plan; Structural Calculations; Utility Company
Approval Documents.

Two are worth volunteering even though the City does not demand them: the
**Single Line Diagram** and the **Site Plan or Roof Plan** — they are the first
things a reviewer looks for if anything about the SolarAPP+ packet is
ambiguous. **Utility Company Approval Documents** matters on interconnection
questions. [source: https://lagunabeachca-energovweb.tylerhost.net/apps/SelfService#/permit/apply/124/0/0, as of 2026-08-10]

## Contacts and licences

- Required: **Applicant, Contractor, Owner**. Optional slots exist for Agent, Architect, Designer, Engineer, Owner/Builder, Special Inspector, Subcontractor, Tenant and others.
- **Contractor and Subcontractor entries are licence-validated** by the portal.
- Separately from the portal: every contractor working in the city must hold a **Laguna Beach business licence**, and permits issue only to an owner-builder or a City-licensed contractor. [source: https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/government/departments/community-development/building-permits, as of 2026-07-08]

[source: https://lagunabeachca-energovweb.tylerhost.net/apps/SelfService#/permit/apply/124/0/0, as of 2026-08-10]

## After you submit

The City's confirmation message on this type is, verbatim:

> Your permit has been issued!

This is the only type in the catalog that issues on submit. It does **not** carry
the "FINAL PERMIT ISSUANCE IS CONDUCTED IN PERSON AT THE BUILDING COUNTER"
warning that 11 of the 14 permit types carry — consistent with an instant-issuance
path, and consistent with LBMC Ch. 14.84's expedited small-residential-rooftop-solar
review. Inspection is still required: requests go by email to
lbinspectors@lagunabeachcity.net before 4:00 p.m. for next-business-day service.
