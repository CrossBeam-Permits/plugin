# Submit: View Preservation — EnerGov type 301

**This is the "my neighbour's vegetation is taking my view" application**, and it
is almost entirely an acknowledgement form. There is nothing to upload and no
plan to draw: 17 fields, nearly all of them checkboxes where you confirm you
understand how the City's view programme works before a planner picks it up. It
cites **LBMC 12.16.040**, and one of the statements you acknowledge is that view
preservation **pertains to vegetation**. It is not a building permit — the card
says so in capitals.

⚠️ **The code behind this form changed.** Effective **2026-08-20**, LBMC
12.16.040(b) and 12.16.100 are amended. The amendments spell out what the City
does after you file — staff photograph the view, the record of views goes in your
property file **and** in your real property report, and vegetation owners within
500 feet get written notice — and they confirm that view decisions **run with the
land**. The portal form itself is unchanged. See "What the City does after you
file" and "The decision binds whoever owns the property next" below.

## Before you start

- **You must be the Owner.** The required contact on this type is **Owner**, not Applicant — the one type in the catalog configured that way. A tenant or a neighbour's agent cannot satisfy it alone.
- **This is the vegetation route, and it is not the only one.** The City also runs Restoration of View, a **Hedge Height Claim**, and design-review-approved landscape plans; tree branches merely extending onto your property are a **civil matter between neighbours**, and trees on the Heritage Tree List are **exempt from view restoration**. Read `planning-applications.md` → "Views, trees, and hedges" and `code-enforcement.md` before assuming this is your form. [source: https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/government/departments/community-development/views-and-trees, as of 2026-07-15]
- **Know what you are agreeing to.** Among the statements you acknowledge: a **contract with public noticing**, that the view is **documented by City staff**, and that the record of view is a **one-time** record. Effective **2026-08-20**, LBMC 12.16.040(b) states the staff documentation and the neighbour noticing directly in code — read "What the City does after you file" below before you check the boxes.
- **This becomes a disclosure that follows the property.** Effective **2026-08-20**, the record of views is noted in any real property report required by LBMC Chapter 14.76, and a view decision runs with the land and binds later owners (LBMC 12.16.100, as amended). File this only if you want a permanent, disclosed record.
- **Nothing to upload.** There are no attachment slots at all.
- **A portal account.** See `how-to-submit.md` → "Before any of this".

[source: https://lagunabeachca-energovweb.tylerhost.net/apps/SelfService#/plan/apply/301/0/0, as of 2026-08-10]

## The questions you will face (17 fields, 2 groups)

In the City's own order.

**Group 1 — RECORD OF VIEWS.** Two informational items, no input: *View
Preservation LBCM 12.16.040* (the City's spelling of LBMC) and *View Preservation
Process*. This is where the City states the rules you are about to acknowledge.

**Group 2 — AFFIRMATION OF UNDERSTANDING.** An opening *View Preservation
Affirmation* statement, then **seven statement-and-checkbox pairs**. Each pair is
a block of City text followed by "Check Box to Acknowledge Statement Below:". The
seven, in order:

1. **Record of View**
2. **View Impairment**
3. **One Time Record of View**
4. **View Documented by City Staff**
5. **View Preservation Pertains to Vegetation**
6. **Contract With Public Noticing**
7. **View Preservation Information Acknowledgement**

⚠️ **A contradiction to know about:** the City's instruction says to check **all**
the boxes, but **none of the seven checkboxes is configured as required** — the
form will submit with them blank. Check all seven anyway; an application that
does not carry the acknowledgements the City asked for is an invitation to a
correction request.

## After you submit

The City's confirmation message, verbatim:

> Thank you for your submittal. A planner will review your application to ensure
> all required information has been submitted and contact you in approximately
> two weeks.

**Approximately two weeks — not the 2–3 business days every other planning type
promises.** Set that expectation up front. Submitting starts a completeness
screen by a planner, not the substantive review, and there is no
in-person-issuance warning: nothing is issued here. Where a claim proceeds, the
**View Restoration Committee** — five residents appointed by City Council — holds
the public hearings and may issue a view restoration order with a vegetation
maintenance schedule. [source: https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/government/departments/community-development/boards-committees-commissions, as of 2024-11-19]
[source: https://lagunabeachca-energovweb.tylerhost.net/apps/SelfService#/plan/apply/301/0/0, as of 2026-08-10]

## What the City does after you file — LBMC 12.16.040(b), amended effective 2026-08-20

The City Council amended LBMC 12.16.040(b) (Record of Views) in the 2026
efficiency ordinance. **The amendment takes effect 2026-08-20.** It writes the
City's own procedure into the code. Four things happen:

1. **Staff visit the site and photograph the view.** City staff conduct a site visit and photographically document the significantly unimpaired view from the viewing locations or areas **you** select. That matches the form's "View Documented by City Staff" acknowledgement. You do not upload photographs — there is nowhere to.
2. **A record of views is prepared and filed against your address.** The City maintains it in the applicable property address files.
3. **It is noted in your real property report.** The record of views is noted in any real property report required by **LBMC Chapter 14.76**. Anyone who buys the property and pulls a report sees it. Real Property Disclosure requests run through the same permit portal — see `planning-applications.md` → "Real property reports / disclosures".
4. **Vegetation owners within 500 feet get written notice.** Staff send written notice to affected vegetation property owners **within five hundred feet**, informing them of the record of views and their associated maintenance responsibilities. Expect the neighbours whose vegetation is at issue to hear from the City in writing. The code does not label this the "contract with public noticing" the form asks you to acknowledge, but it is the noticing the code requires.

[source: LBMC 12.16.040(b) as amended, Laguna Beach efficiency ordinance amending LBMC Chapters 7.25, 12.08, 12.16 and 17.50 — introduced 2026-06-23, adopted 2026-07-21, effective 2026-08-20 line 123. eCode360 had not republished Title 12 as of 2026-08-14.]

## The decision binds whoever owns the property next — LBMC 12.16.100, amended effective 2026-08-20

LBMC 12.16.100 (View decisions binding on future owners — Disclosure
obligations) is amended in the same ordinance, **effective 2026-08-20**:

- Decisions made by City staff and the View Restoration Committee on view restoration and view preservation **run with the land**. They are binding on the claimant, on the vegetation owner, and on their **heirs, successors and assigns**.
- To the extent permitted, such decisions **are recorded in the official records of the County of Orange**.
- Such decisions **are set forth in any real property report required by Chapter 14.76**, and **should be disclosed by each owner to prospective purchasers** of their property.

What that means in practice. A view decision does not lapse when a neighbour
sells. If a maintenance obligation lands on the vegetation owner, it follows that
property to the next owner, and that owner is expected to tell a buyer about it.
The same is true in reverse: if you own the vegetation and a decision goes
against you, selling does not clear it, and you carry a disclosure obligation.

[source: LBMC 12.16.100 as amended, same ordinance, effective 2026-08-20 line 143.]
