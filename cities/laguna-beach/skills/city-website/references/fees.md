# Laguna Beach — fee topics (Master Fee Schedule)

**Modeled fees never get a dollar amount from this file.** Permits, plan check,
planning applications, sewer connection, and the other catalog items below are
computed from a CrossBeam reference that is not included in this plugin and served
through the fee estimate. Call that tool for any figure. Do not quote a permit or
planning fee from this page.

Unmodeled topics are grouped at the bottom. the fee estimate does not return
them. Only **City Attorney hourly rates** and **annual sewer service charges**
may include figures here; every other unmodeled topic is named without an amount.

**Source document:** the City of Laguna Beach Master Fee Schedule, document id
**26193**, ticks `639184240951470000`, whose own footer reads "Update: May 12,
2026". The schedule takes effect July 1, 2026. Community Development and Fire
Inspection fees take effect July 12, 2026.
https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/home/showpublisheddocument/26193/639184240951470000

The City updates the Master Fee Schedule roughly annually. Tell the resident the
as-of date on any answer, and tell them to confirm the current amount with the
City before paying.

## What the fee estimate covers

Building permit, plan check, grading, trade, inspection, certificate,
administration, sewer connection, planning application, and the modeled impact /
in-lieu fees below. For any amount in this catalog, call the fee estimate.

### Building permit fee by valuation

Building permit fees are based on the valuation (market value of labor and
materials) of the work. Mechanical, electrical, and plumbing permits are billed
on separate itemized fees. **Fees must be paid at first plan check submittal and
at permit issuance.**
[source: https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/government/departments/community-development/building-permits, as of 2026-07-08]

The valuation table in `fee-schedule.yaml` computes the building permit fee the
way EnerGov does. Call the fee estimate for it; do not work a formula by hand or
state a valuation-based building permit fee from this page.

### Flat-rate building permits

Re-roofs, residential and commercial solar, the four demolition categories,
extensions, swimming pool and spa, the combination trade permit, permit issuance,
and the technology surcharge are published as flat rates or as a percentage of
another fee.

### Grading permit by volume

The grading permit fee is a stepped bracket on cubic yards. Grading plan review
is a published percentage of that permit fee. Both may carry the technology
surcharge. Ask the resident for cubic yards, then call the fee estimate.

Curb and gutter, paving, and erosion control are charged the same as building
permit fees. Drainage permits for alteration to drainage and shoring-only permits
are separate flat fees.

### Plan review and plan check

Plan review for buildings or structures is a percentage of the building permit
fee. Mechanical, electrical, and plumbing plan review is a percentage of the MEP
permit fee. Pools and spas, solar, demolitions, fire sprinkler and alarm work, and
re-roofs each have a flat plan review fee. Plan check review is charged hourly.
Expedited plan review is a multiple of the regular plan review fee with a
minimum charge.

### Inspections

Inspections are charged per square foot with a minimum. Off-hours inspections,
other inspections, and re-inspections after uncompleted corrections are charged
hourly.

**Unpermitted work is charged double permit and plan check fees.**
[source: https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/home/showpublisheddocument/26193/639184240951470000, as of 2026-05-12]

### Certificates and reports

Temporary Certificate of Occupancy, Renewable Temporary Certificate of Occupancy,
Residential Building Report, and the State strong motion instrumentation fee are
each published in the Master Fee Schedule.

### Trade-permit unit fees

Electrical, mechanical, and plumbing permits are billed as itemized unit fees —
per outlet, per fixture, per appliance, per rating band for power apparatus, per
service size, and so on. There are roughly 80 of these unit rows. Call
the fee estimate with the trade scope and quantities.

### Administration

Accessibility hardship board ratification, Appeals Board hearing, authorization to
duplicate record drawings, flood zone determination, modification to UBC or
alternate materials and methods, special inspector licences, and miscellaneous
administrative review are each published in the Master Fee Schedule.

### Planning and zoning fees

Every named planning application — Administrative Use Permit, appeals, Coastal
Development Permits, Conditional Use Permits, Design Review at each level, historic
applications, subdivisions, sign permits, tree removal, variances, view
preservation, wireless facility permits, and Zoning Plan Check — is published with
a fee. Call the fee estimate with the application name, for example
the fee estimate. That call needs no
construction project.

Some planning applications are billed at actual cost against a minimum deposit
rather than at a flat fee: condominium conversion, final map, General Plan
amendment, Local Coastal Program amendment, Specific Plan amendment, zone change
or ordinance amendment, tentative map amendment, tentative parcel or tract map,
plan revision, peer review, road extension, street easement abandonment or
vacation, view claim arborist services, Major Design Review, and CEQA documents
(Initial Study, Negative Declaration, Mitigated Negative Declaration, and
Environmental Impact Report). Call the fee estimate for the current deposit amount,
and tell the resident the deposit is a minimum, not the final cost.

### Impact and in-lieu fees (modeled)

Art in Public Places, the library impact fee, General Plan maintenance,
landscape plan review, CARITS, the Diamond/Crestview Specific Plan fees, and
Central Drainage Area fees for subdivisions are modeled in `fee-schedule.yaml`.
Art in Public Places and the library impact fee are set by the Municipal Code,
not the Master Fee Schedule; the rest are in the Master Fee Schedule.

### Sewer connection fees

Sewer connection fees are published per dwelling unit for single family,
condominiums, and apartments; per 100 square feet for ADUs and
additions/remodels; per EDU for commercial, with a light, medium, or heavy
strength multiplier on estimated gallons per day; and as a base plus an area rate
for hotels, restaurants, industrial buildings, and all other buildings. Car wash
connections have their own rate, halved when the wash recycles 50% or more of the
water it uses.

**The sewer connection fee applies to all new residential, commercial, and
industrial additions of 500 square feet and over.**
[source: https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/home/showpublisheddocument/26193/639184240951470000, as of 2026-05-12]

## Unmodeled — do not call the fee estimate for these

### Personnel rates

The Master Fee Schedule publishes an hourly rate table for the City Planner
classification (charity, regular, and overtime). the fee estimate does not return
staff hourly rates. Do not quote them from memory. The current table is in
document 26193 (link above).

### Housing in-lieu, habitat mitigation, in-lieu parking, plan retrieval

The housing in-lieu fees, biological habitat impact mitigation, in-lieu parking
certification, and plan retrieval do not appear in Master Fee Schedule document
26193, and the fee estimate does not model them. Do not quote an amount. Send the
resident to the Planning Division — see `../../city-reference/references/contacts-hours.md`.

### Park and recreation dedication in-lieu

A residential subdivision must dedicate park land, or pay a fee in lieu of that
dedication. The required dedication is a percentage of park land that rises with
the density of the subdivision. The in-lieu fee equals the value of the land that
would otherwise be dedicated. The City sets that land value from an annually
calculated average sales price per acre of vacant residentially zoned property.

The Master Fee Schedule (document 26193) does not publish the density percentage
schedule or the current land value per acre. Send the resident to the Planning
Division for both — see `../../city-reference/references/contacts-hours.md`. Do not quote a percentage or a land
value from an older document.
[source: https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/home/showpublisheddocument/26193/639184240951470000, as of 2026-05-12 — amount not published in this document]

### Investigation fees

Code enforcement, building, electrical, plumbing, mechanical, and grading
investigations, requested site visits, and any investigation not tied to an active
permit are charged at the applicable hourly rate for the City staff member. The
Master Fee Schedule does not publish a separate Community Development
investigation fee row, so do not state an amount — confirm the applicable hourly
rate with the City.

This is not the same as the double permit and plan check fee for unpermitted work
(see Inspections above), and not the same as the Fire Department's investigation
charge for work started without a fire permit.
[source: https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/home/showpublisheddocument/26193/639184240951470000, as of 2026-05-12]

### City Attorney review charges

Telecom projects, Temporary Use Permits, Conditional Use Permits, and applications
that require City Attorney review for CEQA, Coastal Act, housing, or other matters
are billed at the City Attorney hourly rates. The City passes through all eligible
outside legal service costs. A deposit may be required case by case.

| Personnel | Charity rate per hour | Regular rate per hour |
|---|---|---|
| City Attorney | $213 | $245 |
| Assistant City Attorney | $144 | $166 |
| Paralegal | $64 | $74 |

[source: https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/home/showpublisheddocument/26193/639184240951470000, as of 2026-05-12]

### Annual sewer service charges

This is an ongoing utility charge billed by the City, not a permit fee.
the fee estimate does not return it.

Residential, annual charge per unit:

| Unit type | Annual charge |
|---|---|
| Ultra-low water user single family | $837.46 |
| Single family | $1,022.94 |
| Condominiums | $1,006.98 |
| Apartments (2 units) | $903.50 |
| Apartments (3+ units) | $829.03 |

Commercial accounts, charge per 100 cubic feet: light $5.70 · medium $8.74 ·
heavy $12.61. Each commercial strength class carries a minimum annual charge per
parcel of $1,005.01.
[source: https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/home/showpublisheddocument/26193/639184240951470000, as of 2026-05-12]
