# Laguna Beach — Building Permits (from the City website)

Process, counter rules, inspections, and what does/doesn't need a permit, as the
City publishes it. For code requirements themselves, use the permit-type skills
under the permit skills in this plugin — this file is about *how the City runs the
counter*, not what the code says.

## Do you need a permit?

- Per the California Building Code, residents and businesses must obtain permits before constructing, enlarging, altering, repairing, moving, demolishing, or changing a building, or an electrical, mechanical, or plumbing system. [source: https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/government/departments/community-development/building-permits/do-you-need-a-permit, as of 2026-06-18]
- A building permit is required whenever you construct, move, alter, convert, improve, repair, or demolish a structure anywhere in the City — interior work as well as exterior. Permitted work must pass City inspections before the project is complete. [source: https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/government/departments/community-development/building-permits, as of 2026-07-08]
- The City's published list of common projects that **do NOT** require a permit: interior painting; wallpapering; flooring; window coverings; portable listed cord-and-plug electrical and mechanical appliances. The City adds: "If your project is not on this list, it probably requires a permit." [source: https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/government/departments/community-development/building-permits/do-you-need-a-permit, as of 2026-06-18]
- All construction projects must comply with City construction regulations whether or not a permit is issued. Handout: https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/home/showpublisheddocument/218/637360372607900000 [source: https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/government/departments/community-development/building-permits/do-you-need-a-permit, as of 2026-06-18]

## Which code edition applies

- **All building permit applications submitted on or after January 1, 2026 are subject to the 2025 Laguna Beach Building Standards Codes.** [source: https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/government/departments/community-development/building-permits, as of 2026-07-08]

## How to apply

- Building permits and zoning plan checks can be submitted online through the Public Permit Portal: register an account, then submit plans and the completed application. Portal: https://lagunabeachca-energovweb.tylerhost.net/apps/SelfService — registration guide: https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/home/showpublisheddocument/20625/638678768735230000 [source: https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/government/departments/community-development/building-permits, as of 2026-07-08]
- To get a permit you must hire a contractor licensed in the City of Laguna Beach, or provide proof of ownership to take on the liability yourself; then submit the required paperwork and pay a fee. [source: https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/government/departments/community-development/building-permits, as of 2026-07-08]
- Homeowner-drawn plans are acceptable for small projects as long as drawings are to scale and clear enough for staff to fully understand the work. [source: https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/government/departments/community-development/building-permits, as of 2026-07-08]
- The plan cover sheet must include: address of the work; property owner's name; name of the person who prepared the plans; scope-of-work description; remodel square footage. [source: https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/government/departments/community-development/building-permits, as of 2026-07-08]
- All businesses, contractors, and professionals doing work in the City must obtain a City business license. [source: https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/government/departments/community-development/building-permits, as of 2026-07-08]

## The full plan-check process (as the City describes it)

1. **Intake.** Front counter staff provide a Development Review Application. Once completed, plans are submitted with the zoning submittal fees and entered for Zoning Division review. [source: https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/government/departments/community-development/building-permits/how-are-building-permits-processed, as of 2026-06-18]
2. **Zoning plan check.** Planning staff check setbacks from property lines, allowed height, required parking, open space, lot coverage, and similar siting matters, and identify any variances that might be needed. Correction-and-resubmittal cycles may repeat two or three times or more. [source: https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/government/departments/community-development/building-permits/how-are-building-permits-processed, as of 2026-06-18]
3. **CEQA screening.** Staff classify the project (categorically exempt, negative declaration, or EIR) and identify special studies (geotechnical, hydrology, grading plan) and required public improvements. [source: https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/government/departments/community-development/building-permits/how-are-building-permits-processed, as of 2026-06-18]
4. **Design Review.** Staff set a hearing date with the applicant; a two-week neighbor-notification period must be allowed. See planning-applications.md for the full nine-step design review process. [source: https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/government/departments/community-development/building-permits/how-are-building-permits-processed, as of 2026-06-18]
5. **State agency review.** Work on Pacific Coast Highway or Laguna Canyon Road may also require a Caltrans permit. Final working drawings can be submitted with the plan-check fee while the State agency reviews. [source: https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/government/departments/community-development/building-permits/how-are-building-permits-processed, as of 2026-06-18]
6. **Building plan check.** Submit **three (3) sets of 24"x36" construction drawings** (architectural, structural, mechanical, electrical, plumbing, energy, and green building sheets or notes) plus **two (2) sets** of geotechnical soils reports, structural calculations, and energy calculations. Each division stamps and signs the approved plans at final review. [source: https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/government/departments/community-development/building-permits/how-are-building-permits-processed, as of 2026-06-18]

## Over-the-counter plan review (BUILDING division)

⚠️ Don't confuse this with **Planning** over-the-counter review, which is a
separate process with its own eligible-project list (re-roofs, fences, AC units,
window replacements) and needs no appointment — see faqs.md. This section is the
Building division's plan review.

Available by appointment in the morning and walk-in in the afternoon (hours in
contacts-hours.md). Limited to projects small enough in scope to review in about
30 minutes.

**Qualifying project types** [source: https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/government/departments/community-development/building-permits/appointment-for-counter-plan-review, as of 2026-06-18]:

1. Residential kitchen and bathroom remodels in the same configuration with minor structural modifications.
2. Residential deck, balcony, and patio-cover repairs with minor structural modifications (no extension or replacement).
3. Window and door replacement within the same openings, or with minor structural modifications.
4. Construction revisions to approved plans and structural calculations — submit two new sets of plans and calculations incorporating the revisions, clouded with a delta number.
5. Exterior siding replacement.
6. Residential electrical meter upgrade or change-out in the same location, clearance, and access up to 200 amps (utility company approval required).
7. Re-roof with the same material or lighter.
8. Residential HVAC change-outs in the same location, clearances, access, and type.
9. Minor plumbing, electrical, or mechanical repairs or replacements.
10. Wood or metal fences, with a completed Survey Waiver form if the fence is within 3 feet of the property line (certification required if no Survey Waiver was submitted at permit issuance).
11. Commercial signs.

**What to bring:** a completed, accurate permit application with the correct site
address, and **two (2) sets of 24"x36" plans** showing an accurate site plan, the
complete scope of work, and a floor plan locating existing and proposed work;
plus two sets of stamped and signed engineered plans and calculations if
applicable, and two sets of Title 24 energy calculations if applicable.
[source: https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/government/departments/community-development/building-permits/appointment-for-counter-plan-review, as of 2026-06-18]

**Does NOT qualify** if the project requires: other City department review and
approval; approvals by outside agencies (County Health, Caltrans, etc.);
geotechnical report review; extensive structural design and modifications; a
change in type of construction; or a change of use to a more restrictive
occupancy.
[source: https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/government/departments/community-development/building-permits/appointment-for-counter-plan-review, as of 2026-06-18]

**Other counter rules:** the review may end in permit issuance or in a list of
required corrections. Applicants are advised to get Zoning/Planning approval and
Design Review (if applicable) *before* the appointment. If a design professional
prepared the plans, that professional must attend the appointment.
[source: https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/government/departments/community-development/building-permits/appointment-for-counter-plan-review, as of 2026-06-18]

## Inspections

- **Request inspections by email to lbinspectors@lagunabeachcity.net**, including: project address; permit number(s) with the type of inspection listed separately for each; type(s) of inspection; request date; building inspector name; requestor's name and mobile number; optionally AM or PM. [source: https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/government/departments/community-development/building-permits, as of 2026-07-08]
- If you have more than one active permit, request the inspection types under each separate permit number — don't put them all on one permit. [source: https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/government/departments/community-development/building-permits, as of 2026-07-08]
- **Requests must be received before 4:00 p.m.** to get an inspection the following business day. **There are no same-day inspections**, and inspections are not scheduled on the alternating Fridays City Hall is closed. [source: https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/government/departments/community-development/building-permits, as of 2026-07-08]
- To confirm the date and time, check Today's Daily Inspections or call the Building Division at (949) 497-0715 after 8:30 a.m. on the morning of the inspection. **You will not receive a confirmation phone call.** [source: https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/government/departments/community-development/building-permits, as of 2026-07-08]
- **Permits expire if inspections are not scheduled and passed every 180 days.** Every permit requires a FINAL passed inspection to complete. [source: https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/government/departments/community-development/building-permits, as of 2026-07-08]
- For new structures and major remodels/additions, a **Project List of Subcontractors is required 30 days before final inspection sign-offs**. [source: https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/government/departments/community-development/building-permits, as of 2026-07-08]

## Work-hour limits (enforced by Code Enforcement)

- **Construction: 7:30 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. weekdays only. No construction on weekends or federal holidays.** [source: https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/government/departments/community-development/building-permits, as of 2026-07-08]
- **Grading hours: 8:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m. Hauling hours: 9:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m.** [source: https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/government/departments/community-development/building-permits, as of 2026-07-08]
- Code Enforcement's own page states construction is allowed weekdays 7:30 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. and not on weekends and legal holidays. [source: https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/government/departments/community-development/code-enforcement/what-do-we-enforce, as of 2026-06-18]

## Records and status

- Permit, plan, inspection, and project status are searchable on the online permitting portal via "Search Public Records": https://lagunabeachca-energovweb.tylerhost.net/apps/SelfService [source: https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/government/departments/community-development/building-permits, as of 2026-07-08]
- To confirm a property has active permits, or to report unpermitted construction, contact the Building Division at (949) 497-0715. [source: https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/government/departments/community-development/code-enforcement/frequently-asked-questions, as of 2026-06-18]
