[source: https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/government/departments/community-development/code-enforcement/what-do-we-enforce, as of 2026-06-18]

- Trashcans and lighting.
- Overgrown vegetation within the public right-of-way.
- Removal of trees without a permit.
- **Leaf blower enforcement — the use of leaf blowers within the City is prohibited.** Handout: https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/home/showpublisheddocument/8840/637513492123130000
- Construction-hour violations. Construction is allowed weekdays 7:30 a.m. to 6:00 p.m.; not allowed on weekends and legal holidays.
- Real property maintenance — https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/home/showpublisheddocument/8842/637513493472130000 · Code Enforcement Brochure — https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/home/showpublisheddocument/12308/637952189186430000 · Sign Brochure — https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/home/showpublisheddocument/12310/637952216925200000
- Substandard housing conditions.

## Leaf blowers — the actual rule

- The use of electrical or gasoline powered blowers, such as those commonly used by gardeners and others for cleaning lawns, yards, driveways, gutters, and other property, **is prohibited at any time within the city limits**. [source: https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/government/departments/community-development/code-enforcement/frequently-asked-questions, as of 2026-06-18]

Exceptions [source: https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/government/departments/community-development/code-enforcement/frequently-asked-questions, as of 2026-06-18]:

1. Emergency property maintenance authorized by the building official.
2. Maintenance, repair, or improvement of any public work or facility by public employees, public-works contractors, or persons acting on behalf of a public agency. This exception does **not** apply to the City of Laguna Beach or its employees, contractors, or agents unless: the city manager or department director determines the work is immediately necessary to maintain public service; the work cannot feasibly be done during normal business hours; or the City Council approved project specifications, contract provisions, or an environmental document specifically authorizing maintenance during otherwise-prohibited hours.
3. Any maintenance that complies with the noise limits in LBMC § 7.25.040.
4. Maintenance activities for certain public-benefit nonprofit art organizations — specifically the Sawdust Festival, Art-A-Fair, and the Laguna Art Museum — are permitted 8:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m. Monday through Friday and 8:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. Saturday and Sunday.

## Overgrown vegetation

- Vegetation growing into the right-of-way or blocking directional/street signage can be addressed by contacting the Code Enforcement office. Overgrown vegetation **blocking views** is handled instead through View Preservation, Restoration of View, a Hedge Height Claim, or a design-review-approved landscape plan. **Tree branches extending onto adjacent private property are a civil matter between neighbors.** [source: https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/government/departments/community-development/code-enforcement/frequently-asked-questions, as of 2026-06-18]

## What Code Enforcement does NOT regulate (civil issues)

[source: https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/government/departments/community-development/code-enforcement/frequently-asked-questions, as of 2026-06-18]

- Overgrown vegetation, foliage, or tree branches passing from one private property to another.
- Lawn furniture and umbrellas.
- Portable BBQs and fireplaces (the Fire Department may be able to enforce).
- EZ-Up type canopies, **except** if used as a carport.
- Potted plants of any size.
- Flags — **unless** a new flagpole is structurally installed in the ground, which is treated as a structure and requires approval.
- Small, portable swing sets and minor play equipment — **unless** they are tree houses or play houses **over 120 square feet and higher than 12 feet**.

## Tenant / landlord disputes

- Typically civil matters unless they involve building safety and/or unpermitted construction. The City refers residents to Fair Housing Orange County (http://www.fairhousingoc.org/contact/) and the Legal Aid Society of the Orange County Bar Association (https://legalaidocba.org/). [source: https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/government/departments/community-development/code-enforcement/frequently-asked-questions, as of 2026-06-18]

## Unpermitted construction

- To confirm that a property has active permits, or to report unpermitted construction, contact the **Building Division at (949) 497-0715**. [source: https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/government/departments/community-development/code-enforcement/frequently-asked-questions, as of 2026-06-18]
- Unpermitted work is charged **double permit and plan check fees**. [source: https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/home/showpublisheddocument/26193/639184240951470000, as of 2026-05-12]

## The "Urgent Safety Issues (Non-Emergency)" tile

The tile on the Code Enforcement landing page is not a phone link at all — it
points to the Police Department's Dispatch page,
`https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/government/departments/police/support-services/dispatch`, which publishes the
number. The City's wording there: "If you have a non-emergency matter that
requires police or fire assistance, please dial (949) 497-0701. If you have an
emergency and need immediate assistance, please dial 9-1-1."
[source: https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/government/departments/police/support-services/dispatch, as of 2026-08-09]
