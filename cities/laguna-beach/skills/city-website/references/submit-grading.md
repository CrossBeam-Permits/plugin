# Submit: Grading Permit — EnerGov type 214

The permit for **moving earth**: cut and fill, site preparation, retaining and
slope work, and the drainage that comes with it. In a hillside coastal city this
is rarely a stand-alone job — a grading permit usually sits alongside a
Residential Permit (183) for the structure and, upstream of both, a planning
entitlement (114). See `submit-residential-permit.md` and
`submit-residential-planning-entitlements.md`.

Two things set this type apart from the light MEP and Sign applications: it
carries the City's **full 41-slot file set** (the same one the Residential Permit
uses), and **Plans is required here**, not optional.

**Apply:** https://lagunabeachca-energovweb.tylerhost.net/apps/SelfService#/permit/apply/214/0/0 (portal account required)
**Catalog name, verbatim:** "Grading Permit" — category GRADING, module PERMITS.
**Catalog description, verbatim:** "This application is for a Grading Permit. \*PLEASE BE ADVISED THAT THE FINAL PERMIT ISSUANCE IS CONDUCTED IN PERSON AT THE BUILDING COUNTER."

## Before you start

- **Download and sign the Building Permit Application** before you start: http://gis.lagunabeachcity.net/MapsLinks/PublicGIS/BuildingPermitApplication.pdf — this type is one of the few whose written instruction links the form by name and adds, verbatim: "Please be sure to sign applications before uploading into the permit portal." [source: https://lagunabeachca-energovweb.tylerhost.net/apps/SelfService#/permit/apply/214/0/0, as of 2026-08-10]
- **Plans are required, not optional.** The instruction lists "Project plans" flatly, with no "if applicable" hedge, and the Plans slot is configured as required.
- **Know the work-hour rule before you bid the job.** The City's published limit for grading is **8:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m.** — narrower than the 7:30 a.m.–6:00 p.m. general construction window — with hauling hours 9:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m. It is enforced by Code Enforcement. [source: https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/government/departments/community-development/building-permits, as of 2026-07-08]
- **A portal account.** See `how-to-submit.md` → "Before any of this".

[source: https://lagunabeachca-energovweb.tylerhost.net/apps/SelfService#/permit/apply/214/0/0, as of 2026-08-10]

## The wizard, step by step

**1 — Location.** "ATTENTION: This application is for a Grading Permit only."
Click **+**, type the address of the primary residence into the search field,
select it once results populate; if it is not found, click **Enter Manually**.
Address is required.

**2 — Basic info.** Description is required, and the City asks for a "detailed
scope of work" — a "comprehensive explanation of the work you plan to do" that
**matches your plans**. Note the City's field warning, verbatim: "\*\* Square
Footage is for office use only. Do Not use." Square footage and valuation are
not required on this type.

**3 — Contacts.** Click **+** to search by person or business name, select the
result and click **Add**; if not found, **Enter Manually**. The City lists:
**Applicant** and **Owner** (required), plus **Contractor**,
**Architect/Designer**, and **Engineer**, each "(If applicable)".

**4 — More info.** Nothing to fill in. The step carries only the City's note that
"Helpful forms can be found on the City's website" —
[Building Applications & Forms](https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/government/departments/community-development/building-permits/applications-forms).

**5 — Attachments.** Signed application **and** plans, both required, plus the
supporting reports below.

**6 — Signature and submit.** An electronic signature is required.

## The questions you will face (0 fields)

**None.** Like the Sign Permit (197), this type has no custom-field groups
configured — no counts, no acknowledgements, no declarations. Everything the City
learns about the earthwork, it learns from your description, your plans, and your
reports. Write the description to match the drawings.

## Optional attachment slots worth knowing

39 more categories exist on this type — the City's full file set. The ones that
actually decide whether a grading submittal is complete:

- **Soils Report or Geotechnical Report** (and **Geotechnical Report Addendum**) — the foundation of any grading review in a hillside city. Nothing about cut, fill, or slope stability gets settled without it.
- **Grading Plans** — a dedicated slot, separate from the required Plans slot. If your grading sheets are a distinct set, put them here as well.
- **Hydrology Report** — where the drainage math goes.
- **WQMP** (.pdf/.doc/.docx/.jpeg/.jpg) and **WQ LID Checklist** — Water Quality Management Plan and the Low Impact Development checklist. Expect Water Quality to be a reviewing division on an earthwork job.
- **Survey** — "Project Survey". Existing and proposed grades come from here.
- **Structural Calculations** (and the addendum, and **Revised Structural Calculations**) — retaining walls and shoring.
- **Fuel Modification or Fire Study** — Laguna is a very-high fire hazard severity zone city.
- **WELO Calculations/Report** — landscape water efficiency, on jobs that re-landscape.
- **Asbestos Report** and **Asbestos Clearance Certification** — on demolition or older-structure work.
- **Plan Check Responses** plus the per-division response slots — **Building**, **Fire**, **Planning**, **Public Works**, and **Water Quality Plan Check Response** — where you upload your answers to a correction letter on a resubmittal. All five divisions have their own slot on this type; that is a fair map of who reviews a grading permit.
- **Health Approval** and **Hydrant Flow Report** — two slots the City added between 2026-10-19 and 2026-10-26. Both are optional, and the portal describes each only by its name. Two City rules use the same names. A project with a fire sprinkler system needs an LBFD Hydrant Flow Report from a water-purveyor flow test no more than 6 months old (LBMC Title 15 §15.01.070, amending NFPA 13 §28.1.3). A project with a food operation or another Environmental Health-regulated use needs Orange County Environmental Health approval (the City's 2026 building issuance-requirements checklist). The portal does not tie either slot to those rules, so ask the Building Division, (949) 497-0715, before telling an applicant a slot is required. [source: https://lagunabeachca-energovweb.tylerhost.net/apps/SelfService#/permit/apply/214/0/0, attachment setup read through the portal's public API, as of 2026-10-26]
- **Revision Form** — its description is now a link to the Building Division's Applications & Forms page, https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/government/departments/community-development/building-permits/applications-forms, which is where the portal points you for the form. [source: https://lagunabeachca-energovweb.tylerhost.net/apps/SelfService#/permit/apply/214/0/0, attachment setup read through the portal's public API, as of 2026-10-26]
- **Plans: Reference Only Digitized Paper (NO REVIEW)** and **For Reference Only** — exactly what they say; do not put review plans here.

The rest of the set — AM&M, Address Assignment Request, Change of Registered
Professional, ESR Sheet, Energy Calculations and addendum, Fabrication or Shop
Drawings, Hold Harmless, Installation Manual, Mechanical Equipment Guide,
Miscellaneous, Revised Permit Application, Revision Form, Roof Material Color
Swatch, Specification Sheets — is inherited from the City's standard file set and
mostly has nothing to do with earthwork. Most accept PDF only; the response slots
also take .doc/.docx/.jpg/.jpeg, and Miscellaneous takes images.
[source: https://lagunabeachca-energovweb.tylerhost.net/apps/SelfService#/permit/apply/214/0/0, as of 2026-08-10]

## Contacts and licences

- Required: **Applicant, Owner**. Optional: Architect, Contractor, Designer, Engineer — a shorter list than the MEP types carry (no Agent, Owner/Builder, Subcontractor, or Tenant slots).
- **Contractor entries are licence-validated** by the portal.
- Permits issue only to an owner-builder or a **City-licensed** contractor, and every business working in the city needs a Laguna Beach business licence. [source: https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/government/departments/community-development/building-permits, as of 2026-07-08]

## After you submit

The City's confirmation message, verbatim:

> Thank you for your Grading Permit application. A permit technician will review
> your application to ensure all required information has been submitted, and
> contact you in 2 - 3 business days for payment.

So submitting does not start plan check — a technician screens it for
completeness first, then invoices. **And the permit itself is not issued
online.** This type carries the City's warning, verbatim: "\*PLEASE BE ADVISED
THAT THE FINAL PERMIT ISSUANCE IS CONDUCTED IN PERSON AT THE BUILDING COUNTER."
Plan on a counter trip to 505 Forest Ave at the end.

**On fees:** this is one of only two types in the entire catalog with **fee
estimation enabled in the back end** — the other is Sign (197). Do not promise a
resident an estimator: **no fee estimator is surfaced anywhere in the portal.**
It is back-end configuration, not a feature you can use. Fees are quoted by staff
after review. For a current amount, call the fee estimate; do not quote figures
from `fees.md`.
[source: https://lagunabeachca-energovweb.tylerhost.net/apps/SelfService#/permit/apply/214/0/0, as of 2026-08-10]
