# Submit: Right-of-Way Permits — EnerGov type 315

**The Public Works permit for working in the street**: trenching, pavement cuts
and restoration, curb and gutter, sidewalk and driveway approaches, potholing and
boring, and the traffic control that goes with them. It is a **permit**, not a
plan — and one of only **two** permit types in the Laguna Beach catalog with
**no** in-person-issuance warning (the other is SolarAPP+ 124). It is also the
one type whose confirmation does not promise an invoice outright: one comes
**"if your application has been approved"**.

**Apply:** https://lagunabeachca-energovweb.tylerhost.net/apps/SelfService#/permit/apply/315/0/0 (portal account required)
**Catalog name, verbatim:** "Right-of-Way Permits" — category RIGHT-OF-WAY, module PERMITS. **Description, verbatim:** "Use this to apply for Right-of-Way permits."

Unless noted otherwise, everything below is the City's own configuration of this
application, read from the portal. The apply URL is login-gated in a browser;
the mirrored pull is the City source document (not included in this plugin).
[source: https://lagunabeachca-energovweb.tylerhost.net/apps/SelfService#/permit/apply/315/0/0, as of 2026-08-10]

## Before you start

- **Check the two moratoriums before you promise anyone a date.** The City links both from inside this form, and they gate *when* you can work in the street: [Slurry Seal Moratorium](https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/home/showpublisheddocument/372/638004904053970000) and [Summer Construction Moratorium](https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/home/showpublisheddocument/374/638003850135530000).
- **Get the City of Laguna Beach business licence first.** The City's note, verbatim: "Business License # - REQUIRED FOR ALL CONTRACTORS DOING WORK IN THE CITY ROW". It is a **required field** on the form, so you cannot submit without the number: https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/government/departments/administrative-services/finance-division/business-licenses
- **Night work needs the City Engineer's approval.** The City's own words: "The City has a Noise Ordinance and night work is allowed only with the City Engineer's approval. The applicant shall provide a written explanation of why night work is required well in advance of the project for City review."
- **Have workers' compensation details to hand** — carrier, policy number, and expiration date are all required fields — plus your contractor licence number and class.
- **No street address for the work?** The City's instruction is to find the nearest address using the [LB Public GIS viewer](https://gis.lagunabeachcity.net/Html5Viewer/index.html?viewer=LagunaBeachPublicGIS), or click "Enter Manually". Additional helpful forms are on the City's [Public Works permit page](https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/government/departments/public-works/public-works-permit).
- **A portal account** — see `how-to-submit.md` → "Before any of this".

[source: https://lagunabeachca-energovweb.tylerhost.net/apps/SelfService#/permit/apply/315/0/0, as of 2026-08-10]

## The wizard, step by step

**1 — Location.** "ATTENTION: This application is for a Public Works
Right-of-Way (ROW) Permit only." Click **+**, enter the address, select it from
the results; if not found, use the LB Public GIS viewer to find the nearest
address or click **Enter Manually**. Address is required.

**2 — Basic info.** Description is required, and the City is specific about what
it must contain: "We require thorough description of the work. The following
information is required: Does the work involve pavement removal and restoration?
Is a utility outage required? Is the work proposed to be done during the day or
night?" — plus the night-work rule above. The City also notes: "Valuation will
determine miscellaneous costs." (Valuation itself is not a required field.)

**3 — Contacts.** Click **+** to search by person or business name, then **Add**;
if not found, **Enter Manually**. The City lists **Applicant** and **Contractor**
as required, plus **Customer/Resident** and **Owner/Builder** "if applicable".

**4 — More info.** The 35 fields below. The City's instruction: "Please enter the
details for Curb and Gutter (LF), Trench (LF), Sidewalk/Driveway (SF),
Potholing/Boring (EA), Pavement Construction (SF), Traffic Control (# of pages)
if applicable."

**5 — Attachments.** Nine slots, none required; see below. **6 — Signature and
submit.** An electronic signature is required.

[source: https://lagunabeachca-energovweb.tylerhost.net/apps/SelfService#/permit/apply/315/0/0, as of 2026-08-10]

## The questions you will face (35 fields, 4 groups, in the City's order)

**Group 1 — Right-of Way Permit Info (14 fields).** **Street Name(s)/
Intersection(s)** and **Business License #** are required; Public Utility
Company, Work Order #, Other (Please Specify), Other Finals, Separate Permits and
Street Classification are not. Then six **quantity fields that drive the fees**:

| Quantity field | Unit |
|---|---|
| Curb and Gutter Construction | linear feet |
| Trench | linear feet |
| Sidewalk Construction | square feet |
| Potholing/Boring | each |
| Non-Utility Pavement Construction | square feet |
| Traffic Control Plan | pages |

Enter only the quantities you are actually doing — these numbers build the invoice.

**Group 2 — OWNER-BUILDER DECLARATION (7 fields).** Three statement-and-checkbox
pairs — Not Intended for Sale, Exclusively Contracted, Owner of Property — plus
an "Exempt from the Contractors' State License Law" statement.

**Group 3 — LICENSED CONTRACTOR'S DECLARATION (3 fields).** An Affirmation
statement, then **License Number** and **License Class** — both **required**.

**Group 4 — WORKERS' COMPENSATION DECLARATION (11 fields).** A Warning statement,
then statement-and-checkbox pairs for Certificate of Consent, Compensation
Insurance, and Performance of Work, with four **required** fields: **Policy
Number**, **Carrier**, a second **Policy Number**, and **Expiration Date**.

[source: https://lagunabeachca-energovweb.tylerhost.net/apps/SelfService#/permit/apply/315/0/0, as of 2026-08-10]

## Required attachments

**None.** All nine slots are optional — but the City's written instruction asks
for two documents anyway, verbatim: "Applicants must submit the following: Site
Location Map (if applicable) / Traffic Control plans (if applicable)". Upload
them to the **Location Map** and **Traffic Control** slots. The same step carries
the standard warning: an incomplete application draws a correction request before
the project plans are reviewed.
[source: https://lagunabeachca-energovweb.tylerhost.net/apps/SelfService#/permit/apply/315/0/0, as of 2026-08-10]

## Optional attachment slots worth knowing

All nine, alphabetically: **Business License**, **Conditions of Approval**,
**Contractor License**, **Insurance**, **Letter of Authorization**, **Location
Map**, **Plans**, **ROW Photos**, **Traffic Control**. Every one takes PDF plus
JPEG/JPG/TIF/TIFF/PNG and unlimited files, except **Plans** ("Project Plans for
Reviews"), which is PDF-only.

- **Location Map** and **Traffic Control** are the two the City's instruction actually asks for; the traffic-control page count also goes in the Traffic Control Plan quantity field.
- **Business License**, **Contractor License**, and **Insurance** hold the credentials behind the required licence and workers'-comp fields.

[source: https://lagunabeachca-energovweb.tylerhost.net/apps/SelfService#/permit/apply/315/0/0, as of 2026-08-10]

## Contacts and licences

- Required: **Applicant, Contractor**. Optional: Customer/Resident, Other, Owner. **There is no required Owner contact on this type** — unusual for a Laguna permit.
- **Contractor entries are licence-validated** by the portal, and the form separately requires **License Number** and **License Class** in the Licensed Contractor's Declaration.
- ⚠️ The City's written instruction names the optional contacts as "Customer/Resident" and "Owner/Builder", but the configured slots are Customer/Resident, Other, and **Owner** — there is no Owner/Builder slot on this type. If you are the owner doing the work yourself, ask Public Works which slot they want rather than guessing.
- A **City of Laguna Beach business licence** is required for all contractors doing work in the City right-of-way, and every business working in the city needs one. [source: https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/government/departments/administrative-services/finance-division/business-licenses, as of 2026-08-10]

[source: https://lagunabeachca-energovweb.tylerhost.net/apps/SelfService#/permit/apply/315/0/0, as of 2026-08-10]

## After you submit

The City's confirmation message, verbatim:

> Thank you for your Right-of-Way Permit application. We will review your
> application to ensure all required information has been submitted and will send
> an invoice to you within 2 - 3 business days for payment if your application
> has been approved.

Read the last clause: unlike every other type, the invoice is **conditional on
approval**, so silence after 2–3 business days may mean the application did not
clear review rather than a slow queue — chase it with Public Works. Note also
what is absent: **no** in-person-issuance warning, one of only two permit types
in the catalog without one (`how-to-submit.md`, Channel D).
[source: https://lagunabeachca-energovweb.tylerhost.net/apps/SelfService#/permit/apply/315/0/0, as of 2026-08-10]
