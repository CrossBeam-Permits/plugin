# Submit: Mechanical Permit — EnerGov type 186

The stand-alone HVAC permit: **furnaces and F.A.U.s, air conditioners and heat
pumps, air handlers, ducts and registers, exhaust and ventilation fans, whole
house fans, gas and factory-built fireplaces, hoods and canopies, boilers and
compressors, VAV boxes, fire/smoke dampers.** Use it when mechanical work is the
whole job. If the mechanical work is part of a larger remodel or addition, it
rides along on the **Residential Permit (183)** instead — see
`submit-residential-permit.md`.

**Apply:** https://lagunabeachca-energovweb.tylerhost.net/apps/SelfService#/permit/apply/186/0/0 (portal account required)
**Catalog name, verbatim:** "Mechanical Permit" — category MECHANICAL, ELECTRICAL, AND PLUMBING, module PERMITS.
**Catalog description, verbatim:** "This application is for a Mechanical Building Permit. \*PLEASE BE ADVISED THAT THE FINAL PERMIT ISSUANCE IS CONDUCTED IN PERSON AT THE BUILDING COUNTER."

Unless noted otherwise, everything below is the City's own configuration of this
application, read from the portal. The apply URL is login-gated in a browser;
the mirrored pull is the City source document (not included in this plugin).
[source: https://lagunabeachca-energovweb.tylerhost.net/apps/SelfService#/permit/apply/186/0/0, as of 2026-08-10]

## Before you start

- **A/C in scope? The Mechanical Equipment Guide is the sharp edge on this type.** The City states it twice on this form — on the More Info step and again on the Attachments step — verbatim: "Mechanical Equipment Guide - REQUIRED FOR ALL A/C PERMITS BY PLANNING". The guide: https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/home/showpublisheddocument/9959/638586590648770000. **This is the one line 187 (Electrical) and 188 (Plumbing) do not carry.** The upload slot for it is filed as *optional* while the City's own text says required — on an A/C job, treat it as required.
- **A signed permit application.** The only required attachment on this type is "Permit Application", described by the City simply as "Building Application". Sign it before you upload.
- **A portal account.** See `how-to-submit.md` → "Before any of this".
- *Our note, not the City's:* the City publishes **two** paper forms that could answer to "Building Application" — the *Building Application* and an *Electrical, Mechanical, and Plumbing Permit Application* — on the Building Applications & Forms page, and the portal does not say which one this slot wants. If in doubt, call the Building Division, (949) 497-0715. [source: https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/government/departments/community-development/building-permits/applications-forms, as of 2026-08-10]

[source: https://lagunabeachca-energovweb.tylerhost.net/apps/SelfService#/permit/apply/186/0/0, as of 2026-08-10]

## The wizard, step by step

**1 — Location.** "ATTENTION: This application is for a Mechanical Permit only."
Click **+**, type the address of the primary residence into the search field,
select it once results populate; if it is not found, click **Enter Manually**.
Address is required.

**2 — Basic info.** Description is required, and the City asks for a "detailed
scope of work" — a "comprehensive explanation of the work you plan to do" that
**matches your plans**. Note the City's field warning, verbatim: "\*\* Square
Footage is for office use only. Do Not use." Square footage and valuation are
not required on this type.

**3 — Contacts.** Click **+** to search by person or business name, select the
result and click **Add**; if not found, **Enter Manually**. The City lists:
**Applicant** and **Owner** (required), plus **Contractor** "(If applicable)".

**4 — More info.** "Please enter the number of items each for Mechanical." Plus
the Mechanical Equipment Guide line above, and a pointer to the City's
[applications and forms page](https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/government/departments/community-development/building-permits/applications-forms).

**5 — Attachments.** Signed application; project plans if applicable. See below.

**6 — Signature and submit.** An electronic signature is required.

[source: https://lagunabeachca-energovweb.tylerhost.net/apps/SelfService#/permit/apply/186/0/0, as of 2026-08-10]

## The questions you will face (36 fields, 1 group)

This is **not** a questionnaire about the project — it is the equipment count
sheet that drives the itemized mechanical fees. One group, all optional, all
numeric, in the City's order:

| Group | Fields | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Mechanical | 36 | F.A.U. up to / over 100K BTU, AC coil, air conditioner or heat pump 0–5 tons and over 5 tons, air handling unit up to / over 10,000 CFM, floor and wall-mounted heaters, duct/register/return each, vents and ventilation fans, heating systems & ducting by BTU band, residential and commercial exhaust systems, evaporative cooler, whole house fan, factory-built and gas fireplaces, hood or canopy, VAV boxes, refrigeration systems by BTU band, boilers or compressors by HP/BTU band, fire/smoke damper, repair or alteration of an appliance, AC disconnect, misc not listed |

Count only what you are actually installing and leave the rest blank. The City
bills mechanical work as separate itemized fees, so these numbers become money.

## Required attachments

One, PDF, one file:

| Attachment | The City's description |
|---|---|
| Permit Application | Building Application |

The City's written instruction on this step reads differently from the
configuration: it says "Applicants must submit the following: Project plans (if
applicable)" and names the Mechanical Equipment Guide — but **Plans is an
optional slot on this type and Permit Application is the only required one.**
The condensed rest of the instruction: submit all required documents to avoid
delays — an incomplete application draws a correction request *before* the plans
are reviewed at all.
[source: https://lagunabeachca-energovweb.tylerhost.net/apps/SelfService#/permit/apply/186/0/0, as of 2026-08-10]

## Optional attachment slots worth knowing

Eight more categories exist on this type, all PDF unless noted, unlimited files
unless noted:

- **Mechanical Equipment Guide** — "REQUIRED FOR ALL A/C PERMITS BY PLANNING". Optional slot, mandatory content. On an A/C job, fill it.
- **Plans** — "Project Plans for Reviews", one file. The City's written instruction asks for project plans "if applicable"; this is where they go.
- **Specification Sheets** — "Project Specification Sheets". The equipment cut sheets a reviewer will look for when the count sheet is ambiguous.
- **Energy Calculations** and **Energy Calculation Addendum** — Title 24 compliance; expected when the scope triggers it.
- **Plan Check Responses** (.doc/.docx/.pdf/.jpg/.jpeg) — where you upload your answers to a correction letter on a resubmittal.
- **Revision Form** — for a change after the permit exists.
- **Miscellaneous** (.pdf/.tif/.tiff/.jpeg/.jpg/.png) — everything else.

[source: https://lagunabeachca-energovweb.tylerhost.net/apps/SelfService#/permit/apply/186/0/0, as of 2026-08-10]

## Contacts and licences

- Required: **Applicant, Owner**. Optional: Agent, Architect, Contractor, Contractor – Prior, Designer, Engineer, Other, Owner - Prior, Owner/Builder, Subcontractor, Tenant.
- **Contractor and Subcontractor entries are licence-validated** by the portal.
- Permits issue only to an owner-builder or a **City-licensed** contractor, and every business working in the city needs a Laguna Beach business licence. [source: https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/government/departments/community-development/building-permits, as of 2026-07-08]

## After you submit

The City's confirmation message, verbatim:

> Thank you for your Mechanical Permit application. A permit technician will
> review your application to ensure all required information has been submitted,
> and contact you in 2 - 3 business days for payment.

So submitting does not start plan check — a technician screens it for
completeness first, then invoices. **And the permit itself is not issued
online.** This type carries the City's warning, verbatim: "\*PLEASE BE ADVISED
THAT THE FINAL PERMIT ISSUANCE IS CONDUCTED IN PERSON AT THE BUILDING COUNTER."
Plan on a counter trip to 505 Forest Ave at the end.
[source: https://lagunabeachca-energovweb.tylerhost.net/apps/SelfService#/permit/apply/186/0/0, as of 2026-08-10]
