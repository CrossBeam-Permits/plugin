# Laguna Beach EV Charger — Code Compliance Checklists (Non-Residential & Multi-Family)

Source: `ev-charging-code-compliance-nonresidential.pdf`
Source: `ev-charging-code-compliance-multifamily.pdf`

These checklists are used for the expedited EV charging station permit process for non-residential buildings and multi-family dwellings. They are more detailed than the residential checklist.

---

## Charging Station Types (All Checklists)

| Type | Power Level | Circuit Rating |
|------|------------|----------------|
| Level 1 | 110/120 VAC | 15 or 20 Amps |
| Level 2 - 3.3 kW (low) | 208/240 VAC | 20 or 30 Amps |
| Level 2 - 6.6 kW (medium) | 208/240 VAC | 40 Amps |
| Level 2 - 9.6 kW (high) | 208/240 VAC | 50 Amps |
| Level 2 - 19.2 kW (highest) | 208/240 VAC | 100 Amps |

## Non-Residential Checklist

### Permit Application Requirements
- EVCS manufacturer's specs and installation guidelines

### Electrical Load Calculation
- Load calculation worksheet included (CEC 220)
- Panel upgrade determination based on load calc
- Charging circuit sized for continuous load at 125%
- For Level 2 ≥9.6kW (≥50A): completed circuit card with electrical calculations + single line diagram

### Site Plan and Single Line Drawing
- Site plan + separate electrical plan with single-line diagram
- Mechanical plan if indoor venting triggered (CEC 625.52(B))
- Fully dimensioned and drawn to scale showing:
  - Location, size, use of all structures
  - Location of electrical panel to charging system
  - Type of charging system and mounting

### California Electrical Code Compliance
- Manufacturer's specs included
- Electrical plan identifies amperage and location of existing panel
- Panel schedule shows room for additional breakers
- **Disconnect required** for units >60A or >150V to ground (CEC 625.43)
- NRTL listing mark (UL 2202/UL 2200)
- Trenching detail if required — 18" minimum cover for direct burial (CEC 300.5)

### California Green Building Standards Code (Non-Residential)
- CALGreen EV Readiness requirements (CGBSC Table 5.106.5.3.3)
- Minimum required number of charging spaces
- Design requirements per CGBSC 5.106.5.3.1 (single) or 5.106.5.3.2 (multiple)

### Accessibility (CBC Chapter 11B)
- Required accessible EVCS features for disabled
- Correct number and type of accessible stalls (Table 11B-228.3.2.1)
- Compliance with 11B-812 and Figure 11B-812.9

## Multi-Family Checklist

### Permit Application Requirements
Same as non-residential.

### Electrical Load Calculation
Same as non-residential.

### Site Plan and Single Line Drawing
Same as non-residential.

### California Electrical Code Compliance
Same as non-residential.

### California Green Building Standards Code (Multi-Family Specific)
- CALGreen EV Readiness: 10% of total parking spaces for new multifamily must be EV spaces (4.106.4.2)
- At least one EV space in common use area available to all residents (4.106.4.2.1)
- Accessibility options for EV spaces (Section 4.106.4.2.2):
  - Adjacent to accessible parking per CBC Chapter 11A, OR
  - On accessible route per CBC Chapter 2, OR
  - Comply with Section 4.106.4.2.2, items 1, 2 and 3

## Permit Timeline (Both Non-Residential & Multi-Family)

- **Expedited**: Up to **3 business days** for qualifying projects (all checklist items = YES)
- **Standard**: Up to **10 business days** for all other EVCS projects
- If any items checked NO → revise plans or go through standard review

## Signing Requirements

- Electrical plans must be completed, stamped and signed by a **California Licensed Electrical Engineer** or a **C-10 electrical contractor**
- EVCS review limited to health and safety requirements
- EVCS permit approval is NOT subject to HOA approval (Civil Code § 4080)
