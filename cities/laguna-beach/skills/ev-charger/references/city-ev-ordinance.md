# Laguna Beach EV Charger — Municipal Code Ch. 14.85

Source: the City source document (not included in this plugin) (ecode360.com/42894732)
Ordinance: Ord. 1643 § 1, 2020

---

## Key Provisions

### Purpose (§14.85.010)
Adopt an expedited, streamlined EVCS permitting process per CA Gov. Code § 65850.7 for timely, cost-effective installations.

### Applicability (§14.85.020)
- Covers ALL electric vehicle charging station systems in the city
- Systems legally established before this chapter → not subject unless materially modified
- Routine maintenance and like-kind replacements → **NO permit required**

### Definition: Electric Vehicle Charging Station (§14.85.030)
Any level of EVSE designed and built in compliance with **CEC Article 625** (or successor regulation) that delivers electricity from an external source into a plug-in electric vehicle.

### System Requirements (§14.85.040)
- Must meet all applicable health/safety standards (city + state)
- Must meet CEC, SAE, NEMA, UL standards, and PUC rules

### Applications and Documents (§14.85.050)
- Building Division developed checklist for expedited review eligibility
- Checklist may reference "Plug-In Electric Vehicle Infrastructure Permitting Checklist" from OPR's *Zero-Emission Vehicles in California: Community Readiness Guidebook*
- All documents available on city website and at Building Division during business hours
- **Electronic submittal** (email/web/fax) must be available
- **Electronic signatures** accepted

### Permit Review (§14.85.060)
**(a)** Administrative, **nondiscretionary** review process

**(b)** Application meeting checklist → deemed complete → promptly processed → all permits issued

**(c)** Incomplete application → **written correction notice** detailing all deficiencies

**(d)** Building official may require **conditional use permit** only if **specific, adverse impact** on public health/safety (based on substantial evidence)

**(e)** Special use permit denial requires:
- Written findings based on substantial evidence
- Specific, adverse impact with no feasible mitigation
- Basis for rejection of feasible alternatives
- **Appealable to Planning Commission** (§25.05.070)

**(f)** Any conditions must mitigate at **lowest possible cost**

**(g)** **NOT subject to HOA approval** (Civil Code § 4080)

## Timeline

The ordinance does not specify an explicit day count like the solar ordinance (Ch. 14.84's "3 business days"). However, the city's code compliance checklists state:
- **Expedited projects**: Up to **3 business days** (all checklist items pass)
- **All other EVCS projects**: Up to **10 business days**
