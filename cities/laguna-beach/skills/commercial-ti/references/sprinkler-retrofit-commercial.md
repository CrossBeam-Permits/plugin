# Laguna Beach — Commercial Sprinkler Retrofit (LBMC §14.50.104)

**Authority:** LBMC **§14.50.104**, the city's local amendment to CBC §903 (sprinkler triggers) and §903.6 (retrofit trigger). Adopted by Ord. 1725/1726 effective 2026-01-01 as part of the 2025 code-cycle adoption.

**Critical disambiguation:**

| Trigger | Code | Scope |
|---------|------|-------|
| Group R sprinkler retrofit | LBMC Title 15 **§1103.5.4** plus the occupancy-specific valuation section | All Group R uses the Fire Code scope trigger. CRC-track houses/townhouses use Ch. 14.52.026/.030 for valuation; CBC-track Group R uses LBMC §14.50.104/CBC §903.6(1),(5). See `../../residential-remodel/references/city-sprinkler-retrofit.md`. |
| Commercial sprinkler retrofit | LBMC **§14.50.104** (amending CBC §903.6) | Other-than-R/U clause requires both a post-work sprinklers-required finding and ≥50% cumulative valuation over any three-year period; clauses (3) and (4) add separate addition triggers. |

Both the residential and the commercial side run a 50%-valuation test, which is why they get confused. They are different sections with different scopes. For commercial TI the framing is **§14.50.104** with valuation math — never §1103.5.4 (Group R scope) and never Ch. 14.52.030 (one- and two-family valuation). Use §14.50.104 for all commercial occupancies.

---

## 1. Tightened §903.2 Thresholds (Laguna Override of State CBC)

LBMC §14.50.104 amends CBC §903.2 to require sprinklers in commercial occupancies at substantially lower thresholds than state law:

| Occupancy | State CBC §903.2 (2025 baseline) | Laguna §14.50.104 |
|-----------|----------------------------------|-------------------|
| **Group A (assembly)** | A-1/A-2/A-3/A-4 sprinklered at >12,000 sf fire area OR occupant load ≥300 (single space) / 100 (multi-theater) OR not on level of exit discharge | **>2 stories OR >5,000 sf**; occupant-load thresholds unchanged (100 multi-theater / 300 single) |
| **Group B (business)** | >12,000 sf fire area OR >3 stories OR combined ≥24,000 sf | **>2 stories OR >5,000 sf** |
| **Group E (educational)** | >12,000 sf fire area | **>2 stories OR >5,000 sf** |
| **Group F-1 (moderate-hazard factory/industrial)** | >12,000 sf fire area OR >3 stories OR combined ≥24,000 sf | **>2 stories OR >5,000 sf** |
| **Group M (mercantile)** | >12,000 sf fire area OR >3 stories OR combined ≥24,000 sf OR upholstered furniture/mattress display >5,000 sf | **>2 stories OR >5,000 sf**; **upholstered furniture/mattress display sprinklered REGARDLESS of size** |
| **Group S-1 (moderate-hazard storage)** | >12,000 sf fire area OR >3 stories OR combined ≥24,000 sf | **>2 stories OR >5,000 sf** |
| **Group S-2 (low-hazard storage)** | >12,000 sf fire area OR >3 stories OR combined ≥24,000 sf | **>2 stories OR >5,000 sf** |

For a commercial TI in an existing building, **first compute the sprinklers-required test on the post-work building** using the Laguna thresholds. This drives whether the §14.50.104(2) retrofit trigger applies.

## 2. Retrofit Trigger §14.50.104(2)

LBMC §14.50.104 amends CBC §903.6(2):

> "An automatic sprinkler system shall be installed throughout a building when… (2) Additions, alterations or repairs are done to **other than a Group R or Group U occupancy** if the building would be required to be sprinklered after the additions, alterations or repairs are completed and the additions, alterations or repairs have a valuation of 50% or more of the building's valuation prior to the additions, alterations or repairs."

**Both prongs must be true to trigger full retrofit:**

1. **Sprinklers-required test** — After the work, would the building be required to be sprinklered under §903.2 as amended (the table in §1 above)? AND
2. **Valuation test** — Is the cumulative valuation of additions/alterations/repairs over a **rolling 3-year window** ≥ **50% of the building's pre-work valuation**?

### Valuation Computation

- Use the City's resolution-based valuation schedule (ICC Building Valuation Data, adjusted by City resolution).
- Cumulative: sum the valuations of all permits issued for additions, alterations, or repairs to the building over the prior **3 years (rolling window)**.
- Compare the cumulative valuation against the pre-work building valuation (also from the City's resolution-based schedule).
- If cumulative ≥ 50% of pre-work valuation AND the post-work building meets the §903.2 sprinkler-required test → full sprinkler retrofit required.

### Practical TI Examples

- **Example A — interior-only TI in 4,800-sf unsprinklered Group B retail**, valuation $400K, pre-work building valuation $750K. Post-work would require sprinklers (5,000-sf threshold tripped if combined with any addition, or 2-story threshold if applicable). Valuation = $400K / $750K = 53% → **retrofit triggered**, even without occupancy change or exterior work.
- **Example B — change of occupancy from Group M (retail) to Group A-2 (restaurant)** in a 4,500-sf single-story building. The Group M occupancy might have slipped under the >5,000-sf threshold. After change to Group A-2, the 100-occupant-load trigger (multi-theater) or 300-occupant-load trigger (single) plus the >5,000-sf threshold both apply. If post-work occupant load triggers the Group A threshold, sprinklers required, and the change-of-occupancy work usually exceeds 50% valuation → **retrofit triggered**.
- **Example C — minor finish-only TI**, $40K valuation in a $2M building. Below 50% valuation → no retrofit even if post-work would require sprinklers under §903.2. However, watch the 3-year rolling window — prior permits in the same building count.

## 3. Additional Independent Retrofit Triggers (§14.50.104(3) and (4))

These are independent triggers — any one fires the retrofit regardless of the valuation math in §2:

**Prong (3): Already-sprinklered building**
- Any addition to a building already equipped with an automatic sprinkler system requires extension of the system throughout the addition, regardless of whether the original system was required.

**Prong (4): Large addition**
- Any addition that is ≥**33% of existing building area** AND **>1,000 sf** within a **2-year period** triggers full sprinkler retrofit throughout the building.

## 4. Group M Upholstered Furniture / Mattress Display

The Laguna amendment retains and tightens the state CBC §903.2.7 exception for upholstered furniture and mattress sales display:

> "Mercantile occupancies with upholstered furniture or mattress display" → **sprinklered regardless of building size**.

For commercial TIs converting general retail to furniture or mattress display, sprinklers are required even in small buildings.

---

## 5. Spec / Shell Buildings — Quick-Response Sprinklers

**§15.01.070** amends NFPA 13 §9.4.3.1 to require **quick-response sprinklers** in shell/spec commercial buildings of undetermined use (other than warehouses). This applies at shell permit issuance; subsequent TI must not downgrade.

---

## 6. FDC Configuration (§15.01.070 amending NFPA 13)

For any sprinkler system, the fire department connection (FDC) must meet:

| Requirement | Spec |
|-------------|------|
| Inlets | Minimum **two 2½" inlets** (four if ≥500 gpm or standpipe system) |
| Paint | **OSHA red** |
| Distance to public hydrant | **≤150 ft** |
| Location | **Street-address side** of the building |

---

## 7. Hydrant Flow Test Currency

§15.01.070 amends NFPA 13 §28.1.3 — the hydrant flow test must be **≤6 months old** at the time of working-plan submittal. Submit on the city's Fire Hydrant Flow Form (doc ID 11178).

---

## 8. Alternative Extinguishing Systems Monitoring (§14.50.105)

Alternative fire-extinguishing systems (e.g., kitchen hood UL-300 wet chemical, clean-agent for IT rooms) must be monitored by the building fire-alarm/monitoring system per NFPA 72. This is a Laguna amendment to CBC §904.3.5.

---

## 9. Class I Standpipes (§14.50.106)

Local amendment to CBC §905.4 — Class I standpipe hose connections required per local amendment. Check the LBMC chapter 14.50 standpipe-trigger language for specific building heights/areas.

Title 15 §15.01.035 amends CFC §905 — "Class I dry standpipe required at >30 ft height differential," which can pull standpipes into multi-level commercial TIs.

---

## 10. Duct Smoke Detectors (§14.50.107)

Local amendment to CBC §907.3.1 — duct smoke detector requirements per local amendment. Relevant to any HVAC modification crossing fire-rated separations or supplying >2,000 cfm.

---

## 11. Atrium Sprinklers (§14.50.052)

Local amendment to CBC §404.3 — sprinklers required throughout atrium-containing buildings.

---

## 12. Fire Alarm — Group A ≥100 Occupant Load (§15.01.035 amending CFC §907.2.1)

Manual fire alarm system with occupant notification required for Group A occupancies with occupant load ≥100. State law triggers at 300 — Laguna lowers to 100.

---

## 13. High-Rise Threshold (§14.50.044)

High-rise + Group I-2 triggered at **>55 ft above lowest fire-department-access level**. Lower trigger than state CBC §403.1. Applies if a commercial TI is in a building approaching this threshold.

---
