# Fire & Life Safety — Commercial TI

This reference covers the three fire-life-safety questions that drive most commercial TI corrections:

1. **Sprinklers** — Does the existing or new occupancy / fire area cross a CBC 903.2 threshold?
2. **Fire alarm** — Does the occupant load cross a CBC 907.2 threshold?
3. **Mixed-occupancy separation** — Where two occupancies share a tenant, is the right rated separation provided?

When the project is a change of occupancy, CEBC 1011.2 retrofits the systems based on the *new* occupancy's CBC Ch.9 requirements.

## 1. Sprinkler Trigger Thresholds (CBC 903.2)

### Group A (CBC 903.2.1)

| Sub-Group | Sprinklers Required When |
|---|---|
| **A-1** (903.2.1.1) | Fire area > 12,000 sf OR occupant load >= 300 OR not on level of exit discharge OR multitheater complex |
| **A-2** (903.2.1.2) | Fire area > 5,000 sf OR occupant load >= 100 OR not on level of exit discharge OR structure > 5,000 sf with multiple A-2 fire areas separated by 4-hr fire walls |
| **A-3** (903.2.1.3) | Fire area > 12,000 sf OR occupant load >= 300 OR not on level of exit discharge OR structure > 12,000 sf with multiple fire areas containing exhibition/display rooms separated by 4-hr fire walls |
| **A-4** (903.2.1.4) | Fire area > 12,000 sf OR occupant load >= 300 OR not on level of exit discharge |
| **A-5** (903.2.1.5) | Enclosed A-5 accessory use areas > 1,000 sf |
| **Multiple A fire areas** (903.2.1.7) | Sharing exit/exit access components AND combined OL >= 300 |
| **A on occupiable roof** (903.2.1.6) | If OL > 100 for A-2 / > 300 for other A, all floors between roof and level of exit discharge sprinklered |

### Group B (CBC 903.2.2)

B is NOT generally sprinklered by occupancy alone. Two narrow triggers:

- **Ambulatory care facilities (903.2.2.1)** — Sprinkler required when 4+ care recipients are incapable of self-preservation, OR 1+ such recipients on a level other than exit discharge. Sprinklers the entire floor + relevant floors.
- **Lithium-ion / lithium metal battery R&D (903.2.2.2)** — Sprinkler the fire areas.

Otherwise, B-occupancy sprinkler requirements come from height/area/fire area concerns picked up via Chapter 5 limits, not occupancy alone.

### Group F-1 (CBC 903.2.4)

| Trigger |
|---|
| F-1 fire area > 12,000 sf |
| F-1 fire area > 3 stories above grade plane |
| Combined F-1 fire areas (all floors + mezzanines) > 24,000 sf |
| F-1 used to manufacture lithium-ion / lithium metal batteries |
| F-1 used to manufacture vehicles / ESS / equipment containing lithium-ion / lithium-metal batteries installed in process |

**Woodworking (903.2.4.1):** F-1 with woodworking operations > 2,500 sf generating fine combustible waste — sprinklers.

**Distilled spirits (903.2.4.2):** Any F-1 fire area used for distilled-spirits manufacture.

**Upholstered furniture / mattresses (903.2.4.3):** F-1 fire area > 2,500 sf — sprinklers.

### Group M (CBC 903.2.7)

| Trigger |
|---|
| M fire area > 12,000 sf |
| M fire area > 3 stories above grade plane |
| Combined M fire areas (all floors + mezzanines) > 24,000 sf |
| Structure > 24,000 sf with multiple M fire areas separated by 4-hr fire walls |

**High-piled storage (903.2.7.1):** Per CFC, any M with high-piled or rack storage.

**Upholstered furniture / mattresses display (903.2.7.2):** M with > 5,000 sf display/sale area.

**Lithium-ion battery storage (903.2.7.3):** Per CFC 320 / Ch.32.

### Group S-1 (CBC 903.2.9)

Same thresholds as F-1 (> 12,000 sf, > 3 stories, > 24,000 sf combined).

**Repair garages (903.2.9.x):** Specific triggers for major repair garages, see Section 406.8.

**High-piled (903.2.9.x):** Per CFC.

### Group S-2 (CBC 903.2.10)

Public parking garages: enclosed garages > 12,000 sf OR > 3 stories OR > 1 story below grade.

## 2. Fire Alarm Trigger Thresholds (CBC 907.2)

Manual fire alarm system (with occupant notification per 907.5) is required as follows. Manual pull stations may be omitted in fully-sprinklered buildings where the notification appliances activate via sprinkler waterflow.

| Group | Trigger |
|---|---|
| **A** (907.2.1) | OL >= 300 in assembly OR Group A OL > 100 above/below level of exit discharge. Group A occupancies not separated per 707.3.10 = single occupancy for purposes of this section. |
| **A** OL >= 1,000 (907.2.1.1) | Emergency voice/alarm communication system (EVACS) per 907.5.2.2 |
| **A** OL >= 10,000 (907.2.1.3) | Public address system + emergency backup power (per HSC 13108.9) — applies to public assemblies of 10,000+ |
| **B** (907.2.2) | Combined Group B OL >= 500 OR > 100 above/below level of exit discharge OR contains ambulatory care facility |
| **F** (907.2.4) | BOTH must exist: (1) Group F occupancy is two or more stories in height AND (2) combined Group F OL >= 500 above/below the lowest level of exit discharge. Conjunctive (AND) — single-story Group F is NOT triggered by occupant load alone, and Group F has NO standalone '>100 above/below' prong (unlike B/M). |
| **M** (907.2.7.1) | Combined M OL >= 500 OR > 100 above/below exit discharge |
| **S** (907.2.10.1) | Group S public- and self-storage occupancies three stories or greater in height — manual fire alarm for interior corridors and interior common areas. STORIES-based (not occupant-load). Manual boxes omitted where fully sprinklered to 903.3.1.1 with waterflow notification. |

### Smoke Detection in Specific Locations (CBC 907.2.13.1.1, 907.2.13.1.2)

Smoke detection in:
- Each mechanical / electrical / transformer / telephone equipment room (unless sprinklered).
- Each elevator machine room, machinery space, control room/space, elevator lobby.
- Duct smoke detection: main return-air and exhaust-air plenum of any A/C system > 2,000 CFM; at each connection to a vertical duct/riser serving 2+ stories from a return-air duct of A/C system.

### CO Detection (CBC 915 — applies to certain occupancies)

CBC 915 (Carbon Monoxide Detection) requires CO alarms in dwelling units and sleeping units, and in certain commercial spaces where fuel-burning appliances exist. For TI in offices, generally not triggered unless a fuel-fired appliance is being added or the space includes sleeping accommodations.

## 3. Mixed-Occupancy Separation (CBC 508 + Table 508.4)

When using the **separated-occupancies** approach (CBC 508.4), fire barriers (per Section 707) or horizontal assemblies (per 711) separate occupancies at the ratings in Table 508.4. Common commercial-TI cases:

| Occupancy A | Occupancy B | Sprinklered (S) | Not Sprinklered (NS) |
|---|---|---|---|
| B | M | 1 hr | 2 hr |
| B | F-1 | 1 hr | 2 hr |
| B | S-1 | 1 hr | 2 hr |
| B | S-2 | 1 hr | 2 hr |
| B | A-3 | 1 hr | 2 hr |
| M | S-1 | 1 hr | 2 hr |
| M | F-1 | 1 hr | 2 hr |
| F-1 | S-1 | 1 hr | 2 hr |
| F-1 | A | 1 hr | 2 hr |
| Any | H | Per Table 508.4 (typically 2 hr / 4 hr) |

(Always confirm against current CBC Table 508.4 — these are the typical values for the construction-type categories most TIs encounter.)

### Accessory Occupancies (CBC 508.2)

Accessory occupancies up to 10% of building area on a story are classified with the main occupancy and need no rated separation. Useful for: office break rooms, small B-office stockrooms in mostly-M tenants, etc.

### Non-Separated Occupancies (CBC 508.3)

All occupancies inherit the most restrictive of construction type, height/area, mixed-occupancy systems, and protection of openings. Most flexible for layout, most demanding on systems.

## 4. Change-of-Occupancy Retrofit (CEBC 1011.2)

### Sprinkler (CEBC 1011.2.1)

> The installation of an automatic sprinkler system shall be required where there is a change of occupancy classification and Chapter 9 of the current California Building Code requires an automatic sprinkler system based on the new occupancy. (CEBC 1011.2.1)

Or where there is a change of use *within* a space and the new fire-protection-system threshold in CBC Ch. 9 differs from what exists. Sprinkler retrofit applies within the change-of-occupancy area + all areas NOT separated by a fire wall from the change.

**Removal of existing sprinklers (1011.2.1.1):** The code official may permit removal where (1) system is not required for new construction, (2) public-exposed portions removed, AND (3) the system wasn't installed as part of any special construction feature or as a condition of approval. Plans + investigation reports must support removal.

### Fire Alarm (CEBC 1011.2.2)

> Where a change in occupancy classification occurs ... that requires a fire alarm and detection system to be provided based on the new occupancy, such system shall be in accordance with Chapter 11 [of CEBC] and Section 907 of the California Fire Code.

### Interior Finish (CEBC 1011.3)

Areas of the change shall have interior finish per current CBC for the new occupancy.

### Egress (CEBC 1011.5)

See `structural-alterations.md` for full Table 1011.5 hazard categories. Change to higher hazard requires full Ch.10 CBC egress; to equal/lower hazard, existing elements per Section 905 of CEBC are accepted with the listed exceptions.

### Exterior Walls (CEBC 1011.7)

See `occupancy-classification.md` Table 1011.7. Change to higher exterior-wall hazard requires updated fire-resistance ratings, opening protectives, etc.

### Vertical Shaft Enclosure (CEBC 1011.8)

When change to higher means-of-egress hazard (Table 1011.5), interior stairways enclosed per current CBC. Existing 1-hr shaft enclosures accepted where higher rating now required.

## 5. Common Commercial-TI Fire/Life-Safety Decisions

### Decision A: B → A-3 yoga (OL = 80) in 6,000-sf tenant of a 100,000-sf building

- Sprinkler: A-3 threshold is 12,000 sf OR OL >= 300. Fire area is 6,000 sf, OL = 80 → A-3 alone does NOT trigger sprinklers.
- Fire alarm: OL = 80 < 300 for Group A — not triggered.
- BUT existing building may already be sprinklered for other reasons (height/area, S-2 garage, etc.). If so, sprinkler heads in new space mandatory per CFC 1103 / NFPA 13.
- Mixed-occupancy: depending on adjacent tenants, demising walls need 1-hr separation (B ↔ A-3 sprinklered).

### Decision B: M → A-2 in 5,200-sf tenant (former retail boutique → café)

- Sprinkler: A-2 threshold = 5,000 sf OR OL >= 100. Fire area is 5,200 sf → SPRINKLERS REQUIRED. Per CEBC 1011.2.1, installation within the area of change + all non-fire-wall-separated areas. **ROUTES TO california-restaurant-ti.**

### Decision C: B office TI in 18,000-sf tenant, full remodel, no occupancy change

- Sprinkler: B doesn't have an occupancy-load sprinkler trigger; existing condition controls.
- Fire alarm: OL @ 150 gross = 120 occupants. < 500 combined. If 18,000 sf is on a single level of exit discharge, no Group-B fire alarm trigger.
- Mixed-occupancy: same-occupancy TI → no 508 issue.
- BUT alteration level may trigger sprinkler retrofit obligations via CEBC Ch.8/9 if the building has known fire-protection deficiencies.

### Decision D: S-1 warehouse partial → B office (carve out 4,000 sf office + 14,000 sf S-1)

- Mixed occupancy: B + S-1.
- B occupant load 4,000 sf / 150 = 27. S-1 load 14,000 / 300 = 47. Combined < 500. No fire alarm trigger.
- Sprinkler: S-1 is 14,000 sf — under 12,000-sf single fire area limit only if separated. Without 2-hr+ fire walls splitting it, the existing single-fire-area S-1 may already require sprinklers (depends on building history). If new B carve-out is sprinklered along with the rest per existing system, no add.
- Mixed-occupancy separation: B ↔ S-1 sprinklered = 1 hr per Table 508.4. Need 1-hr fire barrier between office and warehouse.
