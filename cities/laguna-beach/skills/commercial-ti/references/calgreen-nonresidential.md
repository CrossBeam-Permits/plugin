# CALGreen Nonresidential — Commercial TI

The California Green Building Standards Code (CALGreen, Title 24 Part 11) is the only state green-building code that is mandatory by law. For commercial TI, CALGreen Chapter 5 (Nonresidential Mandatory Measures) applies on a graduated trigger:

- **Section 301.3** scope: full Ch. 5 mandatory measures apply to new construction, additions >= 1,000 sf, or **alterations with permit valuation >= $200K**.
- **Section 301.3.2** universal trigger: §5.408 (waste diversion) applies to ALL permitted alterations regardless of valuation.
- **Section 301.3.1** + Civil Code §1101.1 retroactive trigger: plumbing-fixture-only updates have separate triggers under CA Civil Code (commercial real property).

This is the most-frequently-missed code in small TI submittals — applicants often skip CALGreen forms entirely.

---

## 1. Scope and Triggers (§301.3)

> [BSC-CG] The provisions of individual sections of Chapter 5 apply to newly constructed buildings, building additions of 1,000 square feet or greater, and/or building alterations with a permit valuation of $200,000 or above (for occupancies within the authority of California Building Standards Commission). Code sections relevant to additions and alterations shall only apply to the portions of the building being added or altered within the scope of the permitted work. (Part 11 §301.3)

The CALGreen text uses [N] banners for new-construction-only sections and [A] banners for sections applying to additions/alterations. Sections without banners apply to both.

### §301.3.1 — Plumbing-Fixture-Only Alterations

When the only alteration is plumbing fixture updates, the CCR §1101.1 et seq. retroactive fixture-replacement obligation can trigger compliance (CA Civil Code §1101.3 defines "commercial real property" subject to this rule). Effective 2014 onward, certain commercial real property must have noncompliant plumbing fixtures replaced under specific circumstances. Reviewers should flag if the project is fixture-only and the building has not previously been brought into CCR §1101.1 compliance.

### §301.3.2 — Universal Waste Diversion

> The requirements of Section 5.408 shall be required for additions and alterations whenever a permit is required for work.

**This is the universal trigger.** Even a $50K TI is subject to §5.408 (65% C&D waste diversion). This is the most-missed CALGreen requirement.

### §301.4 — Public schools / community colleges have separate triggers (not applicable to private commercial TI).

## 2. Chapter 5 Mandatory Measures Overview

### Division 5.1 — Planning and Design
- §5.103 Site selection
- §5.104 Site preservation
- §5.105 Deconstruction and reuse of existing structures
- §5.106 Site development (storm water control, light pollution, EVCS infrastructure)

For TI, Division 5.1 is mostly relevant on additions. Pure interior alterations rarely hit 5.1.

### Division 5.3 — Water Efficiency and Conservation

| Section | Requirement |
|---|---|
| §5.303 Indoor water use | Max flow rates: water closets <= 1.28 gpf (or dual-flush 0.95 avg), urinals <= 0.125 gpf for wall-mount or 0.5 gpf for other, public lavatory <= 0.5 gpm, public showers <= 1.8 gpm, residential showers <= 1.8 gpm at 80 psi, kitchen faucets <= 1.8 gpm |
| §5.304 Outdoor water use | Landscape water budget (MWELO) where altered/added landscape |
| §5.305 Water reuse systems | Where included in scope |

For TI replacing or adding fixtures, all new fixtures must meet §5.303 flow limits. Existing fixtures that remain are subject to CCR §1101.1 retroactive replacement.

### Division 5.4 — Material Conservation and Resource Efficiency

| Section | Requirement |
|---|---|
| §5.407 Water resistance and moisture management | Exterior wall envelope, flashing, waterproofing |
| **§5.408 Construction waste reduction (UNIVERSAL TRIGGER)** | Recycle / salvage >= 65% of nonhazardous C&D waste via §5.408.1.1 plan, §5.408.1.2 waste management company, OR §5.408.1.3 ≤2 lb/sf threshold |
| §5.408.1.4 Documentation | Documentation provided to enforcing agency demonstrating compliance |
| **§5.408.2 Universal waste** [A] | For additions >=1,000 sf OR alterations with permit valuation >=$200K: verify prohibited universal-waste materials (fluorescent lamps & ballasts, mercury thermostats, batteries, e-waste, CRT glass/tubes, aerosol cans) are diverted from landfills; include a list of prohibited universal-waste materials in the construction documents |
| §5.408.3 Excavated soil and land-clearing debris | 100% of trees, stumps, rocks, vegetation and soils resulting primarily from land clearing reused or recycled |
| §5.409 Life cycle assessment | Where elected |
| §5.410 Building maintenance & operation | O&M manual, commissioning documentation |

**§5.408 detail:**

> Recycle and/or salvage for reuse a minimum of 65 percent of the nonhazardous construction and demolition waste in accordance with Section 5.408.1.1, 5.408.1.2 or 5.408.1.3; or meet a local construction and demolition waste management ordinance, whichever is more stringent. (§5.408.1)

Three compliance paths:
1. **Construction Waste Management Plan (§5.408.1.1)** — Identifies materials to be diverted, source-separated vs single-stream, diversion facilities, calculation method (weight OR volume, not both).
2. **Waste Management Company (§5.408.1.2)** — Use a waste hauler providing verifiable documentation of the 65% diversion.
3. **Waste Stream Reduction Alternative (§5.408.1.3)** — Combined new-construction disposal not exceeding 2 lb/sf of building area is deemed to meet the 65% requirement.

**Exceptions to §5.408.1.1 and §5.408.1.2:**
1. Excavated soil and land-clearing debris.
2. Alternate waste-reduction methods if facilities don't exist (worked out with local agencies).
3. Demolition waste meeting local ordinance OR calculated against local market.

**Local ordinance wins if more stringent.** Many CA cities have stricter C&D rules (e.g., 75% diversion).

### Division 5.5 — Environmental Quality

| Section | Requirement |
|---|---|
| §5.503 Fireplaces | Direct-vent appliances, sealed combustion |
| **§5.504 Pollutant control** | **VOC limits on adhesives, sealants, paints, coatings, carpet/cushion/adhesive, resilient flooring, composite wood** — §5.504.4 series |
| §5.504.4.1 Adhesives, sealants, caulks | Per SCAQMD Rule 1168 + CARB-published limits |
| §5.504.4.3 Paints / coatings | Per CARB Suggested Control Measure 2007 / SCAQMD Rule 1113 limits |
| §5.504.4.4 Carpet systems | CRI Green Label Plus |
| §5.504.4.5 Composite wood products | CARB ATCM 93120 (formaldehyde) |
| §5.504.4.6 Resilient flooring | FloorScore / CDPH Standard Method v1.1 (Spec 01350) certified |
| §5.504.5.3 Filters | MERV 13 minimum on return-air openings of operating HVAC serving regularly-occupied space during construction (labeling per §5.504.5.3.1) |
| §5.505 Indoor moisture control | Moisture-control measures during construction |
| §5.506 Indoor air quality | Construction-stage IAQ management; flush-out OR air testing prior to occupancy |
| §5.507 Environmental comfort | Acoustic performance |
| §5.508 Outdoor air quality | Refrigerant replacement / disposal |

For commercial TI, §5.504 (pollutant control) and §5.506 (IAQ) are the high-frequency triggers. Reviewer checks specs / product cut-sheets for VOC compliance.

## 3. Common Commercial-TI CALGreen Triggers

### Trigger A: $30K paint-and-carpet refresh (no permit valuation > $200K, but permitted)

- §5.408 waste diversion **REQUIRED** (universal per §301.3.2). C&D waste plan submitted.
- Section 5 paint/carpet products → §5.504 VOC limits **RECOMMENDED** even though full §5.504 doesn't trigger.
- §5.303 fixture flow → N/A (no fixtures touched).

### Trigger B: $250K full office TI

- Full Chapter 5 applies (§301.3 — alteration permit valuation >= $200K).
- §5.408 waste diversion (universal + part of full set).
- §5.303 indoor water use — any altered/new fixtures comply.
- §5.504 pollutant control — adhesives, sealants, paints, flooring, composite wood VOC limits.
- §5.504.5.3 MERV 13 filtration during construction.
- §5.506 IAQ — flush-out or air-test before occupancy.
- §5.410 Building maintenance — O&M manual delivered.

### Trigger C: 1,500-sf addition to existing tenant ($180K)

- Addition > 1,000 sf → full Ch. 5 applies (§301.3).
- §5.408 waste diversion.
- All §5.5 IEQ measures apply to the new portion.
- The portion of existing building not within the addition is outside scope.

### Trigger D: Plumbing-fixture-only replacement on a 1995-vintage 25,000-sf retail tenant

- §301.3.1 + CCR §1101.1 retroactive trigger likely — verify the building is a "noncompliant" property under Civil Code §1101.3 and whether prior remodel triggered §1101.1.
- §5.303 indoor water use applies to new fixtures regardless.

## 4. Voluntary Tiers (§304) — NOT Mandatory

CALGreen Appendix A5 contains **voluntary** Tier 1 and Tier 2 measures. These are NEVER mandatory unless the local jurisdiction adopts them in a local ordinance. If the applicant claims Tier 1 or Tier 2 compliance voluntarily, all the relevant A5 measures must be documented.

---

## 5. Local Ordinances

Many CA cities (e.g., San Francisco, Berkeley, Santa Monica, San Jose, Pasadena) have local CALGreen "reach codes" that:
- Lower the $200K alteration trigger,
- Raise the C&D diversion percentage (often to 75% or 80%),
- Require electrification (no new gas appliances),
- Require EV-ready infrastructure beyond §5.106.

This skill speaks only for state-level CALGreen. The city overlay skill captures local reach codes.
