# Laguna Beach — WUI / VHFHSZ Requirements for Commercial TI

**Authority:** LBMC Chapter 14.70 (Wildland-Urban Interface Code) with extensive local amendments. LBMC §14.50.120 (Class A roof citywide). 2025 California WUI Code (CWUIC) adopted with these local amendments effective 2026-01-01.

**Critical context:** Laguna Beach is predominantly within a Very High Fire Hazard Severity Zone (VHFHSZ) — the citywide coverage figure is in `../../city-reference/SKILL.md`. Most commercial corridors — Forest Ave, Heisler Park area, Coast Hwy frontages, Laguna Canyon Rd, Aliso/Wood Canyons-adjacent commercial — are inside VHFHSZ. Verify parcel status against the CAL FIRE FHSZ map and the city's GIS layer; the default assumption is "VHFHSZ unless proven otherwise."

**The most important citywide rule:** Class A roof is required for **any** roof or reroof on **any** building in Laguna Beach, VHFHSZ or not (§14.50.120 + §14.70.020).

---

## 1. Scope (§14.70.015)

§14.70.015 amends CWUIC §101.2:

> "Applies to building materials, systems and/or assemblies used in the exterior design and construction of **new buildings, additions, alterations, and repairs** located within a wildland-urban interface (WUI) area."
>
> **Exception:** "Minor repairs or replacement of exterior materials may be done with the same materials as exists on the building."

For commercial TI, the operative test is: **does the scope touch exterior building materials, systems, or assemblies?** If yes, Ch. 14.70 applies unless the work is minor like-for-like replacement.

Examples of commercial-TI scope that triggers Ch. 14.70:

- Storefront replacement (new framing, glazing system, doors)
- Awning / canopy replacement or addition
- Stucco patching beyond minor repair
- New exterior siding (any portion of envelope)
- New windows (Group U commercial windows in VHFHSZ — see §14.70.035)
- New skylights (see §14.70.040)
- New roof or reroof (see §14.50.120 / §14.70.020)
- New rooftop mechanical equipment (vent openings — see §14.70.025)

Examples that do NOT trigger (minor like-for-like):

- Repainting in similar paint system
- Replacing broken stucco patch with same stucco
- Replacing individual broken roof tiles with same tile (Class A)

## 2. Class A Roof — Citywide (§14.50.120 + §14.70.020)

LBMC **§14.50.120** amends CBC §1505:

> "A Class A roof assembly or roof covering is the minimum class of roofing that can be applied on **any building in the City** for either new roofing or reroofing."

This applies **citywide, regardless of VHFHSZ status**. Any commercial TI involving roof work — including patching beyond minor repair, mechanical-equipment penetrations, or skylight installation — must verify Class A roof assembly compliance.

Class A roof systems include:

- Built-up roof (BUR) with mineral surfacing per UL 790
- Modified bitumen with Class A surfacing
- Concrete or clay tile (most common)
- Standing-seam metal (with Class A underlayment)
- Class A asphalt composition shingles
- Stone-coated metal

PVC, TPO, and EPDM single-ply membrane roofs require Class A assembly listing (UL 790) — verify the listing on the cut sheet at plan check.

## 3. Noncombustible / Ignition-Resistant Exterior Walls (§14.70.030)

For commercial buildings in VHFHSZ, exterior walls must be:

- **Noncombustible material**; OR
- **Ignition-resistant building material** labeled for exterior use; OR
- **Fire-retardant-treated wood** per CBC §2303.2; OR
- **Qualified fire-retardant-treated shingles** (where shingles are part of a wall assembly).

Common compliant assemblies:

- Stucco (3-coat system over fire-rated sheathing) — noncombustible
- Fiber-cement siding (Hardie or equivalent) — ignition-resistant, labeled
- Cementitious panel systems
- Metal panel systems
- Brick / stone / CMU veneer

Non-compliant in VHFHSZ:

- Untreated wood siding (lap or board-and-batten) on a TI exterior wall replacement
- Vinyl siding
- Untreated cedar shingles

---

## 4. Vinyl Windows (§14.70.035)

Vinyl windows in VHFHSZ must have:

- **Welded corners** (not mechanically fastened)
- **Metal reinforcing** in the interlock area

This applies to any new or replacement vinyl windows in commercial TIs touching the exterior envelope. Aluminum, steel, fiberglass, and wood-clad windows are not subject to this specific amendment but must still meet Chapter 7A noncombustibility / ignition resistance.

---

## 5. Skylights (§14.70.040)

Skylights in VHFHSZ must be **glass only**. **Plastic skylights and plastic sloped glazing are prohibited.**

This is a frequent commercial-TI issue when daylighting is added to interior retail or office spaces. Use tempered or laminated glass per CBC §2407.

---

## 6. Vent Openings (§14.70.025)

Each individual ventilation opening into attics, eaves, rafter cavities, or underfloor spaces must be:

- **≤144 sq in** (each individual opening); OR
- Protected by **1⅜" solid-core wood door** or equivalent (the "equivalent" is typically a 1/8" mesh ember screen behind a louvered cover, or a listed ember-resistant vent like Brandguard / Vulcan).

Commercial TIs adding rooftop mechanical equipment, exhaust fans, kitchen hood penetrations, or makeup-air openings must verify each penetration meets the 144-sq-in limit or carries listed ember protection. Wall louvers serving HVAC plenums are commonly oversized in non-fire-zone designs — these require ember protection in VHFHSZ.

---

## 7. Defensible Space (§14.70.060 + CFC §304.1.3.2)

All existing habitable buildings/structures in VHFHSZ must maintain defensible space per the **August 2021 Defensible Space Guideline**.

This is a maintenance obligation, **not tied to permit status**. However, commercial-TI submittals that include site improvements (landscape, fencing, parking) must show defensible-space compliance on the site plan.

Defensible-space zones (per CFC §304.1.3.2 and the city guideline):

- **Zone 0** (0–5 ft from structure) — ember-resistant, no combustible vegetation
- **Zone 1** (5–30 ft) — lean, clean, green — limited combustible vegetation
- **Zone 2** (30–100 ft) — reduced fuel — spacing and pruning requirements

---

## 8. Fuel Modification (§14.70.070 + CFC §324)

For new construction or major remodels in **Fuel Modification designation areas**:

- Preliminary fuel modification plans submitted concurrent with the tentative map (or with planning application for non-subdivision projects).
- Final fuel modification plans approved by the Fire Code Official **before building permit issuance**.

For commercial TI, fuel modification is typically only triggered when the TI is part of a larger entitlement (new building or major reconstruction) on a parcel in a Fuel Modification designation area. Pure interior TI does not usually trigger.

## 9. Required Findings Output

For each commercial TI WUI review, output:

```markdown
**VHFHSZ status:** In VHFHSZ | Not in VHFHSZ | Verify via CAL FIRE map / city GIS
**Exterior scope present:** Yes (describe) | No
**Minor like-for-like replacement claim:** Valid (same material) | Invalid
**Class A roof (§14.50.120 — citywide):** PASS | FAIL | FLAG | N/A (no roof work)
**Exterior wall material (§14.70.030):** PASS | FAIL | FLAG | N/A
**Vinyl window welded-corner / metal reinforcing (§14.70.035):** PASS | FAIL | N/A
**Skylights — glass only (§14.70.040):** PASS | FAIL | N/A
**Vent openings ≤144 sq in or ember-protected (§14.70.025):** PASS | FAIL | FLAG | N/A
**Defensible-space site plan (§14.70.060):** PASS | FAIL | FLAG | N/A
**Fuel modification (§14.70.070):** Required | N/A
```

Cite §14.50.120 / §14.70.020 for Class A roof citywide, §14.70.015 for scope, §14.70.030 for walls, §14.70.035 for vinyl windows, §14.70.040 for skylights, §14.70.025 for vents, §14.70.060 for defensible space, §14.70.070 for fuel modification.
