# Accessibility Upgrades — Commercial TI

California amends federal ADA via CBC Chapter 11B. For commercial TIs, two questions drive the entire accessibility review:

1. **Element compliance (11B-202.3)** — Does each altered element comply with Division 2 of Chapter 11B?
2. **Path of travel (11B-202.4)** — Has the primary accessible path from the public way to the altered area been made compliant — entrance, route, restroom, drinking fountain, signage, telephones — subject to the 20% disproportionality cap?

Both are mandatory. Both are enforced by the local Building Department. Both are *also* federal ADA Title III obligations independent of California compliance.

---

## 1. Element Compliance (CBC 11B-202.3)

> Where existing elements or spaces are altered, each altered element or space shall comply with the applicable requirements of Division 2, including Section 11B-202.4. (CBC 11B-202.3)

### Three exceptions to 11B-202.3 alone:

1. **Reserved.**
2. **Technically infeasible.** When the enforcing authority determines compliance is technically infeasible, alterations provide equivalent facilitation OR comply to the maximum extent feasible. The infeasibility finding must be recorded in the agency's files. (11B-202.3 Exception 2)
3. Residential dwelling units not required to be accessible. (Not applicable to commercial TI.)

### Prohibited reduction (11B-202.3.1)

An alteration that decreases (or has the effect of decreasing) accessibility below current new-construction requirements is prohibited.

### Extent of application (11B-202.3.2)

An alteration of an existing element does not impose accessibility requirements *greater* than required for new construction.

### Alteration of single elements aggregated (11B-202.3.3)

If multiple single-element alterations together amount to altering an entire room/space, the **entire room/space** must be made accessible.

---

## 2. Path of Travel (CBC 11B-202.4)

> When alterations or additions are made to existing buildings or facilities, an accessible path of travel to the specific area of alteration or addition shall be provided. (CBC 11B-202.4)

The primary accessible path of travel includes:

1. **A primary entrance** to the building or facility,
2. **Toilet and bathing facilities** serving the area,
3. **Drinking fountains** serving the area,
4. **Public telephones** serving the area, and
5. **Signs.**

### The 10 Exceptions (11B-202.4 Exceptions 1-10)

| # | Exception | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Residential dwelling units | Use 11B-233.3.4.2 instead |
| 2 | Prior-edition compliance | If the PoT element was upgraded to the **immediately preceding** edition's standards, not required to refit for incremental changes in current edition. Limited to one edition back only — not cumulative. |
| 3 | Single-element accessibility scope projects | If the entire project is altering ONE entrance, ONE existing toilet facility, ONE existing elevator, ONE existing stair, or ONE existing handrail — limited to that scope only, no PoT obligation |
| 4 | Barrier-removal-only projects under federal ADA / 11B | Project is purely barrier-removal (ramps, curb cuts, repositioning fixtures, widening doors, installing offset hinges, grab bars in toilet stalls, etc.) — limited to that scope, no PoT |
| 5 | Parking lot resurfacing / restriping | Limited to scope, no PoT |
| 6 | Signs / identification devices | Limited to scope, no PoT |
| 7 | HVAC / reroof / electrical (not affecting switches/receptacles) / cosmetic painting / computer terminals / office equipment | No PoT unless they affect usability of the building |
| 8 | **20% Disproportionality Cap (THE KEY CAP)** | See below |
| 9 | **Multistory privately-funded buildings** without elevators (3+ stories, 3,000+ sf/floor) | Areas above/below ground floor subject to 20% provisions even if project exceeds threshold. Lists office buildings, physicians' offices, shopping centers, and other 3-story buildings where reasonable services available on accessible level. |
| 10 | **EVCS installation only** | When the alteration is solely for EVCS installation — if vehicle fueling/parking is a primary function, PoT applies to max extent feasible without exceeding 20% of the EVCS work cost. If not primary function, no PoT obligation. |

### Exception 8 — The "20% Rule" Deep Dive

This is the most-cited and most-misunderstood exception in commercial TI. Verbatim summary:

**Case A: Adjusted construction cost <= valuation threshold**
- PoT compliance is REQUIRED.
- The cost of PoT compliance is **capped at 20%** of the adjusted construction cost.
- Provide as much PoT compliance as possible without exceeding 20%.

**Case B: Adjusted construction cost > valuation threshold**
- Full PoT compliance is REQUIRED.
- The enforcing agency MAY find unreasonable hardship in writing, in which case full compliance is not required.
- Even with hardship finding, PoT spend must be **not less than 20%** of adjusted construction cost.
- Hardship finding goes into agency files, subject to Chapter 1 §1.9.1.5 (Special Conditions for Persons with Disabilities Requiring Appeals Action Ratification).

**Aggregation rule:** If a PoT element was previously skipped during an alteration in the prior 3 years, the cumulative cost across all those alterations counts toward determining disproportionality.

**Priority order** when partial compliance is provided (top-down):
1. Accessible entrance.
2. Accessible route to altered area.
3. At least one accessible restroom (per sex OR one unisex).
4. Accessible telephones.
5. Accessible drinking fountains.
6. Other (parking, signs, storage, alarms).

**Adjusted construction cost EXCLUDES the cost of PoT alterations.** Don't double-count.

### The Valuation Threshold

The valuation threshold is defined in **CBC Chapter 2 (Definitions, §202)** and is **published annually by the Division of the State Architect (DSA)** for use with §11B-202.4 Exception 8. The figure is the January 1981 base of $50,000 escalated by the ENR 20-Cities Construction Cost Index each January, so it rises every year. The **current DSA figure is $209,208.00, effective for projects submitted after January 19, 2026** (2025 was $203,611.00; 2024 was $200,399.00). DSA republishes each January. Confirm the current-year figure against the DSA publication before finalizing PoT scope; do not rely on a stale number carried from a prior project.

**Reviewer guidance:** When the applicant cites the threshold, demand a citation to the *current year's* DSA publication, not a stale figure carried from a prior project.

## 3. Element-Specific Requirements Triggered by Alteration

When 11B-202.3 element compliance applies, the altered element must meet Division 2 standards. The most frequent commercial-TI triggers:

### Restrooms (CBC 11B-213, 11B-603, 11B-604)

If a restroom is altered:
- Mounting heights, fixture spacing, maneuvering clearances must meet 11B-603/604.
- Grab bars per 11B-604.5, 11B-609.
- 60-inch turning circle per 11B-304 OR T-turn per 11B-304.3.
- Lavatory clearance, knee/toe per 11B-606.
- Door hardware per 11B-309.4 (single-effort operation).
- Pipes insulated per 11B-606.5.

### Accessible Parking (CBC 11B-208 + Table 11B-208.2)

If parking is provided or restriped:

| Total Spaces | Min Accessible |
|---|---|
| 1-25 | 1 |
| 26-50 | 2 |
| 51-75 | 3 |
| 76-100 | 4 |
| 101-150 | 5 |
| 151-200 | 6 |
| 201-300 | 7 |
| 301-400 | 8 |
| 401-500 | 9 |
| 501-1,000 | 2% of total |
| 1,001+ | 20 + 1 for each 100 (or fraction) over 1,000 |

**Van spaces (11B-208.2.4):** 1 of every 6 accessible spaces (or fraction of 6) must be a van space.

**Hospital outpatient (11B-208.2.1):** 10% of patient/visitor spaces.
**Rehab facilities / outpatient PT (11B-208.2.2):** 20% of patient/visitor spaces.

**Location (11B-208.3.1):** Accessible spaces on the shortest accessible route to an accessible entrance.

### Accessible Routes (CBC 11B-206, 11B-402)

Continuous unobstructed path connecting all accessible elements. Slope <= 1:20 (else it's a ramp), cross-slope <= 1:48, width >= 36" (48" at turns), maneuvering clearances at all doors per 11B-404.2.4.

### Signs (CBC 11B-216, 11B-703)

Identification signs (room ID, restroom signs, exit signs) require: tactile characters, raised >= 1/32", non-glare, contrast, character height per 11B-703.2-.5, Braille (Grade II California Braille per 11B-703.3), International Symbol of Accessibility where applicable.

Pictograms in restrooms per California-specific 11B-703.7.2.6.

### Drinking Fountains (CBC 11B-211, 11B-602)

Where drinking fountains provided, at least two required: one for wheelchair user (max 36" spout AFF), one for standing user. Bottle filling stations counted per 11B-211.

### Dining/Work Surfaces (CBC 11B-226, 11B-902)

In dining facilities or work surface areas, 5% of dining surfaces (min 1) must be accessible per 11B-226.1. Accessible counter section >= 28" long, 34" max AFF, knee/toe clearance per 11B-306.

### Sales / Service Counters (CBC 11B-227, 11B-904)

Cash wraps, customer-service counters: at least one section >=36" long and <=36" AFF (with optional max 38" AFF when integrated with adjacent counter heights for "associated dining surfaces" — see 11B-904.4).

## 4. Federal ADA Title III — Parallel Track

Federal ADA Title III (42 USC 12181-12189; regulations at 28 CFR Part 36) overlays on California 11B for public accommodations and commercial facilities. Key differences a reviewer must catch:

- **28 CFR 36.403** is the federal "20% path of travel" rule. CA 11B-202.4 Exception 8 was written to track it but California adds the valuation threshold concept. **Federal rule applies regardless of CA threshold.**
- **Primary function area concept (28 CFR 36.403(b))**: Federal PoT obligation hinges on alterations to a "primary function area" — a major activity of the facility. California 11B-202.4 doesn't use the exact phrase but the same idea drives the EVCS exception (11B-202.4 Exception 10).
- **Federal readily achievable barrier removal (42 USC 12182(b)(2)(A)(iv))**: An ongoing obligation on operators of public accommodations to remove architectural barriers when "readily achievable" — independent of any alteration permit. The Building Department doesn't enforce this directly, but a reviewer should note in the corrections letter that compliance with 11B does not relieve federal ADA obligations.
- **Local jurisdiction CANNOT waive federal ADA.** Even a written CA unreasonable-hardship finding does not relieve the project owner of federal ADA exposure.

## 5. Existing-Building Carve-Outs (CBC 11B-202.5, Appendix B)

**Qualified Historic Buildings (11B-202.5):** Comply with State Historical Building Code (Part 8, Title 24) instead — different, less-prescriptive standards.

**CEBC Appendix B** ("Supplementary Accessibility Requirements for Existing Buildings and Facilities") — supplementary guidance for technical infeasibility findings.
