# Structural Alterations & Existing Building Code

Every commercial TI is an alteration to an existing building, so the California Existing Building Code (Part 10) applies. CBC Chapter 34 was REMOVED from California — all existing-building rules now live in CEBC Part 10. If a submittal cites "CBC 3401" it is incorrect and the citation must be reframed to CEBC.

This reference walks through the CEBC pipeline: classify the alteration (Ch. 6), apply level-specific requirements (Ch. 7/8/9), and overlay change-of-occupancy requirements where applicable (Ch. 10).

---

## 1. Classification of Work (CEBC Chapter 6)

### Alteration Levels (CEBC 602-604)

| Level | Trigger (CEBC) | Applicable Chapters |
|---|---|---|
| **Level 1** (602) | Removal/replacement/covering of existing materials with same-purpose materials. Cosmetic, like-for-like. | Ch. 7 only |
| **Level 2** (603) | Addition/elimination of doors/windows, reconfiguration/extension of systems, additional equipment. Work area <= 50% of building area. | Ch. 7 + Ch. 8 |
| **Level 3** (604) | Same scope as Level 2, but work area > 50% of building area. | Ch. 7 + Ch. 8 + Ch. 9 |

**Work area** (CEBC 601.2) must be clearly identified on the construction documents.

**Movable fixtures exception (603.1):** Movement or addition of non-fixed and movable fixtures, cases, racks, counters, and partitions not over 5'9" in height is NOT a Level 2 alteration. (Common in mercantile remerchandising / office furniture rearrangement.)

### Change of Occupancy (CEBC 605)

If the project is a change of occupancy (per CEBC 202 + Ch. 10), Chapter 10 requirements apply in addition to the alteration-level requirements (Ch. 7-9).

### Additions (CEBC 606)

Additions (work that increases building area, height, or number of stories) go through Chapter 11. Most commercial TIs are not additions; this reference focuses on alterations + change of occupancy.

### Scope Exclusions (CEBC 601.1)

Use of Ch. 6-11 is NOT permitted in:
- H occupancies
- I, L, R-2.1, R-3.1 occupancies
- High-rise buildings
- BSC-listed occupancies/applications

For excluded scopes, default to current CBC entirely. For commercial TI this excludes hospitals, jails, residential care, lab suites, and high-rises — out of scope for this skill.

## 2. Level 1 Alterations (CEBC Chapter 7)

Cosmetic / like-for-like work. Applies to ALL alteration levels (Ch. 7 is the floor).

| Requirement | Section |
|---|---|
| Building shall not be made less safe than the existing condition | 701.2 |
| New materials must comply with current CBC | 302.4 |
| Existing materials may remain unless dangerous to life/health/welfare | 302.3 |
| Alterations maintain the existing level of fire protection | 703.1 |

---

**Discipline routing note:** CEBC §§703–705 do not establish a combined requirement to maintain or improve plumbing-fixture, electrical-service, and ventilation capacity. Check plumbing, electrical, and mechanical requirements separately under the provisions applicable to the project; do not apply a combined capacity criterion without a direct, scoped source.

## 3. Level 2 Alterations (CEBC Chapter 8)

Includes everything in Level 1 PLUS Chapter 8 requirements. Most commercial TIs fall here.

### Highlights — Building Elements, Fire Protection, and Egress (Ch. 8 Sections 802-804)

| Requirement | Trigger | Section |
|---|---|---|
| Vertical openings enclosed with min 1-hr fire-resistance | Existing vertical openings in the work area, subject to listed exceptions | 802.2 |
| Exception | Certain low-hazard, sprinklered, or limited-story vertical openings need not be enclosed | 802.2.1 exceptions |
| Smoke barriers comply with current CBC | Group I-2 sleeping-room stories with >30 care recipients | 802.3 |
| Interior finish in exits and corridors complies with current CBC | New interior finish in exits/corridors of the work area | 802.4 |
| Fire-resistance ratings | Approved review after automatic sprinklers are added throughout | 802.6 |
| Sprinklers per CBC/CFC 903 | Required where Chapter 8 fire-protection provisions trigger them | 803.2 |
| Fire alarm per CFC 907 and 1103.7 | Required where Chapter 8 fire-protection provisions trigger it | 803.4 |
| Means of egress: corridor walls, dead-end corridors, exit signs, illumination — required upgrades | Per detailed list in 804 | 804.1-804.14 |
| Existing handrails compliance | If stairs altered | 804.13 |
| Existing guards | If altered in the egress path | 804.14 |

### Highlights — Fire Protection (Ch. 8 Section 803)

Automatic sprinkler and fire-alarm obligations for Level 2 work are in §803. Fire-resistance rating review for building elements is §802.6.

### Highlights — Structural (Ch. 8 Sections 801.4 and 805)

| Requirement | Trigger | Section |
|---|---|---|
| Existing gravity load-carrying members altered → must meet current CBC | Design dead, live, or snow load increases >5%, or gravity capacity decreases | 805.2 |
| Existing lateral load-resisting elements affected | Lateral loads increase, prohibited irregularity is created, or lateral capacity decreases; <=10% DCR carve-out may apply | 805.3 and 805.3 Exception 1 |
| Rooftop equipment / PV lateral carve-outs | Qualifying rooftop equipment or PV dead-load additions | 805.3 Exceptions 2-3 |
| New structural members and connections | New work complies with current CBC; alternative design criteria may be used where allowed | 801.4 and 801.4 Exception 6 |
| Voluntary lateral-system upgrade allowed | Owner option, no penalty if §805.4 conditions are met | 805.4 |

### Highlights — Electrical, Mechanical, and Energy (Ch. 8 Sections 806-809)

Electrical work is §806, mechanical ventilation is §807, §808 is reserved in California, and energy conservation is §809. The California CEBC Chapter 8 map ends there.

## 4. Level 3 Alterations (CEBC Chapter 9)

Includes Level 1 + Level 2 + Chapter 9 requirements. Triggered when work area > 50% of building area.

### Highlights — Fire Protection, Means of Egress, Structural, and Energy (Ch. 9 Sections 904-907)

| Requirement | Section |
|---|---|
| Egress lighting and exit signs from highest work area to exit discharge per CBC | 905.2-905.3 |
| Sprinklers per CBC for new construction | 904.1 |
| Fire alarm per CBC for new construction | 904.2 |
| Structural elements — gravity & lateral load evaluation | 805.2-805.3, 906 |
| Means of egress — current CBC for new construction | 905 |
| Energy conservation — current CBC for new construction | 907 |

Level 3 essentially treats the work area as new construction for systems, with limited grandfathering of existing elements (e.g., existing stairways with riser/tread mismatches per 1011.5 exceptions).

## 5. Change of Occupancy (CEBC Chapter 10)

When the project is also a change of occupancy, the work must meet alteration-level requirements (Ch. 7/8/9 by work-area percentage) PLUS Chapter 10. The Chapter 10 obligations follow.

### Sections 1002-1011 Overview

| Section | Topic |
|---|---|
| 1002 | Special use & occupancy — Ch.4 CBC compliance |
| 1003 | Building elements & materials (defer to 1011) |
| 1004 | Fire protection (sprinklers / fire alarm thresholds — see §1011.2) |
| 1005 | Means of egress (defer to 1011) |
| 1006 | Structural — gravity loads, wind/snow risk category, seismic, access to Risk Category IV |
| 1007 | Electrical — special occupancies, unsafe conditions, service upgrade, outlets |
| 1008 | Mechanical — kitchen exhaust / increased ventilation per CMC |
| 1009 | Plumbing — increased demand, food-handling, interceptors, chemical waste |
| 1010 | Light & ventilation per CBC for new occupancy |
| 1011 | Change of occupancy classification (master section) |

### Structural Triggers (CEBC 1006) — Often Missed

**1006.1 Live loads:** Structural elements carrying tributary live loads from change-of-occupancy area must meet current CBC §1607 design loads. Exception: structural elements with DCR within 5% of previously-approved design loads.

**1006.2 Wind/snow loads:** If change results in higher Risk Category, building meets CBC §1608/§1609 for new RC.
- Exception: if new occupancy area < 10% of building area. Cumulative effect over time considered.

**1006.3 Seismic:** If change results in higher Risk Category OR change from S/U to any other occupancy (other than S/U), lateral force-resisting system must comply with CEBC §304.3.1 for new RC.
- If change results in RC IV + SDC D/F, nonstructural components meet CBC §1613 OR ASCE 41 with operational nonstructural performance at BSE-1N hazard level.
- **Exception 1:** Change from RC I/II to III where SDS < 0.33 — exempt.
- **Exception 2:** New occupancy area < 10% of building, not changing from S/U, not RC IV — exempt.
- **Exception 4:** Change from S/U with no RC change — §304.3.2 permitted.

**1006.4 Access to Risk Category IV:** Any structure providing operational access to an adjacent RC IV structure (as a result of the change) itself complies with §1608/§1609 + §304.3.1. Where access < 10 ft from interior lot line or another structure, falling-debris protection required.

### Electrical Triggers (CEBC 1007)

For changes to one of the following special occupancies (CEC Articles 500-516, 518-525): hazardous locations, commercial garages, aircraft hangars, gasoline/service stations, bulk storage plants, spray application/dipping/coating, places of assembly, theaters / audience areas, motion picture / TV studios, projectors, ag buildings — wiring/equipment meets current CEC.

**1007.2 Unsafe conditions:** Must correct without requiring full CEC compliance.

**1007.3 Service upgrade:** Electrical service must be upgraded to meet CEC for the new occupancy.

**1007.4 Outlets:** Number of outlets must comply with CEC for the new occupancy.

### Mechanical Triggers (CEBC 1008)

Where new occupancy is subject to different kitchen exhaust requirements OR increased mechanical ventilation per CMC → comply with the respective CMC provisions.

### Plumbing Triggers (CEBC 1009)

- **1009.1 Increased demand:** Different/higher CPC fixture / water supply requirements → comply with intent. Exception: only when occupant load increases by > 20% does the story require fixtures based on increased OL.
- **1009.2 Food-handling:** Existing sanitary waste lines above food/drink prep/storage areas must be panned/protected. New drainage lines NOT installed above food areas; must be protected per CPC.
- **1009.3 Interceptor:** Required if new occupancy produces grease/oil-laden waste. (Restaurants — `california-restaurant-ti`.)
- **1009.4 Chemical wastes:** If incompatible with existing piping, neutralize before drainage system OR change pipe material. No discharge to public sewer without sewage authority approval.

### Means-of-Egress (CEBC 1011.5 + Table 1011.5)

See `occupancy-classification.md` for the full Table 1011.5. Direction rule:

- **Change to HIGHER hazard category** (lower number) → means of egress complies with Chapter 10 CBC.
  - 8 exceptions (stairway enclosure, existing stairways with handrails/guards, riser/tread conditions for replacement stairs, existing corridor walls with wood lath & plaster or 1/2" gyp, existing dead-end corridors, EERO via operable window per 1011.5.6, etc.)
- **Change to EQUAL or LOWER hazard** → existing per Section 905 of CEBC for the new classification. Newly constructed/configured egress complies with Ch.10 CBC.

### Heights & Areas (CEBC 1011.6 + Table 1011.6)

- **HIGHER hazard category** → heights/areas per Chapter 5 CBC for new occupancy.
  - Exception 1: high-rise constructed under prior permit — type-of-construction reduction per §403.2.1 permitted if fully sprinklered.
  - Exception 2: buildings with 1.5-hr floor assemblies — not required to comply with Ch.5 if requirements are Type IB, no H occupancies, and fully sprinklered.
- **EQUAL or LOWER hazard** → height/area of existing building deemed acceptable.

**Fire wall alternative (1011.6.1.1):** In non-H/F-1/S-1, fire barriers (§707) + horizontal assemblies (§711) may substitute for fire walls to subdivide building into separate "buildings" for area limits — sprinkler-required, max allowable area without sprinkler increase, FRR not less than Table 706.4. Exception reduces horizontal assembly FRR by 1 hr if §504 sprinkler height/story increases not used.

### Exterior Walls (CEBC 1011.7 + Table 1011.7)

- **HIGHER hazard category** → exterior walls comply with current CBC.
  - Exception: 2-hr FRR allowed where building <= 3 stories and is A-2/A-3 with OL < 300, B, F, M, or S.
- **EQUAL or LOWER hazard** → existing accepted.
- **Opening protectives (1011.7.3):** Sum of openings <= 50% of wall area per story. Exceptions: where CBC permits more, Group R <= 3 stories and >= 3 ft from lot line, sprinklered building, change to equal/lower hazard.

### Vertical Shaft Enclosure (CEBC 1011.8)

- Vertical shafts meet CBC for atriums OR Section 1011.8.
- Stairways: interior stairways enclosed per CBC if change to higher means-of-egress hazard. Exceptions: existing stairways with single-floor service, sprinklered building with 1-hr floor separation + sprinklered corridors, existing penetrations protected per CBC.
- Other vertical shafts (elevators, service/utility shafts): enclosed per CBC if change to higher hazard category. Exceptions: existing 1-hr enclosures accepted where higher required, non-Group-I buildings connecting < 6 stories not required to enclose if fully sprinklered.
- Openings: 1-hr fire-rated, self-closing or automatic-closing on smoke detector.

### Interior Finish (CEBC 1011.3)

Areas of change → interior finish complies with current CBC for new occupancy.

### Enhanced Classroom Acoustics (CEBC 1011.4)

In Group E with Level 3 alteration, classrooms <= 20,000 cubic ft get enhanced acoustics per ICC A117.1 §808. (E is largely out of scope for this skill.)

## 6. Common Commercial TI Structural Decisions

### Decision A: New rooftop unit (RTU) on existing tilt-up — Level 2 alteration

- Gravity load: New RTU adds dead load to the existing roof. CEBC 805.2 — structural element altered (roof joist/girder bearing reverification required if design dead, live, or snow load increases by more than 5% or gravity capacity is decreased).
- Separately check lateral DCR impacts under CEBC 805.3; rooftop equipment and PV carve-outs are in §805.3 Exceptions 2-3.
- Else: roof member upgrade or new structural support.
- Lateral system not triggered if no new RC.

### Decision B: Office TI in 3-story B-occupancy, work area 22,000 sf of 30,000-sf building (73% — Level 3)

- CEBC Ch. 7 + Ch. 8 + Ch. 9 all apply.
- Egress lighting / exit signs from work area to discharge — §905.2-905.3 (Level 3).
- Sprinklers per CBC for new construction — B doesn't have OL trigger, but check height/area / fire-area thresholds.
- No change of occupancy → no Ch. 10.
- Major scope to review.

### Decision C: M → B change of occupancy (former retail 8,000 sf becoming open-plan office)

- Hazard categories — Table 1011.5: M=3, B=4 — LOWER. Table 1011.6: M=3, B=4 — LOWER. Table 1011.7: M=2, B=3 — LOWER.
- Existing egress, height/area, exterior walls all accepted.
- Sprinkler: B has no OL/area-based sprinkler trigger. Existing M's existing sprinkler system can remain.
- Plumbing: B uses different fixture ratios. If OL increases by > 20%, fixture upgrade required (1009.1).
- Mechanical: No kitchen exhaust trigger. Ventilation per CMC — generally equivalent or less for B.
- Structural: RC unchanged (both II) — no seismic trigger.

### Decision D: S-1 → B change of occupancy + Level 3 alteration

- Hazard categories: T1011.5: S-1=4, B=4 — EQUAL. T1011.6: S-1=3, B=4 — LOWER. T1011.7: S-1=2, B=3 — LOWER.
- §1006.3 seismic trigger CHECKS: change from S/U to non-S/U — IF same RC, §304.3.2 permitted (existing accepted). IF RC bumps (warehouse RC II to office could remain RC II → usually no change). Usually OK.
- Egress generally OK.
- BUT Level 3 alteration is hugely demanding — current-CBC egress lighting / exit signs / sprinkler / alarm system for new construction in the work area.
