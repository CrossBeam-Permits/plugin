# Fire-Resistant Construction

California Residential Code, Title 24, Part 2.5 -- 2025 Edition
Section R302

## 1. Exterior Walls by Fire Separation Distance

Exterior wall construction, projections, openings, and penetrations must comply with
Table R302.1(1) or, for fully sprinklered dwellings, Table R302.1(2) (CRC R302.1).

**Fire separation distance** is measured at a right angle from the wall face to the
closest interior lot line, centerline of a street, alley, or public way, or an imaginary
line between two buildings or townhouse units on the lot.

### Table R302.1(1) -- Exterior Walls (Non-Sprinklered)

| Element | Condition | Min. Fire-Resistance Rating | Min. Fire Separation Distance |
|---|---|---|---|
| Walls | Fire-resistance rated | 1 hour (tested per ASTM E119/UL 263), exposure from both sides | 0 ft |
| Walls | Not fire-resistance rated | 0 hours | >= 5 ft |
| Projections | Not allowed | N/A | < 2 ft |
| Projections | Fire-resistance rated | 1 hour on underside, or heavy timber, or FRTW | >= 2 ft to < 5 ft |
| Projections | Not fire-resistance rated | 0 hours | >= 5 ft |
| Openings in walls | Not allowed | N/A | < 3 ft |
| Openings in walls | 25% max of wall area | 0 hours | 3 ft |
| Openings in walls | Unlimited | 0 hours | 5 ft |
| Penetrations | All | Comply with R302.4 | < 3 ft |
| Penetrations | All | None required | >= 3 ft |

Notes (CRC R302.1):
- Eave overhang fire-resistance rating may be reduced to 0 hours if fireblocking is
  provided from wall top plate to underside of roof sheathing.
- Rake overhang may be reduced to 0 hours where no vent openings communicate with
  the attic.

### Table R302.1(2) -- Exterior Walls (Sprinklered per R309)

| Element | Condition | Min. Fire-Resistance Rating | Min. Fire Separation Distance |
|---|---|---|---|
| Walls | Fire-resistance rated | 1 hour (exposure from outside only) | 0 ft |
| Walls | Not fire-resistance rated | 0 hours | >= 3 ft |
| Projections | Not allowed | N/A | < 2 ft |
| Projections | Fire-resistance rated | 1 hour on underside, or heavy timber, or FRTW | >= 2 ft |
| Projections | Not fire-resistance rated | 0 hours | >= 3 ft |
| Openings in walls | Not allowed | N/A | < 3 ft |
| Openings in walls | Unlimited | 0 hours | >= 3 ft |
| Penetrations | All | Comply with R302.4 | < 3 ft |
| Penetrations | All | None required | >= 3 ft |

Note: In fully-sprinklered subdivisions, walls not fire-resistance rated and
fire-resistance-rated projections may be reduced to 0 ft fire separation distance, and
unprotected openings and penetrations may be unlimited, where the adjoining lot provides
an open setback yard >= 6 ft on the opposite side of the property line (CRC Table
R302.1(2), Note a).

### Exceptions to R302.1

1. Walls perpendicular to the line used to determine fire separation distance.
2. Walls between individual dwelling units and their accessory buildings on the same lot.
3. Detached tool sheds, storage sheds, playhouses, and similar structures exempt from
   permits (projections shall not extend over the lot line).
4. Detached garages within 2 ft of a lot line may have roof eave projections up to 4 in.
5. Foundation vents installed per code are permitted.

## 2. Townhouse Separation Walls (R302.2)

Townhouse units shall be separated by one of the following (CRC R302.2):

### Double Walls (R302.2.1)
Two separate 1-hour fire-resistance-rated wall assemblies, one for each unit.

### Common Walls (R302.2.2)
A single common wall rated for fire exposure from both sides:
- **With sprinklers (R309):** not less than 1-hour fire-resistance rating.
- **Without sprinklers:** not less than 2-hour fire-resistance rating.

Common walls may extend to and be tight against exterior sheathing and the underside of
roof sheathing, terminate at the inside face of an exterior wall without stud cavities,
or use the exception permitting termination at the inside face where the intervening
cavity is filled with at least two nominal 2-in-thick wood studs. No openings, plumbing,
mechanical equipment, ducts, or vents are permitted in the common wall cavity; water-filled
sprinkler piping and electrical work complying with the CEC and R302.4 are permitted
(CRC R302.2.2).

### Continuity (R302.2.3)
The fire-resistance-rated separation must be continuous from the foundation to the
underside of the roof sheathing, deck, or slab, extending the full length of the wall.

### Parapets (R302.2.4)
Parapets are required for the same-elevation and within-30-in roof conditions unless an
R302.2.4 exception applies. The Class C alternative requires a 4-ft zone on each side
using noncombustible/FRTW deck or 5/8-in Type X gypsum supported by nominal 2-in ledgers,
with no openings or penetrations in that zone. A separate exception applies where the
higher roof deck is more than 30 in above the lower deck and the common wall is 1-hour
rated from both sides with no openings.

## 3. Two-Family Dwelling Unit Separation (R302.3)

Dwelling units in two-family dwellings shall be separated by fire-resistance-rated
assemblies (vertical, horizontal, or combination) (CRC R302.3.1).

| Configuration | Fire-Resistance Rating |
|---|---|
| Without sprinklers | 1 hour |
| With sprinklers (R309) | 1/2 hour |

Assemblies must be continuous and supported by construction of equal or greater
fire-resistance rating (CRC R302.3.3, R302.3.4).

### Shared Accessory Rooms (R302.3.6)

| Separation | Material |
|---|---|
| From dwelling units and attics | Not less than 1/2-inch gypsum board or equivalent on accessory room side |
| From habitable rooms above/below | Not less than 5/8-inch Type X gypsum board or equivalent |
| Structures supporting floor/ceiling assemblies | Not less than 1/2-inch gypsum board or equivalent |

Doors from shared accessory rooms: solid wood >= 1-3/8 in. thick, solid/honeycomb-core
steel >= 1-3/8 in. thick, or 20-minute fire-rated door with self-closing/automatic-closing
device. No openings directly into sleeping rooms (CRC R302.3.6.1).

## 4. Dwelling-Garage Separation (R302.5, R302.6)

This is an important residential plan-review topic.

### Opening Protection (R302.5.1)

- **No openings** from a garage directly into a sleeping room.
- Other openings between garage and dwelling require:
  - Solid wood door >= 1-3/8 in. thick, OR
  - Solid or honeycomb-core steel door >= 1-3/8 in. thick, OR
  - 20-minute fire-rated door.
- Doors must be **self-latching** and **self-closing or automatic-closing**.

Exception: Where both the residence and garage are sprinklered per R309 and R317.5.1,
doors between garage and residence (except to sleeping rooms) need only be self-closing
and self-latching (CRC R302.5.1).

### Duct Penetration (R302.5.2)
Ducts in or penetrating the garage-dwelling separation must be minimum No. 26 gauge
sheet steel or other approved material, with no openings into the garage.

### Other Penetrations (R302.5.3)
Protected per R302.11, Item 4.

### Table R302.6 -- Dwelling-Garage/Carport Separation

| Separation | Material |
|---|---|
| From dwelling unit and attics | Not less than 1/2-inch gypsum board or equivalent applied to garage side |
| From portions of dwelling above garage or carport | Not less than 5/8-inch Type X gypsum board or equivalent |
| Structure supporting floor/ceiling assemblies for this separation | Not less than 1/2-inch gypsum board or equivalent |
| Garages < 3 ft from a dwelling on the same lot | Not less than 1/2-inch gypsum board or equivalent on interior side of exterior walls within this area |

Notes:
- Wall separation does not apply to garage walls perpendicular to the adjacent dwelling
  unit wall (CRC R302.6).
- No separation required between dwelling and carport if the carport is entirely open on
  two or more sides with no enclosed areas above.

## 5. Penetrations Through Fire-Rated Assemblies (R302.4)

### Through Penetrations (R302.4.1)
Must be installed as tested in the approved fire-resistance-rated assembly, or protected
by an approved penetration firestop system tested per ASTM E814/UL 1479 with F rating
>= the required fire-resistance rating. Before requiring either route, apply both
R302.4.1 pipe exceptions with every listed annular-space, material, diameter,
opening-area, time-temperature, and pressure condition, including the separate
water-filled sprinkler-piping path.

### Membrane Penetrations (R302.4.2)
In walls or partitions rated not more than 2 hours, steel electrical boxes not exceeding
16 sq in. are permitted if:
- Aggregate area of openings does not exceed 100 sq in. per 100 sq ft of wall area.
- Annular space between wall membrane and box does not exceed 1/8 in.
- Boxes on opposite sides are separated by >= 24 in. horizontally (with noncommunicating
  stud cavities), or by the depth of the wall cavity filled with cellulose/rockwool/slag
  insulation, by solid fireblocking, by listed putty pads, or by other listed materials
  and methods.

Sprinkler pipe penetrations: annular space covered by a metal escutcheon plate
(CRC R302.4.2, Exception 3).

## 6. Under-Stair Protection (R302.7)

Enclosed space under stairs accessed by a door or access panel must have walls,
under-stair surface, and soffits protected on the enclosed side with 1/2-inch gypsum
board (CRC R302.7).

## 7. Fireblocking (R302.11)

Required in combustible construction to cut off concealed draft openings:

1. In stud wall concealed spaces: vertically at ceiling/floor levels; horizontally at
   <= 10 ft intervals.
2. At interconnections between concealed vertical and horizontal spaces (soffits, drop
   ceilings, cove ceilings).
3. In concealed spaces between stair stringers at top and bottom of run.
4. At openings around vents, pipes, ducts, cables, and wires at ceiling and floor level.
5. At chimneys and fireplaces (see R1003.19).
6. At cornices of two-family dwellings at the line of dwelling unit separation.

### Fireblocking Materials (R302.11.1)
- 2-inch nominal lumber
- Two thicknesses of 1-inch nominal lumber with broken lap joints
- 23/32-inch wood structural panels with joints backed by 23/32-inch panels
- 3/4-inch particleboard with joints backed by 3/4-inch particleboard
- 1/2-inch gypsum board
- 1/4-inch cement-based millboard
- Batts/blankets of mineral wool or glass fiber, or other approved materials,
  securely retained
- Cellulose insulation tested per ASTM E119/UL 263

## 8. Fire Protection of Floors (R302.13)

Floor assemblies not otherwise required to be fire-resistance rated must have a membrane
on the underside: 1/2-inch gypsum wallboard, 5/8-inch wood structural panel, or
equivalent (CRC R302.13).

Exceptions:
1. Floors directly over a space with automatic sprinklers under R309, NFPA 13D, or
   another approved equivalent system.
2. Floors over crawl spaces not intended for storage or fuel-fired or electric-powered
   heating appliances.
3. Unprotected portions up to 80 sq ft per story with fireblocking at perimeter.
4. Wood floor assemblies >= 2x10 nominal or demonstrating equivalent fire performance.
5. Wood floors < 600 sq ft in detached accessory structures with no habitable space above.

## 9. Flame Spread and Smoke-Developed Index (R302.9)

| Surface | Max Flame Spread Index | Max Smoke-Developed Index | Test Standard |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wall and ceiling finishes (baseline) | 200 | 450 | ASTM E84 / UL 723 |

Apply the trim, doors/windows, and qualifying thin-material exceptions in R302.9.1.
NFPA 286 is permitted as the R302.9.4 alternative; HDPE and PP finishes follow the
specific NFPA 286 rule in R302.9.5.

## 10. Insulation Flame Spread and Smoke-Developed Index (R302.10)

Insulation in floor-ceiling, roof-ceiling, and wall assemblies, crawl spaces, and attics
has a baseline maximum flame-spread index of 25 and smoke-developed index of 450 under
R302.10.1. Apply its separate exceptions for qualifying facings, cellulose loose-fill
insulation, and foam plastic insulation.
