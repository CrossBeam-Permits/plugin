# Laguna Beach Objective Design Standards — Form Group B

**Source:** *Objective Design Standards for Single-Family and Two-Family Residential Development*, Chapter 3B, City document 26790, published August 2026.

Use this reference only after the applicant has selected Form Group B: **Southern California Spanish Revival or Mediterranean**. Load it with `objective-design-standards-2026.md`; do not combine its standards with another form group. Ask the applicant to choose when the form group is not stated—the source does not assign groups by zone, neighborhood, or context.

Section anchors: §3B.1 Roofs; §3B.2 Exterior Walls; §3B.3 Exterior Openings; §3B.4 Porches, Decks, and Balconies; §3B.5 Equipment Screening; §3B.6 Colors. The applicant's checklist must identify the plan-sheet location for every applicable standard.

### 3B.1.1 (PDF p. 38)

Roofs, allowed types. Utilize at least one of the following roof types. All other roof types are prohibited.
a. Gabled.
b. Cross-gabled.
c. Hipped.
d. Cross-hipped.
e. Flat, not to exceed 20 percent of the total roof area.

### 3B.1.2 (PDF p. 38)

Roof material. Provide one of the following materials for pitched roofs. Flat roofs shall be built-up or hot-mop roofing.
a. Spanish, Pantile, Mission, or Roman terracotta tile; straight or tapered barrel.
b. Plain concrete tile.

### 3B.1.3 (PDF p. 38)

Roof pitch. Roof pitches shall be a minimum 2:12 slope and a maximum 4:12 slope.

### 3B.1.4 (PDF p. 38)

Optional: roof rake. If provided, roof rakes shall be a maximum of 12 inches in height and horizontally project no more than six inches inclusive of the rake tile.

### 3B.1.5 (PDF p. 38)

Optional: roof eave. If provided, eaves shall horizontally project a minimum of six inches and a maximum of 12 inches, measured from the edge of the exterior wall to the edge of the eave.

### 3B.2.1 (PDF p. 39)

Exterior wall materials and finishes. Exterior walls shall be a natural finish stucco or a pigmented and painted stucco utilizing one of the following finishes:
a. Cat Face finish.
b. English finish.
c. Fine Sand Float finish.
d. Glacier finish.
e. Light Dash finish.
f. Medium Dash finish.
g. Heavy Dash finish.
h. Medium Sand Float finish.
i. Monterey finish.
j. Sand/float 20/30 finish.
k. Santa Barbara finish.
l. Spanish finish.
m. Smooth finish.
n. Trowel Sweep finish.
o. Tunnel Dash finish.

### 3B.2.2 (PDF p. 39)

Optional: secondary wall material. If provided, secondary wall materials shall not exceed 10 percent of each elevation surface area and be composed of one of the following. At building corners, all materials shall wrap around the corner for a minimum distance of two feet.
a. Board and batten fiber cement or wood siding.
b. Cast stone.
c. Matte or non-glossy ceramic, decorative, porcelain, or terracotta tiles.
d. Stone.

### 3B.3.1 (PDF p. 40)

Exterior openings: percentage of wall. Exterior openings on building walls with the same orientation, inclusive of doors and windows, shall be a minimum of 15 percent and a maximum of 30 percent.

### 3B.3.2 (PDF p. 40)

Exterior opening types. Provide a minimum of two exterior opening sizes, inclusive of doors and windows.

### 3B.3.3 (PDF p. 40)

Exterior opening shapes. Openings shall be rectangular, square, segmental arch, and/or round arch in shape.

### 3B.3.4 (PDF p. 40)

Exterior opening surrounds. Opening surrounds, when provided at the exterior, shall comply with the dimensions per the table below.
Window Surround Dimension Apron 3 inches minimum Head 3 inches minimum Jamb 3 inches minimum

### 3B.3.5 (PDF p. 40)

Exterior opening materials. With the exception of the front door, provide one of the following exterior opening materials:
a. Wood, pre-finished, stained, or painted per the color requirements of §3B.6.
b. Metal-clad wood, pre-finished or painted per the color requirements of §3B.6.
c. Steel, pre-finished or painted per the color requirements of §3B.6.
d. Composite, acrylic, or fiberglass set a minimum of two inches inward from the adjacent exterior surface and finished per the color requirements of §3B.6.

### 3B.3.6 (PDF p. 41)

Window types. Provide at least one and a maximum of two of the following window types:
a. Casement.
b. Double-hung.
c. Fixed.
d. Ganged casement or double-hung windows.
If casement windows are utilized, double-hung windows are prohibited. If double hung windows are utilized, casement windows are prohibited.

### 3B.3.7 (PDF p. 41)

Optional: window recess. Windows shall be recessed at the sill, jamb, and header of the window a minimum of two inches from the adjoining surface.
Window sills, decorative surrounds at openings established by cast stone or plaster are permitted to establish the recess but shall surround all sides of the window.

### 3B.3.8 (PDF p. 41)

Window sashes, rails, and stiles. Window sashes, rails, and stiles shall be a minimum of two inches wide (jambs) or two inches high (sills or heads).

### 3B.3.9 (PDF p. 41)

Optional: window muntins, mullions, and sticking. If windows that include muntins, mullions, and/or sticking are provided, they shall incorporate full sticking establishing true divided glazing and have a minimum ½-inch profile.
Simulated sticking is prohibited. Snap on and/or between glass muntins is also prohibited.

### 3B.3.10 (PDF p. 41)

Door leaves. Provide no more than two contiguous door leaves.

### 3B.3.11 (PDF p. 41)

Door recess. Doors shall be recessed a minimum of two inches, measured from the face of the door to the face of the surround or adjoining building wall.

### 3B.3.12 (PDF p. 41)

Optional: transoms and sidelites. If provided, the maximum width of sidelites shall be half the width of the door. Transom windows shall not extend beyond the door or sidelite edges, and shall not exceed 18 inches in height.

### 3B.3.13 (PDF p. 41)

Door location. Doors shall be placed in one of the following locations:
a. Doors are centered within arches.
b. Doors are a minimum 18 inches distant from a wall.

### 3B.4.1 (PDF p. 42)

Entry element. Provide two or more of the following entry elements:
a. Street-facing entries placed either at grade or a maximum 18 inches above the adjacent finished grade shall comply with at least one of the following:
i. Recessed a minimum of two feet and a maximum of five feet from a building plane that faces a street.
ii. Framed by an arched or segmented arched opening(s).
iii. Incorporated into a projecting minor mass that does not exceed the eave line by more than three feet or a tower element that does not exceed the otherwise allowed height limit.
b. An entry path no greater than five feet in width shall connect at least one street to the primary residential entry. Within ten feet of the entry, the path may exceed this width but shall not exceed in this area adjacent to the entry a width of more than eight feet. The entry path shall incorporate one or more of the following materials:
i. Colored and scored concrete with score lines placed no more than 30 inches apart.
ii. Scored concrete with score lines placed no more than 30 inches apart.
iii. Concrete or stone pavers no larger than 30 inches by 30 inches.
iv. Brick used as a uniform or as an accent material.
c. Porches with columns and/or piers, comprising all of the following standards:
• Porches shall make up a maximum of 80 percent of the primary frontage.
• Porch depth shall be a minimum of five feet and a maximum of ten feet.
• Columns shall be spaced at a minimum eight feet on center.
d. Arcade with arches or segmented arches, comprising all of the following standards:
• Arcades shall make up a maximum of 80 percent of the primary frontage.
• Arcade depth shall be a minimum of five feet and a maximum of ten feet.

### 3B.4.2 (PDF p. 43)

Fences and wall materials. Fences, walls, and guardrails facing streets or rights-of-way shall be constructed of the following materials or any combination of the following materials:
a. Wood, stained or painted per the requirements in §3B.6.
b. Stucco or stucco plaster, integrally colored or painted and matching the same color as the main residential structure.
c. Stone, face stone, and/or veneer stone.
d. Steel or wrought iron pre-finished or painted per the requirements of §3B.6.

### 3B.5.1 (PDF p. 44)

Optional: building elements. If the following building elements are incorporated into a design, they shall meet the following criteria:
a. Chimneys, utilizing one of the following materials:
i. Brick, naturally fired or painted to match the same color as the main residential structure.
ii. Metal chimney cap, brushed finish, treated to prevent reflectivity and glare, or painted to match the requirements of standard B.5.2.c.
iii. Stucco, painted and matching the same color as the main residential structure.
b. Exterior stairs and ramps that connect the site and walkways to the interior floor level shall comply with all of the following standards:
• Exterior stair railings shall utilize one of the following materials:
1. Metal, pre-finished or painted per the requirements of §3B.6.
2. Steel or wrought iron, pre-finished or painted per the requirements of §3B.6.
3. Wood, painted or stained per the requirements of §3B.6.
• Treads and risers shall be constructed of one or more of the following materials:
1. Brick.
2. Standard gray or colored concrete.
3. Pavers and tiles meeting the criteria of standard B.4.1.b.

### 3B.5.2 (PDF p. 45)

Building components and details. Buildings and structures shall incorporate a minimum of three of the following architectural details:
a. Fabric awnings above openings with a maximum five-foot horizontal projection, colored per the requirements of §3B.6.
b. Brackets under eaves and overhangs, either wood, metal, or engineered polymer.
c. Chimney caps utilizing at least one of the following:
i. Stucco or brick coping cap the same length and depth as the chimney.
ii. Stucco or brick cornice and cap no more than 16 inches in height and projecting no more than three inches.
iii. Inward inclining or stepped back and inward from the face of the chimney stucco or brick cap no more than 30 inches in height.
iv. Terracotta clay chimney pots.
d. Decorative shutters, painted per the color requirements in §3B.6.
e. Decorative surrounds at openings facing streets utilizing cast stone, columns, patterned ceramic tiles, terracotta tiles, painted wood, pilasters, or stonework.
f. Wood door designed with panels or paneled with true divide glazing.
g. Wall or gable vents utilizing one of the following materials:
i. Masonry or stone wall vents.
ii. Mild steel or wrought iron vents.
iii. Terracotta clay cylinder vents.
iv. Wrought iron grills, pre-finished or painted per the color requirements in §3B.6.
h. Porte-cochere projecting across a driveway for vehicles to pass through, architecturally treated to meet the form group standards of the primary structure by implementing at least one of the following components:
i. Consistent use of roof forms and if sloped, roof materials and colors.
ii. Consistent use of facade materials.
iii. Consistent use of primary wall colors.
i. Prefinished or painted rafters, rafter tails, or false rafters at eaves with plain or decorative tails.
j. Shutters, solid cased or louvered and proportioned to fully cover the adjacent opening, pre-finished or painted.
k. Oriel window, no more than one on a street-facing elevation.
l. Window muntins, mullions, and sticking that comply with standard B.3.8.
m. Window grilles on a minimum of two street-facing openings, either natural, pre-finished, or painted wood spindlework grilles, or painted mild steel or wrought iron grilles.
n. Wingwall facing a street, matching the primary material, and with an arcaded or opening element.

### 3B.5.3 (PDF p. 46)

Visible exterior lighting, shell and casing material. The shell and casing of exterior lighting fixtures with visible light sources shall utilize one of the following materials:
a. Metal, mild steel or wrought iron, painted per the requirements of §3B.6.
b. Copper or oil-rubbed bronze, naturally finished.
