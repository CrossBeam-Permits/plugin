## New SFR Sprinklers

Section 14.52.030 amends CRC R309.2:

- Automatic residential fire sprinkler systems are required in one- and two-family dwellings and garages attached to them.
- A garage is considered attached if it is within 10 ft of the dwelling.
- Firewalls, fire barriers, and fire partitions do not define separate buildings or fire areas for this rule.
- The same section also carries a **retrofit** duty for existing dwellings — the 50% / three-year valuation trigger. See "Existing Building Warning" below.

Title 15 Section 15.01.035 also requires approved automatic sprinkler systems throughout all new buildings and structures and specifically in all new one-family, two-family, and multi-family dwellings, regardless of square footage or number of stories.

Practical correction:

- New SFR plans must carry a "FIRE SPRINKLERS REQUIRED" note.
- Residential sprinkler plans may be deferred, but the permit set must identify the deferred submittal.

## NFPA 13D Local Amendments

Section 14.52.031 amends CRC R309.3:

- Residential sprinkler systems must be designed and calculated under the most current NFPA 13D standard.
- Flow switch must activate an electric exterior alarm.
- Flow switch must also activate all interconnected interior smoke detectors.

Title 15 Section 15.01.070 amends NFPA 13D (2022):

- Spare sprinklers: at least 2 of each type, with wrench and cabinet.
- Separate control valve only if supervised by central station, proprietary, or remote station alarm service.
- At least one water pressure gauge at the riser.
- Exterior alarm must be listed for outside service and audible/visible from the addressed street; front/side location subject to fire code official approval.
- Interior audibility must be provided throughout the structure, with sleeping-area sound levels at least 15 dBA above ambient and not less than 75 dBA.
- Sprinklers are required in attached garages, attached carports, open attached porches, and similar structures.
- Sprinklers are required in detached garages and carports within 30 ft of the dwelling.
- Sprinklers are required in specified accessible concealed spaces, all attics at attic penetrations, and exterior overhangs 48 in and greater.

## WUI / VHFHSZ Scope

Chapter 14.70 applies to exterior materials, systems, and assemblies for new buildings, additions, alterations, and repairs in WUI areas within Laguna Beach.

Treat WUI/VHFHSZ as active unless parcel-specific evidence shows otherwise. The city registry marks Laguna Beach with wildfire overlay `all`; the citywide VHFHSZ coverage figure is in `../../city-reference/SKILL.md`.

## Fuel Modification

For new buildings in areas containing City of Laguna Beach Fuel Modification designation:

- Preliminary fuel-modification plans must be submitted and approved concurrent with tentative map submittal, when applicable.
- Final fuel-modification plans must be approved by the fire code official before building permit issuance.
- Plans must meet the LBFD **Landscape/Fuel Modification Standards and Maintenance Program (L/FMSMP)**, referenced by §14.70.060. Per the city's Fire Prevention page (`lagunabeachcity.net/government/departments/fire/fire-prevention/wildfire-mitigation-vegetation-management/fuel-modification-for-development`): the current operative document is the **2019 L/FMSMP** (not "Aug 2021" as earlier skill drafts incorrectly cited). Applies to ALL new construction and major remodel projects within the FM Zone (as designated by City GIS). Memorandum updates supplement but do not supersede the 2019 framework.
- Alterations to approved fuel-modification areas require prior fire code official approval.

### Fuel-Modification Zones (Zone A / B / C / D) — verbatim from 2019 L/FMSMP

Source: the **2019 LBFD Landscape/Fuel Modification Standards and Maintenance Program** (Exhibit B to the Feb 19, 2020 Fire Marshal Memorandum). Local copy: the City source document (not included in this plugin) (which includes the full L/FMSMP as Exhibit B). Earlier skill drafts had width estimates that did not match the standards document — the verbatim widths are below.

| Zone | Width (verbatim) | Treatment |
|------|------------------|-----------|
| **Zone A** — Setback Irrigated | **20 ft minimum**, measured horizontally from the structure point closest to Zone A; surrounds the structure 360° | **No combustible construction allowed** anywhere in Zone A. Automatic irrigation to maintain healthy, high-moisture-content vegetation. Irrigation kept outside the drip line of native oak (Quercus). Pruning of foliage to reduce fuel load and remove plant litter / dead wood. Complete removal of fire-prone species (L/FMSMP Attachment 7), with only minimal retention of selected native vegetation. |
| **Zone B** — Irrigated | **50 ft to 75 ft** in width | Permanently irrigated; drought-tolerant, deep-rooted, moisture-retentive plants. Irrigation kept ≥20 ft from drip line of native oak. Cleared of all undesirable species (Attachment 7); planted from the **LBFD-approved or OCFA Plant List**. Exceptions for desirable existing species reviewed by the Fire Chief case-by-case. **No combustible construction** (gazebos, trellis, shade covers) allowed in Zone B. |
| **Zone C** — Thinning Non-Irrigated | **50 ft to 75 ft** in width | **50% thinning**. Remove all dead and dying vegetation. Remove all undesirable species (Attachment 7). Not irrigated. **No combustible construction** allowed. |
| **Zone D** — Thinning Non-Irrigated | **75 ft to 100 ft** in width | **30% thinning**. Remove all dead and dying vegetation. Remove all undesirable species (Attachment 7). Not irrigated. **No combustible construction** allowed. Can extend ≥125 ft into adjacent wildland areas. |

**Total fuel-modification corridor: 195 ft to 270 ft** measured from the structure outward (20 + 50 + 50 + 75 minimum; 20 + 75 + 75 + 100 maximum). LBFD may require greater widths on steep slopes or in identified extreme-fire-behavior canyons (Bluebird Canyon, Top of the World, Arch Beach Heights canyon). Slope-based zone-width adjustments are made per L/FMSMP Attachment 2 (Configuration Options) and Attachment 3 (Incline Measurement for Selected Slopes).

**Off-site fuel modification:** Required when adjacent properties (including HOA common areas) contain fuel within the project's required modification corridor. Off-site easement / written maintenance agreement required before plan approval (see §14.70.070 + L/FMSMP "Off-Site Fuel Modification Requirements").

**Plant palette and prohibited species:** Plans must use only species from the LBFD-approved or OCFA Plant List. The "undesirable plants" list (L/FMSMP Attachment 7) must be absent from all zones, including Zone D.

Required outputs for a new SFR fuel-mod plan review:

- Preliminary fuel-mod plan stamped/approved by LBFD **before** DRB action.
- Final fuel-mod plan approved by the fire code official **before** building permit issuance (Section 14.70.070).
- Plant palette restricted to the LBFD approved list; prohibited-plant species absent from all zones.
- Zone widths dimensioned on the site/landscape plan from each combustible structure edge.
- Off-site fuel-mod areas (when zones cross the property line onto neighboring or HOA-owned land) need a recorded fuel-modification easement or HOA-maintenance covenant.

## Existing Building Warning

If an application calls itself "new SFR" but retains a substantial existing dwelling, also run `laguna-beach-residential-remodel`. An existing building has **two independent sprinkler retrofit triggers, and either one requires the retrofit**:

- **Ch. 14.52.030** (valuation) — the same section quoted above also states that when the cumulative value of additions, alterations and repairs to a one- or two-family dwelling and attached garage in any three-year period exceeds 50% of the pre-work valuation, the entire dwelling and attached garage must be sprinklered throughout. Valuation is per the City's building-permit fee resolution.
- **Title 15 §1103.5.4** (scope) — alteration of ≥50% of the building, OR work on ≥50% of the exterior, OR an added story.

Full rule: `../../residential-remodel/references/city-sprinkler-retrofit.md`.
