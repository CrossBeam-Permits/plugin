# Plumbing Requirements for Single-Family Residential

California Plumbing Code (CPC), Title 24, Part 5 — 2025 Edition.
This reference covers minimum fixtures, water supply sizing, drainage
sizing, traps, vents, water heaters, hose bibbs, and fire sprinkler
water supply for single-family dwellings.

---

## 1. Minimum Fixture Requirements

### Dwelling Unit Minimum Fixtures (CPC 422.0)
The R-3 one- and two-family dwelling row of CPC Table 422.1 requires at minimum:

| Fixture | Minimum Quantity |
|---|---|
| Water closet | 1 |
| Lavatory | 1 |
| Bathtub or shower | 1 |
| Kitchen sink | 1 |
| Automatic clothes-washer connection | 1 |

The complete minimum is the five-item Table 422.1 list above; do not add an
unsupported bedroom-count rule.

## 3. Water Supply Pipe Sizing

### General Requirements
- Building supply pipe minimum: 3/4 inch diameter (CPC 610.8)
- System shall be sized to prevent maximum velocities from being exceeded
  (CPC 610.1)
- Copper tube: max 8 ft/s cold, 5 ft/s hot (CPC 610.12.1)
- Pressure loss through filters, softeners, backflow preventers, and
  tankless heaters shall be included in calculations (CPC 610.2)

### Sizing Methods
1. **Table 610.4 method** — select based on pressure range, developed
   length, and total fixture units
2. **Appendix A method** — detailed engineering calculation per CPC 610.5
3. **Parallel distribution systems** — per manufacturer listing (CPC 610.4)

### Table 610.4 Quick Reference (30-45 psi range)
| Meter (in) | Supply (in) | Max FU at 100 ft | Max FU at 200 ft |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3/4 | 3/4 | 12 | 6 |
| 3/4 | 1 | 21 | 15 |
| 1 | 1 | 25 | 17 |
| 1 | 1-1/4 | 38 | 28 |
| 1 | 1-1/2 | 65 | 48 |

## 4. Drainage Pipe Sizing

### Materials for Residential DWV (Table 701.2)
Permitted materials for single-family drainage piping include:
- ABS (Schedule 40)
- PVC (Schedule 40)
- Cast iron
- Copper (Type DWV)
- Co-extruded composites

Note: ABS and PVC are limited to not more than two stories of residential
accommodation per HCD (CPC 701.2(2)(a)).

### Minimum Drain Slope
Horizontal drainage piping shall maintain 1/4 inch per foot slope
(CPC 708.1). Where that slope is impractical because of street-sewer depth,
structural features, or the building/structure arrangement, a building drain 4
inches or larger may use 1/8 inch per foot only after AHJ approval.

### Maximum Fixture Units by Drain Size (Table 703.2 — partial)
| Pipe Size (in) | Max DFU Horizontal Drainage Piping |
|---|---|
| 1-1/2 | 1 |
| 2 | 8 |
| 3 | 35 |
| 4 | 216* |

*For 4-inch-and-larger piping, Table 703.2 values are based on 1/4 in./ft
slope and are multiplied by 0.8 where the approved 1/8 in./ft slope is used.
Apply all other table notes and scope directly.

## 5. Trap Requirements

### General
- Each fixture shall be separately trapped with an approved type of liquid-seal
  trap, subject to the integral-trap and shared-trap exceptions (CPC 1001.2)
- S-traps, bell traps, and crown-vented traps are prohibited (CPC 1004.1)
- No fixture shall be double-trapped (CPC 1004.1)
- Tailpiece shall not exceed 24 inches (CPC 1001.2)

### Trap Seal
- Minimum liquid seal: 2 inches (CPC 1005.1)
- Maximum liquid seal: 4 inches (CPC 1005.1)

### Trap Arm Distance to Vent (Table 1002.2)

| Trap Arm Diameter (in) | Min Distance Trap to Vent (in) | Max Distance Trap to Vent (in) |
|---|---|---|
| 1-1/4 | 2-1/2 | 30 |
| 1-1/2 | 3 | 42 |
| 2 | 4 | 60 |
| 3 | 6 | 72 |
| 4 | 8 | 120 |

Notes:
- Maintain 1/4 inch per foot slope
- Water closet trap arm max developed length: 6 feet (measured from closet
  flange to inner edge of vent)
- Wet-vented bathtubs/showers: max 6 ft for 1-1/2 in, 8 ft for 2 in

### Trap Sizing
Trap shall be the same size as the trap arm (CPC 1003.3). Size shall be
not less than nor more than one pipe size larger than Table 702.1 values.

## 6. Vent System

### General
Each fixture trap shall be protected against siphonage and back-pressure
by a vent pipe (CPC 901.2). The vent system shall prevent trap seal
exposure to more than 1 inch water column pressure differential
(CPC 901.3).

### Individual Vent Sizing
- Minimum 1-1/4 inches diameter
- Not less than half the diameter of the drain served (CPC 904.1)

### Vent Termination
- Extend through roof flashing, terminating not less than 6 inches above
  the roof (CPC 906.1)
- Not less than 1 foot from any vertical surface (CPC 906.1)
- Not less than 10 feet from, or 3 feet above, any openable window, door,
  opening, air intake, or vent shaft (CPC 906.2)
- Not less than 3 feet from a lot line, except at an alley or street (CPC 906.2)
- ABS/PVC exposed to sunlight: protect with water-based synthetic latex
  paint (CPC 906.1)

### Vent Pipe Rise
Each vent shall rise vertically to not less than 6 inches above the
flood-level rim of the fixture served before offsetting horizontally where
structural conditions permit. A vent below that elevation uses drainage
fittings and approved drainage-pipe material and grade (CPC 905.3).

### Wet Venting (Residential)
Wet venting is limited to vertical drainage piping receiving discharge
from 1-and-2 fixture unit fixtures; not exceeding 4 fixtures; all within
the same story; max 6 feet developed length (CPC 908.1).

### Horizontal Wet Venting — Bathroom Group (CPC 908.2)
A bathroom group on the same floor may be wet-vented where:
- One or two vented lavatories serve as the wet vent
- Only one wet-vented fixture drain discharges upstream of the dry-vented
  connection
- Wet vent sized: 2 inches for 4 DFU or less; 3 inches for 5+ DFU
- Water closet connects downstream of other fixture connections

### Common Vent
Two fixtures may share a common vent where each wastes into an approved
double fitting at the same level (CPC 905.6).

## 7. Water Heater Requirements

### Location
Fuel-burning water heaters in bedrooms/bathrooms shall be (CPC 504.1):
1. Direct-vent type, OR
2. In an exclusive-use closet with a listed gasketed door and listed
   self-closing device, threshold and bottom seal, combustion air from outdoors
   under CPC 506.4, and compliance with CPC 504.1.1/504.1.2

### Temperature and Pressure Relief (T&P) Valve
- Required on all water heaters (CPC 504.0, referenced standards)
- Discharge pipe shall flow by gravity through an air gap into the drainage
  system or terminate outside pointing down 6-24 inches above ground
  (CPC 504.6, 608.5)
- Discharge pipe shall not be threaded at termination (CPC 504.0)
- Discharge pipe shall not be smaller than the T&P valve outlet

### Drain Pan
Water heaters installed in locations where leakage could cause damage
shall be provided with a drain pan of corrosion-resistant material.
The pan shall drain to an approved location.

### Seismic Strapping
California requires water heaters to be anchored or strapped to resist
earthquake motion. Where strapping is used, place one strap in the upper third
and one in the lower third of the unit, with the lower strap at least 4 inches
above the controls. Strapping shall comply with manufacturer instructions and
local requirements.

### Combustion Air
Fuel-burning water heaters within CMC 701 use the applicable combustion-air
method and manufacturer instructions; direct-vent appliances use the CMC 701.1
exception (see mechanical-hvac.md Section 3).

### Energy Code Requirements
See California Energy Code Section 110.3 for mandatory water heating
system requirements and CEnC 150.0(n) for residential-specific
requirements (CPC 501.2).

## 8. Backwater Valve (CPC 710.0)

### Backflow Protection (CPC 710.1)
Fixtures installed on a floor level that is lower than the next upstream
manhole cover of the public or private sewer shall be protected from
backflow of sewage by installing an approved type of backwater valve.

**Key qualifiers:**
- Fixtures on the same floor level that are **not** below the next
  upstream manhole cover are not required to have a backwater valve
- Fixtures on floor levels **above** the upstream manhole elevation
  shall **not** discharge through the backwater valve
- Cleanouts for drains that pass through a backwater valve shall be
  clearly identified with a permanent label stating "backwater valve
  downstream"

### Backwater Valve Standards (CPC 710.6)
Backwater valves shall comply with ASME A112.14.1 or IAPMO IGC 305
and shall be located where accessible for inspection and repair.

## 9. Hose Bibbs and Backflow Prevention

### Backflow Prevention
Potable outlets with hose attachments use the protection in CPC 603.5.7,
except water-heater drains, boiler drains, and clothes-washer connections.

### Vacuum Breakers
An in-scope hose connection uses a nonremovable hose-bibb backflow preventer,
nonremovable hose-bibb vacuum breaker, or atmospheric vacuum breaker at least
6 inches above the highest downstream usage point. A freezing-climate
installation uses a listed self-draining frost-proof hose bibb with integral
protection.

### Fixture Units
- First hose bibb: 2.5 water supply fixture units
- Each additional hose bibb: 1.0 fixture unit (reduced loading for
  sizing total building demand) (Table 610.3, Note 8)

## 10. Residential Fire Sprinkler Water Supply

### General
Where residential fire sprinkler systems are required (per CRC R309),
CPC 612.1 directs compliance to the CRC or CFC. CRC R309 permits its
prescriptive system or NFPA 13D, as applicable; preserve the existing-work and
qualifying-ADU exceptions in R309.2.

### Integration with Domestic Supply
Residential fire sprinkler systems in one- and two-family dwellings
may be supplied from the domestic water supply system, provided the
system meets the applicable multipurpose-system rules. Where no provision
prevents domestic flow during sprinkler operation, add the conditional 5-gpm
domestic allowance at the R309.3.5 connection; full simultaneous domestic
fixture demand is not added.

### Sizing
Water supply piping serving residential sprinkler systems shall be sized
for the R309.3.4 controlling-room design flow, including two sprinklers where
the controlling room has two or more, at the R309.3.5 required pressure. Use
the R309.3.6 prescriptive method or an NFPA 13D hydraulic calculation.

### Cross-reference
See CRC R309 (Automatic Sprinkler Systems) for when residential
sprinkler systems are required.
