## Plan Set Count (per doc 5103, current 2025-cycle version)

- **Four (4) complete plan sets** at **24" x 36"** for new SFRs and large additions (≥500 sf). Distribution: (1) Building, (2) Planning/Zoning, (3) Fire Department, (4) Water Quality.
- **Two (2) sets** for small additions (<500 sf) and interior renovations.
- **11"x17" minimum** permitted for minor projects ONLY with prior written approval from the Building Official or Zoning Administrator. Scale must be maintained.
- Typical scales: **1/8" for site plan and plot plan; 1/4" for floor plan, elevations, sections.** Other scales require prior written approval.

## Fees Tied to Submittal (per doc 5103)

These checks are payable in separate checks to the City of Laguna Beach at submittal:

| Item | Fee | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| WQMP 3rd-party review (Atkins) | **$1,700 deposit** | Plus $233 processing fee. Wet-signed 3rd-party-approved WQMP and report required. |
| WQMP processing | **$233** | City processing fee accompanying the Atkins deposit. |
| Soils report peer review | **$5,000 deposit** | When a soils report is required per "When a soils report is required" handout. |

Flag any incomplete submittal that omits these checks — doc 5103 explicitly states they accompany the WQMP and soils-report packages.

## Required Sheets / Documents

The Building Plan Check handout requires or commonly triggers the following for new SFR plan check:

- Application forms and title sheet.
- Record of survey for new construction.
- Site/plot plan at minimum 1/8 scale showing property lines, lot dimensions, setbacks, all structures, distance to property lines, distance to bluff top if applicable, easements, north arrow, and adjacent structures.
- Site survey when boundaries are not clearly established/verifiable.
- Roof plan, floor plans, elevations, sections, foundation plan, floor framing, roof framing, details, demolition plan if applicable.
- Mechanical, electrical, and plumbing plans.
- Utility service work order for new service, modified service, temporary power, or panel upgrades.
- Fire Department site access plan if required during zoning/planning.
- Energy analysis when envelope, mechanical, water heating, or lighting systems are affected.
- CALGreen mandatory measures.
- Grading and drainage plans.
- Erosion-control BMPs.
- Water Quality Management Plan (WQMP) with wet-signed third-party consultant report when required.
- Site/right-of-way improvement plan when required.
- Coastal hazards/wave runup report when required during zoning/planning for CDP.
- Wet-stamped hydrology calculations when required.
- Wet-stamped structural calculations and structural plans.
- Soils/geotechnical report when required, with peer review deposit.
- WELO documents when required.
- Construction staging plan and affidavit for constrained sites or when required by zoning/planning.

## Cover Sheet / Building Data

Cover sheet should identify:

- Project address, owner, designer, legal description, and APN.
- Occupancy and type of construction.
- Existing and proposed floor areas.
- Lot area and building site coverage.
- Applicable codes: 2025 CBC, CRC, CMC, CPC, CEC, CALGreen, Energy Code, and City of Laguna Beach regulations.
- Fire sprinkler note when required: `FIRE SPRINKLERS REQUIRED`.
- Storm-drain pollution prevention note.

## Deferred Fire Sprinkler Submittal

When sprinklers are required, the handout allows deferred residential sprinkler plans. Require 2 copies for residential sprinkler plans and track as a deferred submittal before installation/inspection.

## Planning/Zoning Table - New SFD Row

The Planning/Zoning table places "Addition >50%, Major Remodel, or New Single Family Dwelling" in the DRB/Planning Commission required tier and marks these plan/information categories for submittal:

- Application forms.
- Title sheet.
- Survey.
- Site plan.
- Staking plan.
- Roof plan.
- Floor plans.
- Elevation views.
- Cross sections.
- Floor-area calculations.
- Grading/drainage when over 20 cubic yards.
- Landscape plan.
- Lighting plan.
- Fire Department site access plan.
- Site and right-of-way improvement plan.

## Bluff / Oceanfront Reports

For new development on oceanfront or oceanfront-bluff property, require a bluff-edge determination and supporting technical reports when the quick guide applies:

- Geologic hazards report.
- Suitability/safe economic life statement.
- Slope stability analysis with minimum factor of safety 1.5 static and 1.2 pseudostatic.
- Sea-level-rise analysis using the 2018 SLR Guidance and the medium-high scenario of about 6.6 ft.
- Wave uprush/impact report considering seasonally eroded beach, 75-year erosion, high tide, 75-year SLR, and 100-year storm or 1982/83 El Nino comparison.

## Completeness Finding

Return `FAIL` for missing required core sheets, missing survey for new construction, missing code cycle statement, or no sprinkler note on a sprinkler-required new SFR. Return `FLAG` when the handout says a document is required only if Planning, Building, Fire, Water Quality, or Public Works determines it is needed.
