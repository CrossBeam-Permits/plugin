# Laguna Beach - Three Arch Bay Overlay Sequencing (Ch. 25.44)

Sources:

- LBMC **Chapter 25.44 — Three Arch Bay Zone** — base scrape plus Ord. 1732 §§37–38 current-law amendments at the City source document (not included in this plugin) (verified 2026-08-15). The integrated eCode page is not yet current.
- City handout: `three-arch-bay-development-process.pdf` (Development Application Procedure — 5-step diagram)
- Citywide reference: `../../city-reference/SKILL.md`

Use this reference whenever a new SFR (or major remodel) is on a parcel inside the Three Arch Bay (TAB) gated community. TAB is a **private HOA-governed overlay** that runs a parallel architectural review track on top of city review. Sequencing matters when a City hearing is required, and private-property consent remains separate from City zoning.

---

## Governing Authorities

| Body | Role |
|------|------|
| **City of Laguna Beach** | Zoning plan check, any applicable Design Review/ADR decision, CDP, building permit, and fire/WUI review. |
| **Three Arch Bay Association (TABA)** | Private HOA — runs its own Architectural Review process; issues a **comment letter** to the city; consent required for any work on TABA-owned property (private streets, common areas, slopes). |
| **Three Arch Bay Community Services District (TABCSD)** | Special district — operates water, sewer, refuse; consent required for any work on TABCSD-owned property; approval letter required separately from TABA. |

Follow the current City handout for comment/consent sequencing, but preserve Ord. 1732's qualifier: §25.44.060 describes TABA plan review and a comment letter **to the extent permitted by law**. Do not assume a City hearing if the project instead matches a Zone Clearance category, and do not convert private review into an unqualified statutory veto.

## TAB-Specific Development Standards (Ch. 25.44.050)

These standards REPLACE the R-1 baseline for TAB parcels. Confirm against the live municipal code at plan check.

| Standard | TAB rule | R-1 rule (for contrast) |
|----------|----------|-------------------------|
| **Site coverage** | Sliding scale 35-44%: ~44% max at lots ≤5,001 sf; ~35% max at lots ≥6,499 sf | Same scale, but interpolation breakpoints differ |
| **Side yard** | **5 ft minimum** | 10% of avg lot width, min 3 ft (4 ft if avg width ≥40 ft) |
| **Rear yard** | **25 ft minimum** | 20 ft (DRB may push to 25 ft for context) |
| **Bluff-top setback** | 25 ft (matches R-1 bluff rule) | 25 ft per Section 25.50.004(B)(4) |
| **Permitted projections** | 2.5 ft into required yards for eaves, cornices, chimneys, window seats, and decks >30 in above grade | Per Section 25.50.004 |
| **Parking** | **4 spaces required**; 2 may be in front yard or abutting street; TABA consent required if any space lands on TABA property | 2 covered + 1 uncovered at ≥3,600 sf GFA |
| **Roof pitch / roof deck** | Minimum **3:12**; roof decks prohibited. Design Review authority may approve not less than **2.5:12** to preserve identified neighborhood view corridors (§25.44.050(M)) | No equivalent TAB exception |

The remaining TAB rules are typically more permissive on yards (5 ft side / 25 ft rear) and more restrictive on parking.

## Current Design Review Findings (§25.44.060)

Ord. 1732 §37 amended rather than repealed §25.44.060. When a project independently matches a Design Review category, the reviewing authority must make the two current TAB findings in addition to §25.05.040(I):

1. The design conforms to the established small-scale building character, sensitive to existing topography and view preservation.
2. Landscape and hardscape address drainage, erosion prevention, wind barrier, shade, sound absorption, dust abatement, and glare reduction; required landscaping follows Resolution 89-104, and residential/landscape design preserves surrounding ocean views and uses vegetation to complement the viewshed where possible.

The former privacy/window-placement, solar visibility, general compatibility, and wall/fence findings were deleted. The former roof provision was moved and revised as an objective development standard in §25.44.050(M); it was not repealed.

## Soils and Geology (Ch. 25.44.070)

- Geotechnical report required for development on coastal or vacant lots in TAB.
- Reports must comply with Title 14 (city building code).
- Peer review deposit applies; the city retains its own reviewing geologist.

This is in addition to the standard hillside/bluff geotech package — TAB does not exempt any TAB parcel from city geotech requirements.

## Sequencing — Order of Operations for a New SFR in TAB

The TAB process runs **in parallel** with City zoning review. Follow the current handout at the applicable milestone and retain the ordinance's “to the extent permitted by law” qualifier. The full sequence:

```
[1] Applicant submits complete plan set to the City.
       |
       v
[2] City performs zoning plan check (R-1 baseline + Ch. 25.44 overlay).
       |
       v
[3] City sends digital plans to TABA (Architectural Review Committee)
    AND TABCSD (district-improvement review) — same day as zoning clearance.
       |
       v
[4a] To the extent permitted by law, TABA conducts its independent private
     architectural review and issues a COMMENT LETTER to the city. The city
     encourages TABA to complete review within 30 DAYS (per the city handout).
     Treat the letter as public correspondence, not an unqualified City veto.
       |
[4b] TABCSD reviews any utility / district improvement impacts; issues a
     consent or conditional-consent letter.
       |
[4c] If any portion of the project lands on TABA-owned property
     (private street ROW, common slopes, easements), TABA OWNER CONSENT
     is required in addition to the comment letter. Same rule for TABCSD
     property.
       |
       v
[5] If a current §25.05.040 Design Review category applies, City schedules
    the appropriate public hearing. Otherwise the City follows the matched
    Zone Clearance/ADR route. Applicable TABA/TABCSD correspondence remains
    part of the file under the City handout and governing property rights.
       |
       v
[6] If a City Design Review hearing occurs, the authority applies current
    Section 25.05.040(H), makes both §25.05.040(I) findings, and also makes the
    two current §25.44.060 TAB findings. TABA review remains independent.
       |
       v
[7] CDP determination (if appealable area, 10-working-day CCC appeal window).
       |
       v
[8] Building permit issuance — requires TABA owner consent recorded for any
    on-TABA-property improvements BEFORE permit issuance.
       |
       v
[9] Construction — gate access, hauling routes, and contractor parking are
    governed by separate TABA construction rules (not city-issued).
```

Common sequencing mistakes to flag at intake:

- Ignoring the City handout's TABA sequencing where private review is legally permitted, or treating the TABA letter as an unconditional statutory veto despite §25.44.060's express qualifier.
- Designing improvements (driveways, drainage, walls, landscaping) that cross onto TABA private streets, common slopes, or TABCSD utility easements without separate owner consent. The owner-consent letter is **separate** from the architectural comment letter.
- Assuming TAB parcels can skip city geotech (Ch. 25.44.070 is additive, not substitutive).

## Output Format Addition

When the parcel is inside Three Arch Bay, add a row to the city overlay output:

```markdown
**Three Arch Bay (Ch. 25.44):** PASS | FAIL | FLAG | N/A
- TAB-specific standards applied: site coverage / side yard 5 ft / rear yard 25 ft / parking 4 spaces / §25.44.050(M) roof rule
- If Design Review applies, both current §25.44.060 additional findings addressed
- TABA architectural comment letter: [On file | Pending | Not submitted]
- TABCSD consent letter: [On file | Pending | Not required]
- TABA / TABCSD owner consent (improvements on private property): [On file | Pending | N/A]
- TAB geotech (Ch. 25.44.070): [Submitted | Pending | N/A]
```

Return `FLAG` when a required TAB letter is missing and identify the current City/private milestone it blocks; do not invent a DRB hearing for a Zone Clearance project.
