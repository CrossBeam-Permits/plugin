# Laguna Beach - R-1 Development Standards for New SFR

Sources:

- LBMC Section 25.10.008, R-1 Residential Low Density Zone: the City source document (not included in this plugin)
- LBMC Section 25.50.004 and 25.50.020, General Yard and Open Space Provisions: the City source document (not included in this plugin)

Use this reference for new single-family dwellings on R-1 parcels. If the parcel is not R-1, identify the actual district and apply that district's standards instead.

---

## R-1 Lot and Density Standards

| Standard | Requirement |
|----------|-------------|
| Minimum lot area | 6,000 sf unless modified by the zoning map suffix under Section 25.10.008(K) |
| Minimum lot width | 70 ft |
| Cul-de-sac lot width at front property line | 40 ft |
| Minimum lot depth | 80 ft |
| Density | 6,000 sf lot area per dwelling unit |
| Dwellings per lot | No more than one dwelling on one lot unless a CUP under Section 25.05.030 authorizes otherwise; ADUs follow Ch. 25.17 |

## Height

Height is measured from finished or natural grade, whichever is more restrictive.

| Slope | Maximum height |
|-------|----------------|
| 0-15% | 25 ft |
| 16% | 26 ft |
| 17% | 27 ft |
| 18% | 28 ft |
| 19% | 29 ft |
| 20%+ | 30 ft |

Through lots measure height from the higher curb or street centerline.

### Additional Rear-Lot / Through-Lot Caps

Rear lot line above street:

| Slope | Height above rear lot line |
|-------|----------------------------|
| 0-5% | 25 ft |
| >5-10% | 20 ft |
| >10% | 15 ft |

Through lot height above upper curb elevation, and rear lot line below street height above curb elevation:

| Slope | Height cap |
|-------|------------|
| 0-5% | 25 ft |
| >5-10% | 22.5 ft |
| >10-15% | 20 ft |
| >15-20% | 17.5 ft |
| >20% | 15 ft |

### Front/Rear Yard Height Setback

Section 25.50.004(D) adds a sloping setback rule: where portions of a new building or addition are more than 15 ft above natural grade of the required front or rear setback, those portions need one additional foot of setback for every foot of height above 15 ft.

### Special Height Standards

Section 25.10.008(D)(5) cross-references Section 25.10.010 for specified areas with special height standards. Flag the parcel if it appears in a special-height area.

## Yards and Setbacks

| Yard | Requirement |
|------|-------------|
| Front | 20 ft minimum |
| Side | Each side yard at least 10% of average lot width, minimum 3 ft; if average lot width is 40 ft or greater, no side yard may be less than 4 ft |
| Irregular side yard | 10% of lot width at any point, minimum 3 ft |
| Rear | 20 ft minimum; DRB may increase to 25 ft where surrounding development patterns have larger rear yards |
| Rear alley garage/carport | 25 ft from opposite side of alley, and no closer than 5 ft to rear lot line |

Front yards and side yards may not be used for accessory buildings, equipment/storage, or regular vehicle parking, except the required additional parking space when approved under the parking chapter.

### Shallow Lots

For average depth under 100 ft, the required front or rear yard need not exceed 20% of average lot depth, but may not be reduced below 10 ft. This applies to either the front yard or the rear yard, not both.

### Steep Front-Yard Reduction

Section 25.50.004(E) allows a reduced front yard when natural grade 40 ft into the lot is at least 10 ft above or below the opposite curb elevation. A required garage/carport may be further reduced to 5 ft if the section's dimensional limits are met.

## Site Coverage

Building site coverage includes habitable enclosed building space plus covered off-street parking.

| Lot area | Maximum site coverage |
|----------|-----------------------|
| <=4,000 sf | 44% |
| 4,000-8,000 sf | `35 + ((8,000 - A) * 9 / 4,000)` percent, where A = lot area in sf |
| >=8,000 sf | 35% |

The DRB may modify the site-coverage requirement only when necessary to preserve views, preserve privacy, reduce heights, or maintain neighborhood development patterns.

### Oceanfront Lots

For oceanfront lots, Section 25.50.020 calculates site coverage using the lot area landward of the building setback lines described in Section 25.50.004(B)(1)-(3) or the oceanfront bluff, whichever is more restrictive. This can reduce the "A" area below the legal lot area.

## Landscaped Open Space

All new homes and major remodels must provide ground-to-sky landscaped open space:

| Lot area | Required landscaped area |
|----------|--------------------------|
| <=2,500 sf | 15% |
| >2,500 and <14,500 sf | `15% + [((A - 2,500) * 20) / 12,000]`, where A = lot size in sf |
| >=14,500 sf | 35% |

Landscaped open area must be living plants and must have a minimum dimension of 3 ft. It may be located in buildable or setback areas.

---

## Compatibility Finding

Section 25.10.008(M) requires all new development to be compatible with the immediate residential neighborhood with respect to scale, mass, and height. This is separate from numeric zoning compliance and is enforced through design review.
