# Foundations

Reference for California single-family residential permit review.
Source: 2025 California Residential Code (CRC), Title 24, Part 2.5.

---

## 1. General Requirements (CRC R401)

### 1.1 Application (CRC R401.1)

Foundation provisions control the design and construction of the foundation
and foundation spaces. Wood foundations are permitted only for buildings with
not more than two floors and a roof, with interior basement and foundation
walls at intervals not exceeding 50 ft. Wood foundations in SDC D0, D1, or D2
require engineered design.

### 1.2 Requirements [CA] (CRC R401.2)

Foundation construction shall accommodate all loads per Section R301 and
transmit resulting loads to supporting soil. Fill soils supporting footings
shall be designed, installed, and tested per accepted engineering practice.

### 1.3 Surface Drainage (CRC R401.3)

- Grade shall fall not fewer than 6 inches within the first 10 feet from
  foundation walls.
- Where physical barriers prevent this, drains or swales are required.
- Impervious surfaces within 10 ft of foundation shall slope >= 2% away.

### 1.4 Soil Tests (CRC R401.4)

Where expansive, compressible, or shifting soils are likely present, the
building official may require soil testing. Where soil testing is performed
for a building in SDC C or greater, the geotechnical report shall include site
class and SDS determination under CBC 1613 and the applicable CRC seismic
design-category assignment table.

**Presumptive Bearing Values** (Table R401.4.1(1)):

| Material | Bearing Pressure (psf) |
|---|---|
| Crystalline bedrock | 12,000 |
| Sedimentary / foliated rock | 4,000 |
| Sandy gravel / gravel (GW, GP) | 3,000 |
| Sand, silty sand, clayey sand, silty/clayey gravel (SW, SP, SM, SC, GM, GC) | 2,000 |
| Clay, sandy/silty clay, silt (CL, ML, MH, CH) | 1,500 |

### 1.5 California Soil Investigation Requirements [CA]

- Where a tentative and final subdivision map and the local ordinance trigger
  apply, a state-registered civil engineer prepares the preliminary soil report;
  the agency may grant the express waiver (CRC R401.4.1.1.1).
- Where that report indicates critically expansive soils or other soil problems
  that could cause structural defects, the ordinance requires a registered-civil-
  engineer individual-lot investigation with corrective recommendations
  (CRC R401.4.1.1.2).
- The building official applies the section's approval criterion and the
  ordinance's permit condition; disputes follow the local appeals-board route
  (CRC R401.4.1.1.3).

## 2. Materials (CRC R402)

### 2.1 Concrete (CRC R402.2)

Minimum specified compressive strength f'c:

| Location | Negligible Weathering | Moderate | Severe |
|---|---|---|---|
| Basement walls, foundations (not exposed) | 2,500 psi | 2,500 psi | 2,500 psi |
| Basement/interior slabs on grade (not garage) | 2,500 psi | 2,500 psi | 2,500 psi |
| Foundation walls exposed to weather | 2,500 psi | 3,000 psi | 3,000 psi |
| Porches, carport slabs, steps, garage floors | 2,500 psi | 3,000 psi | 3,500 psi |

(Table R402.2)

Concrete in moderate/severe weathering exposed to weather shall be
air-entrained (5-7% by volume).

### 2.2 Masonry (CRC R402.4)

Minimum specified compressive strength: 1,500 psi.

## 3. Footings (CRC R403)

### 3.1 General (CRC R403.1)

Exterior walls shall be supported on continuous solid or fully grouted masonry
or concrete footings, crushed-stone footings, wood foundations, or another
approved structural system within the applicable section scope.
Footings shall be supported on undisturbed natural soils or engineered fill.

### 3.2 Minimum Size (CRC R403.1.1)

Concrete footings governed by R403.1.1 have minimum dimensions of 12 inches
wide x 6 inches thick.

Footing width and thickness from Table R403.1(1) based on:
- Ground snow load or roof live load
- Number of stories and foundation type (slab, crawl space, basement)
- Load-bearing value of soil (1,500 to 4,000 psf)

**Common California values** (20 psf roof LL, light-frame):

| Stories | Soil >= 2,000 psf | Soil = 1,500 psf |
|---|---|---|
| 1-story slab-on-grade | 12 x 6 | 12 x 6 |
| 1-story with crawl space | 12 x 6 | 12 x 6 |
| 2-story slab-on-grade | 12 x 6 | 13 x 6 |
| 2-story with crawl space | 12 x 6 | 15 x 6 |
| 3-story slab-on-grade | 12 x 6 | 16 x 6 |

(Table R403.1(1), width x thickness in inches)

Footing projection P shall be >= 2 inches and shall not exceed footing
thickness.

### 3.3 Continuous Footings in SDC D0, D1, D2 (CRC R403.1.2)

Exterior walls and the table-dependent interior braced wall panels in SDC D0-D2
shall be supported by continuous footings under Table R403.1.2.

Key rules from Table R403.1.2:
- Continuous footings under exterior walls: **always required** (all SDCs,
  all building sizes).
- Continuous footings under interior braced wall panels:
  - 1-story, <= 50 ft plan: not required in D0, D1, D2
  - 1-story, > 50 ft plan: required in D0, D1, D2
  - 2-story, <= 50 ft plan: required only in D2
  - 2-story, > 50 ft plan: required in D0, D1, D2
  - 3-story: always required

For every `R` cell carrying footnote a, qualifying interior braced wall panels
may be supported on continuous foundations at intervals not exceeding 50 ft
only when all three cripple-wall, floor-support, and bracing-line-distance
conditions are met.

### 3.4 Footing Reinforcement in SDC D0, D1, D2 (CRC R403.1.3)

Concrete footings in SDC D0-D2 shall have minimum reinforcement:

**Concrete stem walls with concrete footings** (CRC R403.1.3.1):
- The following arrangement applies where a construction joint occurs between
  the concrete footing and stem wall.
- No. 4 vertical bars at 4 ft o.c. max, standard hook to bottom of footing,
  extend 14 inches into stem wall.
- No. 4 horizontal bar within 12 inches of top of stem wall.
- No. 4 horizontal bar 3-4 inches from bottom of footing.

**Masonry stem walls with concrete footings** (CRC R403.1.3.2):
- Same vertical and horizontal reinforcement as concrete stem walls.
- Masonry stem walls shall be solid grouted.

**Slabs-on-ground with turned-down footings** (CRC R403.1.3.3):
- Monolithic: 1 No. 4 top + 1 No. 4 bottom, or 1 No. 5 or 2 No. 4 in
  middle third.
- Non-monolithic: No. 3+ vertical dowels with standard hooks at 4 ft o.c.

**Interior bearing/braced wall footings** (CRC R403.1.3.4):
- Interior footings cast monolithically with slab: extend >= 12 inches
  below top of slab.

**Reinforcement requirements** (CRC R403.1.3.5):
- Steel: ASTM A615, A706M, or A996M; minimum Grade 40 (40,000 psi yield).
- Cover: 3 inches against earth; 1-1/2 inches (No. 5 and smaller) or
  2 inches (No. 6+) for removable forms exposed to weather; 3/4 inch for
  forms not exposed to weather.

### 3.5 Minimum Depth (CRC R403.1.4)

Exterior footings: not less than **12 inches below undisturbed ground
surface**.

Where R403.1.4.1 applies, use the locally adopted Table R301.2 frost-line depth:
extend below it, use R403.3, follow ASCE 32, or bear on solid rock. Apply both
qualifying small free-standing-accessory-structure exceptions; foundations may
not bear on soil that is not permanently frozen. Do not infer a statewide
0-6-in frost depth.

### 3.6 Footing Slope (CRC R403.1.5)

- Top surface: level.
- Bottom surface: not to exceed 1:10 slope (10%).
- Step where elevation change needed.

## 4. Foundation Anchorage (CRC R403.1.6)

### 4.1 General Anchor Bolt Requirements

Wood sole plates at all exterior walls on monolithic slabs, wood sole plates of
interior braced wall panels on monolithic slabs, and all wood sill plates use
nuts and washers with the following bolts or an approved anchor/strap providing
equivalent anchorage. Exception 1 applies only to connecting walls 24 inches or
shorter, and Exception 2 only to connecting walls 12 inches or shorter; use the
connector arrangement stated in the applicable exception:
- **1/2-inch diameter** anchor bolts
- Spaced not greater than **6 feet on center**
- Extending not less than **7 inches** into concrete
- Located in **middle third** of plate width
- Not fewer than **2 bolts per plate section**
- One bolt within **12 inches** of each end of plate section (and not less
  than 7 bolt diameters from end)

### 4.2 Enhanced Anchorage in SDC C, D0, D1, D2 (CRC R403.1.6.1)

Additional requirements for wood light-frame in SDC D0-D2 (and townhouses
in SDC C):

1. **Plate washers** (0.229" x 3" x 3") required for all anchor bolts on
   braced wall lines (or approved anchor straps). Cut washers permitted for
   non-braced wall lines.
2. Interior braced wall plates: bolts at **6 ft o.c. max**, within **12
   inches** of plate ends, on continuous foundation.
3. Interior bearing wall sole plates: same as item 2.
4. Buildings over two stories: max bolt spacing = **4 feet o.c.**
5. Stepped cripple walls per R602.11.2.

## 5. Footings on Slopes (CRC R403.1.7)

Applies where slopes exceed 1V:3H (33%).

- **Foundation elevation** [CA]: On a graded site, the top of exterior
  foundation shall extend above the street gutter at the point of discharge or
  the inlet of an approved drainage device by >= 12 inches + 2%; the building
  official may approve an alternate elevation where drainage is demonstrated
  (CRC R403.1.7.3).
- Clearance from ascending slopes and setback from descending slopes per
  Figure R403.1.7.1.

## 6. Expansive Soils (CRC R403.1.8)

Foundations and floor slabs on expansive soils shall be designed under CBC
1808.6 unless the building official approves the prior-performance exception.

Soils are classified as expansive when meeting Items 1, 2, and 3 together, or
the Item 4 alternative:
1. Plasticity Index >= 15 (ASTM D4318)
2. > 10% passing No. 200 sieve
3. > 10% particles < 5 micrometers
4. Expansion Index > 20 (ASTM D4829)

## 7. Foundation Walls (CRC R404)

### 7.1 Concrete and Masonry Foundation Walls (CRC R404.1)

Engineered design required when:
- Walls subject to hydrostatic pressure; or
- Walls supporting > 48 inches unbalanced backfill without permanent lateral
  support at top or bottom.

### 7.2 Plain Masonry Foundation Walls

Minimum nominal wall thickness from Table R404.1.2.1(1), based on:
- Unsupported wall height (5-9 ft)
- Unbalanced backfill height
- Exact table soil groups, including GW/GP/SW/SP, the middle group containing
  GM/GC/SM-SC, and the group distinguishing MH, ML-CL, and inorganic CL

Example values (8 ft unsupported height):

| Backfill Height | GW/GP/SW/SP | Middle table soil group | MH / ML-CL / inorganic CL group |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4 ft | 6 solid or 8 | 6 solid or 8 | 8 |
| 5 ft | 6 solid or 8 | 10 | 12 |
| 6 ft | 10 | 12 | 12 solid |
| 7 ft | 12 | 12 solid | Use a reinforced-masonry table or provide a design |
| 8 ft | 10 grout | 12 grout | Use a reinforced-masonry table or provide a design |

(Table R404.1.2.1(1))

### 7.3 Reinforced Masonry Foundation Walls

8-inch walls with d >= 5 inches: Table R404.1.2.1(2).
10-inch walls with d >= 6.75 inches: Table R404.1.2.1(3).

Reinforcement spacing in SDC D0-D2 shall not exceed 48 inches.

## 8. Foundation Drainage (CRC R405)

### 8.1 Requirements (CRC R405.1)

Drains required around concrete or masonry foundations that retain earth and
enclose habitable/usable spaces below grade.

Acceptable systems:
- Drainage tiles
- Gravel or crushed stone drains
- Perforated pipe
- Other approved systems

Installation:
- At or below top of footing, or below bottom of slab.
- Discharge by gravity or mechanical means to approved drainage system.
- Gravel/crushed stone drains: extend >= 1 ft beyond outside edge of
  footing, >= 6 inches above top of footing, covered with approved filter
  membrane.
- Perforated pipe: set on >= 2 inches of washed gravel, covered with >=
  6 inches of same material.

**Exception**: Drainage not required on well-drained Group I soils
(GW, GP, SW, SP, GM, SM per Table R401.4.1(2)).

## 9. Slab-on-Grade (CRC R506)

### 9.1 General (CRC R506.1)

Concrete slab-on-ground floors: minimum **3-1/2 inches thick**. For
expansive soils, see Section R403.1.8. Compressive strength per R402.2.

### 9.2 Site Preparation (CRC R506.3)

- Remove all vegetation, topsoil, and foreign material within foundation
  walls.
- **Fill**: free of vegetation/foreign material, compacted for uniform
  support. Max depth: 24 inches (clean sand/gravel) or 8 inches (earth)
  unless approved (CRC R506.3.1).
- **Base course**: 4-inch-thick clean graded sand, gravel, crushed stone,
  crushed concrete, or crushed blast-furnace slag passing a 2-inch sieve,
  placed on prepared subgrade where slab is below grade (CRC R506.3.2).
  - Exception: Not required on well-drained Group I soils.

### 9.3 Vapor Retarder (CRC R506.3.3)

Minimum **6 mil polyethylene** (or approved vapor retarder), joints lapped
>= 6 inches, placed between slab and base course (or prepared subgrade).

**Exceptions** — vapor retarder not required for:
1. Garages, utility buildings, unheated accessory structures
2. Unheated storage rooms < 70 sf and carports
3. Driveways, walks, patios not likely to be enclosed/heated later
4. Where approved by building official based on local conditions

**[CA] Capillary break** (CRC R506.3.3.1): When vapor retarder is required,
a capillary break shall be installed per California Green Building Standards
Code, Chapter 4, Division 4.5.

### 9.4 Reinforcement (CRC R506.3.4)

Where provided, reinforcement shall be supported to remain in place from
center to upper one-third of slab during concrete placement.

## 10. Frost-Protected Shallow Foundations (CRC R403.3)

Permitted for heated buildings (monthly mean temp >= 64 deg F) in lieu of
extending footings below frost line. Not for unheated spaces (garages,
carports, porches).

**California applicability**: All counties except Nevada and Sierra have
air-freezing index <= 1,500, requiring only 12-inch minimum footing depth
and R-4.5 vertical insulation (Table R403.3(1)).
