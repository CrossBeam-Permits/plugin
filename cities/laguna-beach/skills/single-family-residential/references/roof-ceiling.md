# Roof-Ceiling Construction and Roof Assemblies

> California Residential Code (CRC), Title 24, Part 2.5
> 2025 Edition (Effective January 1, 2026)

This reference covers wood roof framing (rafters, ceiling joists, ridges, trusses), roof sheathing, roof ventilation, attic access, and roof covering requirements for single-family residential construction in California.

---

## 1. Ridge Requirements

A ridge board connecting opposing rafters shall be (CRC R802.3):

- Not less than **1 inch** nominal thickness
- Not less in depth than the cut end of the rafter

### Structural vs. Non-Structural Ridge

- **Non-structural ridge board**: Permitted when ceiling joists or rafter ties provide continuous ties across the structure per R802.5.2
- **Structural ridge beam**: Required when ceiling joists or rafter ties do NOT provide continuous ties. Must be designed per accepted engineering practice and supported at each end by a wall or column (CRC R802.3)

Where roof pitch is **less than 3:12**, structural members supporting rafters,
such as ridges, hips, and valleys, shall be designed as beams (CRC R802.4.4)
and provide the bearing required by R802.6.

### Rafter Span Adjustment Factor (Table R802.4.1(9))

Where ceiling joists or rafter ties are located above the top of the wall
plate, multiply the tabulated rafter span by the Table R802.4.1(9) adjustment
factor for HC/HR: tie height above the support-wall plate divided by roof-ridge
height above that plate (CRC R802.4.1, footnote a).

### Rafter Framing

- Rafters shall be framed opposite from each other to the ridge board, offset not more than **1-1/2 inches** (CRC R802.4.2)
- Connected with collar tie or ridge strap per R802.4.6 or directly opposite to a gusset plate

### Ceiling Joist and Rafter Connections

Ceiling joists and rafter connections at the heel joint (bird's mouth) are governed by Table R802.5.2(1), which specifies number and type of nails based on rafter span, spacing, and roof slope (CRC R802.5.2).

**Rafter ties**: Not less than 2x4, installed per Table R802.5.2(1), maximum 24" o.c. (CRC R802.5.2.2).

## 4. Bearing

Rafter and ceiling joist bearing requirements (CRC R802.6):

| Bearing Surface       | Minimum Bearing |
|-----------------------|-----------------|
| Wood or metal         | 1-1/2 inches    |
| Masonry or concrete   | 3 inches        |

A 2" nominal sill plate shall provide minimum 48 sq in bearing area on masonry/concrete. Where roof pitch >= 3:12 with continuous tension ties, vertical bearing of rafter against ridge board satisfies this requirement (CRC R802.6).

---

## 5. Cutting, Drilling, and Notching

Roof framing members comply with the same limitations as floor joists per R502.8.1 (CRC R802.7.1):

- Notches: max 1/6 depth and 1/3 member depth in length, not in the middle
  1/3 of span; end notches max 1/4 depth; no tension-side notch in a member 4"
  nominal or thicker except at its end
- Bored holes: max 1/3 depth, min 2" from edges, other holes, and notches

**Cantilevered rafter exception**: Notches on cantilevered portions permitted if remaining rafter depth >= 3-1/2" and cantilever does not exceed 24" (CRC R802.7.1.1).

**Ceiling joist taper cut**: End taper cuts shall not exceed 1/4 of member depth (CRC R802.7.1.2).

Engineered wood products (trusses, I-joists, LVL, glulam, CLT) shall not be cut, notched, or drilled except per manufacturer or registered design professional (CRC R802.7.2).

---

## 6. Lateral Support and Bridging

Roof framing and ceiling joists with a depth-to-thickness ratio exceeding
**5:1** shall have lateral support at bearing points (CRC R802.8). Roof trusses
are excepted and are braced under R802.10.3.

Rafters and ceiling joists with depth-to-thickness ratio exceeding **6:1** require bridging at intervals not exceeding **8 feet** — solid blocking, diagonal bridging, or continuous 1x3 strip (CRC R802.8.1).

---

## 7. Framing of Openings

Openings in roof/ceiling framing (skylights, chimneys, etc.) require header and trimmer joists (CRC R802.9):

- Header span <= 4 ft: single member permitted
- Single trimmer: permitted when header is within 3 ft of bearing
- Header span > 4 ft: doubled trimmers and headers
- Header span > 6 ft: approved hangers required
- Tail joists > 12 ft: supported by framing anchors or 2x2 ledger strips

## 8. Roof Sheathing

### Lumber Sheathing (Table R803.1)

| Rafter/Beam Spacing | Minimum Net Thickness |
|---------------------|-----------------------|
| 24"                 | 5/8"                  |
| 48"                 | 1-1/2" T&G            |
| 60"                 | 1-1/2" T&G            |
| 72"                 | 1-1/2" T&G            |

Spaced lumber sheathing is permitted for wood shingle/shake roofing per R905.7/R905.8 but is **not allowed in SDC D2** (CRC R803.1).

### Wood Structural Panel Sheathing

- Shall conform to DOC PS 1, DOC PS 2, CSA O325, or CSA O437 (CRC R803.2.1)
- Maximum spans per Table R503.2.1.1(1) or APA E30 (CRC R803.2.2)
- Shall not cantilever more than **9 inches** beyond gable end wall unless supported by overhang framing (CRC R803.2.3)

### Common Panel Sheathing Spans

| Span Rating | Min Panel Thickness | Max Roof Span (with edge support) |
|-------------|--------------------|------------------------------------|
| 24/0        | 3/8"               | 24"                                |
| 32/16       | 15/32" or 1/2"     | 32"                                |
| 40/20       | 19/32" or 5/8"     | 40"                                |
| 48/24       | 23/32" or 3/4"     | 48"                                |

### Roof Sheathing Fastening

Select the Table R602.3(1) Item 31 or 32 row that matches the panel thickness
and construction. Those rows permit 8d common or RSRS-01 roof nails at 6" o.c.
at panel edges and intermediate supports. At gable ends and within 48" of roof
edges/ridges where wind speed > 130 mph Exposure B (or > 110 mph Exposure C),
footnote f reduces spacing to 4" o.c. where framing specific gravity is at
least 0.42; use RSRS-03 where specific gravity is at least 0.35 but below 0.42.

## 9. Roof Ventilation

### Minimum Vent Area

The minimum net free ventilating area shall be **1/150** of the area of the vented space (CRC R806.2).

**Exception — 1/300 ratio** permitted when BOTH conditions are met:
1. In Climate Zones 6, 7, and 8: Class I or II vapor retarder installed on warm-in-winter side of ceiling
2. 40-50% of required vent area located in upper portion of attic (not more than 3 ft below ridge), balance in bottom 1/3 of attic space

### Vent Openings

- Minimum dimension: 1/16" min, 1/4" max
- Larger openings: corrosion-resistant screening with 1/16" to 1/4" openings
- Openings shall open directly to outside air (CRC R806.1)

### Insulation Clearance

Not less than **1-inch** space between insulation and roof sheathing at eave/cornice vents (CRC R806.3).

### California WUI Requirement

In Wildland-Urban Interface areas, ventilation openings shall comply with Part 7, California Wildland-Urban Interface Code to resist burning ember intrusion (CRC R806.1.1).

### Unvented Attic Assemblies

Unvented attics and enclosed rafter assemblies are permitted under CRC R806.5.
The following is a high-level screen, not a substitute for checking every
applicable subitem, especially Items 5.1 through 5.3:

1. Space is completely within building thermal envelope
2. No interior Class I vapor retarders on ceiling side
3. Wood shingles/shakes: minimum 1/4" vented airspace above sheathing
4. Climate Zones 5-8: air-impermeable insulation shall be Class II vapor retarder
5. Insulation and condensation control comply with Item 5.3 plus either Item
   5.1 or 5.2, including every applicable vapor-diffusion-port, membrane,
   weather-protection, clearance, slope, insulation-placement, conditioned-air,
   and temperature-analysis condition

### Condensation Control for Unvented Assemblies (Table R806.5)

| Climate Zone         | Min Rigid Board / Air-Impermeable R-Value |
|---------------------|-------------------------------------------|
| 2B, 3B (tile only)  | None required                             |
| 1, 2A, 2B, 3A-3C   | R-5                                       |
| 4C                  | R-10                                      |
| 4A, 4B              | R-15                                      |

## 10. Attic Access

Buildings with attics having >= 30" vertical height over >= 30 sq ft shall have an access opening (CRC R807.1):

| Requirement              | Minimum Dimension              |
|--------------------------|-------------------------------|
| Rough-framed opening     | 22" x 30"                     |
| Location                 | Hallway or other ready access  |
| Wall opening             | 22" wide x 30" high           |
| Headroom above ceiling access | 30" minimum (along one side) |

See California Mechanical Code for additional access requirements where mechanical equipment is located in attics.

## 11. Roof Covering Requirements

### Fire Classification

Roof decks shall be covered with materials per R904 or R905. Minimum Class A, B, or C roofing required where edge of roof deck is less than 3 ft from lot line (CRC R902.1).

#### California-Specific Requirements

- **Fire Hazard Severity Zones / WUI areas**: Roofing shall also comply with Part 7, California Wildland-Urban Interface Code (CRC R902.1.1)
- **All other areas**: At least Class C is required for every new structure and
  for roof covering applied in alteration, repair, or replacement of an
  existing structure. Where more than 50% of the roof is replaced in one year,
  the entire roof must comply (CRC R902.1.2)
- **Fire-retardant-treated shakes/shingles**: Must comply with ICC-ES EG107 and
  have passed at least 5 years of the 10-year natural-weathering test stated in
  CRC R902.2

### Roof Covering Types and Minimum Slopes

| Covering Type                    | Section | Minimum Slope |
|---------------------------------|---------|---------------|
| Asphalt shingles                | R905.2  | 2:12          |
| Clay and concrete tile          | R905.3  | 2.5:12        |
| Metal roof shingles             | R905.4  | 3:12          |
| Mineral-surfaced roll roofing   | R905.5  | 1:12          |
| Slate shingles                  | R905.6  | 4:12          |
| Wood shingles                   | R905.7  | 3:12          |
| Wood shakes                     | R905.8  | 3:12          |
| Metal roof panels               | R905.10 | 3:12 for lapped nonsoldered seams without lap sealant; 1/2:12 with applied lap sealant; 1/4:12 for standing seam |

### Underlayment

Underlayment required for most roof covering types per Section R905.1.1 and Tables R905.1.1(1), R905.1.1(2), and R905.1.1(3). General rules:

- **Slopes 2:12 to < 4:12** (asphalt shingles): Two-layer underlayment application required
- **Slopes >= 4:12**: Single-layer underlayment, lapped 2", end laps 4" offset 6 ft
- **Self-adhering polymer modified bitumen** (ASTM D1970) is a listed
  application option across the otherwise permitted asphalt-shingle slope range

In wind design areas, enhanced underlayment application and fastening is required.

### Asphalt Shingle Wind Resistance

Asphalt shingles tested per ASTM D7158, classified per Table R905.2.4.1:

| Max Ultimate Wind Speed | ASTM D7158 Class |
|------------------------|-------------------|
| 110 mph                | D, G, or H        |
| 129 mph                | G or H             |
| 142 mph                | G or H             |
| 168 mph                | H                  |

### Asphalt Shingle Fastening

Use the specified corrosion-resistant roofing nails complying with ASTM F1667,
minimum 12 gage with 3/8" heads. Penetration is at least 3/4" into sheathing,
or through sheathing less than 3/4" thick. Follow the manufacturer's greater
minimum where specified, with at least four fasteners per strip shingle or two
per individual shingle (CRC R905.2.5, R905.2.6).

### Drip Edge

Required at eaves and rakes of shingle roofs. Must extend >= 1/4" below sheathing and >= 2" back onto roof deck, fastened at 12" o.c. Underlayment installed OVER drip edge at eaves and UNDER drip edge at rakes (CRC R905.2.8.5).

## 12. Flashing and Drainage

### Flashing Locations

Required at (CRC R903.2.1):

- Wall and roof intersections
- Changes in roof slope or direction
- Around roof openings
- Where eave of sloped roof intersects vertical sidewall

Metal flashing: minimum 0.019" (No. 26 galvanized sheet) corrosion-resistant metal.

### Crickets and Saddles

Required on ridge side of any chimney or penetration more than **30 inches
wide** measured perpendicular to slope (CRC R903.2.2). Exception: unit
skylights installed in accordance with R324.6 and flashed per manufacturer's
instructions.

### Roof Drainage

Unless roofs are sloped to drain over edges, roof drains shall be installed at each low point (CRC R903.4). Secondary overflow drains or scuppers required where roof perimeter could entrap water (CRC R903.4.1).

## 13. Wood Trusses

Wood trusses shall be designed per accepted engineering practice. Metal-plate-connected trusses shall comply with ANSI/TPI 1 (CRC R802.10.2).

### Truss Design Drawings Required

Truss design drawings shall be prepared by a registered design professional
under R802.10.2 and include the minimum information in R802.10.1:

1. Slope/depth, span, and spacing
2. Joint locations
3. Required bearing widths
4. Design loads (top/bottom chord live/dead, concentrated, wind/seismic)
5. Design-value adjustments for conditions of use
6. Each reaction force and direction
7. Lumber size, species, and grade for each member
8. Joint connector details
9. Connection requirements
10. Calculated deflection ratios
11. Maximum axial compression forces for bracing design
12. Required permanent bracing locations

### Truss Alterations

Truss members shall **not be cut, notched, spliced, or altered** without approval of a registered design professional (CRC R802.10.4).

## 14. Roof Tie-Down / Uplift Resistance

Roof assemblies shall have uplift resistance per R802.11. Connections from roof to wall shall resist uplift forces determined from Table R802.11 or accepted engineering practice (CRC R802.11).

**Exception**: Toe-nailing per Table R602.3(1) is sufficient where specific gravity >= 0.42 and uplift does not exceed 200 lbs per rafter/truss per Table R802.11 (CRC R802.11, Exception 1).

For trusses, determine uplift forces from the truss design drawings or
construction documents, Table R802.11 where applicable, or accepted
engineering practice (CRC R802.11.1). For individual rafters, determine uplift
forces from Table R802.11 or accepted engineering practice; roof-system beam
connections require engineered design (CRC R802.11.2).
