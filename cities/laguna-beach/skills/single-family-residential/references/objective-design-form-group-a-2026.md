# Laguna Beach Objective Design Standards — Form Group A

**Source:** *Objective Design Standards for Single-Family and Two-Family Residential Development*, Chapter 3A, City document 26790, published August 2026.

Use this reference only after the applicant has selected Form Group A: **Arts and Crafts, Shingle, or Bungalow**. Load it with `objective-design-standards-2026.md`; do not combine its standards with another form group. Ask the applicant to choose when the form group is not stated—the source does not assign groups by zone, neighborhood, or context.

Section anchors: §3A.1 Roofs; §3A.2 Exterior Walls; §3A.3 Exterior Openings; §3A.4 Porches, Decks, and Balconies; §3A.5 Equipment Screening; §3A.6 Colors. The applicant's checklist must identify the plan-sheet location for every applicable standard.

### 3A.1.1 (PDF p. 28)

Roofs, allowed types. Utilize at least one of the following roof types. All other roof types are prohibited:
a. Front-gabled.
b. Cross-gabled.
c. Hipped.

### 3A.1.2 (PDF p. 28)

Roof material. Provide one of the following roof materials:
a. Composite shingles and tiles that simulate shake, slate, and concrete.
b. Concrete tiles.
c. Metal roofing and standard seam metal roofing meeting the color standards of §3A.6.
d. Slate shingles.
e. Dimensional architectural asphalt shingles.

### 3A.1.3 (PDF p. 28)

Roof pitch. Roof pitches shall be a minimum 3:12 slope and a maximum 8:12 slope.

### 3A.1.4 (PDF p. 29)

Roof rake. Roof rakes shall comply with all of the following standards:
• Measured from the main roof form to the exterior wall, rakes shall horizontally project a minimum of 18 inches to a maximum of three feet.
• Measured from the dormer to the exterior wall, rakes shall horizontally project a minimum of eight inches and a maximum of 14 inches.

### 3A.1.5 (PDF p. 29)

Roof eave. Roof eaves shall comply with all of the following standards.
• Measured from the main roof form, eaves shall horizontally project a minimum of two-and-a-half feet.
• At the dormer, eaves shall horizontally project a minimum of eight inches.

### 3A.2.1 (PDF p. 30)

Exterior wall materials and finishes. Utilize any of the following materials for exterior walls. At building corners, all materials shall wrap around the corner for a minimum distance of two feet.
a. Wood or fiber cement board and batten, minimum 50 percent of the total elevation surface area.
b. Wood or fiber cement lap siding, minimum 50 percent of the total elevation surface area.
c. Wood or fiber cement shingle, minimum 50 percent of the total elevation surface area.
d. Natural finish stucco, or a pigmented and painted stucco utilizing one of the following finishes and a maximum 50 percent of the total elevation surface area.
i. 16/20 Medium Sand Float finish.
ii. 16/20 Heavy Sand Float finish.
iii. 20/30 Fine Sand Float finish.
iv. English finish.
v. Light Dash finish.
vi. Medium Dash finish.
vii. Heavy Dash finish.
viii. Tunnel Dash finish.
ix. Santa Barbara or Mission finish.
x. Smooth finish.

### 3A.2.2 (PDF p. 30)

Optional: additional wall material. If provided, additional wall materials shall not exceed 30 percent of each elevation surface area and be composed of one of the following. At building corners, all materials shall wrap around the corner for a minimum distance of two feet.
a. Concrete, painted per the requirements of §3A.6.
b. River rock.
c. Brick.
d. Cast stone.
e. Stone.

### 3A.3.1 (PDF p. 31)

Exterior openings: percentage of wall. Exterior openings on all building walls with the same orientation, inclusive of doors and windows, shall be a minimum of 15 percent and a maximum of 35 percent of the total wall area.

### 3A.3.2 (PDF p. 31)

Exterior openings types. Provide a minimum of three distinct exterior opening sizes, inclusive of doors and windows.

### 3A.3.3 (PDF p. 31)

Exterior opening shapes. Openings shall be rectangular or circular in shape.

### 3A.3.4 (PDF p. 31)

Exterior opening surrounds. Opening surrounds, when provided at the exterior edge of a window or door opening, shall comply with the dimensions per the table below:
Window Surround Dimension Apron 3 inches minimum Head 3 inches minimum Jamb 3 inches minimum

### 3A.3.5 (PDF p. 31)

Exterior opening materials. With the exception of the front door, provide one of the following exterior opening materials with clear glazing:
a. Wood, pre-finished, stained, or painted per the color requirements of §3A.6.
b. Metal-clad wood, pre-finished or painted per the color requirements of §3A.6.
c. Steel, pre-finished or painted per the color requirements of §3A.6.
d. Composite, acrylic, or fiberglass set a minimum of two inches inward from the adjacent exterior surface and finished per the color requirements of §3A.6.

### 3A.3.6 (PDF p. 32)

Window types. Provide at least one and a maximum of two of the following window types:
a. Awning.
b. Casement.
c. Double-hung.
d. Fixed.
e. Ganged, with a maximum three windows either fixed all across, casement all across, or casement sandwiched by fixed.
f. Single-hung.

### 3A.3.7 (PDF p. 32)

Window recess. Not applicable.

### 3A.3.8 (PDF p. 32)

Window sashes, rails, and stiles. Window sashes, rails, and stiles shall be a minimum of two inches wide (jambs) or two inches high (sills or heads).

### 3A.3.9 (PDF p. 32)

Window muntins, mullions, and sticking. Window muntins, mullions, and sticking shall comply with all of the following standards:
• Provide muntins, mullions, or sticking to create true divides at all window sashes with a glazing area greater than one square foot in size, as well as at glazing at doors with a glazing area greater than one square foot in size. When a double-hung window, transom window, or ganged window is utilized, a divided window sash over an undivided window may be utilized.
• Window muntins, mullions, and sticking that divide window sash shall have a minimum ½-inch profile.
• Simulated, snap-on, and between glass muntins or sticking is prohibited.

### 3A.3.10 (PDF p. 32)

Door leaves. Provide no more than two contiguous door leaves.

### 3A.3.11 (PDF p. 32)

Door recess. Not applicable.

### 3A.3.12 (PDF p. 32)

Optional: transoms and sidelites. If provided, transom windows and/or sidelites shall comply with all of the following standards.
• The maximum width of sidelites shall be half the width of the door.
Transom windows shall not extend beyond the door or sidelite edges, and shall not exceed 18 inches in height.
• Provide one of the following lite types.
i. Vision glass, with a minimum of three parts.
ii. Half glass, with a minimum of six parts.
iii. Full glass, with a minimum of four inches in width.

### 3A.3.13 (PDF p. 32)

Door location. Doors shall be placed a minimum 18 inches distant from a wall.

### 3A.4.1 (PDF p. 33)

Entry element. Provide two of the following entry elements:
a. Street-facing entries placed either at grade or a maximum 18 inches above the adjacent finished grade shall comply with at least one of the following.
i. Recessed a minimum of two feet and a maximum of five feet from a building plane that faces a street.
ii. Framed by an arched or segmental arched opening(s).
iii. Incorporated into a projecting minor mass that does not exceed the eave line by more than three feet or a tower element that does not exceed the otherwise allowed height limit.
b. An entry path no greater than four feet in width shall connect at least one street to the primary residential entry. Within ten feet of the entry, the path may exceed this width but shall not exceed in this area adjacent to the entry a width of more than eight feet. The entry path shall incorporate one or more of the following materials:
i. Colored or stamped concrete.
ii. Scored concrete with score lines placed no more than 30 inches apart.
iii. Concrete or stone pavers no larger than 30 inches by 30 inches.
iv. Brick used as a uniform or as an accent material.
c. Porches with columns and/or piers, comprising all of the following standards:
• Porches shall make up a maximum of 60 percent of the primary frontage.
• Columns shall be one of the following shapes.
1. H-column.
2. I-beam.
3. Square.
• Columns shall be stone or wood that is stained or painted per the requirements in §3A.6, or a combination of both.
d. Roof overhangs comprising all of the following standards:
• Overhangs shall provide a minimum horizontal projection of three feet.
• Overhangs may be supported by brackets.
e. Trellises, a minimum of three feet in depth, either stained, natural, or painted per the requirements in §3A.6.

### 3A.4.2 (PDF p. 34)

Fence and wall materials. Fences, walls, and guardrails facing streets or rights-of-way shall be constructed of the following materials or any combination of the following materials:
a. Wood, stained or painted per the requirements in §3A.6.
b. Stucco or stucco plaster, integrally colored or painted and matching the same color as the main residential structure.
c. Stone, face stone, and/or veneer stone.
d. Brick utilizing a bond pattern, whether regular or irregular, inclusive of both stretchers and headers, or any of the following: chequered brickwork, corbelling, clinkers, herringbone brickwork, honeycomb brickwork, lacing courses of flint, cobble, pebble or stone, lap projections, rustication, soldier courses, toothing, and decorative bricks and tiles.

### 3A.5.1 (PDF p. 35)

Building elements. If the following building elements are incorporated into a design, they shall meet the following criteria:
a. Chimneys that comply with all of the following standards:
• Chimneys shall utilize one of the following materials:
1. Brick, naturally fired.
2. River rock or rounded cobbles.
3. Stucco, finished and painted to match the same color as the main residential structure.
• Chimneys shall incorporate one of the following elements:
1. Metal chimney cap, brushed finish, treated to prevent reflectivity and glare, or painted to match the requirements of standard §3A.6.
2. Chimney hood protecting the chimney opening.
3. Chimney ledge with the ledge projecting ¾-inch minimum.
4. Chimney cornice.
b. Exterior stairs and ramps that connect the site and walkways to the interior floor level shall comply with all of the following standards:
• Exterior stair railings shall utilize one of the following materials:
1. Metal, pre-finished or painted per the requirements of §3A.6.
2. Steel or wrought iron, pre-finished or painted per the requirements of §3A.6.
3. Wood or cement fiber with a wood grain finish, painted or stained per the requirements of §3A.6.
• Treads and risers shall be constructed of one or more of the following materials:
1. Brick.
2. Standard gray or colored concrete.
3. Pavers and tiles.
4. Stone.
5. Wood, painted or stained per the requirements of §3A.6. or pre-finished composite wood to match the requirements of §3A.6.

### 3A.5.2 (PDF p. 36)

Building components and details. Buildings and structures shall incorporate a minimum of three of the following components and details:
a. Battered or sloping foundation.
b. Brackets and knee braces under ridge lines, eaves, and overhangs, either wood, plain, or decorative.
c. Decorative surrounds at openings utilizing wood or composite material that simulates wood.
d. Door, paneled, pre-finished, natural, or painted.
e. Dormers, gabled or shed.
f. Entry door that is either wood paneled, pre-finished, or natural, with glazing a minimum of one square foot in area and a minimum of one sidelite.
g. Porch supports utilizing columns above brick, river rock or cobble, or rough stone or masonry piers.
h. Rafters, rafter tails, or false rafters under eaves or projecting beyond eaves with plain or decorative tails.
i. Roof beams under roof ridges and rakes.
j. Roof planes, multiple.
k. Trellised porch, with a minimum five-foot depth trellis.
l. Window garden boxes.

### 3A.5.3 (PDF p. 36)

Visible exterior lighting, shell and casing material. The shell and casing of exterior lighting fixtures with visible light sources shall utilize one of the following materials:
a. Steel or wrought iron, painted per the requirements of §3A.6.
b. Copper or oil-rubbed bronze, naturally finished.
c. Wood, painted or stained per the requirements of §3A.6.
d. Engineered polymer, painted per the requirements of §3A.6.
