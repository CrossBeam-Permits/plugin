# Floor Framing and Construction

> California Residential Code (CRC), Title 24, Part 2.5
> 2025 Edition (Effective January 1, 2026)

This reference covers wood floor framing, floor sheathing, concrete slab-on-grade floors, and exterior deck construction for single-family residential buildings in California.

---

## 1. General Requirements

Floor construction shall accommodate all loads per Section R301 and transmit resulting loads to supporting structural elements (CRC R501.2). Compressible floor coverings (compressing more than 1/32" under 50 lbs over 1 sq in, thicker than 1/8" uncompressed) shall not extend beneath walls, partitions, or columns fastened to the floor (CRC R601.2.1).

### Floor Cantilevers

Floor cantilever spans shall not exceed the nominal depth of the joist (CRC R502.3.3). Specific cantilever tables:

- **Table R502.3.3(1)**: Cantilevers supporting a light-frame exterior
  bearing wall and roof only (backspan ratio >= 3:1)
- **Table R502.3.3(2)**: Cantilevers supporting exterior balconies (backspan ratio >= 2:1)

For SDC D0, D1, D2 detached dwellings and SDC C-D2 townhouses, see R301.2.2.6 for additional cantilever limitations.

## 3. Bearing and Support

### Minimum Bearing Length

| Bearing Surface | Minimum Bearing |
|----------------|-----------------|
| Wood or metal  | 1-1/2 inches    |
| Masonry or concrete | 3 inches   |

Alternatively, approved joist hangers may be used, or joist ends may be
supported on a 1x4 ribbon strip nailed to the adjacent stud. A 2" nominal sill
plate shall provide minimum 48 sq in bearing area on masonry/concrete (CRC
R502.6).

### Joists Under Bearing Partitions

Joists under parallel bearing partitions shall be adequately sized. Double joists separated for piping shall be solid blocked at not more than 4 ft o.c. Bearing partitions perpendicular to joists shall not be offset from supporting girders more than the joist depth unless sized for the additional load (CRC R502.4).

### Joist Framing Lap

Joists framing from opposite sides over a bearing support shall lap not less than **3 inches** and be nailed with minimum three 10d face nails. Wood or metal splice of equal strength is permitted (CRC R502.6.1).

### Joist-to-Girder Connection

Joists framing into the side of a wood girder shall be supported by approved framing anchors or on ledger strips not less than nominal 2x2 (CRC R502.6.2).

## 4. Lateral Restraint and Bridging

### Lateral Restraint at Supports

Joists shall be supported laterally at ends by (CRC R502.7):

- Full-depth solid blocking (minimum 2" nominal), or
- Attachment to full-depth header, band, or rim joist, or
- Attachment to an adjoining stud, or
- Other lateral support that prevents rotation

Trusses, structural composite lumber, glulam, and I-joists may instead be
supported laterally in accordance with the manufacturer's recommendations.

In SDC D0, D1, and D2, lateral restraint shall be provided at each intermediate support (CRC R502.7, Exception 2).

### Bridging

Joists exceeding nominal 2x12 shall be supported laterally by (CRC R502.7.1):

- Solid blocking
- Diagonal bridging (wood or metal)
- Continuous 1x3 strip nailed across bottom of joists at intervals not exceeding **8 feet**

This prescriptive bridging rule does not apply to trusses, structural
composite lumber, glulam, or I-joists supported laterally in accordance with
the manufacturer's recommendations.

## 5. Cutting, Drilling, and Notching

### Sawn Lumber Joists (R502.8.1)

| Limitation | Requirement |
|-----------|-------------|
| Maximum notch depth | 1/6 of member depth |
| Maximum notch length | 1/3 of member depth |
| Notch location | Not in middle 1/3 of span |
| End notch maximum | 1/4 of member depth |
| Tension side notching | Not permitted on members >= 4" nominal (except at ends) |
| Maximum bore diameter | 1/3 of member depth |
| Bore edge clearance | Minimum 2" from top or bottom |
| Bore-to-bore clearance | Minimum 2" |
| Bore-to-notch clearance | Minimum 2" |

### Engineered Wood Products

Cuts, notches, and holes in trusses, structural composite lumber, glulam, CLT, or I-joists are prohibited except where permitted by manufacturer or considered by a registered design professional (CRC R502.8.2).

## 6. Framing of Openings

Openings in floor framing require header and trimmer joists (CRC R502.10):

- Header span <= 4 ft: single header same size as floor joist
- Single trimmer permitted when header is within 3 ft of trimmer bearing
- Header span > 4 ft: doubled trimmers and doubled header of sufficient cross section

## 8. Concrete Floors on Ground (Slab-on-Grade)

Concrete slab-on-ground floors shall comply with Section R506 or ACI 332 (CRC R506.1).

### Key Requirements

| Item | Requirement |
|------|-------------|
| Minimum slab thickness | 3-1/2 inches (CRC R506.1) |
| Compressive strength | Per Section R402.2 |
| Site preparation | Remove all vegetation, topsoil, foreign material (CRC R506.3) |
| Base course | 4" thick clean graded sand, gravel, crushed stone, crushed concrete, or crushed blast-furnace slag passing a 2" sieve — required where slab is below grade (CRC R506.3.2) |
| Base course exception | Not required on well-drained or sand-gravel Group I soils (CRC R506.3.2, Exception) |
| Vapor retarder | Minimum 6 mil polyethylene or an approved vapor retarder, joints lapped 6" minimum, between slab and base course, or between slab and prepared subgrade where no base course exists (CRC R506.3.3) |
| Capillary break | Required when vapor retarder is required, per CA Green Building Standards Code Div. 4.5 (CRC R506.3.3.1) |
| Fill material | Free of vegetation/foreign material and compacted for uniform slab support; except where approved, max 24" for clean sand/gravel and max 8" for earth (CRC R506.3.1) |
| Reinforcement support | Where reinforcement is provided, support it from the center to upper 1/3 of slab during placement (CRC R506.3.4) |

### Vapor Retarder Exceptions

Vapor retarder is not required for (CRC R506.3.3):

1. Garages, utility buildings, and unheated accessory structures
2. Unheated storage rooms < 70 sq ft and carports
3. Driveways, walks, patios, and other flatwork not likely to be enclosed and
   heated later
4. Where approved by building official based on local site conditions

### Post-Tensioned Slabs

Post-tensioned slabs on expansive or stable soils shall be designed per PTI DC10.5 (CRC R506.2).

## 9. Exterior Decks

Wood-framed decks shall comply with Section R507. Decks shall be designed for the live load per R301.5 or ground snow load, whichever is greater (CRC R507.1).

### Deck Footings

- Minimum depth: **12 inches** below undisturbed ground surface (CRC R507.3.2)
- Sized per Table R507.3.1 based on tributary area and soil bearing capacity
- Frost protection required where attached to frost-protected structure (CRC R507.3.3)
- Footings not required for free-standing decks with joists directly on grade,
  or for free-standing decks <= 200 sq ft whose joists bear directly on
  grade-level precast blocks without beams or posts and whose walking surface
  is <= 20" above grade at any point within 36" horizontally from the edge
  (CRC R507.3, Exceptions)

### Deck Posts

Post sizing is per Table R507.4. California decks require at least the 60 psf
live load in Table R301.5, or the ground snow load where greater (R507.1).
Do not use the table's 40 psf rows for a California SFR deck; select the
applicable 60 psf species, size, tributary-area, and height row from the full
table.

### Deck Joist Spans (Table R507.6 Excerpt)

Use the 60 psf rows that match the proposed species, grade, member size, and
spacing. The 40 psf rows in Table R507.6 do not satisfy California's current
minimum deck live load; ground snow load controls where greater (R507.1 and
R301.5).

### Deck Joist Bearing and Lateral Restraint

- Minimum bearing: 1-1/2" on wood/metal, 3" on concrete/masonry (CRC R507.6.1)
- Lateral restraint required at joist ends and bearing locations (CRC R507.6.2)
- Rim joists: minimum three 10d nails or three No. 10 x 3" screws to each joist end

### Ledger Connections

Deck ledgers shall be minimum 2x8 nominal and No. 2 grade or better. Permitted
materials are preservative-treated Southern pine, preservative-treated incised
hem-fir, or decay-resistant naturally durable wood (CRC R507.9.1.1). Ledger
connections to band joists are per Table R507.9.1.3(1), using 1/2" lag screws
or through-bolts.

Key ledger requirements:

- Where a ledger attaches to wood-frame construction, flashing is required
  above the ledger to prevent water entry, except at an opening less than 2"
  above the ledger or where the ledger is spaced at least 1/4" from the wall
  covering for drainage and ventilation (CRC R507.9.1.5)
- Flashing shall extend vertically >= 2" above the ledger and either >= 4"
  horizontally beyond its face, or to the ledger face and at least 1/4" down
  that face, subject to the same exceptions
- The water-resistive barrier shall lap the flashing leg by at least 2" or the
  leg height, whichever is less, and continue down behind the flashing and
  ledger; R507.9.1.6 also permits its counterflashing and wall-spacing
  alternatives
- Ledgers shall not support concentrated loads from beams/girders (CRC R507.9.1.1)
- Ledgers shall not be supported on stone or masonry veneer

### Lateral Load Connection

Deck lateral loads shall transfer to the ground or to a structure capable of
transmitting them to the ground. Where the connection uses the prescriptive
hold-down methods, install the tension devices per Figure R507.9.2(1) or
R507.9.2(2) to transfer load to the primary structure (CRC R507.9.2).

### Deck Guards

Guards shall comply with Sections R301.5, R321, and R507.10 (CRC R507.10):

- Where guards are supported on deck framing, guard loads shall transfer
  through a continuous load path to the deck joists; top-mounted and other
  guards follow the separate R507.10.1.2 and R507.10.4 paths (CRC R507.10.1)
- 4x4 wood posts supporting guard loads shall **not be notched** at connection to supporting structure (CRC R507.10.2)
- Where guards connect to the interior or exterior side of a deck joist or
  beam, the connection shall not rely only on fasteners in end-grain withdrawal
  (CRC R507.10.1.1)

### Decking

Wood decking attached with minimum two 8d deformed shank nails or two No. 8 wood screws per bearing (CRC R507.7). Maximum joist spacing for decking:

| Decking Type    | Perpendicular (Single Span) | Perpendicular (Multi Span) | Diagonal (Single) | Diagonal (Multi) |
|----------------|---------------------------|--------------------------|-------------------|-----------------|
| 1-1/4" thick   | 12"                       | 16"                      | 8"                | 12"             |
| 2" thick       | 24"                       | 24"                      | 18"               | 24"             |

## 10. Wood Floor Trusses

Wood floor trusses shall be designed per accepted engineering practice and comply with ANSI/TPI 1 for metal-plate-connected trusses. Truss design drawings prepared by a registered design professional are required (CRC R502.12.1).

- Trusses shall be braced per construction documents and truss design drawings
  when they specify bracing; otherwise use accepted industry practice such as
  the SBCA BCSI guide (CRC R502.12.2)
- Truss members shall **not be cut, notched, spliced, or altered** without approval of a registered design professional (CRC R502.12.3)

---

## 11. Floor Framing Supporting Guards

For guard assemblies not exceeding 44" at open floor edges, use accepted
engineering practice or one of the prescriptive R502.11.1/.2 paths below.
Truss or I-joist edge members must be designed with the guard-load effects
specifically considered (CRC R502.11):

- **Conventional edge framing**: Net width >= 3", net depth >= 9-1/4", with roll bracing aligned with each guard post (CRC R502.11.1)
- **Timber edge framing**: Minimum 6x10 sawn timber or 5-1/8" x 9-1/4" glulam, roll bracing at 48" o.c. max (CRC R502.11.2)
- **Roll bracing**: Joist/blocking matching edge-member depth and extending >=
  16" from the edge; provide at least six 16d nails at blocking ends,
  continuous floor sheathing for at least 24", at least twelve 10d nails to
  each brace, and at least twelve 10d nails to the edge member within 12" of
  the brace (CRC R502.11.3)
