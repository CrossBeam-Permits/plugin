# CALGreen Residential Mandatory Measures

California Green Building Standards Code (CALGreen), Title 24, Part 11 —
2025 Edition, Chapter 4. This reference covers mandatory measures for
new single-family residential construction including stormwater, EV
charging, water conservation, construction waste, pollutant control,
indoor air quality, and verification requirements.

---

## 1. Storm Water / Site Development (Section 4.106)

### Storm Water During Construction (4.106.2)
Projects disturbing less than 1 acre (and not part of a larger common
plan disturbing 1+ acre) shall manage stormwater drainage during
construction using one or more of the following:
- Retention basins of sufficient size to retain storm water on site
- Barrier system, wattle, or other filtration where water is conveyed
  to a public drainage system
- Compliance with a local stormwater management ordinance

Note: Projects disturbing 1+ acre are regulated by the State Water
Resources Control Board.

### Grading and Paving (4.106.3)
Construction plans shall indicate how site grading or drainage will
manage all surface water flows to keep water from entering buildings.
Methods include:
- Swales
- Water collection and disposal systems
- French drains
- Water retention gardens
- Other approved measures

Exception: Additions and alterations not altering the drainage path.

## 2. EV Charging Infrastructure (Section 4.106.4)

### New Single-Family Dwellings with Attached Garage (4.106.4.1)
For each dwelling unit, provide one of the following:

**Option A — EV Capable (raceway only):**

| Requirement | Detail |
|---|---|
| Raceway size | Trade size 1 minimum (1-inch nominal ID) |
| Raceway routing | From main panel/subpanel to EV charger location |
| Raceway continuity | Required at enclosed, inaccessible, or concealed areas |
| Panel capacity | Space and capacity for 40A, 208/240V dedicated branch circuit |
| OCPD space | Reserved space in panel for branch circuit breaker |

**Option B — EV Ready (circuit installed):**
- Minimum 40A, 208/240V dedicated branch circuit installed to the
  proposed EV charger location

### Labeling (4.106.4.1.1)
- Panel circuit directory: reserved space marked "EV CAPABLE"
- Raceway termination: permanently and visibly marked "EV CAPABLE"

### Exceptions
- ADUs and JADUs without additional parking facilities are exempt
- Local enforcing agency may determine infeasibility where:
  - No local utility power supply or inadequate power
  - Additional utility infrastructure costs would adversely impact
    construction cost

### Electrical Code Compliance
All EVSE shall comply with the California Electrical Code, Article 625
(CALGreen 4.106.4).

## 3. Water Conservation — Indoor (Section 4.303)

### Plumbing Fixture Flow Rate Requirements

| Fixture/Fitting | Maximum Flow Rate | Standard/Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Water closets | 1.28 GPF | Tank-type: EPA WaterSense certified (4.303.1.1) |
| Urinals (wall-mounted) | 0.125 GPF | (4.303.1.2) |
| Urinals (other) | 0.5 GPF | (4.303.1.2) |
| Showerheads (single) | 1.8 GPM at 80 psi | EPA WaterSense certified (4.303.1.3.1) |
| Multiple showerheads (one valve) | 1.8 GPM combined | Or allow only one outlet at a time (4.303.1.3.2) |
| Residential lavatory faucets | 1.2 GPM at 60 psi | Min 0.8 GPM at 20 psi (4.303.1.4.1) |
| Public/common area lav faucets | 0.5 GPM at 60 psi | (4.303.1.4.2) |
| Metering faucets | 0.2 gal/cycle | (4.303.1.4.3) |
| Kitchen faucets | 1.8 GPM at 60 psi | May temporarily increase to 2.2 GPM; must default to 1.8 (4.303.1.4.4) |

### Noncompliant Fixture Replacement
For residential property built and available for use on or before January 1,
1994, Civil Code 1101.4(a) requires noncompliant fixtures to be replaced as a
condition of final completion, occupancy, or final approval for a qualifying
alteration or improvement. Apply the historical-site, technical-infeasibility,
and disconnected-water exceptions in Civil Code 1101.7. See the note to
CALGreen 4.303.1; do not apply this trigger to every residential property.

### Fixture Standards
All fixtures and fittings shall be installed per the California Plumbing
Code and meet standards in CPC Table 1701.1 (4.303.3).

## 4. Water Conservation — Outdoor (Section 4.304)

### MWELO Compliance (4.304.1)
Residential developments shall comply with a local water efficient
landscape ordinance or the Model Water Efficient Landscape Ordinance
(MWELO), whichever is more stringent.

MWELO is located in California Code of Regulations, Title 23, Chapter
2.7, Division 2. Key requirements include:
- Maximum Applied Water Allowance (MAWA) calculation
- Irrigation design based on plant water needs
- Qualifying new landscapes enter MWELO at 500 sq ft inclusive. A project
  below 2,500 sq ft may use the prescriptive route; at 2,500 sq ft or more,
  the performance route includes the soil-management report/analysis.
- Permanent irrigation under the applicable route may use either an
  evapotranspiration/weather-based or soil-moisture/sensor-based automatic
  controller.
- Under the performance-route irrigation-design provisions, residential
  landscape area of 5,000 sq ft or more requires a submeter or dedicated
  irrigation meter.

## 5. Material Conservation — Construction Waste (Section 4.408)

### Minimum Diversion Rate (4.408.1)
Recycle and/or salvage for reuse a minimum of **65 percent** of
nonhazardous construction and demolition waste.

The three state-code compliance routes are:
1. **Construction waste management plan** (4.408.2)
2. **Waste management company** providing verifiable documentation (4.408.3)
3. **Waste stream reduction alternative** — total landfill disposal
   does not exceed 3.4 lbs/sq ft of building area (4.408.4), or
   2.0 lbs/sq ft for the reduced alternative (4.408.4.1)

Alternatively, a more stringent local construction and demolition
waste-management ordinance is an accepted compliance path under 4.408.1.

### Construction Waste Management Plan Requirements (4.408.2)
1. Identify C&D waste materials to be diverted
2. Specify source-separated or single-stream sorting
3. Identify diversion facilities
4. Identify waste reduction construction methods
5. Specify calculation by weight or volume (not both)

### Exceptions
- Excavated soil and land-clearing debris
- Where capable diversion facilities are unavailable or not reasonably close,
  alternate waste-reduction methods are developed with local agencies
- The enforcing agency may grant an exception for an isolated jobsite beyond
  the haul boundary of available diversion facilities

### Documentation (4.408.5)
Documentation demonstrating compliance shall be provided to the
enforcing agency.

## 6. Pollutant Control (Section 4.504)

### Duct Protection During Construction (4.504.1)
At rough installation, during site storage, and until final HVAC startup,
all duct and air distribution openings shall be covered with tape,
plastic, sheet metal, or other approved methods to prevent entry of
water, dust, and debris.

### Adhesive VOC Limits (Table 4.504.1)

| Adhesive Type | VOC Limit (g/L) |
|---|---|
| Indoor carpet adhesive | 50 |
| Wood flooring adhesive | 100 |
| Subfloor adhesive | 50 |
| Ceramic tile adhesive | 65 |
| Drywall and panel adhesive | 50 |
| Multipurpose construction adhesive | 70 |
| Contact adhesive | 80 |
| PVC welding | 510 |
| ABS welding | 325 |

### Sealant VOC Limits (Table 4.504.2)

| Sealant Type | VOC Limit (g/L) |
|---|---|
| Architectural | 250 |
| Non-membrane roof | 300 |
| Single-ply roof membrane | 450 |
| Other sealants | 420 |

### Finish Material Requirements (4.504.2)
- **Adhesives, sealants, and caulks:** shall meet Tables 4.504.1 and
  4.504.2, any more stringent local/regional rule, the Rule 1168
  toxic-compound prohibitions, and the separate Title 17 rule for aerosol
  adhesives and qualifying small containers (4.504.2.1).
- **Paints and coatings:** shall meet the ARB Architectural Suggested Control
  Measure and Table 4.504.3 unless more stringent local limits apply
  (4.504.2.2).
- **Aerosol paints and coatings:** shall meet the 17 CCR 94522 MIR,
  toxic-compound, and ozone-depleting-substance requirements, plus Bay Area
  Rule 49 where applicable (4.504.2.3).

### Carpet Systems (4.504.3)
- Interior carpet shall meet the CDPH Standard Method for Testing and
  Evaluation of VOC Emissions from Indoor Sources Using Environmental
  Chambers, Version 1.2, January 2017
- Carpet cushion shall meet that same CDPH Standard Method (4.504.3.1)
- Carpet adhesive: shall meet VOC limits in Table 4.504.1 (4.504.3.2)

### Resilient Flooring (4.504.4)
At least 80 percent of the floor area receiving resilient flooring shall meet
the CDPH Standard Method, Version 1.2, January 2017. FloorScore is not the only
permitted compliance credential.

### Composite Wood Products (4.504.5)
Hardwood plywood, particleboard, and MDF used on the interior or
exterior shall meet formaldehyde limits per ARB Air Toxics Control
Measure (17 CCR 93120 et seq.):

| Product | Formaldehyde Emission Limit |
|---|---|
| Hardwood plywood (veneer core) | 0.05 ppm |
| Hardwood plywood (composite core) | 0.05 ppm |
| Particleboard | 0.09 ppm |
| Medium density fiberboard (MDF) | 0.11 ppm |
| Thin MDF | 0.13 ppm |

When the enforcing agency requests verification, permitted evidence includes
chain-of-custody certifications, product labels or invoices, qualifying
exterior-grade markings, and other approved methods (4.504.5.1).

## 7. Interior Moisture Control (Section 4.505)

### Concrete Slab Foundations (4.505.2)
Where CBC Chapter 19 or CRC Chapter 5 requires a vapor retarder, the
slab-on-grade foundation shall comply with the 4.505.2 moisture-protection
requirements.

### Capillary Break (4.505.2.1)
For a slab within the 4.505.2 vapor-retarder scope, provide one complete
4.505.2.1 capillary-break path: the prescribed aggregate/vapor-retarder
assembly, an approved equivalent, or the licensed design professional's
specified alternate design.

### Moisture Content of Building Materials (4.505.3)
- Building materials with visible water damage shall not be installed
- Wall and floor framing shall not be enclosed when exceeding 19%
  moisture content
- At least three random readings are taken 2-4 ft from the grade-stamped end of
  each piece with a probe/contact meter or enforcing-agency-approved equivalent,
  and the verification is documented
- Visibly wet or high-moisture insulation is replaced or allowed to dry before
  enclosure; wet-applied insulation follows its manufacturer's drying
  recommendations

## 8. Indoor Air Quality and Exhaust (Section 4.506)

### Bathroom Exhaust Fans (4.506.1)
Each bathroom shall be mechanically ventilated with the following
requirements:
- Fan is ENERGY STAR compliant
- Exhaust to the building exterior
- Unless functioning as part of a whole-house ventilation system, fans
  must be controlled by a humidity control
- Humidity controls shall be adjustable between 50% RH or less, up to
  a maximum of 80% RH
- Humidity control may be separate from or integral to the fan

Note: For this section, a bathroom is a room containing a bathtub,
shower, or tub/shower combination.

### Fireplaces (4.503.1)
- Any installed gas fireplace shall be a **direct-vent sealed-combustion
  type**
- Any installed woodstove or pellet stove shall comply with US EPA NSPS
  emission limits and have a permanent certification label
- Woodstoves, pellet stoves, and fireplaces shall also comply with
  applicable local ordinances

## 9. Inspections and Verification Requirements

### Operation and Maintenance Manual (4.410.1)
At final inspection, a manual (or equivalent media) shall be placed in
the building containing:

1. Direction that the manual shall remain with the building for its
   life cycle
2. Operation and maintenance instructions for:
   - Equipment and appliances (water-saving devices, HVAC, PV, EV
     chargers, water heating, major appliances)
   - Roof and yard drainage (gutters, downspouts)
   - Space conditioning systems (condensers, air filters)
   - Landscape irrigation systems
   - Water reuse systems
3. Local utility and waste recovery provider information
4. Public transportation and carpool options
5. Educational material on indoor relative humidity (30-60%)
6. Water-conserving landscape and irrigation information
7. Gutter/downspout maintenance and 5-foot foundation drainage clearance
8. Routine maintenance measures (caulking, painting, grading)
9. State solar energy incentive program information
10. Copy of all special inspection verifications
11. Defensible space maintenance (from CAL FIRE)
12. Location of grab bar reinforcements

### Rodent Proofing (4.406.1)
Annular spaces around pipes, cables, conduits, or other openings in
sole/bottom plates at exterior walls shall be sealed with cement mortar,
concrete masonry, or similar approved method.

### Recycling by Occupants (4.410.2)
Where 5 or more multifamily dwelling units are on a site, provide
accessible recycling areas. (Not applicable to single-family detached.)
