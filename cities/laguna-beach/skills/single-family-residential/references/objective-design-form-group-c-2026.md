# Laguna Beach Objective Design Standards — Form Group C

**Source:** *Objective Design Standards for Single-Family and Two-Family Residential Development*, Chapter 3C, City document 26790, published August 2026.

Use this reference only after the applicant has selected Form Group C: **Tudor, English Cottage, French Cottage, Norman, or Storybook**. Load it with `objective-design-standards-2026.md`; do not combine its standards with another form group. Ask the applicant to choose when the form group is not stated—the source does not assign groups by zone, neighborhood, or context.

Section anchors: §3C.1 Roofs; §3C.2 Exterior Walls; §3C.3 Exterior Openings; §3C.4 Porches, Decks, and Balconies; §3C.5 Equipment Screening; §3C.6 Colors. The applicant's checklist must identify the plan-sheet location for every applicable standard.

### 3C.1.1 (PDF p. 49)

Roofs, allowed types. Utilize at least one of the following roof types. All other roof types are prohibited.
a. Front-gabled, with straight and/or curved edges.
b. Cross-gabled, with straight and/or curved edges.
c. Clipped-gabled.
d. Conical.
e. Hexagonal, for tower elements only.
f. Hipped.

### 3C.1.2 (PDF p. 49)

Roof material. Provide one of the following roof materials:
a. Composite shingles and tiles that simulate shake and slate.
b. Concrete tiles.
c. Slate tiles.
d. Synthetic shake roofing.
e. Three-dimensional architectural asphalt shingles.

### 3C.1.3 (PDF p. 49)

Roof pitch. Roof pitches shall be a minimum of 5:12 and a maximum of 12:12.

### 3C.1.4 (PDF p. 49)

Roof rake. Measured from the roof form to the exterior wall, rakes shall horizontally project a maximum of 12 inches.

### 3C.1.5 (PDF p. 49)

Roof eave. Eaves shall horizontally project a minimum of six inches and a maximum of 18 inches, measured from the edge of the exterior wall to the edge of the roof tile.

### 3C.2.1 (PDF p. 50)

Exterior wall materials and finishes. Provide at least one of the following materials/finishes for exterior walls. At building corners, all materials shall wrap around the corner for a minimum distance of two feet.
a. Brick utilizing a bond pattern, whether regular or irregular.
b. Wood or fiber cement lap siding
c. Wood or fiber cement shingles.
d. Natural finish stucco, or a pigmented and painted stucco utilizing one of the following finishes.
i. 16/20 Medium Sand Float finish.
ii. 20/30 Fine Sand Float finish.
iii. 16/20 Heavy Sand Float finish.
iv. Briar finish.
v. Cat face finish.
vi. Light Dash finish.
vii. Medium Dash finish.
viii. Heavy Dash finish.
ix. Tunnel Dash Finish.
x. Knockdown Dash finish.
xi. English finish.
xii. Glacier finish.
xiii. Light Lace finish.
xiv. Heavy Lace finish.
xv. Sand/float 20/30 finish.
xvi. Santa Barbara finish.
xvii. Smooth finish.
xviii. Wet Groove Plaster finish.
e. Stone or stone veneer.

### 3C.2.2 (PDF p. 50)

Secondary wall material. Provide a secondary wall material utilizing the materials noted in standard C.2.1 that does not exceed 50 percent of the total wall area of any structure. Notwithstanding this standard, only one stucco finish may be utilized. At building corners, all materials shall wrap around the corner for a minimum distance of two feet.

### 3C.3.1 (PDF p. 51)

Exterior openings: percentage of wall. Exterior openings on all building walls, inclusive of doors and windows, shall be a minimum of 15 percent and a maximum of 30 percent.

### 3C.3.2 (PDF p. 51)

Exterior opening types. Provide a minimum of three distinct exterior opening sizes, inclusive of doors and windows.

### 3C.3.3 (PDF p. 51)

Exterior opening shapes. Openings shall be rectangular, segmental arch, or pointed arch in shape.

### 3C.3.4 (PDF p. 51)

Exterior opening surrounds. Opening surrounds, when provided at exterior openings at heads, jambs, and sills, shall comply with the dimensions per the table below.

### 3C.3.5 (PDF p. 51)

Exterior opening materials. With the exception of the front door, provide one of the following exterior opening materials:
a. Wood, pre-finished, stained, or painted per the color requirements of §3C.6.
b. Metal-clad wood, pre-finished or painted per the color requirements of §3C.6.
c. Steel, pre-finished or painted per the color requirements of §3C.6.
d. Composite, acrylic, or fiberglass set a minimum of two inches inward from the adjacent exterior surface and finished per the color requirements of §3C.6.

### 3C.3.6 (PDF p. 52)

Window types. Provide at least one and a maximum of two of the following window types:
a. Casement.
b. Single-hung.
c. Double-hung.
d. Fixed.
e. Ganged, with a maximum three windows either fixed all across, casement all across, or casement sandwiched by fixed.

### 3C.3.7 (PDF p. 52)

Window recess. Not applicable.

### 3C.3.8 (PDF p. 52)

Window sashes, rails, and stiles. Window rails and stiles shall be a minimum of two inches in height or width.

### 3C.3.9 (PDF p. 52)

Window muntins, mullions, and sticking. Window muntins, mullions, and sticking shall comply with all of the following standards:
• Sticking and muntins that divide windows shall be a minimum of ¾-inch in profile. Simulated sticking and snap on or between glass muntins is prohibited.
• If provided, multipane windows shall incorporate one of the following patterns:
i. Fully divided with orthogonal sticking with a minimum of four divides per sash.
ii. Lattice/diamond sticking.

### 3C.3.10 (PDF p. 52)

Door leaves. Provide no more than two contiguous door leaves.

### 3C.3.11 (PDF p. 52)

Door recess. Not applicable.

### 3C.3.12 (PDF p. 52)

Optional: transoms and sidelites. If provided, transoms and/or sidelites shall comply with all of the following standards.
• The maximum width of sidelites shall be half the width of the door.
Transom windows shall not extend beyond the door or sidelite edges and shall not exceed 18 inches in height.
• Provide one of the following lite types.
i. Vision glass, with a minimum of three parts.
ii. Half glass, with a minimum of six parts.
iii. Full glass, with a minimum of four inches in width.

### 3C.3.13 (PDF p. 52)

Door location. Doors shall be placed a minimum 18 inches distant from a wall.

### 3C.4.1 (PDF p. 53)

Entry element. Provide two or more of the following entry elements.
a. Street-facing entries placed either at grade or a maximum 18 inches above the adjacent finished grade shall comply with at least one of the following:
i. Recessed a minimum of two feet and a maximum of five feet from a building plane that faces a street.
ii. Framed by an arched or segmented arched opening(s).
iii. Incorporated into a projecting minor mass that does not exceed the eave line by more than three feet or a tower element that does not exceed the otherwise allowed height limit.
iv. Entry accessed by exterior stairs.
b. An entry path no greater than five feet in width shall connect at least one street to the primary residential entry. Within ten feet of the entry, the path may exceed this width but shall not exceed in this area adjacent to the entry a width of more than eight feet. The entry path shall incorporate one or more of the following materials:
i. Colored and scored concrete with score lines placed no more than 30 inches apart.
ii. Scored concrete with score lines placed no more than 30 inches apart.
iii. Concrete or stone pavers no larger than 30 inches by 30 inches.
iv. Brick used as a uniform or as an accent material.
c. Porches with columns and/or piers, comprising all of the following standards:
• Porches shall make up a maximum of 60 percent of the primary frontage.
• Columns shall be one of the following shapes:
1. H-column.
2. I-beam.
3. Square.
• Columns shall be stone or wood that is stained or painted per the requirements in §3C.6, or a combination of both.
d. Roof overhangs comprising all of the following standards:
• Overhangs shall provide a minimum horizontal projection of three feet.
• Overhangs may be supported by brackets.
e. Trellises, either stained, natural, or painted per the requirements in §3C.6.

### 3C.4.2 (PDF p. 54)

Fences and wall materials. Fences, walls, and guardrails facing streets or rights-of-way shall be constructed of the following materials or any combination of the following materials:
a. Wood, stained or painted per the requirements in §3C.6.
b. Stucco or stucco plaster, integrally colored or painted and matching the same color as the main residential structure.
c. Brick.
d. Stone, face stone, and/or veneer stone.

### 3C.5.1 (PDF p. 54)

Optional building element: exterior stairs and ramps. If exterior stairs and ramps connect site and walkways to the interior floor level, they shall meet the following criteria:
• Exterior stair railings shall utilize one of the following materials:
i. Metal, pre-finished or painted per the requirements of §3C.6.
ii. Steel or wrought iron, pre-finished or painted per the requirements of §3C.6.
iii. Wood, painted or stained per the requirements of §3C.6.
• Treads and risers shall be constructed of one or more of the following materials:
i. Brick.
ii. Standard gray or colored concrete.
iii. Pavers and tiles meeting the criteria of standard C.4.1.b.

### 3C.5.2 (PDF p. 55)

Building components and details. Buildings and structures shall incorporate a minimum of three of the following components and details.
least 3
a. Brackets and knee braces under ridge lines, eaves, and overhangs, either wood, plain, or decorative.
b. Chimneys, utilizing one of the following materials:
i. Brick, naturally fired or painted to match the same color as the main residential structure.
ii. River rock or rounded cobbles.
iii. Stucco, painted and matching the same color as the main residential structure.
c. Chimney caps utilizing at least one of the following components:
i. Chimney hood protecting the chimney opening.
ii. Chimney ledge, with the ledge projecting ¾-inches minimum.
iii. Chimney cornice.
iv. Metal or copper chimney cap. If metal, the chimney cap shall be pre-finished or painted to match the requirements of standard C.5.2.b.
v. Terracotta clay chimney pots.
vi. Stucco or brick coping cap the same length and depth as the chimney.
vii. Stucco or brick cornice no more than 16 inches in height and projecting no more than three inches.
e. Diamond pattern divided windows.
f. Dormers, gabled or shed.
g. Decorative surrounds at openings, either wood, fiber cement, cast stone, ceramic tiles, terracotta tiles, or stone.
h. Wood entry door, either pre-finished or painted per the requirements of §3C.6., with glazing that is at least one square foot in area.
i. Half-timbering.
j. Oriel or bay window, no more than one on a street-facing elevation.
k. Entry porch utilizing posts or columns.
l. Porte-cochere that matches the architecture of the primary building.
m. Roof planes, multiple.
n. Tower element with the top of the tower not to exceed the highest ridgeline.

### 3C.5.3 (PDF p. 56)

Visible exterior lighting, shell and casing material. The shell and casing of exterior lighting fixtures with visible light sources shall utilize one of the following materials:
a. Steel or wrought iron, painted per the requirements of §3C.6.
b. Copper or oil-rubbed bronze, naturally finished.
