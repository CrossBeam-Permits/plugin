# Mechanical and HVAC Requirements for Single-Family Residential

California Mechanical Code (CMC), Title 24, Part 4 — 2025 Edition.
This reference covers ventilation, exhaust, duct systems, combustion air,
appliance installation, dryer venting, and fireplace/chimney requirements
for single-family dwellings.

---

## 1. Whole-House Ventilation

### General Requirement
Rooms or occupied spaces within residential occupancies where occupants are
nontransient shall be designed to have mechanical ventilation and exhaust
air (CMC 405.1).

### Natural Ventilation Exception
Natural ventilation is permitted only where approved by the AHJ and only
in Climate Zone 1, Climate Zone 2, or buildings thermally conditioned
for fewer than 876 hours per year (CMC 405.1.1).

### Whole-Dwelling Airflow
California residential ventilation requirements reference ASHRAE 62.2 as
amended by the California codes. CMC 405.2 and CEnC 150.0(o)1C provide the
enforceable California base calculation: 0.03 times floor area plus 7.5 times
the quantity (number of bedrooms plus one), followed by the applicable Energy Code
infiltration/system-factor calculation and compliance documentation.

### Heat/Energy Recovery Ventilators
HRV and ERV systems shall be installed per their listings and comply
with manufacturer instructions. Non-ducted HRVs shall comply with UL
1815; ducted HRVs with UL 1812. Ducts shall comply with CMC Chapter 6
(CMC 504.5).

## 2. Local Exhaust Requirements

### Kitchen Exhaust
- Range hoods shall discharge to the outdoors through a single-wall duct
  (CMC 504.3(6))
- Shall not terminate in an attic or crawl space (CMC 504.3(6))
- Domestic kitchen exhaust ducts shall be constructed of metal with smooth
  interior surfaces, sealed with duct mastic or metal tape per UL 181A
  (CMC 504.3(5)), unless the complete approved under-slab Schedule 40 PVC
  downdraft exception and all four of its conditions apply
- Ductless range hoods are permitted where installed per manufacturer
  instructions (CMC 504.1 Exception 1)
- CEnC 150.0(o)1G requires a nonenclosed kitchen to use a demand-controlled
  range hood meeting CEnC Table 150.0-G or another demand-controlled fan at >= 300
  CFM; a qualifying enclosed kitchen may use continuous exhaust at >= 5 ACH.
  Apply the separate CMC 405.4.1 thresholds where that section governs.

### Bathroom Exhaust
- Minimum exhaust rate: 50 CFM demand-controlled or 20 CFM continuous
  (CEnC 150.0(o)1G, Tables 150.0-E and 150.0-F)
- Each bathroom shall be mechanically ventilated (CALGreen 4.506.1)
- Fan shall be ENERGY STAR compliant and exhaust to the exterior
  (CALGreen 4.506.1)
- Unless functioning as whole-house ventilation, fans must be controlled
  by a humidity control adjustable between 50-80% RH (CALGreen 4.506.1)

### Backdraft Protection
Exhaust ducts shall terminate outside the building and be equipped with
backdraft dampers or motorized dampers that auto-close when the system
is not in use (CMC 504.1.1). Apply its exceptions for a continuously running
fan not discharging to a common plenum, unconditioned served spaces, and
positive-pressure served spaces, plus the agency-specific continuous-fan
exception where relevant.

## 3. Combustion Air for Gas Appliances

### Indoor Air Method
Minimum room volume: 50 cubic feet per 1,000 BTU/h of total input
rating for all appliances in the space (CMC 701.4.1).

### Known Air Infiltration Rate Method
Where infiltration rate is known, use equations in CMC 701.4.2.
For infiltration rates below 0.40 ACH, the known-rate method is
required (CMC 701.4).

### Outdoor Combustion Air — Two Opening Method
Two permanent openings, one within 12 inches of top and one within
12 inches of bottom of enclosure (CMC 701.6.1):

| Connection Type | Minimum Free Area per 1,000 BTU/h |
|---|---|
| Direct to outdoors or vertical duct | 1 sq in per 4,000 BTU/h each opening |
| Horizontal duct to outdoors | 1 sq in per 2,000 BTU/h each opening |

### Outdoor Combustion Air — One Opening Method
One permanent opening within 12 inches of the top of enclosure
(CMC 701.6.2):
- Minimum free area: 1 sq in per 3,000 BTU/h
- Not less than sum of all vent connector areas
- Appliance clearances: 1 inch sides/back, 6 inches front

### Mechanical Combustion Air Supply
Minimum rate: 0.35 CFM per 1,000 BTU/h for all appliances. Appliances
must be interlocked to prevent operation when supply is off (CMC 701.9).

### Direct Vent Appliances
Direct vent appliances are exempt from Chapter 7 combustion air
requirements (CMC 701.1 Exception 1).

### Louvers and Screens
- Wood louvers: assume 25% free area
- Metal louvers/grilles: assume 75% free area
- Screens: not smaller than 1/4 inch mesh (CMC 701.10)

## 4. Duct Systems

### Materials
- Metallic ducts: shall comply with SMACNA HVAC Duct Construction
  Standards (CMC 602.4)
- Flexible metallic ducts: shall comply with UL 181 (CMC 602.4)
- Flexible nonmetallic ducts: shall comply with UL 181 (CMC 602.5.5)

### Duct Sizing
Residential duct systems shall be sized per ACCA Manual D, ACCA Manual
Zr, or other approved methods (CMC 601.2 Exception).

### Return Duct Sizing
California Energy Code Tables 150.0-B and 150.0-C specify return duct
sizing requirements as an alternative to field-verified airflow testing
(CMC 601.2.1).

### Flexible Duct Installation
| Requirement | Standard |
|---|---|
| Minimum length | Use minimum required to make connection (CMC 603.4(1)) |
| Horizontal support interval | 4 feet maximum (CMC 603.4(2)) |
| Vertical support interval | 6 feet maximum (CMC 603.4(3)) |
| Maximum sag | 1/2 inch per foot of spacing (CMC 603.4(4)) |
| Bend radius | Not less than 1 duct diameter (CMC 603.4(6)) |
| Residential length limit | Residential occupancies are excepted only from the CMC 603.4.1 5-ft maximum; other installation, listing, support, and performance constraints still apply |

### Duct Sealing
All ducts regardless of pressure classification shall be sealed to Seal
Class A (CMC 603.1.1). Joints shall be made airtight by tapes, mastics,
gasketing, or other means (CMC 603.9).

| Ductwork Type | Standard | Closure Type | Marking |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rigid metallic/fiberglass | UL 181A | Pressure-sensitive tape | 181A-P |
| Rigid metallic/fiberglass | UL 181A | Mastic tape | 181A-M |
| Flexible air ducts | UL 181B | Pressure-sensitive tape + mechanical fastener | 181B-FX |
| Flexible air ducts | UL 181B | Mastic + mechanical fastener | 181B-M |

### Duct Leakage Testing
For single-family buildings, duct leakage testing requirements are
specified in California Energy Code Section 150.0(m)(11) (CMC 603.9.2.1).

### Duct Insulation
Ducts conveying air exceeding 140 degrees F shall be insulated to maintain
surface temperature at or below 140 degrees F (CMC 605.1). Insulation
shall have flame-spread index not exceeding 25 and smoke-developed index
not exceeding 50 (CMC 605.1.2).

Before applying the 140-degree rule, evaluate all five CMC 605.1 exceptions,
including qualifying factory-installed components, conditioned-space ducts,
short runouts, outlet backs/plenums, and evaporative-cooling-only systems.

## 5. Appliance Clearances

### Furnace Location
- Bedrooms/bathrooms: furnace in closet with listed gasketed self-closing
  door and listed self-closing device, threshold and bottom seal, outdoor
  combustion air under 904.2.1/904.2.2, and exclusive closet use; or a
  direct-vent type (CMC 904.2)
- Under-floor: if supported by ground, on 3 inch minimum concrete slab
  above grade (CMC 904.4.1.1)
- Under-floor: if hung from above, 6 inch minimum clearance from
  finished grade (CMC 904.4.1.2)

### Listed Furnace Clearances
Listed furnaces: per manufacturer instructions and listing (CMC 904.3.1).

### Unlisted Furnace Clearances (Table 904.3.2)
For an unlisted central heating furnace or boiler, use the current Table
904.3.2 appliance, bonnet/plenum, draft-hood, and connector columns directly.
Do not apply "per listing" values to an unlisted unit or rely on a simplified
three-row transcription.

### Supply Duct Clearances from Listed Furnaces
Not less than furnace supply plenum clearance for first 3 feet; no
clearance required beyond 3 feet (CMC 603.13.2).

## 6. Water Heater (see plumbing.md for full requirements)

A fuel-burning water heater in a bedroom/bathroom shall be direct vent, or
installed in an exclusive-use closet with a listed gasketed door and listed
self-closing device, threshold and bottom seal, outdoor combustion air under
CPC 506.4, and compliance with CPC 504.1.1/504.1.2 (CPC 504.1).

## 7. Dryer Venting

### Exhaust Duct Requirements
- Minimum 4 inch diameter rigid metal duct with smooth interior surfaces
  (CMC 504.4.2)
- Thickness not less than 0.016 inch (CMC 504.4.2)
- Shall not connect to a vent connector, gas vent, or chimney (CMC 504.4)
- Shall not terminate in a crawl space, attic, or concealed space (CMC 504.4)
- No screws or fasteners extending into the duct that are capable of catching
  lint and reducing exhaust-system efficiency (CMC 504.4)
- Equipped with backdraft damper at termination; no screens (CMC 504.4)

### Maximum Dryer Duct Length
14 feet total combined horizontal and vertical length, including two
90-degree elbows. Deduct 2 feet for each additional 90-degree elbow
beyond two, unless otherwise permitted or required by approved dryer
manufacturer instructions (CMC 504.4.2.1).

Exception: Exhaust duct power ventilator extends the length per
manufacturer instructions (CMC 504.4.2.3).

### Transition Ducts
Listed transition ducts not exceeding 6 feet in length may connect
the dryer to the exhaust duct. Transition ducts shall not be concealed
within construction (CMC 504.4.2.2).

### Makeup Air
A closet designed for dryer installation shall have a makeup air opening
of not less than 100 square inches (CMC 504.4.1(1)).

### Duct Supports
Supports at intervals not exceeding 4 feet (CMC 504.4.5).

## 8. Fireplace and Chimney Requirements

### Gas Fireplaces
Any newly installed gas fireplace shall be a direct-vent sealed-combustion
type (CMC 912.3(1), CALGreen 4.503.1).

### Factory-Built Fireplaces
Shall comply with UL 127 and be installed per manufacturer instructions
(CMC 913.1). Gasketed doors shall not be installed unless tested per
UL 127 (CMC 913.1.1).

### Chimney Termination
- Gas vents <= 12 in. and >= 8 ft from a vertical wall or similar obstruction:
  terminate per Table 802.6.1 based on roof slope. Other cases use the
  CMC 802.6.1 item 1 alternatives, including the applicable 2-ft/10-ft rule.
- Minimum 5 feet vertical above highest connected draft hood (Type B/L)
- At least 3 feet above a forced air inlet within 10 feet (CMC 802.6.1)

| Roof Slope | Minimum Height Above Roof (ft) |
|---|---|
| Flat to 6/12 | 1.0 |
| Over 6/12 to 7/12 | 1.25 |
| Over 7/12 to 8/12 | 1.5 |
| Over 8/12 to 9/12 | 2.0 |
| Over 9/12 to 10/12 | 2.5 |
| Over 10/12 to 11/12 | 3.25 |

### Masonry Chimney Lining
Masonry chimneys shall be lined with approved clay flue lining, a listed
lining system per UL 1777, or other approved corrosion/heat-resistant
material (CMC 802.5.3).

### Cross-reference
See CRC Chapter 10 for residential fireplace and chimney construction
requirements (firebox dimensions, hearth extensions, clearances to
combustibles).

## 9. Refrigerant Lines (Residential Split Systems)

### Installation
Refrigeration coils installed with forced air furnaces require the blower
to have sufficient capacity for both heating and cooling airflow
(CMC 904.8(1)). Coils shall be installed in parallel with or downstream
of the furnace to avoid condensation on the heating element; a furnace
specifically listed for downstream installation is excepted, and parallel
flow requires tight control dampers or another approved means (CMC 904.8(3)).
Means shall be provided for condensate disposal (CMC 904.8(4)).

### Refrigerant Piping
Refrigerant piping for residential split systems shall comply with
CMC Chapter 11 (Refrigeration) and be installed per manufacturer
instructions. Space-conditioning piping shall comply with CEnC 150.0(j)1B
and its four exceptions for qualifying factory-installed piping, framing
penetrations, QII wall conditions, and piping surrounded by specified
wall/crawlspace/attic insulation.
