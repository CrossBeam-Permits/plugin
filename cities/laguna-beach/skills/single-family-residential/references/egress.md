# Egress, Emergency Escape, Guards, and Habitability

California Residential Code, Title 24, Part 2.5 -- 2025 Edition
Sections R312, R313, R318-R321, R324, R325

## 1. Means of Egress (R318)

Every dwelling unit must have a continuous and unobstructed path from all portions of
the unit to the required egress door **without requiring travel through a garage**. The
egress door must open directly into a public way, or to a yard or court that opens to a
public way (CRC R318.1).

### Egress Door (R318.2)
- Minimum one egress door per dwelling unit.
- Must be **side-hinged**.
- **Clear width:** not less than 32 in. (measured between face of door and stop at
  90 degrees open).
- **Clear height:** not less than 78 in. (from top of threshold to bottom of stop).
- Must be readily openable from inside without key, special knowledge, or effort.
- Other doors in the dwelling are not required to meet these minimums.

### Floors and Landings at Exterior Doors (R318.3)
- Landing or floor required on each side of each exterior door.
- Landing width: not less than the door served.
- Landing depth: not less than 36 in. in the direction of travel.
- Exterior landing slope: max 2% (1/4 in. per 12 in.).
- Exception: an exterior balcony under 60 sf accessed only from a door may have less than
  36 in. depth in the travel direction.

**Floor elevations at the required egress door (R318.3.1):**
- Interior side: landing not more than 1-1/2 in. below top of threshold.
- Exterior side: not more than 7-3/4 in. below top of threshold if door does not swing
  over the landing.

**Floor elevations at other exterior doors (R318.3.2):**
- Landing not more than 7-3/4 in. below top of threshold.
- No exterior landing required where an exterior stairway of not more than 2 risers
  exists and the door does not swing over the stairway.

### Hallways (R318.6)
Minimum hallway width: **3 ft** (CRC R318.6).

### Vertical Egress (R318.4)
For habitable levels or basements more than one story above or below an egress door, the
maximum travel distance from any occupied point to a stairway or ramp providing egress
is **50 ft** (CRC R318.4).

## 2. Stairways (R318.7)

### Key Dimensions

| Dimension | Requirement | Reference |
|---|---|---|
| **Clear width** (above handrail height) | >= 36 in. | R318.7.1 |
| Clear width at/below handrail (one handrail) | >= 31-1/2 in. | R318.7.1 |
| Clear width at/below handrail (two handrails) | >= 27 in. | R318.7.1 |
| **Headroom** | >= 6 ft 8 in. | R318.7.2 |
| **Max vertical rise** per flight | 12 ft 7 in. | R318.7.3 |
| **Max riser height** | 7-3/4 in. | R318.7.5.1 |
| **Min tread depth** | 10 in. | R318.7.5.2 |
| Max riser variation within a flight | 3/8 in. | R318.7.5.1 |
| Max tread variation within a flight | 3/8 in. | R318.7.5.2 |
| Nosing projection | 3/4 in. to 1-1/4 in.; open-riser maximum exception | R318.7.5.3 |
| Max nosing variation within a flight | 3/8 in. | R318.7.5.3 |

Nosing not required where tread depth >= 11 in. (CRC R318.7.5.3, Exception 1).

Risers must be vertical or sloped no more than 30 degrees from vertical. Open risers
more than 30 in. above grade must not allow passage of a 4-inch sphere, except openings
between adjacent spiral-stair treads (CRC R318.7.5.1).

### Winder Treads (R318.7.5.2.1)
- Tread depth >= 10 in. at the walkline (12 in. from inside of turn).
- Tread depth >= 6 in. at any point within clear stair width.
- Largest winder tread at walkline must not exceed smallest by more than 3/8 in.

### Landings (R318.7.6)
- Floor or landing at top and bottom of each flight.
- Landing width >= stair width served.
- Landing depth (straight run) >= 36 in.

Exceptions:
1. Top landing of interior stair may be on opposite side of a door if door does not
   swing over stairs.
2. In enclosed garage, top landing may be up to 7-3/4 in. below threshold.
3. Exterior stair of <= 2 risers does not require a top landing if door does not
   swing over the stairway.
4. The bottom landing may be omitted for exterior stairs to grade with <= 3 risers that
   serve a deck, porch, or patio and are not the required access to grade serving the
   egress door.

### Stairway Walking Surface (R318.7.7)
Treads and landings: max slope 2% (1:48). At landings required to drain, max 5% (1:20)
in direction of travel.

### Handrails at Stairs (R318.7.8)
Required on at least one side of each flight with **four or more risers**.

### Spiral Stairways (R318.7.11.1)

| Dimension | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Clear width at/below handrail | >= 26 in. |
| Walkline radius | <= 24-1/2 in. |
| Tread depth at walkline | >= 6-3/4 in. |
| Max riser height | 9-1/2 in. |
| Headroom | >= 6 ft 6 in. |
| Treads | Must be identical |

### Stair Illumination (R318.7.10, R325.6, R325.7)
- Interior stairs: artificial light capable of >= 1 footcandle at center of treads and
  landings. Wall switch at each floor level if stairway has >= 6 risers, unless remote,
  central, or automatic control satisfies the R325.6 exception.
- Exterior stairs: artificial light at top landing (and bottom landing for basement
  access from grade).

## 3. Ramps (R318.8)

| Ramp Type | Max Slope |
|---|---|
| Serving required egress door | 1:12 (8.3%) |
| Other ramps | 1:8 (12.5%) |

R318.8 does not govern a ramp that is not within or serving a building, porch, or deck.
Where site constraints make 1:12 technically infeasible, a required-door ramp may use
the R318.8.1 1:8 exception.

Landings required at top and bottom, where doors open onto ramps, and where ramps
change direction. Landing depth >= 36 in. (CRC R318.8.2).

Handrails required on at least one side of ramps exceeding 1:12 slope (CRC R318.8.3).

## 4. Emergency Escape and Rescue Openings (R319)

### Where Required (R319.1)
An operable EERO is required in:
- Every sleeping room
- Basements
- Habitable attics
- The room to which a sleeping loft is open

Must open directly to a public way, or to a yard or court that opens to a public way.

**Exceptions:**
1. Basements with ceiling height < 80 in.
2. Basements or sleeping rooms with an exit or exit access door opening directly to a
   public way or to a yard, court, or exterior egress balcony that opens to one.
3. Basements without habitable spaces and <= 200 sf.
4. Storm shelters per ICC 500.
5. In sprinklered dwellings (R309), basement sleeping rooms are exempt if the basement
   has one means of egress (R318) plus one EERO, or two means of egress.

### Minimum Dimensions (R319.2)

| Dimension | Requirement | Reference |
|---|---|---|
| **Net clear opening area** | >= 5.7 sf | R319.2.1 |
| Net clear opening area (grade floor) | >= 5.0 sf | R319.2.1 Exception |
| **Min net clear opening height** | 24 in. | R319.2.2 |
| **Min net clear opening width** | 20 in. | R319.2.2 |
| **Max sill height** from floor | 44 in. | R319.2.3 |

Dimensions are the result of normal operation of the opening. Under decks/porches, the
opening must be fully openable with a path >= 36 in. wide and >= 36 in. high to a yard
or court (CRC R319.2.4).

### Operational Requirements (R319.1.1)
- Must be operable from inside without keys, tools, or special knowledge.
- Window opening control devices per ASTM F2090 are permitted and must be <= 70 in.
  above finished floor.
- Security bars (burglar bars) installed on or after July 1, 2000 must comply with
  CBC Part 12, Chapter 12-3.

### Area Wells (R319.4)
Required where the bottom of the EERO clear opening is below adjacent grade.
- Min horizontal area: 9 sf, with projection and width >= 36 in.
- If vertical depth > 44 in., a permanently affixed ladder or steps is required.
- Ladder rungs: inside width >= 12 in., project >= 3 in. from wall, spaced <= 18 in. OC.
- A ladder or steps may encroach up to 6 in. into the required well dimensions.
- Connect to the foundation drainage system unless the well-drained/specified Group I
  soil exception in R319.4.3 applies.

## 5. Handrails (R320)

### Height (R320.2)
- **34 to 38 in.** measured vertically from the sloped plane adjoining tread nosing or
  ramp surface.
- Alternating tread devices and ship's ladders: 30 to 34 in.
- Listed fittings and bendings may exceed 38 in.; a volute, turnout, or starting easing
  may be located over the lowest tread.

### Projection (R320.3)
Handrails normally project no more than 4-1/2 in. on either side. At passing handrails,
landing/floor/passing-flight nosings may permit up to 6-1/2 in. where required width and
clearance remain.

### Clearance (R320.4)
Not less than 1-1/2 in. between handrail and adjacent wall.

### Continuity (R320.5)
Continuous for the full length of the flight, from directly above the nosing at the top
landing to directly above the lowest nosing. Ends must return to the wall, guard, or
walking surface. Returns must not create a gap > 1/4 in. from the wall.

Interruption permitted by a newel post at turns, landings, or over the lowest tread
(CRC R320.5). A volute, turnout, or starting easing may also terminate over the lowest
tread.

### Grip Size (R320.6)

| Type | Cross-Section Requirements |
|---|---|
| Type I (circular) | Outside diameter: 1-1/4 to 2 in. |
| Type I (non-circular) | Perimeter: 4 to 6-1/4 in.; max cross-section: 2-1/4 in. |
| Type II (perimeter > 6-1/4 in.) | Graspable finger recess on both sides; width above recess: 1-1/4 to 2-3/4 in. |

Equivalent graspability is permitted. This table is a summary: Type II rails must meet
all R320.6 recess start, depth, and continuation geometry, and both types must meet the
section's edge-radius requirements.

## 6. Guards and Window Fall Protection (R321)

### Guards -- Where Required (R321.1.1)
Required at open-sided walking surfaces (floors, stairs, ramps, landings) located more
than **30 in.** above the floor or grade below, measured vertically at any point within
36 in. horizontally to the edge of the open side.

### Guard Height (R321.1.2)

| Location | Min. Guard Height |
|---|---|
| Open-sided walking surfaces, porches, balconies, landings | **42 in.** above adjacent walking surface |
| Open sides of stairs | **34 in.** from line connecting nosings |
| Where top of guard serves as handrail on open side of stairs | **34 to 38 in.** from nosing line |

### Opening Limitations (R321.1.3)
- Must not allow passage of a **4-inch sphere** from walking surface to guard height.
- Triangular openings at stair risers: must not allow passage of a **6-inch sphere**.
- Open side of stairs: must not allow passage of a **4-3/8-inch sphere**.

### Window Fall Protection (R321.2)
Required where **all** of the following conditions exist:
- Bottom of clear opening of an operable window is **less than 24 in.** above finished
  floor.
- Window is **greater than 72 in.** (6 ft) above finished exterior grade.

Compliance options (CRC R321.2.1):
1. Window openings do not allow a 4-inch sphere to pass in the largest opened position.
2. Window opening control devices or fall prevention devices per ASTM F2090.

Where an operable window serves as an EERO, the fall prevention device must still allow
the window to fully open to meet EERO net clear opening requirements (CRC R321.2.2).

## 7. Minimum Room Areas (R312)

| Requirement | Dimension | Reference |
|---|---|---|
| Min floor area (habitable rooms) | **70 sf** | R312.1 |
| Min horizontal dimension (habitable rooms) | **7 ft** | R312.2 |
| Min horizontal dimension -- kitchens | No minimum | R312.1/R312.2 Exceptions |

The 7-ft dimension also has the limited-density owner-built rural dwelling exception in
R301.1.1.1.
Portions of a room with a sloping ceiling less than 5 ft or a furred ceiling less than
7 ft above finished floor do not count toward the minimum required habitable area
(CRC R312.3).

## 8. Ceiling Height (R313)

| Space | Min. Ceiling Height | Reference |
|---|---|---|
| **Habitable space, hallways** | **7 ft** | R313.1 |
| **Bathrooms, toilet rooms, laundry rooms** | **6 ft 8 in.** | R313.1 |
| Non-habitable basement portions | 6 ft 8 in. | R313.1.1 |
| Habitable attics/basements in existing buildings | 6 ft 8 in. (bathrooms: 6 ft 4 in.) | R313.1.2 |

### Sloped Ceiling Rule (R313.1, Exception 1)
Required floor area must have a ceiling height >= 5 ft, and not less than 50% of the
required floor area must have a ceiling height >= 7 ft.

### Obstructions
- In nonhabitable basement portions, beams, girders, ducts, and other obstructions may
  project to 6 ft 4 in. under R313.1.1.
- Beams, girders, ducts in basements with habitable space: may project to within 6 ft
  4 in. of finished floor (CRC R313.1, Exception 3).
- Beams and girders spaced >= 36 in. clear width: may project to 78 in. from finished
  floor (CRC R313.1, Exception 4).
- Shower/tub with showerhead: >= 6 ft 8 in. over area >= 30 in. x 30 in. at showerhead
  (CRC R313.1, Exception 2).

## 9. Light, Ventilation, and Heating (R325)

### Natural Light (R325.1.1)
Habitable rooms must have aggregate glazing area >= **8% of the floor area**.

Exceptions:
1. Glazing may face into a roofed porch/deck/patio adjacent to a yard/public way if the
   longer side is >= 65% unobstructed and ceiling height >= 7 ft.
2. Glazing may face into an adjacent sunroom only under the listed adjacency condition.
3. Not required where artificial light provides >= 6 footcandles at 30 in. above floor.
4. Apply the separate R325.1.1 eave exception where its geometry is met.
5. Apply the passive-solar-collector exception with all of its opening conditions.

### Natural Ventilation (R325.1.2)
Habitable rooms must have openable area >= **4% of the floor area** to the outdoors.

Exceptions:
1. Not required (except kitchens) where whole-house mechanical ventilation provides
   0.35 ACH per the California Mechanical Code.
2. Kitchens exempt where local exhaust is installed per California Mechanical Code.
3. Apply the qualifying roofed-area exception and its opening conditions.
4. Apply the thermally isolated sunroom exception and its opening conditions.
5. Apply the passive-solar-collector exception and its opening conditions.

### Bathrooms (R325.2)
- Glazing: >= 3 sf, half openable, OR
- Artificial light + local exhaust (50 cfm intermittent or 20 cfm continuous) exhausted
  directly outdoors.

Each bathroom with a bathtub, shower, or tub/shower must be mechanically ventilated per
the California Mechanical Code for humidity control. Window operation alone is not
sufficient for bathroom exhaust (CRC R325.2.1).

### Required Heating (R325.8)
Where winter design temp < 60 degrees F: heating facilities must maintain >= 68 degrees F
at 3 ft above floor and 2 ft from exterior walls. Portable heaters are not acceptable
(CRC R325.8).

## 10. Safety Glazing (R324)

### Hazardous Locations Requiring Safety Glazing

| Location | Conditions | Reference |
|---|---|---|
| **Glazing in doors** | All fixed/operable panels in swinging, sliding, bifold doors | R324.4.1 |
| **Adjacent to doors** | Bottom edge < 60 in. above floor AND within 24 in. of door edge (or 24 in. of hinge side of in-swinging door on a wall < 180 degrees from door plane) | R324.4.2 |
| **In windows** | All four conditions met: pane > 9 sf, bottom edge < 18 in. above floor, top edge > 36 in. above floor, walking surface within 36 in. | R324.4.3 |
| **Guards and railings** | All glazing in guards/railings regardless of area or height | R324.4.4 |
| **Near wet surfaces** | Bottom edge < 60 in. above standing/walking surface, within 60 in. horizontally of water's edge of tub/spa/pool or edge of shower | R324.4.5 |
| **Adjacent to stairs/ramps** | Bottom edge < 36 in. above walking surface of flights, ramp runs, and landings | R324.4.6 |
| **Adjacent to bottom stair landing** | < 36 in. above landing, within 60-in. arc of bottom tread nosing | R324.4.7 |

Apply the location-specific exceptions before treating glazing as hazardous:

- R324.4.1: decorative glazing and openings that cannot pass a 3-in. sphere.
- R324.4.2: decorative glazing, an intervening barrier, qualifying shallow
  closets/storage, and glazing adjacent to a fixed patio-door panel.
- R324.4.3: decorative glazing, a qualifying protective rail, and the specified outboard
  pane at least 25 ft above exterior horizontal surfaces.
- R324.4.6: a qualifying protective rail or glazing at least 36 in. horizontally from the
  walking surface.
- R324.4.7: an R321 guard where the glazing plane is more than 18 in. from the guard.

R324.3.1 defaults to the applicable CPSC 16 CFR 1201 category and table variation. ANSI
Z97.1 Class A is permitted only by the section's exception outside doors and listed wet
enclosures, subject to Table R324.3.1(2).

### Skylights and Sloped Glazing (R324.6)
- R324.6.2 permits laminated glass, fully tempered, heat-strengthened, wired glass, and
  approved rigid plastics subject to its material-specific conditions; laminated glass
  must meet the stated interlayer, pane-area, and height criteria. Apply the separate
  retention-screen provisions and exceptions in R324.6.3-R324.6.5.
- Curbs required for skylights on roofs with pitch < 3:12 -- minimum 4 in. above roof
  plane, unless the manufacturer's installation instructions specify otherwise
  (CRC R324.6.8).
