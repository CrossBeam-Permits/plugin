# Laguna Beach Objective Design Standards — Form Group D

**Source:** *Objective Design Standards for Single-Family and Two-Family Residential Development*, Chapter 3D, City document 26790, published August 2026.

Use this reference only after the applicant has selected Form Group D: **Ranch, Minimal Traditional, Mid-20th-Century, or Contemporary**. Load it with `objective-design-standards-2026.md`; do not combine its standards with another form group. Ask the applicant to choose when the form group is not stated—the source does not assign groups by zone, neighborhood, or context.

Section anchors: §3D.1 Roofs; §3D.2 Exterior Walls; §3D.3 Exterior Openings; §3D.4 Porches, Decks, and Balconies; §3D.5 Equipment Screening; §3D.6 Colors. The applicant's checklist must identify the plan-sheet location for every applicable standard.

**Source erratum:** PDF page 68 prints the accent-color rule as “3B.6.4” inside Chapter 3D. This reference uses the structurally correct identifier **3D.6.4** and preserves the source text.

### 3D.1.1 (PDF p. 58)

Roofs, allowed types. Utilize at least one of the following roof types. All other roof types are prohibited:
a. Gabled.
b. Cross-gabled.
c. Hipped.
d. Shed.

### 3D.1.2 (PDF p. 58)

Roof material. Provide one of the following roof materials:
a. Flat roofs only: built-up roofing with pea gravel ballast. Composite shingles and tiles that simulate slate and concrete.
b. Concrete tiles.
c. Metal roofing and standing seam metal roofing.
d. Slate shingle tile.

### 3D.1.3 (PDF p. 58)

Roof pitch. Roof pitches shall be a minimum 2:12 slope and maximum 8:12 slope.

### 3D.1.4 (PDF p. 59)

Optional: roof rake. If provided, measured from the exterior wall to the end of the rake, rakes shall horizontally project a minimum of 18 inches and a maximum of three feet.

### 3D.1.5 (PDF p. 59)

Optional: roof eave. If provided, eaves shall horizontally project a minimum of 18 inches.

### 3D.2.1 (PDF p. 60)

Exterior wall materials and finishes. Provide one of the following materials for exterior walls. At building corners, all materials shall wrap around the corner for a minimum distance of two feet.
a. Natural finish stucco, or pigmented or painted stucco utilizing one of the following finishes:
i. Combed finish.
ii. Light Dash finish.
iii. Medium Dash finish.
iv. 16/20 Medium Sand Float finish.
v. 20/30 Fine Sand Float finish.
vi. Santa Barbara finish.
vii. Smooth finish.
b. Wood or fiber cement, horizontally or vertically oriented lap or plank siding, shingles, or board and batten siding.
c. Fiber cement architectural panels with no individually expressed panel larger than 30 inches by five feet in size.

### 3D.2.2 (PDF p. 60)

Optional: secondary wall materials. When a secondary wall material is used, provide no more than one of the following secondary wall materials not to exceed 20 percent of the area of all of the walls facing a single orientation.
At building corners, all materials shall wrap around the corner for a minimum distance of two feet.
a. Board-formed gray concrete, natural or stained or painted per the requirements of §3D.6.
b. Salt finished concrete, natural or stained or painted per the requirements of §3D.6.
c. Wash pebbled concrete, naturally finished.
d. Textured concrete.
e. Brick.
f. Ceramic tile.
g. Stone or veneer stone with a natural finish.

### 3D.3.1 (PDF p. 61)

Exterior openings percentage of wall. Exterior openings on building walls with the same orientation, inclusive of doors and windows, shall be a minimum of 15 percent and a maximum of 30 percent.

### 3D.3.2 (PDF p. 61)

Exterior opening types. Provide a minimum of three distinct exterior opening sizes, inclusive of doors and windows.

### 3D.3.3 (PDF p. 61)

Exterior opening shapes. Windows shall be rectangular and/or right trapezoid in shape.

### 3D.3.4 (PDF p. 61)

Exterior opening surrounds. Opening surrounds, when provided at the exterior, shall comply with the dimensions per the table below.
Window Surround Dimension Apron 4 inches minimum Head 4 inches minimum Jamb 4 inches minimum

### 3D.3.5 (PDF p. 61)

Exterior opening materials. With the exception of the front door, provide one of the following exterior opening materials with clear glazing:
a. Wood, pre-finished, stained, or painted per the color requirements of §3D.6.
b. Metal-clad wood, pre-finished or painted per the color requirements of §3D.6.
c. Metal or steel that is clear anodized, oil-rubbed bronze, or pre-finished and painted per the color requirements of §3D.6.
d. Composite, acrylic, or fiberglass set a minimum of two inches inward from the adjacent exterior surface and finished per the color requirements of §3D.6.

### 3D.3.6 (PDF p. 62)

Window types. Provide at least one and a maximum of two of the following window types:
a. Awning.
b. Casement.
c. Fixed.
d. Hopper.
e. Sliding.

### 3D.3.7 (PDF p. 62)

Window recess. Not applicable.

### 3D.3.8 (PDF p. 62)

Window muntins, mullions, and sticking. Windows that include muntins, mullions, and/or sticking shall incorporate full sticking establishing true divided glazing. Simulated sticking and snap-on or between glass muntins are prohibited. Snap on and/or between glass muntins is also prohibited.
Sticking, stiles, and muntins that divide windows shall be a minimum of one inch in profile.

### 3D.3.9 (PDF p. 62)

Window sashes, rails, and stiles. Not applicable.

### 3D.3.10 (PDF p. 62)

Door leaves. Not applicable.

### 3D.3.11 (PDF p. 62)

Door recess. Not applicable.

### 3D.3.12 (PDF p. 62)

Optional: transoms and sidelites. If provided, the maximum width of sidelites shall be half the width of the door. Transom windows shall not extend beyond the door or sidelite edges and shall not exceed 18 inches in height.

### 3D.3.13 (PDF p. 62)

Door location. Doors shall be placed off-center in relation to the overall length of the street-facing facade(s) or located on a wall perpendicular to the street-facing facade within a street-facing facade recess.

### 3D.4.1 (PDF p. 63)

Entry element. Provide two or more of the following entry elements.
a. Street-facing entries placed either at grade or a maximum 18 inches above the adjacent finished grade shall comply with at least one of the following:
i. Recessed a minimum of two feet and a maximum of five feet from a building plane that faces a street.
ii. Framed by an arched or segmental arched opening(s).
iii. Incorporated into a projecting minor mass that does not exceed the eave line by more than three feet or a tower element that does not exceed the otherwise allowed height limit.
b. An entry path no greater than five feet in width shall connect at least one street to the primary residential entry. Within ten feet of the entry, the path may exceed this width but shall not exceed in this area adjacent to the entry a width of more than eight feet. The entry path shall incorporate one or more of the following materials:
i. Colored and scored concrete with score lines placed no more than 30 inches apart.
ii. Scored concrete with score lines placed no more than 30 inches apart.
iii. Concrete or stone pavers no larger than 30 inches by 30 inches.
iv. Brick used as a uniform or as an accent material.
c. Porches with columns and/or piers, comprising all of the following standards:
• Porches shall make up a maximum of 60 percent of the primary frontage.
• Columns shall be one of the following shapes.
1. H-column.
2. I-beam.
3. Square.
• Columns shall be wood, stained or painted per the requirements in §3D.6.
d. Roof overhangs comprising all of the following standards.
• Overhangs shall provide a minimum horizontal projection of three feet.
• Overhangs may be supported by brackets.
e. Trellises, either stained, natural, or painted per the requirements in §3D.6.

### 3D.4.2 (PDF p. 64)

Fences and wall materials. Fences and walls facing streets or rights-of-way shall be constructed of the following materials or any combination of the following materials:
a. Wood, stained or painted per the requirements in §3D.6.
b. Stucco or stucco plaster, integrally colored or painted and matching the same color as the main residential structure.

### 3D.5.1 (PDF p. 65)

Optional: building elements. If the following building elements are incorporated into a design, they shall meet the following criteria:
a. Chimneys, utilizing one of the following materials:
i. Brick, naturally fired or painted to match the same color as the main residential structure.
ii. Stucco, painted and matching the same color as the main residential structure.
b. Chimney caps utilizing at least one of the following:
i. Stucco or brick coping cap the same length and depth as the chimney.
ii. Stucco or brick cornice and cap no more than 16 inches in height and projecting no more than three inches.
c. Exterior stairs and ramps that connect the site and walkways to the interior floor level shall comply with all of the following standards:
• Exterior stair railings shall utilize one of the following materials:
1. Metal, pre-finished or painted per the requirements of §3D.6.
2. Steel or wrought iron, pre-finished or painted per the requirements of §3D.6.
3. Wood, painted or stained per the requirements of §3D.6.
• Treads and risers shall be constructed of one or more of the following materials:
1. Brick.
2. Standard gray or colored concrete.
3. Pavers and tiles meeting the criteria of standard D.4.1.b.

### 3D.5.2 (PDF p. 66)

Building components and details. Buildings and structures shall incorporate a minimum of three of the following architectural details:
a. Use of secondary wall material (see standard D.2.2.) at the entry facade and abutting primary entry door.
b. Awnings above openings with a maximum three-foot horizontal projection, colored per the requirements of §3D.6.
c. Corner window expression.
d. Entry porch a minimum of five feet in depth inclusive of recesses, under the main roof form.
e. Entry door, paneled, pre-finished, natural, or painted with a minimum of three square feet of glazed area.
f. Roof eaves per the requirements of standard D.1.5.
g. Roof rake per the requirements of standard D.1.4.
h. Roof ridges, a minimum of two distinct heights with a vertical separation between adjoining roof ridges of at least one foot.
i. Picture window facing a street, with a minimum of 48 square feet of opening area.

### 3D.5.3 (PDF p. 66)

Visible exterior lighting, shell and casing material. The shell and casing of exterior lighting fixtures with visible light sources shall utilize one of the following materials:
a. Steel or wrought iron, painted per the requirements of §3D.6.
b. Copper or oil-rubbed bronze, naturally finished.
