# Energy Code Requirements for Single-Family Residential

California Energy Code, Title 24, Part 6 — 2025 Edition.
This reference covers mandatory measures (Subchapter 7), prescriptive
and performance compliance paths (Subchapter 8), insulation, fenestration,
HVAC, duct sealing, PV requirements, water heating, battery storage, and
Energy Code Compliance (ECC) field verification and diagnostic testing for
newly constructed single-family residential buildings.

---

## 1. Compliance Paths

### Two Paths to Compliance (Section 150.1)
Single-family residential buildings must comply with the Energy Code
through one of two approaches:

1. **Performance approach** — Demonstrate that the proposed building
   energy use does not exceed the energy budget for the standard
   building design using approved energy compliance software (e.g.,
   CBECC-Res). Allows trade-offs between building components.

2. **Prescriptive approach** — Meet all individual component requirements
   specified in Section 150.1 and Table 150.1-A without trade-offs.
   Every component must meet or exceed the prescribed values.

Both paths require compliance with all mandatory measures in Section 150.0.

## 2. Mandatory Measures (Section 150.0)

### (a) Roof Deck, Ceiling, and Rafter-Roof Insulation
- Roof-deck, ceiling, and rafter-roof assemblies shall meet the applicable
  Section 150.0(a) U-factor/R-value and sealed-contact requirements
- Attic access shall meet the applicable 150.0(a) insulation and gasketing rules
- Loose-fill insulation: minimum installed weight per square foot shall
  conform to manufacturer's labeled R-value (Section 150.0(b))

### Quality Insulation Installation (QII)
- QII is a field verification protocol ensuring insulation is properly
  installed with no gaps, voids, compressions, or misalignments
- QII is a mandatory requirement for prescriptive compliance in all
  climate zones
- Field verified by a certified ECC-Rater under Reference Residential Appendix
  3.5 at the applicable framing and insulation-installation stages
- The 2025 prescriptive package requires QII; a non-QII design must establish
  compliance through an allowed performance approach rather than higher
  prescriptive R-values

### (c) Fenestration
- All fenestration products shall be rated and labeled by NFRC
- U-factor and SHGC values shall be from NFRC-certified ratings
- Default values apply where NFRC ratings are unavailable

### (m) Air Distribution System (Duct Sealing and Testing)
- All duct joints and seams shall be sealed with mastic, tape, or
  other approved closure system (Section 150.0(m)(1))
- Building cavities shall not be used as ducts or plenums except where
  specifically permitted
- Duct leakage testing is mandatory for new construction
  (Section 150.0(m)(11)):
  - **(A) Post-construction (air handler installed, ducts connected):**
    total duct leakage shall not exceed **5 percent of air handler
    airflow** (per RA3.1.4.3.1)
  - **(B) Rough-in stage:**
    - (i) Air handler installed: total leakage shall not exceed
      **5 percent of air handler airflow** (per RA3.1.4.3.2)
    - (ii) Air handler not yet installed: total leakage shall not
      exceed **4 percent of air handler airflow** (per RA3.1.4.3.2)
  - Air handler airflow for compliance targets determined per
    RA3.1.4.2
- Testing performed by a certified ECC-Rater using duct pressurization
  diagnostic procedures specified in Reference Residential Appendix
  RA3.1

### (h) HVAC System Sizing
- Loads shall be calculated using one of the methods allowed by 150.0(h)1,
  including ACCA Manual J
- Equipment selection and sizing shall follow Manual S-2023 and the
  equipment-specific limits in 150.0(h)5; do not apply a blanket maximum to
  heat-pump heating capacity
- Residential duct design separately follows CMC 601.2 using Manual D,
  Manual Zr where applicable, or another approved method
- Load-calculation results are documented on the CF1R Certificate of
  Compliance; the applicable CF2R certifies installed HVAC features and the
  controls/tests required by 150.0(h)

### (n) Water Heating — Mandatory Requirements
- Service water heating systems shall comply with Section 110.3 for
  all mandatory requirements
- Residential water heating systems shall comply with Section 150.0(n)
- Heat pump water heaters are the prescriptive standard for all
  climate zones in the 2025 code
- Solar water heating systems, where installed, shall comply with
  applicable Section 110.3 requirements and be certified and rated by SRCC,
  IAPMO R&T, or another Executive-Director-approved listing agency under
  150.0(n)2

### Solar Photovoltaic (PV) System Requirements — Section 150.1(c)14

New single-family residential buildings are required to include a solar
PV system (Section 150.1(c)14), subject to the section's exceptions. This
is a California-specific mandate effective since the 2019 code cycle.

**Minimum PV System Size:**

- Determined using Equation 150.1-C and the building's conditioned floor
  area, dwelling-unit count, and climate-zone adjustment factors
- Expressed as kWdc of PV installed capacity
- Table 150.1-C provides CFA and dwelling unit adjustment factors for
  Equation 150.1-C

**PV System Requirements:**

- PV systems or modules shall meet the minimum qualification requirements
  in Joint Appendix JA11
- Minimum annual electrical output is the smaller of the Equation 150.1-C
  result or the applicable solar access roof area (SARA) cap
- PV systems shall comply with CEC Article 690
- Where a PV system operates in parallel with the utility grid, it shall also
  comply with the applicable CEC Article 705 and utility interconnection rules

**Exceptions to PV Requirement:**

- SARA excludes roof area with less than 70 percent annual solar access
- For steep-slope roofs, SARA excludes roof area with a northerly azimuth
  from 300 degrees through 90 degrees from true north; no PV system is
  required if the remaining SARA is less than 80 contiguous square feet
- No PV system is required if the minimum size under Section 150.1(c)14
  is less than 1.8 kWdc
- Other exceptions in Section 150.1(c)14 include qualifying approved snow-load
  roof designs and specified pre-2020 local-planning conditions

### (s) Battery Energy Storage System (BESS) Ready

Section 150.0(s) applies to single-family residences with one or two
dwelling units where the load-serving entity provides electrical service
greater than 125 amperes. It requires:

- Either BESS-ready interconnection equipment with at least 60 amperes of
  backed-up capacity and the specified branch circuits, or a dedicated
  raceway at least 1 inch in trade size from the main service to the
  panelboard supplying those circuits
- At least four identified branch circuits collocated at one panelboard,
  including the refrigerator, lighting near the primary egress, and a
  sleeping-room receptacle circuit
- A main panelboard with a busbar rating of at least 225 amperes
- Reserved space for future system-isolation or transfer equipment within
  3 feet of the main panelboard, with connecting raceways
- A building with a BESS already installed is excepted from the readiness
  requirements

### Solar-Ready Requirements — Sections 150.0(r) and 110.10

Section 110.10 is a solar-readiness provision. For single-family residences,
it applies to qualifying subdivisions with ten or more residences where a PV
system is not installed.

- A designated solar zone of 250 sq ft minimum, subject to the exceptions
  in Section 110.10(b), shall be provided on the roof or overhang
- Solar-zone sections on steep-sloped roofs shall have an azimuth between
  90 and 300 degrees from true north
- A pathway for conduit from the solar zone to the electrical panel
  area shall be provided
- The main electrical service panel shall have at least a 200-ampere busbar
  and reserved breaker space marked for a future solar electric installation

### Air Sealing Requirements
- Building envelope shall be sealed to minimize air leakage
  (Section 110.7)
- Penetrations in the building envelope (electrical, plumbing,
  mechanical) shall be sealed with caulk, foam, gaskets, or other
  approved materials
- Recessed ceiling luminaires shall have no screw-base socket, be labeled
  airtight below 2.0 cfm at 75 Pa, be gasketed/caulked and sealed, and comply
  with CEC 410.116, subject to the fire-rated and noninsulated-ceiling
  exceptions. Apply Type IC only where the electrical-code insulation-contact
  condition requires it.
- Whole-building air leakage testing is not mandatory for prescriptive
  compliance but is credit-eligible under the performance approach

### 3.7 Water Heating (Section 150.1(c)8)

Prescriptive compliance requires option A, B, or C — any option applies in all 16 climate zones:

- **Option A:** One 240 V heat-pump water heater (HPWH) installed in garage or conditioned space; **plus** compact hot-water distribution in CZ 1 and CZ 16 (field verified per RA4.4.6); **plus** drain-water heat recovery in CZ 16 (field verified per RA3.6.9).
- **Option B:** One 240 V HPWH meeting NEEA Advanced Water Heater Specification Tier 3 or higher; **plus** drain-water heat recovery in CZ 16 with storage tank in garage or conditioned space.
- **Option C:** Solar water-heating with electric backup meeting RA4 and annual solar savings fraction ≥ 0.7.

Exception: Point-of-use electric for dwelling units ≤ 500 sq ft CFA (per RA4.4.5). Exception: 120 V HPWH allowed in lieu of 240 V for one-bedroom-or-fewer units.

## 4. ECC Field Verification and Diagnostic Testing

### Measures and Applicability
The following measures require field verification and diagnostic testing by a
certified ECC-Rater when the cited system, climate-zone, or compliance-option
trigger applies:

| Measure | ECC Verification Type / Trigger |
|---|---|
| Duct leakage | Duct pressurization where 150.0(m)11 applies |
| Quality Insulation Installation (QII) | RA3.5 inspection stages for a prescriptive package or claimed performance measure |
| Refrigerant charge | Applicable Table 150.1-A climate zones/system types |
| Airflow across indoor coil | Applicable system or compliance option |
| Fan watt draw | Applicable system or compliance option |
| Return duct design or testing | Selected sizing-verification/static-pressure option |
| Envelope sealing | Blower-door test only when performance credit is taken |
| HVAC system installation | Applicable Certificates of Installation/Verification |

### ECC-Rater
- Must be certified by an approved ECC-Provider
- Performs each required verification at the stage specified by the applicable
  Reference Residential Appendix 3 procedure
- Registers Certificates of Verification with the ECC-Provider data registry

### Certificates
- **CF1R** — Certificate of Compliance (design phase, by designer/builder)
- **CF2R** — Certificate of Installation (by installing contractor)
- **CF3R** — Certificate of Verification (by an ECC-Rater where field verification applies)

The applicable CF1R and CF2R documents are required; CF3R documents are required only
for measures needing field verification. Complete and approve all required project
documents by the applicable inspection or occupancy milestone under Admin 10-103,
including its limited waiver/simplified-document provisions.

Under the Section 150.1(b) performance-path exception, an Energy
Commission-approved community shared solar or other renewable generation
system, or community shared BESS, may offset part or all of the required
solar-generation and demand-flexibility Energy Design Rating.

## 6. Additions and Alterations (Section 150.2)

### Additions
- Apply the distinct Section 150.2(a) framework: applicable Sections 110.0-110.9
  and listed 150.0 provisions plus the required prescriptive or performance
  path and all six threshold/scope exceptions
- Under the 150.2(a)1 prescriptive approach, additions over 700 sf follow the
  modified 150.1(c) route and additions at or below 700 sf follow their
  separate rules. The 150.2(a)2 performance approach remains a separate option
  with its own energy-budget routes. Additions do not trigger PV.
- The existing building does not need to be brought into full compliance
  unless 150.2 specifically triggers an existing duct, equipment, or component

### Alterations
- Altered components and newly installed equipment meet the applicable
  150.2(b) provisions, including each subsection's thresholds, alternate
  criteria, and exceptions
- A covered space-conditioning equipment installation or replacement triggers
  150.2(b)1E duct sealing unless ducts were previously verified, total under
  40 linear ft, or contain qualifying asbestos; apply the separate garage rule
- Replacement fenestration generally follows 150.2(b)1B, including the <= 75-sf
  vertical-fenestration alternative, skylight alternative, CZ15 SHGC rule, and
  glass-in-existing-sash/frame repair note
- A roof alteration follows 150.2(b)1I only within its roof-area/scope and
  climate-zone triggers, reflectance/insulation tradeoffs, and material,
  building-integrated-solar, heavy-roof, and roof-insulation exceptions
