# Attached Patio-Cover Wall Connection Routing

Source: 2025 California Residential Code (Title 24, Part 2.5), Appendix BF and
applicable Chapters 3, 8 and 9.

Appendix BF requires the patio cover to resist its applicable dead, live/snow
and wind loads. It does not supply a statewide wall-ledger, fastener, flashing
or lateral-hold-down schedule.

CRC R507.9 is a **deck-ledger** provision. Its joist-span fastener table,
placement table, flashing details and hold-down options do not automatically
apply to a patio roof. Use an R507 detail only when an applicable approved design
source expressly adopts it for the patio-cover configuration and loads.

## Configuration

### Attached cover

An attached cover transfers some combination of gravity, lateral and uplift
forces into the existing building. The construction documents must identify the
existing wall/roof framing, connection location, member sizes and materials,
and the complete load path through the existing structure to the foundation.

### Freestanding cover

A genuinely freestanding cover has its own support and lateral-force-resisting
system and does not depend on the dwelling wall for structural support. It still
requires a complete gravity/wind/uplift load path. Appendix BF does not require
knee braces at every post or provide a statewide freestanding connection
schedule.

## Connection Design Inputs

Before accepting an attached-cover connection, verify:

1. actual BF105.1 dead, live/snow and R301.2.1 wind loads;
2. rafter/beam reactions, uplift and lateral demands;
3. existing wall, rim, stud, header, roof or masonry structural material and
   condition;
4. sheathing, cladding, veneer and water-resistive-barrier conditions;
5. fastener/anchor type, diameter, embedment, spacing, edge/end distances and
   corrosion compatibility;
6. bearing and connection capacity under R802.6 and R802.11.2 where applicable;
7. decay protection under R304 where triggered; and
8. the downstream load path through the existing building.

Do not infer that stucco, siding or masonry veneer is structural support. An
anchor into concrete/masonry, steel framing, engineered rim material, or an
existing assembly with uncertain capacity needs a source/design that addresses
that actual substrate and condition.

## Acceptable Source Routes

Exact connection and fastener details may come from:

- an applicable CRC provision that expressly covers the condition;
- accepted engineering under R802.2/R802.11.2;
- current listed-product evaluation and installation instructions; or
- a current jurisdiction standard plan that expressly covers the patio-cover
  type, loads, substrate and project conditions.

Every local or external numerical recipe must include:

1. named jurisdiction, agency, manufacturer or standards publisher;
2. current document/product/standard title and edition or effective date;
3. exact page, section, table or detail;
4. geographic and project-type scope; and
5. confirmation that the source remains current.

Without all five, do not use an inherited lag-screw, bolt, ledger-size,
edge-distance, hold-down, anchor or connection number as a requirement.

## Water Management

The submitted detail must preserve the existing wall's water-management system
and provide the flashing/counterflashing required by the selected roof covering,
wall assembly and approved design. Use the covering-specific R905 provisions
and manufacturer instructions; apply applicable wall-cladding/WRB provisions
for the actual assembly.

Do not import R507 deck-ledger flashing dimensions, a universal caulking bead,
or one-quarter-inch spacing exception into a patio-roof connection without an
approved source that expressly covers the condition.

## Review Checklist

| Check | Result basis |
|---|---|
| Attached vs freestanding configuration is unambiguous | Plans and structural load path |
| BF105.1 reactions and uplift/lateral demands are documented | Applicable calculations/design |
| Existing structural substrate is identified and verified | Existing-condition documentation |
| Connector/fastener/anchor capacity and installation are sourced | Applicable code, accepted design, listed product or fully provenanced local plan |
| Edge distance, embedment, spacing and corrosion compatibility are addressed | Same approved source |
| Water-resistive barrier and roof/wall flashing path is complete | Applicable R905/wall assembly/manufacturer design |
| Veneer or finish is not treated as structural support | Actual load-path verification |
| Freestanding option, if used, is structurally independent | Foundation and lateral-system design |

Use **FLAG** when the existing substrate, loads, connection source or water-
management detail is missing. Use **FAIL** only when demonstrated project facts
contradict an applicable identified requirement.
