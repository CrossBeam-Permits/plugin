# Waterproofing — Kitchen & Bathroom Remodel

> Reference file for the California Kitchen & Bath Remodel skill.
> Sources: CPC (Title 24, Part 5), CRC (Title 24, Part 2.5), 2025 Edition

---

## Shower Pan / Receptor Requirements

### Site-Built Shower Receptors (CPC 408.8)
- Shower receptors built on-site shall be **watertight** and constructed from approved-type dense, nonabsorbent, and noncorrosive materials
- Each receptor shall be adequately reinforced
- Shall be provided with an approved flanged floor drain designed to make a watertight joint on the floor
- Shall have smooth, impervious, and durable surfaces

### Dam / Curb / Threshold (CPC 408.6)
- Finished dam, curb, or threshold shall be not less than **1 inch lower** than the sides and back of the receptor
- Dam/threshold depth: minimum **2 inches**, maximum **9 inches** (measured from top of dam to top of drain)
- Threshold width: sufficient to accommodate a minimum **22-inch** door
- Shower doors shall open to maintain not less than a **22-inch** unobstructed opening for egress
- Where no threshold is present, the floor space within the same room is considered a wet location

### Finished Floor Slope
- Receptor floor shall slope uniformly from sides toward drain:
  - Minimum: **1/8 inch per foot** (10.4 mm/m)
  - Maximum: **1/2 inch per foot** (41.6 mm/m)

## Shower Lining Requirements (CPC 408.8)

### Lining Materials
Shower receptors shall have the subfloor and rough side of walls lined to a height of **not less than 3 inches above the top of the finished dam or threshold** with one of the following:
- Sheet plastic (PVC or CPE)
- Sheet lead (min 4 lb/ft2, coated with asphalt paint)
- Sheet copper (min 12 oz/ft2 or No. 24 B&S Gauge)
- Other durable and watertight materials

### Permanent Seat/Seating Area
- Showers with a built-in permanent seat shall have lining materials extending **not less than 3 inches above horizontal surfaces** of the seat or seating area

### Lining Installation
- Lining materials shall be pitched **1/4 inch per foot** to weep holes in the subdrain of a smooth and solidly formed subbase
- Lining shall extend upward on rough jambs of the shower opening to not less than 3 inches above the seat surface, top of finished dam/threshold
- Lining shall extend outward over the top of the rough threshold and be turned over and fastened on the outside face

### Approved Lining Materials
| Material | Standard | Requirements | Code Section |
|---|---|---|---|
| PVC sheets | ASTM D4551 | Joined by solvent cementing per manufacturer | CPC 408.8.1 |
| CPE sheets | ASTM D4068 | Joined per manufacturer instructions | CPC 408.8.2 |
| Sheet lead | -- | Min 4 lb/ft2, asphalt paint coating, insulated from conductors, joined by burning | CPC 408.8.3 |
| Sheet copper | ASTM B152 | Min 12 oz/ft2, insulated from conductors, joined by brazing or soldering | CPC 408.8.4 |
| Built-up (non-metallic) | -- | Min 3 layers 15 lb asphalt felt, hot-mopped, corners reinforced with webbing (50 lb/ft2 tensile) | CPC 408.8 |

### Subdrain Requirements
- Approved subdrain shall be installed with shower subpan or lining
- Subdrain shall set flush with subbase
- Subdrain shall have clamping ring or device for tight connection between lining and drain
- Subdrain shall have **weep holes** into the waste line
- Weep holes in subdrain clamping ring shall be protected from clogging

### Lining Fastening
- Linings shall be properly recessed and fastened to approved backing so as not to occupy wall-covering space
- Shall NOT be nailed or perforated at a point less than **1 inch above** the finished dam or threshold

## Shower Pan Testing (CPC 408.8.5)

- Shower receptors shall be tested for watertightness by filling with water to a depth of **not less than 2 inches** for **not less than 15 minutes**
- Where no threshold is present, a 2-inch barrier shall be temporarily constructed for testing
- Test plug shall be placed so both upper and under sides of the subpan are subjected to the test at the drain clamp point

---

## Tile Substrate Requirements

### Prohibited Materials
- **Drywall green board (moisture-resistant gypsum) shall NOT be used as a tile substrate in shower enclosures**

### Approved Substrate Materials
- Cement board (Durock, Hardiebacker, or other approved materials)
- Shall be installed over a water barrier with approved fasteners

---

## Bathtub/Shower Wall Finish

### Wall Surface Requirements
- Bathtub and shower walls with shower head installed shall be finished with a **smooth, non-absorbent surface to 72 inches above the standing surface**
- This applies to all walls within the shower spray zone

---

## Shower Compartment Size (CPC 408.7)

- Minimum finished interior: **1024 square inches** (approximately 32" x 32")
- Must be capable of encompassing a **30-inch circle**
- Measured at height equal to top of threshold, tangent to centerline
- Area and dimensions maintained to **70 inches** above the shower drain outlet
- No protrusions allowed other than: fixture valves, showerheads, soap dishes, shelves, and safety grab bars or rails
- **Exception:** shower receptor with overall dimensions not less than 30 inches wide and 60 inches long

---

## Safety Glazing (CRC 308.4.5)

- Safety glazing required for all glazing:
  - Located less than **60 inches** above the standing or walking surface
  - Located within **60 inches** (measured horizontally in a straight line) from the water's edge of the tub or shower

---

## Nailing Flange Requirements

- Each shower receptor shall be provided with a nailing flange, either integral or field installed per manufacturer instructions (CPC 408.6)
- Flange shall be watertight and extend vertically **not less than 1 inch** above the top of the sides of the receptor
