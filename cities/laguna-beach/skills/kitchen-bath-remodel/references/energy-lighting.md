# Energy Code & Lighting — Kitchen & Bathroom Remodel

> Reference file for the California Kitchen & Bath Remodel skill.
> Sources: Energy Code (Title 24, Part 6), 2025 Edition

---

## Lighting Efficacy Requirements (Section 150.0(k))

### General Rule for Alterations
- Altered lighting systems shall meet the requirements of Section 150.0(k) (Energy Code 150.2(b)1K)
- All altered luminaires shall meet the luminaire efficacy requirements
- Luminaires must be **JA8 compliant** (Joint Appendix 8) -- high efficacy

### High Efficacy Standard
- Luminaires shall meet minimum efficacy of **45 lumens per watt**
- LED luminaires and lamps meeting JA8 requirements are the standard compliance path
- Screw-base sockets in existing ceiling-recessed luminaires: removal not required if new JA8-compliant trim kits or lamps designed for recessed downlights are installed (Energy Code 150.2(b)1K)

### Kitchen Lighting
- Residential kitchen lighting must meet energy efficiency standards per Section 150.0(k)
- All kitchen luminaires shall be high efficacy OR controlled by a vacancy sensor
- Example compliant fixtures:
  - LED recessed cans (max 40W relamping wattage, IC and AT rated, meeting JA8/Section 150.0(k))
  - Fluorescent recessed cans with pin-based compact fluorescent lamp (electronic ballast, min efficacy, IC, AT rated)
  - LED or fluorescent under-cabinet lights

### Bathroom Lighting
- Bathroom lighting shall be **high efficacy luminaires** OR controlled by a **vacancy (occupancy) sensor** certified to comply with Section 150.0(k)
- Vacancy sensor requirements:
  - Must be **manual on, auto off** device approved by the Energy Commission
  - Automatic on/off devices are NOT approved
  - Devices with an override switch position are NOT approved
- High efficacy lighting, incandescent lighting, and exhaust fans must be **switched separately**

## Recessed Luminaire Requirements

### Insulated Ceilings
- Recessed luminaires in insulated ceilings shall be:
  - **IC rated** (Insulation Contact / zero clearance)
  - **AT rated** (Air Tight)
  - Sealed with gasket between ceiling and housing

### Rated Separations
- For occupancies with horizontal (floor/ceiling assembly) rated separation:
  - Recessed fixtures shall be protected to the rating of the separation (typically 1 hour)
  - OR be listed for the required protection
  - This typically applies to condominium construction with units above or below

### Attic Insulation Coverage (Triggered by Alteration)
- In Climate Zones 1-4 and 8-16, recessed downlight luminaires in the ceiling shall be covered with insulation to the same depth as the rest of the ceiling (Energy Code 150.2(b)1Jiii)
- Luminaires not rated for insulation contact must be replaced or retrofitted with a fireproof cover

## Water Heating (Alterations — Section 150.2(b)1H)

### Altered or Replacement Water Heaters
When a water heater is altered or replaced as part of the remodel, it must comply with one of the following:
1. A natural gas or propane water-heating system
2. A single heat pump water heater -- storage tank not located outdoors, placed on incompressible rigid insulated surface (min R-10), installed with communication interface (Section 110.12(a) or ANSI/CTA-2045-B port)
3. A single heat pump water heater meeting NEEA Advanced Water Heater Specification Tier 3 or higher
4. If existing is electric resistance: a consumer electric water heater
5. A system determined by the Executive Director to use no more energy than option 1 (or option 4 if no gas connected)

### Pipe Insulation
- For newly installed and existing accessible piping, insulation requirements of Section 150.0(j)1 shall be met (Energy Code 150.2(b)1Hi)
- All domestic hot water pipes buried below grade must be insulated and installed in a non-crushable casing or sleeve

### Distribution System
- For recirculation distribution systems serving individual dwelling units: only **demand recirculation systems** with manual on/off control are permitted (Energy Code 150.2(b)1Hii; RA4.4.9)

## Fenestration (Windows) in Kitchen/Bath

### Replacement Windows
- New fenestration products replacing existing products of the same area shall meet U-factor and SHGC requirements of Section 150.1(c)3A and 150.1(c)4 (Energy Code 150.2(b)1B)
- **Exception:** Replacement of vertical fenestration no greater than 75 square feet with U-factor no greater than 0.40 and SHGC no greater than 0.35 in Climate Zones 2, 4, and 6-15

### Added Windows
- Alterations that add vertical fenestration shall meet total fenestration area, west-facing fenestration area, U-factor, and SHGC requirements (Energy Code 150.2(b)1A)

## Insulation Triggered by Alteration

### Ceiling/Attic Insulation (Energy Code 150.2(b)1J)
If the remodel includes a vented attic space and triggers insulation upgrades:
- Climate Zones 1-4, 6, 8-16: weighted U-factor of 0.020 or R-49 insulation at ceiling level
- Air sealing of accessible ceiling plane areas in Climate Zones 2, 4, 8-16
- Attic ventilation shall comply with CRC Section R806

**Exceptions** (any one exempts):
- Dwelling units with at least R-38 existing insulation at ceiling level
- Dwelling units where alteration would disturb asbestos
- Dwelling units with knob and tube wiring in attic
- Insufficient attic space for required R-value (fill entire accessible space)
