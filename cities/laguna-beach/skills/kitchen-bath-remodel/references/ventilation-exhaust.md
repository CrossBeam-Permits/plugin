# Ventilation & Exhaust — Kitchen & Bathroom Remodel

> Reference file for the California Kitchen & Bath Remodel skill.
> Sources: CMC (Title 24, Part 4), Energy Code (Title 24, Part 6), CALGreen (Title 24, Part 11), ASHRAE 62.2, 2025 Edition

---

## Bathroom Exhaust Ventilation

### Minimum Exhaust Rates
- **Bathroom exhaust fan required** regardless of the presence of a window (CMC 405.1; ASHRAE 62.2)
- Minimum exhaust rate: **50 CFM intermittent** or **20 CFM continuous** (ASHRAE 62.2 Section 6.4; Energy Code 150.0(o)1G)
- Maximum sound rating: **3 Sone** at rated airflow (ASHRAE 62.2 Section 7.1)
- A "bathroom" for this purpose is a room that contains bathing facilities (bathtub, shower, or tub/shower combination)

### Fan Requirements
- Bathroom exhaust fans shall be **ENERGY STAR compliant** (CALGreen 4.506)
- Exhaust must vent to outdoors through an approved duct -- recirculating fans are NOT code-compliant
- Back draft damper required (CMC 504.3)
- Unless the bathroom exhaust fan is part of the Whole House Ventilation System, fans must be controlled by a **humidistat** (CALGreen 4.506)
  - Humidistat shall be readily accessible
  - Humidistat controls shall be capable of adjustment between 50% to 80% relative humidity
  - Compliance may be a separate humidistat fan control (switch) or a control integral to the exhaust fan

### Duct Requirements
- Metal, smooth interior surface duct required -- flex duct is not an approved duct material for exhaust (CMC 504.3)
- Exhaust duct termination: minimum **3 feet** from any opening into the building (windows, doors, skylights, attic vents) or property line (CMC 502.2.1)
- All environmental air ducts must terminate minimum 3 feet from property lines or building openings (CMC 502.2.1)

### Existing Fan Exception
- Alterations that do not replace or relocate the existing fan, or when ceiling finishes are not removed and there is no access available for installation, may continue to use existing exhaust fans provided they vent to outside air (CALGreen 4.506)
- Bathrooms without an existing fan must install a compliant fan at the time of renovation (CALGreen 4.506)

## Kitchen Exhaust Ventilation

### Range Hood / Exhaust Requirements
- A **ducted residential exhaust hood** is required over the cooking appliance (CMC 504.3; Energy Code 150.0(o)1G)
- Kitchen local exhaust ventilation: minimum **100 CFM** meeting ASHRAE 62.2 requirements (Energy Code 150.0(o)1G)
- Maximum sound rating: **3 Sone** at 100 CFM (ASHRAE 62.2)
- **Recirculation-type hoods are not allowed by code** -- must be ducted to outdoors

### Duct Requirements
- Metal, smooth interior surface duct required on vent hood or down-draft exhaust vent (CMC 504.3)
- Flex duct is NOT an approved duct material for kitchen exhaust
- Back draft damper required (CMC 504.3)
- Exhaust duct termination: minimum 3 feet from any opening into the building or property line (CMC 502.2.1)

### Clearances
- Floor-mounted and built-in household cooking tops require at least **30 inches**
  vertical clearance to combustible material or metal cabinets, unless one of
  the permitted 24-inch protection/hood configurations or the applicable
  listed-appliance installation path is used (CMC 919.4.2, 919.5.2)
- Minimum **36 inches** clear passageway between counter fronts and appliances and counters and walls (CBC 1208.1)

### Equipment Documentation
- Provide installation instructions for all listed equipment to field inspector at time of inspection (CMC 303.1)

### Altered Kitchen Exhaust (Energy Code)
- If the kitchen local ventilation fan is altered or replaced (Energy Code 150.2(b)1Mii):
  - Dwellings previously required to comply with Section 150.0(o)1G must meet or exceed applicable airflow or capture efficiency requirements
  - Dwellings previously required to install a vented kitchen range hood must install a replacement fan that meets or exceeds the airflow required by previous permit, or 100 CFM, whichever is greater
  - Dwellings not previously required to have a kitchen exhaust system are not required to comply

## Whole-House Ventilation (Triggered by Remodel)

### When Required
- Entirely new or complete replacement ventilation systems must comply with all applicable requirements in Energy Code Section 150.0(o) (Section 150.2(b)1L)
- Altered ventilation system components shall comply with Section 150.0(o) as applicable (Section 150.2(b)1M)
- For alterations, replacement ventilation fans must be rated for airflow and sound per ASHRAE 62.2 Sections 7.1 and 7.3
