# Plumbing Fixtures — Kitchen & Bathroom Remodel

> Reference file for the California Kitchen & Bath Remodel skill.
> Sources: CPC (Title 24, Part 5), CALGreen (Title 24, Part 11), 2025 Edition

---

## Water Closets (Toilets)

### Maximum Flush Volume
- **1.28 gallons per flush** maximum effective flush volume (CPC 411.2; CALGreen 4.303.1.1)
- Tank-type water closets shall be certified to the US EPA WaterSense Specification for Tank-Type Toilets (CPC 411.2.4; CALGreen 4.303.1.1)
- Dual flush water closets: composite average of two reduced flushes and one full flush shall not exceed 1.28 gpf (CPC 411.2.1)

### Clearance Requirements
- **15 inches minimum** from center of fixture to side wall or obstruction (CPC 402.5)
- **30 inches minimum** center-to-center to a similar fixture (CPC 402.5)
- **24 inches minimum** clear space in front of fixture (CPC 402.5)
- Maximum **3 water closets** on a 3-inch waste line; 4th requires a 4-inch line

### Installation
- Water closet bowls for public use shall be elongated type (CPC 411.1)
- Seats shall be smooth, non-absorbent material (CPC 411.3)
- Floor-outlet or floor-mounted fixtures shall be rigidly secured to drainage connection and floor (CPC 402.3)
- Closet rings (closet flanges) shall be approximately 7 inches in diameter (CPC 402.6.1)

## Lavatories (Bathroom Sinks)

### Maximum Flow Rates
- **Residential lavatory faucets:** 1.2 gpm maximum at 60 psi (CPC 407.2.2; CALGreen 4.303.1.4.1)
- Minimum flow rate: 0.8 gpm at 20 psi (CPC 407.2.2; CALGreen 4.303.1.4.1)
- **Public/common area lavatory faucets:** 0.5 gpm maximum at 60 psi (CPC 407.2.3; CALGreen 4.303.1.4.2)
- **Metering faucets:** 0.2 gallons maximum per cycle (CPC 407.2.4; CALGreen 4.303.1.4.3)

### Standards
- Lavatories shall comply with ASME A112.19.1/CSA B45.2, ASME A112.19.2/CSA B45.1, or equivalent (CPC 407.1)
- Waste outlet and fixture tailpiece: not less than 1-1/4 inches in diameter (CPC 407.5)
- Hot water for public lavatories limited to 120 degrees F maximum (CPC 407.3)

## Kitchen Sinks

### Maximum Flow Rates
- **Kitchen faucets:** 1.8 gpm maximum at 60 psi (CPC 420.2.2; CALGreen 4.303.1.4.4)
- Kitchen faucets may temporarily increase flow to 2.2 gpm at 60 psi but must default back to 1.8 gpm (CPC 420.2.2; CALGreen 4.303.1.4.4)
- Where complying faucets are unavailable, aerators or other means may be used to achieve reduction (CALGreen 4.303.1.4.4 Note)

### Standards
- Sinks shall comply with ASME A112.19.1/CSA B45.2, ASME A112.19.2/CSA B45.1, or equivalent (CPC 420.1)
- Waste outlet and fixture tailpiece: not less than 1-1/2 inches in diameter (CPC 420.4)
- Waste outlets shall be provided with an approved strainer (CPC 420.4)

## Showerheads

### Maximum Flow Rates
- **Single showerhead:** 1.8 gpm maximum at 80 psi (CPC 408.3; CALGreen 4.303.1.3.1)
- Showerheads shall be certified to US EPA WaterSense Specification for Showerheads (CALGreen 4.303.1.3.1)
- **Multiple showerheads serving one shower:** combined flow rate of all showerheads/outlets controlled by a single valve shall not exceed 1.8 gpm at 80 psi, OR the shower shall be designed to allow only one outlet to operate at a time (CPC 408.3.2; CALGreen 4.303.1.3.2)
- **Body sprays:** 1.8 gpm maximum at 80 psi (CPC 408.3)
- A hand-held shower is considered a showerhead (CPC 408.3 Note)

### Anti-Scald Protection
- Showers and tub-shower combinations shall have pressure balance, thermostatic, or combination valves (CPC 408.4)
- Maximum water temperature from showerhead: **120 degrees F** (CPC 408.4.2)
- Valves shall comply with ASSE 1016/ASME A112.1016/CSA B125.16 (CPC 408.4)

## Bathtubs and Whirlpool Bathtubs

### Standards
- Bathtubs shall comply with ASME A112.19.1/CSA B45.2, ASME A112.19.2/CSA B45.1, or equivalent (CPC 409.1)
- Whirlpool bathtubs shall comply with ASME A112.19.7/CSA B45.10 (CPC 409.1)
- Waste outlet: not less than 1-1/2 inches in diameter (CPC 409.2)

### Temperature Limiting
- Maximum hot water temperature from bathtub filler: **120 degrees F** (CPC 409.4)
- Regulated by ASSE 1070/ASME A112.1070/CSA B125.70 limiting device or ASSE 1084 water heater (CPC 409.4)
- Water heater thermostat alone is NOT an acceptable control for meeting this provision

### Whirlpool/Spa Requirements
- Readily accessible access panel required (CPC 409.6)
- Circulation pump shall be located above the crown weir of the trap (CPC 409.6)
- Pump and circulation piping shall be self-draining (CPC 409.6)
- Suction fittings shall comply with ASME A112.19.7/CSA B45.10 (CPC 409.6.1)
- Accessible disconnects and GFCI protection required for pump, aerator, and heater (CEC 210.8)

## General Fixture Installation

### Hot Water Valve Position
- Left-hand control shall control hot water when facing the fixture (CPC 417.5)
- Single-handle mixing valves shall have hot water corresponding to markings (CPC 417.5)

### Access Panels
- Fixtures with concealed slip joint connections require a **12 inch x 12 inch minimum** access panel (CPC 402.10)
- Exception: weld-on (glue) waste and overflow and p-trap eliminates the access door requirement

### Backflow Protection
- Water supply to bathtub and whirlpool filler valves shall be protected by air gap or per CPC 417.0 (CPC 409.5)
- Handheld showers shall have backflow prevention per ASME A112.18.3 or ASSE 1014 (CPC 417.3)
