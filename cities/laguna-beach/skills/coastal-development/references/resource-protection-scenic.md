# Scenic and Visual Resource Policies (PRC §30251-30253)

## Core Visual Protection Rule — §30251

The scenic and visual qualities of coastal areas shall be considered and protected as a resource of public importance. Permitted development shall:

1. **Protect views** to and along the ocean and scenic coastal areas
2. **Minimize alteration** of natural land forms
3. Be **visually compatible** with the character of surrounding areas
4. Where feasible, **restore and enhance** visual quality in visually degraded areas

**Highly scenic areas** (designated in the California Coastline Preservation and Recreation Plan or by local government): new development shall be **subordinate to the character of its setting**.

## Development Location — §30250

**(a)** New residential, commercial, or industrial development shall be located:
- Within, contiguous with, or in close proximity to existing developed areas able to accommodate it, OR
- In other areas with adequate public services where it will not have significant adverse effects on coastal resources

Land divisions outside existing developed areas: permitted only where 50% of usable parcels are developed and created parcels are not smaller than average surrounding parcels.

**(b)** New hazardous industrial development: locate away from existing developed areas where feasible.

**(c)** Visitor-serving facilities that can't be in developed areas: locate in existing isolated developments or selected visitor attraction points.

## Access Enhancement — §30252

New development should maintain and enhance public access by:
1. Facilitating transit service provision/extension
2. Providing commercial facilities within/adjoining residential development
3. Providing nonautomobile circulation within development
4. Providing adequate parking or substitute public transportation
5. Assuring transit potential for high-intensity uses
6. Correlating development amount with park acquisition/recreation plans

## Hazard Minimization and Community Protection — §30253

New development shall:
**(a)** Minimize risks to life and property in areas of high geologic, flood, and fire hazard
**(b)** Assure stability and structural integrity; neither create nor contribute to erosion, geologic instability, or destruction — **shall not require construction of protective devices that would substantially alter natural landforms along bluffs and cliffs**
**(c)** Be consistent with air pollution requirements
**(d)** Minimize energy consumption and VMT
**(e)** Where appropriate, protect special communities and neighborhoods that are popular visitor destination points

## Application to Development Review

For any project in a scenic area or visible from public vantage points:
1. Will the project protect or obstruct views to/along the ocean?
2. Does it minimize alteration of natural landforms?
3. Is it visually compatible with surrounding development character?
4. In highly scenic areas: is the development subordinate to its setting?
5. Does the project location comply with §30250 (within/near developed areas)?
6. Does it minimize hazard risks and avoid requiring shoreline protective devices that alter bluffs/cliffs?
