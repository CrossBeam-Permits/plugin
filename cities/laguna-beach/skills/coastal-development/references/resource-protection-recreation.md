# Recreation and Visitor-Serving Policies (PRC §30220-30224)

## Core Rules

### Protection of Water-Oriented Recreation (§30220)
Coastal areas suited for water-oriented recreational activities that cannot readily be provided at inland water areas shall be protected for such uses.

### Oceanfront Land for Recreation (§30221)
Oceanfront land suitable for recreational use shall be protected for recreational use and development **unless** present and foreseeable future demand for public or commercial recreational activities is already adequately provided for in the area.

### Land Use Priority (§30222)
Private lands suitable for **visitor-serving commercial recreational facilities** designed to enhance public opportunities for coastal recreation shall have priority over:
- Private residential development
- General industrial development
- General commercial development

But **NOT** priority over:
- Agriculture
- Coastal-dependent industry

### Aquaculture (§30222.5)
Oceanfront land suitable for coastal dependent aquaculture shall be protected for that use, with priority for aquaculture proposals — except over other coastal dependent developments.

### Upland Support Areas (§30223)
Upland areas necessary to support coastal recreational uses shall be reserved for such uses, where feasible.

### Boating Facilities (§30224)
Increased recreational boating use encouraged by:
- Developing dry storage areas
- Increasing public launching facilities
- Providing additional berthing in existing harbors
- Limiting non-water-dependent uses that congest access corridors
- Providing harbors of refuge
- New boating facilities in natural harbors and protected water areas

## Application to Development Review

When a project could displace or affect recreational/visitor-serving uses:
1. Is the site suited for water-oriented recreation that can't be provided inland?
2. Would the project displace visitor-serving commercial recreation with a lower-priority use?
3. Are upland areas needed for recreational support being preserved?
4. For harbor/marina projects: does it increase or decrease boating access?
