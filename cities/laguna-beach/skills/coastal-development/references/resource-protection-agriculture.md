# Agricultural Land Policies (PRC §30241-30244)

## Prime Agricultural Land — §30241

The maximum amount of prime agricultural land shall be maintained in agricultural production through:

**(a)** Establishing stable urban-rural boundaries, including buffer areas
**(b)** Limiting agricultural land conversions around urban periphery to lands where:
- Agricultural viability is already severely limited by urban conflicts, OR
- Conversion would complete a logical, viable neighborhood and establish a stable urban limit
**(c)** Permitting conversion of agricultural land surrounded by urban uses where consistent with §30250
**(d)** Developing non-agricultural lands first before converting agricultural lands
**(e)** Assuring public services and non-agricultural development don't impair agricultural viability
**(f)** Assuring land divisions and adjacent development don't diminish prime agricultural land productivity

## Agricultural Viability Analysis — §30241.5

When viability of existing agricultural uses is at issue in an LCP or amendment:

The determination of "viability" must include an **economic feasibility evaluation** with at minimum:
1. Gross revenue analysis from agricultural products grown in the area for the preceding 5 years
2. Operational expenses analysis (excluding land cost) for the preceding 5 years

The evaluation must be submitted by the local government with its LCP or amendment. If lacking staff expertise, may use a consultant selected jointly with the Commission's executive director.

## Other Agricultural Lands — §30242

All other lands suitable for agricultural use shall not be converted to non-agricultural uses **unless:**
1. Continued or renewed agricultural use is not feasible, OR
2. Conversion would preserve prime agricultural land or concentrate development per §30250

Any permitted conversion shall be compatible with continued agricultural use on surrounding lands.

## Soils and Timberlands — §30243

Long-term productivity of soils and timberlands shall be protected. Conversions of coastal commercial timberlands to other uses limited to necessary timber processing and related facilities.

## Archaeological Resources — §30244

Where development would adversely impact archaeological or paleontological resources (as identified by the State Historic Preservation Officer), reasonable mitigation measures shall be required.

## Application to Development Review

For projects on or near agricultural land:
1. Is the site prime agricultural land? If yes, conversion faces the strict §30241 standards.
2. Has agricultural viability been demonstrated or disproved with a proper economic analysis?
3. If non-prime: is continued agricultural use feasible? Would conversion preserve prime land or concentrate development?
4. Is the project compatible with continued agriculture on surrounding lands?
5. Are archaeological/paleontological resources affected? Mitigation required if so.
