# Appealable Development Areas

## Commission Appeal Jurisdiction — §30603(a)

After LCP certification, the following categories of local CDP action are appealable to the Commission:

### 1. Between Sea and First Public Road
Development approved by the local government between the sea and the **first public road paralleling the sea**, or within **300 feet** of the inland extent of any beach or the mean high tide of the sea where there is no beach — **whichever is the greater distance**.

### 2. Tidelands, Wetlands, Streams, and Coastal Bluffs
Other approved development on **tidelands, submerged lands, or public-trust lands**; within **100 feet** of any wetland, estuary, or stream; or within **300 feet** of the top of the seaward face of a coastal bluff.

### 3. Sensitive Coastal Resource Areas
Other approved development in a **sensitive coastal resource area**. This category does not apply to a residential development project.

### 4. Coastal-County Non-Principal Uses
Development approved by a coastal county that is not designated as the principal permitted use under the certified zoning ordinance or zoning map. This category does not apply to a residential development project, and "coastal county" excludes a local government that is both a city and county.

### 5. Major Public Works and Energy
Any **major public works project** or **major energy facility**, regardless of location within the coastal zone.

The Commission's adopted maps depict the §30603(a)(1)-(2) boundaries but do not replace the additional §30603(a)(3)-(5) tests. (14 CCR §13576)

## Commission Permit Jurisdiction — §30519(b), §30601

After LCP certification, the delegation of development-review authority does not apply to development proposed or undertaken on tidelands, submerged lands, or public-trust lands, whether filled or unfilled. PRC §30519(b) also states specific port and state-university/college rules and an exception for certain port or harbor districts whose development plans are included in the certified LCP.

Before LCP certification, PRC §30601 requires a Commission CDP, where applicable in addition to a local permit, for its enumerated shoreline, public-trust/wetland/stream/bluff, major-public-works, and major-energy categories.

## Consolidated Permit Applications — §30601.3

If a project requires a CDP from both a certified-LCP local government and the Commission, the Commission may process a consolidated application only with the applicant's, local government's, and Commission's consent and without substantially impairing public participation. Chapter 3 is the standard of review, with the LCP used as guidance.

## Jurisdiction Boundary Maps — 14 CCR §13576-13577

In conjunction with final LCP certification or delegation under PRC §30600.5, whichever occurs first, the Commission adopts official maps showing its permit and appeal jurisdiction and files later revisions with the affected jurisdiction. Those maps portray the mapped statutory boundaries but do not replace the additional appeal categories.

Use the boundary-determination criteria in §13577 for a precise parcel determination. The map itself warns that it may not show every place where Commission permit or appeal jurisdiction is retained.

## Practical Determination

For any project in a certified-LCP local government, whether a city or county:

1. **Check the Commission-adopted official jurisdiction map** — is the site in the mapped permit or appeal jurisdiction area?
2. If a local approval is between the sea and first public road or within 300 ft of a beach → **appealable under §30603(a)(1)**.
3. If another local approval is within 100 ft of a wetland/estuary/stream or within 300 ft of a coastal-bluff top → **appealable under §30603(a)(2)**.
4. If on tidelands/submerged lands → **Commission original jurisdiction** (not just appeal)
5. If it is a coastal-county approval, test the non-principal-use and residential-project qualifications in §30603(a)(4); do not assume every county action is appealable.
6. If a major public works or energy project → **appealable under §30603(a)(5)**.
