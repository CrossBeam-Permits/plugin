# CDP Appeals to the Coastal Commission

## What Can Be Appealed — §30603

After LCP certification, the following local CDP decisions may be appealed to the Commission:

### Appeal Categories — §30603(a)
1. Development approved between the sea and the first public road paralleling the sea, or within 300 feet of the inland extent of a beach or mean high tideline where there is no beach, whichever distance is greater.
2. Other approved development on tidelands, submerged lands, or public-trust lands; within 100 feet of a wetland, estuary, or stream; or within 300 feet of the top of the seaward face of a coastal bluff.
3. Other approved development in a sensitive coastal resource area. This category does not apply to a residential development project.
4. Development approved by a coastal county that is not designated as the principal permitted use under the certified zoning ordinance or map. This category excludes residential development projects and a local government that is both a city and county.
5. Any development constituting a major public-works project or major energy facility.

Use the certified LCP's mapped appeal jurisdiction to apply these statutory categories. Do not substitute a generic "beach/recreation area" or "all county actions" test for §30603(a).

## Who May Appeal — §30625(a)

- The **applicant**
- Any **aggrieved person**
- Any **two members** of the Commission

An applicant or aggrieved person must exhaust local appeals before filing with the Commission. Apply 14 CCR §13111(a) together with §13573(a) to determine when local appeals are exhausted or unavailable. The separate two-Commission-member appellant route is addressed by §13573(b).

## Grounds of Appeal — §30603(b), 14 CCR §13113

- For an approval appealable under §30603(a), the grounds are limited to alleged nonconformity with the certified LCP or the Coastal Act's public-access policies.
- For denial of a permit for a §30603(a)(5) major public-works project or major energy facility, the grounds are limited to an allegation that the development conforms with the certified LCP and the public-access policies.

## Appeal Timeline

| Step | Deadline | Citation |
|---|---|---|
| Appeal must be filed | **10 working days** from Commission receipt of local final action notice | PRC §30603(c) |
| Commission hearing on appeal | Set no later than **49 working days** after filing | PRC §30621(a) |
| Commission decision after hearing | Within **21 days** | PRC §30622 |
| Stay of local action | **Automatic** upon appeal filing | PRC §30623 |

If Commission takes no action within the time limits, the local government decision becomes final (unless applicant waives the deadline). (§30625(a))

## Appeal Process — 14 CCR §13110-13120

### Filing — §13111
An appeal must be submitted on Commission appeal form with:
- Name and address of appellant
- Description of development
- Date of local government action
- Specific grounds within PRC §30603(b), citing the relevant Coastal Act or LCP provisions

### Substantial Issue Determination — §13115
At the next meeting after filing (or as soon as practical), the executive director recommends whether the appeal raises a **substantial issue** within the meaning of §30625(b).

If the Commission finds **no substantial issue:** appeal is denied and local decision stands.

If the Commission finds **substantial issue:** proceeds to de novo hearing.

### De Novo Hearing — §13114
For an applicant or aggrieved-person appeal, de novo review follows the required local exhaustion and a final local decision. Do not impose that exhaustion prerequisite on the separate two-Commission-member route in 14 CCR §13573(b). If the Commission reaches de novo review, it may approve, modify, or deny the proposed development under PRC §30625(a).

### Standard of Review — §13119
- The appeal standard applies PRC §30604(b): conformity with the certified LCP.
- For development between the nearest public road and the sea or the shoreline of any body of water in the coastal zone, §30604(c) additionally requires a specific finding of conformity with Chapter 3's public-access and public-recreation policies.

## Key Rules

- An appeal **automatically stays** the local decision — no development may proceed until the appeal is resolved. (§30623)
- The Commission can hear the appeal on a **substantial issue** threshold before doing full de novo review — this is an efficiency mechanism.
- For development between the nearest public road and the sea or the shoreline of any body of water in the coastal zone, the §30604(c) public-access/public-recreation finding supplements rather than replaces certified-LCP conformity under §30604(b).

## Application to Development Review

1. Is the project in an appealable area? Check the geographic triggers in §30603(a).
2. If appealed: is there a substantial issue? (Commission's threshold determination)
3. If substantial issue is found, apply §30604(b) and, where its geography applies, the additional §30604(c) finding.
4. Applicant should be aware that an appeal automatically stays the project.
