# Key Definitions

## Critical Definitions

### Development — §30106
"Development" means, on land, in or under water, the placement or erection of any solid material or structure; discharge or disposal of any dredged material or of any gaseous, liquid, solid, or thermal waste; grading, removing, dredging, mining, or extraction of any materials; change in the density or intensity of use of land, including subdivision; change in the intensity of use of water, or of access thereto; construction, reconstruction, demolition, or alteration in the size of any structure, including any facility of any private, public, or municipal utility; and the removal or harvesting of major vegetation other than for agricultural purposes, kelp harvesting, and timber operations.

> This is an extremely broad definition. Almost any physical change to property in the Coastal Zone constitutes "development" requiring a CDP (unless exempt under §30610).

### Environmentally Sensitive Habitat Area (ESHA) — §30107.5
Any area in which plant or animal life or their habitats are either **rare or especially valuable** because of their special nature or role in an ecosystem and which could be easily disturbed or degraded by human activities and developments.

> ESHA triggers the most restrictive Coastal Act policy (§30240): only resource-dependent uses allowed within ESHA, and adjacent development must prevent degradation.

### Feasible — §30108
Capable of being accomplished in a successful manner within a reasonable period of time, taking into account economic, environmental, social, and technological factors.

### Local Coastal Program (LCP) — §30108.6
A local government's:
- (a) Land use plans
- (b) Zoning ordinances
- (c) Zoning district maps
- (d) Within sensitive coastal resource areas, other implementing actions

...which, when taken together, meet the requirements of and implement the Coastal Act at the local level.

### Coastal Development Permit (CDP) — §30101.5
A permit for any development within the coastal zone that is required pursuant to §30600(a).

### Coastal Zone — §30103
That land and water area of the State of California from the Oregon border to the border of Mexico, extending seaward to the state's outer limit of jurisdiction (3 nautical miles), and extending inland generally 1,000 yards but varying by location — as precisely described in Part 2 of the California Coastal Zone Conservation Act of 1972.

> In urban areas the coastal zone may be very narrow; in rural areas it can extend several miles inland.

## Other Important Definitions

| Term | Definition | Section |
|---|---|---|
| Coastal-dependent development | Requires a site on or adjacent to the sea to function at all | §30101 |
| Coastal-related development | Dependent on a coastal-dependent development or use | §30101.3 |
| Cumulatively | Incremental effects reviewed with past, current, and probable future projects | §30105.5 |
| Energy facility | Any facility for processing, producing, generating, storing, transmitting, or recovering energy | §30107 |
| Fill | Earth or material placed in a submerged area, including pilings | §30108.2 |
| Land use plan | Portions of general plan indicating kinds, location, intensity of land uses + resource protection policies | §30108.5 |
| Implementing actions | Ordinances, regulations, programs implementing certified LCP or Coastal Act | §30108.4 |
| Aggrieved person | Any person who, in person or through a representative, appeared at a hearing in connection with the decision or who informed the decision-making body in writing before the decision | 14 CCR §13006 |
| Emergency | Sudden unexpected occurrence demanding immediate action to prevent/mitigate loss of or damage to life, health, property, or essential public services | 14 CCR §13009 |
| Working day | Any day which is not a Saturday, Sunday, or state holiday | 14 CCR §13012.1 |
