# CDP Application and Review Process

## Who Issues the CDP?

### Pre-LCP Certification (§30600(b)-(c))
- **Local government option:** May establish procedures to issue CDPs within its coastal-zone jurisdiction under §30600(b).
- **Delegated local authority:** Section 30600.5 can delegate permit issuance after certification of a land use plan but before the complete LCP takes effect. Under §30600.5(c), the local government—or Commission on appeal—uses conformity with the certified land use plan; local action is appealable under §30602 through §30600.5(d).
- **Commission route:** If the local government does not exercise §30600(b), or the development is outside that subdivision, §30600(c) routes the CDP to the Commission unless another local route applies. Section 30601 can require a Commission CDP in addition to a local permit for its enumerated categories.

### Post-LCP Transfer (§30519, §30600(d))
- After the applicable LCP is certified and affected implementing actions are effective, the local government issues CDPs under the certified LCP
- Commission retains appeal jurisdiction over specified actions and permit jurisdiction described in §30519(b); check the adopted jurisdiction map and the statutory categories.

## Required Findings — §30604

### Nondelegated Pre-LCP Route — §30604(a)
A CDP shall be issued if the issuing agency (or Commission on appeal) finds:
1. The proposed development **conforms with Chapter 3** (resource protection policies, §30200+)
2. The permitted development **will not prejudice** the local government's ability to prepare a conforming LCP

A denial on ground (2) must include a specific finding stating the basis.

### Interim Delegated Route — §30600.5(c)-(d)
The local government, or the Commission on appeal under §30602, finds conformity with the certified land use plan. Do not substitute the §30604(a) Chapter 3/prejudice findings for this delegated route.

### Post-LCP Certification — §30604(b)
A CDP shall be issued if the proposed development **conforms with the certified LCP**.

### Shoreline Development — §30604(c)
Every CDP for development between the nearest public road and the sea or the shoreline of any body of water located within the coastal zone must include a **specific finding of conformity with public access and public recreation policies** of Chapter 3.

### Affordable Housing — §30604(f)-(g)
For low/moderate income housing: the issuing agency may NOT require measures that reduce residential densities below what the applicant seeks (if within zoning + density bonus) **unless** substantial evidence shows the density can't be accommodated consistent with Chapter 3 or the LCP.

### Environmental Justice — §30604(h)
When acting on a CDP, the issuing agency or Commission may consider environmental justice or equitable distribution of environmental benefits.

## Timeline

| Step | Deadline | Citation |
|---|---|---|
| Application completeness determination | Varies by local/Commission rules | 14 CCR §13056 |
| Commission hearing after application or appeal filed | Set no later than **49 working days** after filing | PRC §30621(a) |
| Commission action after hearing | Within **21 days** | PRC §30622 |
| Appeal stay of local action | Automatic upon filing | PRC §30623 |
| Commencement under a Commission-approved permit | Generally 2 years after the Commission vote unless a different limit is imposed; 5 years for qualifying 100% affordable housing | 14 CCR §13156(g); extension route: §13169 |

## Administrative Permits — §30624

The executive director (or delegated local official) may issue CDPs **without full hearing** for:
- Emergency developments
- Improvements to existing structures
- Single-family dwellings
- 4 or fewer dwelling units within incorporated areas (no demolition required)
- Other development not exceeding $100,000 (not a division of land)
- Principal permitted uses in areas with certified LUP

**Safeguard:** 1/3 of Commission members can block an administrative permit at the next meeting, requiring full hearing process.

## Minor Development Waiver — §30624.9

After LCP certification, local government may waive public hearing for "minor development" (consistent with LCP, no discretionary approvals needed, no adverse effect) if:
1. Notice sent to all persons who would normally be notified
2. No request for public hearing received within **15 working days**

## Commission Findings — 14 CCR §13096

When voting to approve, modify, or deny a CDP, the Commission shall adopt findings. The findings must address compliance with Chapter 3 policies (or certified LCP) and any conditions imposed.

## Permit Conditions

The Commission (or local government) may impose reasonable conditions on CDPs to:
- Ensure compliance with the applicable §30604 standard (Chapter 3, the certified LCP, and any additional §30604(c) finding)
- Mitigate adverse environmental effects
- Provide public access where required
- Address sea level rise

Conditions must be related to the impacts of the proposed development.
