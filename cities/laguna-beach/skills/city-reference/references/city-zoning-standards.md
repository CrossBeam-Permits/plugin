## 1. Residential Zone Districts

Laguna Beach uses a traditional Euclidean zoning structure. The primary residential zones are:

| Zone | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| **R-1** | Single-family residential — the dominant residential zone citywide |
| **R-2** | Two-family residential (duplex) |
| **R-3** | Multi-family residential |
| **Three Arch Bay (TAB)** | Special overlay zone (Chapter 25.44) with unique standards |

The vast majority of Laguna Beach residential parcels are zoned R-1. Nearly the entire city is within the **Coastal Zone**, which adds a Coastal Development Permit (CDP) requirement to most development.

> **NOTE**: The R-1 development standards and SFR parking/access provisions have now been scraped for the new single-family-residential overlay. See `../../single-family-residential/references/zoning-r1-development-standards.md` and `../../single-family-residential/references/parking-access-standards.md` for the current R-1 numbers. R-2/R-3 and special districts still require parcel-specific verification.

## 2. Development Standards — R-1 Zone

The following standards are drawn from the scraped R-1, general-yard/open-space, parking, and ADU provisions. Verify non-R-1 districts, specific plans, and parcel-specific overlays against the current Municipal Code before issuing a final zoning finding.

### Height
- R-1 height is slope-based and measured from finished or natural grade, whichever is more restrictive (LBMC §25.10.008(D)).
- 0-15% slope: **25 ft** maximum.
- 16% / 17% / 18% / 19% slope: **26 / 27 / 28 / 29 ft** maximum.
- 20%+ slope: **30 ft** maximum.
- Through lots measure from the higher curb or street centerline.
- Additional rear-lot/through-lot caps and §25.50.004(D) front/rear-yard height setback rules may further reduce the envelope.
- Section 25.10.010 may impose special height standards in specified areas.

### Setbacks
- R-1 front yard: **20 ft** minimum.
- R-1 rear yard: **20 ft** minimum; DRB may increase to 25 ft where surrounding development patterns have larger rear yards.
- R-1 side yards: each side yard at least **10% of average lot width**, minimum 3 ft; if average lot width is 40 ft or greater, no side yard may be less than 4 ft.
- Bluff setback: min 25 ft from top of oceanfront bluff or applicable stringline, whichever is more restrictive.
- Watercourse setback: min 25 ft from centerline of watercourse (per ADU chapter)
- Additional setbacks may apply per certified Local Coastal Program (LCP) for hazardous areas, environmentally sensitive areas, wetlands, public views, public trails and accessways (LBMC 25.50.004(D))

### Lot Coverage / Floor Area
- R-1 site coverage includes habitable enclosed building space plus covered off-street parking.
- Lots <=4,000 sf: **44%** maximum site coverage.
- Lots >=8,000 sf: **35%** maximum site coverage.
- Lots between 4,000 and 8,000 sf: `35 + ((8,000 - A) * 9 / 4,000)` percent, where A is lot area in sf.
- Oceanfront lots calculate A using the building setback lines or oceanfront bluff, whichever is more restrictive (LBMC §25.50.020).
- Floor area calculations and open space calculations are required submittal items for most projects (per zoning plan check table)

### Open Space
- Open space calculations required for new multi-family (2+ units), additions >50%, major remodels, and new SFDs.
- R-1 landscaped open space for new homes/major remodels: 15% for lots <=2,500 sf; 35% for lots >=14,500 sf; formula between those lot sizes is `15% + [((A - 2,500) * 20) / 12,000]`.

## 3. ADU/JADU Zoning Standards (LBMC Chapter 25.17)

### Where Allowed
- Any lot zoned for single-family or multi-family residential use with an existing or proposed dwelling
- Lots zoned non-residential but developed with legal nonconforming single/multi-family dwellings

### Number of Units
- One ADU (attached, detached, or converted) + one JADU per single-family lot
- Multifamily lots: either converted or detached ADUs (not both)
  - Converted: min 1 ADU, up to 25% of existing MF units (from non-livable space)
  - Detached: max 2 ADUs on multifamily lots

### Size Limits

| Type | Minimum | Maximum |
|------|---------|---------|
| ADU (studio/1BR, detached) | 150 sf | 850 sf |
| ADU (2+ BR, detached) | 150 sf | 1,000 sf |
| ADU (ADA compliant) | 150 sf | 1,000 sf |
| ADU (studio/1BR, attached) | 150 sf | 850 sf or 50% of primary dwelling, whichever is less |
| ADU (2+ BR, attached) | 150 sf | 1,000 sf or 50% of primary dwelling, whichever is less |
| JADU | 150 sf | 500 sf |

Guaranteed allowance (overrides zone standards): one attached/detached ADU up to 850 sf (or 1,000 sf for 2+ BR) with 4 ft min side and rear setbacks.

### ADU Height Limits

| Scenario | Max Height |
|----------|-----------|
| Detached ADU on SFR/MF lot (standard) | **16 ft** |
| Detached ADU within 0.5 mi of major transit stop or high-quality transit corridor | **18 ft** (+2 ft to match primary roof pitch) |
| Detached ADU on multifamily multistory lot | **18 ft** |
| Attached ADU | **25 ft** or zone height limit, whichever is lower |

### ADU Setbacks

| Standard | Dimension |
|----------|-----------|
| Interior side and rear (attached/detached) | **4 ft min** |
| Bluff setback | **25 ft** from top of oceanfront bluff |
| Watercourse | **25 ft** from centerline |
| Space between detached ADU and other structures | Min required by Building & Fire Code |

Nonconforming structures converted to ADUs may maintain existing nonconforming setbacks; expansions beyond existing footprint must comply.

### ADU Parking

| Requirement | Details |
|-------------|---------|
| General | 1 space per ADU |
| May be tandem | Yes, on driveway |
| Min from side property line | 3 ft |
| Not within required turnaround area | Correct |
| Replacement parking (Coastal Zone) | 1:1 ratio when garage demolished for ADU; may be uncovered/tandem |

**Parking exemptions** (no parking required) — any of:
1. JADU
2. Converted ADU from existing habitable space or accessory structure
3. Deed-restricted affordable ADU
4. ADA-compliant ADU
5. Within 0.5 mi walking distance of public transit or Downtown Specific Plan area
6. Within 100 ft of free on-street parking with adequate supply
7. On California/City historic register property
8. Within locked-gate community
9. On-street permits required but not offered to ADU occupant
10. Car-share vehicle within one block
11. Proposed as part of existing primary structure
12. Submitted with new primary dwelling application

### ADU Design Standards
- Exterior materials, finish, color, and roof design/pitch of ADU >12 ft height must match primary dwelling (if visible from public/private roadways, excluding alleys)
- Outdoor lighting: hooded, fully shielded, aimed downward; no light trespass/glare
- Outdoor living space (porches, decks, patios): max 20% of ADU floor area; must comply with height/setback standards
- Elevated decks >3 ft above grade require design review
- Roof decks prohibited on ADUs

### ADU Processing
- Ministerial review (no public hearing) if meets objective standards
- 60-day shot clock from completed application (SFR/MF with existing dwelling)
- Coastal Zone: CDP required before building permit (except converting existing livable space, which is exempt)
- JADU: exempt from CDP under certified LCP

## 5. Design Review — Triggers and Process (LBMC 25.05.040)

Urgency Ordinance No. 1732 inverted the former default. Exterior work is not automatically discretionary. Match the project against the closed lists: 16 Design Review categories in (B)(1), 28 Zone Clearance categories in (B)(2), and 17 Administrative Design Review categories in (B)(3).

Key residential matches:

| Project type | Current route |
|---|---|
| Major remodel | Design Review, (B)(1)(a) |
| New building | Design Review, (B)(1)(b), except qualifying ADUs and Chapter 25.13 primary dwellings |
| Improvement over 15 ft | Design Review, (B)(1)(c) |
| ESA development | Design Review, (B)(1)(e), absent the enacted peer-reviewed professional determination |
| Grading over 20 CY outside footprint | Design Review, (B)(1)(f) |
| Retaining wall over 5 ft exposed height | Design Review, (B)(1)(g) |
| Pool/spa over 3 ft above adjacent legal grade | Design Review, (B)(1)(j), except portable spas no more than 600 gallons |
| Non-major demolition, alteration, or addition | Zone Clearance, (B)(2)(a), unless another trigger applies |
| Improvement no more than 15 ft | Zone Clearance, (B)(2)(b), unless another trigger applies |
| In-kind repair or interior-only modification | Zone Clearance, (B)(2)(c)-(d) |
| Retaining wall no more than 5 ft | Zone Clearance, (B)(2)(j) |
| Deck/patio no more than 3 ft above grade | Zone Clearance, (B)(2)(m) |
| Pool/spa no more than 3 ft above grade or portable spa no more than 600 gallons | Zone Clearance, (B)(2)(t) |
| Qualifying new primary dwelling under Chapter 25.13 | Zone Clearance, (B)(2)(z) |
| Deck/patio over 3 ft above grade | Administrative Design Review, (B)(3)(a)(iii) |
| Mechanical equipment failing §7.25.130(C) | Administrative Design Review, (B)(3)(a)(v) |
| White/light or potentially glaring reroof | Administrative Design Review, (B)(3)(a)(ix) |
| Roof geometry change | Administrative Design Review, (B)(3)(a)(x) |
| Skylight without an operable automatic night shade | Administrative Design Review, (B)(3)(a)(xii) |

See `../../residential-remodel/references/city-design-review-tiers.md` for the current complete routing index. A CDP may still be independently required. If no closed-list match can be established, return `needs_source` instead of applying the former exterior-work default.

### Design Review Authority

| Project Type | Approval Authority |
|-------------|-------------------|
| Single-family or two-family development | **Design Review Board (DRB)** |
| Multi-family (3+ units) | **Planning Commission** |
| Non-residential development | **Planning Commission** |
| Downtown Specific Plan area projects | **Planning Commission** |
| Public works / CIP projects | **Planning Commission** |
| Administrative design review | **Director of Community Development** |

### Design Review Criteria (11 criteria in LBMC 25.05.040(H))

Access; Design Articulation; Environmental Context; General Plan Compliance; Historic Preservation; Landscaping; Lighting and Glare; Neighborhood Compatibility; Pedestrian Orientation; Privacy; and View Equity.

> **Critical**: The property development standards represent the **maximum allowable building envelope**. Actual development after design review criteria are applied will typically be **less** than the maximum. A project with no variances has no presumptive "entitlement" to the maximum envelope.

### Staking Requirements
- All new construction, major remodels, additions subject to DRB: staking poles + ribbons representing full extent of proposed structure (including decks and eaves)
- Must be certified by licensed surveyor or civil engineer
- In place **21 calendar days** before first DRB hearing
- Administrative design review: **14 calendar days** before hearing
- Staking deposit required; removal within 20 days after final decision

### Early Neighbor Communication (Required)
- Contact all property owners within **300 ft** before finalizing design
- Prepared and mailed by professional listing service
- Must be completed before hearing date is scheduled

### Hearing Limits
- Residential projects usually limited to **2 hearings** maximum
- Third hearing only if DRB finds good faith effort and community interest
- Up to 2 continuances allowed where no substantive discussion
- Concept review limited to 1 hearing (counts toward total)

### Biological Report Requirement
- Per §25.05.040(C)(3), a biological report prepared by a qualified wildlife biologist or botanist is required for any proposal to clear native vegetation on undeveloped parcels located within or adjacent to areas designated high or very high biological value on the city's biological values maps, and any other areas containing environmentally sensitive habitat resources (except annual weed abatement and approved fuel modification programs)

### Reapplication Waiting Period
- Per §25.05.040(L), after denial of a project, no application for a project on the same parcel or building site may be filed or accepted for 2 months

### Design Review Lapse and Extensions
- Per §25.05.040(K), design review approval lapses 2 years after effective date unless building permit issued and construction begun
- Design review becomes effective 14 calendar days after decision unless appealed (§25.05.040(K)(1))
- One 2-year extension + one final 1-year extension available through administrative design review process
- Extensions require findings that (a) zoning codes haven't materially changed and (b) neighborhood character hasn't materially changed (§25.05.040(K)(4))

## 6. Fence, Wall, and Hedge Height Regulations

Fence/wall/hedge heights are regulated in LBMC 25.50.012, with specific standards by zone and yard location. The full height tables require verification in the unscraped chapters, but key provisions from cross-references:

### From Block Wall Detail and Design Review Provisions:
- Fences, walls, and hedges that comply with zoning regulations are **exempt** from design review (except permanent chain link, woven metal, and vinyl not simulating natural material)
- Hedges that comply with §25.50.012 take Zone Clearance under §25.05.040(B)(2)(l); noncompliant height requires parcel- and scope-specific routing rather than the repealed `(B)(1)(t)` citation
- Decorative fence posts, brick or stone columns may extend up to **12 inches above maximum allowable height** within the front yard (administrative design review)
- No railing/guard/screen where wall + attachment exceeds **6 ft** above lowest adjacent grade (block wall standard detail)
- Pedestrian entry features outside ROW that do not impact line of sight and conform to 25.50.012(B)(6): **administrative design review**
- Trash storage in side yard: screened from public view by wall/fence at least **4 ft** height (exempt from DR)

### Permit Triggers for Block Walls
- CMU block wall **>30 inches** from adjacent grade: **building permit required**
- CMU block wall **any height within 3 ft of property line**: **building permit required**
- Wood or metal fence: OTC review (survey waiver available if within 3 ft of property line)
- **No wall/fence within 36 inches of fire hydrant**
- **No wall/fence/foundation within 12 inches of water meter**

## 7. Accessory Structures

### Temporary On-Grade Accessory Structures (Exempt from Design Review)
- Play sets, swing sets, unenclosed recreation equipment, storage sheds
- Max **120 sq ft**
- Max height **12 ft** above adjacent ground elevation
- Must meet minimum setbacks of underlying zone
- If setbacks not met: administrative design review required

### Permanent Accessory Structures
- Subject to underlying zone setback and height standards
- Pool/spa structures route by height: over 3 ft above adjacent legal grade → Design Review `(B)(1)(j)`; no more than 3 ft, or a portable spa no more than 600 gallons → Zone Clearance `(B)(2)(t)`
- Pool/spa equipment (and other mechanical equipment): routed by the **Section 7.25.130(C) six-part objective test**, as amended by the 2026 Efficiency Ordinance (adopted 2026-07-21, **effective 2026-08-20**). The six parts: (1) ground-mounted or wall-mounted at ground level; (2) not within required front or side yard setbacks; (3) 5-ft clearance from all property lines; (4) manufacturer's spec sheet with decibel rating, max 60 dB day / 50 dB night at the property line, **two sound attenuation techniques** used and maintained per manufacturer's specifications for the life of the equipment; (5) completed city-provided sound-level form computed per ARI Standard 275 (1984, as amended); (6) does not obstruct a **3-ft path of travel** around any structure.
  - **Meets all six:** approved **without design review** (Section 7.25.130(C)) — the Zone Clearance path at Section 25.05.040(B)(2)(v), per Section 25.05.045.
  - **Fails any part:** **administrative design review** (Section 7.25.130(D); Section 25.05.040(B)(3)(a)(v)).
  - Zone Clearance is unavailable in an environmentally sensitive area absent a peer-reviewed licensed-professional determination; those projects go to design review under Section 25.05.040(B)(1).
  - This governs equipment only. It does not change design review for the pool or spa structure itself.

## 8. Three Arch Bay Overlay Zone (LBMC Chapter 25.44)

Three Arch Bay (TAB) is a private gated community with its own overlay zone and development standards.

### Governing Code
- LBMC Chapter 25.44 (not yet scraped — refer directly to Municipal Code)
- Standards focus on: mass, scale, architectural character, privacy, ocean view preservation, bluff protection

### Development Standards (25.44.050)
- Yard setbacks (TAB-specific)
- Permitted projections
- Building site coverage
- Floor area
- Building stringline
- Building height standards
- Parking: **4 spaces required** (2 may be in front yard or abutting street; if on TABA property, TABA consent required)
- Roof pitch: minimum **3:12** and roof decks prohibited; Design Review authority may approve not less than **2.5:12** to preserve identified neighborhood view corridors (§25.44.050(M))
- Access and improvement standards
- Fences, walls, and hedges
- Design review
- Signs

### Current Additional Design Review Findings (25.44.060)

Ord. 1732 §37 amended §25.44.060. When Design Review independently applies, the reviewing authority must make two TAB findings in addition to §25.05.040(I): small-scale character sensitive to topography/view preservation, and a landscape/hardscape finding addressing drainage, erosion, wind, shade, sound, dust, glare, Resolution 89-104, and surrounding ocean views. Former privacy/window-placement, solar-visibility, general-compatibility, and wall/fence findings were deleted. The 3:12 roof-pitch/no-roof-deck provision moved to new §25.44.050(M), with a not-less-than-2.5:12 view-corridor exception.

### Special Process
1. Applicant submits to City; City sends digital plans to TABA and TABCSD
2. City performs zoning plan check and grants zoning clearance
3. Applicant obtains TABA comment letter to the extent permitted by law and TABCSD approval where the current process requires them
4. Project follows the current §25.05.040 closed-list route; do not assume every project receives a DRB hearing
5. If Design Review applies, City uses §25.05.040(H)/(I) plus the two current §25.44.060 findings
6. TABA review is independent; comment letters treated as public correspondence
7. Improvements on TABA or TABCSD property require their consent before building permit

### Soils and Geology (25.44.070)
- Geotechnical report required for development on coastal or vacant lots in TAB
- Reports must comply with Title 14

## 9. Nonconforming Structures

### From ADU Chapter (25.17)
- Conversions of legal nonconforming structures to ADUs/JADUs shall NOT be required to correct nonconforming zoning conditions
- Must meet current building, electrical, and fire code standards
- Nonconformities in existing primary structures shall NOT be required to be corrected as condition of ADU/JADU approval
- ADUs created in the same location as a legally nonconforming structure may maintain nonconforming setbacks
- Expansions beyond the existing footprint must comply with current setbacks

### From Design Review Chapter (25.05.040)
- Non-major additions route under the current closed lists; a nonconformity does not resurrect the retired 10%-50% project-tier row
- Variance requests (25.05.025) available for nonconforming conditions — heard by Board of Adjustment or Planning Commission
- If variance is approved and the nonconforming structure is removed during construction, a revocation hearing must be held

### Major Remodel and Nonconformities
- Major remodel routes to Design Review under §25.05.040(B)(1)(a) and is classified as a new building under §25.56.011
- Two calculation methods: coastal zone appealable areas (more restrictive) vs. coastal zone non-appealable areas; walls counted on each floor; each building or structure tested independently (Major Remodel Interpretation Quick Guide, Updated June 2026)
- When a principal structure undergoes a major remodel, all associated accessory structures and improvements "shall lose their legal, nonconforming status" (same handout)
- ADU construction is EXEMPT from major remodel calculation

## 10. Appeals Process (LBMC 25.05.070)

### Who May Appeal
- The applicant
- Any property owner within **300 ft** of the subject property
- Any City Council member (via request for review — no statement of reasons or fee required)
- City Manager (when city is applicant or aggrieved owner)

### Filing Requirements
- Written statement of grounds for appeal
- Filed with City Clerk within **14 calendar days** of decision
- Appeal fee required (set by City Council resolution)
- Multiple appeals may be filed for design review decisions (fee prorated)

### Automatic Appeal
- Any decision where a commission/board member has ownership, leasehold, or consulting interest in the property

### Hearing Timeline
- City Council hearing set **14-60 calendar days** after receipt of appeal
- Public notice per Section 25.05.065 (mailed 10+ calendar days before)

### Standard of Review

**For non-design-review decisions:**
- De novo review (City Council exercises independent judgment)
- Not limited to stated grounds of appeal
- Not limited to evidence presented below
- Presumption that lower decision was reasonable — appellant has burden of proof

**For design review decisions with 4+ affirmative votes:**
- NOT de novo — limited to stated grounds and evidence below
- Council reviews for (1) proper procedure and (2) substantial evidence
- More deferential standard

**For design review decisions without 4+ affirmative votes:**
- De novo but limited to stated grounds and evidence below
- Council exercises independent judgment on the existing record

### Council Actions
- 3+ affirmative votes required to reverse a decision
- Council may uphold and add more restrictive conditions (height reduction, setback increase, view/privacy protections) without finding error
- Council may remand revised plans back to approval authority

## 11. Variance Process (LBMC 25.05.025)

### Required Findings (all four must be made)
1. Special circumstances (size, shape, topography, location) cause strict application to deprive property of privileges enjoyed by others in same zone
2. Variance necessary for preservation of substantial property right possessed by others under like conditions
3. Will not be detrimental to public health, safety, convenience, welfare, or injurious to nearby property
4. Will not be contrary to zoning ordinance objectives or General Plan

### Approval Authority
- **Board of Adjustment** (most variances)
- **Planning Commission** when:
  - In conjunction with matter already before Commission
  - Related to subdivisions (lot dimension, area, yard, access)
  - For indirect access
  - For construction on lots with substandard-width street access

### Expiration
- Variance lapses **2 years** after effective date unless building permit issued and construction begun
- One 2-year extension + one final 1-year extension available

## 12. Conditional Use Permits (LBMC 25.05.030)

### Required Findings
1. Site adequate in size and topography for proposed use
2. Adequate street access (width and pavement type)
3. No substantial adverse effect on abutting property
4. Consistent with General Plan objectives and policies
5. Conditions necessary to protect public health, safety, welfare

### Approval: Planning Commission (public hearing)
### Expiration: 2 years (same extension rules as variances)
### If CUP and variance both needed: CUP reviewed and approved first

## 13. Coastal Development Permits (LBMC Chapter 25.07)

Nearly all of Laguna Beach is in the Coastal Zone. Key provisions:

- **CDP required** for any development not exempt under 25.07.008
- Categories: de minimis, minor development, standard development
- Fees: $125 (de minimis), $952 (minor/standard)
- ADUs converting existing livable space: exempt from CDP
- JADUs: exempt from CDP
- Appealable to California Coastal Commission in appealable areas
- City cannot consider private view impacts when evaluating CDP for ADUs — only public views

### CDP Exemption Categories (§25.07.008(A))

The following development types are exempt from CDP requirements:

1. **Improvements to existing SFDs** (§25.07.008(A)(1)) — includes structures on the same lot (garages, pools, fences, sheds, landscaping), **except**:
   - ADUs other than conversions of existing habitable space within the primary dwelling (§25.07.008(A)(1)(a))
   - Structures/improvements on a beach, wetland, stream, seaward of mean high tide, within 50 ft of coastal bluff edge, in ESHA, or in areas designated highly scenic per certified LUP (§25.07.008(A)(1)(b))
   - Structures/improvements between the sea and first public road paralleling the sea with >10% floor area increase (aggregate) or >10% height increase, or new detached structures (§25.07.008(A)(1)(c))
   - Water well or septic expansion/construction (§25.07.008(A)(1)(d))
   - Significant alteration of land forms or vegetation removal on beach, wetland, sand dune, within 50 ft of bluff edge/stream, or in ESHA (§25.07.008(A)(1)(g))

2. **Improvements to existing non-SFD structures** (§25.07.008(A)(2)) — exempt unless involving change in intensity of use, condo/co-op conversion, or significant land form alteration in sensitive areas

3. **Repair and maintenance** (§25.07.008(A)(3)) — of existing structures, provided no addition/enlargement/expansion; exceptions for shoreline protective works involving substantial foundation alteration, >20% material replacement, or mechanized equipment in sensitive areas

4. **Utility connections** (§25.07.008(A)(4)) — installation/replacement of utility connections for development approved under this chapter; city may condition for coastal resource mitigation

5. **Replacement of structures destroyed by disaster** (§25.07.008(A)(5)) — same use, same location, floor area/height/bulk not exceeding destroyed structure by more than 10%

6. **Temporary events on public beaches** (§25.07.008(A)(6)) — exempt unless the event meets ALL of: (a) held Memorial Day–Labor Day, (b) occupies sandy beach area, and (c) involves admission/seating charge where none currently exists

## 14. Project Submittal Requirements (by Review Level)

From the city's Zoning Plan Check Table:

### Ministerial (No Public Hearing — Certain Conditions)
- ADU (attached/detached): App forms, title sheet, survey, site plan, staking plan (if req.), roof plan, floor plans, elevations, cross sections, floor area calcs, open space calcs, grading/drainage (if req.), landscape (if req.)
- ADU (conversion): App forms, title sheet, site plan, floor plans, elevations, cross sections, floor area calcs
- Addition <10% (no upper level, <=15 ft): App forms, title sheet, survey, site plan, staking (if req.), roof plan, floor plans, elevations, cross sections, floor area calcs, landscape (if req.)
- Exterior materials: App forms, title sheet, elevations
- Retaining walls: App forms, title sheet, survey, site plan, staking (if out of grade), cross sections, grading (if req.)

### Administrative Design Review (Director Hearing)
- Deck addition: App forms, title sheet, survey, site plan, roof plan, elevations, cross sections, floor area calcs, open space calcs
- Pedestrian entry feature: App forms, title sheet, site plan (if in req. setback), elevations, lighting (if req.)
- Skylights: App forms, title sheet, roof plan, elevations (if req.)
- Pre-fab spa: App forms, title sheet, survey, site plan, cross sections

### Design Review Board Hearing (if nonconformities)
- Addition 10-50%: App forms, title sheet, survey, site plan, roof plan, floor plans, elevations, cross sections, floor area calcs, open space calcs, landscape (if req.), lighting

### Full Design Review Board or Planning Commission Hearing
- Addition (aggregate): All plan sheets + grading/drainage, landscape, lighting
- Addition >50% / major remodel / new SFD: All plan sheets + grading/drainage, landscape, lighting, fire dept. access, site and ROW improvements
- New multi-family (2+ units): All plan sheets + open space calcs, grading/drainage, landscape, lighting, fire dept. access, site and ROW improvements
- Upper level additions or >15 ft above grade: App forms, title sheet, survey, site plan, roof plan, floor plans, elevations, cross sections, floor area calcs, grading/drainage (if req.), landscape, lighting
