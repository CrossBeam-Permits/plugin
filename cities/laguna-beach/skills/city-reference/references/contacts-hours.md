### ⚠️ Planning phone discrepancy — UNRESOLVED, do not silently pick one

The City's own site is internally inconsistent about the Planning Division's
direct line. The **displayed text is always "(949) 497-0713"**, but the
underlying click-to-call link differs by page:

- Ten pages display 0713 but link to **tel:9494970714**: `boards-committees-commissions`, `planning-zoning`, `planning-zoning/applications-handouts`, `planning-zoning/land-use-plans`, `planning-zoning/public-notices`, `planning-zoning/current-projects`, `planning/public-hearing-notices`, `planning/current-projects/accessory-dwelling-units`, `planning/current-projects/infill-housing-regulations-1364`, `views-and-trees`. [source: https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/government/departments/community-development/planning-zoning, as of 2026-06-18]
- Two pages display 0713 and link to **tel:9494970713** (consistent): the Community Development FAQ page and the Community Development Updates page. [source: https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/government/departments/community-development/frequently-asked-questions, as of 2026-06-18]
- The department landing page displays 0713 with an **empty `mailto:` link** (a broken link, not a phone number). [source: https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/government/departments/community-development, as of 2026-07-16]

**How to answer:** give the published number, **(949) 497-0713**, and if the
caller can't get through, offer the Community Development main line
**(949) 497-0712**. Do not volunteer 0714 as "the real number" — it has never
been published as text anywhere on the site. The City has not yet confirmed
which is correct.

### ⚠️ Code Enforcement phone discrepancy — a mislink, NOT a typo

The Code Enforcement landing page displays "(949) 497-0301" but links to
**tel:9494970701**. Every other page that lists Code Enforcement (What Do We
Enforce, Code Enforcement FAQ, the CD FAQ, Boards & Committees, CD Updates)
displays and links **(949) 497-0301** consistently, and the Code Enforcement FAQ
repeats 0301 in body text as the contact number.

**0701 is not a typo — it is the Laguna Beach Police non-emergency dispatch
line**, published on the Police Department's own Dispatch page at 505 Forest
Avenue. So the link goes somewhere real, just not to Code Enforcement.
[source: https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/government/departments/police/support-services/dispatch, as of 2026-08-09]

**How to answer:** for a code-enforcement matter give **(949) 497-0301**. Give
**(949) 497-0701** only when the resident describes something needing police or
fire attention that is not an emergency — that is what the City publishes it for.
Emergencies are **911**.
[source: https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/government/departments/community-development/code-enforcement, as of 2026-06-18]
[source: https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/government/departments/community-development/code-enforcement/frequently-asked-questions, as of 2026-06-18]

## Hours

### Permit counter (Community Development)

- Open **Monday through Thursday, 7:30 a.m. – 2:00 p.m.** [source: https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/government/departments/community-development, as of 2026-07-16]
- **Hard last sign-in is 12:45 p.m.**, so all business can be completed by the 2:00 p.m. close. This note appears on every Community Development page. [source: https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/government/departments/community-development, as of 2026-07-16]
- Staff begin monitoring the check-in queue roughly **1½ hours before closing**. Once staff determine the queue has reached capacity during that final 1½ hours, **no additional sign-ins are accepted** for the day. Residents are told to arrive early or book an appointment. [source: https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/government/departments/community-development, as of 2026-07-16]

#### ⚠️ Counter-hours discrepancy — two different closing times, both live

Two City pages published two different sets of permit-counter hours, both live on
2026-08-10:

- **Community Development landing page**, "Important Notice – Permit Center Counter Hours": **Monday through Thursday, 7:30 a.m. – 2:00 p.m.**, with the hard 12:45 p.m. last sign-in and the queue-capacity cut-off described above. No Friday hours. [source: https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/government/departments/community-development, as of 2026-07-16]
- **Building Applications and Forms page**, sidebar "Counter Hours": **Monday – Thursday, 7:30 a.m. – 3:00 p.m.**, plus **alternating Fridays 7:30 a.m. – 12:00 p.m.** No queue cut-off mentioned. [source: https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/government/departments/community-development/building-permits/applications-forms, as of 2026-08-10]

An hour of closing time and a whole Friday separate the two. **How to answer:**
never assert either set as fact. Give the earlier close (2:00 p.m.) as the safe
planning assumption, say the City publishes a later one elsewhere, and steer the
resident to book the appointment
(https://lbwaitlist.cxmflow.com/OABS/Appointment/Index/2c052fc7-571f-4b76-9790-7e91f103c408)
or call (949) 497-0712 to confirm before driving over. Alternating Fridays are
published on the City calendar: https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/live-here/city-calendar

### Building plan review (over-the-counter)

This is one of the two homes for over-the-counter (OTC) hours. The other is
`../SKILL.md` §"Permit Processing Tracks". No permit-type skill
states these hours.

- **By appointment: Monday through Thursday, 7:30 a.m. – 11:00 a.m.** Half-hour intervals, 30-minute maximum, first-come first-served. [source: Building Plan Review Services handout, City of Laguna Beach Building Division, edition 06/08/2026 — the City source document (not included in this plugin)]
- **Walk-in, no appointment: Monday through Thursday, 7:30 a.m. – 2:00 p.m., last check-in 12:45 p.m.** Without an appointment, wait times can be lengthy. [source: same handout, 06/08/2026]
- Both tracks are subject to plan-reviewer availability, and cover first review, rechecks and revisions. An applicant who employs a design professional must have that design professional present. [source: same handout, 06/08/2026]
- Book at https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/government/departments/community-development/building-permits/appointment-for-counter-plan-review

#### ⚠️ The City's appointment web page still publishes 2021 hours

The appointment page says **"walk-ins are served in the afternoon"** with no appointment
necessary. That is the **04/19/2021** edition of the handout, which set walk-in OTC at
**12:30 – 4:00 p.m.** The City replaced it on **06/08/2026** with the 7:30 a.m. – 2:00 p.m.
window and the 12:45 p.m. last check-in. The web page has not been updated. [source: https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/government/departments/community-development/building-permits/appointment-for-counter-plan-review, as of 2026-06-18]

**How to answer:** give the handout hours — a resident who arrives at 8 a.m. expecting an
afternoon-only walk-in window loses a morning. Say the City's page still shows the old
afternoon wording, and tell them to call (949) 497-0715 to confirm before driving over.

## Fraud warning worth repeating to applicants

- A phishing scam is targeting applicants with pending Planning applications across many local jurisdictions. **The City will never email you asking you to wire money.** City online payments happen only by credit card, or in person by cash or check. Official City email always comes from an `@lagunabeachcity.net` address. If in doubt, call the City using a phone number published on the official website. [source: https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/government/departments/community-development, as of 2026-07-16]
