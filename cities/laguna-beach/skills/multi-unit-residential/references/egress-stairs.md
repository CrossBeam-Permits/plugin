## Egress Stairs — Stairway Geometry, Handrails, Guards, and Exit Enclosures

### Which Code Governs Which Stair

Multi-unit residential projects often have two sets of stair rules on the same site:

| Stair Location | Governing Code | Key Differences |
|---------------|---------------|-----------------|
| **Within individual townhouse units** (R-3, ≤3 stories, own exit) | CRC R318.7 | 7-3/4" max rise, 10" min run, 36" width, handrail one side only |
| **Common exit stairs** serving corridors, R-2 buildings | CBC 1011 | 7" max rise, 11" min run, 44" width (OL >50), handrails both sides |
| **Within dwelling units in R-2** (apartments) | CBC 1011 with R-2 dwelling unit exceptions | 7-3/4" max rise, 10" min run per Exception 3; handrail one side per 1011.11 Exception 1 |

For a 35-unit townhome project: each unit's internal stairs follow CRC R318.7 geometry; any common exterior or corridor exit stairway serving multiple units follows CBC 1011.

### Stair Width

#### CBC (Common Stairs) — 1011.2
- **44 inches minimum** clear width for stairways serving an occupant load of more than 50 (CBC 1011.2)
- **36 inches minimum** for stairways serving an occupant load of 50 or fewer (CBC 1011.2, Exception 1)
- Capacity calculation per CBC 1005.1 may require greater width for high-occupancy buildings
- Accessible means of egress stairways must also comply with CBC 1009.3

#### CRC (Within Townhouse Units) — R318.7.1
- **36 inches minimum** clear width above the permitted handrail height and below the required headroom (CRC R318.7.1)
- **31-1/2 inches minimum** at and below handrail height where a handrail is installed on one side (CRC R318.7.1)
- **27 inches minimum** at and below handrail height where handrails are installed on both sides (CRC R318.7.1)

### Rise and Run Geometry

| Dimension | CBC 1011.5.2 (Common Stairs) | CBC Exception 3 (R-2 Dwelling Units) | CRC R318.7.5 (Townhouse Units) |
|-----------|------------------------------|--------------------------------------|--------------------------------|
| **Maximum riser height** | 7 inches | 7-3/4 inches | 7-3/4 inches |
| **Minimum riser height** | 4 inches | 4 inches | — |
| **Minimum tread depth** | 11 inches | 10 inches | 10 inches |
| **Winder at walkline** | 11 inches | 10 inches | 10 inches |
| **Winder minimum** | 10 inches | 6 inches | 6 inches |

**Uniformity**: The tolerance between the largest and smallest riser height or tread depth shall not exceed **3/8 inch** in any flight of stairs (CBC 1011.5.4; CRC R318.7.5.1, R318.7.5.2).

**Nosing projection**: Where tread depth is less than 11 inches, a nosing projection of not less than **3/4 inch** and not more than **1-1/4 inches** is required (CBC 1011.5.2, Exception 3; CRC R318.7.5.3).

### Headroom

- **CBC**: Not less than **80 inches** (6'-8") measured vertically from a line connecting the nosings, continuous the full width of the stairway and landing (CBC 1011.3)
- **CRC**: Not less than **6 feet 8 inches** (2032 mm) measured vertically from the sloped line adjoining the tread nosing or floor surface of the landing (CRC R318.7.2)
- **Spiral stairways**: 78 inches minimum per CBC 1011.10; 6 feet 6 inches minimum per CRC R318.7.11.1

### Landings

#### CBC (Common Stairs) — 1011.6
- Required at **top and bottom** of each stairway (CBC 1011.6)
- Width (perpendicular to travel): not less than the **width of the stairway served** (CBC 1011.6)
- Depth (parallel to travel): equal to the **width of the stairway or 48 inches, whichever is less** (CBC 1011.6)
- Doors opening onto a landing shall not reduce the landing to less than **one-half the required width** (CBC 1011.6)
- When fully open, a door shall not project more than **7 inches** into the required width (CBC 1011.6)

#### CRC (Within Townhouse Units) — R318.7.6
- Required at **top and bottom** of each flight (CRC R318.7.6)
- Width: not less than the **width of the flight served** (CRC R318.7.6)
- Depth for straight-run stairs: not less than **36 inches** (CRC R318.7.6)
- Top landing of an interior stairway may be on the other side of a door at the top, provided the door does not swing over the stairs (CRC R318.7.6, Exception 1)

### Vertical Rise

- **CBC**: Maximum **12 feet** between floor levels or landings per flight (CBC 1011.8)
- **CRC**: Maximum **12 feet 7 inches** between floor levels or landings per flight (CRC R318.7.3)

### Handrails

#### CBC (Common Stairs) — 1011.11, 1014
- Required on **both sides** of every flight of stairs (CBC 1011.11)
- **Exception within dwelling units**: Flights within dwelling units and spiral stairways are permitted to have a handrail on **one side only** (CBC 1011.11, Exception 1)
- **Height**: Uniform, not less than **34 inches** and not more than **38 inches** measured from a line connecting the nosings (CBC 1014.2)
- **Extensions**: At the top, extend horizontally not less than **12 inches** beyond the top landing nosing; at the bottom, continue to slope for the depth of one tread beyond the bottom nosing (CBC 1014.7). Exception within non-accessible dwelling units: handrails need only extend from top riser to bottom riser (CBC 1014.7, Exception 1)
- **Intermediate handrails**: Required so all portions of the stairway minimum width are within **30 inches** of a handrail (CBC 1014.10)
- **Clearance**: Not less than **1-1/2 inches** between handrail and wall (CBC 1014.8)
- **Graspability**: Circular cross-section 1-1/4" to 2" diameter, or non-circular 4" to 6-1/4" perimeter (CBC 1014.4.1)
- **Projections**: Handrails may project not more than **4-1/2 inches** into the required stairway width at each side (CBC 1014.9)

#### CRC (Within Townhouse Units) — R318.7.8, R320
- Required on not less than **one side** of each flight with four or more risers (CRC R318.7.8)
- **Height**: Not less than **34 inches** and not more than **38 inches** (CRC R320.2)
- **Continuity**: Continuous for the full length of the flight, from a point directly above the top landing nosing to directly above the lowest nosing (CRC R320.5). May be interrupted by a newel post at a turn or landing (CRC R320.5, Exception 1)
- **Clearance**: Not less than **1-1/2 inches** between handrail and wall (CRC R320.4)
- **Projection**: Not more than **4-1/2 inches** on either side of the stairway (CRC R320.3)
- **Grip size**: Type I (circular 1-1/4" to 2" diameter) or Type II (perimeter >6-1/4" with finger recesses) (CRC R320.6)

### Guards

#### CBC (Common Stairs) — 1015
- Required where open-sided walking surfaces are more than **30 inches** above the floor or grade below (CBC 1015.2)
- **Height**: Not less than **42 inches** measured vertically from the adjacent walking surface or line connecting nosings (CBC 1015.3)
- **Exception within R-2 dwelling units**: Guards on the open sides of stairs may be not less than **34 inches** from the nosing line (CBC 1015.3, Exception 1). Where the top of the guard serves as a handrail, height shall be **34 to 38 inches** (CBC 1015.3, Exception 2)
- **Opening limitations**: Shall not allow passage of a **4-inch** diameter sphere from the walking surface to the required guard height (CBC 1015.4). Triangular openings at open sides of stairs (riser/tread/bottom rail): **6-inch** sphere maximum (CBC 1015.4, Exception 2). Within R-2 dwelling units: **4-3/8-inch** sphere on the open side of stairs (CBC 1015.4, Exception 6)

#### CRC (Within Townhouse Units) — R321
- Required where open-sided walking surfaces are more than **30 inches** above the floor or grade below (CRC R321.1.1)
- **Height**: Not less than **42 inches** (CRC R321.1.2). Exception on open sides of stairs: not less than **34 inches** from the nosing line (CRC R321.1.2, Exception 1). Where the top serves as a handrail: **34 to 38 inches** (CRC R321.1.2, Exception 2)
- **Opening limitations**: **4-inch** sphere maximum (CRC R321.1.3). Triangular openings at stairs: **6-inch** sphere (CRC R321.1.3, Exception 1). Open side of stairs: **4-3/8-inch** sphere (CRC R321.1.3, Exception 2)

### Interior Exit Stairway Enclosures (CBC 1023)

Common exit stairways in multi-story multi-unit buildings must be enclosed:

| Stories Connected | Required Fire-Resistance Rating | Reference |
|------------------|-------------------------------|-----------|
| **Less than 4 stories** | 1 hour | CBC 1023.2 |
| **4 or more stories** | 2 hours | CBC 1023.2 |

Stories counted include basements but NOT mezzanines (CBC 1023.2). The enclosure rating shall not be less than the floor assembly penetrated, but need not exceed 2 hours (CBC 1023.2).

**Key enclosure requirements:**
- Constructed as **fire barriers** per CBC 707 or **horizontal assemblies** per CBC 711 (CBC 1023.2)
- Shall **not be used for any purpose other than egress and circulation** (CBC 1023.1)
- Must lead directly to the exterior or be extended via an **exit passageway** per CBC 1024 (CBC 1023.1)
- **Openings**: Limited to those needed for exit access from normally occupied spaces and for egress from the enclosure; opening protectives per CBC 716 (CBC 1023.4)
- **Elevators shall not open** into interior exit stairways (CBC 1023.4)
- **Penetrations**: Prohibited except for ventilation, fire protection, security, communication systems, electrical raceway for the stairway, and structural elements (CBC 1023.5)
- **Ventilation**: Must be independent of other building ventilation systems; intake and exhaust directly from/to outdoors (CBC 1023.6)
- **Barrier at exit discharge level**: Required to prevent persons from continuing below the level of exit discharge (CBC 1023.8)
- **Stairway identification signs**: Required where connecting more than 3 stories — minimum 18" x 12", floor-level designations, roof access status (CBC 1023.9)
- **Smokeproof enclosures**: Required for high-rise buildings (CBC 403.5.4), underground buildings (CBC 405.7.2), or aircraft hangars (CBC 412.2.2.1); compliant with CBC 909.20 (CBC 1023.12)

### Enclosures Under Stairways

- **Interior**: Walls and soffits within enclosed usable spaces under stairways require **1-hour fire-resistance-rated construction** or the rating of the stairway enclosure, whichever is greater. Access shall not be from within the stairway enclosure (CBC 1011.7.3). Exception: Spaces under stairs within a single dwelling unit may use **1/2-inch gypsum board** on the enclosed side (CBC 1011.7.3, Exception)
- **Exterior**: Enclosed usable space under exterior exit stairways requires **1-hour fire-resistance-rated construction**. Open space under exterior stairways shall not be used for any purpose (CBC 1011.7.4)

### Winder Treads

- **CBC common stairs**: Winders are **not permitted** in means of egress stairways except within a dwelling unit (CBC 1011.5.3). Exceptions: curved stairways per 1011.9 and spiral stairways per 1011.10
- **Within dwelling units (CBC and CRC)**: Winders are permitted. Minimum tread depth at walkline: **10 inches** (CRC R318.7.5.2.1); minimum at any point within the clear width: **6 inches** (CRC R318.7.5.2.1)
- **Walkline**: Measured **12 inches** from the inside of the turn (CBC 1011.4; CRC R318.7.4)

### Spiral Stairways

- **CBC**: Permitted only within dwelling units or from spaces not more than **250 sq ft** serving not more than **5 occupants** (CBC 1011.10)
- **CRC**: Permitted within dwelling units (CRC R318.7.11.1)
- **Geometry (both codes align)**:
  - Minimum clear tread depth at 12 inches from the narrow edge: **6-3/4 inches**
  - Maximum riser height: **9-1/2 inches**
  - Minimum headroom: **78 inches** (CBC); **6 feet 6 inches** (CRC)
  - Minimum clear width at and below handrail: **26 inches**
  - Maximum walkline radius: **24-1/2 inches** (CRC R318.7.11.1)

### Stairway Construction

- Materials must be consistent with the type of construction of the building (CBC 1011.7)
- Wood handrails are permitted in **all types of construction** (CBC 1011.7, Exception 1)
- Walking surfaces: not sloped steeper than **2 percent** in any direction; solid surface; finish floors securely attached (CBC 1011.7.1; CRC R318.7.7)

### Stairway to Roof

In buildings **four or more stories** above grade plane, one stairway shall extend to the roof surface unless the roof slope exceeds 4:12 (CBC 1011.12). Roofs containing elevator equipment must be accessed by a stairway (CBC 1011.12.1).

### For the 35-Unit Townhome Test Case

- **Within each townhouse unit**: CRC R318.7 governs — 36" wide, 7-3/4" max rise, 10" min run, handrail on one side, 42" guards (34" on open stair sides)
- **Common exit stairs** (if any serve corridors or shared areas with occupant load >50): CBC 1011 — 44" wide, 7" max rise, 11" min run, handrails both sides, guards 42"
- **Exit stairway enclosures**: 1-hour for buildings ≤3 stories, 2-hour for 4+ stories (CBC 1023.2). Most 35-unit townhome projects are 2-3 stories, so 1-hour applies
- **Winders**: Permitted only within individual dwelling units, not in common exit stairs
- **Spiral stairs**: Permitted only within individual dwelling units
- **Under-stair storage within units**: 1/2-inch gypsum board on enclosed side (CBC 1011.7.3, Exception)
