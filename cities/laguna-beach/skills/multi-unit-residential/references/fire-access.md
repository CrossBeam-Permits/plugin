## Fire Access — Apparatus Roads, Hydrant Spacing, Dead-End Turnarounds, and Construction-Phase Access

### Fire Apparatus Access Roads — General (CFC 503)

Every building must be served by an approved fire apparatus access road that extends to within **150 feet** of all portions of the facility and all portions of the exterior walls of the first story, measured by an approved route around the exterior of the building (CFC 503.1.1).

**Exception — 150-ft distance may be increased** where any of the following apply (CFC 503.1.1, Exception 1):
1. Building is equipped throughout with an approved automatic sprinkler system per Section 903.3.1.1, 903.3.1.2, or 903.3.1.3
2. Access roads cannot be installed due to topography, waterways, nonnegotiable grades, or similar conditions, AND an approved alternative means of fire protection is provided
3. Not more than two Group R-3 or Group U occupancies

The fire code official may require **more than one** fire apparatus access road based on potential impairment from vehicle congestion, terrain, climatic conditions, or other limiting factors (CFC 503.1.2).

### Road Dimensions and Specifications (CFC 503.2)

| Requirement | Standard | Code Section |
|-------------|----------|-------------|
| **Minimum width** | 20 feet unobstructed, exclusive of shoulders | CFC 503.2.1 |
| **Width where hydrant on road** | 26 feet minimum, exclusive of shoulders | CFC Appendix D D103.1 |
| **Vertical clearance** | 13 feet 6 inches minimum | CFC 503.2.1 |
| **Surface** | All-weather driving surface, designed to support imposed loads of fire apparatus | CFC 503.2.3 |
| **Load capacity** | Asphalt, concrete, or other approved surface capable of supporting **75,000 pounds** | CFC Appendix D D102.1 |
| **Maximum grade** | 10 percent (steeper grades only with fire code official approval) | CFC Appendix D D103.2 |
| **Turning radius** | Determined by fire code official based on department apparatus | CFC 503.2.4 |
| **Angles of approach/departure** | Within limits established by fire code official | CFC 503.2.8 |

**California amendment (CCR Title 19, Division 1, §3.05(a)):** Required access roads from every building to a public street shall be all-weather hard-surfaced (suitable for use by fire apparatus) right-of-way not less than **20 feet in width**. Such right-of-way shall be unobstructed and maintained only as access to the public street. The enforcing agency may waive or modify this requirement if such all-weather hard-surfaced condition is not necessary in the interest of public safety and welfare.

### Dead-End Turnaround Requirements (CFC Appendix D, Table D103.4)

Dead-end fire apparatus access roads in excess of 150 feet shall be provided with width and turnaround provisions:

| Dead-End Length | Minimum Width | Turnaround Required |
|----------------|---------------|---------------------|
| **0–150 ft** | 20 ft | None required |
| **151–500 ft** | 20 ft | 120-ft hammerhead, 60-ft "Y", or 96-ft diameter cul-de-sac |
| **501–750 ft** | 26 ft | 120-ft hammerhead, 60-ft "Y", or 96-ft diameter cul-de-sac |
| **Over 750 ft** | Special approval required | Special approval required |

(CFC Appendix D, Table D103.4; turnaround configurations per Figure D103.1)

### Aerial Fire Apparatus Access (CFC Appendix D, D105)

Where the vertical distance between grade plane and the highest roof surface exceeds **30 feet**, approved aerial fire apparatus access roads shall be provided (D105.1). The highest roof surface is measured to the eave of a pitched roof, the intersection of roof to exterior wall, or the top of parapet walls, whichever is greater.

**Aerial access road requirements:**
- Minimum unobstructed width of **26 feet**, exclusive of shoulders (D105.2)
- Located not less than **15 feet** and not greater than **30 feet** from the building (D105.3)
- Positioned **parallel to one entire side** of the building (D105.3)
- **No overhead utility or power lines** over the aerial access road or between the road and the building (D105.4)

**Exception:** Buildings of Type IA, IB, or IIA construction equipped throughout with an automatic sprinkler system per 903.3.1.1, with firefighter access through an enclosed stairway with a Class I standpipe from the lowest level of fire department vehicle access to all roof surfaces (D105.1, Exception).

### Multiple-Family Residential Access (CFC Appendix D, D106)

| Project Size | Access Roads Required | Exception |
|-------------|----------------------|-----------|
| **≤100 dwelling units** | 1 | — |
| **101–200 dwelling units** | 2 separate and approved roads | 1 road permitted if ALL buildings (including nonresidential) are equipped throughout with approved automatic sprinkler system per 903.3.1.1 or 903.3.1.2 |
| **>200 dwelling units** | 2 separate and approved roads | None — two roads required regardless of sprinklers |

Where two access roads are required, they shall be placed a distance apart equal to not less than **one-half the length of the maximum overall diagonal dimension** of the property or area served, measured in a straight line between accesses (D106.3).

### Hydrant Spacing and Distribution (CFC Appendix C, Table C102.1)

The number and spacing of fire hydrants is determined by fire-flow requirement:

| Fire-Flow (gpm) | Min. Hydrants | Avg. Spacing (ft) | Max. Distance from Street Frontage to Hydrant (ft) |
|-----------------|---------------|-------------------|---------------------------------------------------|
| **1,750 or less** | 1 | 500 | 250 |
| **1,751–2,250** | 2 | 450 | 225 |
| **2,251–2,750** | 3 | 450 | 225 |
| **2,751–3,250** | 3 | 400 | 225 |
| **3,251–4,000** | 4 | 350 | 210 |
| **4,001–5,000** | 5 | 300 | 180 |
| **5,001–5,500** | 6 | 300 | 180 |
| **5,501–6,000** | 6 | 250 | 150 |
| **6,001–7,000** | 7 | 250 | 150 |
| **7,001 or more** | 8 or more | 200 | 120 |

(CFC Appendix C, Table C102.1)

**Key spacing modifiers:**
- **Reduce spacing by 100 ft** for dead-end streets or roads (Table C102.1, note a)
- **Reduce max distance by 50 ft** for dead-end streets or roads (Table C102.1, note d)
- **50% spacing increase** where building is equipped throughout with an approved automatic sprinkler system per CFC 903.3.1.1 (Table C102.1, note f)
- **25% spacing increase** where building is equipped throughout with an approved automatic sprinkler system per CFC 903.3.1.2 or 903.3.1.3, or CRC Section R309 (Table C102.1, note g)
- For fire flows ≥7,001 gpm: **one hydrant for each 1,000 gpm** or fraction thereof (Table C102.1, note e)
- Streets with median dividers firefighters cannot cross, or arterials with 4+ lanes and >30,000 vehicles/day: hydrant spacing shall average **500 feet on each side**, arranged on an alternating basis (Table C102.1, note b)

**Hydrant distance from building:** Where a portion of the building is more than **400 feet** from a hydrant on a fire apparatus access road, on-site fire hydrants and mains shall be provided (CFC 507.5.1). For Group R-3 and Group U occupancies with approved sprinklers, the distance increases to **600 feet** (CFC 507.5.1, Exception).

**Hydrant for standpipe systems:** Buildings with standpipes per Section 905 shall have a fire hydrant within **100 feet** of fire department connections (CFC 507.5.1.1).

**Clear space around hydrants:** A **3-foot** clear space shall be maintained around the circumference of fire hydrants (CFC 507.5.5). Unobstructed access to hydrants must be maintained at all times (CFC 507.5.4).

### Fire Lane Markings and Signs (CFC 503.3, Appendix D D103.6)

Where required by the fire code official, approved signs or markings that include the words **"NO PARKING — FIRE LANE"** shall be provided for fire apparatus access roads (CFC 503.3). Markings shall be maintained in clean and legible condition at all times.

**Sign specifications (CFC D103.6):**
- Minimum dimensions: **12 inches wide by 18 inches high**
- **Red letters on a white reflective background**

**Posting requirements:**
- Roads **20 to 26 feet** wide: signs on **both sides** (D103.6.1)
- Roads **more than 26 feet but less than 32 feet** wide: signs on **one side** (D103.6.2)

Fire apparatus access roads shall **not be obstructed in any manner**, including the parking of vehicles. Minimum widths and clearances shall be maintained at all times (CFC 503.4). Traffic calming devices are prohibited unless approved by the fire code official (CFC 503.4.1).

### Security Gates on Access Roads (CFC 503.6, Appendix D D103.5)

Security gates across fire apparatus access roads require fire code official approval and an approved means of emergency operation:

- Single gate minimum width: **20 feet**; divided roadway gate: **12 feet** minimum per gate (D103.5, Item 1)
- Gates shall be horizontal swing, horizontal slide, vertical lift, or vertical pivot type (D103.5, Item 2)
- Construction shall allow **manual operation by one person** (D103.5, Item 3)
- Electric gates shall have an emergency opening device approved by the fire code official (D103.5, Item 5)
- Electric gate operators listed per **UL 325**; automatic gates per **ASTM F2200** (D103.5, Items 7–8)

### Fire Safety During Construction (CFC Chapter 33)

#### Site Safety Plan (CFC 3303.1)

The owner or authorized agent is responsible for an approved written site safety plan establishing a fire prevention program at the project site. The plan must be **submitted and approved before a building permit is issued**. Required components include (CFC 3303.1.1):

1. Name and contact of site safety director
2. Training documentation for site safety director and fire watch personnel
3. Emergency reporting procedures
4. **Fire department vehicle access routes**
5. Location of fire protection equipment (extinguishers, standpipes, FDCs, hydrants)
6. Smoking and cooking policies
7. Temporary heating equipment locations
8. Hot work permit plan
9. Combustible waste control plans
10. Flammable/combustible liquid storage locations
11. Site security and fire watch provisions

#### Construction-Phase Access Roads (CFC 3307.1, 3303.3)

Approved vehicle access for firefighting shall be provided to all construction or demolition sites. Vehicle access shall be provided to within **100 feet** of temporary or permanent fire department connections (CFC 3307.1). Access shall be by temporary or permanent roads, capable of supporting vehicle loading under all weather conditions, and maintained until permanent fire apparatus access roads are available.

**Daily inspection requirement (CFC 3303.3, Item 6):** Fire apparatus access roads shall be maintained clear of obstructions that reduce the usable roadway width to less than **20 feet**.

**Daily inspection requirement (CFC 3303.3, Item 7):** Fire hydrants shall be clearly visible from access roads and not obstructed.

#### Timing of Installation (CFC 501.4)

Where fire apparatus access roads or water supply for fire protection are required, such protection shall be **installed and made serviceable prior to and during the time of construction** except where approved alternative methods of protection are provided. Temporary street signs shall be installed at each street intersection where construction of new roadways allows passage by vehicles (CFC 501.4, 505.2).

#### Water Supply During Construction (CFC 3307.2)

Water supply must be available as soon as combustible building materials arrive on site:
- **500 gpm minimum** fire flow when combustible materials are delivered (CFC 3307.2.1)
- Hydrant within **500 feet** of combustible building materials (CFC 3307.2.1)
- Where standpipes are required, hydrant within **100 feet** of the fire department connection (CFC 3307.4)

#### Fire Watch During Construction (CFC 3303.5)

A fire watch is required during nonworking hours for:
- New construction exceeding **40 feet in height** above the lowest adjacent grade (CFC 3303.5.1)
- New multistory construction with aggregate area exceeding **50,000 sq ft per story** (CFC 3303.5.1)
- As required by the fire code official

### Buildings Over 30 Feet — Additional Access (CFC Appendix D, D104)

Buildings or facilities exceeding **30 feet or three stories** in height shall have not fewer than **two means of fire apparatus access** for each structure (D104.1).

Buildings or facilities with a gross building area of more than **62,000 sq ft** shall be provided with two separate and approved fire apparatus access roads (D104.2). Exception: Up to 124,000 sq ft may use a single access road where all buildings are equipped throughout with approved automatic sprinkler systems.

Where two access roads are required, they shall be placed a distance apart equal to not less than **one-half the maximum overall diagonal dimension** of the lot or area served (D104.3).

### California-Specific Amendments

**CCR Title 19, Division 1, §3.05(a)** — The California amendment to CFC 503.2 requires all-weather hard-surfaced access roads not less than 20 feet in width from every building to a public street. This is more prescriptive than the base IFC, which does not specify "hard-surfaced" or explicitly require connectivity to a public street.

**CCR Title 19, Division 1, Chapter 5** — Private fire hydrants: annual inspection and flow test; fire service mains: visual inspection annually, flow test every 5 years (CFC 507.5.3).
