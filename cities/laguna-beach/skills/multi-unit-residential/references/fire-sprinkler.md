## Fire Sprinkler Systems — NFPA 13/13R/13D Selection, Standpipes, and Alarm Requirements

### When Sprinklers Are Required — Group R (CBC 903.2.8)

All new buildings with a Group R fire area require an automatic sprinkler system installed per CBC 903.3. This is a blanket requirement — there is no size threshold, no occupant-load trigger, and no construction-type exception for new Group R occupancies.

The CRC independently mandates sprinklers for **townhouses** (CRC R309.1) and **one- and two-family dwellings** (CRC R309.2). Both require design per CRC R309 or NFPA 13D.

**Exceptions to the Group R mandate** (CBC 903.2.8, CFC 903.2.8):
1. Existing R-3 converted to R-3.1 not housing bedridden clients, not housing nonambulatory clients above the first floor, and not housing clients above the second floor
2. Existing R-3 converted to R-3.1 housing only one bedridden client per CBC 435.8.3.3
3. H&S Code 13113 — ambulatory children only, building ≤2 stories, with smoke-detector fire alarm
4. H&S Code 13143.6 — protective social care, ambulatory clients only, no children or elderly

**R-2.1 limitation**: NFPA 13D shall not be used for Group R-2.1 or R-4 occupancies. NFPA 13R is permitted in R-2.1 only when not used for height or area increases (CBC 903.2.8, Exception 4 tail note).

### NFPA Standard Selection — 13 vs 13R vs 13D

The choice of sprinkler standard is the single most consequential fire-protection decision. It determines piping coverage, attic protection, balcony sprinkler requirements, water supply sizing, and whether the building qualifies for construction-type trade-offs.

#### NFPA 13D — One/Two-Family and Townhouses Only (CBC 903.3.1.3)

NFPA 13D systems are permitted **only** in:
- One- and two-family dwellings
- Group R-3 occupancies
- Townhouses

(CBC 903.3.1.3, CFC 903.3.1.3)

CRC R309.1 makes sprinklers mandatory for all new townhouses; CRC R309.1.1 requires design per CRC R309 or NFPA 13D. CRC R309.2 does the same for one- and two-family dwellings.

**Key 13D characteristics:**
- Sprinklers required in all areas of the dwelling unit except: attics/crawl spaces without fuel-fired appliances, small closets/pantries (≤24 sq ft, ≤3 ft smallest dimension, gypsum walls/ceiling), bathrooms ≤55 sq ft, garages, carports, exterior porches, unheated entry areas (CRC R309.3.1.2)
- Single dwelling limit — piping beyond the service valve shall not serve more than one dwelling (CRC R309.3.3.3)
- Design flow based on the two most demanding sprinklers operating simultaneously in the largest-demand room (CRC R309.3.4.2)
- Water supply duration: 7 minutes for single-story under 2,000 sq ft; 10 minutes for two or more stories or ≥2,000 sq ft (CRC R309.3.5.2)
- Multipurpose piping (combined domestic/fire) is permitted (CRC R309.3.1)
- Electronic supervision NOT required (CBC 903.4.1, Exception 1)

**NFPA 13D is NOT permitted for:**
- R-2 apartment buildings
- R-2.1 or R-4 care facilities (CBC 903.2.8 tail note)
- Any building that is not a one/two-family dwelling, R-3, or townhouse

#### NFPA 13R — Allowed for R-2 Under Height Limits (CBC 903.3.1.2)

NFPA 13R is a reduced system permitted for Group R occupancies meeting **all three** of the following conditions (CBC 903.3.1.2, CFC 903.3.1.2):

1. **Four stories or fewer** above grade plane
2. **Height limit**:
   - R-2 occupancies: roof assembly **less than 45 feet** above the lowest level of fire department vehicle access. Height is measured from the lowest required fire vehicle access road surface to the eave of the highest pitched roof, the intersection of the highest roof to the exterior wall, or the top of the highest parapet — whichever yields the greatest distance
   - Other R occupancies (R-1, R-3, R-4): floor level of the highest story ≤30 feet above fire department vehicle access
3. Floor level of the **lowest story** is 30 feet or less **below** the lowest level of fire department vehicle access

For buildings designed per CBC 510.2 or 510.4 (podium construction), story count is measured from grade plane (CBC 903.3.1.2, last paragraph).

**Key 13R characteristics compared to 13D:**
- Sprinkler coverage extends to corridors and balconies in means of egress under specific conditions (903.3.1.2.2)
- Balcony/deck sprinklers required for Type V construction where there is a roof or deck above (903.3.1.2.1)
- Attic sprinklers required where used for living/storage or where fuel-fired equipment is present; additional attic requirements for Type III/IV/V buildings over 55 feet to roof (903.3.1.2.3)
- Electronic supervision not required where common supply main serves domestic and sprinkler with no separate shutoff valve (CBC 903.4.1, Exception 3)
- Combined domestic/sprinkler water supply is explicitly allowed (CBC 903.3.5.2)

#### NFPA 13 — Full System, Required When 13R Does Not Qualify (CBC 903.3.1)

NFPA 13 is the default standard. When the code requires sprinklers and neither 13R nor 13D is permitted, NFPA 13 applies (CBC 903.3.1).

A full NFPA 13 system is required when:
- Building exceeds 4 stories above grade plane
- R-2 roof assembly is 45 feet or more above fire department vehicle access
- Non-R-2 Group R highest story floor level exceeds 30 feet above vehicle access
- Lowest story exceeds 30 feet below vehicle access
- Building is R-2.1 using sprinklers for height or area increases

**Key 13 differences from 13R:**
- Complete coverage including attics, concealed spaces, and mechanical rooms
- Higher water supply demands (density/area method per NFPA 13)
- Hose stream allowance and water supply duration per NFPA 13 (typically 60-90 min)
- Fire department connection (FDC) required per CBC 903.3.7 / Section 912

**Bathroom exemption (all standards):** In Group R occupancies, sprinklers are not required in bathrooms ≤55 sq ft within individual dwelling units, provided walls and ceilings (including behind tub/shower) are noncombustible or limited-combustible with 15-minute thermal barrier (CBC 903.3.1.1.2, CFC 903.3.1.1.2).

### Sprinkler Impact on Other Code Provisions

The presence and type of sprinkler system triggers multiple code trade-offs:

| Code Provision | Without Sprinklers | With Sprinklers | Reference |
|---------------|-------------------|-----------------|-----------|
| **Townhouse party wall** | 2-hour rated | 1-hour rated | CRC R302.2.2 |
| **Structural independence** | Required, limited exceptions | Additional exception for shared elements | CRC R302.2.6, Exception 6 |
| **Exterior wall fire separation** | Table R302.1(1) — stricter | Table R302.1(2) — reduced distances | CRC R302.1 |
| **Height increase** | Base table only | +20 ft and +1 story (CBC Tables 504.3, 504.4) | CBC Tables 504.3, 504.4 |
| **Area increase** | Base table only | Up to 3x with sprinkler allowance | CBC Table 506.2 |
| **Dwelling unit separation** | 1 hour (Types IIB, IIIB, VB) | 1/2 hour (Types IIB, IIIB, VB only, with NFPA 13) | CBC 708.3, Exception 2 |
| **Corridor fire-resistance** | Per Table 1020.2 | Reduced per Table 1020.2 exceptions | CBC 1020.2 |

**Critical note on height/area increases:** If the project uses sprinkler-based height or area increases (CBC Tables 504.3, 504.4, 506.2), a 13R system may not be sufficient. Verify that the sprinkler standard being used is acceptable for the increases being claimed.

### Floor Control Valves (CBC 903.3.10, CFC 903.3.10)

Floor control valves and waterflow detection assemblies are required at each floor where any of the following occur:
1. Building highest story floor level is more than 30 feet above fire department vehicle access
2. Building is four or more stories in height
3. Building is two or more stories below the highest level of fire department vehicle access

**Exception:** Group R-3 and R-3.1 occupancies are exempt (CBC 903.3.10, CFC 903.3.10).

### Standpipe Requirements (CBC 905)

#### When Required (CBC 905.3.1)

Class III standpipe systems are required throughout each floor (except R-3 and R-3.1) where any of the following occur:
1. Highest story floor level is more than **30 feet** above fire department vehicle access
2. Building is **four or more stories** in height
3. Lowest story floor level is more than **30 feet below** fire department vehicle access
4. Building is **two or more stories below** the highest level of fire department vehicle access

**Key exceptions:**
- Class I standpipes (fire department hose connections only) are allowed in lieu of Class III where building is equipped throughout with NFPA 13 or 13R sprinklers (905.3.1, Exception 1)
- **R-2 townhouses are exempt** from standpipe requirements (905.3, Exception 1)
- **R-3 occupancies are exempt** from standpipe requirements (905.3, Exception 2)

#### Standpipe Location (CBC 905.4)
Hose connections required in every required interior exit stairway at each floor, on each side of horizontal exit openings, and at entrance from exit passageways.

### Supervision and Alarms (CBC 903.4)

**Electronic supervision** (CBC 903.4.1): Valves, pumps, tanks, water levels, temperatures, air pressures, and waterflow switches shall be electrically supervised by a listed fire alarm control unit.

Exceptions relevant to multi-unit residential:
1. Systems protecting one- and two-family dwellings
3. NFPA 13R systems where a common supply main serves domestic and sprinkler without a separate shutoff valve

**Waterflow alarms** (CBC 903.4.3): An approved audible and visual sprinkler waterflow alarm device on the building exterior is required. Where a fire alarm system is provided, sprinkler activation shall actuate the building fire alarm.

Exception: One- and two-family dwellings.

### Smoke Alarms and CO Alarms (CRC R310, R311 — Brief Summary)

While not part of the sprinkler system, smoke and CO alarms are frequently reviewed alongside sprinkler plans:

- **Smoke alarms**: Required in each sleeping room, outside each sleeping area, and on each story including basement (CRC R310.3). Hardwired with battery backup, interconnected
- **CO alarms**: Required outside each sleeping area in dwelling units with fuel-fired appliances or attached garages (CRC R311). Hardwired or plug-in with battery backup

These requirements apply regardless of sprinkler installation.

### For the 35-Unit Townhome Test Case

**Occupancy classification determines sprinkler standard:**
- If classified as **R-3 townhouses** under CRC (each unit has separate means of egress, ≤3 stories): **NFPA 13D** is permitted per CBC 903.3.1.3 and CRC R309.1.1
- If classified as **R-2** (common corridors, shared egress): Must use **NFPA 13R** (if within height limits) or **NFPA 13** (if exceeding limits)

**For the test case (3-story, Type V-A):**
- Three stories above grade plane — satisfies the 4-story maximum for NFPA 13R
- A 3-story Type V-A building typically has a roof elevation well under 45 feet — satisfies the R-2 roof height limit
- If classified R-3 townhouses: NFPA 13D is the standard; no standpipe required (905.3, Exception 1 for R-2 townhouses, Exception 2 for R-3)
- If classified R-2: NFPA 13R is likely permitted; standpipe required if 4+ stories or highest floor >30 ft above vehicle access (905.3.1) — at 3 stories this may trigger depending on building height
- Party wall rating: 1-hour with sprinklers (CRC R302.2.2)
- Floor control valves: Required if 4+ stories (903.3.10) — at 3 stories, likely not triggered unless highest floor exceeds 30 ft above vehicle access
- Balcony/deck sprinklers: Required for Type V construction where roof or deck is above (903.3.1.2.1)
- Smoke alarms and CO alarms: Required per CRC R310, R311 regardless of sprinkler standard
