# Parking Requirements for Multi-Unit Residential

Source: California Government Code; 2025 California Building Code (Title 24, Part 2), Chapters 11A and 11B; 2025 CALGreen Standards (Title 24, Part 11); effective January 1, 2026.

---

## State vs. Local Parking Rules

California does **not** set statewide minimum parking ratios for residential development. Parking minimums -- the number of vehicle spaces required per dwelling unit -- are established by each city or county through its local zoning ordinance (municipal code). Typical local ratios range from 1.0 to 2.5 spaces per unit depending on unit size, bedroom count, density zone, and project type, but these numbers vary widely by jurisdiction.

The state's role is limited to:
1. **Prohibiting** local parking minimums in certain contexts (transit proximity -- AB 2097)
2. **Mandating** accessible parking when any parking is provided (CBC 11B-208, 11A-1109A)
3. **Requiring** EV charging infrastructure at parking spaces (CALGreen 4.106.4)
4. **Requiring** bicycle parking (CALGreen 4.106.4.4)

A plan reviewer must always look to the **applicable city zoning code** to determine the base parking ratio. State code then layers accessibility, EV, and bicycle requirements on top.

## AB 2097 -- Transit Proximity Parking Exemption

### No Parking Minimums Near Major Transit (Gov. Code 65863.2)

Assembly Bill 2097, effective January 1, 2023, prohibits a public agency from imposing or enforcing any minimum automobile parking requirement on a residential, commercial, or other development project located within **one-half mile of a major transit stop**. (Gov. Code 65863.2(a))

### What Qualifies as a Major Transit Stop

A "major transit stop" is defined in Public Resources Code 21064.3 as any of the following:
- An existing rail or bus rapid transit station
- A ferry terminal served by either a bus or rail transit service
- The intersection of two or more major bus routes with a frequency of service interval of **15 minutes or less** during the morning and afternoon peak commute periods

(Pub. Resources Code 21064.3; Gov. Code 65863.2)

### Key Provisions

- A project **may still voluntarily provide parking** -- AB 2097 eliminates the minimum, not the option (Gov. Code 65863.2(a))
- A public agency **may not** impose a minimum parking requirement as a condition of approval, permit, or entitlement for a project within the half-mile radius (Gov. Code 65863.2(a))
- The half-mile distance is measured as a **walking distance** from the parcel boundary to the transit stop, not as the crow flies
- AB 2097 does not override **maximum** parking limits that a city may impose

### Exceptions

A public agency may impose minimum parking requirements within the half-mile radius only if the agency makes **written findings**, supported by a preponderance of the evidence, that not imposing minimum parking requirements would have a substantially negative impact, supported by a peer-reviewed study. Even then, the agency may not require more than 0.5 spaces per unit for projects meeting certain affordability thresholds. (Gov. Code 65863.2(b))

### AB 1317 -- Guest Parking for Affordable Housing

Assembly Bill 1317 (effective 2024) further prohibits a city from requiring **guest parking** for affordable housing developments located near qualifying transit. This is narrower than AB 2097 and applies specifically to deed-restricted affordable projects.

## Accessible Parking Requirements (CBC 11B-208)

When parking spaces are provided for a multi-unit residential project, accessible parking must comply with CBC 11B-208 and CBC 11B-502. The number of accessible spaces is determined by the **total number of parking spaces provided**, not by a per-unit ratio. (CBC 11B-208.1)

### Minimum Number of Accessible Spaces -- Table 11B-208.2

| Total Parking Spaces Provided | Minimum Accessible Spaces Required |
|-------------------------------|-----------------------------------|
| 1 to 25 | 1 |
| 26 to 50 | 2 |
| 51 to 75 | 3 |
| 76 to 100 | 4 |
| 101 to 150 | 5 |
| 151 to 200 | 6 |
| 201 to 300 | 7 |
| 301 to 400 | 8 |
| 401 to 500 | 9 |
| 501 to 1000 | 2% of total |
| 1001 and over | 20, plus 1 for each 100 (or fraction thereof) over 1000 |

(CBC Table 11B-208.2)

Where more than one parking facility is provided on a site, the number of accessible spaces shall be calculated according to the number of spaces required for **each** parking facility. (CBC 11B-208.2)

### Residential Facility Parking Rules (CBC 11B-208.2.3)

Multi-unit residential projects have specific rules layered on top of Table 11B-208.2:

**Parking for residents (CBC 11B-208.2.3.1):**
- Where at least one parking space is provided per dwelling unit: at least one accessible parking space per dwelling unit required to provide **mobility features** (CBC 11B-809.2 through 11B-809.4)
- Where fewer than one parking space is provided per dwelling unit: use Table 11B-208.2 to determine accessible space count

**Additional resident parking (CBC 11B-208.2.3.2):**
- Where total parking exceeds one space per dwelling unit, **2%** (but no fewer than one) of the excess parking spaces shall be accessible

**Guest/employee/non-resident parking (CBC 11B-208.2.3.3):**
- Use Table 11B-208.2 based on the total number of guest/visitor parking spaces provided

### Housing Accessibility Parking (CBC 11A-1109A)

Chapter 11A applies specifically to **covered multifamily dwellings** (buildings with 4+ units):

- **Assigned parking:** At least 2% of assigned parking spaces shall be accessible in each type of parking facility; at least one space of each type even if the total exceeds 2% (CBC 11A-1109A.4)
- **Unassigned and visitor parking:** At least **5%** shall be accessible and provide access to grade-level entrances (CBC 11A-1109A.5)
- **On request:** When assigned parking is provided, designated accessible parking for a dwelling unit shall be provided on request of residents with disabilities, on the same terms and with the full range of choices available for other residents (CBC 11A-1109A.6)

## Van-Accessible Parking Spaces

### CBC 11B-208.2.4

For every **6 or fraction of 6** accessible parking spaces required by Table 11B-208.2, at least **1** shall be a van parking space complying with CBC 11B-502. (CBC 11B-208.2.4)

### CBC 11A-1109A.8.6

Under the housing accessibility chapter, **1 in every 8** accessible spaces (but not less than one) shall be van accessible. (CBC 11A-1109A.8.6)

**Note:** Where both Chapter 11A and Chapter 11B apply (public housing and covered multifamily), the more stringent requirement governs. For most multi-unit residential projects, the 11B ratio of 1 per 6 is more stringent than 11A's 1 per 8.

### Van Space Dimensions (CBC 11B-502.2)

- **Width:** 144 inches (12 feet) minimum, or 108 inches (9 feet) minimum where the access aisle is 96 inches (8 feet) wide minimum
- **Length:** 216 inches (18 feet) minimum
- **Access aisle:** Located on the **passenger side** of the parking space (CBC 11B-502.3.4)
- **Vertical clearance:** 98 inches (8 feet 2 inches) minimum for spaces, access aisles, and vehicular routes (CBC 11B-502.5)

### Van Space Identification

Signs identifying van parking spaces shall contain additional language or an additional sign with the designation **"Van Accessible"**. (CBC 11B-502.6)

## Accessible Parking Space Design Standards (CBC 11B-502)

### Space Dimensions

| Element | Car Space | Van Space |
|---------|-----------|-----------|
| Width | 108 in (9 ft) minimum | 144 in (12 ft) minimum (or 108 in with 96-in aisle) |
| Length | 216 in (18 ft) minimum | 216 in (18 ft) minimum |
| Access aisle width | 60 in (5 ft) minimum | 60 in (5 ft) minimum (or 96 in if space is 108 in wide) |
| Access aisle location | Either side | Passenger side only |
| Vertical clearance | 98 in (8 ft 2 in) minimum | 98 in (8 ft 2 in) minimum |

(CBC 11B-502.2, 11B-502.3, 11B-502.5)

### Surface and Slope (CBC 11B-502.4)

- Access aisles at the same level as the parking spaces they serve
- Slope not exceeding **1:48** (2.083%) in any direction
- No changes in level and no detectable warnings permitted within parking spaces or access aisles

### Location (CBC 11B-208.3)

- Accessible parking shall be on the **shortest accessible route** from parking to an entrance complying with CBC 11B-206.4
- In residential facilities, parking for mobility-feature units shall be on the shortest accessible route to the **dwelling unit entrance** they serve (CBC 11B-208.3.2)
- Additional accessible spaces shall be **dispersed** throughout all types of parking (CBC 11B-208.3.2)

### Access Aisle Marking (CBC 11B-502.3.3)

- Blue painted borderline around the perimeter
- Hatched lines maximum 36 inches on center in contrasting color (preferably blue or white)
- **"NO PARKING"** in white letters minimum 12 inches high, visible from adjacent vehicular way

### Signage (CBC 11B-502.6)

- International Symbol of Accessibility: white on blue background, reflectorized, 70 square inches minimum area
- Posted 60 inches minimum above ground (80 inches minimum within circulation paths)
- **"Minimum Fine $250"** below the symbol
- Surface marking: International Symbol of Accessibility, 36 inches by 36 inches minimum, white on blue
- Tow-away sign at each entrance or adjacent to accessible parking (17 by 22 inches minimum, 1-inch lettering)

**Residential exception:** Where parking spaces are **assigned to specific dwelling units**, identification signs are not required. (CBC 11B-216.5.1, Exception 2)

## EV Parking Requirements

EV charging infrastructure is required for all new multi-unit residential construction under CALGreen 4.106.4. EV requirements are detailed in the companion reference file. Key points relevant to parking layout:

- At least **one EV-ready receptacle per dwelling unit** at assigned parking spaces (CALGreen 4.106.4.2.2(1))
- **25% of unassigned/common use spaces** must have Level 2 EV chargers (CALGreen 4.106.4.2.2(2))
- EV charging spaces in multifamily must comply with **CBC Chapter 11A, Section 1109A** for accessibility (CALGreen 4.106.4.2.2.1.2)
- EVCS space dimensions: 18 feet long minimum, 9 feet wide minimum (CALGreen 4.106.4.2.2.1.1)
- 1 in every 25 EVCS spaces (minimum 1) must have an 8-foot wide accessible aisle

See: [electrical-ev-charging.md](electrical-ev-charging.md)

## Bicycle Parking (CALGreen 4.106.4.4)

CALGreen requires both short-term and long-term bicycle parking for multifamily buildings.

### Short-Term Bicycle Parking (CALGreen 4.106.4.4.1)

- **Ratio:** 1 bicycle parking space per 10,000 square feet of building area, but **not less than 2 spaces**
- **Location:** Within 200 feet of building entrances, readily visible to passers-by, conveniently accessed from the street
- **Acceptable facilities:** Permanently anchored bicycle racks or lockers; may be covered or uncovered

### Long-Term Bicycle Parking (CALGreen 4.106.4.4.2)

- **Ratio:** 1 bicycle parking space for every **2 dwelling units**
- **Acceptable facilities:** Covered lockable enclosures, lockable storage rooms, or lockable weatherproof permanently anchored bicycle lockers, all with permanently anchored racks or devices
- Must be conveniently accessed from the street

### Calculation Example

For a 35-unit building at approximately 30,000 square feet:
- **Short-term:** 30,000 / 10,000 = 3 bicycle spaces (minimum 2) -- **3 spaces**
- **Long-term:** 35 / 2 = 17.5, rounded up = **18 spaces** in covered, lockable storage

## 35-Unit Test Case Application

For a 35-unit multi-unit residential project:

### Base Parking Ratio

The number of vehicle parking spaces is **set by the applicable city zoning code**, not by state law. For example, Buena Park Municipal Code sets the parking ratio for multi-family residential based on bedroom count and project location. If the city requires 1.5 spaces per unit, that yields 53 total spaces. If the project is within 1/2 mile of a major transit stop, AB 2097 eliminates the minimum entirely.

### Accessible Parking (assuming 53 total spaces)

| Requirement | Calculation | Result |
|-------------|-------------|--------|
| Accessible spaces (Table 11B-208.2) | 53 spaces falls in the 51-75 range | **3 accessible spaces** |
| Van-accessible spaces (CBC 11B-208.2.4) | 1 per every 6 or fraction of 6: 3/6 = 0.5, fraction rounds to 1 | **1 van-accessible space** |
| Assigned accessible parking (CBC 11A-1109A.4) | 2% of assigned spaces: 0.02 x 35 = 0.7, minimum 1 | **1 accessible assigned space** (at least) |
| Unassigned/visitor accessible (CBC 11A-1109A.5) | 5% of unassigned: 0.05 x 18 = 0.9, minimum 1 | **1 accessible visitor space** |

The 11B requirements (3 accessible, 1 van) typically govern the total count. The 11A requirements address how those spaces are distributed between assigned and unassigned parking.

### EV Parking

See [electrical-ev-charging.md](electrical-ev-charging.md) for the full 35-unit calculation. In summary: 35 EV receptacles at assigned spaces, plus 9 Level 2 chargers at unassigned/common spaces.

### Bicycle Parking

| Requirement | Calculation | Result |
|-------------|-------------|--------|
| Short-term (CALGreen 4.106.4.4.1) | ~30,000 sf / 10,000 = 3 (min 2) | **3 short-term spaces** |
| Long-term (CALGreen 4.106.4.4.2) | 35 units / 2 = 17.5, rounded up | **18 long-term spaces** (covered, lockable) |

### Transit Proximity Check

If the site is within 1/2 mile walking distance of a qualifying major transit stop (rail station, ferry terminal, or bus intersection with 15-minute peak frequency), then **no minimum parking may be required** by the city under AB 2097. The project may still voluntarily provide parking, in which case all accessible parking, EV, and bicycle requirements apply to whatever parking is actually built.
