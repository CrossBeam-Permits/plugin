# Building Envelope — Mandatory Energy Requirements (Multifamily)

Source: 2025 California Energy Code (Title 24, Part 6), Section 160.1 — Mandatory Requirements for Building Envelopes (Subchapter 10, Multifamily Buildings). Effective January 1, 2026.

Section 160.1 sets mandatory minimum envelope performance for all new multifamily buildings. These are absolute minimums — a project using the prescriptive path (Section 170.2) or the performance path must still meet every requirement below. The prescriptive path (Table 170.2-A) often imposes stricter values on top of these mandatory floors.

---

## Low-Rise vs. High-Rise Distinction

The Energy Code distinguishes multifamily buildings by habitable story count:

- **Low-rise (3 habitable stories or fewer):** Uses residential compliance procedures (Reference Residential Appendices RA2, RA3). Field verification by ECC-Rater. (CEC 160.2(b)2Aivb2)
- **High-rise (4 or more habitable stories):** Uses nonresidential compliance procedures (Reference Nonresidential Appendices NA1, NA2). Field verification may be by ECC-Rater or certified Mechanical Acceptance Test Technician (CMATT). (CEC 160.2(a)1)

This distinction affects testing protocols for air leakage, duct leakage, and ventilation verification, but the **mandatory envelope U-factors in Section 160.1 apply identically** regardless of building height.

## Section 160.1(a) — Roof and Ceiling Insulation

All opaque portions of ceilings and roofs separating conditioned space from unconditioned space or ambient air must comply.

### Attic Roofs (160.1(a)1)

- Area-weighted average U-factor shall not exceed **U-0.043**, OR
- Insulation between wood framing members with installed R-value of **R-22 or greater** (insulation alone) (CEC 160.1(a)1A)
- For **vented attics**: insulation at the ceiling level
- For **unvented attics**: insulation at either ceiling or roof level
- Attic access doors must have **permanently attached insulation** (adhesive or mechanical fasteners) and be **gasketed** to prevent air leakage (CEC 160.1(a)1B)
- Loose-fill insulation must meet manufacturer's installed design weight per sq ft at the labeled R-value (CEC 160.1(a)1C)

### Non-Attic Roofs (160.1(a)2)

| Framing Type | Maximum U-factor |
|--------------|-----------------|
| **Metal building** | U-0.098 |
| **Wood-framed and others** | U-0.075 |

(CEC 160.1(a)2A, 160.1(a)2B)

**Insulation placement rule:** When insulation is installed at the roof level, fixed vents or openings to the outdoors shall **not** be installed. The space between ceiling and roof becomes conditioned and is not treated as an attic for CBC attic ventilation purposes. (CEC 160.1(a)2C)

Exception: Vents designed for wind resistance of roof membranes (not penetrating the roof deck) are permitted. (Exception to CEC 160.1(a)2C)

### General Insulation Contact Requirement (160.1(a)3)

Insulation must be installed in **direct contact** with the roof or ceiling, which must be sealed to limit air infiltration/exfiltration per Section 110.7. Insulation may be placed above or below the roof deck, or on top of a drywall ceiling. (CEC 160.1(a)3)

## Section 160.1(b) — Wall Insulation

All opaque portions of above-grade walls separating conditioned space from unconditioned space or ambient air.

### Maximum U-factors by Wall Type

| Wall Type | Maximum U-factor |
|-----------|-----------------|
| **Metal building** | U-0.113 |
| **Metal framed** | U-0.151 |
| **Wood framed — 2x4** | U-0.095 |
| **Wood framed — 2x6** | U-0.069 |
| **Wood framed — other assemblies** | U-0.102 |
| **Light mass (6"+ hollow CMU)** | U-0.440 |
| **Heavy mass (8"+ hollow CMU)** | U-0.690 |
| **Spandrel panels / curtain wall** | U-0.280 |

(CEC 160.1(b)1–6)

### Demising Walls (160.1(b)7)

Opaque portions of framed demising walls (walls between dwelling units or between units and common areas):

| Framing | Maximum U-factor |
|---------|-----------------|
| **Wood framed** | U-0.099 |
| **Metal framed** | U-0.151 |

(CEC 160.1(b)7A, 160.1(b)7B)

### Bay/Bow Window Roofs and Floors (160.1(b)8)

Bay or bow window roofs and floors must be insulated to meet the wall insulation requirements of **Table 170.2-A** (prescriptive). (CEC 160.1(b)8)

## Section 160.1(c) — Floor and Soffit Insulation

Opaque portions of floors and soffits separating conditioned space from unconditioned space or ambient air.

| Floor Type | Maximum U-factor | Equivalent R-value |
|------------|-----------------|-------------------|
| **Raised mass floor** (metal deck) | U-0.269 (or 3" lightweight concrete over metal deck) | — |
| **Raised wood floor** | U-0.037 | R-19 or greater between framing |
| **Other floors** | U-0.071 | — |

(CEC 160.1(c)1–3)

**Heated slab on grade** must meet the requirements of Section 110.8(g). (CEC 160.1(c)4)

### Crawl Space Exception (Exception to 160.1(c))

A building with a controlled ventilation or unvented crawl space may **omit raised floor insulation** if ALL of the following are met:
1. Foundation walls insulated to meet wall minimums per Table 170.2-A (CEC 160.1(c), Exception A)
2. Class I or Class II vapor retarder over entire crawl space floor (CEC 160.1(c), Exception B)
3. Vents between crawl space and outdoors have automatically operated louvers (temperature-actuated) (CEC 160.1(c), Exception C)
4. Compliance with Reference Residential Appendix RA4.5.1 (CEC 160.1(c), Exception D)

---

## Section 160.1(d) — Vapor Retarder

- **All Climate Zones (1–16):** Unvented crawl space earth floors require a Class I or Class II vapor retarder. Also required for controlled ventilation crawl spaces using the floor insulation exception above. (CEC 160.1(d)1)
- **Climate Zones 14 and 16 only:** A Class I or Class II vapor retarder is required on the **conditioned space side** of all insulation in exterior walls, vented attics, and unvented attics with air-permeable insulation. (CEC 160.1(d)2)

**For CZ 8 (Buena Park):** Only the crawl space vapor retarder applies. No wall/attic vapor retarder is required.

## Section 160.1(e) — Fenestration (Windows, Skylights, Glazed Doors)

All fenestration separating conditioned space from unconditioned space or outdoors.

### U-factor Requirement

Maximum U-factor: **0.58** (CEC 160.1(e)1)

Compliance methods:
1. **Each individual product** has a U-factor not exceeding 0.58, OR
2. **Area-weighted average** of all fenestration (including skylights) does not exceed U-0.58 (CEC 160.1(e)2)

### Exceptions to U-factor Requirement

- **Exception 1:** Up to **0.5% of conditioned floor area** is not required to comply with the U-0.58 maximum (CEC 160.1(e)1, Exception 1)
- **Exception 2:** Dual-glazed greenhouse or garden windows — up to **30 sq ft per dwelling unit** exempt (CEC 160.1(e)1, Exception 2)
- **Wildfire zone exception:** Fenestration in buildings meeting Part 7 (California Wildland-Urban Interface Code) in Fire Hazard Severity Zones or WUI Fire Areas as designated by the local jurisdiction (Exception to CEC 160.1(e))

### SHGC Requirements

The mandatory Section 160.1 does **not** specify SHGC limits directly. SHGC requirements are established by the **prescriptive standards** in Table 170.2-A and vary by climate zone:

- **Climate Zone 8 (Buena Park):** SHGC requirements are moderate — coastal Southern California has milder cooling loads than inland zones
- **Inland CZs (10, 13, 14, 15):** Stricter SHGC limits due to high cooling demand
- **Coastal CZs (1–7):** More lenient SHGC limits

The performance path (Section 170.1) allows trading SHGC against other envelope or mechanical measures, but the mandatory U-0.58 maximum cannot be traded away.

## Section 160.1(f) — Fireplaces and Decorative Gas Appliances

If a masonry or factory-built fireplace is installed, it must comply with Section 110.5 and CALGreen Section 4.503, and must have:

1. **Closable metal or glass doors** covering the entire firebox opening (CEC 160.1(f)1)
2. **Outside combustion air intake** of at least 6 sq in with an operable, tight-fitting damper (CEC 160.1(f)2)
3. **Flue damper** with readily accessible control (CEC 160.1(f)3)

Exception to combustion air intake: Not required if the fireplace is over concrete slab and not on an exterior wall. (Exception to CEC 160.1(f)2)

Exception to flue damper: When a gas log/lighter is installed, the flue damper must be blocked open if required by CMC or manufacturer. (Exception to CEC 160.1(f)3)

---

## Section 160.1(g) — Slab Edge Insulation

Slab edge insulation specifications:

1. Water absorption rate not greater than **0.3%** per ASTM C272, Test Method A (24-hour immersion) (CEC 160.1(g)1)
2. Water vapor permeance not greater than **2.0 perm/inch** per ASTM C272 (CEC 160.1(g)2)
3. Must be protected from **physical damage and UV deterioration** (CEC 160.1(g)3)
4. Heated slab floors: insulation per Section 110.8(g) (CEC 160.1(g)4)

## Air Leakage and Compartmentalization Testing

Section 160.1 establishes the envelope; air leakage testing is required under the ventilation section (160.2) but is directly tied to envelope performance:

- **Compartmentalization:** Air leakage rate shall not exceed **0.3 cfm at 50 Pa per sq ft of dwelling unit envelope surface area** (CEC 160.2(b)2Aivb2)
- Confirmed by ECC-Rater field verification per Reference Appendix RA3.8 (low-rise) or NA2.3 (high-rise)
- For buildings with 4+ habitable stories, testing may alternatively be performed by a certified CMATT per NA1.9

### Ventilation System Requirement

All dwelling units in a multifamily building must use **balanced or supply ventilation** and pass compartmentalization testing. (CEC 160.2(b)2Aivb)

The whole-dwelling ventilation rate is calculated per Equation 160.2-B:

**Q_tot = 0.03 x A_floor + 7.5 x (N_br + 1)**

Where A_floor = dwelling unit floor area (sq ft), N_br = number of bedrooms (min 1).

## Climate Zone Dependency — California's 16 Climate Zones

The mandatory U-factors in Section 160.1 are **statewide minimums** — they do not vary by climate zone. However, climate zone affects:

1. **Prescriptive requirements (Table 170.2-A):** May require stricter U-factors beyond the mandatory minimum
2. **SHGC requirements:** Vary significantly by climate zone in Table 170.2-A
3. **Vapor retarder requirements:** CZ 14 and 16 require wall/attic vapor retarders (CEC 160.1(d)2)
4. **Defrost timer exceptions:** CZs 1, 6–10, 15, 16 exempt from 90-minute delay timer (CEC 160.3(b)7, Exception)
5. **Performance path calculations:** Outdoor design conditions and energy budgets are climate zone specific

### CZ 8 — Buena Park (Coastal Southern California)

For the 35-unit test case in Buena Park:
- **Mild heating, moderate cooling** — CZ 8 is the mild coastal zone in Orange/LA County
- **No wall/attic vapor retarder** required (only CZs 14, 16)
- Prescriptive SHGC from Table 170.2-A will be moderate
- The mandatory envelope minimums from Section 160.1 form the performance floor; the prescriptive path will likely push walls and roofs to higher performance levels
- Compartmentalization testing at **0.3 cfm/sq ft at 50 Pa** is required regardless of climate zone
