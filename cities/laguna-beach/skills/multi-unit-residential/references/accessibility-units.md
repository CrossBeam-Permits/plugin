## Accessibility — Covered Multifamily Dwelling Units

### When Chapter 11A Applies

CBC Chapter 11A governs housing accessibility for **newly constructed covered multifamily dwellings** — the key threshold is **3 or more dwelling units** (apartments) or **4 or more dwelling units** (condominiums). It applies to:

- Apartment buildings with 3+ dwelling units (CBC 1102A.1, Item 1)
- Condominiums with 4+ dwelling units (CBC 1102A.1, Item 2)
- Congregate residences with 3+ sleeping units (CBC 1102A.1, Item 4)
- Dormitories with 3+ guest rooms (CBC 1102A.1, Item 7)
- Shelters for homeless persons (CBC 1102A.1, Item 6)
- Efficiency dwelling units, 3+ units (CBC 1102A.1, Item 5)

Chapter 11A does **not** apply to alterations, repairs, or maintenance of multifamily dwellings constructed for first occupancy on or before March 13, 1991 (CBC 1102A.2). Additions are subject to Chapter 11A only if the addition alone meets the definition of a covered multifamily dwelling (CBC 1101A.1, Item 3).

**Public housing** triggers the more stringent Chapter 11B (DSA-AC) instead of — or in addition to — Chapter 11A (CBC 1102A.1, Item 10).

### CRC Townhouses — The Critical Exemption

Townhouse-style units built under the California Residential Code (CRC) are **not** covered multifamily dwellings when they meet all three CRC scoping criteria:

1. Each unit has a **separate means of egress** (independent exterior exit)
2. Not more than **3 stories** above grade plane
3. Classified as detached one-/two-family dwelling or townhouse

(CRC R101.2)

When there is **no elevator** and each unit has its own separate exterior entry directly to the outside, the building is not an "elevator building" and the individual townhouse units are not "covered multifamily dwellings" under Chapter 11A.

**However**, even for CRC townhouse projects, site-level accessibility requirements still apply: accessible parking (CBC 1109A), accessible routes to common-use areas (CBC 1110A), and common-area accessibility (CBC 1127A).

### Elevator Building vs Non-Elevator Building

This distinction is the single most important factor in determining accessibility scope:

**Non-elevator building** (CBC 1104A.1):
- Only **ground-floor dwelling units** must be adaptable and on an accessible route
- Upper-floor units are exempt from Chapter 11A
- Ground floor includes any floor above grade that contains dwelling units and is the first such floor (CBC 1104A.2)
- Site impracticality tests (CBC 1150A) may exempt even ground-floor units if terrain prevents an accessible route

**Elevator building** (CBC 1106A.1, Item 2):
- When an elevator provides access to dwelling units on floors other than the ground floor, it is an "elevator building"
- **All dwelling units in the building** become covered multifamily dwellings
- The elevator must provide accessibility to all dwelling units regardless of terrain slope
- For multistory units in elevator buildings, the floor served by the elevator is the primary entry floor (CBC 1102A.3.2)

**Important nuance**: An elevator provided *only* as a means of creating an accessible route to ground-floor units does **not** make the building an "elevator building" — only ground-floor units must comply (CBC 1106A.1, Item 2, Note).

### Multistory Dwelling Units — Percentage Requirements

When dwelling units span multiple stories within a building (e.g., two-story townhouse-style units inside a larger building), special counting rules apply:

**In non-elevator buildings** (CBC 1102A.3.1):
- At least **10 percent** (but not less than one) of all multistory ground-floor dwelling units must comply
- Compliance means: primary entry on accessible route, at least one bathroom on entry level, all rooms/spaces on entry level on accessible route and meeting Division IV standards
- The 10% is calculated from the total multistory units on the building site, rounded up to the next whole number
- Applies to permits submitted on or after July 1, 2005

**In elevator buildings** (CBC 1102A.3.2):
- **All** multistory units must comply on the elevator-served entry floor
- The elevator-served floor is the "ground floor" / primary entry level for each unit
- At least one bathroom and one kitchen must be on the primary entry level
- All rooms on the primary entry level must be accessible per Division IV

### Covered Dwelling Unit Requirements (Division IV)

All covered multifamily dwelling units must be "adaptable and accessible into and throughout the dwelling unit" (CBC 1128A.1). The specific requirements are organized by building element:

#### Accessible Route Within Units (CBC 1130A)

- An accessible route must pass through **all rooms and spaces** of the dwelling unit (CBC 1130A.1)
- Must pass through the primary entry door and connect all additional exterior doors, kitchen appliances, and bathroom fixtures
- Minimum width: **36 inches** throughout the unit (CBC 1130A.2)
- Exception: accessible route not required from interior to basement or garage (CBC 1130A.1, Exception)

#### Doors (CBC 1132A)

**Primary entry and exit doors:**
- Minimum 36-inch door, minimum 32-inch clear opening at 90 degrees (CBC 1126A.1)
- Maneuvering clearance: 44-inch minimum length on dwelling-unit side (CBC 1132A.5.1)
- 18-inch strike-edge clearance on swing side (CBC 1132A.5.2)

**Interior doors and secondary exterior doors:**
- Minimum 6 ft 8 in height (CBC 1132A.3, Item 1)
- Minimum 32-inch net clear opening width at 90 degrees (CBC 1132A.3, Item 2)
- Maneuvering clearance: 42-inch minimum length on both sides (CBC 1132A.5.1)
- Exception: 39-inch length acceptable when 34-inch clear opening is provided (CBC 1132A.5.1, Exception)

**Thresholds:**
- Primary entry/exit doors: max 1/2 inch (CBC 1132A.4.1)
- Secondary exterior doors including sliding tracks: max 3/4 inch (CBC 1132A.4.1)
- Interior door thresholds: max 1/2 inch (CBC 1132A.4.1)

**Hardware:**
- Lever-type, panic bars, push-pull — no ability to grasp required (CBC 1132A.8)
- Centered 30 to 44 inches above floor (CBC 1132A.8)
- Max operating force: 8.5 lbs exterior, 5 lbs interior (CBC 1132A.6)

**Door signal device** at every primary entrance — doorbell/buzzer mounted max 48 inches AFF (CBC 1132A.10)

#### Kitchens (CBC 1133A)

- Kitchen on accessible route (CBC 1133A.1)
- 30 x 48-inch clear floor space at range/cooktop (parallel approach), sink, all other appliances (parallel or forward) (CBC 1133A.2)
- U-shaped kitchens with parallel approach at base: 60-inch minimum clear width (CBC 1133A.2.1, Item 1)
- All other kitchen layouts: 48-inch minimum clear width (CBC 1133A.2.1, Item 3)
- Removable base cabinets under sinks and work surfaces for knee space (CBC 1133A.3)
- 30-inch minimum countertop for sink + 30-inch minimum for work surface (CBC 1133A.4)
- **Repositionable countertops** in at least **5 percent** of covered units — sinks/work surfaces adjustable to 28-inch height (CBC 1133A.4.1)
- Lower shelving/drawers no higher than 48 inches AFF (CBC 1133A.5)
- Lever-operated or push-type faucet controls, max 5 lbs force (CBC 1133A.6)
- Insulated water supply/drain pipes under sinks (CBC 1133A.7.1)

#### Bathrooms and Toilet Facilities (CBC 1134A)

Two compliance options:

**Option 1** — All bathrooms in the unit comply with full accessibility requirements (CBC 1134A.2, Option 1)

**Option 2** — Only one bathroom in the unit is fully accessible; all other bathrooms must still comply with accessible route, door clearance, maneuvering space, switch/outlet heights, and grab bar reinforcement requirements (CBC 1134A.2, Option 2)

**Key bathroom requirements (both options):**
- Sufficient maneuvering space for wheelchair entry, door closure, fixture use, and exit (CBC 1134A.4)
- Where door swings in: 30 x 48-inch clear maneuvering space outside door swing (CBC 1134A.4)
- Accessible route into and through the bathroom (CBC 1134A.2, Items 6/8)
- 18-inch minimum clear maneuvering space at strike edge of door (CBC 1134A.2, Items 8/10)

**Water closets** (CBC 1134A.7):
- 48-inch minimum clear width at fixture (36-inch acceptable adjacent to lavatories/walls extending max 24 inches deep)
- 36-inch clear depth past front edge
- Centerline 17 to 18 inches from grab bar wall; 18-inch minimum from non-grab-bar obstacles
- Reinforced walls for grab bars on at least one side (CBC 1134A.7, Item 2)
- Seat height: 15-inch minimum (CBC 1134A.7, Item 3)

**Bathtubs** (CBC 1134A.5):
- 48 x 30-inch minimum clear floor space parallel to tub side
- Reinforced walls for grab bars — back wall and both ends, 32 to 38 inches AFF (CBC 1134A.5, Item 2)
- Controls at foot end, operable with one hand, max 5 lbs (CBC 1134A.5, Item 3)

**Showers** (CBC 1134A.6):
- At least one accessible shower stall: 42 x 48-inch (36-inch opening), or 30 x 60-inch (60-inch opening), or 36 x 60-inch (36-inch opening) (CBC 1134A.6, Item 1)
- Max 2-inch threshold, beveled (CBC 1134A.6, Item 5)
- Continuous grab bar reinforcement at 32 to 38 inches AFF (CBC 1134A.6, Item 4)

**Powder rooms** (CBC 1134A.3):
- When the only toilet facility on an accessible level, must comply with full water closet and lavatory accessibility requirements
- Otherwise must comply with accessible route, door clearance, maneuvering space, switch heights, and grab bar reinforcement

#### Electrical, Switches, and Controls (CBC 1136A)

- Receptacles: 15-inch minimum to 48-inch maximum AFF (CBC 1136A.1)
- Switches and controls: 15-inch minimum to 48-inch maximum AFF (CBC 1136A.2)
- Over obstructions (e.g., kitchen base cabinets): within reach ranges per 1138A.3; obstructions max 25 inches from wall (CBC 1136A.1, 1136A.2)

#### Laundry Within Units (CBC 1135A)

- When washer/dryer provided in unit, one of each type shall be provided
- Where front-loading washers are not provided, management must supply assistive devices on request for top-loading washers (CBC 1135A.1)

### Interior Space Dimensions (CBC 1208) — Minimum Dimensions Affecting Unit Layout

These are baseline habitability requirements for all dwelling units (not accessibility-specific) but directly constrain accessible unit design:

**Room widths** (CBC 1208.1):
- Habitable spaces (other than kitchen): minimum **7 feet** in any plan dimension
- Kitchens: minimum **3-foot** clear passageway between counter fronts and opposing surfaces

**Ceiling heights** (CBC 1208.2):
- Habitable spaces and corridors: minimum **7 ft 6 in** (but corridors within dwelling units: 7 ft minimum per Exception 4)
- Bathrooms, kitchens, storage, laundry: minimum **7 ft**
- Sloped ceilings: required height in at least half the area; portions under 5 ft excluded from area calculations

**Dwelling unit size** (CBC 1208.3):
- Minimum **190 sq ft** of habitable space per dwelling unit

**Room area** (CBC 1208.4):
- At least one room: minimum **120 sq ft**
- All other habitable rooms: minimum **70 sq ft**
- Kitchens exempt from minimum area

### Common-Use Area Accessibility (CBC 1127A)

Even when individual units may be exempt (e.g., upper floors in non-elevator buildings), all **common-use areas** must be accessible:

- Lobbies, corridors, community rooms, fitness rooms, game rooms (CBC 1127A.1)
- Common-use toilet/bathing facilities: at least 1% but not less than one of each type accessible (CBC 1127A.2)
- Common-use laundry rooms: at least one of each appliance type accessible per room (CBC 1127A.10)
- Fixed seating/tables: 5% but not less than one accessible (CBC 1127A.12)
- Common-use storage: at least one of each type accessible (CBC 1127A.11)
- Interior accessible routes serving 10+ occupant load: 44-inch minimum width (CBC 1119A.3)
- Interior accessible routes serving fewer than 10: 36-inch minimum width (CBC 1119A.3)

### Applying the 35-Unit Test Case

**Scenario A: Townhouses with no elevator and separate exterior entries (CRC project)**
- Each unit has independent exterior egress — CRC governs individual units
- Chapter 11A **does not apply** to individual dwelling units (not "covered multifamily")
- No Type A or Type B unit requirements for individual units
- Site-level accessibility still required: accessible parking, accessible route to any common amenities, common area ADA compliance

**Scenario B: Apartment building with elevator (CBC R-2)**
- This is an "elevator building" — **all 35 units** are covered multifamily dwellings (CBC 1106A.1, Item 2)
- Every unit on every floor must be adaptable per Division IV (accessible routes, 32-inch door clearances, adaptable kitchen/bathroom, reinforced walls for grab bars, electrical within reach ranges)
- 5% of units (2 units minimum) must have repositionable kitchen countertops (CBC 1133A.4.1)
- For any multistory units within the building: the elevator-served floor is the primary entry and must have at least one bathroom and kitchen on that level (CBC 1102A.3.2)

**Scenario C: Non-elevator building (CBC R-2, walk-up apartments)**
- Only **ground-floor units** must be adaptable/accessible (CBC 1104A.1)
- If ground floor has, say, 12 of the 35 units — those 12 are covered; the remaining 23 on upper floors are exempt from Division IV
- If any multistory units exist on the ground floor: at least 10% of those (rounded up) must have accessible primary entry level (CBC 1102A.3.1)
- Site impracticality may exempt even ground-floor units if terrain tests under 1150A are met
