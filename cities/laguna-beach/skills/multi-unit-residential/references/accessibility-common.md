# Accessibility -- Common Area Requirements for Multi-Unit Residential

Source: 2025 California Building Code (Title 24, Part 2), Chapters 11A and 11B; effective January 1, 2026.

---

## Accessible Route Requirements

### Site Arrival Points (CBC 11B-206.2.1)
At least one accessible route shall be provided within the site from accessible parking spaces, accessible passenger drop-off and loading zones, public streets and sidewalks, and public transportation stops to the accessible building or facility entrance they serve. Where more than one route is provided, all routes must be accessible.

### Exterior Accessible Routes (CBC 11A-1110A.1)
When a building or portion of a building is required to be accessible or adaptable, an accessible route shall be provided to all portions of the building, accessible building entrances, and between the building and the public way. The accessible route shall be the most practical direct route and, to the maximum extent feasible, coincide with the route for the general public and building residents.

Exterior accessible routes shall provide:
1. At least one accessible route from public transportation stops, accessible parking, passenger loading zones, and public streets/sidewalks to the accessible building entrance they serve
2. At least one accessible route connecting accessible buildings, facilities, elements, and spaces on the same site
3. At least one accessible route connecting accessible building entrances with all accessible spaces, elements, and covered multifamily dwelling units
4. An accessible route connecting at least one accessible entrance of each covered multifamily dwelling unit with exterior spaces and facilities that serve the dwelling unit

### Within a Site (CBC 11B-206.2.2)
At least one accessible route shall connect accessible buildings, accessible facilities, accessible elements, and accessible spaces that are on the same site.

### Multistory Buildings (CBC 11B-206.2.3)
At least one accessible route shall connect each story and mezzanine in multistory buildings and facilities. In residential facilities, an accessible route is not required to connect stories where residential dwelling units with mobility features (CBC 11B-809.2 through 11B-809.4), residential dwelling units with adaptable features (CBC 11B-809.6 through 11B-809.12), all common use areas serving those units, and public use areas are on an accessible route.

### Accessible Route Components (CBC 11B-402.2)
Accessible routes shall consist of one or more of the following components: walking surfaces with a running slope not steeper than 1:20, doorways, ramps, curb ramps excluding the flared sides, elevators, and platform lifts.

## Path of Travel -- Walking Surfaces

### Slope (CBC 11B-403.3)
- **Running slope:** Not steeper than 1:20 (5%)
- **Cross slope:** Not steeper than 1:48 (2.083%)

### Clear Width (CBC 11B-403.5.1)
- **General:** 36 inches minimum
- **Corridors serving occupant load of 10 or more:** 44 inches minimum
- **Sidewalks and walks:** 48 inches minimum (may be reduced to 36 inches when right-of-way restrictions or existing conditions create unreasonable hardship)
- **Aisles serving elements on one side:** 36 inches minimum
- **Aisles serving elements on both sides:** 44 inches minimum

### Walks and Sidewalks in Housing (CBC 11A-1113A)
Walks and sidewalks shall have a continuous common surface, not interrupted by steps or abrupt changes in level exceeding 1/2 inch (CBC 11A-1113A.1). Width shall be 48 inches minimum, except walks serving an individual dwelling unit in covered multifamily buildings may be reduced to 36 inches clear width except at doors (CBC 11A-1113A.1.1). Surface cross slopes shall not exceed 1:48 (2.083%) (CBC 11A-1113A.1.2).

When the slope in the direction of travel exceeds 1:20 (5%), the walk must comply with ramp provisions (CBC 11A-1113A.3).

### Changes in Level (CBC 11B-303)
- **Up to 1/4 inch:** Permitted to be vertical without edge treatment (CBC 11B-303.2)
- **Between 1/4 inch and 1/2 inch:** Must be beveled with slope not steeper than 1:2 (CBC 11B-303.3)
- **Greater than 1/2 inch:** Must be ramped per CBC 11B-405 or 11B-406 (CBC 11B-303.4)

### Passing Spaces (CBC 11B-403.5.3)
An accessible route with clear width less than 60 inches shall provide passing spaces at intervals of 200 feet maximum. Passing spaces shall be 60 inches minimum by 60 inches minimum, or a T-shaped intersection.

### Continuous Gradient (CBC 11B-403.7)
All walks with continuous gradients shall have resting areas 60 inches in length at intervals of 400 feet maximum. The resting area shall be at least as wide as the walk, with slope of 1:48 maximum in all directions.

## Ramps (CBC 11B-405)

| Element | Requirement |
|---------|-------------|
| Running slope | 1:12 (8.33%) maximum |
| Cross slope | 1:48 maximum |
| Clear width | 48 inches minimum (36 inches minimum between handrails in residential uses serving occupant load of 50 or less) |
| Rise per run | 30 inches maximum |
| Top landing | 60 inches wide minimum, 60 inches long minimum |
| Bottom landing | 72 inches minimum in direction of ramp run |
| Handrails | Required per CBC 11B-505 (exceptions: curb ramps; at door landings, ramps less than 6 inches rise or 72 inches length) |
| Edge protection | Curb or barrier preventing passage of 4-inch sphere within 4 inches of floor |

## Accessible Parking

### Minimum Number of Accessible Spaces -- Table 11B-208.2

| Total Parking Spaces Provided | Minimum Accessible Spaces Required |
|-------------------------------|-----------------------------------|
| 1 to 25 | 1 |
| 26 to 50 | 2 |
| 51 to 75 | 3 |
| 76 to 100 | 4 |
| 101 to 150 | 5 |
| 151 to 200 | 6 |
| 201 to 300 | 7 |
| 301 to 400 | 8 |
| 401 to 500 | 9 |
| 501 to 1000 | 2% of total |
| 1001 and over | 20, plus 1 per each 100 (or fraction) over 1000 |

### Residential Facility Parking Rules (CBC 11B-208.2.3)
- **Parking for residents:** Where at least one parking space is provided per dwelling unit, at least one accessible space shall be provided for each unit required to have mobility features (CBC 11B-809.2 through 11B-809.4). Where fewer than one space per unit is provided, use Table 11B-208.2 (CBC 11B-208.2.3.1).
- **Additional resident parking:** Where total parking exceeds one space per unit, 2% (but no fewer than one) of the excess parking shall be accessible (CBC 11B-208.2.3.2).
- **Guest/employee/non-resident parking:** Use Table 11B-208.2 (CBC 11B-208.2.3.3).

### Housing Accessibility Parking (CBC 11A-1109A)
- **Assigned parking:** At least 2% of assigned parking spaces serving covered multifamily dwelling units shall be accessible in each type of parking facility; at least one space of each type even if the total exceeds 2% (CBC 11A-1109A.4).
- **Unassigned and visitor parking:** At least 5% shall be accessible and provide access to grade-level entrances of covered multifamily dwellings and facilities (pools, clubhouses, recreation areas, laundry rooms) (CBC 11A-1109A.5).

### Van-Accessible Spaces (CBC 11B-208.2.4)
For every 6 or fraction of 6 accessible parking spaces required, at least 1 shall be a van parking space complying with CBC 11B-502.

Under CBC 11A-1109A.8.6, one in every 8 accessible spaces (but not less than one) shall be van accessible.

### Accessible Parking Space Dimensions (CBC 11B-502)
- **Car spaces:** 108 inches (9 feet) wide minimum, 216 inches (18 feet) long minimum
- **Van spaces:** 144 inches (12 feet) wide minimum (or 108 inches wide with 96-inch access aisle), 216 inches long minimum
- **Access aisle:** 60 inches (5 feet) wide minimum, extending full length of parking space
- **Access aisle for van spaces:** Located on passenger side of vehicle
- **Vertical clearance:** 98 inches (8 feet 2 inches) minimum for parking spaces, access aisles, and vehicular routes
- **Surface slope:** 1:48 maximum in any direction; changes in level and detectable warnings not permitted (CBC 11B-502.4)

### Parking Location (CBC 11B-208.3)
Accessible parking spaces shall be located on the shortest accessible route from parking to an entrance complying with CBC 11B-206.4. In residential facilities, parking for mobility-feature units shall be located on the shortest accessible route to the dwelling unit entrance they serve (CBC 11B-208.3.2).

Accessible parking spaces shall not require persons with disabilities to wheel or walk behind parked cars other than their own (CBC 11A-1109A.7).

### Parking Identification (CBC 11B-502.6)
- International Symbol of Accessibility sign: white on blue background, 70 square inches minimum, posted 60 inches minimum above ground (80 inches minimum within circulation paths)
- Van spaces: additional "Van Accessible" designation
- "Minimum Fine $250" below the symbol
- Surface marking: International Symbol of Accessibility, 36 inches by 36 inches minimum, in white on blue background
- Access aisle: blue painted borderline perimeter with hatched lines max 36 inches on center, "NO PARKING" in white letters 12 inches high minimum

**Residential exception:** Where parking spaces are assigned to specific dwelling units, identification signs are not required (CBC 11B-216.5.1, Exception 2).

### Additional Tow-Away Sign (CBC 11B-502.8)
An additional sign (17 inches by 22 inches minimum, 1-inch lettering minimum) shall be posted at each entrance to off-street parking or immediately adjacent to accessible parking, stating unauthorized vehicles will be towed at owner's expense with reclaim location and phone number.

## Common Area Accessibility (CBC 11A-1127A)

### General Requirement (CBC 11A-1127A.1)
When provided, common use areas and facilities in covered multifamily housing developments shall be accessible to persons with disabilities. Common use facilities include, but are not limited to:
- **Lobbies**
- **Toilet and bathing facilities**
- **Laundry facilities**
- **Community rooms and clubhouses**
- **Health and fitness facilities**
- **Game rooms**
- **Common use tenant storage**

All entrances, doors, fixtures, and controls shall be on an accessible route. Facilities and fixtures required to be accessible shall comply with CBC 11A-1127A provisions for doors, clear floor space, water closets, lavatories, controls, bathing/shower facilities, and toilet facilities.

### Laundry Rooms (CBC 11A-1127A.10)
Where common use laundry rooms are provided, at least one of each type of appliance in each laundry area shall be accessible and on an accessible route. Appliances include washers, dryers, soap dispensers, wash sinks, tables, and storage areas.

- Clear floor space: 30 inches by 48 inches minimum in front of accessible washers and dryers
- Washing machines and dryers: must be front loading; bottom of laundry compartment opening 15 inches minimum and 36 inches maximum above finish floor (CBC 11A-1127A.10.4)
- Controls: operable with one hand, no tight grasping/pinching/twisting, 5 lbs force maximum, located 15 to 48 inches above finish floor (CBC 11A-1127A.10.3)
- Where laundry rooms are on floors of an elevator building, each laundry room shall be accessible. Where one laundry room is on the ground floor of each building, each shall be accessible.

### Storage (CBC 11A-1127A.11)
At least one of each type of fixed storage facility (cabinets, shelves, closets, drawers) shall be accessible, with 30-by-48-inch clear floor space and reach ranges per CBC 11A-1138A.3.

### Fixed Seating, Tables, and Counters (CBC 11A-1127A.12)
Where provided for residents or guests, 5% but not less than one shall be accessible. Table/counter tops shall be 28 to 34 inches above finish floor. Clear floor space and knee/toe clearance per CBC 11A-1138A.

### Common Use Areas in Public Housing (CBC 11B-248)
Common use areas shall comply with Chapter 11B. This encompasses all areas shared by building occupants: corridors, lobbies, mail rooms, trash enclosures, recreation areas, pools, and community spaces.

## Stairways in Accessible Buildings (CBC 11B-504)

| Element | Requirement |
|---------|-------------|
| Riser height | 4 inches minimum, 7 inches maximum |
| Tread depth | 11 inches minimum |
| Open risers | Not permitted (exterior: 1/2-inch gap allowed at base) |
| Tread surface | Comply with CBC 11B-302; no changes in level or slopes exceeding 1:48 |
| Contrasting stripe | Interior: upper approach and lower tread; Exterior: upper approach and all treads. Stripe 2 to 4 inches wide, within 1 inch of nosing, full width of step. |
| Nosings | 1/2-inch radius maximum at leading edge; projections 1-1/4 inches maximum over tread below |
| Handrails | Required per CBC 11B-505 |
| Floor identification | Signs at each landing in enclosed stairways in buildings 2+ stories; raised characters per CBC 11B-703; star at exit discharge level |
| Wet conditions | Treads and landings designed to prevent water accumulation |

## Signage

### Exterior Accessible Route Signs (CBC 11A-1110A.2)
At every primary public entrance and at every major junction where the accessible route diverges from the circulation path, a sign displaying the International Symbol of Accessibility shall be provided. Signs shall indicate direction to accessible building entrances and facilities and comply with CBC 11A-1143A.

### Accessible Parking Signs (CBC 11B-216.5)
Parking spaces complying with CBC 11B-502 shall be identified by signs complying with CBC 11B-502.6 and 11B-502.8.

**Residential exception:** Where parking spaces are assigned to specific residential dwelling units, identification of accessible parking spaces is not required (CBC 11B-216.5.1, Exception 2).

### Entrances (CBC 11B-216.6)
In existing buildings where not all entrances are accessible, accessible entrances shall be identified by the International Symbol of Accessibility. Directional signs to the nearest accessible entrance shall be provided at non-accessible entrances.

### Toilet Rooms and Bathing Rooms (CBC 11B-216.8)
Accessible toilet/bathing rooms shall be identified by the International Symbol of Accessibility. Directional signs to nearest accessible facilities shall be provided where existing facilities do not comply. Geometric symbols (triangle for men's, circle for women's, combined for unisex) shall be provided at entrances per CBC 11B-216.8.1 and CBC 11A-1127A.7.2.

### Common Use Facility Signs (CBC 11A-1143A)
Signs identifying permanent rooms and spaces shall comply with visual character, raised character, and Braille requirements. Visual characters: non-glare finish, contrasting background, conventional (not decorative) form, minimum heights per Table 1143A.5. Raised characters: 1/32-inch minimum above background, sans serif uppercase, 5/8-inch minimum to 2-inch maximum height.

## Covered Multifamily Dwellings (CBC 11A-1104A)

### Ground Floor Units
All ground-floor dwelling units in non-elevator buildings shall be adaptable and on an accessible route, unless site impracticality provisions apply (CBC 11A-1150A). Where the first floor containing dwelling units is above grade, all units on that floor are considered covered multifamily dwelling units.

### Elevator Buildings (CBC 11B-233.3.1.2.1)
A residential building with one or more elevators shall have an elevator serve all floors with a residential dwelling unit primary entrance and all floors with common use areas. Dwelling units on floors served by an elevator shall be adaptable.

### Mobility Feature Units (CBC 11B-233.3.1.1)
At least 5% (but no fewer than one) of the total residential dwelling units shall provide mobility features per CBC 11B-809.1 through 11B-809.4 and be on an accessible route.

### Adaptable Units (CBC 11B-233.3.1.2)
In buildings with 3 or more residential dwelling units, adaptable units shall be provided on accessible routes per CBC 11B-809.6. The number of required adaptable units is reduced by the number of mobility-feature units.

### Communication Features (CBC 11B-233.3.1.3)
At least 2% (but no fewer than one) of the total residential dwelling units shall provide communication features per CBC 11B-809.5.

### Dispersion (CBC 11B-233.3.5)
Mobility-feature and communication-feature units shall be dispersed among the various types of dwelling units in the facility and provide comparable choices integrated with those available to other residents.

## 35-Unit Test Case Application

For a 35-unit multi-unit residential project, the following accessibility requirements apply:

### Parking
- Assume approximately 52 parking spaces (1.5 spaces per unit): **3 accessible spaces** required per Table 11B-208.2 (51-75 range)
- **1 van-accessible space** required (1 per every 6 or fraction of 6 accessible spaces)
- Accessible spaces located on shortest accessible route to building entrance
- 98-inch minimum vertical clearance for accessible spaces and vehicular routes

### Accessible Units
- **Mobility feature units:** At least 5% of 35 = 2 units minimum (CBC 11B-233.3.1.1)
- **Adaptable units:** All ground-floor units in non-elevator buildings; all units on elevator-served floors in elevator buildings (minus the 2 mobility-feature units)
- **Communication feature units:** At least 2% of 35 = 1 unit minimum (CBC 11B-233.3.1.3)

### Accessible Route
- At least one accessible route from public sidewalk, public transportation stops, and accessible parking to building entrance
- Sidewalks/walks: 48 inches wide minimum, 1:20 running slope maximum, 1:48 cross slope maximum
- No abrupt changes in level exceeding 1/2 inch on walking surfaces
- Route connects building entrance to all common use areas and to accessible/adaptable dwelling units
- Where route exceeds 200 feet, comply with passing space requirements (CBC 11A-1110A.4)

### Common Areas
- Lobby, mail room, laundry room, community room, trash enclosure, and all other common use areas must be on an accessible route
- At least one of each type of laundry appliance accessible and front-loading
- 5% (but not less than one) of fixed seating/tables accessible
- All fixtures and controls operable with one hand, max 5 lbs force, within reach range
- Common use toilet/bathing facilities: at least 1% of fixtures (minimum one of each type) accessible

### Signage
- International Symbol of Accessibility at primary public entrance and at junctions where accessible route diverges from circulation path
- Accessible parking identification (unless spaces are assigned to specific units)
- Geometric symbols at common use toilet/bathing room doors
- Directional signs to accessible features where path diverges
