# Laguna Beach R-2 / R-3 Zoning Envelope

Use for ground-up multi-family (3+ units) zoning compliance. For ADU additions to existing multi-family lots, use Chapter 25.17 (Urgency Ord. 1713, LBMC 25.17.010(d)) via the ADU skill — NOT Ch. 25.14.

## R-3 Residential High Density (Ch. 25.14)

| Standard | Value | Source |
|----------|-------|--------|
| **Density (base)** | **1 dwelling unit / 2,000 sf net lot area** (per dwelling unit, guest house, or guest room) | §25.14.008 (Density and Bulk Standards (B)(1)) |
| **Maximum height** | **30 ft on slopes of 0-15%, rising 1 ft per slope percent to 35 ft at 20% and over**, measured from finished or natural grade, whichever is more restrictive. A second table limits height above the rear lot line or curb (30 / 25 / 20 ft by slope). The citywide 36 ft ceiling (§25.51.010) is never the R-3 limit | §25.14.008(D)(1)-(2) |
| **Front yard** | **≥ 20 ft** | §25.14.008(C)(2) |
| **Side yards** | **10% of average lot width, max 12 ft, min 4 ft** (each side) | §25.14.008(C)(3) |
| **Rear yard** | **≥ 10 ft** | §25.14.008(C)(4) |
| **Open space** | Required for each dwelling unit, guest house, and guest room per §25.50.010, **+50 sf per attached dwelling unit, guest house, or guest room** | §25.14.008(C)(6) |
| **Subterranean parking** | Permitted to encroach into yards: within 5 ft of front PL and 10 ft of curb face, provided structure does not project above adjacent natural grade | §25.14.008(C)(9) |
| **Cross-lot building** | Conditional Use Permit available from board of adjustment to build across or attach at common lot line, provided total units do not exceed what could be built on each lot separately | §25.14.008(B)(2) |
| **Design Review** | "The provisions of Section 25.05.040 shall apply" | §25.14.008(H) |

**Density math example:**
- 12,000 sf net lot = 12,000 / 2,000 = **6 units max** by-right under base R-3 density.
- State Density Bonus Law (Gov. Code §65915) can stack additional bonus % on top of base entitlement; AB 1287 (2023) allows up to 100% stacked bonus for very-low + moderate-income projects.

## R-2 Residential Medium Density (Ch. 25.12)

| Standard | Value | Source |
|----------|-------|--------|
| **Density (base)** | 2,000 sf of net lot area per dwelling unit, guest house or guest room (more where the zoning map adds a number to the zone symbol) | §25.12.008(B) |
| **Minimum lot** | 6,000 sf; 70 ft wide; 80 ft deep | §25.12.008(A) |
| **Maximum height** | **25 ft on slopes of 0-15%, rising 1 ft per slope percent to 30 ft at 20% and over**; second table limits height above the rear lot line or curb (25 / 20 / 15 ft, or 25 / 22.5 / 20 ft) | §25.12.008(D)(1)-(2) |
| **Front yard** | At least 20 ft | §25.12.008(C)(2) |
| **Side / rear yards** | Sides 10% of average lot width, max 12 ft, min 4 ft; rear at least 10 ft | §25.12.008(C)(3)-(4) |
| **Open space** | Per dwelling unit, guest house and guest room under §25.50.010 on lots with more than one unit | §25.12.008(C)(6) |
| **Lot coverage** | Only for a lot with one single-family dwelling (§25.10.008(E)(1)(c)) | §25.12.008(C)(9) |
| **Design Review** | Required for two-family construction; Administrative Design Review threshold typically lower than R-3 | Ch. 25.05 |

R-2 is two-family (duplex) — only triggers this multi-unit skill if combined with state-preempted streamlined paths that stack additional units (e.g., SB 9 duplex + ADU stack, or Density Bonus). For straight two-family construction, route through `../../single-family-residential/SKILL.md` because R-2 development is treated essentially as SFR + accessory unit at the building-code level.

## Citywide 36 ft Ceiling (§25.51.010)

"Notwithstanding any section to the contrary, no building shall exceed thirty-six feet in height" (§25.51.010). It is a ceiling over every zone, not a zone's height limit. Apply the zone table first: R-2 tops out at 30 ft and R-3 at 35 ft, so in those zones the zone table always controls. The 36 ft ceiling matters only where a zone or an exception allows more (for example C-1, LBP and CH-M at 36 ft, or the DSP design-excellence exception).

Ways past the zone limit or the ceiling:

1. **State Density Bonus Law waivers** (Gov. Code §65915(e)). See `state-preemption.md`.
2. **Specific plan exceptions.** The DSP sets its own, much lower general limit (12 ft and 1 floor). See `dsp-mixed-use.md`.

**Views:** the reviewing authority weighs view equity under the Design Review criteria (§25.05.040) even when a project meets the numeric limit. Apply the current staking provisions when staking is required for the hearing.

## Design Review (§25.05.040)

- **Hearing body for new multi-unit buildings:** Planning Commission under §25.05.040(F). The new building is a Design Review category under §25.05.040(B)(1)(b); other scopes must be classified against the current closed lists.
- **Schedule:** Verify the current Planning Commission hearing calendar; do not use the Design Review Board schedule.
- **Noticing:** 21 calendar days for Design Review hearings (§25.05.065(D)).
- **Appeals:** 14 calendar days to City Council (§25.05.070(B)(2)).
- **Pre-DRB submittal:** massing study, story poles where required, materials/color board.
- **Findings:** both §25.05.040(I) findings, applying the 11 current criteria: Access; Design Articulation; Environmental Context; General Plan Compliance; Historic Preservation; Landscaping; Lighting and Glare; Neighborhood Compatibility; Pedestrian Orientation; Privacy; and View Equity.
- **Fire Safety Standards Review** BEFORE design-review action for new construction and major remodels: Fire Department or Building Division, as applicable (§25.05.040(C)(4)).
