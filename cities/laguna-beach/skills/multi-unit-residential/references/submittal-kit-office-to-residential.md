# Planning Submittal Kit — Adding Apartments to a Commercial Building

Use this file to tell an owner or designer what to put in the **Planning** submittal for converting commercial floor area (office, retail) into apartments, or adding apartments to a commercial building, in C-N, C-1 or SLV. For LBP, CH-M, M-1 and DSP sites, start from the same list and add the zone's use permit (see `mixed-use-conversion.md` Section 2). The building permit comes later and is not covered here.

Sources: the City's July-August 2026 application forms as encoded in a CrossBeam reference that is not included in this plugin (coastal-development-permit.md, design-review.md, zone-clearance.md, conditional-use-permit.md) and a CrossBeam reference that is not included in this plugin; the Plan Sheet Submittal Matrix (a CrossBeam reference that is not included in this plugin, read-only). Code rules are in `mixed-use-conversion.md`. City practice is in a CrossBeam reference that is not included in this plugin.

## 1. Which matrix row — UNSETTLED

The City's Plan Sheet Submittal Matrix has no row for a commercial-to-residential conversion. Two rows could fit:

- **"Tenant Improvement"** (ministerial heading): App. Forms, Title Sheet, Site Plan, Floor Plans, with elevations or sections "if req."
- **"New Multi-family (2+ units)"** (Design Review Board or Planning Commission heading): all 16 columns.

**This kit builds to the "New Multi-family (2+ units)" row.** That is the conservative choice: it is the superset, and it matches how the City treats added units (intensity of use, PC hearing body for three or more families under §25.05.040(F)(1)). Staff may accept less. **UNSETTLED; confirm the row with Planning at pre-application.** Staff also note on every matrix: "Staff may request additional items after review of an initial submittal."

## 2. Pick the path first

| Path | Fits when | Planning applications |
|---|---|---|
| **A. Interior only** | No exterior change; not a major remodel; no floor area added | CDP. Plus a Zone Clearance application unless Planning says the CDP alone is enough (UNSETTLED; the Zone Clearance form is for projects that "DO NOT require a discretionary planning entitlement, such as Design Review or a Coastal Development Permit") |
| **B. Exterior work** | New windows, egress openings, stairs, decks, railings, roof changes or added floor area | CDP plus Design Review if any §25.05.040(B)(1) item applies: major remodel (B)(1)(a), "Any improvement that exceeds a height of 15 feet" (B)(1)(c) (for work on an existing upper floor, apply the guide's Step 4f City practice first), commercial parking and loading changes (B)(1)(l). Otherwise Zone Clearance under (B)(2)(a) |

Both paths: a **Coastal Development Permit** is required because adding units changes the intensity of use (§25.07.008(A)(2)(b)). Path A usually fits an administrative CDP (§25.07.013) when the CDP is the only discretionary permit. Path B with Design Review goes to the Planning Commission (§25.05.040(F)(1)(ii)).

Measure major remodel before choosing the path (`../../residential-remodel/references/city-major-remodel-methodology.md`). Crossing it moves the project to path B and turns off nonconforming protection (§25.56.011), except the §25.96.030(C)(3) relief for a major remodel caused only by a residential floor-area addition.

## 4. Plan sheets (New Multi-family row)

| Matrix column | What to show for a conversion | Path A note |
|---|---|---|
| Title Sheet | Project description naming existing and proposed uses by floor, unit count and size; zoning data table; parking table summary | Required |
| Survey | Topographic and boundary survey | Physically unchanged site; ask Planning to waive at pre-application. If a survey is filed, a title report dated within one year is also required |
| Site Plan | Parking layout with spaces assigned to units, bike and trash storage, access to the units, transit stop walking route if claiming 0.5 per unit | Required |
| Staking Plan | Only with exterior massing change | Not applicable: no exterior change |
| Roof Plan | Roof, equipment, any roof deck | Show existing; no change |
| Floor Plans | Existing and proposed, every level; label each room and unit; show unit gross floor area | Required |
| Elevation Views | Existing and proposed; new openings | Show existing; note "no exterior change" |
| Cross Sections | Floor-to-floor and overall height; existing floor count against the zone limit (C-N: 30 ft and two floors, §25.19.008(E)(2)) | Required where the building is near or over the height or floor limit |
| Floor Area Calcs | Existing and proposed floor area by use and floor; each unit's GFA (700 sf line for parking and the setback waiver); major-remodel worksheet | Required |
| Open Space Calcs | 25% of nonresidential GFA (C-N §25.19.008(B)(5)); new nonresidential base after conversion | Required |
| Grading & Drainage | Only when over 20 cubic yards | Not applicable |
| Landscape Plan | Required with any improvement over 15 ft (§25.05.040(B)(1)(c)) or landscape work | Not applicable unless landscaping changes |
| Lighting Plan | Same trigger as landscape; parking-area lighting | Not applicable unless lighting changes |
| Fire Dept. Site Access Plan | Fire access to the residential floor | Ask Fire at pre-application |

Under the row choice, a sheet marked "not applicable" still appears in the matrix. File a short sheet or note that says why it does not apply, or get a waiver in writing at pre-application.

## 5. Supporting documents for a conversion

These carry the case. Staff asked for each of them on recent projects (see a CrossBeam reference that is not included in this plugin).

1. **Parking table for every use on the site.** Existing and proposed uses, floor area, the §25.52.012(G) ratio for each, spaces provided, and the prior legal use's demand. Show that office at 1 per 250 sf needs more than the units (§25.52.004(E)(1) is triggered only by a change to a use with a greater requirement). State any credit claimed under §25.52.004(E)(1)(a)-(d). If claiming 0.5 per unit, show the walking route and distance to the fixed transit stop.
2. **Nonconformity statement.** List each legal nonconformity (height, floors, parking, open space, setbacks) with its basis (original permit, Real Property Report). For an over-height floor, add a structural engineer's letter that the work does not trigger the rebuild-to-conform rule of §25.56.009.
3. **Major-remodel calculation** using the October 2026 Quick Guide method for the site's coastal area.
4. **§25.96.030 exceptions**, if used: name each one and state that the owner will record the covenant against short-term lodging before building permit (§25.96.030(C)).
5. **Code analysis summary.** Change of occupancy from office to R-2, occupancy separation, exits, accessibility. Staff asked for these items at entitlement on 1391, 670 and 2940. The building-code rules are in the California Multi-Unit Residential rules (Change of Occupancy — Office (B) to R-2 Apartments), which come from the CrossBeam connector (see "California Code Compliance" in `../SKILL.md`).
6. **Fire Protection Plan** when the site is in the Very High Fire Hazard Severity Zone (CDP application, item n; LBMC 15.01.010 and 14.70.045).
7. **Tenant Mix Table** and **Hazardous Waste & Substances Form (Exhibit K)**: required by the CUP form only. Without a CUP, include them as advisory for a multi-tenant mixed-use site; staff asked for the Tenant Mix Table on that project and marked the Hazardous Waste Form at 670.
8. **Before a hearing** (path B or a requested CDP hearing): property-owner noticing at least 30 days before the hearing; early neighbor communication within 300 ft for Design Review (§25.05.040(C)(1)).

## 6. Not required, and why

| Item | Why not |
|---|---|
| ADU application | No existing dwellings to convert (Gov. Code §66323(a)(3)); the units are ordinary apartments. See the California ADU (Accessory Dwelling Unit) rules (General Zoning Rules), which come from the CrossBeam connector (see "California Code Compliance" in `../../adu/SKILL.md`) |
| One application per unit | One project covers all units |
| SB 330 preliminary application | Optional. A mixed-use project is a "housing development project" only with at least two-thirds of floor area residential (Gov. Code 65589.5(h)(2)) |
| Variance | Only if the design breaks a standard no exception covers |
| Inclusionary housing plan | Under 10 units (§25.96.070(C)) |
| Traffic study | City trigger is "subdivisions, new commercial developments, and major commercial renovations." Not expected for a small interior conversion |
| Parking study | Not for a residential project that falls short of parking (application note); needed for 4 or more in-lieu certificates (§25.52.006(E)) or a CUP parking request |
| WQMP | Only a new home or major remodel on a slope over 25% |
| Coastal hazards study, geotechnical study | Oceanfront, bluff or vacant sites only |
| Colored elevations, 3D renderings | Only for additions, new development or an LCP major remodel |

## 7. Worked example: three-story C-N office building, top floor to units

Facts as reported from site facts and the City archive (verify from plans): C-N zone, 8,400 sf lot, coastal zone outside the appeal area, Very High Fire Hazard Severity Zone; a three-story office building finaled in 1984 with 16 on-site spaces of record. Proposal: three or four units of about 300-350 sf on the top floor; ground-floor commercial stays.

- **Use:** permitted under §25.19.002(DD) (mixed-use site, above the ground floor).
- **CDP:** required; administrative CDP possible on path A.
- **Parking:** 3 units at 0.5 = 1.5, rounded to 2 (§25.52.012(D)), if the transit walk is a quarter mile or less. Four units = 2. Office floor area leaving at 1 per 250 sf lowers demand.
- **Key documents:** the parking table; a nonconformity statement for the third floor against the C-N two-floor limit, with the engineer's §25.56.009 letter; the Fire Protection Plan; the code analysis for B to R-2 and exits from the third floor.
- **Ask at pre-application:**
  - the matrix row;
  - Zone Clearance plus CDP, or CDP alone;
  - assigning 2 of the 16 spaces to the units;
  - legal nonconforming status of the third floor;
  - the City Attorney deposit.
