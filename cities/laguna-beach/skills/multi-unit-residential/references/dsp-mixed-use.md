# Downtown Specific Plan — Housing over Commercial

Use when a project adds dwelling units inside the Downtown Specific Plan (DSP) boundary, usually upper-floor units over ground-floor retail, restaurant or office. Inside the DSP, the DSP districts govern, not Title 25 R-3 and not the Ord. 1712 commercial-zone rules (`mixed-use-conversion.md`). Where the DSP is silent, the rest of the LBMC applies (§25.40.010(A)).

Sources:
- LBMC Ch. 25.40 (corpus, codified through 2026-04-28). §25.40.030: "All development regulations for the downtown specific plan area are contained within the text of the Downtown Specific Plan."
- DSP Chapter 5 extract: the City source document (not included in this plugin) (Downtown Specific Plan, amended February 15, 2022, Coastal Commission certified). The extract is partly paraphrased. Quote the certified PDF before relying on exact wording.
- Chapter 4 extract: the City source document (not included in this plugin).

## 1. Phase II is not adopted

The DSP Phase II Update is a proposal, not law. Do not apply it.

- The Notice of Preparation for its EIR was issued 2026-01-27 (SCH 2026010840). The scoping meeting was 2026-02-04 (City website, the City source document (not included in this plugin)).
- It "generally proposes amendments to several housing-related provisions, including increased building height, new density standards, provisions for lot merger regulations, and revisions to the Urban Design Guidelines, permitted uses, and parking requirements" (City CEQA page, `planning--ceqa.md`).
- On 2026-07-21, staff asked the City Council to pause it pending the General Plan update and the 7th-cycle Housing Element (City record, staff report of that date; not in the repo).

The rules below are the certified 2022 DSP as amended by the Phase I update. Phase I was adopted in 2020 and addressed business permitting and parking standards (City website, same page).

| Multiple-Family Residential | AUP | AUP | AUP | AUP | P | -- | AUP | AUP |
| Artists' Work/Live Units | P | P | P | P | -- | -- | P | P |

Key: P permitted; AUP Administrative Use Permit (§25.05.020); CUP Conditional Use Permit (§25.05.030); "--" not allowed.

**Conflict, CBD VC (UNSETTLED).** The corpus extract shows multifamily as AUP in CBD VC. City notes on one recent project (2026), citing the certified DSP Table 5-1, record "Residential" and "Mixed-Use with Residential on Second Floor" as **CUP** in CBD VC. Until the certified PDF is checked, tell the user a use permit is required and that its level (AUP or CUP) must be confirmed with Planning at pre-application. Changes to DSP permit levels need an LCP amendment (§25.40.010(B)).

## 3. Development limits

| Item | DSP rule | Authority |
|---|---|---|
| **Height** | "shall not exceed 12 feet and 1 floor." Roof, chimney, vents, equipment, elevator shafts and stairways may add up to 6 ft "if approved by the design review authority." Compensatory height allowed where the first habitable level must be raised above the floodplain | General Development Standard (GDS) E |
| **Existing over-height buildings** | Legal nonconforming. They "may add 10% of original gross floor area or 100 sq ft (whichever is less) without violating the existing building or maximum height envelope, subject to Design Review" | GDS E |
| **Second-floor exception, CBD Office** | By CUP, a second floor for long-term low-income housing, City employee housing, senior or disabled housing, or professional offices; max 24 ft including roof elements | CBD Office standards C(1) |
| **Design-excellence exception, Arts** | By CUP; envelope not over 36 ft | Arts District standards C |
| **Density, CBD-1, CBD-2, CBD Office** | No more than 1 unit per 2,000 sf of lot area. Historic-register houses may add units at 1 per 1,000 sf. CBD Office: 1 per 1,000 sf if at least 50% of units are long-term low-income, senior or disabled housing | GDS H |
| **CBD Office reduction** | Allowable units reduced by 1 unit per 1,000 sf of commercial floor area in the building | GDS H |
| **CBD VC** | Residential limited to the second floor, at most 50% of gross floor area; no more than 1 unit per 1,000 sf of lot area | GDS H |
| **CBD MFR** | 1 unit per 2,000 sf of lot area (1 per 1,000 sf for historic-register structures) | CBD MFR standards C |
| **Lot merger** | No merger for development over 5,000 sf, except City-ownership projects | GDS A |
| **Open space** | New development or expansion over 10% added floor area: lots over 2,500 sf provide open space equal to 25% of gross floor area, at least 40% vegetated; lots under 2,500 sf, 10% of lot area ground-to-sky | GDS C(2) |
| **Mixed-use flexibility** | "Yard area, setback, open space and coverage standards may be modified by Planning Commission for mixed-use residential/commercial development" | GDS D(3) |
| **CBD Office residential yards** | Per LBMC §25.12.008(C)(1)-(4) (R-2 yards), except City-participation senior, low-income or disabled housing | CBD Office standards A(4) |

Reading notes:
- The 12 ft and 1 floor limit means most DSP sites cannot add a second floor for housing without an exception, a variance, or a state-law waiver.
- An existing two-story building is legal nonconforming. Adding units inside its walls does not add height. Adding floor area is capped at the lesser of 10% or 100 sf.
- The VC 50% cap is measured against gross floor area. One recent project's concept letter (2026-08-20) applied it as 50% of floor area on the second floor and paired it with the 12 ft limit and the 10% / 100 sf expansion cap. That matches GDS E and H.

## 4. Parking (GDS O)

LBMC Ch. 25.52 applies except:
- Non-residential: minimum 3 spaces per 1,000 sf of gross floor area (office, retail, food service).
- Residential: "Minimum 1 space per studio or 1-bedroom unit; 2 spaces per unit with 2+ bedrooms."
- Long-term low-income, senior or disabled housing: parking may be reduced or waived by CUP with a parking study.

The Ord. 1712 rate of 0.5 space per small unit near transit applies in C-N, C-1 and SLV only. It does not apply in the DSP.

City notes on one recent project quote the certified DSP parking section: "All uses having legal nonconforming parking conditions may be maintained or changed to uses with the same or lesser parking requirements." That sentence is **not in the corpus extract**. Treat it as City record and confirm it against the certified PDF. Read with GDS O, a change from retail or office (3 per 1,000 sf) to small units (1 each) can be same-or-lesser for the converted area. Show the comparison.

On that project the City found the project about 9 spaces short and offered no reduction path (concept letter 2026-08-20).

## 5. Permits and review

- **Use permit:** AUP in most districts (see the VC conflict in Section 2). CUP findings in the DSP add "a balanced mix of uses" and "village character" tests (DSP CUP General Findings 1-4).
- **Design Review:** every project in the DSP goes to the **Planning Commission**: "all projects located in the downtown specific plan area" (§25.05.040(F)(1)(ii), Ord. 1732). Chapter 4 Guideline 2.9 (Second Story Residential) applies: privacy and security for units, private residential entrances and stairs, landscaping at the commercial-to-residential transition, night lighting kept off units, private open space where possible.
- **Coastal Development Permit:** the DSP is in the coastal zone, and adding units is a change in intensity of use (§25.07.008(A)(2)(b)). Check the appeal area from site facts. Use `mixed-use-conversion.md` Section 9 for the CDP rules.
- **Variances:** on one recent project staff listed at least five variances and said the findings likely could not be made (City record).

## 6. State-law routes

The DSP limits above are the local floor that state law can override.
- **Density Bonus** (Gov. Code §65915; LBMC §25.96.010) waivers can reach height, floors, density and parking. On that project the City pointed to a 100% affordable project under State Density Bonus Law as the alternative to redesign (City record).
- **AB 2011, SB 6, AB 507:** see the State alternatives table in `mixed-use-conversion.md` Section 11 and `state-preemption.md`.

Building-code content (occupancy separation, sprinklers, accessibility, exits) is state floor. Use `california-multi-unit-residential`, starting with `references/change-of-occupancy-commercial-to-r2.md`. Laguna has no DSP-specific building-code delta.
