## Fire Separation — Party Walls, Dwelling Unit Separation, and Sound Transmission

### Fire Separation Hierarchy

The CBC defines a hierarchy of fire-rated separations. Each type has different rating requirements, continuity rules, and permitted openings:

| Separation Type | Typical Rating | Use in Multi-Unit | Openings Allowed | Reference |
|----------------|---------------|-------------------|------------------|-----------|
| **Fire wall** (706) | 2-3 hours | Lot-line party walls, creating separate buildings | None in party walls (706.1.1) | CBC 706 |
| **Fire barrier** (707) | 1-2 hours | Separating different occupancies, exit enclosures | Protected openings per 716 | CBC 707 |
| **Fire partition** (708) | 1 hour | Dwelling unit separation, corridor walls | Protected openings per 716 | CBC 708 |
| **Horizontal assembly** (711) | 1-2 hours | Floor/ceiling between units | Protected penetrations per 714 | CBC 711 |

### Townhouse Party Walls (CRC R302.2)

For townhouses governed by the CRC (R-3, ≤3 stories, separate means of egress), there are two options for walls separating units:

#### Option 1: Double Walls (CRC R302.2.1)
Each townhouse unit is separated by **two 1-hour fire-resistance-rated wall assemblies** tested per ASTM E119, UL 263, or CBC Section 703.2.2.

#### Option 2: Common Walls (CRC R302.2.2)
A single common wall is shared between two townhouse units. The common wall must be:
- **1-hour fire-resistance rated** where an automatic sprinkler system per Section R309 is provided
- **2-hour fire-resistance rated** where sprinklers are NOT provided
- Rated for fire exposure from **both sides**
- Constructed **without openings, plumbing or mechanical equipment, ducts or vents** other than water-filled fire sprinkler piping in the wall cavity
- Electrical installations per the California Electrical Code; electrical outlet box penetrations per R302.4

(CRC R302.2.2)

#### Continuity (CRC R302.2.3)
The fire-resistance-rated wall or assembly separating townhouse units shall be **continuous from the foundation to the underside of the roof sheathing, roof deck or slab**. The rating shall extend the full length of the wall, including extensions through and separating attached enclosed accessory structures.

#### Parapets (CRC R302.2.4)
Parapets are required as extensions of exterior walls or common walls separating townhouse units:
- Where adjacent roofs are at the same elevation: parapet extends **not less than 30 inches above roof decks**
- Where adjacent roofs differ by ≤30 inches: parapet extends **not less than 30 inches above the lower roof**

**Exception (commonly used):** A parapet is NOT required where:
- Roof covering is minimum Class C per ASTM E108 or UL 790, AND
- Roof deck/sheathing is noncombustible or fire-retardant-treated wood for **4 feet on each side** of the wall, OR one layer of 5/8-inch Type X gypsum board is installed directly beneath the roof deck for 4 feet on each side, AND
- No openings/penetrations within 4 feet of the common wall

(CRC R302.2.4)

#### Structural Independence (CRC R302.2.6)
Each townhouse unit shall be **structurally independent**.

Exceptions (shared elements permitted):
1. Foundations supporting exterior walls or common walls
2. Structural roof and wall sheathing fastened to common wall framing
3. Nonstructural wall and roof coverings
4. Flashing at termination of roof covering over common wall
5. Units separated by common wall per R302.2.2
6. Units with automatic sprinkler system per R309 or NFPA 13D

#### Penetrations Through Rated Walls (CRC R302.4)
Penetrations of fire-resistance-rated wall or floor-ceiling assemblies must be protected:
- **Through penetrations**: Installed as tested in the approved fire-resistance-rated assembly, OR protected by an approved penetration firestop system per ASTM E814 or UL 1479 with F rating not less than the required fire-resistance rating
- **Membrane penetrations**: Electrical outlet boxes on opposite sides of a common wall must be separated by a horizontal distance of not less than **24 inches** unless protected by listed putty pads or boxes listed for fire-resistance-rated assemblies

(CRC R302.4.1, R302.4.2)

### Apartment Dwelling Unit Separation (CBC 708 — Fire Partitions)

For R-2 buildings (apartments, common corridors), dwelling units are separated by **fire partitions**:

**Fire-resistance rating**: Not less than **1 hour** (CBC 708.3)

Exception: In Types IIB, IIIB, and VB construction, dwelling unit separations may be **1/2 hour** in buildings with NFPA 13 sprinkler systems (CBC 708.3, Exception 2)

**Continuity**: Fire partitions extend from the top of the foundation or floor/ceiling assembly below to the underside of the floor or roof sheathing, deck or slab above, OR to the underside of a rated floor/ceiling or roof/ceiling assembly with equal or greater fire-resistance rating (CBC 708.4)

**Corridor walls**: Required per CBC 1020.2 and included in the fire partition requirements (CBC 708.1, Item 3)

**Walls separating R-1 and R-2 units**: Specifically required to be fire partitions per CBC 708.1, Item 7

### Fire Walls on Lot Lines (CBC 706)

Where a wall is located on a lot line between adjacent buildings, it shall be constructed as a **fire wall** per CBC 706.

#### Fire Wall Ratings (Table 706.4)

| Occupancy Group | Rating Required |
|----------------|----------------|
| **R-1, R-2, R-2.1, R-2.2** | 3 hours |
| **R-3, R-4** | 2 hours |

**Exception for Type II and V construction**: Walls in Type II or V construction may have a **2-hour** rating instead of 3-hour (Table 706.4 footnote a)

(CBC Table 706.4)

#### Key Fire Wall Requirements
- **Party walls** (706.1.1): Constructed **without openings** and create separate buildings
- **Structural stability** (706.2): Must allow collapse of structure on either side without collapse of the wall. Exception in SDC D-F: floor and roof sheathing ≤3/4 inch may be continuous through double fire wall assemblies in light frame construction
- **Materials** (706.3): Noncombustible materials required. Exception: Buildings of Type V construction
- **Horizontal continuity** (706.5): From exterior wall to exterior wall, extending **not less than 18 inches** beyond exterior surface. Exceptions allow termination at interior surface with specific protection conditions
- **Vertical continuity** (706.6): From foundation to not less than **30 inches above both adjacent roofs**. Exceptions per 706.6 allow termination at underside of roof sheathing with specific conditions
- **Openings** (706.8): Not permitted in party walls. Other fire walls: max 156 sq ft per opening, aggregate width max 25% of wall length
- **Ducts and air transfer openings** (706.11): **Shall not penetrate fire walls**
- **Penetrations** (706.9): Must comply with Section 714

### Sound Transmission Requirements (CBC 1206)

In addition to fire-resistance, walls and floor/ceiling assemblies separating dwelling units must meet acoustic standards:

#### Airborne Sound (CBC 1206.2)
Walls, partitions, and floor-ceiling assemblies separating dwelling units from each other or from public/service areas:
- **STC 50 minimum** (laboratory test per ASTM E90), OR
- **NNIC 45 minimum** (field test per ASTM E336)

Penetrations or openings for piping, electrical devices, recessed cabinets, bathtubs, soffits, or HVAC ducts shall be **sealed, lined, insulated, or otherwise treated** to maintain the required ratings. This does not apply to entrance doors, but doors must be tight-fitting.

#### Structure-Borne Sound (CBC 1206.3)
Floor-ceiling assemblies between dwelling units or between a dwelling unit and public/service area:
- **IIC 50 minimum** (laboratory test per ASTM E492), OR
- **NISR 45 minimum** (field test per ASTM E1007)

Exception: Not required for floor-ceiling assemblies over nonhabitable rooms (garages, mechanical rooms, storage).

#### Interior Noise Levels (CBC 1206.4)
Interior noise levels attributable to exterior sources shall not exceed **45 dB** (Ldn or CNEL) in any habitable room.

### Exterior Wall Fire Separation Distance (CRC R302.1)

For townhouses, exterior wall fire-resistance depends on fire separation distance:

**Without sprinklers (Table R302.1(1)):**
| Element | 0 ft | <3 ft | 3 ft | ≥5 ft |
|---------|------|-------|------|-------|
| Walls | 1-hr rated | 1-hr rated | 1-hr rated | 0-hr |
| Openings | Not allowed | Not allowed | 25% max area | Unlimited |
| Projections | — | Not allowed | — | 0-hr |

**With sprinklers (Table R302.1(2)):**
| Element | 0 ft | <3 ft | ≥3 ft |
|---------|------|-------|-------|
| Walls | 1-hr rated | 1-hr rated | 0-hr |
| Openings | Not allowed | Not allowed | Unlimited |

(CRC R302.1, Tables R302.1(1) and R302.1(2))

### For the 35-Unit Townhome Test Case

- **Party walls between units**: CRC R302.2.2 — 1-hour common wall (with mandatory R309 sprinklers)
- **Continuous from foundation to roof sheathing**: CRC R302.2.3
- **No openings, ducts, or mechanical** in common wall cavity (except fire sprinkler piping)
- **Parapets**: 30-inch extension OR use the Class C roof + 4-ft treated deck exception
- **Sound transmission**: STC 50 minimum for walls, IIC 50 for floors (CBC 1206)
- **Structural independence**: Required with exceptions for shared foundations and sheathing (R302.2.6)
- **Each unit creates a separate building** when separated by fire walls per CBC 503.1
