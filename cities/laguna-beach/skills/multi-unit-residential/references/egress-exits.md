## Egress and Exits — Number of Exits, Separation, Travel Distance, and Emergency Escape Openings

### Number of Exits per Story (CBC 1006.3.3)

Every story and occupiable roof must have a minimum number of separate and distinct exits based on the aggregate occupant load served:

| Occupant Load per Story | Minimum Exits Required |
|------------------------|----------------------|
| 1-500 | 2 |
| 501-1,000 | 3 |
| More than 1,000 | 4 |

(CBC Table 1006.3.3)

The required number of exits from any story shall be maintained until arrival at the exit discharge or a public way (CBC 1006.3.3).

#### Single Exit Exceptions for R-2 (CBC 1006.3.4)

A single exit is permitted from a story in R-2 occupancies only when ALL of the following are met:

- Building is equipped throughout with an automatic sprinkler system per Section 903.3.1.1 or 903.3.1.2
- Emergency escape and rescue openings are provided per Section 1031
- The story is a basement, first, second, or third story above grade plane (or occupiable roof over first or second story)
- Maximum **4 dwelling units** on that story
- Maximum **125 feet** exit access travel distance

(CBC Table 1006.3.4(1))

For stories fourth and above in R-2: single exit is **not permitted** (CBC Table 1006.3.4(1)).

For R-2 occupancies consisting of sleeping units (not dwelling units), use Table 1006.3.4(2) instead, which limits single-exit stories to 10 occupants with 75 ft travel distance, first story above or below grade only.

#### Spaces within a Story — When Two Exits Are Required (CBC 1006.2.1)

A space within a story requires two exits or exit access doorways when the occupant load or common path of egress travel exceeds the values in Table 1006.2.1. Key values for residential:

| Occupancy | Max Occupant Load (1 exit) | Common Path of Egress Travel (sprinklered) |
|-----------|---------------------------|-------------------------------------------|
| **R-1** (hotels) | 10 | 75 ft |
| **R-2** (apartments) | 20 | 125 ft |
| **R-3** (townhouses) | 20 | 125 ft |

(CBC Table 1006.2.1)

**Without sprinklers**: R-1, R-2, R-3, and R-4 occupancies are **not permitted** to have spaces with a single exit (CBC Table 1006.2.1, "NP" entries for unsprinklered conditions).

Three exits are required from any space with 501-1,000 occupants; four exits for more than 1,000 (CBC 1006.2.1.1).

### Exit Separation Requirements (CBC 1007.1.1)

When two exits are required, they must be separated by a distance of not less than **one-half the length of the maximum overall diagonal dimension** of the building or area served, measured in a straight line between them (CBC 1007.1.1).

**Sprinkler reduction**: Where a building is equipped throughout with an automatic sprinkler system per Section 903.3.1.1 or 903.3.1.2, the required separation distance is reduced to **one-third of the diagonal dimension** (CBC 1007.1.1, Exception 2).

**Corridor interconnection**: Where interior exit stairways or ramps are interconnected by a 1-hour fire-resistance-rated corridor per Section 1020, the separation is measured along the shortest direct line of travel within the corridor rather than a straight line (CBC 1007.1.1, Exception 1).

Measurement points (CBC 1007.1.1.1):
- Exit doorways: measured to any point along the width of the doorway
- Exit access stairways: measured to the closest riser
- Exit access ramps: measured to the start of the ramp run

For three or more exits: at least two must meet the separation requirement above; additional exits must be "a reasonable distance apart so that if one becomes blocked, the others will be available" (CBC 1007.1.2).

### Common Path of Egress Travel

The common path of egress travel is the portion of exit access that must be traversed before two separate and distinct paths of egress travel are available. For residential occupancies with sprinklers:

| Occupancy | Maximum Common Path (sprinklered) |
|-----------|----------------------------------|
| R-1 | 75 ft |
| R-2 | 125 ft |
| R-3, R-4 | 125 ft |

(CBC Table 1006.2.1)

Without sprinklers, R-1, R-2, R-3, and R-4 occupancies are **not permitted** to have any common path of egress travel regardless of occupant load (CBC Table 1006.2.1 — NP for all unsprinklered columns).

### Exit Access Travel Distance (CBC 1017.2)

Exit access travel distance is the total distance from the most remote point in any room or space to the entrance of the nearest exit, measured along the natural and unobstructed path of travel (CBC 1017.3). Limits for residential:

| Occupancy | Without Sprinklers | With Sprinklers |
|-----------|-------------------|-----------------|
| **R (all: R-1, R-2, R-3, R-4)** | **200 ft** | **250 ft** |
| **R-2.1** | Not Permitted | **250 ft** |

(CBC Table 1017.2)

Sprinkler type matters: the 250 ft sprinklered distance requires a system per Section 903.3.1.1 or 903.3.1.2 (CBC Table 1017.2, footnote b). For R-3 and R-4 with NFPA 13R systems per Section 903.3.1.3, the travel distance is limited to **125 ft** for R-3 and **75 ft** for R-4 (CBC 1006.2.2.6).

**Exterior egress balcony increase**: Travel distance may be increased up to an additional **100 ft** where the last portion of exit access is on an exterior egress balcony per Section 1021, provided the balcony length equals or exceeds the increase taken (CBC 1017.2.1).

**Measurement**: Travel distance is measured from the most remote point of each room, area, or space along the natural and unobstructed path of horizontal and vertical egress travel to the entrance of the nearest exit. Travel on exit access stairways is included in the measurement (CBC 1017.3, 1017.3.1).

### Exit Width Calculation (CBC 1005)

The required width of egress components is determined by the occupant load served, using capacity factors:

#### Standard Capacity Factors (CBC 1005.3.1, 1005.3.2)

| Component | Base Factor | Sprinklered + Emergency Voice/Alarm Factor |
|-----------|------------|-------------------------------------------|
| **Stairways** | 0.3 in/occupant | **0.2 in/occupant** |
| **Other components** (corridors, doors, ramps) | 0.2 in/occupant | **0.15 in/occupant** |

The reduced factors (0.2 in for stairs, 0.15 in for other) apply only where the building is equipped throughout with BOTH an automatic sprinkler system per 903.3.1.1 or 903.3.1.2 AND an emergency voice/alarm communication system per 907.5.2.2 (CBC 1005.3.1 Exception 1, 1005.3.2 Exception 1). These reduced factors do **not** apply to Group H or I-2 occupancies.

**Minimum component widths** still apply regardless of calculation — doors, corridors, and stairs each have their own minimum width requirements specified elsewhere in Chapter 10.

**Distribution rule**: Where more than one exit is required, loss of any single exit shall not reduce available capacity or width to less than **50%** of the required capacity or width (CBC 1005.5).

**Continuity**: The minimum width or required capacity from any story shall not be reduced along the path of egress travel until arrival at the public way (CBC 1005.4).

### CRC Egress Requirements for Townhouses (CRC R318)

The CRC applies to townhouse units (R-3 occupancy, ≤3 stories above grade, separate means of egress per unit). Each unit is treated independently for egress purposes.

#### Egress Door (CRC R318.2)

Each dwelling unit requires not less than **one egress door** that:
- Is **side-hinged**
- Provides a clear width of not less than **32 inches** (measured between face of door and stop with door open 90 degrees)
- Has a clear height of not less than **78 inches** (measured from top of threshold to bottom of stop)
- Opens directly into a **public way** or to a yard or court that opens to a public way
- Is readily openable from inside **without a key or special knowledge or effort**

(CRC R318.1, R318.2)

The means of egress must provide a continuous and unobstructed path from all portions of the dwelling unit to the egress door **without requiring travel through a garage** (CRC R318.1).

#### Floors and Landings (CRC R318.3)

A landing or floor is required on each side of each exterior door:
- Width not less than the door served
- Depth not less than **36 inches** in direction of travel
- Exterior landing slope not exceeding **2%** (1/4 in 12)

At the required egress door: landing/floor not more than **1-1/2 inches** below top of threshold. Exterior side may be up to **7-3/4 inches** below threshold if door does not swing over the landing (CRC R318.3.1).

#### Vertical Egress (CRC R318.4)

Habitable levels or basements not directly served by an egress door require stairways or ramps. For levels more than one story above or below an egress door, the maximum travel distance from any occupied point to a stairway or ramp providing egress shall not exceed **50 feet** (CRC R318.4).

#### Hallways (CRC R318.6)

Minimum hallway width: **3 feet** (CRC R318.6).

#### Stairways (CRC R318.7)

Key dimensions for residential stairways within townhouse units:
- Width: not less than **36 inches** clear above handrail height; **31-1/2 inches** clear at handrail with one handrail; **27 inches** with handrails on both sides (CRC R318.7.1)
- Headroom: not less than **6 feet 8 inches** (CRC R318.7.2)
- Maximum vertical rise per flight: **12 feet 7 inches** (CRC R318.7.3)
- Riser height: not more than **7-3/4 inches**; greatest-to-smallest variation not more than 3/8 inch (CRC R318.7.5.1)
- Tread depth: not less than **10 inches**; greatest-to-smallest variation not more than 3/8 inch (CRC R318.7.5.2)
- Handrails required on at least one side for flights with 4 or more risers (CRC R318.7.8)

### Emergency Escape and Rescue Openings (CRC R319)

#### Where Required (CRC R319.1)

The following spaces each require not less than **one operable emergency escape and rescue opening**:
- Every **sleeping room**
- **Basements** (each sleeping room in a basement needs its own opening)
- **Habitable attics**
- The room to which a **sleeping loft** is open

Openings must open directly into a **public way**, or to a yard or court that opens to a public way (CRC R319.1).

**Exceptions** — not required where:
- Basement ceiling height is less than 80 inches (CRC R319.1, Exception 1)
- Room has an exit door opening directly to a public way, yard, court, or exterior egress balcony (CRC R319.1, Exception 2)
- Basement has no habitable space and is 200 sf or less (CRC R319.1, Exception 3)
- Dwelling unit has sprinklers per R309: sleeping rooms in basements may substitute either (a) one means of egress per R318 plus one emergency escape opening, or (b) two means of egress per R318 (CRC R319.1, Exception 5)

#### Minimum Size (CRC R319.2)

| Requirement | Standard | At Grade Floor |
|-------------|----------|---------------|
| **Net clear opening area** | **5.7 sq ft** minimum | **5.0 sq ft** minimum |
| **Minimum clear height** | **24 inches** | **24 inches** |
| **Minimum clear width** | **20 inches** | **20 inches** |
| **Maximum sill height** | **44 inches** above floor | **44 inches** above floor |

(CRC R319.2.1, R319.2.2, R319.2.3)

The net clear opening dimensions must be achieved through **normal operation** of the window — no special tools, keys, or knowledge required (CRC R319.1.1, R319.2.2).

#### Operational Requirements (CRC R319.1.1)

- Must be operable from the inside without keys, tools, or special knowledge
- Window opening control devices and fall prevention devices per ASTM F2090 are permitted, installed not more than **70 inches** above finished floor
- Security bars, if installed, must have an approved interior release device (and exterior release where required by AHJ)

#### Below-Grade Openings — Area Wells (CRC R319.4)

Emergency escape openings below adjacent grade require an area well:
- Minimum **9 sq ft** horizontal area
- Minimum **36 inches** horizontal projection and width
- Area wells deeper than **44 inches** require a permanently affixed ladder or steps
- Ladders: minimum 12-inch inside width, project 3 inches from wall, rungs spaced max 18 inches on center
- Drainage required, connecting to foundation drainage system

(CRC R319.4.1, R319.4.2, R319.4.3)

#### Emergency Escape Doors (CRC R319.3)

Where a door serves as the emergency escape and rescue opening, it must be a **side-hinged door or sliding door** (CRC R319.3).

### For the 35-Unit Test Case

**Townhouse units (CRC):**
- Each townhouse unit has its own egress door directly to the exterior — minimum 32-inch clear width, 78-inch clear height (CRC R318.2)
- No shared corridors or common stairs required between units
- Every sleeping room needs an emergency escape and rescue opening: 5.7 sf net clear, 24-in min height, 20-in min width, 44-in max sill height (CRC R319.2)
- Travel distance within each unit to a stairway or ramp: max 50 ft for levels more than one story above/below egress door (CRC R318.4)
- Individual exits satisfy exit count requirements — each unit's egress door is its own exit (CBC 1006.3.4, Item 4: R-3 occupancies are permitted one exit per story)

**Apartment units (CBC — R-2 occupancy):**
- Each story needs a minimum of **2 exits** for up to 500 occupants (CBC Table 1006.3.3)
- Single-exit story permitted only where: sprinklered, 4 or fewer dwelling units on that story, travel distance ≤ 125 ft, emergency escape openings provided, and limited to 3 stories above grade (CBC Table 1006.3.4(1))
- Exit separation: not less than **1/2 diagonal** of the floor plate (or **1/3 diagonal** with sprinklers) (CBC 1007.1.1)
- Exit access travel distance: **250 ft with sprinklers**, 200 ft without (CBC Table 1017.2)
- Common path of egress travel: **125 ft** maximum with sprinklers (CBC Table 1006.2.1)
- Exit stair width: **0.2 in/occupant** with sprinklers + emergency voice/alarm; 0.3 in/occupant otherwise (CBC 1005.3.1)
- Corridor/door width: **0.15 in/occupant** with sprinklers + emergency voice/alarm; 0.2 in/occupant otherwise (CBC 1005.3.2)
- Loss of any single exit cannot reduce available capacity below 50% of required (CBC 1005.5)
