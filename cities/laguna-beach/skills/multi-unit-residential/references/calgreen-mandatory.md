## CALGreen Residential Mandatory Measures — Multifamily

### Scope

CALGreen Chapter 4 applies to all new residential construction in California, including multifamily (R-2) buildings. These are mandatory minimum requirements — they are not optional or elective. The 2025 edition is effective January 1, 2026. (CALGreen 4.101.1)

---

## Indoor Water Conservation (Section 4.303)

### Water Closets (4.303.1.1)

All water closets shall have an effective flush volume not exceeding **1.28 gallons per flush (GPF)**. Tank-type water closets must be certified to the **US EPA WaterSense Specification for Tank-type Toilets**. (CALGreen 4.303.1.1)

Dual-flush toilets: the effective flush volume is defined as the composite average of two reduced flushes and one full flush. The composite must still not exceed 1.28 GPF.

### Urinals (4.303.1.2)

- Wall-mounted urinals: not to exceed **0.125 GPF** (CALGreen 4.303.1.2)
- All other urinals: not to exceed **0.5 GPF** (CALGreen 4.303.1.2)

Relevant for common areas, lobbies, amenity spaces, and management offices in multifamily buildings.

### Showerheads (4.303.1.3)

- Single showerhead: maximum **1.8 GPM at 80 psi**, certified to **US EPA WaterSense Specification for Showerheads** (CALGreen 4.303.1.3.1)
- Multiple showerheads serving one shower: combined flow of all heads/outlets controlled by a single valve shall not exceed **1.8 GPM at 80 psi**, or the shower must be designed to allow only one outlet to operate at a time (CALGreen 4.303.1.3.2)

A hand-held shower counts as a showerhead.

### Faucets (4.303.1.4)

| Fixture | Max Flow Rate | Pressure | Code Section |
|---------|--------------|----------|--------------|
| **Residential lavatory faucet** | 1.2 GPM (min 0.8 GPM at 20 psi) | 60 psi | 4.303.1.4.1 |
| **Common/public area lavatory faucet** | 0.5 GPM | 60 psi | 4.303.1.4.2 |
| **Metering faucet** | 0.2 gallons per cycle | — | 4.303.1.4.3 |
| **Kitchen faucet** | 1.8 GPM (temporary boost to 2.2 GPM permitted, must default back to 1.8 GPM) | 60 psi | 4.303.1.4.4 |

(CALGreen 4.303.1.4)

**Common area distinction:** Lavatory faucets in lobbies, corridors, clubhouses, fitness centers, amenity rooms, and other areas outside dwelling units or sleeping units are limited to 0.5 GPM — significantly lower than the 1.2 GPM residential limit. Plans must clearly distinguish between in-unit and common-area fixtures.

Where compliant faucets are unavailable, aerators or other means may be used to achieve the required flow reduction. (CALGreen 4.303.1.4, Note)

### Fixture Standards (4.303.3)

All plumbing fixtures and fittings shall be installed per the California Plumbing Code and meet the standards referenced in CPC Table 1701.1. (CALGreen 4.303.3)

## Water Submetering (Section 4.303.2)

**Individual water submeters are required for each dwelling unit** in multifamily buildings and dwelling units in mixed-use residential/commercial buildings. Submeters shall be installed to measure water usage of individual rental dwelling units in accordance with the California Plumbing Code. (CALGreen 4.303.2)

This requirement applies to all new R-2 occupancy buildings. The purpose is to enable tracking of individual unit water consumption and billing, which drives conservation behavior.

**For a 35-unit building: 35 individual water submeters required.**

## Outdoor Water Use (Section 4.304)

### Landscape Water Efficiency (4.304.1)

Residential developments shall comply with either:
1. A **local water-efficient landscape ordinance**, or
2. The current California Department of Water Resources' **Model Water Efficient Landscape Ordinance (MWELO)**

Whichever is **more stringent** controls. (CALGreen 4.304.1)

MWELO is located in the California Code of Regulations, Title 23, Chapter 2.7, Division 2. A water budget calculator and supporting documents are available at https://www.water.ca.gov/.

For multifamily projects, this means landscape plans must demonstrate compliance with MWELO water budgets (or stricter local ordinance) before permit issuance. Irrigation plans must show efficient equipment (weather-based controllers, drip irrigation, etc.) and plant palettes must match the applicable water budget.

## Site Development and Pollutant Control

### Storm Water Drainage During Construction (4.106.2)

Projects disturbing less than one acre of soil (and not part of a larger common plan disturbing one acre or more) shall manage storm water drainage during construction using one or more of the following:

1. **Retention basins** of sufficient size to retain storm water on site
2. **Barrier system, wattle, or other filtration** where storm water is conveyed to a public drainage system
3. Compliance with a **lawfully enacted storm water management ordinance**

(CALGreen 4.106.2)

Projects disturbing one acre or more must comply with the State Water Resources Control Board Construction General Permit (SWRCB CGP).

### Grading and Paving (4.106.3)

Construction plans shall indicate how the site grading or drainage system will manage all surface water flows to keep water from entering buildings. Methods include swales, collection/disposal systems, French drains, water retention gardens, and other approved measures. (CALGreen 4.106.3)

**Exception:** Additions and alterations not altering the drainage path.

### Erosion and Sediment Control

Construction site management practices must prevent:
- Flooding of adjacent property
- Erosion from the construction site
- Soil runoff leaving the site

These are enforced through the construction waste management plan and site inspections.

## Bicycle Parking (Section 4.106.4.4)

### Short-Term Bicycle Parking — Multifamily, Hotels, Motels (4.106.4.4.1)

| Parameter | Requirement |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Ratio** | 1 space per 10,000 sf of building area |
| **Minimum** | 2 spaces (regardless of building size) |
| **Location** | Within 200 feet of building entrances, readily visible to passers-by |
| **Type** | Permanently anchored racks, devices, or lockers (covered or uncovered) |

(CALGreen 4.106.4.4.1)

### Long-Term Bicycle Parking — Multifamily Buildings (4.106.4.4.2)

| Parameter | Requirement |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Ratio** | 1 space per 2 dwelling units |
| **Location** | Conveniently accessed from the street |
| **Type** | Must be in a **lockable enclosure** — covered lockable enclosures with permanently anchored racks, lockable bicycle storage rooms, or lockable weatherproof permanently anchored bicycle lockers |

(CALGreen 4.106.4.4.2)

Long-term bicycle parking must provide security (lockable) and weather protection (covered). Open racks alone do not satisfy the long-term requirement.

### 35-Unit Test Case: Bicycle Parking

| Type | Calculation | Result |
|------|-------------|--------|
| Short-term | Depends on building sf — e.g., 35,000 sf building: 35,000/10,000 = 3.5, round up | **4 short-term spaces** (min 2) |
| Long-term | 35 units / 2 = 17.5, round up | **18 long-term spaces** (lockable enclosure) |

## Construction Waste Reduction, Disposal and Recycling (Section 4.408)

### Minimum Diversion Requirement (4.408.1)

**Recycle and/or salvage for reuse a minimum of 65% of nonhazardous construction and demolition waste.** Compliance must be achieved through one of three paths:

1. **Construction waste management plan** (Section 4.408.2)
2. **Approved waste management company** with verifiable documentation (Section 4.408.3)
3. **Waste stream reduction alternative** — keeping total landfill waste below a per-sf threshold (Section 4.408.4)

A more stringent local construction and demolition waste management ordinance supersedes if applicable. (CALGreen 4.408.1)

**Exceptions:**
1. Excavated soil and land-clearing debris
2. Alternate waste reduction methods if diversion/recycling facilities do not exist or are not reasonably close to the jobsite
3. Isolated jobsites beyond the haul boundaries of a diversion facility (enforcing agency may grant exception)

### Construction Waste Management Plan (4.408.2)

The plan must include all five of the following and be available during construction for inspection:

1. **Identify materials** to be diverted from disposal (by recycling, on-site reuse, or salvage)
2. **Specify sorting method** — source-separated (on-site) or bulk mixed (single stream)
3. **Identify diversion facilities** where materials will be taken
4. **Identify construction methods** that reduce waste generation
5. **Specify measurement method** — by weight or volume, but not both

(CALGreen 4.408.2)

### Waste Stream Reduction Alternative (4.408.4)

As an alternative to tracking the 65% diversion rate, projects may demonstrate compliance by keeping total landfill-disposed waste below a per-sf threshold:

| Path | Maximum Landfill Waste | Code Section |
|------|----------------------|--------------|
| Standard alternative [LR] | 3.4 lbs per sf of building area | 4.408.4 |
| **Reduced threshold** | **2.0 lbs per sf of building area** | **4.408.4.1** |

Projects meeting the 2.0 lbs/sf threshold automatically satisfy the 65% diversion requirement without separate tracking. (CALGreen 4.408.4.1)

### Documentation (4.408.5)

Documentation demonstrating compliance must be provided to the enforcing agency. CalRecycle maintains a directory of mixed C&D processors. Sample compliance forms are available from HCD at http://www.hcd.ca.gov/building-standards/calgreen/cal-green-forms.html. (CALGreen 4.408.5)

### 35-Unit Test Case: Waste Diversion

- **65% minimum diversion** of all nonhazardous C&D waste from landfill
- Construction waste management plan required before start of construction
- Alternative: keep total landfill waste at or below **2.0 lbs/sf** of building area (Section 4.408.4.1)
- For a 35,000 sf building using the alternative: max landfill waste = 35,000 x 2.0 = **70,000 lbs (35 tons)**

## Indoor Environmental Quality

### Finish Material Pollutant Control (4.504)

#### Adhesives, Sealants, and Caulks (4.504.2.1)

All adhesives, sealants, and caulks must comply with SCAQMD Rule 1168 VOC limits (or more stringent local rules). Key limits from Table 4.504.1:

| Product | VOC Limit (g/L) |
|---------|-----------------|
| Indoor carpet adhesive | 50 |
| Wood flooring adhesive | 100 |
| Subfloor adhesive | 50 |
| Drywall and panel adhesive | 50 |
| Multipurpose construction adhesive | 70 |

(CALGreen Table 4.504.1)

Products are prohibited from containing chloroform, ethylene dichloride, methylene chloride, perchloroethylene, and trichloroethylene (per SCAQMD Rule 1168).

#### Paints and Coatings (4.504.2.2)

Must comply with ARB Architectural Suggested Control Measure VOC limits (Table 4.504.3):

| Coating Category | VOC Limit (g/L) |
|------------------|-----------------|
| Flat coatings | 50 |
| Nonflat coatings | 100 |
| Nonflat-high gloss coatings | 150 |
| Primers, sealers, undercoaters | 100 |
| Stains | 250 |

(CALGreen Table 4.504.3)

#### Carpet Systems (4.504.3)

All interior carpet must meet the California Department of Public Health Standard Method (Specification 01350) for VOC emissions testing. Carpet cushion (4.504.3.1) and carpet adhesive (4.504.3.2) must also comply. (CALGreen 4.504.3)

#### Resilient Flooring (4.504.4)

At least **80% of floor area** receiving resilient flooring must meet CDPH Specification 01350 for VOC emissions. (CALGreen 4.504.4)

#### Composite Wood Products (4.504.5)

Interior and exterior composite wood products must meet ARB Air Toxics Control Measure formaldehyde limits:

| Product | Max Formaldehyde (ppm) |
|---------|----------------------|
| Hardwood plywood (veneer or composite core) | 0.05 |
| Particleboard | 0.09 |
| Medium density fiberboard | 0.11 |
| Thin MDF (max 5/16") | 0.13 |

(CALGreen Table 4.504.5, per ASTM E1333 testing)

### Duct Protection During Construction (4.504.1)

All duct openings and air distribution component openings shall be covered at rough installation, during site storage, and until final HVAC startup — using tape, plastic, sheet metal, or other approved methods. (CALGreen 4.504.1)

### Fireplaces (4.503.1)

- Gas fireplaces: must be **direct-vent sealed-combustion** type
- Woodstoves/pellet stoves: must comply with US EPA NSPS emission limits and carry permanent certification labels
- Must also comply with applicable local ordinances

(CALGreen 4.503.1)

## Interior Moisture Control (Section 4.505)

### Concrete Slab Capillary Break (4.505.2.1)

Where vapor retarders are required by CBC Chapter 19 or CRC Chapter 5, a capillary break must also be provided:

1. 4-inch-thick base of 1/2-inch or larger clean aggregate, vapor retarder in direct contact with concrete, with appropriate mix design for bleeding/shrinkage/curling, or
2. Other equivalent methods approved by the enforcing agency, or
3. Slab design specified by a licensed design professional

(CALGreen 4.505.2.1)

### Moisture Content of Building Materials (4.505.3)

- No materials with visible water damage shall be installed
- Wall and floor framing shall not be enclosed when moisture content exceeds **19%**
- Verification: probe-type or contact-type moisture meter, readings taken 2–4 feet from grade-stamped end
- At least **3 random moisture readings** required on wall and floor framing, with documentation for enforcing agency

(CALGreen 4.505.3)

## Bathroom Exhaust (Section 4.506)

Each bathroom (room containing a bathtub, shower, or tub/shower combination) must be mechanically ventilated:

1. Fans must be **ENERGY STAR compliant** and ducted to terminate outside the building
2. Unless part of a whole-house ventilation system, fans must be controlled by a **humidity control** adjustable between 50% and 80% relative humidity (may be manual or automatic, separate or integral to the fan)

(CALGreen 4.506.1)

## Building Maintenance and Operation (Section 4.410)

### Operation and Maintenance Manual (4.410.1)

At final inspection, a manual must be placed in the building covering:

- Equipment and appliance operation (water-saving devices, HVAC, PV, EV chargers, water heaters)
- Roof and yard drainage maintenance
- Space conditioning system maintenance (condensers, air filters)
- Landscape irrigation systems
- Local utility/water/waste provider information on further resource reduction
- Public transportation and carpool options
- Interior humidity guidance (30–60% RH recommended)
- Water-conserving landscape and irrigation design
- Gutter/downspout maintenance (divert water 5+ feet from foundation)
- Routine maintenance (caulking, painting, grading)
- State solar energy and incentive programs
- Special inspection verifications
- Cal Fire defensible space information
- Grab bar reinforcement location drawings

(CALGreen 4.410.1)

### Recycling Areas — Multifamily (4.410.2)

Where **5 or more multifamily dwelling units** are constructed on a building site, provide readily accessible recycling area(s) serving all buildings on the site. Areas must be identified for depositing, storage, and collection of nonhazardous materials including at minimum: **paper, corrugated cardboard, glass, plastics, organic waste, and metals**. A more restrictive local recycling ordinance supersedes. (CALGreen 4.410.2)

**Exception:** Rural jurisdictions meeting the exemption in Public Resources Code Section 42649.82(a)(2)(A) et seq. are not required to comply with the organic waste portion.

---

## HVAC Design (Section 4.507)

Heating and air-conditioning systems must be sized and designed using:

1. **Load calculation:** ANSI/ACCA 2 Manual J-2016, ASHRAE handbooks, or equivalent
2. **Duct sizing:** ANSI/ACCA 1 Manual D-2016, ASHRAE handbooks, or equivalent
3. **Equipment selection:** ANSI/ACCA 3 Manual S-2014 or equivalent

(CALGreen 4.507.2)

Alternate design temperatures may be used where necessary to ensure system function.

---

## Rodent Proofing (Section 4.406)

Annular spaces around pipes, electric cables, conduits, or other openings in sole/bottom plates at exterior walls shall be sealed against rodent passage using cement mortar, concrete masonry, or similar approved method. (CALGreen 4.406.1)
