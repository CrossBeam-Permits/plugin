## Plumbing Fixtures, Drainage, Venting, and Water Supply Sizing

### Minimum Fixtures per Dwelling Unit (R-2 Apartment)

Table 422.1 defines minimum plumbing facilities for R-2 Residential occupancy (long-term or permanent), apartment house/unit:

| Fixture | Minimum per Unit | Notes |
|---------|-----------------|-------|
| **Water closet** | 1 per apartment | Gravity tank 1.28 gpf max (CPC 411.2, CALGreen 4.303.1.1) |
| **Lavatory** | 1 per apartment | 1.2 gpm max residential faucet (CPC 407.2.2) |
| **Bathtub or shower** | 1 per apartment | 1.8 gpm max showerhead (CALGreen 4.303.1.3) |
| **Kitchen sink** | 1 per apartment | 1.8 gpm max faucet (CPC 420.2.2, CALGreen 4.303.1.4.4) |
| **Laundry connection** | 1 per unit, OR 1 for each 12 units | Laundry tray or automatic clothes washer connection (CPC Table 422.1, R-2) |

(CPC Table 422.1, R-2 Apartment house/unit)

**Important:** No drinking fountain or service sink is required within individual apartment units. Employee-use fixtures are separate and apply to management/maintenance areas only.

## Drainage Sizing

### Building Drain and Sewer Sizing (Table 703.2)

Maximum fixture units on drainage piping by pipe size (at 1/4" per foot slope):

| Pipe Size (inches) | Vertical (max DFU) | Horizontal (max DFU) | Max Vertical Length (ft) | Branch Interval (max DFU) |
|--------------------|--------------------|--------------------|------------------------|--------------------------|
| 1-1/2 | 2 | 1 | 65 | -- |
| 2 | 16 | 8 | 85 | -- |
| 3 | 48 | 35 | 212 | Not to exceed 6 WCs or 5 six-unit traps |
| 4 | 256 | 216 | 300 | -- |
| 5 | 600 | 428 | 390 | -- |
| 6 | 1380 | 720 | 510 | -- |
| 8 | 3600 | 2640 | 750 | -- |

(CPC Table 703.2)

**Slope notes:**
- Standard slope: 1/4 inch per foot (2%) for all sizes (CPC 708.1)
- Reduced slope: 1/8 inch per foot (1%) permitted for pipes 4 inches and larger when approved by the AHJ (CPC 708.1)
- For 1/8" per foot slope, multiply horizontal fixture unit values by 0.8 (CPC Table 703.2, Note 5)

**Key constraint for 3-inch pipe:** A 3-inch building drain at 1/4" slope is limited to 35 DFU horizontal, and not more than 6 water closets. A single apartment unit at 13 DFU means a 3-inch branch serves at most 2 units. A 4-inch building drain at 1/4" slope handles up to 216 DFU.

### Building Sewer Sizing (Table 717.1)

For building sewers (pipe connecting building drain to public sewer):

| Pipe Size (inches) | Slope 1/8" per foot (max DFU) | Slope 1/4" per foot (max DFU) |
|--------------------|------------------------------|------------------------------|
| 6 and smaller | Per Table 703.2 | Per Table 703.2 |
| 8 | 2800 | 3900 |
| 10 | 4900 | 6800 |
| 12 | 8000 | 11,200 |

(CPC Table 717.1)

**Building sewer slope:** Minimum 1/4 inch per foot. Pipes 4"–6" may be reduced to 1/8" per foot with AHJ approval. Pipes 8" and larger may be reduced to 1/16" per foot with AHJ approval (CPC 718.1).

**Building sewer minimum:** No building sewer shall be smaller than the building drain (CPC 717.1).

## Vent System Sizing

### General Vent Requirements

- Every fixture trap shall be protected against siphonage and backpressure by a vent pipe (CPC 901.2)
- The vent system shall prevent a trap seal from being exposed to a pressure differential exceeding 1 inch water column (CPC 901.3)
- Minimum individual vent diameter: 1-1/4 inches, and not less than one-half the diameter of the drain it serves (CPC 904.1)

### Vent Pipe Sizing (Table 703.2 — Vent portion)

Maximum fixture units and lengths for vent piping:

| Vent Size (inches) | Max DFU | Max Length (ft) |
|--------------------|---------|----------------|
| 1-1/4 | 1 | 45 |
| 1-1/2 | 8 | 60 |
| 2 | 24 | 120 |
| 3 | 84 | 212 |
| 4 | 256 | 300 |
| 5 | 600 | 390 |
| 6 | 1380 | 510 |

(CPC Table 703.2, Vent Piping row)

**Length limitation:** Not more than one-third of the total permitted vent length may be installed horizontally. Where a minimum-sized vent is increased one pipe size for its entire length, the maximum length limitation does not apply (CPC 904.2).

### Vent Stack Aggregate Sizing

The drainage piping of each building shall be vented by one or more vent pipes whose aggregate cross-sectional area is not less than that of the largest required building sewer (CPC 904.1).

### Multi-Story Vent Stack Requirements (10+ Stories)

For drainage stacks extending 10 or more stories (CPC 907.1):
- A **parallel vent stack** is required, extending undiminished in size from its upper terminal
- The vent stack connects to the drainage stack at or immediately below the lowest fixture drain
- **Yoke vents** connect the vent stack to the drainage stack at every 5th floor, counting down from the uppermost fixture drain
- Yoke vent diameter: not less than the smaller of the drainage stack or vent stack diameter
- Yoke vent connection to vent stack: not less than 42 inches above the floor level (CPC 907.2)
- Yoke vent connection to drainage stack: via wye-branch fitting below the lowest drainage branch connection serving that floor

### Vent Termination Through Roof

- Each vent pipe shall extend through the roof undiminished in size (CPC 905.4)
- Vent terminal: minimum 6 inches above the roof (CPC 906.1)
- Minimum 1 foot from any vertical surface (CPC 906.1)
- Minimum 10 feet from, or 3 feet above, any openable window, door, opening, air intake, or vent shaft (CPC 906.2)
- Minimum 3 feet from any lot line (alleys and streets excepted) (CPC 906.2)
- Where roof is used for assembly or parking: vents within 10 feet shall extend not less than 7 feet above the roof (CPC 906.3)

### Wet Venting (CPC 908)

Applicable to bathroom groups within a single story:
- Limited to vertical drainage piping receiving discharge from 1- and 2-fixture-unit fixtures
- Not exceeding 4 fixtures total
- Wet vent shall not exceed 6 feet in developed length (CPC 908.1)
- Wet vent sizing: not less than 2 inches for 4 DFU or less, not less than 3 inches for 5 DFU or more (CPC 908.2.2)

### Circuit Venting (CPC 911)

For common-area or corridor bathrooms:
- Maximum 8 floor-outlet water closets, showers, bathtubs, or floor drains on a horizontal branch (CPC 911.1)
- Circuit vent size per Table 703.2, minimum 2 inches (CPC 911.2)
- A 2-inch relief vent required when 4 or more water closets discharge into a drainage stack receiving upper-floor waste (CPC 911.3)

### Suds Relief (CPC 711)

Applies to multi-unit buildings 3+ stories:
- No drainage connections within 8 feet of a vertical-to-horizontal change of direction of a stack containing suds-producing fixtures (CPC 711.1)
- Suds-producing fixtures: bathtubs, laundries, washing machine standpipes, kitchen sinks, dishwashers
- **Exceptions:** Single-family residences; stacks receiving discharge from less than 3 stories of plumbing fixtures (CPC 711.1)

## Water Supply Sizing

### Fixture Unit Method (Table 610.4)

Table 610.4 provides maximum fixture units for a given meter size, building supply pipe size, developed length, and available pressure. The sizing procedure (CPC 610.7, 610.8):

1. Determine total fixture units from Table 610.3
2. Determine developed length from meter to the most remote outlet
3. Determine elevation difference between meter and highest fixture (add/subtract 0.5 psi per foot of elevation)
4. Determine available pressure at the meter (use minimum pressure where fluctuation occurs)
5. Select the appropriate pressure range group in Table 610.4
6. Follow the length column to find the meter and supply pipe size

|-------------|-------------|--------|--------|--------|--------|

| 1 | 1-1/4 | 38 | 28 | 23 | 17 |

(CPC Table 610.4, 30–45 psi range, selected entries)

### Minimum Pressure Requirements

- Minimum supply pipe size: 3/4 inch (CPC 610.8)
- The water piping system shall be designed on the basis of the minimum pressure available where fluctuation occurs throughout the day (CPC 610.7(5))
- Pressure loss through filters, softeners, backflow preventers, and tankless water heaters shall be included in calculations (CPC 610.2)

### Velocity Limits

- Copper tube cold water: 8 ft/s maximum (CPC 610.12.1)
- Copper tube hot water: 5 ft/s maximum (CPC 610.12.1)
- Copper fittings in non-copper tubing: same limits apply (CPC 610.12.2)

## Water Heater Sizing Considerations for Multi-Unit

### Individual vs. Central Systems

Multi-unit residential buildings may use:
- **Individual water heaters per unit** — sized per unit demand, typically 40–50 gallon tank or tankless
- **Central boiler/recirculation system** — sized for total building demand with diversity factor

### Hot Water Branch Sizing

For separate hot water branches, fixture unit values may be taken as three-quarters of the listed total WSFU value (CPC Table 610.3, Note 3). This reflects that hot and cold demands do not occur simultaneously at full rate.

### Pipe Insulation

Hot water piping shall be insulated per California Energy Code (Title 24, Part 6) requirements. All accessible hot water piping in multi-unit buildings shall be insulated to reduce standby losses.

## Water Meter Sizing for Multi-Unit Buildings

### Master Meter vs. Individual Meters

- Multi-unit buildings may be served by a **master meter** sized for the total building demand
- Individual unit meters (submeters) are addressed separately under CALGreen requirements (see below)
- Meter sizing follows Table 610.4: select meter size based on total WSFU, developed length, and available pressure

### Submetering Requirement (CALGreen 4.303.2)

California Green Building Standards Code requires:
- **Individual water submeters** for each dwelling unit in new multi-unit residential buildings
- Submeters shall be installed for both **hot water and cold water** or for total water use per unit
- This requirement applies to all new R-2 occupancy buildings
- Purpose: enable tracking of individual unit water consumption and billing

(CALGreen Section 4.303.2)

## 35-Unit Test Case: Sizing Summary

For a 35-unit apartment building, using the standard unit fixture profile (WC + lav + tub/shower + kitchen sink + clothes washer + dishwasher):

### Drainage Fixture Units

| Item | Count | DFU Each | Total DFU |
|------|-------|----------|-----------|
| Water closets (1.6 gpf gravity tank) | 35 | 3.0 | 105 |
| Lavatories | 35 | 1.0 | 35 |
| Bathtub/shower combinations | 35 | 2.0 | 70 |
| Kitchen sinks | 35 | 2.0 | 70 |
| Clothes washers (domestic) | 35 | 3.0 | 105 |
| Dishwashers (domestic) | 35 | 2.0 | 70 |
| **Total building DFU** | | | **455** |

**Building drain sizing:** 455 DFU requires a **4-inch building drain** at 1/4" slope (max 216 DFU) — **does not fit**. A **5-inch building drain** at 1/4" slope handles up to 428 DFU — still insufficient. A **6-inch building drain** at 1/4" slope handles up to 720 DFU — **compliant** (CPC Table 703.2).

**Building sewer sizing:** 455 DFU on a 6-inch sewer at 1/4" slope handles up to 720 DFU — **compliant** (CPC Table 703.2/717.1).

### Water Supply Fixture Units

| Item | Count | WSFU Each | Total WSFU |
|------|-------|-----------|------------|
| Water closets (1.6 gpf gravity tank) | 35 | 2.5 | 87.5 |
| Lavatories | 35 | 1.0 | 35 |
| Bathtub/shower combinations (fill) | 35 | 4.0 | 140 |
| Kitchen sinks | 35 | 1.5 | 52.5 |
| Clothes washers | 35 | 4.0 | 140 |
| Dishwashers | 35 | 1.5 | 52.5 |
| **Total building WSFU** | | | **507.5** |

**Water meter and supply sizing (at 46–60 psi, 200 ft developed length):**
- 2-inch meter with 2-1/2-inch supply: 500 WSFU capacity — borderline, verify with exact developed length
- At 300 ft developed length: 440 WSFU — **insufficient**, need larger meter/supply
- A **2-1/2 inch or 3-inch meter** may be required depending on developed length and available pressure

(CPC Table 610.4)

### Vent Stack Sizing

- 455 total DFU requires vent aggregate cross-section not less than the building sewer (6-inch)
- A 4-inch vent stack handles up to 256 DFU — insufficient for the full building
- A **6-inch vent stack** or multiple vents with aggregate area equal to a 6-inch pipe are required
- If 3 or fewer stories: no parallel vent stack required (CPC 907.1 applies only at 10+ stories)
- Each fixture group requires individual or wet venting per CPC 908
