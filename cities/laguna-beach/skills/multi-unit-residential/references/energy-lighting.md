# Multi-Unit Residential Energy & Lighting Requirements

Source: 2025 California Energy Code (Title 24, Part 6), Subchapters 10 and 11; Reference Joint Appendix JA8.

---

## Dwelling Unit Lighting — Section 160.5(a)

### High-Efficacy Light Source Requirement (Section 160.5(a)1A)

All permanently installed luminaires and light sources in dwelling units shall comply with **Reference Joint Appendix JA8** and shall be certified and marked as required by JA8. In practice, this means **all interior fixtures must be LED** or equivalent high-efficacy sources meeting JA8 criteria.

**Exceptions to Section 160.5(a)1A:**
1. **Integrated device lighting** — lighting integral to exhaust fans, kitchen range hoods, bath vanity mirrors, garage door openers, and ceiling fan kits subject to DOE Appliance Standards
2. **Navigation lighting** rated less than 5 watts (night lights, step lights, path lights)
3. **Cabinet lighting** with efficacy of 45 lumens per watt or greater, located internal to drawers, cabinetry, and linen closets
4. **Specific light source types:**
   - LED light sources installed outdoors
   - Inseparable SSL luminaires with colored light sources for decorative lighting
   - HID light sources (pulse start metal halide, high-pressure sodium)
   - Luminaires with hardwired high-frequency generator and induction lamp

### Recessed Downlight Requirements (Section 160.5(a)1C)

Luminaires recessed into ceilings shall meet **all** of the following in addition to JA8 compliance:
- No screw-base lamp sockets
- Certified airtight with air leakage **less than 2.0 CFM at 75 Pascals** per ASTM E283
- Sealed with gasket or caulk between luminaire housing and ceiling; all air leak paths between conditioned and unconditioned spaces sealed
- Meet clearance and installation requirements of California Electrical Code Article 410.116

**Exception:** Recessed luminaires marked for fire-rated installations and luminaires in noninsulated ceilings are exempt from the airtight and sealing requirements.

### Elevated Temperature Marking (Section 160.5(a)1D)

Lamps and separable light sources in enclosed or recessed luminaires shall comply with JA8 **elevated temperature requirements**, including marking.

### Blank Electrical Box Limit (Section 160.5(a)1E)

Electrical boxes more than 5 feet above finished floor without a luminaire or device shall not exceed the **number of bedrooms**. These boxes shall be served by a dimmer, vacancy sensor control, low-voltage wiring, or fan speed control.

## Dwelling Unit Lighting Controls — Section 160.5(a)2

### Manual Controls (Section 160.5(a)2A)

All dwelling unit lighting shall have **readily accessible wall-mounted controls** that allow manual ON and OFF.

**Exception:** Ceiling fans may provide integrated lighting control via remote control.

### Automatic-OFF Controls (Section 160.5(a)2E)

At least one installed luminaire shall be controlled by an **occupancy or vacancy sensor** providing automatic-OFF in the following spaces:
- Bathrooms
- Garages
- Laundry rooms
- Utility rooms
- Walk-in closets

Lighting internal to drawers and cabinetry with opaque fronts shall have controls that turn lighting OFF when the drawer or door is closed.

### Dimming Controls (Section 160.5(a)2F)

Lighting in **habitable spaces** (living rooms, dining rooms, kitchens, bedrooms) shall have readily accessible wall-mounted dimming controls for manual adjustment up and down. Forward phase-cut dimmers controlling LED light sources shall comply with NEMA SSL 7A.

**Exceptions to dimming requirement:**
1. Ceiling fan integrated lighting controlled via remote
2. Luminaires controlled by occupancy/vacancy sensor with automatic-OFF
3. Navigation lighting rated less than 5 watts; lighting internal to drawers/cabinetry

### Independent Controls (Section 160.5(a)2G)

Lighting integrated with exhaust fans shall be **controlled independently from the fans**. The following shall be controlled separately from ceiling-installed lighting:
- Undercabinet lighting
- Undershelf lighting
- Interior lighting of display cabinets
- Switched outlets

## Dwelling Unit Outdoor Lighting Controls — Section 160.5(a)3

Outdoor lighting attached to a building and separately controlled from inside a dwelling unit shall meet:
- Manual ON/OFF control switch that permits automatic actions; **and**
- One of the following:
  - Photocell **and** motion sensor
  - Photocell **and** automatic time-switch control
  - Astronomical time clock control

Overrides to ON shall automatically return to normal operation within **6 hours**.

## Common Area Lighting — Section 160.5(b)

Lighting systems in multifamily common-use service areas shall comply with Sections 160.5(b)1 through 160.5(b)4.

**Exception:** Common areas providing shared provisions for living, eating, cooking, or sanitation to dwelling units that would otherwise lack these provisions may instead comply with dwelling unit rules in Section 160.5(a).

### Luminaire Classification and Power (Section 160.5(b)1)

All luminaire wattages shall be labeled per manufacturer on a permanent, preprinted, factory-installed label per UL 1574, 1598, 2108, or 8750. Wattage determination rules:
- Line-voltage lamps without drivers/ballasts: maximum rated wattage per label
- Luminaires with ballasts: operating input wattage of rated lamp/ballast combination
- Inseparable SSL luminaires: maximum rated input wattage per UL or IES LM-79
- LED tape lighting: installed length times rated linear power density (W/ft) or maximum rated input wattage of driver
- Modular lighting systems (track): greater of 30 W/linear foot of track or rated wattage of all luminaires

### Mandatory Indoor Lighting Controls — Section 160.5(b)4

#### Manual Controls (Section 160.5(b)4A)

Each common-use space shall have lighting controls that allow manual ON and OFF. Controls shall be:
- Readily accessible (exception: restrooms with 2+ stalls, parking areas, stairwells, corridors, and public-access spaces may use controls not accessible to unauthorized personnel)
- Located so controlled lighting or its status is visible
- Provide **separate control** of general, display, ornamental, and special effects lighting

Continuous egress illumination up to **0.1 W/ft2** is permitted without manual controls.

#### Multi-Level Lighting Controls (Section 160.5(b)4B)

Required for any space **100 sq ft or larger** with connected lighting load **greater than 0.5 W/ft2**. Controls shall provide **continuous dimming from 100% to 10% or lower**.

**Exceptions:** Spaces with a single luminaire; restrooms; HID/induction general lighting (one step between 30-70% required instead).

#### Automatic Shut-OFF Controls (Section 160.5(b)4C)

All installed common area indoor lighting shall be equipped with controls to automatically reduce lighting power when unoccupied:
- Occupant sensing control with **maximum 20-minute time delay**, automatic time-switch, or equivalent
- **Separate controls per floor** (except stairwell lighting)
- **Separate control zones not exceeding 5,000 sq ft** per zone enclosed by ceiling-height partitions
- Countdown timer switches: permitted only in closets less than 70 sq ft, 10-minute max
- Automatic time-switch manual override: lighting remains on for **no more than 2 hours**
- Automatic holiday shut-OFF feature required for time-switch controls (24-hour minimum OFF, then resume normal schedule)

#### Occupancy Sensors for Specific Spaces (Section 160.5(b)4Cv)

**Required** in the following spaces with automatic shut-OFF in **20 minutes or less**:
- Offices 250 sq ft or smaller
- Multipurpose rooms less than 1,000 sq ft
- Conference rooms
- Restrooms

In areas requiring multi-level controls, occupant sensing controls shall function as:
- Partial-ON (auto-activate between 50-70% of controlled power); **or**
- Vacancy sensing (manual ON only)

#### Corridor, Stairwell, and Parking Garage Controls (Section 160.5(b)4Cvi)

**Corridors and stairwells:** Lighting controlled by occupant sensing controls that **reduce power by at least 50%** when unoccupied. Controls auto-activate from all designed paths of egress.

**Parking garages, parking areas, loading/unloading areas:**
- Occupant sensing controls shall uniformly reduce lighting power to **between 20% and 50%** of full power
- No more than **500 watts** of rated lighting power per single control zone
- Controls shall auto-activate from all designed paths of egress

#### Daylight Responsive Controls (Section 160.5(b)4D)

Required in the following common area locations:
- **Skylit daylit zones** where general lighting wattage is **75 watts or greater**
- **Primary sidelit daylit zones** where general lighting wattage is **75 watts or greater**
- **Secondary sidelit daylit zones** where general lighting wattage is **75 watts or greater** (controlled independently from primary)
- **Parking garage sidelit daylit zones** where general lighting wattage is **60 watts or greater**

Daylight responsive controls shall:
- Provide continuous dimming where multi-level controls are required
- Ensure combined illuminance from controlled lighting and daylight is not less than lighting-only illuminance
- Reduce controlled lighting power by **minimum 90%** when daylight exceeds 150% of controlled lighting illuminance (non-garage areas)
- Reduce controlled lighting power by **100%** for parking garages when daylight exceeds 150% threshold
- Have photosensors not readily accessible to unauthorized personnel

## Outdoor Lighting Controls — Section 160.5(c)

### Luminaire Shielding (Section 160.5(c)1)

All outdoor luminaires of **6,200 initial luminaire lumens or greater** shall comply with **BUG (Backlight, Uplight, Glare)** requirements per ANSI/IES TM-15-20, Annex A, and Title 24 Part 11 Section 5.106.8.

**Key exceptions:** Signs; building facade/monument/art lighting; temporary outdoor lighting; dwelling-unit-controlled outdoor lighting; luminaires illuminating the public right of way.

### Outdoor Lighting Controls (Section 160.5(c)2)

Outdoor lighting shall be **independently controlled** from other electrical loads and shall meet:

**Daylight availability (Section 160.5(c)2A):** All outdoor lighting controlled by photo control, astronomical time-switch, or equivalent to auto-shut-OFF when daylight is available.

**Automatic scheduling (Section 160.5(c)2B):**
- Installed for all outdoor lighting
- Capable of reducing outdoor lighting power by **50-90%** and separately capable of turning lighting OFF during unoccupied periods
- Minimum **two nighttime periods** with independent lighting levels
- Override allowed for no more than **2 hours**

**Motion sensing controls (Section 160.5(c)2C):**
- Required for outdoor luminaires for general hardscape, parking lots, and outdoor canopies where mounted **24 feet above grade or lower**
- Reduce lighting power by **50-90%** or turn OFF during unoccupied periods
- Return to dim/OFF state within **15 minutes** after area vacated
- No more than **1,500 watts** per single sensor or zone
- **Exception:** Luminaires with maximum rated wattage of 40 watts each; facade, ornamental hardscape, or outdoor dining lighting

---

## Sign Lighting Controls — Section 160.5(d)

- **Indoor signs:** Controlled with automatic time-switch or astronomical time-switch (except exit signs)
- **Outdoor signs:** Controlled with photocontrol **plus** automatic time-switch, or astronomical time-switch
- Outdoor signs ON both day and night shall have a dimmer providing **minimum 65% power reduction** during nighttime hours

## Lighting Control Acceptance Testing — Section 160.5(e)

Before occupancy permit is granted, the following systems shall be certified per Reference Nonresidential Appendix NA7.6 and NA7.8:
- Daylight responsive controls (NA7.6.1)
- Lighting shut-OFF controls (NA7.6.2)
- Demand responsive lighting controls (NA7.6.3)
- Outdoor lighting controls (NA7.8)
- Institutional tuning power adjustment factor systems (NA7.6.4)

Testing shall be performed by a **Certified Lighting Controls Acceptance Test Technician (CLCATT)** when required by Part 1 Section 10-103.1.

## Indoor Lighting Power Density (LPD) — Section 170.2(e)

### Compliance Method (Section 170.2(e)1)

Common area lighting complies if the **adjusted indoor lighting power** (all areas combined) does not exceed the **allowed indoor lighting power** calculated using the Area Category Method.

Dwelling unit lighting is governed by mandatory Section 160.5(a) requirements (JA8), not LPD calculations.

### Area Category Method — LPD Limits (Table 170.2-M)

| Common Area Type | Allowed LPD (W/ft2) | Qualifying Additional Allowances |
|---|---|---|
| **Corridor** | 0.40 | +0.25 decorative/display |
| **Lobby, Main Entry** | 0.70 | +0.25 display; wall display allowances per linear foot |
| **Conference/Multipurpose** | 0.75 | +0.25 display; wall/floor display per mounting height |
| **Office > 250 sq ft** | 0.60 | +0.20 decorative/display/portable |
| **Office <= 250 sq ft** | 0.65 | +0.20 decorative/display/portable |
| **Parking Garage — Parking Zone & Ramps** | 0.10 | +100W first ATM; +50W each additional |
| **Parking Garage — Daylight Adaptation Zone** | 1.00 | None |
| **Stairwell** | 0.60 | +0.35 decorative/display |
| **Restroom** | 0.65 | +0.35 display/decorative |
| **Storage** | 0.40 | None |
| **Laundry Area** | 0.45 | None |
| **Locker Room** | 0.45 | None |
| **Lounge/Breakroom/Waiting** | 0.55 | +0.25 display/decorative |
| **Concourse and Atrium** | 0.60 | +0.25 display/decorative |
| **Exercise/Fitness/Gym** | 0.50 | None |
| **Kitchen/Food Prep** | 0.95 | None |
| **Dining — Cafeteria/Fast Food** | 0.45 | +0.25 display/decorative |
| **All Other** | 0.40 | None |

**Aging Eye/Low-Vision spaces** (licensed senior care, adult day care): Higher LPD allowances apply (e.g., Lobby 0.85, Corridor 0.70, Stairwell 0.80).

Daylight adaptation zones in parking garages shall be **no longer than 66 feet** from the entrance.

### LPD Calculation Rules (Section 170.2(e)3)

- Conditioned area and unconditioned area lighting power allotments calculated **separately** — no trade-offs between them
- Indoor and outdoor lighting power allotments calculated **separately** — no trade-offs between them
- LPD allowances for general lighting may be traded between areas of the same conditioning type, but additional allowances (display, decorative, task) may **not** be traded

### Power Adjustment Factors (Table 170.2-L)

Installed watts may be reduced for LPD calculations when qualifying controls are provided **in addition to** mandatory controls:

| Control Type | Area | PAF |
|---|---|---|
| Daylight continuous dimming + OFF | Skylit/primary sidelit daylit zones | 0.10 |
| Occupant sensing — open office > 250 ft2 | Zone <= 125 sq ft per sensor | 0.30 |
| Occupant sensing — open office > 250 ft2 | Zone 126-250 sq ft per sensor | 0.20 |
| Institutional tuning | Nondaylit areas | 0.10 |
| Institutional tuning | Daylit areas | 0.05 |
| Demand responsive control | General lighting not in scope of 110.12(c) | 0.05 |

PAFs are earned by controls **above and beyond** what is mandatory. Only one PAF per luminaire unless specifically noted as combinable.

## Outdoor Lighting Power Budget — Section 170.2(e)6

### General Hardscape Allowance (Table 170.2-R)

| Lighting Zone | Area Wattage Allowance (AWA) | Initial Wattage Allowance (IWA) |
|---|---|---|
| LZ0 | No allowance (continuous lighting prohibited) | No allowance |
| LZ1 | 0.026 W/ft2 | 300 W |
| LZ2 | 0.030 W/ft2 | 350 W |
| LZ3 | 0.038 W/ft2 | 400 W |
| LZ4 | 0.055 W/ft2 | 450 W |

The illuminated hardscape area is defined as any hardscape within a square pattern around each luminaire that is **ten times the luminaire mounting height**, less areas within buildings, beyond hardscape, beyond property lines, or obstructed.

One IWA is permitted per site.

### Additional Allowances for Specific Applications (Table 170.2-S)

| Application | LZ1 | LZ2 | LZ3 | LZ4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Building entrance/exit (per door) | 9 W | 15 W | 19 W | 21 W |
| Primary entrance, senior care (per entrance) | 20 W | 40 W | 57 W | 60 W |
| ATM lighting (per ATM, within 50 ft) | 100 W first; 35 W each additional | same | same | same |
| Hardscape ornamental lighting | None | 0.007 W/ft2 | 0.013 W/ft2 | 0.019 W/ft2 |
| Building facade | None | 0.100 W/ft2 | 0.170 W/ft2 | 0.225 W/ft2 |
| Canopies and tunnels | 0.057 W/ft2 | 0.137 W/ft2 | 0.270 W/ft2 | 0.370 W/ft2 |
| Outdoor dining | 0.004 W/ft2 | 0.030 W/ft2 | 0.050 W/ft2 | 0.075 W/ft2 |
| Security camera (hardscape) | None | 0.018 W/ft2 | 0.018 W/ft2 | 0.018 W/ft2 |

Outdoor lighting not subject to LPD limits: temporary lighting, FAA/Coast Guard, public streets/highways, sports fields, public monuments, landscape lighting, sign lighting per Section 170.2(e)7, stair/ADA ramp lighting, and theatrical/performance lighting.

## Title 24 Joint Appendix JA8 — High-Efficacy Light Sources

### Summary

JA8 defines the requirements for light sources to be classified as "high efficacy" for dwelling unit compliance under Section 160.5(a). Key requirements include:

- **Minimum efficacy:** Light sources must meet the lumen-per-watt thresholds defined in JA8 (generally 45+ lm/W for most source types)
- **Certification and marking:** All JA8-compliant products must be certified to the California Energy Commission and marked accordingly
- **Elevated temperature performance:** Light sources in enclosed or recessed luminaires must be tested and marked for elevated temperature operation per JA8
- **Color quality:** JA8 specifies minimum color rendering index (CRI) and color consistency requirements
- **Flicker:** JA8 includes flicker performance requirements for LED sources

In practice, **LED luminaires and LED lamps** are the standard path to JA8 compliance. Specifiers should verify products are listed on the CEC Appliance Database as JA8-compliant.

## Electric Power Distribution for Lighting — Section 160.6

### Metering (Section 160.6(a))

Each electrical service or feeder providing power to common-use areas shall have permanently installed metering for kW demand and kWh tracking. Requirements scale with service rating (Table 160.6-A).

### Circuit Separation (Section 160.6(b))

Lighting circuits (including exit, egress, and exterior lighting) shall be separated from other load types for monitoring purposes:
- Services rated 50 kVA or less: not required
- Services > 50 kVA to 250 kVA: all lighting in aggregate
- Services > 250 kVA: all lighting disaggregated by floor, type, or area

## 35-Unit Test Case Application

For a typical 35-unit multi-family building (e.g., 3- to 4-story apartment or condo project):

### Dwelling Units (all 35)

- **Every permanently installed luminaire** must be JA8-compliant (LED)
- Recessed downlights: no screw-base sockets, airtight rated (< 2.0 CFM at 75 Pa), sealed with gasket/caulk
- Blank electrical boxes above 5 feet limited to number of bedrooms per unit
- Dimming controls required in living rooms, dining rooms, kitchens, bedrooms
- Occupancy/vacancy sensors required in bathrooms, garages, laundry rooms, utility rooms, walk-in closets
- Exhaust fan lighting controlled independently from fan operation

### Common Areas (corridors, lobby, parking garage, stairs, amenity spaces)

- **LPD compliance required** using Area Category Method (Table 170.2-M)
- Corridors: max 0.40 W/ft2 with occupancy sensors reducing power by 50% minimum when unoccupied
- Lobby: max 0.70 W/ft2 with manual controls, multi-level dimming, and shut-OFF controls
- Parking garage: max 0.10 W/ft2 with occupancy sensors reducing to 20-50% when unoccupied (max 500W per zone), daylight responsive controls at entries (60W threshold)
- Stairwells: max 0.60 W/ft2 with occupancy sensors reducing power by 50% minimum when unoccupied
- All common spaces 100 sq ft or larger with > 0.5 W/ft2: continuous dimming from 100% to 10%
- Daylight responsive controls in skylit and sidelit zones with 75W or more of general lighting
- Automatic shut-OFF with max 20-minute timeout, separate zones per floor, max 5,000 sq ft per zone

### Exterior Lighting

- Determine applicable lighting zone (LZ0-LZ4) per Part 1 Section 10-114
- Calculate allowed wattage: (illuminated hardscape area x AWA) + IWA
- Add specific application allowances (building entrances, canopies, etc.)
- Photo controls or astronomical time-switch for daylight shut-OFF
- Automatic scheduling with two nighttime levels
- Motion sensing on all fixtures mounted 24 feet or lower (except those 40W or less)
- BUG compliance for luminaires 6,200 lumens or greater

### Acceptance Testing

- Daylight responsive controls: NA7.6.1
- Shut-OFF controls: NA7.6.2
- Outdoor lighting controls: NA7.8
- All testing by CLCATT when required by Part 1
