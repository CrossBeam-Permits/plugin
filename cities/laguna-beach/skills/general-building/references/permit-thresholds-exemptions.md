# When Minor Work Needs a Permit

Source: 2025 California Residential Code (Title 24, Part 2.5) §R105.2, Work Exempt from Permit;
2025 California Building Code (Title 24, Part 2) §105.2; CBC Appendix J §J103 for earthwork.

The state code lists work that needs no building permit. A city may amend the list downward, and
the local number governs. Read this file to decide whether there is anything to review; read the
city delta skill before clearing anything on a state threshold.

---

## State thresholds this skill reaches

| Work | Permit required under state code | Section |
|---|---|---|
| One-story detached accessory structure used as a tool or storage shed, floor area at or below 120 sq ft | No | CBC 105.2 |
| Fence not over 7 ft high | No | CRC R105.2 |
| Retaining wall not over 4 ft measured bottom of footing to top of wall, level backfill, no surcharge, no Class I/II/IIIA liquids | No | CRC R105.2 |
| Deck at or below 200 sq ft, not more than 30 in above grade at any point, not attached to the dwelling, not serving the required exit door | No | CRC R105.2 |
| Repair that cuts a structural member, removes a load-bearing support, or changes the means of egress | Yes, always | CRC R105.2.2 |
| Earthwork above the Appendix J thresholds the city has adopted | Yes, a grading permit | CBC Appendix J §J103 |

**A permit that is not required is still a finding.** Say the work is exempt and cite the
section rather than reviewing an exempt project as if it were regulated. The applicant may still
want the permit on record, and the city may still issue one.

---

## Reading a threshold correctly

- **Shed floor area** is the structure's own floor area, not the pad. A structure above the
  threshold, or one with plumbing, electrical service, sleeping space, or an engineered
  foundation, is no longer a simple accessory structure.
- **Retaining wall height** is measured from the bottom of the footing to the top of the wall,
  not the exposed face. A wall with a 3 ft face and a 2 ft footing is a 5 ft wall.
- **Fence versus retaining wall**: a wall that holds back soil is a retaining wall at any height
  and uses the 4 ft threshold. A wall standing on level ground with a yard on both sides is a
  fence. Get this right before anything else — the whole review changes.
- **Deck height** is measured at any point within 36 in of the edge, not at the door.

---

## Cities amend these numbers

Local amendments to the exemption lists are common and they only go one direction: down. A city
may require a permit for a 48 sq ft shed, a 12 in fence, or a 12 in retaining wall. Always load
the city delta before applying a state threshold. When the city delta is silent, the state
threshold governs and the review says so explicitly.
