## Roof Assembly, Eaves, Gutters, Vents & Spark Arrestors

### 1. Roof Assembly — Class A Required

All roofs in a WUI area must have a **Class A** fire classification roof assembly tested per **ASTM E108 or UL 790**. (CWUIC § 504.2.)

#### Exempt from Testing (Deemed Class A)
The following are automatically equivalent to Class A without testing:
1. Brick, masonry, or exposed concrete roof deck
2. Ferrous or copper shingles/sheets, metal sheets and shingles, clay or concrete roof tile, or slate — installed on **noncombustible decks** (or ferrous/copper/metal installed without deck on noncombustible framing)
3. Minimum 16 oz/sq ft copper sheets over **combustible** roof decks
4. Slate over ASTM D226 Type II underlayment over combustible decks

(CWUIC § 504.2, Exceptions 1–4.)

#### Fire-Retardant-Treated Wood Shingles/Shakes
Must be OSFM-listed and comply with CBC § 1505.6. Must achieve Class A when part of an assembly. (CWUIC § 503.2.3.)

### 2. Roof Covering Voids — Airspace Under Covering

Where the roofing profile creates an airspace under the covering over a combustible deck:

**Option A — Cap Sheet Method (§ 504.2.1.1):**
- Install 72 lb cap sheet complying with ASTM D3909 over the roof deck; **Exception:** cap sheet not required if ≥1 inch mineral wool board or other noncombustible material is between roofing and framing/deck
- Bird stops at eaves (where profile fits) to prevent debris entry
- Hip and ridge caps mudded in to prevent fire/ember intrusion

**Option B — Listed Underlayment Method (§ 504.2.1.2):**
- Listed/labeled underlayment tested as part of the assembly per ASTM E108 / UL 790 for Class A
- Install per manufacturer's instructions and listing
- Bird stops at eaves; hip/ridge mudded in
- **Exception:** Underlayment need not be Class A where roof sheathing is exterior FRTW per CBC § 2303.2

### 3. Roof Valleys

Valley flashings (where provided) must be **≥0.019-inch (No. 26 galvanized sheet gage) corrosion-resistant metal** installed over a **36-inch-wide** underlayment of one layer of 72-pound mineral-surfaced non-perforated cap sheet (ASTM D3909) running the full length of the valley. (CWUIC § 504.2.2.)

### 4. Enclosed Eaves and Soffits

The **exposed underside of enclosed roof eaves and soffits** must be protected by one of the following (CWUIC § 504.3):
1. Noncombustible materials
2. Ignition-resistant building materials (§ 503.2)
3. Materials approved for ≥1-hour fire-resistance-rated construction (exterior side), per ASTM E119 or UL 263
4. 2-inch nominal dimension lumber
5. 1-inch nominal FRTW lumber or ¾-inch nominal FRTW plywood, labeled for exterior use (CBC § 2303.2)
6. Boxed-in soffit assembly meeting performance criteria in § 504.7.2 when tested per ASTM E2957
7. Boxed-in soffit assembly meeting performance criteria per SFM Standard 12-7A-3

**Facias (enclosed eaves):** Required and must be ignition-resistant building material, FRTW labeled for exterior use (CBC § 2303.2), ≥1-hour fire-resistance-rated material, or 2-inch nominal dimension lumber.

### 5. Open (Unenclosed) Eaves

The **exposed roof deck on the underside of unenclosed roof eaves** must comply with one of (CWUIC § 504.3.1):
1. Noncombustible material
2. Ignition-resistant building material
3. FRTW labeled for exterior use (CBC § 2303.2)
4. Materials approved for ≥1-hour fire-resistance-rated construction on exterior side (ASTM E119 or UL 263)
5. One layer of 5/8-inch Type X gypsum sheathing behind an exterior covering on the underside of the roof deck
6. Exterior portion of a 1-hour fire-resistance-rated exterior assembly (ASTM E119 / UL 263), applied to underside of deck — including Gypsum Association Fire Resistance Design Manual assemblies

**Fascias (open eaves):** If provided, must be FRTW (CBC § 2303.2), ignition-resistant building material, ≥1-hour fire-resistance-rated material, or 2-inch nominal lumber.

### 6. Gutters and Downspouts

- Must be **noncombustible material** (§ 504.4)
- Must be provided with an **approved means to prevent accumulation of leaves and debris** (i.e., gutter guard/cover)
- Vinyl/plastic gutters do not comply
- Aluminum and steel gutters comply

### 7. Vents — Ember and Flame Intrusion

All ventilation openings (attic, gable ends, ridge, under eaves, cornices, eave soffits, enclosed rafter spaces, underfloor, foundation, crawl space, or any other) must resist building ignition from ember/flame intrusion. (CWUIC § 504.10.)

#### Option A — Performance-Tested WUI Vents (§ 504.10.1)
Openings must be fully covered with:
- **OSFM-listed** "Wildfire Flame and Ember Resistant" vents; **OR**
- Vents tested per **ASTM E2886** meeting all three criteria:
  1. No flaming ignition of cotton material during Ember Intrusion Test
  2. No flaming ignition during Integrity Test (Flame Intrusion Test)
  3. Unexposed-side temp does not exceed 662°F (350°C)

> This is the required path for all horizontal/low-slope and enclosed-space vents (attic, soffit, crawl, foundation).

#### Option B — Off-Ridge and Ridge Vents on Sloped Roof (§ 504.10.2)
For vents installed on a sloped roof (dormer vents):
- Mesh openings: **minimum 1/16 inch, maximum 1/8 inch** in diameter
- Both vent and mesh must be **noncombustible**
- Both vent and mesh must be **corrosion-resistant**

> Standard aluminum insect screen (typically 1/16 inch) can comply if noncombustible and corrosion-resistant. Fiberglass screen fails.

#### Vent Setback (§ 504.10.3)
- **Gable-end and dormer vents** must be located **≥10 feet from lot lines**
- Underfloor ventilation openings must be located **as close to grade as practical**

### 8. Skylights

- **Fixed skylights:** Must comply with exterior glazing requirements — multilayered with ≥1 tempered pane, or glass block, or fire-rated glazing. (§ 504.8.)
- **Operable skylights:** Must be protected by a **noncombustible mesh screen** with openings **≤1/8 inch**. (§ 504.8.2.)

### 9. Reroof / Roof Replacement Trigger

When a building or structure **existing prior to code adoption** has its roof covering **replaced**, or has **50% or more replaced within any 12-month period**, the replaced area must meet Class A requirements for new construction (§§ 504.2 and 504.2.1). (CWUIC § 507.1.)

All portions of roof applied during **any addition, alteration, or repair** must be at least **Class A**. (CWUIC § 507.1.)

> This means even a partial reroof on an existing building in a FHSZ triggers the Class A requirement for the portion being replaced.

### 10. Spark Arrestors — Chimneys and Stovepipes

Required on all chimneys serving fireplaces, barbecues, incinerators, or decorative heating appliances that burn solid or liquid fuel. (CWUIC § 605.1.)

**Mesh specification:**
- Woven or welded wire screening
- 12 USA standard gauge wire (0.1046 inch / 2.66 mm)
- Openings **≤1/2 inch (12.7 mm)**

**Net free area:** Spark arrestor net free area must be **≥4× the net free area** of the chimney outlet. (CWUIC § 605.2.)

**Voluntary guidance (Appendix G):** Covers should have 3/8-inch to 1/2-inch openings; remove all tree branches within 10 feet of any chimney or stovepipe outlet.

### Key Plan Check Items — Roof/Eaves/Vents

| Item to Show on Plans | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Roof assembly specification | Product name, listing, Class A rating, ASTM E108 / UL 790 test data or deemed-Class A category |
| Valley flashing detail | 0.019″ corrosion-resistant metal over 72-lb cap sheet, 36″ wide |
| Cap sheet or underlayment under tile/metal | ASTM D3909 cap sheet OR listed Class A underlayment |
| Bird stops at eaves | Required for profiled roofing (tile, metal) |
| Hip/ridge mudding | Required for profiled roofing |
| Soffit/eave underside material | One of the 7 listed options (§ 504.3) with product identification |
| Fascia material | Noncombustible, FRTW, or 2-inch nominal lumber |
| Gutter material | Noncombustible (aluminum, steel, copper) — NOT vinyl |
| Gutter leaf/debris guard | Any approved means |
| Attic/eave/soffit vents | OSFM-listed WUI vent with product name/model |
| Ridge/dormer slope vents | 1/16″–1/8″ noncombustible mesh |
| Gable-end vent setback from lot line | ≥10 ft |
| Operable skylight screen | Noncombustible mesh ≤1/8″ openings |
| Spark arrestor on fireplace/fire pit | 12-gauge mesh, ≤1/2″ openings, net free area ≥4× flue |
