## Exterior Walls, Underfloor, Projections, Decks, Windows & Doors

### 1. Exterior Walls

Exterior walls must be constructed using **one or more of these nine methods** (CWUIC § 504.5):

1. 1-hour fire-resistance-rated construction (exterior side)
2. Noncombustible materials
3. Heavy timber — sawn lumber or glue-lam with smallest nominal dimension ≥4 inches; or planks splined/T&G/close-set and spiked
4. Log wall construction (solid wood members, smallest horizontal dimension ≥6 inches)
5. Wall assemblies tested per ASTM E2707 (10-minute direct flame contact), meeting conditions of acceptance in § 504.9.3
6. Wall assemblies per SFM Standard 12-7A-1 (10-minute direct flame contact)
7. Wall assemblies with one layer of 5/8-inch Type X gypsum sheathing behind the exterior wall covering (exterior side of framing)
8. Wall assemblies with 1-hour fire-resistance rating rated from exterior side, per ASTM E119 or UL 263
9. Fire-retardant-treated wood labeled for exterior use, per CBC § 2303.2

**Extent:** Wall material must extend **from top of foundation to underside of roof sheathing**. (§ 504.5, last sentence.)

**Exception:** If the wall is covered with an exterior wall covering that complies with § 504.5.2 and 504.5.2.1, the base wall need not independently meet the above list.

#### 1A. Wall-to-Ground/Deck/Roof Flashing (§ 504.5.1)
Minimum **6-inch metal flashing or noncombustible material**, applied vertically on the exterior wall, at **ground, decking, and roof intersections**.

#### 1B. Exterior Wall Coverings (§ 504.5.2)
Exterior wall coverings must comply with one or more:
1. Noncombustible material
2. Ignition-resistant building material labeled for exterior use
3. FRTW labeled for exterior use (CBC § 2303.2)
4. FRTW shingles/shakes qualifying as "Class B" roof covering (CBC § 1505.6) — acceptable as wall covering over solid sheathing

**Exception:** Wall coverings that are a component of an approved assembly complying with § 504.5 (the full wall assembly test methods listed above).

**Extent of wall covering (§ 504.5.2.1):** Must extend **from top of foundation to roof**; must terminate at 2-inch nominal solid wood blocking between rafters at roof overhangs, or at the enclosure for enclosed eaves.

#### Common Siding Products — How They Qualify

| Product | Qualification Path |
|---|---|
| Stucco (cement plaster over metal lath) | Noncombustible — qualifies under Option 1 |
| Fiber cement siding (e.g., HardiePlank) | Must be listed as ignition-resistant building material with FSI ≤25 on 30-min ASTM E84 test AND OSFM listing. Standard fiber cement without OSFM listing may not qualify. |
| Metal siding (steel, aluminum ≥0.019″) | Noncombustible — qualifies |
| FRTW siding | Must be labeled for exterior use, CBC § 2303.2 |
| Composite/engineered wood siding | Must have FSI ≤25 on 30-minute E84 test and OSFM listing; weathering per § 503.2.4.3 |
| Vinyl/PVC siding | Combustible; does NOT qualify unless tested to FSI ≤25 with weathering performance — rare. Typically fails. |
| Plain wood/cedar/redwood siding | Combustible; does not qualify unless FRTW treated and labeled |
| Log wall (solid ≥6 inch) | Qualifies under Log Wall Construction definition |

> **Surface coatings, paints, stains do NOT qualify** as an approved method of protection. (§ 503.2.5.)

### 2. Underfloor Enclosure

Buildings must have **underfloor areas enclosed to the ground** with exterior walls complying with § 504.5. (CWUIC § 504.6.)

**Exception:** Complete enclosure not required where:
- Underside of exposed floors AND exposed structural columns, beams, and supporting walls are protected as required for:
  - Exterior 1-hour fire-resistance-rated construction; OR
  - Heavy timber construction; OR
  - FRTW labeled for exterior use (CBC § 2303.2)

### 3. Projections — Balconies, Cantilevers, Appendages (Not Decks)

Unenclosed accessory structures **attached to habitable buildings**, and **projections other than decks**, must be heavy timber construction or one of the following (CWUIC § 504.7):
1. Noncombustible materials
2. FRTW labeled for exterior use (CBC § 2303.2)
3. Ignition-resistant building materials (§ 503.2)
4. Materials approved for ≥1-hour fire-resistance-rated construction (exterior side), per ASTM E119 or UL 263
5. One layer 5/8-inch Type X gypsum sheathing behind exterior covering on underside of ceiling
6. Exterior portion of 1-hour assembly (ASTM E119/UL 263) applied to underside of ceiling — including Gypsum Association assemblies
7. Underside of floor projection meeting § 504.7.2 conditions when tested per ASTM E2957
8. Underside of floor projection meeting SFM Standard 12-7A-3

**Underside of projections (§ 504.7.1):** Must be enclosed to grade per chapter requirements, or underside of exposed underfloor protected by one of 8 listed methods (identical to § 504.7 list above).

**Exception to § 504.7.1:** Structural columns and beams need not be individually protected when constructed of sawn lumber or glue-laminated wood with smallest minimum nominal dimension ≥4 inches.

### 4. Decks, Porches, Balconies, and Stairs

#### 4A. Flashing at Deck-to-Wall (§ 504.7.3.1)
Minimum **6-inch metal flashing**, applied vertically on the exterior wall, at all **deck-to-wall intersections**. (Same requirement as § 504.5.1 — must be shown on detail.)

#### 4B. Walking Surface Material (§ 504.7.3.2)
The walking surface of decks, porches, balconies, and stairs must be one of:
1. Material tested per ASTM E2632 AND ASTM E2726, meeting conditions in § 504.7.3.3
2. Ignition-resistant building material per § 503.2.4 (FSI ≤25, 30-min ASTM E84)
3. Material meeting SFM Standard 12-7A-4 AND § 503.2.4
4. FRTW labeled for exterior use (CBC § 2303.2)
5. Noncombustible material (concrete, porcelain tile, stone, etc.)
6. Material meeting SFM Standard 12-7A-4A **+ attached exterior wall covering** is noncombustible or ignition-resistant. Exception: wall may be any code-compliant material if decking meets Class B FSI per ASTM E84/UL 723.
7. Material meeting ASTM E2632 (peak HRR ≤25 kW/ft²) **+ attached exterior wall covering** is noncombustible or ignition-resistant. Exception: same Class B wall exception as Option 6.

#### Conditions of Acceptance for ASTM E2632/E2726 Tests (§ 504.7.3.3)
- **ASTM E2632:** Peak heat release rate ≤25 kW/ft² (269 kW/m²); no sustained flaming or glowing at end of 40-minute observation; no burning falling particles reaching floor
- **ASTM E2726:** Using Class A size roof test brand; no sustained flaming or glowing at 40 minutes; no burning falling particles
- Must also pass ASTM E84/UL 723 with FSI ≤25

#### Common Decking Products

| Product | Qualification Path |
|---|---|
| Concrete / masonry | Noncombustible — qualifies |
| Porcelain or ceramic tile | Noncombustible — qualifies |
| Natural stone | Noncombustible — qualifies |
| Composite decking (e.g., Trex) | Must show ASTM E2632/E2726 test data or OSFM listing; many composite brands have obtained WUI listing |
| FRTW decking | Must be labeled for exterior use, CBC § 2303.2 |
| Steel/aluminum grating | Noncombustible — qualifies |
| Untreated wood decking (redwood, cedar) | Does NOT qualify — combustible, not ignition-resistant |
| PVC/vinyl decking | Must show ASTM E2632 ≤25 kW/ft² and weathering test; check OSFM listing |

### 5. Exterior Glazing — Windows, Window Walls, Glazed Doors, Skylights

All exterior windows, window walls, glazed doors, windows within exterior doors, and skylights must be one of the following (CWUIC § 504.8):

1. **Multilayered glazed panels** with at least **one pane of tempered glass** complying with CBC § 2406
2. **Glass block**
3. **Glazing with fire-protection rating of ≥20 minutes** (NFPA 257 or UL 9, exempt from hose stream test)
4. **Glazing meeting SFM Standard 12-7A-2** performance requirements

> **Option 1 is the typical path** for standard window and door products. This means dual-pane windows where **at least one pane is tempered**. Most modern dual-pane windows (standard IGU) use at least one tempered pane — verify with manufacturer's specs.

> **Single-pane windows, single-pane skylights, and standard dual-pane without tempering do NOT comply.**

**Structural glass veneer (§ 504.8.1):** The wall assembly behind structural glass veneer must comply with § 504.5 (exterior wall requirements).

**Operable skylights (§ 504.8.2):** Must be protected by a noncombustible mesh screen with openings **≤1/8 inch**. (Also see roof-eaves-vents.md.)

### 6. Exterior Doors

Exterior doors must comply with one of the following (CWUIC § 504.9):
1. Noncombustible construction
2. Solid-core wood, **≥1¾ inches thick** (44 mm)
3. Solid-core wood with stiles/rails **≥1⅜ inches** thick and raised panels **≥1¼ inches** thick (except perimeter taper to tongue ≥3/8 inch)
4. Fire-protection rating of **≥20 minutes** (NFPA 252, UL 10B, or UL 10C)
5. Exterior surface/cladding meeting § 504.9.3 conditions when tested per ASTM E2707
6. Exterior surface/cladding tested per SFM Standard 12-7A-1

Windows within doors and glazed doors must comply with the glazing requirements of § 504.8.

**Conditions of Acceptance for ASTM E2707 (§ 504.9.3):**
- No flame penetration through the wall assembly at any time
- No evidence of glowing combustion on interior surface at end of 70-minute test

#### Garage Doors

**Battery backup (§ 504.9.1):** Automatic garage door openers for **vehicle doors serving a residential building** must be equipped with a **battery backup function**.

**Ember gap (§ 504.9.2):** Exterior garage doors must resist ember intrusion by limiting gaps at bottom, sides, and top to **≤1/8 inch**. Control gaps with one or more of:
1. Weather-stripping meeting ASTM D638 tensile strength testing (before and after ASTM G155 weathering 2,000 hours, max 10% tensile difference) AND UL 94 flammability rating **V-2 or better**
2. Door overlaps onto jambs and headers
3. Jambs and headers covered with **metal flashing**

### Key Plan Check Items — Walls/Windows/Doors/Decks

| Item to Show on Plans | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Exterior wall assembly ID | Method from § 504.5 (items 1–9); product listings if ignition-resistant or FRTW |
| Siding/cladding product | OSFM listing or test data (FSI ≤25, 30-min E84) if not noncombustible or FRTW |
| Siding extent | Foundation to roof; terminated at rafter blocking or eave enclosure |
| Wall-to-ground flashing | 6″ vertical noncombustible metal flashing at grade, deck, and roof intersections |
| Underfloor enclosure | Walls to grade per § 504.5 or protected underside per § 504.6 exception |
| Window specifications | Multilayered with ≥1 tempered pane — confirm with window schedule/submittal |
| Skylight specification | Same as windows + noncombustible screen ≤1/8″ if operable |
| Glazed door specification | Same as exterior windows |
| Exterior door type | Solid-core ≥1¾″, noncombustible, or 20-min rated |
| Garage door battery backup | Note on plans for all motorized residential garage doors |
| Garage door ember gap | Confirm ≤1/8″ gap control method — weather stripping, overlap, or metal flashing |
| Deck material | Product name, ASTM E2632/E2726 test data or OSFM listing; or noncombustible |
| Deck-to-wall flashing | 6″ vertical noncombustible metal flashing at all deck-wall intersections |
| Projection underside | Protected per § 504.7 / 504.7.1 — show material and method |
| 5/8″ Type X gypsum note | If using gypsum sheathing path, must be on exterior side of framing |
