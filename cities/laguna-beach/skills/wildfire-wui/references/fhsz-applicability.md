## FHSZ / WUI Applicability

### What Triggers These Requirements

CWUIC Chapter 5 (ignition-resistant construction) applies to **buildings and structures hereafter constructed, modified, or relocated** into or within a wildland-urban interface area. (CWUIC § 503.1.)

A **wildland-urban interface area** means:
- Any geographical area identified by the State Fire Marshal as a **Fire Hazard Severity Zone (FHSZ)** per PRC §§ 4201–4204 and Gov. Code §§ 51175–51189; **OR**
- Any other area designated by the enforcing agency to be at significant risk from wildfires. (CWUIC § 202, definition of "Wildland-Urban Interface Area.")

### Two Types of Responsibility Areas

| Area Type | Designation Authority | Who Sets Requirements |
|---|---|---|
| **SRA (State Responsibility Area)** | Board of Forestry / CAL FIRE — includes forests and wildlands | State minimum (CWUIC Part 7) applies directly |
| **LRA (Local Responsibility Area)** | Cities and counties adopting per Gov. Code §§ 51175–51189 | VHFHSZ and HFHSZ designations trigger state minimum; local agency may be more restrictive |

**VHFHSZ (Very High Fire Hazard Severity Zone)** — highest designation. All state requirements apply in full.

**HFHSZ (High Fire Hazard Severity Zone)** — state requirements also apply. Some local jurisdictions apply additional overlays.

> Note: The 2023 OSFM FHSZ map update (dated September 29, 2023) is incorporated by reference into the 2025 CWUIC. (CWUIC § 202.) Always check the current OSFM online map — designations change as CAL FIRE re-evaluates every 5 years.

### How to Verify Zone Status

1. **OSFM Wildfire Hazard Severity Zone Viewer:** [https://egis.fire.ca.gov/FHSZ](https://egis.fire.ca.gov/FHSZ) — enter parcel APN or address.
2. **County GIS / planning portal** — many counties (including San Diego County) layer FHSZ onto their public parcel search.
3. **City fire department or building department confirmation** — written confirmation in the permit file is the safest record.

### San Diego County — Key WUI Areas

Unincorporated SD County communities broadly in VHFHSZ/HFHSZ:
- **Lakeside, Ramona, Alpine, Julian, Borrego Springs, Fallbrook, Bonsall, Valley Center, Pine Valley, Jamul** — nearly all of the wildland interface communities in unincorporated SD are designated.
- City jurisdictions with significant FHSZ overlays: **Poway** (eastern), **Escondido** (eastern hillsides), **Santee** (eastern areas), **City of San Diego** (Mission Trails, Scripps Ranch, Rancho Peñasquitos, Black Mountain Ranch, Tierrasanta, Carmel Valley hillsides).

### Scope of Work — What Must Comply

#### New Construction (all types)
All buildings and structures newly constructed in a WUI area must fully comply with CWUIC Chapter 5. This includes:
- New SFR, duplex, townhouse
- New ADU (attached or detached) — treated as a new building; all Ch. 5 requirements apply to the ADU
- New commercial or mixed-use buildings in WUI

#### Additions
The added portion must comply with Chapter 5 requirements for the added/altered scope. The code requires compliance for buildings "hereafter constructed, modified, or relocated." A substantial addition that expands the building envelope triggers full compliance for the addition.

#### Relocation
A building relocated into a WUI area must comply as if new construction.

#### Repairs and Replacement
- **Reroof:** If 50% or more of the roof covering is replaced within any 12-month period, the entire replaced area must be a Class A assembly. All portions applied during any alteration must be at least Class A. (CWUIC § 507.1.)
- **Partial repair of other components:** The code's modification trigger means that the repaired/replaced component should meet current standards when being replaced as part of a permitted project.

#### Accessory Structures — Applicability Thresholds

| Situation | Rule | Section |
|---|---|---|
| Group U, ≤120 sq ft, located **≥50 ft** from any applicable building | **Exempt** from Chapter 5 | § 501.1 Exception 1 |
| Group U agricultural building, **≥50 ft** from applicable buildings | **Exempt** from Chapter 5 | § 501.1 Exception 2 |
| Accessory building **>120 sq ft**, located **3 ft or more but <50 ft** from applicable building | Must use noncombustible or ignition-resistant building materials (§ 503.2.4) | § 504.11.3 |
| Accessory building **≤120 sq ft**, located **3 ft or more but <50 ft** | At code official discretion | § 504.11.4 |
| Any miscellaneous structure (trellis, patio cover, arbor, gazebo) located **<3 ft** from applicable building | Noncombustible or ignition-resistant materials required | § 504.11.2 |
| Miscellaneous structure **3 ft or more but <50 ft** | At code official discretion | § 504.11.5 |
| Roofs of required accessory structures | Class A roof assembly required in FHSZ | § 504.11.6 |

> **"Applicable building"** means a building with residential, commercial, educational, institutional, or similar occupancy type. (CWUIC § 202.)

#### ADU-Specific Note
ADUs in FHSZ/WUI areas are treated as new buildings. All CWUIC Chapter 5 requirements apply to the ADU itself (roof, eaves, vents, siding, windows, doors, decks). The ADU's construction does not automatically require the existing primary dwelling to be upgraded — but additions that are physically attached may affect the shared components. Local jurisdictions (e.g., San Diego County) may require a fire protection plan.

### What These Rules Do NOT Require
- Automatic upgrade of the existing primary dwelling when an ADU is added (the ADU itself must comply; the existing dwelling is governed by the pre-existing approval unless "modified")
- Fire sprinklers (governed by CFC / CBC Chapter 9 and Gov. Code § 66314(d)(12) for ADUs — separate analysis)
- Defensible space clearance is separately required under PRC § 4291 and CWUIC Chapter 6 (ongoing maintenance obligation on the property owner, not a plan-check item per se — but fire protection plans may be required for new subdivisions)
