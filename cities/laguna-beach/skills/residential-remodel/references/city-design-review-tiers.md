# Laguna Beach — Current Design Review Routing (Ord. 1732)

**Law as of:** 2026-07-22

**Authority:** LBMC §§25.05.040 and 25.05.045 as repealed/replaced or added by Urgency Ordinance No. 1732

**Official signed ordinance:** https://ecode360.com/LA4953/laws/LF2759074.pdf

eCode360's integrated Title 25 still showed the April 28, 2026 edition when checked on 2026-08-15. The signed ordinance controls over the pre-ordinance eCode text and over the former Zoning Plan-Check project-tier table.

## The default inverted

Do **not** route a project to discretionary Design Review merely because it changes a building exterior. Ordinance 1732 replaced that former default with three closed lists:

1. §25.05.040(B)(1): 16 Design Review categories.
2. §25.05.040(B)(2): 28 Design Review exemptions that require Zone Clearance.
3. §25.05.040(B)(3): 17 Administrative Design Review categories.

First match the project to one of those lists. Then apply the environmentally sensitive area (ESA) override, the Director's referral authority, and any separate Coastal Development Permit requirement.

## Approval authorities

| Route | Approval authority | Procedure |
|---|---|---|
| Design Review, §25.05.040(B)(1) | Design Review Board or Planning Commission, as applicable | Noticed public hearing |
| Zone Clearance, §25.05.040(B)(2) and §25.05.045 | Community Development Department | Ministerial compliance check; no Design Review |
| Administrative Design Review, §25.05.040(B)(3) | Community Development Director or designee | Public hearing unless the enacted waiver procedure applies |

**Which body hears Design Review (§25.05.040(F)(1)).** "The design review board shall conduct a noticed public hearing on design review applications for all single-family or two-family developments, including associated public right-of-way improvements." The Planning Commission hears "all other design review applications, including but not limited to: residential development of three-families or more, all projects located in the downtown specific plan area, all non-residential development, public works projects, and projects for which the planning commission is the primary discretionary review authority, such as approval of a conditional use permit." A single-family or two-family project in the Downtown Specific Plan area, or one that also needs a CUP, fits both clauses: UNSETTLED, confirm with Planning at pre-application.

The Director may refer a Zone Clearance or Administrative Design Review project to the DRB or Planning Commission when the project may affect an unusually sensitive/highly visible area, may affect surrounding properties, or presents other circumstances warranting referral. All three routes may still require a CDP under Chapter 25.07.

## ESA override

The Zone Clearance and Administrative Design Review lists cannot be used for development in an ESA unless a licensed professional determines, subject to peer review, that the development does not impact the ESA. Otherwise route to Design Review under (B)(1)(e). The Ord. 1732 definition of ESA expressly includes slopes of 30% or greater.

## Administrative hearing waiver

Except for elevated decks/slabs/terraces/patios under (B)(3)(a)(iii) and minor historic-resource alterations under (B)(3)(a)(iv), the Director may waive an Administrative Design Review hearing only when no other entitlement is required and the complete notice/comment procedure in (B)(3)(b) is satisfied. The mailed and published notice precedes a 15-working-day public-comment period. A timely hearing request defeats the waiver.

Do not describe Administrative Design Review categorically as either "no hearing" or "a 14-day notice." The new rule is the conditional waiver above; if a hearing occurs, the separate public-notice provisions apply.

## Fire Safety Standards Review

Current §25.05.040(C)(4) requires Fire Department or Building Division review, as applicable, before deliberation on a Design Review application for **new construction and major remodels**, including proposed fuel-modification programs. Public-safety conditions from that review authority may not be modified without its concurrence.

Do not use the former trigger ("new construction and alterations involving 50% or more of an existing structure") or treat the Fire Department as the sole review authority.

## Staking, notice, and appeal timing

- DRB/Planning Commission staking and surveyor certification: at least 21 calendar days before the first public hearing.
- Administrative Design Review staking, when the project must be staked: at least 14 calendar days before its public hearing.
- Mailed public-hearing notice under §25.05.065(D): at least 21 calendar days for Design Review and 14 calendar days for Administrative Design Review.
- Public-hearing posting under §25.05.065(E): three public places, including the project site, at least 10 calendar days before the hearing.
- Appeal filing and Design Review effective date: 14 calendar days.
- Design Review approval ordinarily lapses after two years unless timely exercised; extensions follow §25.05.040(K).

For these two tracks, the 21- and 14-day values apply independently to both staking (when required) and mailed public-hearing notice.

## Criteria and findings

The current §25.05.040(H) criteria are 11 closed headings: Access; Design Articulation; Environmental Context; General Plan Compliance; Historic Preservation; Landscaping; Lighting and Glare; Neighborhood Compatibility; Pedestrian Orientation; Privacy; and View Equity.

Approval requires both findings in §25.05.040(I): compliance with applicable LBMC provisions (including an applicable specific plan), and design/site-development consistency with the 11 criteria.
