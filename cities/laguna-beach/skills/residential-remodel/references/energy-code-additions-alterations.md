# Title 24 Part 6 §150.2 — Energy Code for Additions and Alterations to Existing SFRs

> **Source:** 2025 California Energy Code §§ 110.0–110.9, 150.0, and 150.2 (Title 24 Part 6, effective January 1, 2026).
> **Authority:** 2025 California Energy Code, Title 24 Part 6
> **Effective:** January 1, 2026

⚠️ **Load-bearing point:** for SFR additions and alterations, the energy code is **component-based**, not percentage-based. There is **no single % threshold** that flips an alteration into "full new-construction Title 24 compliance." The performance path is always available as an alternative.

The skill must apply the component triggers below — never invent a global %-of-floor-area threshold.

---

## §150.2(a) — Additions

**Verbatim opening of §150.2(a):** *"Additions to existing single-family residential buildings shall meet the requirements of Sections 110.0 through 110.9; Sections 150.0(a) through (n), (p) and (q); and either Section 150.2(a)1 or 2."*

**Two compliance paths:**
- **§150.2(a)1 — Prescriptive approach.** Component-by-component requirements vary by addition size band.
- **§150.2(a)2 — Performance approach.** Long-term system cost (LSC) energy budget; can be (a) addition alone, (b) existing+alteration+addition, or (c) whole building.

**Key prescriptive thresholds for additions (§150.2(a)1):**

| Addition size | Key requirements |
|---------------|------------------|
| ≤300 sf | Exempt from cool-roof requirements of §150.1(c)11 (Exception 1 to §150.2(a)). |
| ≤500 sf (water heater) | An electric water heater with point-of-use distribution per RA4.4.5 is permitted (§150.2(a)1Diii). |
| ≤700 sf | Reduced prescriptive package: roof/ceiling insulation, radiant barrier, wall cavity insulation, west-facing fenestration limit, QII waiver, etc. (§150.2(a)1B). |
| >700 sf | Must meet §150.1(c) with modifications: extension of existing wood-framed walls may retain dimensions with R-15/R-21 cavity insulation; max fenestration 175 sf or 20% of addition floor area; max west-facing 70 sf or §150.1(c) requirement. (§150.2(a)1A) |
| ≤1,000 sf addition | Whole-dwelling mechanical ventilation per §150.0(o)1C/E/F **not** required (existing dwelling exempt). (§150.2(a)1Ci) |
| >1,000 sf addition | Whole-dwelling mechanical ventilation airflow required, sized to entire dwelling unit (existing + addition). |
| ≤1,000 sf addition | Ventilation cooling requirements of §150.1(c)12 do not apply (Exception 5 to §150.2(a)). |
| Photovoltaic systems | NOT required for additions (Exception 6 to §150.2(a)). |

**Note:** none of these is a "if addition is ≥X% of building area then full Title 24 applies" trigger. The thresholds are absolute square footage bands tied to component requirements.

## §150.2(b) — Alterations

**Verbatim opening of §150.2(b):** *"Alterations to existing single-family residential buildings or alterations in conjunction with a change in building occupancy to a single-family residential occupancy shall meet either Item 1 or 2 below."*

**Two compliance paths:**
- **§150.2(b)1 — Prescriptive approach.** Component-by-component triggers (the long list below).
- **§150.2(b)2 — Performance approach.** LSC energy budget; standard design table per altered component.

### §150.2(b)1 component triggers (the operative list)

For SFR alterations, **Title 24 compliance fires component-by-component** based on what is being altered. Each subsection below is an independent trigger.

| Subsection | Triggered by | Requirement (verbatim summary; full text in source) |
|------------|--------------|------------------------------------------------------|
| §150.2(b)1A | Adding fenestration | Vertical + skylight area meets §150.1(c)3A and Table 150.1-A. Exception 2: ≤16 sf added → exempt from total-area requirements. |
| §150.2(b)1B | Replacing existing fenestration | Replacement glazing meets U-factor and SHGC of §150.1(c)3A and §150.1(c)4. Exception 1: ≤75 sf replacement, U ≤0.40 (CZ 1–16) and SHGC ≤0.35 (CZ 2, 4, 6–15). |
| §150.2(b)1C | Entirely new or complete replacement space-conditioning system | New system meets §150.0(h), (i), (j)1, (j)2, (m)1–10, (m)12, (m)13, §150.1(c)7, §150.2(b)1Fii, (b)1G, and Table 150.2-A. |
| §150.2(b)1D | Altered duct systems (>25 ft new/replacement) | Comply with §150.0(m)1–10 and Table 150.2-D insulation; field-verified duct sealing per RA3.1, leakage criteria specified. |
| §150.2(b)1E | Altered space-conditioning system equipment | Connected duct system must be sealed and field-verified. Three exceptions (previously sealed, <40 ft, asbestos). |
| §150.2(b)1F | Altered air conditioner / heat pump (refrigerant components, compressor, coil, refrigerant lines) | Setback thermostat per §110.2(c); minimum airflow per RA3.3; refrigerant charge verified per RA3.2. |
| §150.2(b)1G | Altered or replacement space-heating system | Shall **not use electric resistance as the primary heat source**, with three exceptions (existing electric resistance, etc.). |
| §150.2(b)1H | Altered or replacement service water-heating system | (i) Pipe insulation per §150.0(j)1 for new + accessible existing piping; (ii) demand-recirc only for individual dwelling units; (iii) water heater type limited to: gas/propane, heat pump w/ R-10 + comm interface, NEEA Tier 3+, or consumer electric (only if existing was electric resistance). |
| §150.2(b)1I | Reroof — replacing >50% of roof exterior surface | Roof complies with §110.8 + cool-roof aged solar reflectance/thermal emittance per Climate Zone (Tables 150.2-E, F). Multiple exceptions for ceiling U-factor, radiant barrier, attic ducts, weight, integrated PV. |
| §150.2(b)1J | Vented attic alteration (CZ 1–4, 6, 8–16) | Insulation U-factor ≤0.020 or R-49+; air-seal accessible ceiling plane; cover recessed downlights with insulation. Multiple exceptions for existing R-19 / R-38 insulation. |
| §150.2(b)1K | Altered lighting | Altered lighting and luminaires meet luminaire efficacy of §150.0(k). JA8-compliant trim kits or lamps for existing screw-base recessed sockets allowed. |
| §150.2(b)1L | Entirely new or replacement ventilation system | All applicable §150.0(o) requirements; new fan + new duct system (≥75% new). |
| §150.2(b)1M | Altered ventilation system components | Whole-dwelling fan, replacement fans, air filter rules per Subsections 1/2; local exhaust per §150.0(o)1G. |
| §150.2(b)1N | Adding exterior door area | U-factor per §150.1(c)5. |

**Important: §150.2(b)1I (Reroof) — the ≥50% trigger inside the energy code**

Verbatim: *"Roofs. Replacements of the exterior surface of existing roofs, including adding a new surface layer on top of the existing exterior surface, shall meet the requirements of Section 110.8 and the applicable requirements of Subsections i and ii where more than 50 percent of the roof is being replaced."*

This is the **only ≥50% trigger** in §150.2(b) for SFRs, and it fires **only the cool-roof + roof-deck-insulation requirements** (Tables 150.2-E and 150.2-F). It does **not** flip the entire alteration to new-construction code. Document this carefully — it is easy to mis-cite as a global threshold.

## §150.2(b)2 — Performance approach

If prescriptive triggers are too restrictive (or compliance is impossible), the performance approach provides an LSC-based alternative. The standard design uses the higher efficiency of (a) existing conditions or (b) the requirements in Table 150.2-G (per altered component). Third-party verification of existing conditions is permitted to use existing efficiency levels as the standard.

**Source for table:** §150.2(b)2B + Table 150.2-G (verbatim list in source file).

**Operational note:** for any project where a single component trigger creates an unworkable cost, the performance path is the escape valve. Always advise applicants this option exists.

---

## §150.2(c) — Whole building option

**Verbatim:** *"Whole building. Any addition or alteration may comply with the requirements of Title 24, Part 6 by meeting the requirements for the entire building."*

The applicant may elect to bring the entire building to current Title 24 standards in lieu of the §150.2(a) or (b) component approach. Rare for typical alterations; sometimes used for whole-house remodels where the cumulative component triggers approach whole-building compliance anyway.

## How the parent skill applies §150.2

For each residential remodel, walk the §150.2(b)1 trigger list and check each subsection against scope:

| Scope element | §150.2 trigger fires |
|---------------|----------------------|
| New windows/skylights added | §150.2(b)1A |
| Existing windows/skylights replaced | §150.2(b)1B |
| Full HVAC system replacement | §150.2(b)1C |
| New or replacement ducting >25 ft | §150.2(b)1D |
| HVAC equipment swap (without full system replacement) | §150.2(b)1E + 1F |
| New or replacement furnace/heater | §150.2(b)1G (no electric resistance primary) |
| Water heater swap | §150.2(b)1H |
| Reroof (>50% of roof) | §150.2(b)1I |
| Vented-attic insulation alteration | §150.2(b)1J |
| New lighting / luminaire alteration | §150.2(b)1K |
| New whole-house ventilation system | §150.2(b)1L |
| Altered range hood / bath fan | §150.2(b)1M |
| New exterior doors added | §150.2(b)1N |

For each fired trigger, output the verbatim requirement and the applicable exceptions.

**Always note:** "Performance path (§150.2(b)2) and whole-building path (§150.2(c)) are available as alternatives if prescriptive compliance is infeasible."
