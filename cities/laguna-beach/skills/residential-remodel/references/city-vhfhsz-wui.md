# Laguna Beach — VHFHSZ + WUI (Ch. 14.70 + CFC §304/§324)

Laguna Beach is predominantly **Very High Fire Hazard Severity Zone (VHFHSZ)** per CAL FIRE maps; the citywide coverage figure is in `../../city-reference/SKILL.md`. Major remodels and new construction in the VHFHSZ — and especially within a city-designated Fuel Modification area — must comply with **both** Ch. 14.70 (the city's WUI Code amendments) **and** the city Fire Code Ch. 3 amendments at Title 15 §15.01.030 (defensible space + fuel modification stack). The state skill `../../wildfire-wui/SKILL.md` covers the underlying CRC Ch. 7A and CBC Ch. 7A frameworks; this file documents the city-specific amendments that apply on top.

---

## Ch. 14.70 — Wildland-Urban Interface Code (LBMC Title 14)

From the City source document (not included in this plugin). The chapter adopts the California Wildland-Urban Interface Code (Ord. 1725, 12/18/2025) with the following local amendments. All section references are to the city's amendments **of** the underlying CWUI sections.

### §14.70.015 (CWUI §101.2) — Scope (Includes Major Remodels)

Verbatim:

> *"**101.2 Scope.** This code applies to building materials, systems and/or assemblies used in the exterior design and construction of new buildings, additions, alterations, and repairs located within a wildland-urban interface (WUI) area within the City of Laguna Beach and contains minimum requirements to mitigate conditions that might cause a fire originating in a structure to ignite vegetation in the wildland-urban interface (WUI) area, and conversely, a wildfire burning in vegetative fuels to transmit fire to buildings and threaten to destroy life, overwhelm fire suppression capabilities or result in large property losses.*
> *Exception: With the approval of the building official minor repairs or replacement of exterior materials may be done with the same materials as exists on the building."*

So **alterations and repairs** (including major remodels) on any structure in the WUI area are within scope.

### §14.70.020 (CWUI §504.2) — Class A Roof Assembly

Verbatim:

> *"**504.2 Roof assembly.** Roofs and all reroofs shall have a roof assembly that complies with a Class A fire classification when tested in accordance with ASTM E108 or UL 790.*
> *Exception: With the approval of the building official small sections of a roof may be reroofed with the same material as exists on the roof."*

Applies to all roofs and all reroofs. Major-remodel scopes that include reroof must comply.

### §14.70.025 (CWUI §504.10.4) — Vent Size

Verbatim:

> *"**504.10.4 Vent size.** Each individual ventilation opening into enclosed attics, enclosed eave soffit spaces, enclosed rafter spaces formed where ceilings are applied directly to the underside of roof rafters, and underfloor spaces shall not exceed 144 square inches in area. Openings exceeding 144 square inches in area shall be protected by a minimum 1 3/8" thick solid core wooden door or equivalent."*

Hard 144 sq in cap on individual ventilation openings into attics/eaves/rafter spaces/underfloor. Larger openings need a 1-3/8" thick solid-core wooden door or equivalent.

### §14.70.030 (CWUI §504.5.2) — Exterior Wall Coverings

Verbatim:

> *"**504.5.2 Exterior wall coverings.** The exterior wall covering, or wall assembly, including insulation shall comply with one of the following requirements:*
> *1. Noncombustible material.*
> *2. Ignition-resistant building material labeled for exterior use.*
> *3. Fire-retardant-treated wood labeled for exterior use and complying with the requirements of Section 2303.2 of the California Building Code.*
> *4. Fire-retardant-treated wood shingles and shakes which have been qualified in accordance with Section 1505.6 of the California Building Code for use as 'Class B' roof covering shall be an acceptable alternative wall covering material where installed over solid sheathing."*

Major-remodel scopes that include exterior wall covering replacement must use one of these four categories. Routine combustible wood siding (untreated) is not permitted.

### §14.70.035 (CWUI §504.8.3) — Vinyl Windows

Verbatim:

> *"**504.8.3 Vinyl windows.** Glazing frames made of vinyl materials shall have welded corners and metal reinforcing in the interlock area."*

Two requirements for any vinyl-frame window: **welded corners** AND **metal reinforcing in the interlock area**.

### §14.70.040 (CWUI §504.8.4) — Skylights and Sloped Glazing

Verbatim:

> *"**504.8.4 Skylights and sloped glazing.** Skylights and sloped glazing must comply with Section 504.8 and must be of glass. Plastic skylights and plastic sloped glazing are not allowed."*

Glass only. Plastic skylights and plastic sloped glazing are categorically prohibited.

### §14.70.045 + §14.70.050 — Fire Protection Plan / Landscape Plan Contents

Verbatim §14.70.045:

> *"**602.2 Contents.** The Fire Protection Plan shall contain the requirements set forth in the 2019 City of Laguna Beach Fire Department Landscape/Fuel Modification Standards and Maintenance Program document as amended from time to time by City Council and pursuant to Resolutions 89.104, 1640, and 1641."*

Verbatim §14.70.050:

> *"**602.3.1 Contents.** The Landscape Plan shall contain the requirements set forth in the 2019 City of Laguna Beach Fire Department Landscape/Fuel Modification Standards and Maintenance Program document as amended from time to time by City Council and pursuant to Resolutions 89.104, 1640, and 1641."*

(City deletes the underlying CWUI §602.2 / §602.3 / §602.3.1 / §602.3.2 and substitutes the 2019 city Landscape/Fuel Modification Standards.)

### §14.70.060 (CWUI §604.1.1) — Defensible Space (Existing Structures)

Verbatim:

> *"**604.1.1 Defensible Space Guideline.** All existing habitable buildings and structures within the Very High Fire Hazard Severity Zone shall maintain defensible space in accordance with the requirements in the Defensible Space Guideline for Existing Structures in the Very High Fire Hazard Severity Zone, dated August 2021, as amended from time to time by Resolution of the City Council.*
> *Exceptions:*
> *1. New construction or major remodels, as defined by local ordinance, to be built or installed in areas containing City of Laguna Beach Fuel Modification designation, shall comply with Section 613.*
> *2. Existing structures built in compliance with the Fuel Modification Guidelines pursuant to Resolution 89.104 shall be maintained in accordance with the approved plan."*

So:
- Existing habitable buildings in VHFHSZ → August 2021 Defensible Space Guideline.
- New construction or major remodels in Fuel Modification designation → §613 fuel-mod plan stack instead.
- Pre-existing fuel-mod-compliant structures → maintained per approved plan.

### §14.70.065 — Documentation of Compliance

Verbatim §14.70.065 (CWUI §604.1.2):

> *"**604.1.2 Documentation of Compliance.** Property owners are required to comply with the Defensible Space Guideline for Existing Structures in the Very High Fire Hazard Severity Zone, or the Fuel Modification Guidelines pursuant to Resolution 89.104, shall obtain documentation of compliance from the fire code official upon inspection verification."*

### §14.70.070 (CWUI §613) — Fuel Modification for New Construction or Major Remodel

Verbatim:

> *"**613 Fuel modification requirements for new construction or major remodel.** All new buildings or major remodels, as defined by local ordinance, to be built or installed in areas containing City of Laguna Beach Fuel Modification designation shall comply with the following:*
> *5. Preliminary fuel modification plans shall be submitted to and approved by the fire code official concurrent with the submittal for approval of any tentative map.*
> *6. Final fuel modification plans shall be submitted to and approved by the fire code official prior to the issuance of a building permit.*
> *7. The fuel modification plans shall meet the criteria set forth in the 2019 City of Laguna Beach Fire Department Landscape/Fuel Modification Standards and Maintenance Program document as amended from time to time by City Council and pursuant to Resolutions 89.104, 1640, and 1641.*
> *8. The fuel modification plan may be altered if conditions change. Any alterations to the fuel modification areas shall have prior approval by the fire code official.*
> *9. All elements of the fuel modification plan shall be maintained in accordance with the approved plan and are subject to the enforcement process outlined in the Fire Code."*

For major remodels in Fuel Modification designation: preliminary plan with tentative-map submittal; final plan approved before building permit issuance.

### §14.70.075 (CWUI §614) — Explosives and Blasting

Verbatim:

> *"**614 Explosives and blasting.** Explosives shall not be possessed, kept, stored, sold, offered for sale, given away, used, discharged, transported or disposed of within wildland-urban interface areas, or hazardous fire areas."*

Categorical prohibition.

## CFC Defensible Space + Fuel Modification (Title 15 §15.01.030 — Fire Code Ch. 3 Amendments)

The city Fire Code Chapter 3 amendments mirror Ch. 14.70 with parallel rules at the CFC level. From the City source document (not included in this plugin) §15.01.030:

### CFC §304.1.3 — Vegetation

Verbatim:

> *"**304.1.3.1 Vegetation.** Weeds, grass, vines or other growth that is capable of being ignited and endangering property, shall be cut down and removed by the owner or occupant of the premises. Vegetation clearance requirements in urban-wildland interface areas and Very High Fire Hazard Severity Zones areas shall be in accordance with Chapter 49 and the Fuel Modification Guidelines pursuant to Resolution 89.104."*

### CFC §304.1.3.2 — Defensible Space Guideline

Verbatim:

> *"**304.1.3.2 Defensible space guideline.** All existing habitable buildings and structures within the Very High Fire Hazard Severity Zone shall maintain defensible space in accordance with the requirements in the Defensible Space Guideline for Existing Structures in the Very High Fire Hazard Severity Zone, dated August 2021.*
> *Exceptions:*
> *1. New construction or major remodels, as defined by local ordinance, to be built or installed in areas containing City of Laguna Beach Fuel Modification designation, shall comply with Section 324.*
> *2. Existing structures built in compliance with the Fuel Modification Guidelines pursuant to Resolution 89.104 shall be maintained in accordance with the approved plan."*

(Mirror of Ch. 14.70.060.)

### CFC §324 — Fuel Modification for New Construction or Major Remodel

Verbatim:

> *"**324 Fuel modification requirements for new construction or major remodel.** All new buildings or major remodels, as defined by local ordinance, to be built or installed in areas containing City of Laguna Beach Fuel Modification designation shall comply with the following:*
> *1. Preliminary fuel modification plans shall be submitted to and approved by the fire code official concurrent with the submittal for approval of any tentative map.*
> *2. Final fuel modification plans shall be submitted to and approved by the fire code official prior to the issuance of a building permit.*
> *3. The fuel modification plans shall meet the criteria set forth in the 2019 City of Laguna Beach Fire Department Landscape/Fuel Modification Standards and Maintenance Program document as amended from time to time by City Council and pursuant to Resolutions 89.104, 1640, and 1641.*
> *4. The fuel modification plan may be altered if conditions change. Any alterations to the fuel modification areas shall have prior approval by the fire code official.*
> *5. All elements of the fuel modification plan shall be maintained in accordance with the approved plan and are subject to the enforcement process outlined in the Fire Code."*

(Mirror of Ch. 14.70.070.)

## §25.05.040(C)(4) — Fire Safety Standards Review

Current law is in the City source document (not included in this plugin), not the pre-ordinance the City source document (not included in this plugin) scrape.

> Prior to deliberation and action on a design review application for **new construction and major remodels**, the Fire Department or Building Division of the Community Development Department, as applicable, shall serve as the review authority for purposes of reviewing and approving or conditionally approving applications, including proposed fuel modification programs.

Fuel-modification / AM&M requests, when required by that review authority, still apply to parcels with an "FM" Fuel Modification designation or VHFHSZ overlay. Public-safety conditions may not be modified by the Design Review authority without that review authority's concurrence.

The former 50%-alteration Fire-Department-only trigger is not current. A major remodel still trips this review because the enacted trigger is now "major remodels," not because a 50% alteration independently remains the statutory hook.
