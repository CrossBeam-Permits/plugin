# Laguna Beach — Nonconforming Structures (Ch. 25.56)

Chapter 25.56 of the LBMC governs Nonconforming Buildings, Lots, and Uses. The chapter does NOT have a single "blanket loss of nonconforming protection at 50%" rule that earlier Laguna research drafts implied. It has **three distinct rules**, each with different applicability and consequences.

A separate rule applies when a structure is a **major remodel**. Check it first (next section).

---

## Major Remodel — Nonconforming Status Ends (October 2026 Major Remodel Quick Guide)

The City's Major Remodel Interpretation Quick Guide, "Updated October 2026", states [source: https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/home/showpublisheddocument/23171/639264703759000000, as of 2026-10-09; the June 2026 edition printed as SB 9 application Exhibit F, https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/home/showpublisheddocument/26958/639232726993730000, has the same sentence]:

> *"Any legal, non-conforming structure shall be brought into compliance when the threshold for a major remodel is met. When a principal structure undergoes a major remodel, all associated accessory structures and improvements shall be subject to the current zoning regulations and all other applicable requirements, and shall lose their legal, nonconforming status."*

The handout's first paragraph states the same direction for the building itself: a major remodel "shall be classified as new development subject to current zoning regulations". LBMC §25.56.011 (Ord. 1732) classifies it as a new building subject to "the current development standards of the subject zone".

### Operative Rule

- Run the major-remodel test first (`city-major-remodel-methodology.md`). The test runs on each building or structure independently.
- A structure that meets the major-remodel threshold must be brought into compliance with current zoning. Its existing nonconformities do not carry forward. The §25.56.008(A) "expansion portion only" limit does not protect it.
- When the **principal structure** is a major remodel, every associated accessory structure and improvement loses its legal, nonconforming status. This applies even when the accessory structure has no work of its own.
- List each nonconforming accessory structure and improvement on the site (for example, a garage in a setback, an over-height wall or fence). Require the plans to show how each one will comply with current zoning.
- Below the major-remodel threshold, apply the three Ch. 25.56 rules below.

## §25.56.008(A) — Any Expansion Triggers Full Title 25 Conformance for the Expansion Portion (operative SFR rule)

From the City source document (not included in this plugin), verbatim:

> *"A legal nonconforming structure may be enlarged or expanded if:*
> *(A) The enlargement or expansion complies in every respect with all applicable provisions of this Title 25 Laguna Beach Municipal Code; and*
> *(B) When design review is required, the approval authority finds that the proposed enlargement or expansion and the project as a whole complies with the Design Review Ordinance Intent and Purpose Section 25.05.040(A) and Design Review Criteria as set forth in Section 25.05.040(H). (The existing nonconformities shall be identified in the public hearing notice.); and*
> *(C) The required number of parking spaces is provided per Chapter 25.52, Parking Requirements. However, existing single-family dwellings that have a nonconforming number of required parking spaces may be enlarged or expanded without complying with the required number of spaces, if the total gross floor area of the residential structure, including the proposed enlargement or addition, does not exceed fifteen hundred square feet and at least one parking space is provided on the property."*

(Ord. 1282 § 1, 1994; Ord. 1416 § 22, 2002; Ord. 1515 § 6, 2009)

### Operative Rule

- **No % threshold.** ANY expansion of a legal nonconforming SFR triggers (A) + (B) + (C).
- **(A) Full Title 25 conformance** — the **expansion portion** must comply in every respect with all applicable provisions of Title 25. Existing nonconformities on the unmodified portion are NOT blanket-extinguished, unless the structure is a major remodel (see the section above).
- **(B) Design Review** — when DR is required (e.g., addition >50%, major remodel, etc.), the approval authority must affirmatively find the enlargement/expansion AND the project as a whole comply with §25.05.040(A) and (H), AND existing nonconformities are identified in the public hearing notice.
- **(C) Parking** — required number of parking spaces per Ch. 25.52, with the **§25.56.008(C) carveout** for nonconforming SFR parking when total floor area (including expansion) ≤1,500 sf AND at least one parking space on site.

### The 1,500 sf Number — Parking Carveout, NOT Major-Remodel Exemption

> **Common misattribution to avoid:** earlier Laguna research drafts cited "1,500 sf SFR exception" as if it were a major-remodel exemption (i.e., SFRs ≤1,500 sf exempt from major-remodel calculation). That is wrong. The 1,500-sf number governs **parking conformance for nonconforming SFRs**, NOT the major-remodel methodology. Major-remodel methodology has only one carveout: ADUs (per `city-major-remodel-methodology.md`).

## §25.56.014 — 50% Damage Restoration: MULTI-FAMILY ONLY

Verbatim:

> *"Notwithstanding the extent of damage, any legal nonconforming building, structure or improvement which has been damaged by fire, flood, wind, earthquake or other disasters may be repaired, restored, replaced or reconstructed up to the original size, placement and density within five years of such damage or destruction, notwithstanding any other provision of this title; provided, however, that **no multiple-family dwelling which has been so damaged to the extent of more than fifty percent of the value of such building, structure or improvement immediately prior to such calamity shall be repaired, restored, replaced or reconstructed unless the provisions of Chapter 25.52 are complied with in full**; and provided further, however, that no shore protective device shall be repaired, restored, replaced or reconstructed unless it is consistent with prevailing zoning regulations and general plan policy."*

(Ord. 1282 § 1, 1994; Ord. 1530 § 1, 2010)

### Operative Rule

- **Default rule for ALL legal nonconforming structures:** repair / restore / replace / reconstruct up to original size, placement, density within 5 years of damage. No % threshold.
- **Multi-family carveout:** if a multi-family dwelling is damaged >50% of its value immediately prior to the calamity, restoration/reconstruction requires **full Ch. 25.52 (parking)** compliance. The 50% damage threshold ONLY applies to multi-family.
- **SFR carveout:** SFRs may be reconstructed up to original size/placement/density within 5 yrs **regardless of damage extent** (still subject to (A) + (B) + (C) of §25.56.008 if expansion is proposed beyond original).
- **Shore protective devices:** separate rule — no repair/restoration unless consistent with prevailing zoning + general plan policy. (This intersects with the bluff overlay in `city-bluff-overlay.md`.)

### Common Misattribution

The "50% damage = blanket loss of nonconforming protection" framing in earlier drafts was an over-generalization. The 50% damage rule is:
- ONLY for multi-family
- ONLY triggers Ch. 25.52 parking compliance, NOT blanket loss of nonconforming protection
- Has a 5-year reconstruction window (matters for force-majeure timing)

## §25.56.020(C) — 50% Density/Intensity Triggers Ch. 25.53 Access Standards

Verbatim:

> *"(C) If additions or alterations exceed fifty percent of the existing population density or intensity of use, the provisions of Chapter 25.53 (Access and Improvement Requirements) shall apply; provided, however, that any improvement requirements that may be imposed may be codified to preexisting, established and paved street or driveway gradients and are limited to those that would normally be required attendant to the application in order to meet the minimum access standards for the subject site, and any improvements based upon the number of building sites served shall be limited to the number of lots or building sites that are the subject of the application."*

(Ord. 1282 § 1, 1994)

### Operative Rule

- **50% trigger** is keyed to "existing population density or intensity of use," NOT square footage or demolition.
- Triggers **Ch. 25.53** (Access and Improvement Requirements) — i.e., access standards (driveway widths, minimum-access-road geometry, etc.) must be brought up to current Title 25.
- **Limited scope** — improvement requirements are limited to (a) what would normally be required for the application to meet minimum access standards for the subject site, and (b) requirements based on number of building sites served are limited to the lots that are the subject of the application.

### Practical Application

- For SFR remodels, "population density or intensity of use" rarely changes — adding bedrooms typically does not cross the 50% intensity threshold unless it's effectively a duplex conversion.
- For multi-family remodels that increase unit count or unit size by >50%, §25.56.020(C) fires.
- For ADU additions (which add a unit), §25.56.020(C) is a flag — if the underlying lot has access nonconformities (substandard driveway width, gradient, etc.), the ADU addition may trigger access conformance.

## §25.56.009 — Modification of Nonconforming Portion (Structural-Integrity Trigger)

Verbatim:

> *"If any part of a nonconforming portion of the structure is substantially removed or modified in such a way that it compromises the structural integrity of the building, that portion must be rebuilt in conformance with zoning regulations."*

(Ord. 1282 § 1, 1994)

### Operative Rule

- If a nonconforming wall/portion is removed or modified during construction such that its structural integrity is compromised, the rebuilt portion must conform to current zoning.
- Mid-construction trigger — applies when the demolition exceeds what was permitted, OR when the original "existing to remain" portion is found to be unsalvageable.
- Interacts with the deterministic phantom rule (`city-deterministic-phantom-rule.md`): a wall whose foundation/lateral system is being upgraded is, by city definition, a new wall — and §25.56.009 then says the rebuilt wall must conform.

## §25.56.002 — Nonconforming Defined; §25.56.004 — Nonconforming Use Defined; §25.56.006 — Change in Building Use; §25.56.010 — Adding to Nonconforming Use; §25.56.012 — New Construction Where Nonconforming Use Exists; §25.56.016 — Future Reclassification; §25.56.018 — Public Utility; §25.56.022 — Nonconforming Lots

Brief notes (full verbatim text in the City source document (not included in this plugin)):

| Section | Rule |
|---------|------|
| §25.56.002 | A nonconforming building is one that lawfully existed at the time the first zoning regulation became effective with which it did not conform. May be continued and maintained, except as otherwise provided. May not be **moved** unless every portion is made to conform. |
| §25.56.004 | "Nonconforming use" = a use of building/land that was carried on the effective date of the codified ordinance and that does not conform to the uses permitted in the zone. |
| §25.56.006 | If a nonconforming use is **abandoned or ceases for ≥12 consecutive months**, OR is changed to a conforming use, it shall not thereafter be reestablished. |
| §25.56.010 | A nonconforming **use** (as opposed to nonconforming structure) shall not be enlarged or expanded. (Distinct from §25.56.008(A) which permits expansion of nonconforming structures with conditions.) |
| §25.56.012 | While a nonconforming use exists on a lot, no new building shall be erected even if it would otherwise conform. The lot becomes available for any conforming purpose only after the nonconforming use/building is entirely removed or made to comply. |
| §25.56.016 | The nonconforming-use rules also apply to uses/buildings rendered nonconforming by future district reclassification. |
| §25.56.018 | Public utility uses existing at time of ordinance adoption are deemed conforming. |
| §25.56.022 | Nonconforming lots that are legal building sites per §25.08.004 may be developed without nonconforming-lot variance, provided density + zoning regs are met. Subdivisions must comply with current lot area + dimension standards. |

## Common Misattributions to Avoid

| Misattribution | Correct Framing |
|----------------|-----------------|
| "Nonconforming protection lost at any 50% threshold in Ch. 25.56" | Ch. 25.56 has no such blanket rule. §25.56.008(A) requires full Title 25 conformance for the expansion portion only (no % threshold). §25.56.014 is multi-family-only. §25.56.020(C) triggers access standards. The loss of nonconforming status at a **major remodel** comes from the October 2026 Major Remodel Quick Guide, not from Ch. 25.56 (see the first section). |
| "1,500 sf SFR exception is a major-remodel carveout" | 1,500 sf is the §25.56.008(C) **parking** carveout for nonconforming SFRs. Not a major-remodel exemption. |
| "50% damage triggers loss of nonconforming protection" | §25.56.014 only triggers Ch. 25.52 **parking** compliance, and ONLY for multi-family. SFRs may reconstruct regardless of damage extent (within 5 yrs). |
