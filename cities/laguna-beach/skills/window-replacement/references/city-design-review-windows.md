# Laguna Beach — Current Window/Door Planning Route

**Law as of:** 2026-07-22

**Authority:** LBMC §25.05.040 as replaced by Urgency Ordinance No. 1732

## Current classification

| Scope | Route |
|---|---|
| In-kind window or exterior-door replacement | Zone Clearance, §25.05.040(B)(2)(c) |
| Other non-major alteration/addition no more than 15 ft | Ordinarily Zone Clearance, §25.05.040(B)(2)(a)-(b), unless another closed-list trigger applies |
| Skylight with operable automatic night shade | Zone Clearance, §25.05.040(B)(2)(s) |
| Skylight without operable automatic night shade | Administrative Design Review, §25.05.040(B)(3)(a)(xii) |
| Work constituting a major remodel | Design Review, §25.05.040(B)(1)(a) |
| Improvement over 15 ft | Design Review, §25.05.040(B)(1)(c) |
| Development in an ESA | Design Review, §25.05.040(B)(1)(e), absent the enacted peer-reviewed professional determination |

The prior window-specific `(B)(2)(k)` exemption, adjacent-neighbor consent exception, and no-consent ADR category were repealed. Do not require neighbor consent or cite those former provisions. If no current closed-list category can be matched, return `needs_source`.

OTC Building review is a separate intake path and does not eliminate Zone Clearance, WUI glazing rules, historic-resource review, or a CDP determination.

## Coastal screen

Like-for-like replacement may qualify for the Chapter 25.07 single-family-improvement or repair/maintenance exemptions. Location-based carveouts, prior CDP conditions, and larger combined project scope can defeat an exemption. Record the CDP determination separately from the §25.05.040 route.

## WUI screen

In VHFHSZ areas, apply the local vinyl-frame and glass-skylight amendments. Vinyl frames need welded corners and metal reinforcing in the interlock area; plastic skylights/sloped glazing are not allowed.
