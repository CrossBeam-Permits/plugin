# Laguna Beach WUI Glazing Amendments

> Source: Laguna Beach Municipal Code, Chapter 14.70 (Wildland-Urban Interface Code)
> Ordinance: Ord. 1725, 12/18/2025
> Effective: January 1, 2026

---

## Overview

Laguna Beach adopts the 2025 California Wildland-Urban Interface Code (Title 24, Part 7) with local amendments that impose additional requirements on glazing materials. These amendments apply in the VHFHSZ; the citywide VHFHSZ coverage figure is in `../../city-reference/SKILL.md`.

The state WUI Code Section 504.8 requires exterior windows to be multi-pane with at least one tempered pane (or equivalent). Laguna Beach adds requirements for vinyl window frames and bans plastic skylights.

---

## Section 504.8.3 -- Vinyl Windows (Ch. 14.70.035)

**Full text of amendment:**

> **504.8.3 Vinyl windows.** Glazing frames made of vinyl materials shall have welded corners and metal reinforcing in the interlock area.

### What This Means

1. **Welded corners** -- Vinyl window frames must have heat-welded (fusion-welded) corner joints, not mechanical fasteners (screws, brackets, or snap-fit corners). Heat welding melts the PVC at the joint, creating a continuous seal that resists flame penetration.

2. **Metal reinforcing in the interlock area** -- The interlock area is where the operable sash meets the frame on sliding windows (horizontal sliders) and hung windows (single-hung, double-hung). This area must contain metal reinforcement (typically steel or aluminum inserts within the vinyl profile) to maintain structural integrity when exposed to heat.

### Applicability

- Applies to ALL vinyl-framed windows and doors in the VHFHSZ
- Applies to new construction, additions, alterations, and replacements
- Does NOT apply to aluminum, fiberglass, wood, or wood-clad frames
- Fixed (non-operable) vinyl windows still need welded corners but the interlock reinforcing requirement is specific to operable units

### Plan Review Verification

When reviewing a window replacement permit specifying vinyl windows:

1. Check the manufacturer's product data sheet for "welded corners" or "fusion-welded"
2. Check for metal reinforcing in the interlock area -- look for terms like "metal-reinforced interlock," "steel reinforced," or "aluminum reinforced sash"
3. If specs do not confirm both features, FLAG and request product documentation
4. Standard builder-grade vinyl windows often have welded corners but may NOT have metal-reinforced interlocks -- this is the common failure point

### Common Compliant Products

Most major vinyl window manufacturers offer product lines with welded corners and metal reinforcing. Premium and WUI-rated lines typically include both features. Budget lines may lack metal interlock reinforcing.

## Section 504.8.4 -- Skylights and Sloped Glazing (Ch. 14.70.040)

**Full text of amendment:**

> **504.8.4 Skylights and sloped glazing.** Skylights and sloped glazing must comply with Section 504.8 and must be of glass. Plastic skylights and plastic sloped glazing are not allowed.

### What This Means

1. **Glass only** -- All skylights must use glass (tempered, laminated, or fire-rated glass per Section 504.8 options). Acrylic (plexiglass), polycarbonate, and other plastic glazing materials are prohibited.

2. **Must also comply with Section 504.8** -- The glass must be multi-pane with at least one tempered pane, or meet one of the other WUI glazing options (glass block, 20-min fire-rated, or SFM 12-7A-2).

3. **Sloped glazing included** -- This applies not just to skylights but to any glazing installed at a slope (e.g., greenhouse-style additions, conservatory roofs).

### Applicability

- Applies in the VHFHSZ
- Applies to replacement skylights -- an existing plastic skylight being replaced must be upgraded to glass
- CBC Section 1505.1 (as amended by Ch. 14.50.120) also exempts skylights from the Class A roof assembly requirement if they comply with CBC Chapter 24

### Plan Review Verification

1. When a skylight replacement is included in a window permit, verify the product is glass (not acrylic/polycarbonate)
2. Verify dual-pane with tempered (or other 504.8 option)
3. FAIL if the specification calls for a plastic/acrylic skylight in the VHFHSZ

## Section 101.2 Exception -- Minor Repair/Replacement (Ch. 14.70.015)

**Full text of exception:**

> **Exception:** With the approval of the building official minor repairs or replacement of exterior materials may be done with the same materials as exists on the building.

### What This Means

The building official has discretion to allow replacement of exterior materials (including windows) with the same materials that currently exist on the building, even if those materials do not comply with current WUI code.

### Application to Window Replacement

- If a homeowner wants to replace a single-pane window with a new single-pane window (non-compliant in VHFHSZ), the building official MAY approve it under this exception
- This is a **discretionary** exception -- the building official is not required to grant it
- "Minor repairs or replacement" suggests small-scale work, not whole-house window replacement
- This exception does NOT override the building permit requirement -- a permit is still needed

### Plan Review Guidance

- If an application appears to rely on this exception (e.g., single-pane replacement in VHFHSZ), FLAG for building official review
- Do not automatically FAIL -- note the exception and let the building official decide
- Document: "Applicant proposes [non-compliant material]. Building official discretion applies per Ch. 14.70.015 Exception."

## WUI Code Scope for Window Replacement (Ch. 14.70.015)

**Full text of scope amendment:**

> **101.2 Scope.** This code applies to building materials, systems and/or assemblies used in the exterior design and construction of new buildings, additions, alterations, and repairs located within a wildland-urban interface (WUI) area within the City of Laguna Beach [...]

Key terms: The WUI code applies to **alterations and repairs**, not just new construction. Window replacement is an alteration, so the WUI code applies. This means:

- Replacement windows must comply with Section 504.8 (multi-pane with tempered)
- Vinyl replacement windows must have welded corners and metal-reinforced interlocks (Section 504.8.3)
- Replacement skylights must be glass (Section 504.8.4)
- The minor repair exception (Section 101.2 Exception) may apply at building official's discretion
