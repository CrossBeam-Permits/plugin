# Laguna Beach — Fire hazard zone, fuel modification and wildfire building rules (resident guide)

**Law as of:** 2026-10-10
**Authority:** LBMC Chapter 14.70 (California Wildland-Urban Interface Code as amended, Ord. 1725,
2025-12-18);
LBMC Title 15 (Fire Code amendments, Ord. 1726, 2025-12-18);
LBMC 25.05.040(C)(4) (Ord. 1732); `../../city-reference/SKILL.md` "Very High Fire Hazard Severity Zone" and
"Sprinkler Retrofit Triggers".
**Use for:** "Am I in a fire zone?", "What does the fire zone mean for my remodel?", "Do I need a
fuel modification plan?", "What roof / siding / windows can I use?", "Do I need sprinklers?"

The Laguna Beach Fire Department (LBFD) is the City's own fire department, not OCFA. The state WUI
overlay skill loads automatically with Laguna permit types; this file covers only the Laguna
additions and the plain-language map.

## 1. Am I in the Very High Fire Hazard Severity Zone? Is the parcel "FM"?

Two separate parcel facts. Get both from the parcel lookup; never guess from a citywide share.

- **VHFHSZ.** The City Council adopted the 2025 Local Responsibility Area map on 2025-06-24. A
  CrossBeam audit found 7,393 of 11,302 addressed parcels in the Very High tier (`../../city-reference/SKILL.md`). The
  VHFHSZ is an overlay; it does not change the zoning (25.05.040(C)(4)).
- **"FM" Fuel Modification designation.** A City GIS parcel flag, separate from the state map.
- **When the two disagree** (for example the City parcel layer and the 2025 state map give different
  answers, or FM is yes while the fire zone is no): say which source says what, and that the owner
  should confirm both with Fire Prevention. Do not pick one silently. The 25.05.040(C)(4) trigger
  reads "on the City's Geographic Information Mapping system", so the City GIS controls that step.

## 2. The fire rule, stated once

| Step | Applies to | What it requires | Authority |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Fire Safety Standards Review** (Planning stage) | **Every** Design Review application for new construction or a major remodel, **in any zone**. Not tied to VHFHSZ or FM. | Fire Department or Building Division reviews and approves before the hearing. Its public-safety conditions cannot later be changed by the design review body without Fire or Building approval. | 25.05.040(C)(4) (Ord. 1732) |
| **Fuel modification program or AM&M request** (Planning stage) | Inside that review, on a parcel with an **FM designation or in the VHFHSZ** on the City GIS | Required "when determined necessary by the applicable review authority". So a new house in the VHFHSZ may need one even without an FM flag. | 25.05.040(C)(4) |
| **Final fuel modification plan** (Building stage) | New buildings and major remodels on land with the City **FM designation** | Preliminary plan with any tentative map; final plan approved by the fire code official **before the building permit issues**; meets the 2019 LBFD program; changes need Fire approval; maintained for good | 14.70.070 (WUI Code 613); Title 15 Fire Code 324 |
| **Fire plan review** (Building stage) | Per the Building matrix: new buildings, new detached ADUs, additions over 1,000 sq ft, major remodels or alterations over 50%, landscape or hardscape in the VHFHSZ, fire access site plans | Fire review of the building permit | Building Division review matrix (Aug. 2026) |
| **WUI materials** | New buildings, additions, alterations and repairs in a WUI area | Section 3 below | 14.70.015-.040 |

A Zone Clearance or ADR project never gets Fire Safety Standards Review, because (C)(4) attaches only
to a Design Review application. Building still applies the WUI rules and the fire matrix.

**A standalone Alternative Materials and Methods (AM&M) request is decided by the Fire Official.**
Lead with: "File the AM&M request with the Fire Department; the fire code official decides it." The
WUI Code puts the decision on the code official: "a request to use an alternative material, design
or method of construction shall be submitted in writing to the code official for approval", who
approves it on the equivalency findings or answers in writing with the reasons it was not approved
(CWUIC 104.2.2.1-104.2.2.2); in Laguna that is the fire code official of the Laguna Beach Fire
Department (`../../city-reference/SKILL.md`; the WUI chapter's fire code official, 14.70.070). When an AM&M rides inside a Design Review, 25.05.040(C)(4) still names the Fire
Department (or Building Division) as the review authority for it, and its conditions bind the design
review body. Planning does not decide an AM&M: it may log the request as a zoning plan check record,
which is a record of the filing, not a Planning approval tier. Do not route an AM&M to ADR or Design
Review.

**The 2019 LBFD Landscape/Fuel Modification Standards and Maintenance Program.** Fire protection
plans and landscape plans must contain what it requires, and new planting may not include its target
species (14.70.045, 14.70.050, 14.70.055). The zone widths (A to D) and plant lists are transcribed
in `../../single-family-residential/SKILL.md`, reference `fire-wui-local-amendments`; the source PDF
it names is not in our repository, so treat those numbers as a transcription and confirm the current
program with Fire Prevention. Do not tell a resident a new house is exempt from fuel modification
because the program text is missing; the requirement comes from the code.

## 2a. Existing homes — defensible space

Every existing habitable building in the VHFHSZ must keep defensible space under the City's
**Defensible Space Guideline for Existing Structures** (August 2021, as amended by Council
resolution). Homes built under an approved fuel modification plan maintain that plan instead.
Owners must get documentation of compliance from the fire code official after inspection (LBMC
14.70.060, 14.70.065; Title 15 Fire Code 304.1.3.2-304.1.3.3). Weeds and other ignitable growth must
be cut and removed (Title 15, Fire Code 304.1.3.1).

The 2021 Guideline is not in our sources. For clearance distances around an existing home, say
**UNSETTLED here — get the LBFD Guideline or call Fire Prevention.** For a new house or major remodel
the 2019 program governs (section 2).

## 3. Building materials (Laguna amendments to the WUI Code)

These apply to **new buildings, additions, alterations and repairs** in a WUI area. With the
Building Official's approval, minor repairs or replacement may use the same material already on the
building (LBMC 14.70.015, WUI Code 101.2 as amended).

| Item | Laguna rule | Authority |
|---|---|---|
| Roofs and **all reroofs** | **Class A** roof assembly (ASTM E108 / UL 790). Small sections may match existing with Building Official approval. | 14.70.020 (504.2) |
| Vents into attics, eaves, rafter spaces, underfloor | Each opening **144 sq in max**; larger openings need a 1-3/8 in solid-core wood door or equal | 14.70.025 (504.10.4) |
| Exterior wall covering | Noncombustible, ignition-resistant labeled for exterior use, fire-retardant-treated wood labeled for exterior use, or qualified FRT shingles over solid sheathing | 14.70.030 (504.5.2) |
| Vinyl windows | Welded corners and metal reinforcing in the interlock | 14.70.035 (504.8.3) |
| Skylights and sloped glazing | **Glass only.** No plastic. Must meet 504.8. | 14.70.040 (504.8.4) |
| Explosives and blasting | Not allowed in WUI or hazardous fire areas | 14.70.075 (614) |

For a specific permit, load it: `../../reroof/SKILL.md`, `../../window-replacement/SKILL.md`,
`../../siding-replacement/SKILL.md`, `../../deck-patio-repair/SKILL.md`.

### Accessory structures and detached ADUs (sauna, shed, studio, pergola, gazebo)

- **State rules apply.** The 2025 California WUI Code sets accessory-structure rules by size and
  distance from a habitable building (CWUIC 501.1 exceptions and 504.11): small Group U buildings
  far from the house are exempt; larger accessory buildings within 50 ft need noncombustible or
  ignition-resistant materials; trellises, arbors and patio covers within 3 ft of the house need
  them too. The state WUI overlay skill loads with Laguna permit types and holds the table; do not
  restate it. Ch. 14.70 adds no separate accessory-structure rule.
- **Laguna additions that reach them:** a Class A roof on every roof (14.70.020), glass-only
  skylights (14.70.040), the 144 sq in vent limit (14.70.025) and the siding rule (14.70.030) apply
  to "new buildings, additions, alterations and repairs" in a WUI area (14.70.015, WUI Code 101.2).
- **Fuel modification plan.** Section 613 applies to "all new buildings or major remodels, as
  defined by local ordinance" on FM-designated land (14.70.070). The Building Division's fire-review
  matrix lists new buildings and new detached ADUs (matrix above), so expect a detached ADU on an FM
  lot to need a fuel modification plan. Whether a small detached accessory building (a sauna or
  studio) counts as a "new building" for Section 613 is not stated. UNSETTLED; confirm with Fire
  Prevention.
- **Attached ADU on an FM lot.** An attached ADU is an addition, not a new building. Section 613
  reaches it only if the work is a major remodel (14.70.070, "as defined by local ordinance"; ADU
  area is excluded from the major remodel count per the October 2026 handout,
  `../../residential-remodel/references/city-major-remodel-methodology.md`). The Building matrix sends
  additions over 1,000 sq ft to fire review. Whether Fire still asks for a fuel modification plan
  for an attached ADU under that size is UNSETTLED; confirm with Fire Prevention.
- **Fire Safety Standards Review** at the Planning stage applies only to a Design Review application
  for new construction or a major remodel (25.05.040(C)(4)). A Zone Clearance accessory building does
  not get it, but Building still applies the WUI rules.
- **Placement.** Fuel modification zones can limit where an accessory structure goes: the
  transcribed 2019 program bars combustible construction (gazebos, trellises, shade covers) in its
  zones (`fire-wui-local-amendments`, unverified copy). Confirm with Fire Prevention.

## 4. Fire sprinklers

Laguna requires sprinklers in **all new one- and two-family homes and attached garages**, and has
**two separate retrofit triggers** for existing homes (a 3-year valuation test and a scope test).
Do not restate them here. Use `../../city-reference/SKILL.md` "Sprinkler Retrofit Triggers" and
`../../residential-remodel/references/city-sprinkler-retrofit.md`. Residential sprinkler plans are filed
as **Fire Residential Permit, EnerGov type 190** (`../../city-website/references/submit-fire-residential.md`).

## 5. Landscaping in the fire zone

- Ord. 1732 makes **fuel modification projects** and **annual weed abatement** Zone Clearance, not
  Design Review (LBMC 25.05.040(B)(2)(f), (g)). Clearing **native vegetation** on undeveloped land
  is Design Review, but City-required weed abatement and fuel modification are excluded
  (25.05.040(B)(1)(h)).
- Hardscape needed to meet fire prevention standards, as determined by the Fire Official, is Zone
  Clearance (25.05.040(B)(2)(k)).
- Landscape and hardscape in the VHFHSZ goes to Building fire review (matrix above).
- See `trees-views-hedges.md` for planting height routes.

## 6. Outdoor fires

Outdoor fires are banned on Red Flag days and under set wind and humidity conditions, or when the
fire code official finds burning hazardous. Sky lanterns are banned (Title 15, Fire Code 305.6 and
308.1.6.3).

## 7. Appeals

A Fire Chief decision may be appealed to the **City Council** by written notice within **30 days**
(Title 15, Fire Code 112.1 as amended). Building Official decisions go to the Planning Commission
sitting as the Board of Appeals (LBMC 14.50.030).

## What to tell the resident (short form)

1. Check the parcel (the parcel lookup): VHFHSZ and FM designation.
2. Small work: name the material rules that apply (Class A roof, glass skylights, vents, siding).
3. New home or major remodel with Design Review, any zone: Fire Safety Standards Review before the
   hearing (25.05.040(C)(4)). In the VHFHSZ or on FM land: a fuel modification program if Fire asks;
   on FM land, a final fuel modification plan before the building permit (14.70.070). Sprinklers.
   Fire rebuild after a wildfire: `disaster-rebuild`.
4. Existing home: defensible space under the 2021 Guideline, with LBFD inspection.
5. Clearance distances and plant lists: get the LBFD documents; confirm with Fire Prevention.
