# Laguna Beach — Building Division review times and "where is my review?" replies

**Practice as of:** 2026-11-17
**Use for:** how long a Building plan review takes, what the portal's due date means, what the
applicant can see while reviews are still open, and how the Building Division answers a status
email ("where is my review", "it's past the due date", "any update?").

**Authority.** A Building Division permit technician who answers these emails, describing the
Division's practice on 2026-11-17. This is City practice, not code: say "our target is", not "the
code requires". Chat attaches this note to a Building record it shows City staff.

## Review times

- **Target, not a deadline.** The target for a new submittal or a resubmittal is about 2 to 3
  weeks of review. The due date the portal shows is that target, not a promise: counter traffic,
  a wave of new plans or a reviewer's time off can push it out a little.
- **Then processing.** When every review is done, the permit technicians have 3 business days to
  process the round and send it back. A portal due date that has just passed often means the
  round is in those 3 days.

## What the applicant can see while reviews are open

- Departments finish at different times. Corrections a reviewer types into a PDF are posted to
  the record's attachments in the portal as soon as that review is done, and the applicant can
  start on them.
- Redlines on the plans are not visible until every review is done and the plans are released,
  so more comments can still come.

## How a status reply reads

- **Say it is in review.** When a round is in review, tell the applicant it is actively in review
  now, and which departments are done and which are still reviewing when the record shows them.
  Never word it as "the next step is plan review" for a set that has sat in review for weeks.
- **Give the time frame** above as a target, kindly, when the applicant is worried about the date.
- **Close with the next contact.** Say the City will send an update when the review is complete.
  The person replying is the Building Division, so do not send the applicant back to the counter
  or the reviewer for the same answer.
