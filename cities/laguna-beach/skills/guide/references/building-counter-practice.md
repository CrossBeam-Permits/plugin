# Laguna Beach — Building Division counter practice: applications, fees, resubmittals

**Practice as of:** 2026-11-15
**Use for:** how the Building Division wants a building filing set up and moved along on the
portal: which applications to file, what each permit description must say, the ADU address,
what goes in its own permit and plan set, the geotechnical peer review deposit, paying fees,
resubmitting after corrections, and picking up printed corrections. Answers an applicant's
"what goes on the application", "how do I pay", "I paid, now what" and "what do I resubmit"
questions, and a staffer's reply to one. How long a review takes and "where is my review" are in
`review-times`.

**Authority.** The Building Division's own completeness, invoice and resubmittal letters to
applicants (Senior Permit Technicians and the Online Permit Services Supervisor, Aug. 2025 to
Oct. 2026), which repeat each rule below in the same words across many records. This is City
practice, not code: say "the Building Division asks", not "the code requires". Fee amounts other
than the deposit and the card fee come from the fee estimate; the rows that decide a screen are in
a CrossBeam reference that is not included in this plugin.

## 1. One application per scope, each with its own description and valuation

- Every scope of work is its own permit application: the new house or the main-house work
  (with its site work), each ADU, demolition, grading over 20 cubic yards, and a pool or spa.
  A JADU may stay on the main-house application (main house + JADU + ADU is two applications). A scope written into another scope's application (a spa or a deck inside the ADU
  application, an ADU on a remodel application) is sent back.
- Each application carries a description and a valuation (labor plus materials) for that scope
  only. Shared or bundled valuations are sent back.
- Each scope with mechanical, electrical or plumbing work also gets its own MEP application. The
  building and MEP applications for a project can be uploaded together in the "Permit
  Application" file category.
- The scopes can be reviewed under one consolidated plan set, but each scope is issued as its own
  permit. The plans and the applications must agree.

## 2. What the permit description must say

The Building Division asks for these in the description on the application:

| Scope | The description gives |
|---|---|
| ADU or JADU | Its size (square feet), attached or detached, number of bedrooms (or studio) and bathrooms, type of kitchen (full kitchen or kitchenette), and grading in cubic yards when under 20 CY |
| New house or main-house work | Total square footage, square footage demolished or removed, deck area removed or resurfaced, and any landscape or hardscape work; remodel square footage that matches the cover sheet |
| Pool or spa | What is proposed, its square footage and grading when under 20 CY; engineered (structural) pool or spa plans are included |
| Windows | Whether all windows are replaced or, if only some, how many |
| Fences, gates and site walls | Lineal footage and maximum height of every proposed fence, gate and wall |

## 3. The ADU's address

Put the ADU's own address on the plan set and on the ADU application's address line. Planning
assigns it, typically a "B" address (Unit B).

## 4. Separate permits and separate plan sets

- **Solar (PV) and fire sprinklers on a house project** are reviewed under their own permits
  with their own plans. Take them off the house plans and submit them separately when ready; fire
  sprinklers are their own permit type reviewed by the Fire Department. A solar-only job on an
  existing roof is one application.
- **Grading over 20 cubic yards** and **demolition** are their own applications (section 1).

## 5. Soils report and the geotechnical peer review deposit

When a project needs a soils (geotechnical) report, the City sends it to a third-party peer
review. The applicant pays a $5,000 deposit **by check only, in person at City Hall** during
counter hours; it cannot be paid by card on the portal. Upload a recent soils report or an update
letter, unsecured (section 7).

## 6. Paying fees

- The Building Division invoices plan check and permit fees through the online portal.
- **Online:** credit card only, with a mandatory 2.6% convenience fee.
- **In person at the City Hall cashier:** cash or check with no convenience fee (a card in person
  also carries the 2.6% fee).
- **Email the Building Division as soon as the payment goes through**, even when paying in person.
  The system does not tell building staff when fees are paid, so plans wait until that email
  arrives. When an applicant writes "I paid", thank them; that email is the confirmation, and the
  Division routes the plans from it.
- Fees are paid before plans route to review; a code enforcement case does not change that.

## 7. Uploading and resubmitting

- **One consolidated plan set** with every discipline in it (structural sheets inside the set,
  not a separate file), matching the sheet index on the title sheet.
- **A resubmittal is the complete revised plan set** as one file, with the response letter and
  any revised calculations. Uploading only the changed sheets is sent back.
- **Resubmit into the original slot.** Use the Resubmit button on the existing document in the
  portal so the new file lands as a new version of that document. A new standalone upload leaves
  the portal counting the original item as outstanding, and it blocks the resubmittal.
- **A response sheet for each plans examiner (department) that had corrections**, not one letter
  for all of them, and a new one for each round.
- **Unsecured files only.** Locked or password-protected files cannot be reviewed or marked up;
  the portal marks them "Required for Resubmittal" and they must be uploaded again unsecured.
- **Wait when a letter says to wait.** When one department has returned corrections and another
  is still reviewing, the letter says not to resubmit until every review is back.

## 8. Printed corrections

When corrections are printed, they are in the outside black pickup cabinet just to the left of
City Hall's front entrance. The cabinet is locked from Friday 4:30 PM until Monday 7:30 AM.

## 9. Counter hours and phone

Read `contacts-hours` (City website pack) for the Building counter's hours and phone; quote them
from there.
