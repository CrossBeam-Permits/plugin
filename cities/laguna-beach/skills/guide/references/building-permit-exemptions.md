# Laguna Beach — Work that needs no building permit, and work that still needs Planning

**Law as of:** 2026-10-10
**Authority:** LBMC 14.50.022 (replaces CBC 105.2, Ord. 1725, 2025-12-18) and LBMC 14.52.014
(adds CRC R101.4) and
the City source document (not included in this plugin); LBMC 25.05.040(B) and 25.05.045 (Ord. 1732); City "Do You Need a
Permit?" page (as of 2026-08-09).
**Use for:** "Do I need a permit to ...?", "Is this too small to need a permit?", "I don't need a
building permit, so I don't need the City, right?"

Two separate questions. Answer both:

1. **Building permit** (Building Division, Title 14).
2. **Planning approval** (Planning Division, Title 25): Zone Clearance, Administrative Design
   Review, Design Review, Coastal Development Permit, or another permit. **No building permit does
   not mean no Planning approval.**

## 1. Building permit: Laguna's own exemption list

Laguna replaced the model exemption list with a shorter one. LBMC 14.50.022 says permits are
**not** required for:

**Building**
1. Walls and fences not over **72 in** high, **unless** within **3 ft** of a property line or the
   public right-of-way.
2. Retaining walls not over **48 in** from the bottom of the footing to the top of the wall,
   **unless** supporting a surcharge, holding flammable liquids, or within **3 ft** of a property
   line or the public right-of-way.
3. Painting, papering, tiling, carpeting, cabinets, counter tops and similar finish work.
4. Temporary motion picture, television and theater sets.
5. Window awnings on an exterior wall of a Group R-3 or U building that project no more than
   **30 in**, do not need more support, and do not project into a restricted fire setback.
6. Temporary on-grade removable storage structures for a one- or two-family home, and play sets
   or swing sets, if the ground area is **120 sq ft or less** and the structure is **under 12 ft**
   above grade plane.
7. Movable fixtures, cases, racks, counters and partitions not over **5 ft 9 in**.

**Electrical:** minor repairs such as replacing lamps or plugging approved portable equipment into
permanent receptacles; radio and TV transmitting equipment (but not power supply, towers or
antennas); temporary testing systems.

**Gas:** portable heating appliances; replacing a minor part that does not change the approval or
make the equipment unsafe.

**Mechanical:** portable heating, ventilation, cooling and evaporative units; steam or water piping
inside equipment; replacing a part without changing its approval; self-contained refrigeration
with 10 lb of refrigerant or less and motors of 1 hp or less.

**Plumbing:** stopping leaks (replacing defective concealed pipe is new work and needs a permit);
clearing stoppages and repairing leaks, and removing and resetting toilets, without replacing or
moving valves, pipes or fixtures.

The exemption never authorizes work that breaks the code or other City law (LBMC 14.50.022,
opening sentence).

**The City's own short list** ("Do You Need a Permit?" page): interior painting, wallpapering,
flooring, window coverings, and portable cord-and-plug appliances. The page says: "If your project
is not on this list, it probably requires a permit."

### Homes built under the Residential Code (one- and two-family dwellings)

- LBMC 14.52.014 adds CRC R101.4: CBC Sections 101 through 114 apply to Residential Code work.
  That brings Laguna's amended 105.2 list into home projects.
- LBMC 14.52.010 also says that where the CRC conflicts with other City code, the more restrictive
  rule governs.
- The state CRC R105.2 list is longer (the City source document (not included in this plugin)).
  It exempts items that are **not** on Laguna's list, for example: one-story detached accessory
  structures up to 120 sq ft generally, fences up to 7 ft, sidewalks and driveways, water tanks on
  grade, prefabricated pools under 24 in deep, awnings up to 54 in, small detached decks (200 sq ft
  or less, 30 in or less above grade), and some low-voltage and breaker-replacement electrical work.
- **Treat any item that is on the state list but not on Laguna's list as needing a permit until
  the Building Division says otherwise.** How the two lists combine has not been ruled on in our
  sources. **UNSETTLED — confirm with Building at (949) 497-0715.**

### Common resident examples

| Project | Building permit? |
|---|---|
| Interior paint, flooring, cabinets, counter tops | No (14.50.022 Building 3) |
| Wood fence 6 ft tall, 5 ft from the property line | No (14.50.022 Building 1). Planning still applies (section 2). |
| Any fence or wall within 3 ft of a property line or street | Yes. OTC item 10 needs a Survey Waiver form or a survey (the City checklists, lane building). `../../fence/SKILL.md` |
| Garden retaining wall 3 ft from footing to top, away from the property line, no surcharge | No (14.50.022 Building 2) |
| Storage shed or play set, 100 sq ft, 8 ft tall | No (14.50.022 Building 6). Planning: Zone Clearance if setbacks are met (section 2). |
| Small ground-level deck | **Not on Laguna's list.** Assume a permit; confirm with Building. `../../deck-patio-repair/SKILL.md` |
| Driveway or patio paving | **Not on Laguna's list.** Confirm with Building. Planning: hardscape rules (section 2). |
| Replace water heater, HVAC unit, reroof, windows, solar, EV charger | Yes. Load the permit type: `water-heater`/`hvac`/`reroof`/`window-replacement`/`solar`/`ev-charger`. |
| Replace a breaker or a light fixture | Lamp replacement is exempt. Other electrical work: confirm with Building. |

Even exempt work must follow City construction rules, including the weekday-only construction
hours (LBMC 7.25.080; see `noise-lighting-equipment.md`).

## 2. Planning approval with no building permit

Under Ord. 1732 the Planning route depends on the three closed lists, not on whether a building
permit is needed. Zone Clearance is the City's check that a use or structure meets zoning
(LBMC 25.05.045(A)). Items commonly done without a building permit that still have a Planning
route:

| Work | Planning route | Authority |
|---|---|---|
| Fences, walls and hedges that meet the height rules (4 ft front, 6 ft side and rear) | Zone Clearance | 25.05.040(B)(2)(l); 25.50.012 |
| **Chain link or woven metal fence, or vinyl that does not look natural** (any height) | **Design Review** | 25.05.040(B)(1)(m) |
| Decorative fence posts or columns up to 12 in over the front-yard limit | Administrative Design Review | 25.05.040(B)(3)(a)(vii) |
| New planting kept under 15 ft in the buildable area, or under 6 ft (side and rear) / 4 ft (front) in setbacks | Zone Clearance | 25.05.040(B)(2)(n), (o) |
| Planting taller than those heights | Administrative Design Review | 25.05.040(B)(3)(a)(i), (ii) |
| Clearing native vegetation on undeveloped land | Design Review | 25.05.040(B)(1)(h) |
| Tree removal | No building permit. A Tree Removal Permit is needed only for a heritage tree (City Council), a tree on a landscape plan approved in Design Review (DRB), or an owner-maintained tree in the public right-of-way (Director); other private trees are exempt (LBMC 12.06.030, 12.06.040, 12.06.080(f)). City-maintained trees: Ch. 12.04. Check native vegetation and Coastal Zone rules before cutting | `trees-views-hedges.md` section 1 |
| Hardscape that does not add impervious surface, or that the Fire Official requires | Zone Clearance | 25.05.040(B)(2)(k) |
| Hardscape that **adds** impervious surface | Not named on any list. **UNSETTLED — confirm with Planning.** | — |
| Grading of 20 cu yd or less outside the building footprint | Zone Clearance | 25.05.040(B)(2)(i) |

| Storage container or construction trailer on site for a project | Temporary Use Permit (Director in R-1, Planning Commission elsewhere); a container kept permanently is an accessory building (`temporary-uses.md`) | 25.05.035(B)(1), (F) |
| Water features | Zone Clearance | 25.05.040(B)(2)(u) |
| Trash storage screened in the side yard | Zone Clearance (front-yard setback: Administrative Design Review) | 25.05.040(B)(2)(q); (B)(3)(a)(xv) |

| Renting for 30 days or less | Administrative Use Permit or CUP, only in certain zones | `short-term-lodging.md` |
| Outdoor lights | No Planning permit named, but the Good Neighbor Outdoor Lighting rules apply | LBMC Ch. 7.70; `noise-lighting-equipment.md` |

**Always add these checks:**

- **Environmentally sensitive area** (including slopes of 30% or more): a Zone Clearance or
  Administrative Design Review item becomes **Design Review** unless a licensed professional finds,
  with peer review, no impact (25.05.040(B)(1)(e), (B)(2), (B)(3)(a)). Use the parcel lookup.
- **Coastal Zone:** "development" under the Coastal Act and LBMC Ch. 25.07 can include grading,
  removing major vegetation and a change in the intensity of use, even with no building permit.
  Zone Clearance and Design Review do not replace a CDP (25.05.040(A)(2)(e); 25.05.045(C)(4)).
  `../../coastal-development/SKILL.md`.
- **Historic resource:** minor work on a historic resource goes to Administrative Design Review
  with a Heritage Committee recommendation (25.05.040(B)(3)(a)(iv)). See `historic-properties.md`.
- **Fire zone:** landscape and hardscape in the Very High Fire Hazard Severity Zone may need Fire
  review. See `fire-hazard-and-wui.md`.
- **Director referral:** the Director may send a Zone Clearance or Administrative Design Review
  item to the DRB or Planning Commission (25.05.040(A)(2)(d)).

**Exterior paint color.** Repainting is not named on any of the three lists. In-kind repair and
maintenance is Zone Clearance (25.05.040(B)(2)(c)). Whether a color change needs any Planning
approval is **UNSETTLED — confirm with Planning.** Roof color has its own rule: a white or
light-colored or glare-producing reroof is Administrative Design Review (25.05.040(B)(3)(a)(ix)).

Ord. 1732 is local law not yet certified by the Coastal Commission; see `design-review-lists.md`.

## What to tell the resident (short form)

1. Say whether a building permit is needed, citing LBMC 14.50.022, and flag any item that is only
   on the state list as "confirm with Building."
2. Say separately which Planning route applies, with the 25.05.040 letter.
3. Add the ESA, Coastal Zone, historic and fire checks when the parcel lookup shows them.
