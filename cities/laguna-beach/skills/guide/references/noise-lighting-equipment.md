# Laguna Beach — Construction hours, equipment noise and outdoor lighting (resident guide)

**Law as of:** 2026-10-10
**Authority:** LBMC Chapter 7.25 Noise (scraped
2026-05-12); LBMC 7.25.130 as amended by the Efficiency Ordinance (adopted 2026-07-21, effective
2026-08-20; excerpt a CrossBeam reference that is not included in this plugin); LBMC Chapter
7.70 Good Neighbor Outdoor Lighting.
**Use for:** "What hours can my contractor work?", "Can they work Saturday?", "Do I need Design
Review for my AC unit / pool pump / generator?", "My neighbor's light shines into my bedroom",
"What outdoor lights are allowed?"

## 1. Construction hours (LBMC 7.25.080)

- **Weekdays:** noisy construction, remodeling, digging, grading or demolition only from
  **7:30 a.m. to 6:00 p.m.** (7.25.080(A)).
- **Weekends and federal holidays:** no loud construction noise at all (7.25.080(B)). There is
  **no Saturday window**.
- Owners, contractors and employers may not let their crews break these rules (7.25.080(C)).
- **Exceptions** (7.25.080(D)): emergency work authorized by the Community Development Director or
  Building Official; certain public works; construction that actually meets the 7.25.040 noise
  limits; and set extended hours for the Sawdust Festival, Art-A-Fair and Laguna Art Museum.
- **Grading** is limited to **8:00 a.m.-4:00 p.m.** and **hauling** to **9:00 a.m.-3:00 p.m.**,
  Monday-Friday, by the City's published construction rules (City building-permits page;
  Building Division construction staging handout). These narrower windows are not in 7.25.080
  itself. `../../city-reference/SKILL.md` "Construction Hours" is the citywide summary.

**Yard and maintenance work** (LBMC 7.25.070): loud maintenance only **8 a.m.-6 p.m.**. Chainsaws
and mulchers: weekdays 8 a.m.-6 p.m. (not federal holidays), Saturdays 10 a.m.-5 p.m., never on
Sundays. **Electric and gas leaf blowers are banned at all times** (7.25.070(D)).

Report violations to Code Enforcement (`../../city-website/references/code-enforcement.md`).

## 2. Noise limits that apply everywhere (LBMC 7.25.040)

Measured on the affected property, 15-minute equivalent level:

| Noise zone | 7 a.m.-10 p.m. | 10 p.m.-7 a.m. |
|---|---|---|
| I — single-, two- and multi-family residential | 60 dBA | 50 dBA |
| II — commercial | 65 dBA | 65 dBA |
| III — residential part of mixed use | 65 dBA | 55 dBA |
| IV — certain Downtown districts (CBD-1, CBD-2, CBD Visitor Commercial, CBD Central Bluffs, Civic Arts) | 70 dBA | 70 dBA |
| V — other uses | 70 dBA | 60 dBA |

A single peak may not exceed the standard plus 20 dBA. If ambient noise is louder, the ambient
level becomes the standard. The zone follows the actual legal use (7.25.030, 7.25.040).

## 3. AC units, heat pumps, pool and spa equipment

Mechanical equipment is routed by the six-part test in **LBMC 7.25.130(C)** (effective
2026-08-20). Equipment in or next to a residential area that meets **all six** is approved
**without Design Review** (Zone Clearance, LBMC 25.05.040(B)(2)(v)):

1. ground-mounted, or wall-mounted at ground level;
2. not in the required front or side yard setback;
3. at least **5 ft** from every property line;
4. manufacturer's spec sheet with the decibel rating; maximum **60 dB day / 50 dB night at the
   property line**; and **two** sound-attenuation methods (for example an insulated enclosure, a
   compressor blanket, a shock-absorbing base), kept up for the life of the equipment;
5. the City's sound-level form, computed with ARI Standard 275;
6. does not block a **3-ft path** around any structure.

Fail **any** part → **Administrative Design Review** (LBMC 7.25.130(D); 25.05.040(B)(3)(a)(v)).
The equipment items govern equipment on any part of a building: a unit on a roof, balcony or deck
fails part 1 and goes to ADR, not to Design Review under the 15-ft catch-all (specific item over the
general one, `planning-approvals.md` Step 4d rule 3; City practice routes units on decks and
balconies to ADR the same way). **In-kind replacement** of an existing roof-mounted unit (same
location, size and type) is (B)(2)(c) in-kind repair, cleared at the counter for houses and
businesses alike, not run through the test (`planning-approvals.md` Step 4d rule 2). A new rooftop
unit is not in kind: run the test, and confirm at pre-application.
Before the permit issues, the installer signs an acknowledgment that the unit will meet the
7.25.040 limits (7.25.130(A)). Commercial HVAC may need an acoustical study (7.25.130(B)).

Before 2026-08-20 an earlier five-part test applied. For plan-level detail, use
`../../hvac/SKILL.md` or `../../swimming-pool/SKILL.md`. The ESA override still applies: equipment
in an environmentally sensitive area goes to Design Review without a peer-reviewed no-impact
finding (25.05.040(B)(2)).

**Standby generators.** The 7.25.130 test names HVAC and pool/spa equipment. Ord. 1732 also lists
"mechanical equipment, including, but not limited to, air conditioners and pool/spa equipment"
(25.05.040(B)(2)(v), (B)(3)(a)(v)). Whether a permanent generator is routed by the same six-part
test is **UNSETTLED — confirm with Planning.** It must still meet the 7.25.040 limits.

## 4. Outdoor lighting (LBMC Chapter 7.70)

Applies to all outdoor lighting installed after the chapter took effect (Ord. 1552, 2011).

**General rules (7.70.050):**

- Light trespass that causes glare is prohibited.
- Outdoor lights must be **hooded, fully shielded and aimed downward**.
- No mercury vapor or low-pressure sodium for street or commercial lighting.

**Exempt (7.70.060), in short:**

- fixtures that are not fully shielded but are **under 3,000 Kelvin and/or 750 lumens** per fixture
  (the code says "and/or");
- **motion-sensor safety lights** up to 2,400 lumens that turn off within 5 minutes;
- **low-voltage landscape lights** up to 400 lumens per fixture or string;
- **residential holiday lights** from Nov. 15 to Jan. 15, off by midnight; at other times no more
  than 7 consecutive days, off by midnight;
- approved period fixtures on a historic register property, up to 750 lumens and 3,000 K, with
  Director approval;
- U.S. flag lighting, traffic signals, emergency and vehicle lights, City-approved event or
  night-construction lighting, and police-approved safety lighting.

**Existing nonconforming fixtures** must be fixed within 90 days after the owner gets written
notice. The owner may ask the Director for an administrative exemption or delay (7.70.040(a)-(b)).

**Complaints:** a neighbor affected by glare or trespass must first talk to the fixture owner.
Only directly affected adjacent owners may file a service request (7.70.040(c)).

**In Design Review:** major remodels, new buildings and improvements over 15 ft must include an
**exterior lighting plan** (LBMC 25.05.040(B)(1)(a)-(c)). The design criterion asks for adequate
safety lighting that does not significantly impact neighbors, and avoidance of glare from
reflective materials such as skylights, white rock roofs and reflective glass (25.05.040(H)(7)).
Skylights **with** operable automatic night shades are Zone Clearance; **without** them,
Administrative Design Review (25.05.040(B)(2)(s), (B)(3)(a)(xii)).

City handout: "Good Neighbor Lighting" (`../../city-website/references/forms-index.md`).

## 5. Other noise items residents ask about

- **Parties and amplified sound:** loud, disturbing noise is unlawful after a peace or code
  officer asks the person to stop (7.25.060). Short-term rentals have stricter outdoor sound hours,
  10 p.m.-10 a.m. (LBMC 25.23.040(D)); see `short-term-lodging.md`.
- **Barking dogs:** handled under LBMC Chapter 6.16, not the noise chapter (7.25.050(H)). Chapter
  6.16 is not in our sources.

## What to tell the resident (short form)

1. Construction: weekdays 7:30 a.m.-6 p.m. only; nothing on weekends or federal holidays (LBMC
   7.25.080). Grading 8-4, hauling 9-3.
2. Equipment: run the six-part 7.25.130(C) test. Pass = Zone Clearance; fail = Administrative
   Design Review.
3. Lighting: shielded and aimed down, or under the 7.70.060 exemption limits; talk to the neighbor
   before filing a complaint.
