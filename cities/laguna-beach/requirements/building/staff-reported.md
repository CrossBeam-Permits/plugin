## Due with the application

### A simple permit filed through the portal is not one the City issues only at the counter

- id: `laguna_staff_counter_permit_route`
- Scopes: Window or door replacement; Patio cover, deck or patio repair; Siding or exterior finish replacement; Minor mechanical, plumbing or temporary-power permit; Fence, block wall or retaining wall; Sign permit; Residential interior remodel (kitchen, bath, room alteration)
- What the City asks for: The disqualifiers are review by another City department, outside-agency approval, geotechnical report review, extensive structural design and modifications, a change of construction type, and a change to a more restrictive occupancy. The Planning sign-in at the counter is part of the counter visit (the same letter: "sign in to see the planning division first"), so a scope that needs only that sign-in is still a simple permit. A scope that needs a Planning hearing item (Design Review, a Coastal Development Permit) is not.

### The work does not belong under a permit already on file for the parcel

- id: `laguna_staff_work_under_existing_permit`

- What the City asks for: Also read this filing's application description, which often names the original permit. Decide whether this filing's work is part of the scope of a listed permit that is open or recently issued. A deferred submittal, a revision to approved plans, or a supplement that names that permit is part of it. A different project on the same parcel is not. Decide from the two descriptions; never from a permit prefix, a date, or a shared word. When the filing names another permit that the block does not list, or the block is absent, name the permit from the filing in the same question.
