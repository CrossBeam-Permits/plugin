### A simple permit filed through the portal is not one the City issues only at the counter

- id: `laguna_staff_counter_permit_route`
- Scopes: Window or door replacement; Deck, balcony or patio cover repair; Siding or exterior finish replacement; Minor mechanical, plumbing or temporary-power permit; Fence, gate or freestanding block wall; Sign permit; Residential interior remodel (kitchen, bath, room alteration); Multi-unit exterior repair (deck, balcony, window, door, siding or minor trade repair)
- What the City asks for: The disqualifiers are review by another City department, outside-agency approval, geotechnical report review, extensive structural design and modifications, a change of construction type, and a change to a more restrictive occupancy. The Planning sign-in at the counter is part of the counter visit (the same letter: "sign in to see the planning division first"), so a scope that needs only that sign-in is still a simple permit. A scope that needs a Planning hearing item (Design Review, a Coastal Development Permit) is not.

- What the City asks for: The City issues the permit to the owner of record in its GIS unless the applicant provides a recorded grant deed. This is a judgment about people and entities, never a string match. The same party: the same person with a different name order, middle initial, spelling or case; the person's own trust ("Smith Family Trust", "Jane Smith, Trustee", "Smith Jane Tr"); one of several co-owners or co-trustees named in GIS; a married couple where the application names one spouse and GIS names both. A different party: a different person, including a relative or a prior owner; a different company or LLC; a trust named for someone else; an LLC on one side and a person or trust on the other, unless the packet shows the same principal on both. Applicant action: The property owner on the application does not match the City's owner of record for this parcel. The permit is issued to the owner of record unless a recorded grant deed is provided; if ownership has changed, please upload a copy of the recorded grant deed.
