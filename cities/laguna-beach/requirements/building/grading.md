## Due with the application

### Grading summary table split outside and inside the building footprint

- id: `laguna_grading_summary_table_on_plans`
- Scopes: Grading permit over 20 cubic yards
- What the City asks for: Show a grading summary table on the plans with cut, fill, and net import or export separated into outside-building-footprint, inside-building-footprint, pool/spa, and total columns. The counter reads the permit quantity and hauling quantity from that table.
- Source: City of Laguna Beach Building Division staff guidance
- Note: Reported by City staff. It is not printed in a published City handout. Confirm with the Building Division.

### Separate grading plan submittal when outside-footprint grading exceeds 20 cubic yards

- id: `laguna_grading_separate_plan_submittal_over_20cy`
- Scopes: Grading permit over 20 cubic yards
- Applies when: Does the greater of cut or fill outside the building footprint exceed 20 cubic yards?
  - If yes: Submit the grading plans as a separate plan submittal.
- What the City asks for: When the greater of cut or fill outside the building footprint is over 20 cubic yards, the grading plans are a separate plan submittal with their own grading permit. At 20 cubic yards or less, grading is reviewed with the construction plans. Grading entirely inside the building footprint still receives grading review but does not count toward the outside-footprint quantity.
- Source: paired GRADING QUANTITY USED FOR PERMIT DETERMINATION page: "OVER 20 YARDS - SEPARATE GRADING PERMIT" and the greater-of-cut-or-fill outside-footprint counting rule
- Note: Reported by City staff. It is not printed in a published City handout. Confirm with the Building Division.
