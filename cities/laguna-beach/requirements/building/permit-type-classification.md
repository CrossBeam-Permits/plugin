# Laguna Beach Building: which permit type to file

_Generated by the plugin publisher from the maintained Laguna Beach requirements source. Do not edit by hand; changes are overwritten._

Source: City of Laguna Beach Building Division, Multifamily Permit Type Guidelines (April 2026), an internal staff guideline reported by City staff. It is not a published applicant handout; confirm with the Building Division.

## The classification

**Residential** — a single-family dwelling, with or without ADUs/JADUs, **or a duplex**.

**A duplex is never multifamily.** The City states this first and states it as a prohibition: *"Do not use the multifamily permit type for duplexes."*

**Multifamily** — **3 or more dwelling units.** Apartments, condominiums, townhomes, and any residential project with 3+ units. *"Use multifamily only for projects with 3 or more dwelling units."*

**Multifamily is no longer a commercial permit type.** The 2026-04-08 guideline changed this: *"Multifamily projects should no longer be processed under commercial permit types."* A 3+ unit residential building filed under a commercial permit type is the wrong permit type, even though it was right before this date.

**Mixed use** — one building containing **both residential and commercial space**. Per City building staff, staff use the EnerGov Mixed Use permit type.

**Commercial** — hotels, motels, and similar lodging uses *"should remain under commercial permit types."* Lodging is commercial no matter how residential the units look.

## A condominium filed as Residential

City Building staff directed on 2026-09-08 that a confirmed condominium filing be submitted as multi-family and that the current residential record would close.

Two units remain Residential. ADUs and JADUs do not count toward the 3-unit line (see below).

## ADUs and JADUs do not make a project multifamily

City permit staff raised the question on 2026-08-19: *"Waiting for confirmation from a plan checker to provide clarification on project classifications on threshold quantities of ADUs and JADUs that change a residential application from single-family to multifamily for our review process."* No written answer has come, but the City's own issuance record since the 2026-04-08 guideline answers it:

- **The type follows the primary dwellings.** One or two primary units is Residential, whatever the ADU/JADU count. Three or more primary units is Multifamily.
- An ADU added to a property that already has three units is filed on the Multifamily type, "Multifamily - ADU - Detached". The Multifamily type has its own ADU Attached and ADU Detached work classes.

**How to apply it.** Count the primary dwellings, not the ADUs and JADUs. The City can still confirm this in writing; a written answer that differs replaces this section.
