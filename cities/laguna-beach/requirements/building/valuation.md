# Laguna Beach Building: project valuation

_Generated by the plugin publisher from the maintained Laguna Beach requirements source. Do not edit by hand; changes are overwritten._

Source: City of Laguna Beach Building Division staff guidance (August 2026) and City review letters. Reported by City staff; confirm with the Building Division.

## The City's rule

> City valuation minimums will be calculated/verified based off of the Occupancy Group(s), construction type(s) and square footage(s) **for new square footage**. The valuation applied will either be the city minimum or the valuation provided by the applicant, **whichever of the two is higher.**

## What the counter checks

**The amount is not the test.** The City does not reject a packet for a number it thinks is too low. It computes its own minimum and applies whichever figure is higher. A wrong, low, or optimistic valuation is corrected inside the City's own process and costs the applicant nothing at intake.

That is the City doing arithmetic, not the applicant filing an incomplete packet.

**What kicks back is structure.** Three shapes fail:

1. **Bundled.** One valuation figure stands for more than one application — a project total copied onto each form, rather than a per-scope figure.
2. **Riding.** A scope shown on the plans is folded into a *different* scope's application, so that application's valuation covers work outside its own scope. The offending application is over-stated and the ridden scope has no valuation of its own.
3. **Absent.** An application carries no valuation at all. (The application form field is blank — not "the number seems wrong".)

## Minimums the counter computes

These are counter calculations, not new applicant completeness tests.

- **Non-habitable area converted to habitable space.** For work such as garage-to-ADU or attic-to-bedroom conversion, staff run the EnerGov Valuation Calculator using R-3, the construction type and remodeled square footage, then use 50 percent of the generated valuation as the conversion basis.
- **Site work has its own valuation.** If the submitted site-work figure is too low, the tech asks for total hardscape square footage, runs that area through the calculator under U occupancy, and uses the greater figure. The application still supplies the site-work valuation; the calculator is City process.
- **Credit demolished square footage.** When staff enter square footage for tax/sewer fee generation, they credit demolished area and enter the net new square footage. This is a fee-input convention, not permission to bundle scopes or valuations.
