- id: `laguna_solar_reset_components_still_available`
- Scopes: Residential solar
- Applies when: Is this a remove-and-reinstall of a previously approved solar system after a reroof?
  - If no: This plan-check determination does not apply to a new roof-mounted PV system.

## Due before the permit issues

### Utility approval for a panel upgrade or relocation before issuance

- id: `laguna_solar_utility_approval_panel_upgrade`
- Scopes: Residential solar
- Applies when: Does the PV scope include a main service panel upgrade or relocation?
  - If yes: Provide the utility approval before permit issuance; it is not due at submittal.
- What the City asks for: Utility approval for a main service panel upgrade or relocation is due before permit issuance, not at submittal. Its absence never makes the incoming solar packet incomplete.
- Source: City of Laguna Beach Building Division staff guidance
- Note: Reported by City staff. It is not printed in a published City handout. Confirm with the Building Division.
