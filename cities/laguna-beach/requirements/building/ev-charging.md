## Due with the application

### Completed Electrical Permit Application

- id: `laguna_evse_electrical_permit_application`
- Scopes: Residential EV charging station; Multi-family EV charging station; Non-residential EV charging station
- What the City asks for: An EV charging station is filed on the City's Electrical Permit Application, not the general Building Permit Application. Laguna publishes a combined Electrical/Mechanical/Plumbing permit application for this purpose.
- Source: Laguna Beach Code Compliance Checklist; Electric Vehicle (EV) Charging, "Permit Submittal Requirements": "Completed Electrical Permit Application" (document 5111; verified live 2026-08-20) (https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/government/departments/community-development/building-permits/applications-forms)

### Signed applicant certification on the City's checklist

- id: `laguna_evse_applicant_certification_signed`

### Charger and parked vehicle shown clear of the public right-of-way

- id: `laguna_evse_no_public_right_of_way_encroachment`
- Scopes: Residential EV charging station; Multi-family EV charging station; Non-residential EV charging station
- Applies when: Is any part of this EV charging installation, or the space where the charging vehicle parks, in an outdoor location?
  - If yes: Show that the equipment and the parked vehicle stay clear of the public right-of-way, or submit the Department of Public Works approval for the encroachment.
- What the City asks for: For an outdoor installation the packet demonstrates that neither the equipment nor the parked vehicle occupies the public right-of-way, including over the sidewalk. The burden is on the applicant to show it. Public Works may approve an encroachment, and that approval is the alternative — not an exemption the Building Division grants.
- Source: Laguna Beach Code Compliance Checklist; Electric Vehicle (EV) Charging: "Installations in outdoor locations must demonstrate that the parked vehicle and all components of the EV charging station will NOT be in the Public Right of Way (i.e. on or over the sidewalk) unless otherwise approved by the Department of Public Works" (document 5111; verified live 2026-08-20) (https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/government/departments/community-development/building-permits/applications-forms)

### Dimensioned schematic site plan

- id: `laguna_evse_schematic_site_plan`
- Scopes: Multi-family EV charging station; Non-residential EV charging station
- What the City asks for: The site plan is fully dimensioned and drawn to scale. It shows the building footprint with distances to the property lines, the parking areas, the location of the electrical service and subpanels, and the location of existing and proposed EV charging stations, together with the location, size and use of all structures, the panel-to-charger routing, and the type and mounting of the charging system. It also carries project information: the project address, the owner's information, and a scope-of-work statement. The City exempts single-family, duplex and ADU installations from this sheet entirely.
- Source: Laguna Beach Code Compliance Checklist; Electric Vehicle (EV) Charging, "Schematic Site Plan (Not required for single-family, duplex or ADU)"; Multi-Family and Non-Residential Eligibility Checklists, "Site Plan and Single Line Drawing" (documents 5111, 11685 and 11683; verified live 2026-08-20) (https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/government/departments/community-development/building-permits/applications-forms)

### Signed electrical plans with a single-line diagram

- id: `laguna_evse_signed_electrical_plans`
- Scopes: Multi-family EV charging station; Non-residential EV charging station
- Must be signed and stamped by a licensed professional
- What the City asks for: A separate electrical plan, stamped and signed by a California licensed electrical engineer or a C-10 electrical contractor. It carries the single-line diagram; the existing electrical service size and the number of meters; the size, type and insulation ratings — voltage and temperature — of all conductors and associated wiring components; and the type, size and material of the raceways. The existing panel schedule shows room for the additional breakers. Single-family, duplex and ADU installations are exempt from this sheet.
- Source: Laguna Beach Code Compliance Checklist; Electric Vehicle (EV) Charging, "Signed Electrical Plans (Not required for single-family, duplex or ADU)"; Multi-Family and Non-Residential Eligibility Checklists: "Electrical plans shall be completed, stamped and signed by a California Licensed Electrical Engineer or a C-10 electrical contractor" (documents 5111, 11685 and 11683; verified live 2026-08-20) (https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/government/departments/community-development/building-permits/applications-forms)

### Electrical load calculation worksheet

- id: `laguna_evse_load_calculation_worksheet`
- Scopes: Multi-family EV charging station; Non-residential EV charging station
- What the City asks for: A load calculation worksheet under CEC Article 220, sized for a continuous load at 125 percent. Where the worksheet shows the existing service cannot carry the charger, the same submittal includes the service panel upgrade — the City does not accept a worksheet that identifies an upgrade and plans that omit it. The voluntary homeowner load estimator the City publishes separately is not this worksheet.
- Source: Laguna Beach Multi-Family and Non-Residential Eligibility Checklists, "Electrical Load Calculation Worksheet": "Is an electrical load calculation worksheet included? (CEC 220)" and "Based on the load calculation worksheet, is a new electrical service panel upgrade required? If yes, do plans include the electrical service panel upgrade?" (documents 11685 and 11683; verified live 2026-08-20) (https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/government/departments/community-development/building-permits/applications-forms)

### Circuit card with electrical calculations for a 50-amp or larger station

- id: `laguna_evse_circuit_card_high_power`
- Scopes: Multi-family EV charging station; Non-residential EV charging station
- Applies when: Is any proposed charging station a Level 2 unit at 9.6 kW or above with a circuit rating of 50 amps or higher?
  - If yes: Include a completed circuit card with electrical calculations alongside the single-line diagram.
  - If no: No circuit card is submitted for stations below a 50-amp circuit rating.
- What the City asks for: At Level 2 — 9.6 kW and a circuit rating of 50 amps or higher, a completed circuit card with electrical calculations travels with the single-line diagram. The City's own power table runs Level 1 at 15 or 20 amps through Level 2 at 20, 30, 40, 50 and 100 amps, and the applicant checks the proposed rating on the checklist.
- Source: Laguna Beach Multi-Family and Non-Residential Eligibility Checklists, "Electrical Load Calculation Worksheet" item D: "If charging equipment proposed is a Level 2 – 9.6 kW station with a circuit rating of 50 Amps or higher, is a completed circuit card with electrical calculations included with the single line diagram?" (documents 11685 and 11683; verified live 2026-08-20) (https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/government/departments/community-development/building-permits/applications-forms)

### Trenching detail with minimum cover called out

- id: `laguna_evse_trenching_detail`
- Scopes: Multi-family EV charging station; Non-residential EV charging station
- Applies when: Does the installation trench any part of the circuit run between the panel and the charging equipment?
  - If yes: Call out the trenching detail on the plans with the minimum cover for the wiring method used.
  - If no: No trenching detail is submitted where the run is not trenched.

## Raised during plan check

### Separate branch circuit serving the charging outlet

- id: `laguna_evse_dedicated_branch_circuit`
- Scopes: Residential EV charging station; Multi-family EV charging station; Non-residential EV charging station
- What the City asks for: The charging outlet sits on its own branch circuit with no other outlets on it, under CEC § 210.17. This is a plan-check item the City lists among its code requirements, so an absent note is a correction rather than a counter rejection.
- Source: Laguna Beach Code Compliance Checklist; Electric Vehicle (EV) Charging, "Electric Vehicle Branch Circuit": "An outlet(s) installed for the purpose of charging electric vehicles shall be supplied by a separate branch circuit. This circuit shall have no other outlets. [CEC §210.17]" (document 5111; verified live 2026-08-20) (https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/government/departments/community-development/building-permits/applications-forms)

### Lockable disconnecting means for high-rated equipment

- id: `laguna_evse_disconnecting_means`
- Scopes: Residential EV charging station; Multi-family EV charging station; Non-residential EV charging station
- Applies when: Is the charging equipment rated more than 60 amperes, or more than 150 volts to ground?
  - If yes: The plans show a readily accessible, in-line-of-sight disconnecting means lockable in the open position.
  - If no: No separate disconnecting means is called for at or below those ratings.
- What the City asks for: For supply equipment rated over 60 amperes or over 150 volts to ground, a disconnecting means is installed in a readily accessible location, in line of sight, and lockable in the open position under CEC § 110.25.
- Source: Laguna Beach Code Compliance Checklist; Electric Vehicle (EV) Charging, "Disconnecting Means" citing CEC §§ 625.42 and 110.25; Multi-Family and Non-Residential Eligibility Checklists item C citing CEC § 625.43 (documents 5111, 11685 and 11683; verified live 2026-08-20) (https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/government/departments/community-development/building-permits/applications-forms)

### CALGreen EV-readiness measures for new multifamily parking

- id: `laguna_evse_calgreen_ev_readiness`
- Scopes: Multi-family EV charging station
- Applies when: Is this charging work part of a new multifamily building, so that the CALGreen EV-readiness installation requirements apply?
  - If yes: The plans demonstrate the 10 percent EV-space provision, locate the spaces including one in a common-use area, and satisfy one of the accessible-route options.
  - If no: The CALGreen new-construction EV-readiness measures are not applied to a charger retrofit at an existing building.
- What the City asks for: Where CALGreen EV-readiness applies, the plans show 10 percent of total parking spaces across all parking facility types as EV spaces capable of supporting future charging stations, with the required count rounded up to the nearest whole number. The construction documents locate the proposed EV spaces, at least one of them in a common-use area available to all residents. Where chargers are installed, each EV space required by § 4.106.4.2.2 item 3 satisfies at least one option: adjacent to an accessible parking space complying with CBC Chapter 11A so the charger can be used from it, on an accessible route as defined by CBC Chapter 2, or compliance with § 4.106.4.2.2 items 1, 2 and 3.
- Source: Laguna Beach Multi-Family Eligibility Checklist, "Compliance with California Green Building Standards Code", citing CALGreen §§ 4.106.4.2, 4.106.4.2.1 and 4.106.4.2.2 (document 11685; verified live 2026-08-20) (https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/government/departments/community-development/building-permits/applications-forms)
