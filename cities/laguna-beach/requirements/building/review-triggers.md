# Laguna Beach Building: outside reviews and their triggers

_Generated by the plugin publisher from the maintained Laguna Beach requirements source. Do not edit by hand; changes are overwritten._

Outside reviews the Building Division routes a project to. Each trigger is the City threshold. Decide each fact from the plans, application and site facts; an unknown fact stays unknown.

## Grading (Building / Grading)

Triggered when any of these is true:

- Project scope is new building
- Project scope is new detached adu
- Addition area (sq ft) is more than 1000
- Basement excavation
- Retaining wall with cut, back-cut or backfill
- Cut-and-fill operation
- Slope restoration, repair or modification
- Landslide repair or removal
- Tract grading
- Civil drawings in the plan set

Source: Matrix for Grading and Residential Fire Plan Review Determination, effective 08/13/2026

## Shoring permit (Building)

Triggered when any of these is true:

- Temporary or permanent shoring

## Residential Fire (Fire Department)

Triggered when any of these is true:

- Project scope is new building
- Project scope is new detached adu
- Creates a new dwelling unit
- Project scope is new attached adu
- Addition area (sq ft) is more than 1000
- Remodel, alteration or renovation (% of the building) is more than 50
- all of: Landscape or hardscape work; and Fire Hazard Severity Zone is one of very_high
- Fire Department site access plan required

Source: Matrix for Grading and Residential Fire Plan Review Determination, effective 08/13/2026

## Geotechnical peer review (Building / City geotechnical consultant)

Triggered when any of these is true:

- Shoring cut depth (ft) is more than 6
- Oceanfront site
- A bluff setback applies to the parcel is more than 0
- Site slope (%) is more than 50
- State liquefaction hazard zone
- Expansive soils
- Mapped major landslide area
- Soil or structure failure
- Non-conventional foundation
- Building Official requires it

Source: When Is a Geotechnical/Soils Report Required?, effective 6/17/2026

## Water Quality Management Plan (Public Works / Water Quality)

Triggered when any of these is true:

- Disturbed earth area (sq ft, including landscape and hardscape) is at least 500
- Impervious area (sq ft) is at least 10000
- all of: Impervious area (sq ft) is at least 5000; and Redevelopment project
- all of: Impervious area (sq ft) is at least 5000; and Site slope (%) is at least 25
- all of: Impervious area (sq ft) is at least 2500; and Water Quality Environmentally Sensitive Area

Source: Water Quality Requirements for Land Development, Section C

## Approvals the Building Division collects

- Planning Division Approval: Planning approval applies to the project
- Fire Department Approval
- Public Works Department Approval: Work, improvements, grading, drainage, or staging within Public Works jurisdiction (submit a separate set of plans)
- Water Quality Management Plan Approval (submit a separate set of plans)
- Orange County Environmental Health Approval: Project includes a food operation or another Environmental Health-regulated use
