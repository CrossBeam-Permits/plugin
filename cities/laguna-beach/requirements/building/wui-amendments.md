### Replacement glazing identified to the WUI glazing standard

- id: `laguna_wui_replacement_glazing`
- Scopes: Window or door replacement
- Site condition: The site is in a very high Fire Hazard Severity Zone.
- What the City asks for: The window schedule or the manufacturer's product specification identifies the replacement glazing against the CWUIC §504.8 standard cited above. Where the frames are vinyl, the same schedule or specification also shows the two things §504.8.3 as added by LBMC 14.70.035 requires: welded corners, and metal reinforcing in the interlock area. Aluminum, fiberglass, wood and wood-clad frames are outside §504.8.3; the §504.8 glazing standard still applies to them.
- Source: Laguna Beach Municipal Code §14.70.015 (Ord. 1725, 12/18/2025), amending California Wildland-Urban Interface Code §101.2 to reach "new buildings, additions, alterations, and repairs" in a WUI area, with the exception for building-official-approved minor repairs or same-material replacement; §14.70.035 (Ord. 1725), adding CWUIC §504.8.3 "Vinyl windows. Glazing frames made of vinyl materials shall have welded corners and metal reinforcing in the interlock area."; §14.70.010 (Ord. 1725), adopting the CWUIC for LRA parcels designated Very High Fire Hazard Severity Zone; underlying glazing standard at CWUIC §504.8, which is met by multi-pane glazing with at least one tempered pane, by glass block, by a fire-resistance-rated assembly of not less than 20 minutes, or by an assembly qualified under SFM Standard 12-7A-2 (https://ecode360.com/48450818)

### Replacement exterior wall covering identified to the WUI material standard

- id: `laguna_wui_replacement_exterior_covering`
- Scopes: Siding or exterior finish replacement
- Site condition: The site is in a very high Fire Hazard Severity Zone.
- What the City asks for: The elevations, the wall sections or the manufacturer's product specification identify the replacement exterior wall covering — or the wall assembly, including insulation — against CWUIC §504.5.2 as amended by LBMC 14.70.030. That section lists four ways to comply: noncombustible material; ignition-resistant building material labeled for exterior use; fire-retardant-treated wood labeled for exterior use and complying with California Building Code §2303.2; or fire-retardant-treated wood shingles and shakes qualified under California Building Code §1505.6 as a Class B roof covering, installed over solid sheathing. Name which one the product meets and carry the label or listing.
- Source: Laguna Beach Municipal Code §14.70.015 (Ord. 1725, 12/18/2025), amending California Wildland-Urban Interface Code §101.2 to reach "new buildings, additions, alterations, and repairs" in a WUI area, with the exception for building-official-approved minor repairs or same-material replacement; §14.70.030 (Ord. 1725), amending CWUIC §504.5.2 "Exterior wall coverings"; §14.70.010 (Ord. 1725), adopting the CWUIC for LRA parcels designated Very High Fire Hazard Severity Zone (https://ecode360.com/48450818)
