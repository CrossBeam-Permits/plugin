# Laguna Beach Building: Standalone demolition

_Generated by the plugin publisher from the maintained Laguna Beach requirements source. Do not edit by hand; changes are overwritten._

Applies to: Standalone demolition permit.

Each item names when it is due and what triggers it. A conditional item applies only when its question is answered yes from the project evidence. Decide that by reading the plans and application, never from a keyword.

## Due with the application

### Completed City demolition form ("demo form")

- id: `laguna_demo_city_demolition_form`
- Scopes: Standalone demolition permit
- What the City asks for: Submit the City's completed form by its counter name, the "demo form."
- Source: City of Laguna Beach Building Division staff guidance
- Note: Reported by City staff. It is not printed in a published City handout. Confirm with the Building Division.

### South Coast AQMD Rule 1403 demolition notification

- id: `laguna_demo_scaqmd_1403_demolition_notification`
- Scopes: Standalone demolition permit
- What the City asks for: Submit the Rule 1403 demolition notification. Laguna's counter does not wait ten days from its Origin Date, so a newly originated notification is not deficient merely because ten days have not elapsed.
- Source: City of Laguna Beach Building Division staff guidance
- Note: Reported by City staff. It is not printed in a published City handout. Confirm with the Building Division.

### Rule 1403 asbestos notification, or the under-100-square-foot not-applicable marking

- id: `laguna_demo_scaqmd_1403_asbestos_notification`
- Scopes: Standalone demolition permit
- Applies when: Is the asbestos-containing area at least 100 square feet?
  - If yes: Submit the Rule 1403 asbestos notification and, if the asbestos has already been abated, the abatement certificate.
- What the City asks for: For at least 100 square feet of asbestos-containing area, submit the Rule 1403 asbestos notification and, if abatement is already complete, the abatement certificate. Below 100 square feet, the applicant instead marks the first and last lines on the City's demo form to show that the asbestos notification is not applicable.
- Source: City of Laguna Beach Building Division staff guidance
- Note: Reported by City staff. It is not printed in a published City handout. Confirm with the Building Division.

### Gas and electric utility meter-removal notice

- id: `laguna_demo_utility_meter_removal_notice`
- Scopes: Standalone demolition permit
- What the City asks for: Accept written notice from the gas and electric utilities that meter removal is scheduled or complete. The page also expressly accepts evidence that the applicant notified both utilities; the notice does not have to be a utility-issued confirmation.
- Source: City of Laguna Beach Building Division staff guidance
- Note: Reported by City staff. It is not printed in a published City handout. Confirm with the Building Division.

### Replacement construction submittal or Building Official demo-only approval on file

- id: `laguna_demo_construction_submittal_on_file`
- Scopes: Standalone demolition permit
- Checked against: the City permit record, not the plan set
- What the City asks for: This is a City-record check, not a document request to the applicant. Staff confirm that the replacement project's formal construction submittal is already on file or that the Building Official approved proceeding with a demolition-only permit.
- Source: City of Laguna Beach Building Division staff guidance
- Note: Reported by City staff. It is not printed in a published City handout. Confirm with the Building Division.

## Due before the permit issues

### Contractor license acceptable for demolition permit issuance

- id: `laguna_demo_licensed_contractor_b`
- Scopes: Standalone demolition permit
- What the City asks for: The counter may issue a demolition permit to a contractor holding a California Class B general building contractor license. The page states what is acceptable, not an exclusive list; it does not exclude a licensed demolition contractor.
- Source: City of Laguna Beach Building Division staff guidance
- Note: Reported by City staff. It is not printed in a published City handout. Confirm with the Building Division.
