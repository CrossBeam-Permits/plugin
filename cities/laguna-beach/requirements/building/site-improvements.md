### Dimensioned site plan locating the work

- id: `laguna_full_review_site_plan`
- Scopes: New, extended or replaced deck, balcony or patio cover; Hardscape, flatwork, site stairs, or outdoor kitchen and site utilities; Engineered retaining wall; Detached accessory or storage structure, shed or container
- What the City asks for: The plan set includes a site plan drawn to scale that shows the property lines, the existing buildings, and where the new deck, cover, wall, hardscape, outdoor kitchen, utility run or accessory structure sits on the lot, with its distance to each property line and to the nearby structures. The cover or first sheet carries the project address. The City's handout says not every item applies to every scope; the site plan is the item every one of these exterior scopes needs, so it is asked without a condition.
- Source: Laguna Beach Building Plan Check Submittal Requirements for New Construction, Additions & Major Remodels (2025), SITE/PLOT PLAN: "Fully dimensioned site plan drawn to scale (1/8\" min). Show the location, size and use of all structures and property line walls on the plan. Identify property lines, setback requirements, lot dimensions, distances from the building(s) & all projecting elements to the property lines", and PLAN INFORMATION: "If the project includes exterior work, the plans shall delineate all projecting elements, and show distance(s) to the property line(s) or adjacent structures"; Building Plan Review Services (edition 06/08/2026), "Building Plan Review Submittal", which routes these project types to full plan review and points to that handout (https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/departments/community-development/building-division)

### Construction drawings of the structure or site work, signed by the preparer

- id: `laguna_full_review_construction_drawings`
- Scopes: New, extended or replaced deck, balcony or patio cover; Hardscape, flatwork, site stairs, or outdoor kitchen and site utilities; Engineered retaining wall; Detached accessory or storage structure, shed or container
- What the City asks for: Beyond the site plan, the set draws the work itself at a readable scale, complete enough to build from: for example the framing, footings, guards and attachment to the house for a deck or patio cover; the wall section from the bottom of the footing to the top, with its drainage, for a retaining wall; the extent, materials, steps, rails and finished grades for hardscape; the layout of a built-in barbecue, firepit or shower for an outdoor kitchen; and the floor plan, elevations, section and foundation for an accessory structure. Every sheet is signed by the person who prepared it, and stamped when a licensed professional prepared it. A product brochure, a contractor's proposal or a photo with notes is not a construction drawing. An 11-by-17 set is acceptable for a minor project when scale and text stay legible.
- Source: Laguna Beach Building Plan Check Submittal Requirements for New Construction, Additions & Major Remodels (2025), PLAN SETS: "Plans must follow standard drafting conventions and be complete. Typically, plan scale is 1/8\" for site plan & plot plan, 1/4\" scale for floor plan, elevations, sections, etc." and "Minor projects may be submitted on 11\" x 17\" minimum size provided scale of drawings and text is maintained"; PLAN INFORMATION: "They may be produced (drawn) by a licensed Architect or Engineer, the Property Owner/Builder, Design Consultant, licensed Contractor or other qualified person ... All plans shall be signed (stamped if applicable) by the person(s) preparing the plan"; INTRODUCTION: "Not all items may be required depending on the scope of work"; Building Plan Review Services (edition 06/08/2026), "Building Plan Review Submittal", which routes these project types to full plan review and points to that handout (https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/departments/community-development/building-division)

### Plan view of the gas, plumbing and electrical work

- id: `laguna_full_review_mep_plan`
- Scopes: New, extended or replaced deck, balcony or patio cover; Hardscape, flatwork, site stairs, or outdoor kitchen and site utilities; Engineered retaining wall; Detached accessory or storage structure, shed or container
- Applies when: Does the work add or extend any gas, water, drain or electrical line, fixture, appliance, outlet or light?
  - If yes: Show each line in plan view from its point of connection, with the fixtures, appliances, outlets and lights it serves; a gas line shows loads, pipe sizes and developed length.
  - If no: Nothing is submitted for a deck, cover, wall, hardscape or structure with no trade work.
- What the City asks for: When the work carries a trade — a gas line to a barbecue, firepit or heater; a water or drain line to an outdoor sink or shower; new outdoor receptacles, lights or a circuit to a deck, cover, shed or kitchen — the set shows it in plan view: where each line runs from its point of connection at the house or meter, and where each fixture, appliance, outlet or light sits. A gas line shows its loads, pipe sizes and developed length. A simple plan view on the site plan is usually enough; a separate utility sheet is not required.
- Source: Laguna Beach Building Plan Check Submittal Requirements for New Construction, Additions & Major Remodels (2025), MECHANICAL, ELECTRICAL, & PLUMBING PLANS (MEP): "Mechanical, Electrical, and Plumbing plans are required. Simple plan view will usually provide the information required. Complex designs may be required to be designed by a Registered Design Professional"; PLAN INFORMATION: "Provide a gas piping plans with all loads, pipe sizes, and developed lengths" and "Show the location and/or layout of all receptacles and outlets"; Building Plan Review Services (edition 06/08/2026), "Building Plan Review Submittal", which routes these project types to full plan review and points to that handout (https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/departments/community-development/building-division)

### Retaining wall height from the bottom of the footing, any surcharge, and distance to the property line and right-of-way

- id: `laguna_retaining_wall_permit_dimensions`
- Scopes: Engineered retaining wall
- What the City asks for: The drawings dimension the wall height measured from the bottom of the footing to the top of the wall, show any surcharge the wall supports (a slope, a driveway, a structure or a load above it), and dimension the wall's distance to each property line and to the public right-of-way. Those facts decide whether Laguna's code requires a permit at all: a retaining wall over 48 inches from the bottom of the footing, one that supports a surcharge, or one within three feet of a property line or the right-of-way needs one. Note the measuring point: the bottom of the footing, not the exposed face. A wall drawn without these dimensions cannot be screened.
- Source: LBMC 14.50.022, amending CBC 105.2, Building item 2: permits are not required for "Retaining walls not over 48 inches high measured from the bottom of the footing to the top of the wall, unless supporting a surcharge, impounding flammable liquids, or located within three feet of a property line or public right-of-way" (https://ecode360.com/42894497)
