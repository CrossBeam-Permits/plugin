# Laguna Beach Building: Multi-unit residential (3 or more units)

_Generated by the plugin publisher from the maintained Laguna Beach requirements source. Do not edit by hand; changes are overwritten._

Applies to: Multi-unit residential new construction; Multi-unit residential addition; Multi-unit residential structural alteration, major remodel, or deck, balcony, stair or guard replacement; Multi-unit residential interior remodel (unit kitchen, bath, layout, damage repair); Multi-unit exterior repair (deck, balcony, window, door, siding or minor trade repair).

The City has published no Building submittal checklist for multi-unit work. The items below are the City's portal and counter rules for multi-unit permits, plus suggestions the City has not confirmed. Interior work and exterior repairs on a multi-unit building also follow the over-the-counter list (`otc.md`), and `permit-type-classification.md` says when a project is multi-unit. Confirm the rest of the submittal with the Building Division.

Each item names when it is due and what triggers it. A conditional item applies only when its question is answered yes from the project evidence. Decide that by reading the plans and application, never from a keyword.

## Due with the application

### Signed Building Permit Application uploaded with the plans

- id: `laguna_multifamily_signed_application`
- Scopes: Multi-unit residential new construction; Multi-unit residential addition; Multi-unit residential structural alteration, major remodel, or deck, balcony, stair or guard replacement; Multi-unit residential interior remodel (unit kitchen, bath, layout, damage repair); Multi-unit exterior repair (deck, balcony, window, door, siding or minor trade repair)
- What the City asks for: The Multifamily application takes two attachments: the City's Building Permit Application, signed, and the plans. The Building Division returned a Multifamily filing whose application was not signed. The signature is on the application form itself; the portal's electronic signature at submit does not replace it.
- Source: Laguna Beach online permit portal, Multifamily Permit (type 322), Required attachments: "Permit Application: Building Application", the signed Building Permit Application PDF, and the instruction to sign applications before uploading (https://lagunabeachca-energovweb.tylerhost.net/apps/SelfService#/permit/apply/322/0/0)

### A separate application, with its own valuation, for each new unit or ADU, demolition, grading over 20 cubic yards and a pool

- id: `laguna_multifamily_separate_application_per_scope`
- Scopes: Multi-unit residential new construction; Multi-unit residential addition; Multi-unit residential structural alteration, major remodel, or deck, balcony, stair or guard replacement
- What the City asks for: When the work adds dwelling units or an ADU, or includes demolition of a structure, grading over 20 cubic yards, or a pool, each is filed as its own application with its own valuation. The Building Division returned new multifamily filings for both. A fire sprinkler or alarm system is a Fire Department submittal on its own application.
- Source: Laguna Beach Building Division completeness returns on multifamily filings: application and plans disagree, with one application per unit and ADU; separate application per scope with Planning first, the wrong permit type, structural calculations, a soils report and an old code cycle
- Note: Reported by City staff. It is not printed in a published City handout. Confirm with the Building Division.

### The exterior elevated element inspection report (SB 721 or SB 326) when the repair follows one

- id: `laguna_multifamily_balcony_inspection_report`
- Scopes: Multi-unit exterior repair (deck, balcony, window, door, siding or minor trade repair); Multi-unit residential structural alteration, major remodel, or deck, balcony, stair or guard replacement
- Applies when: Does this balcony, deck, stair or walkway repair follow an SB 721 or SB 326 inspection?
  - If yes: Include the inspection report and the inspector's repair recommendations.
- What the City asks for: A suggestion, not a City requirement: Laguna has not confirmed it. Balconies, decks, stairs and walkways more than six feet above grade on apartment and condominium buildings are inspected under SB 721 and SB 326. When the repair answers such an inspection, the inspector's report and repair recommendations tell plan check what was found and what must be fixed. Deck and balcony repairs are the largest group of multifamily exterior work in Laguna's record.
- Source: Health & Safety Code 17973 (SB 721, apartments) and Civil Code 5551 (SB 326, condominiums) require an inspector's report with repair recommendations, and non-emergency repairs to be permitted within 120 days of the report
- Note: A suggestion drawn from other cities' lists, the code or the City's own record. The City has not confirmed it. Confirm with the Building Division.

## Raised during plan check

### Detail of the fire- and sound-rated wall, floor or ceiling where work opens an assembly shared with another unit

- id: `laguna_multifamily_shared_assembly_detail`
- Scopes: Multi-unit residential interior remodel (unit kitchen, bath, layout, damage repair); Multi-unit residential structural alteration, major remodel, or deck, balcony, stair or guard replacement
- Applies when: Does the work open a wall, floor or ceiling shared with another unit?
  - If yes: Show the rated assembly and how it is restored.
- What the City asks for: A suggestion, not a City requirement: Laguna has not confirmed it. Walls and floor-ceilings between dwelling units are fire-rated separations (CBC 420) and sound-rated assemblies (CBC 1206). When the work opens one (a new recessed light, a relocated drain, a removed wall at the demising line), plan check needs the assembly detail or the building's original plans to confirm it is restored. A plan-check item: it never sends a filing back.
- Source: CBC 420.2 and 420.3 dwelling unit separations; CBC 1206 sound transmission
- Note: A suggestion drawn from other cities' lists, the code or the City's own record. The City has not confirmed it. Confirm with the Building Division.

## Due before the permit issues

### Asbestos survey, and the SCAQMD Rule 1403 notice where asbestos is disturbed, before work that opens walls, ceilings or floors

- id: `laguna_multifamily_asbestos_survey`
- Scopes: Multi-unit residential interior remodel (unit kitchen, bath, layout, damage repair); Multi-unit residential structural alteration, major remodel, or deck, balcony, stair or guard replacement; Multi-unit residential addition
- What the City asks for: A suggestion, not a City requirement: Laguna has not confirmed it. An apartment or condominium building never takes the single-residence exemption in SCAQMD Rule 1403, so a remodel, repair or interior demolition that disturbs walls, ceilings or floors needs an asbestos survey first, and a notice to the air district when asbestos is found. Two of Laguna's multifamily filings were asbestos removals. It is a before-issuance item: it never sends a filing back.
- Source: Cal. Health & Safety Code 19827.5 bars a demolition permit until the notification is shown; Huntington Beach asks for the survey, and Irvine and Laguna Niguel for the notification before demolition
- Note: A suggestion drawn from other cities' lists, the code or the City's own record. The City has not confirmed it. Confirm with the Building Division.

### Owner-builder may not pull a Multifamily permit, except a triplex owner living in one unit

- id: `laguna_multifamily_owner_builder_limit`
- Scopes: Multi-unit residential new construction; Multi-unit residential addition; Multi-unit residential structural alteration, major remodel, or deck, balcony, stair or guard replacement; Multi-unit residential interior remodel (unit kitchen, bath, layout, damage repair); Multi-unit exterior repair (deck, balcony, window, door, siding or minor trade repair)
- What the City asks for: A Multifamily permit issues to a licensed contractor, not an owner-builder. The one exception the City states: an owner-builder may pull the permit for a triplex when the owner lives in one of the units. When the application names an owner-builder on a building of four or more units, or on a triplex with no evidence the owner lives there, raise it to staff.
- Source: City of Laguna Beach Building Division staff guidance
- Note: Reported by City staff. It is not printed in a published City handout. Confirm with the Building Division.
