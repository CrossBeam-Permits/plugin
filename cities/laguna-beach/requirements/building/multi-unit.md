# Laguna Beach Building: Multi-unit residential (3 or more units)

_Generated by the plugin publisher from the maintained Laguna Beach requirements source. Do not edit by hand; changes are overwritten._

Applies to: Multi-unit residential new construction; Multi-unit residential addition; Multi-unit residential structural alteration or major remodel; Multi-unit residential interior remodel (unit kitchen, bath or layout); Multi-unit exterior repair (deck, balcony, window, door, siding or minor trade repair).

The City has published no Building submittal checklist for multi-unit new construction, additions or alterations. The item below is the one City rule specific to multi-unit permits; exterior repairs on a multi-unit building follow the over-the-counter list (`otc.md`), and `permit-type-classification.md` says when a project is multi-unit. Confirm the rest of the submittal with the Building Division.

Each item names when it is due and what triggers it. A conditional item applies only when its question is answered yes from the project evidence. Decide that by reading the plans and application, never from a keyword.

## Due before the permit issues

### Owner-builder may not pull a Multifamily permit, except a triplex owner living in one unit

- id: `laguna_multifamily_owner_builder_limit`
- Scopes: Multi-unit residential new construction; Multi-unit residential addition; Multi-unit residential structural alteration or major remodel; Multi-unit residential interior remodel (unit kitchen, bath or layout); Multi-unit exterior repair (deck, balcony, window, door, siding or minor trade repair)
- What the City asks for: A Multifamily permit issues to a licensed contractor, not an owner-builder. The one exception the City states: an owner-builder may pull the permit for a triplex when the owner lives in one of the units. When the application names an owner-builder on a building of four or more units, or on a triplex with no evidence the owner lives there, raise it to staff.
- Source: City of Laguna Beach Building Division staff guidance
- Note: Reported by City staff. It is not printed in a published City handout. Confirm with the Building Division.
