## Due with the application

### The deferred item was listed as a deferred submittal on the approved plan set

- id: `laguna_deferred_item_listed_on_approved_set`
- Scopes: Residential deferred submittal (roof trusses, elevator, fire sprinklers); Commercial or multi-unit deferred submittal (roof trusses, elevator, fire sprinklers)
- What the City asks for: A deferred submittal finishes a permit that already exists. The main permit's cover sheet must have listed the item as deferred, and the package must be approved and permitted before the framing inspection. A packet that includes the approved cover sheet showing the deferral is supporting evidence. Eligibility: the City does not approve life-safety items for deferral, except fire sprinklers and roof trusses. The City has accepted elevator calculations as a deferred item, and the existing residential deferral row names fire-sprinkler, roof-truss and elevator packages.
- Source: Laguna Beach Building Plan Check Submittal Requirements for New Construction, Additions & Major Remodels (2025), PLAN INFORMATION: "Deferred Submittal items shall be clearly noted on the cover sheet and will be required to be submitted, approved and permitted prior to the framing inspection. Life safety items (except fire sprinkler and roof trusses) are not approved for deferred submittal" (https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/departments/community-development/building-division)

### Plans and calculations for the deferred item

- id: `laguna_deferred_package_plans_calcs`
- Scopes: Residential deferred submittal (roof trusses, elevator, fire sprinklers); Commercial or multi-unit deferred submittal (roof trusses, elevator, fire sprinklers)
- What the City asks for: The package is the drawings and calculations for the deferred item itself: the truss layout and truss calculations, the elevator or its guiderail and support calculations, or the fire-sprinkler plans and hydraulic calculations. Structural drawings and calculations are stamped and signed by the registered Civil Engineer, Structural Engineer or Architect who prepared them. The package names the main permit it completes. A cover letter or a manufacturer's brochure alone is not the package.
- Source: Laguna Beach Building Plan Check Submittal Requirements for New Construction, Additions & Major Remodels (2025), "FIRE SPRINKLER PLANS (DEFERRED SUBMITTAL): Three copies (for commercial) and two copies (for residential) of Fire sprinkler plans and calculations" and "STRUCTURAL PLANS & CALCULATIONS: Two (2) sets of structural plans, details, and supporting calculations prepared, wet stamped and signed by a registered Civil Engineer, Structural Engineer or Architect"; Building Plan Review Services (edition 06/08/2026), residential full-review list naming "Elevator" and "Roof trusses (for deferred submittal)" (https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/departments/community-development/building-division)
