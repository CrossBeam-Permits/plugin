### Contractor CSLB record and City business license, from the City's own records

- id: `laguna_contractor_credentials_on_record`
- Scopes: Residential new dwelling; Residential addition; Accessory dwelling unit or JADU; Residential major remodel or structural alteration; Residential interior remodel (kitchen, bath, room alteration); Residential EV charging station; Multi-family EV charging station; Non-residential EV charging station; Standalone demolition permit; Pool or spa permit; Grading permit over 20 cubic yards; Residential solar; Window or door replacement; Siding or exterior finish replacement; Patio cover, deck or patio repair; Fence, block wall or retaining wall; Minor mechanical, plumbing or temporary-power permit; Multi-unit residential new construction or addition; Commercial new construction; Commercial addition; Commercial structural alteration; Commercial interior alteration; Commercial or restaurant tenant improvement; Commercial demolition; Sign permit
- Checked against: the City permit record, not the plan set
- Applies when: Is a licensed contractor pulling this permit, rather than the property owner as an Owner-Builder?
  - If yes: Staff read the City business-license register and the CSLB record for the contractor named on the application, and for any named subcontractors.
  - If no: There is no contractor license or City business license to look up on an Owner-Builder permit. The Owner-Builder rows govern on the scopes where the City publishes them.
- What the City asks for: The Building Permit Application names the contractor as shown on the CSLB license, with the license number and class, and the form's printed note requires a current City business license for that contractor AND for every subcontractor, under LBMC 5.08.200. A license PDF or a letterhead in the packet is supporting evidence and is never conclusive — it can be expired, superseded, or issued to a different entity than the one on the application. Outside the panel-upgrade, water-heater and reroof permits, no Laguna checklist asks the applicant to submit these documents, so a submittal that omits them is not incomplete.

### Water heater replacement within the in-kind e-permit scope

- id: `laguna_water_heater_scope_limit`
- Scopes: Residential interior water heater replacement e-permit
- Applies when: Is this a tank-type residential interior water heater being replaced in the same location with a unit of the same type, size and rating?
  - If yes: The work is inside the e-permit scope and the checklist above is the whole packet.
  - If no: The work is outside the e-permit scope. It is not issuable as an e-permit; it goes in as a standard permit application, and this express checklist does not describe what that submittal needs.

### Coastal Commission Appendix B filing in the Deferred Certification Zone (Planning lane pointer)

- id: `laguna_coastal_deferred_appendix_b`
- Scopes: Residential reroof; Commercial reroof
- Checked against: the City permit record, not the plan set
- Site condition: The site is in an area of deferred certification (the Coastal Commission issues the CDP).

## Due before the permit issues

### Current City of Laguna Beach business license

- id: `laguna_contractor_business_license`
- Scopes: Residential panel upgrade e-permit (to 400A / 2 meters); Residential interior water heater replacement e-permit; Residential reroof; Commercial reroof
- Applies when: Is a licensed contractor pulling this permit, rather than the property owner as an Owner-Builder?
  - If yes: The contractor's current City business license is on file or submitted with the application.
- What the City asks for: The contractor holds a current Laguna Beach business license, issued in the same name as the CSLB record. The Building Permit Application form states the rule for every building permit and extends it past the general contractor: "All contractors, including subcontractors, will need to obtain a city business license pursuant to section 5.08.200 of the Laguna Beach Municipal Code." It is due at ISSUANCE, not at submittal. An applicant who cannot produce one is sent to the Finance counter, which is where the license is verified. Its absence therefore never makes a submittal incomplete.
- Source: Laguna Beach Panel Upgrade E-Permit guide and Water Heater Replacement E-Permit guide, "Contractors to possess a current city business license"; Reroofing guide, "Contractors: Must have a valid City Business License matching CSLB records"; Building Permit Application form, contractor block ("CONTRACTOR (As Shown on License)… License #… Class") and printed note "City Business License required. All contractors, including subcontractors, will need to obtain a city business license pursuant to section 5.08.200 of the Laguna Beach Municipal Code" (https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/departments/community-development/building-division)

### Waste Management Plan and security deposit for tear-off reroofs

- id: `laguna_reroof_waste_management_plan`
- Scopes: Residential reroof; Commercial reroof
- Applies when: Does this reroof tear off the existing roofing, or is it an overlay over the existing material?
  - If yes: A tear-off requires the Waste Management Plan and the separate security deposit, by check or cash, under LBMC 7.19.
  - If no: An overlay with no tear-off is expressly exempt: no Waste Management Plan and no security deposit.
- What the City asks for: Two deliverables, not one: the Waste Management Plan, and a separate security deposit paid by check or cash. The City's Solid Waste Division administers the C&D requirements at (949) 464-6677 or Recycle@LagunaBeachCity.net; deposit amounts changed effective 2026-07-01, so read the current fee schedule rather than quoting a figure. Both are due at ISSUANCE, not at submittal. Their absence never makes a submittal incomplete.
- Source: Laguna Beach Reroofing guide, "Rules & Color Restrictions": "Submit a Waste Management Plan and separate security deposit (check or cash) for tear-off reroofs, per Municipal Code 7.19 (exempt if no tear-off)"; LBMC Ch. 7.19, Construction and Demolition Waste Disposal; § 7.19.050 submission of waste management plan, § 7.19.070 security deposit (https://ecode360.com/42886987)
