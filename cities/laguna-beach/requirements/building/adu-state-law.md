| Item | How it applies | Why |
|---|---|---|
| PV required for a new detached ADU | State law already sets this rule; it is not a Laguna addition. | The current California Energy Code already controls PV for newly constructed detached ADUs, with its own exceptions. Restating it as Laguna law would obscure those exceptions. |

| Fire and sound separation; rated eave and exterior wall | Applies as an objective building, health or fire-safety standard. | Objective building, health, and fire-safety standards remain applicable to ADUs; the city row points to the applicable CRC assembly tables rather than adding a zoning barrier. |
| Minimum 100 amp electrical service | Applies only when the design proposes a separate electrical service. | CEC section 230.79(C) governs a one-family dwelling service, but it does not itself require an ADU to install a separate service. Converted section 66323(a)(1) ADUs cannot be forced into a new or separate utility connection. Apply the rating only when the design actually proposes a separate service. |
| Sewer and water connections optional, notes required | Applies; it is more permissive than state law. | Optional connections are no more restrictive than the statewide converted-ADU protection and are more permissive for other ADUs. Plans must still identify the chosen connection approach. |
