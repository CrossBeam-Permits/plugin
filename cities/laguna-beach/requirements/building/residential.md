## Due with the application

### A separate Building Permit Application for each listed scope

- id: `laguna_separate_application_per_scope`
- Scopes: Residential new dwelling; Residential addition; Accessory dwelling unit or JADU; Residential major remodel or structural alteration; Residential solar; New, extended or replaced deck, balcony or patio cover; Hardscape, flatwork, site stairs, or outdoor kitchen and site utilities; Engineered retaining wall; Detached accessory or storage structure, shed or container
- What the City asks for: Laguna files one application per scope off one complete plan set, each with its own description and valuation. The baseline scopes are: new SFR/addition/remodel with site work and grading of 20 CY or less outside the building footprint; demolition; ADU/JADU; grading of more than 20 CY outside the building footprint; and pool/spa. Each ADU or JADU normally has its own permit, but a JADU may ride with the main house; main house + JADU + ADU is two applications. Demolishing a unit requires its own demolition permit; demo-only normally also needs a construction submittal on file or Building Official approval. GRADING TEST: count only grading OUTSIDE the building footprint, using the greater of cut or fill. More than 20 CY outside the footprint needs its own grading application, valuation, permit and plan submittal; 20 CY or less rides with construction. Grading inside the footprint never counts toward the 20 CY, though it still gets review and a fee. Apply the test in one of three cases. (1) The plans or application state the inside and outside quantities: apply the test to the outside quantity. Evidence of outside grading includes grading or civil sheets, a referenced civil grading plan that is not in the packet, site or retaining walls, a new driveway or pad cut, and a subterranean garage or basement whose excavation extends beyond the footprint. Decide which case applies by reading the plans; never from a keyword. Shoring may issue with grading, but the plan checker decides which permit carries it. A house with an ADU may share one plan check but issues as two permits, one per address. Application pages may be combined into one PDF. When the City record for the named permit is available, compare its description with this filing's scope, the way a Planning record is compared: a grading record opened for another permit's work does not cover this filing's grading.

### Water Efficient Landscape Ordinance (WELO) documentation

- id: `laguna_welo`
- Scopes: Residential new dwelling; Residential addition; Accessory dwelling unit or JADU; Residential major remodel or structural alteration
- Checked against: the City permit record, not the plan set
- Applies when: Did Planning call out WELO (prescriptive or performative) on the ZPC notes for THIS work? If the notes are silent or unclear, also say whether the cover sheet or landscape sheet states a landscape area that could meet a 23 CCR section 491(a) threshold — 500 sq. ft. or more of new landscape, or 2,500 sq. ft. or more of existing landscape area. Landscape area is irrigated planting, turf and water features only (section 490.2(a)(41)).
  - If yes: Staff look up the Planning record. Record the Planning call when it is clear (prescriptive or performative). Cover-sheet landscape area rides along as supporting evidence.
  - If no: Planning clearly did not call WELO for this work, and the packet does not state a landscape area that could meet a section 491(a) threshold. Omit. A rehabilitated landscape of 1,768.7 sq. ft. is below the 2,500 sq. ft. section 491(a)(2) threshold and is supporting context only.
- What the City asks for: The published thresholds remain the legal context and match current MWELO at 23 CCR section 491(a), effective 2025-01-02 (the same state text was numbered section 490.1 before that amendment; section 490.1 is now General Provisions): (a)(1) a NEW CONSTRUCTION project — a new building with a landscape area, or another new landscape project — with a landscape area of 500 sq. ft. or more requiring a building permit, plan check or design review; (a)(2) a REHABILITATED LANDSCAPE project with a modified landscape area of 2,500 sq. ft. or more. Section 490.2(a)(41) defines landscape area as the irrigated planting, turf and water-feature areas only; it excludes building and structure footprints, hardscape, and non-irrigated areas. Section 491(b) lets any project at or under 2,500 sq. ft. of landscape area choose the prescriptive option in section 492 rather than the performance path.
