## Building permit fee

The row base plus the per-unit amount for the valuation above the row floor. The per-$1,000 amount is prorated: a partial $1,000 is charged as a fraction. Half-cents round to the even cent.

Matches EnerGov: 28 of 31 FY26/27 Building Permit Fee ledgers to the cent. The City has not confirmed this formula in writing.

## Who collects each fee

The estimator keeps the Building Division subtotal apart from fees that another City division or another agency collects. Each line names who collects it. Do not add the two subtotals into one "permit fee".

The school developer fee is paid to Laguna Beach Unified School District, not to the City. The estimate shows the published maximum rate; the district sets the amount. For an ADU, evaluate each unit separately and apply only when that unit is over 750 sq ft; never add multiple ADU areas together. Do not apply to conversion of existing habitable space.

- Laguna Beach Unified School District: School developer fee
- State of California: State building standards fee
- Public Works / Water Quality: Sewer connection fee
- Planning: General Plan Maintenance
- City tax / Finance: Building Construction Tax — single-family dwelling; Building Construction Tax — ADU over 750 sq ft; Building Construction Tax — commercial/industrial
- Community Development: Library impact fee — new residential unit; Library impact fee — ADU proportional rule
- Building / Fire: Fire review of building plan/permit; Fire inspection of building plan/permit
- Cultural Arts / Planning: Art in Public Places in-lieu contribution (1.25%)
- Public Works: Construction & Demolition waste diversion administration
- OCPW / City Building: OCPW Moulton Parkway road fee — single-family; OCPW Moulton Parkway road fee — ADU proportional rule; OCPW Moulton Parkway road fee — multifamily; OCPW Moulton Parkway road fee — commercial; OCPW CARITS Zone 2A/2B road fee — single-family; OCPW CARITS Zone 2A/2B road fee — ADU proportional rule; OCPW CARITS Zone 2A/2B road fee — multifamily; OCPW CARITS Zone 2A/2B road fee — commercial; OCPW CARITS Zone 2C/2D road fee — single-family; OCPW CARITS Zone 2C/2D road fee — ADU proportional rule; OCPW CARITS Zone 2C/2D road fee — multifamily; OCPW CARITS Zone 2C/2D road fee — commercial

## Billed outside the permit

- Inspector-determined trash service fees: Determined during inspection, calculated and collected by Finance, and never added to the permit fee list. Billed by City Finance.
- Inspector-determined sewer service fees: Separate from the modeled sewer connection fee; determined during inspection, calculated and collected by Finance, and never added to the permit fee list. Billed by City Finance.
