`fee-data.json` holds the data. `scripts/estimate-fees.py` turns it into an estimate. Always show the notice above with any number.

## Building permit fee (valuation table)

Row base plus $10.39 for each $1,000 of valuation above the row floor, prorated (not rounded up to the next $1,000), rounded to the cent half-to-even. Row 1 is a flat $179.67 up to $2,000. The City prints this table without saying how to handle a partial $1,000, and the published rows make the fee drop at the $1,000,000 and $5,000,000 boundaries. Treat the result as an estimate and confirm it with the Building Division.

| 8 | Over $5,000,000 | $39,441.24 | $10.39 |

Plan review: 65% of the permit fee. Permit issuance: $80.90 for the first permit, $40.45 for each additional permit. Surcharges: Technology Surcharge 1.75%, General Plan Maintenance 3.50% on the permit and plan review fees.

The City computes its own minimum valuation from occupancy, construction type and new square footage, and uses the higher of that minimum and the applicant valuation.
