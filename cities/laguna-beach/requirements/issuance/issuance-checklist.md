# Laguna Beach: before the Building permit issues

_Generated by the plugin publisher from the maintained Laguna Beach requirements source. Do not edit by hand; changes are overwritten._

Source: City of Laguna Beach Building Division handout, "Building Submittal Requirements Prior to Building Permit Issuance" (current as of September 2026). "When it applies" is the City permit-technician criterion for each handout row.

These items come after Building plan check approval and before the permit issues. They are not part of the application completeness check. Items marked with * on the handout depend on the City record for the parcel.
