# Laguna Beach Planning: Residential plan sheets

_Generated by the plugin publisher from the maintained Laguna Beach requirements source. Do not edit by hand; changes are overwritten._

Source: City of Laguna Beach Residential Review Worksheet (7-14-25), Plan Submittal Requirements.

What each Planning plan sheet must show. Which sheets a project type needs is in `../plan-sheets/plan-sheet-matrix.md`; which application to file is in `application-index.md`; how the City judges a sheet at intake, and which items it takes later, is in `counter-practice.md`.

"Required" items are on every submittal that includes the sheet. "Conditional" items apply only when their trigger is true for the project. Decide triggers from the plans, application and site facts.

## TITLE SHEET

- **Title Sheet** (Required) — include a detailed project description, project address, assessor's parcel number (APN), and legal description of the site.
  - Provide name and contact information for the property owner, project architect or designer, civil engineer, and other design professionals (name(s), address(es), phone number(s), and email address(es).
  - List the current discretionary approvals for the site and all project conditions of approval (if applicable).
  - Include completed Project Summary Table.
  - Include Plan Sheet Index.

## SURVEY

- Indicate plan scale.
- **Topographic Survey** (Required) — prepared, signed, and stamped by a licensed surveyor or civil engineer; shows current property conditions; and complies with the Site Survey Requirements Handout.
- **Record of Survey** (Required) — Applies only when the project involves boundary or monument work, such as resolving unclear lot lines or locating, resetting, or setting survey monuments. When that scope applies, provide a record of survey prepared, signed, and stamped by a licensed surveyor or civil engineer and recorded by the County Surveyor's Office. It must show:
  - All property boundary lines
  - All public and private easements
  - Any new monuments set to show boundary lines

## SITE PLAN

- **Site Plan** (Required) — indicate plan scale and provide the following information.
  - Include: north arrow (magnetic and project north).
  - Include: legend (line types, hatched or shaded areas, etc.).
  - Lot Analysis:
  - Dimension all lot lines
  - Show and label all buildings and structures on the property
  - Provide topographic contours at two-foot intervals, covering the entire property to the center of the right-of-way.
  - Dimension existing, required, and proposed (narrowest) setbacks
  - Provide average lot width and depth [LBMC 25.08.022]
  - Hardscape/Enclosures:
  - Label, locate, and dimension all proposed hardscape improvements
  - Identify height(s) and material(s) for all existing and proposed fences, walls, gates, and required pool security enclosures
  - Note height elevations at the following locations:
  - Fixed benchmark elevation (datum point) from the survey that represents a permanent, identifiable marker in the field
  - Top of curb or centerline of the street right-of-way, midway between the projected side lot lines [LBMC 25.08.016]
  - Centerline of the street opposite the driveway and at the intersection of the driveway and front property line [LBMC 25.52.008(I)(3)(b)]
  - All property corners [LBMC 25.08.034]
  - Right-of-Ways (Streets and Alleys):
  - Label, locate, and dimension the officially recorded and actual improved right-of-way centerlines
  - Dimension the width of all abutting right-of-ways (e.g., streets and alleyways)
  - Label all existing and proposed improvements within the right-of-way (e.g., driveways, sidewalks, meters, utility boxes/poles)
  - Easements: Show, dimension, label all existing and proposed public or private easements and all areas to be dedicated to the City.
  - Driveway: Note average driveway slope that spans from property line to face of garage/parking space(s) [LBMC 25.52.008(I)(3)]
  - Vacant and/or Oceanfront Properties: Accurately show immediate outline of existing structures on adjacent contiguous properties
  - Trash Storage: Locate, dimension, and note material [LBMC 25.53.012].
  - New Primary Dwellings, Major Remodels, and Additions Exceeding 50 percent of Existing Structure:
  - Include this note: "All utility lines to the nearest existing pole will be underground" [LBMC 25.53.010].
  - Label type and note location of the utility connection (pole or box).
  - Indicate size of all utility boxes.
  - Sewer Line: Show, label and dimension any connections to the public sewer system.

## ROOF PLAN

- **Roof Plan** (Required) — indicate plan scale and provide the following information.
  - Provide existing and proposed (separate) roof plans:
  - Provide roof dimensions on both plans. Dimension eaves.
  - On the existing roof plan, identify and dimension the proposed demolition areas (eaves are included in roof area).
  - On proposed roof plan, differentiate existing vs. new construction areas.
  - Provide a table that tallies the existing and demolition roof areas.
  - For each roof plan:
  - Show roof ridges, hips, valleys, crickets, parapets, etc.
  - Note elevation heights of ridges, eaves, skylights, and chimneys
  - Note roof slopes, pitch
  - Dimension solar-ready roof area and eave overhangs

## STAKING PLAN

- **Staking Plan** (Conditional) — when story-pole staking applies, indicate plan scale and provide the following information. Current routing is component-specific: a B(1) first hearing requires constructed/certified staking 21 calendar days before the hearing; ADR requires staking 14 days before its public hearing only after the Director/designee decides it is required; directed restaking is 14 days; Zone Clearance alone requires none. An ADU or local ADU CDP does not inherit staking. Review any deck, access, grading, wall, or other component separately under LBMC 25.05.040(B).
  - Show all property lines, building footprint, street names, north arrow and location of story poles. Poles should be placed to clearly represent the proposed building envelope. Roof ridges, eaves, decks, chimneys and other features that would be of significance to other property owners should be staked.
  - Note the fixed datum point (same as datum point used on site plan).
  - Note the appropriate height elevation of the top of each pole relative to the datum point.
  - Provide horizontal dimensions to at least one pole relative to the property lines as a reference point to assure the poles are correctly located on the site.
  - Note that story poles shall be strung and connected with ribbon or string to depict the building outline.
  - Include a certification table, with the pole number and proposed maximum elevation columns completed, and signature form. See the Information Guide for Staking for the correct format.

## FLOOR PLANS (As-Builts & Proposed)

- **Floor Plans (As-Builts & Proposed)** (Required) — indicate plan scale and provide the following information.
  - Orient the plans to match the site plan, including a north arrow (magnetic and project north) and property lines.
  - Indicate the use and dimensions of all existing and proposed rooms and interior garages.
  - Dimension and show the existing walls and the proposed to be demolished, rebuilt, and new walls on the as-built floor plan. Provide a table that tallies the existing and demolished wall lengths (in terms of linear feet). Using standard drafting techniques/legend, provide a legend that clearly identifies existing walls to remain (plain), existing walls to be rebuilt (shaded), walls to be demolished (dashed), and proposed new walls (dark shade).
  - Note the finish floor elevation of each level, including finish surface elevations for all decks, patios, terraces, and landings.

## ELEVATION VIEWS (As-Builts & Proposed)

- **Elevation Views (As-Builts & Proposed)** (Required) — indicate plan scale and provide the following information.
  - Label height elevations of roofs, floors, plate lines, decks, and railings. Dimension ceiling heights.
  - Differentiate new versus existing construction using shading or crosshatching. Provide a legend stating what the shading or crosshatch represents.
  - Show existing (dashed line) and proposed (solid line) grades.
  - Using dashed lines, illustrate the maximum allowed building envelope [LBMC 25.50.004(d)].
  - Include landscape site walls exceeding five feet in height on the elevation drawings.
  - On as-built elevations show (using shading or crosshatch) and dimension demolition areas. Provide a legend stating what the shading or crosshatch represents.

## CROSS SECTIONS

- **Cross Sections** (Required) — indicate plan scale and provide the following information.
  - Provide a minimum of two perpendicular views through the complete site showing the structure and lines of existing and proposed grade.
  - Include the maximum allowed building envelope and elevation heights.
  - Include site improvements such as walls, terraces, and pools/spas.
  - Existing and proposed building heights are measured from the outside structural walls, including retaining walls when below grade.

## FLOOR AREA CALCULATIONS

- **Floor Area Calculation Sheet** (Required) — indicate plan scale and provide dimensioned calculation plans separate from the floor-plan sheets.
  - Provide dimensioned floor plans that are separate from the requisite floor plan sheets:
  - Utilizes geometric shapes (e.g., rectangles and triangles) to show how existing and proposed floor areas are calculated.
  - Include and label all living areas, garages, decks, storage and mechanical spaces.
  - Provide a table that itemizes and tallies each area (square feet).
  - Floor area is calculated from the outside structural wall, including retaining walls when below grade.

## OPEN SPACE CALCULATIONS

- **Open Space Calculation Sheet** (Conditional) — for residential projects other than single-family residential, indicate plan scale and provide:
  - Provide a separate site plan that shows and dimensions open space areas and includes a table that tallies up its calculations [LBMC 25.50.010].
  - Note and show how the property meets the required minimum open space (40% x total gross living area on the property).
  - Note and show how the property meets the minimum required open space that must be located at ground level and be open from ground-to-sky (60% x required min. open space).
  - Note and show how the open space will be entirely located within the buildable area of the lot.
  - Note and show how the open space will not include land or structures devoted to parking, storage, trash areas, driveways or other uses, except those normally considered as outdoor living.
  - Note and show how the open space will meet the minimum required dimension of 6 feet or 10 feet if the area is designed for through pedestrian access.

## GRADING & DRAINAGE PLAN

- **Grading & Drainage Plan** (Conditional) — required for the triggered scopes below; indicate plan scale.
  - Trigger conditions:
  - The project proposes grading and cut and/or fill outside the existing building footprint exceeds 20 cubic yards; grading inside that footprint does not count.
  - Provide:
  - Preliminary grading and drainage plan with the proposed cut and fill area delineated.
  - Include a sufficient number of slope profiles to illustrate the extent of the proposed grading.
  - Contours are to be shown for existing conditions and proposed work.
  - For project sites containing a mapped significant watercourse or sensitive habitat:
  - Show temporary protective barriers beyond the required setback for the protection of the watercourse and sensitive habitat during construction.

## LANDSCAPE PLAN

- **Landscape Plan** (Conditional) — Require it for a major remodel; a new building subject to the (B)(2) ADU and new-primary-dwelling carve-out; an improvement over 15 feet high or a new upper level [LBMC 25.05.040(B)(1)(a)–(c)]; or proposed landscaping modifications. For the ordinance triggers, an as-built landscape plan satisfies intake when no landscape work is proposed. A matrix checkmark or addition percentage alone does not trigger it. A pool, ground-level lounge deck, patio cover or incidental lawn alone does not trigger it. Preserve ministerial ADU protections. When required, indicate plan scale and meet the following general requirements:
  - Landscape plans must include and differentiate new and existing-to-remain plant material.
  - See the landscape submittal checklist for a comprehensive list of requirements
  - For new or major remodel of single-family homes:
  - Provide a separate site plan that shows and dimensions open space areas and includes a table that tallies up its calculations [LBMC 25.10.008(O)].
  - Note and show how:
  - The property meets the required minimum required open space and open space that must be located at ground level and be open from ground-to-sky
  - The open space will be located within the buildable or setback areas.
  - The open space will meet the minimum required dimension of 3 feet.
  - The landscaped open area or landscaped area shall be any combination of living plants (such as grass, ground cover, shrubs, vines, hedges or trees).

## LIGHTING PLAN

- **Exterior Lighting Plan** (Required) — Require it only for LBMC 25.05.040(B)(1)(a)–(c): a major remodel; a new building subject to the (B)(2) ADU and new-primary-dwelling carve-out; or an improvement over 15 feet high or a new upper level. Preserve ministerial ADU protections. An attached ADU, deck, landscaping modification, addition percentage or matrix checkmark alone does not trigger it. When required, indicate plan scale.
  - Show exterior site lighting, egress doors and all lighting fixtures proposed for the exterior of the building (soffit, surface mounted, etc.)
  - The lighting plan must include a summary table, keyed to the plan, indicating proposed fixture type, quantity, lumens and wattage. Include lighting cut sheets for verification with the City's Good Neighbor Outdoor Lighting requirements [LBMC 7.70].

## FIRE DEPARTMENT - SITE ACCESS PLAN

- **Fire Department Site Access Plan** (Conditional) — Required only when proposed new building-type construction (a new home, detached ADU, or other detached structure) could block existing fire access. Attached ADUs, additions, grading alone, interior work, roof or deck work, and surface replacement never trigger it. Neither VHFHSZ nor another hazard overlay triggers it. A legible, project-identifiable site-access depiction satisfies intake. Completed Fire review and technical adequacy belong to Building/Fire.

## SITE & RIGHT-OF-WAY IMPROVEMENT PLAN

- **Site & Right-of-Way Improvement Plan** (Conditional) — Applies for a new home or a major remodel under the Local Coastal Program's 50-percent test; for other projects, evaluate it only when Public Works/Water Quality improvements are actually in scope. Utility laterals or an attached ADU’s utility connections alone are not Public Works improvements and never trigger this branch. When applicable, provide at plan scale a topographic survey (including public and private easements) and an overlay reflecting existing and proposed improvements.

## ADDITIONAL REQUIRED INFORMATION FOR CERTAIN SITES

- **Arch Beach Heights neighboring-window exhibit** (Conditional)
- **Diamond Crestview Construction Staging Plan and Constraint Analysis** (Conditional)
- **Historic-structure assessment** (Conditional)
- **WELO documentation** (Conditional) — at Planning, only the landscape area figure when the project installs or modifies landscape; the Landscape Documentation Package itself is a building plan check submittal [LBMC 19.01.006(a); City WELO Information Guide].
- **Water Quality Management Plan (WQMP) sheet** (Conditional) — An intake requirement only for a new home or major remodel on a slope greater than 25 percent, established from the survey or site facts. A slope alone does not establish new-home or major-remodel scope. At 25 percent or below, use the LID checklist. When the intake trigger applies, provide the WQMP and applicable submittal form; the same requirement governs its full-sized plan sheet.
