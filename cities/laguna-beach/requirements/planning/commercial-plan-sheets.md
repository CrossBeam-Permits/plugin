# Laguna Beach Planning: Commercial and mixed-use plan sheets

_Generated by the plugin publisher from the maintained Laguna Beach requirements source. Do not edit by hand; changes are overwritten._

Source: City of Laguna Beach Planning Division applications (July and August 2026) for the specialized lists; the City has not published a commercial review worksheet.

The City has not published a commercial Planning review worksheet. Apart from the lists that point to a City application, the sheet sections below are a first-pass list built from the residential worksheet and ordinary commercial Design Review practice. Confirm them with the Planning Division. A sign or wireless application uses its own checklist (`sign-permit.md`, `wireless-facility-permit.md`) in place of this list. On a Conditional Use Permit where the City accepts its Exhibit B basic site plan and basic floor plan (`conditional-use-permit.md`), those exhibits replace the Survey, Site Plan, Floor Plans, Elevations and Parking sections here.

What each Planning plan sheet must show. Which sheets a project type needs is in `../plan-sheets/plan-sheet-matrix.md`; which application to file is in `application-index.md`; how the City judges a sheet at intake, and which items it takes later, is in `counter-practice.md`.

"Required" items are on every submittal that includes the sheet. "Conditional" items apply only when their trigger is true for the project. Decide triggers from the plans, application and site facts.

## TITLE SHEET

- **Title Sheet** (Required) — include a detailed project description (proposed use(s), scope of work, tenant), project address, assessor's parcel number (APN), and legal description of the site.
  - Provide name and contact information for the property owner and each design professional (architect/designer, civil engineer, landscape architect): name, address, phone, email.
  - List the current discretionary approvals and conditions of approval for the site (if any).
  - Include a completed Project Summary Table: zoning, existing/proposed use, gross floor area by use, lot area, building height, occupant load, and parking required vs. provided.
  - Include a Plan Sheet Index.

## SURVEY

- **Topographic / Boundary Survey** (Required) — indicate plan scale; it must be prepared, signed, and stamped by a licensed surveyor or civil engineer, showing property lines, easements, and existing conditions.

## SITE PLAN

- **Site Plan** (Required) — indicate plan scale; include north arrow and legend.
  - Dimension all lot lines; show and label all existing and proposed buildings/structures.
  - Dimension existing, required, and proposed setbacks.
  - Show the parking layout, drive aisles, loading zone(s), and vehicular + pedestrian circulation.
  - Show, dimension, and label all existing and proposed public/private easements and rights-of-way.
  - Locate, dimension, and note the trash/recycling enclosure.
  - Label existing and proposed improvements within the abutting right(s)-of-way (driveways, sidewalks, meters, utility boxes/poles).

## FLOOR PLANS

- **Existing and Proposed Floor Plans** (Required) — provide all levels, drawn to scale and fully dimensioned.
  - Label the use of each area/room and the gross floor area by use.
  - Indicate occupancy/occupant load assumptions used for the parking calculation.

## ELEVATIONS

- **Existing and Proposed Building Elevations** (Required) — provide all elevations, drawn to scale.
  - Dimension building height(s) and label the height datum/benchmark.
  - Identify exterior materials, colors, and finishes (a materials/colors board or schedule).

## PARKING & CIRCULATION

- **Parking & Circulation Plan/Calculation** (Required) — provide a parking calculation by use (required stalls per the applicable ratio vs. provided), including accessible (ADA) stalls and any required loading and bicycle parking.
  - Show vehicular circulation, drive-aisle widths, and pedestrian access/path of travel.

## LANDSCAPE

- **Landscape Plan** (Conditional) — when landscape work or WELO review applies, provide proposed planting and hardscape.
  - Note WELO applicability separately; a landscape sheet alone does not prove WELO applies.

## SIGNAGE

- **Sign Program / Sign Details** (Conditional) — if signage is proposed, show location, dimensions, area, height, materials, illumination) for any proposed signage.

## COASTAL / OVERLAY (when applicable per site facts)

- **Coastal Development Permit materials** (Conditional) — when the site/scope requires them, (public-access and visual-resource exhibits as applicable).
- **Historic Register materials** (Conditional)
- **Geotechnical / coastal-hazard report** (Conditional) — for bluff-top or coastal-hazard parcels, per the site facts (same as the residential coastal items).
