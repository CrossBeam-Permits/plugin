# Laguna Beach Planning: City counter practice at intake

_Generated by the plugin publisher from the maintained Laguna Beach requirements source. Do not edit by hand; changes are overwritten._

City counter practice: how City staff apply the published lists at intake, as staff have stated it or as City review decisions show it. It is not code and not a published handout, and it can change. Confirm with the City before relying on it.

The City's published lists (the plan-sheet matrix, `residential-plan-sheets.md` and each application) say what a submittal contains. This page says how the Planning intake check applies them: when an item is due, and when a sheet is complete enough to accept.

## When a required sheet is complete enough

For the Site Plan, Title Sheet, Cross Sections, Elevation Views and Floor Plans, the intake planner accepts the sheet when the elements the City lists for it are substantially shown. As observed at the City's intake, a sheet is generally accepted when at least 50% of the elements that apply to the project are shown. An element that does not apply to the project is not counted. When a sheet falls short, the City names each missing element in the words of its application, not just the sheet.

Basis: Planning Division staff guidance, September 2026; Planning Division staff guidance, May 2026

### Digital site photos

The photographs themselves are required. The elements are labels the applicant writes for each photo: the direction of the photo; a description.

Not required at this scope: The scope of work is minor exterior work. The City's example is a Design Review for a minor exterior alteration. New development, new floor area, a new unit, or a major remodel is NOT minor exterior work.

City practice: At this scope the City does not require the direction label or the description. The photographs themselves are still required.

Basis: Planning Division staff guidance, September 2026

## When each item is due

### Landscape plan

- Covers: Landscape Plan, including the same-named Plan Sheet Matrix column and application exhibits.
- When: Needed only when a trigger stated here applies.
- Applies when: Due for a major remodel (LBMC 25.05.040(B)(1)(a)); a new building subject to the (B)(2) ADU and new-primary-dwelling carve-out (B)(1)(b); an improvement over 15 feet high or a new upper level (B)(1)(c); or proposed landscaping modifications. A pool, ground-level lounge deck, patio cover or incidental lawn alone is not a trigger. For the first three triggers, an as-built landscape plan satisfies intake when no landscape work is proposed.
- Exception: A trigger above is established and the selected matrix cell is not blank.
  - When: Due at intake. The City returns an application that lacks it.
  - The plan is required at intake; it must meet the published content requirements and the as-built landscape allowance where applicable.
- Exception: A trigger is established but the selected matrix cell is blank.
  - When: The City decides case by case. Ask the City.
  - To settle with the City: Does the ordinance or the blank Plan Sheet Matrix cell govern Landscape Plan intake for this scope?
- Basis: Planning Division staff guidance, September 2026

### Exterior lighting plan

- Covers: Exterior Lighting Plan, including the Lighting Plan matrix column.
- When: Needed only when a trigger stated here applies.
- Applies when: Under LBMC 25.05.040(B)(1)(a)–(c): a major remodel; a new building subject to the (B)(2) ADU and new-primary-dwelling carve-out; or an improvement over 15 feet high or a new upper level.
- Exception: A trigger above is established and the selected matrix cell is not blank.
  - When: Due at intake. The City returns an application that lacks it.
  - The plan is required at intake; it must meet the published content requirements.
- Exception: A trigger is established but the selected matrix cell is blank, including Upper-Level Additions or >15 ft above grade.
  - When: The City decides case by case. Ask the City.
  - To settle with the City: LBMC 25.05.040(B)(1)(c) requires an exterior lighting plan, but the matrix cell is blank. Which governs at intake?
- Basis: Planning Division staff guidance, September 2026

### Historic assessment

- Covers: The historic resource assessment (character-defining features; what is removed and retained).
- When: Not needed for the intake check. The City asks for it at a later step, stated below.
- City practice: The City initiates the historic assessment at the applicant's first resubmittal, because the design may change in response to first-round comments. Not a sufficiency item at intake.
- Exception: The proposed work is truly minor and akin to repair, maintenance, or in-kind replacement of a historic resource (the City's example: roof replacement). An addition, new floor area, or a new unit is NOT minor in-kind work.
  - When: The City decides case by case. Ask the City.
  - To settle with the City: Minor in-kind work on a historic resource: the City initiates the historian's review at intake. Confirm the packet contains either (i) a deposit for a City-retained historic assessment, or (ii) the applicant's consultant assessment plus the peer-review deposit — and in either case an executed Advance Deposit and Reimbursement Agreement.
- Basis: Planning Division staff guidance, August 2026

### Colored elevations

- Covers: Colored elevations of all sides (Design Review application).
- When: Not needed for the intake check. The City asks for it at a later step, stated below.
- City practice: Colored elevations may be deferred to the applicant's resubmittal. The City does not treat them as an application-sufficiency issue.
- Basis: Planning Division staff guidance, August 2026

### Staking plan (story poles)

- Covers: The staking plan (story-pole layout, datum-referenced pole elevations, certification table).
- When: Not needed for the intake check. The City asks for it at a later step, stated below.
- City practice: The staking plan may be deferred — the City recognizes that project designs often change between first and second iterations.
- Basis: Planning Division staff guidance, August 2026

### Objective Design Standards checklists (Chapter 25.13 pathway)

- Covers: The completed Chapter 2 General Design Standards checklist and exactly one applicant-selected Chapter 3 Form Group checklist, with plan-sheet locators, for an elected Chapter 25.13 pathway.
- When: The City decides case by case. Ask the City.
- Applies when: The applicant elects Chapter 25.13 for a new single-family or two-family primary dwelling, including SB 9.
- City practice: City document 26790 says both checklists belong in the application package, but the intake consequence has not been confirmed by staff.
- To settle with the City: Confirm whether omission of either completed objective-design checklist is intake-blocking, and whether the City provides a separately fillable checklist instead of the pages in document 26790.
- Basis: City of Laguna Beach Objective Design Standards for Single-Family and Two-Family Residential Development, City document 26790 (August 2026)

### Public-hearing noticing (posting, mailing labels, mailed notices, affidavit)

- Covers: Public-hearing noticing, including property posting, listing-service payment, mailing labels, mailed notices, and affidavit of mailing.
- When: Not needed for the intake check. The City asks for it at a later step, stated below.
- City practice: Noticing is due before the City notices a hearing, not at zoning-plan-check intake.
- Basis: Planning Division staff guidance, September 2026

### Early neighbor communication (Design Review)

- Covers: Early Neighbor Communication materials, certified mailing list, outreach notice, and outreach report for a Design Review project.
- When: Not needed for the intake check. The City asks for it at a later step, stated below.
- City practice: Neighbor communication is due before hearing scheduling, not at zoning-plan-check intake.
- Basis: Planning Division staff guidance, September 2026

### Coastal Development Permit noticing

- Covers: CDP-specific noticing for a hearing, de minimis waiver notice, or minor-development notice under the Coastal Development Permit application.
- When: Not needed for the intake check. The City asks for it at a later step, stated below.
- City practice: CDP noticing is due when the City prepares the later notice.
- Basis: Planning Division staff guidance, September 2026

### Paint swatches at the site

- Covers: The two-foot-square exterior paint swatches installed at the project site before a Design Review hearing.
- When: Not needed for the intake check. The City asks for it at a later step, stated below.
- City practice: Paint swatches are a later pre-hearing site action, not an electronic zoning-plan-check intake item.
- Basis: Planning Division staff guidance, September 2026

### JADU deed restriction

- Covers: The recorded deed restriction required for a JADU that shares sanitation facilities.
- When: Not needed for the intake check. The City asks for it at a later step, stated below.
- City practice: The deed restriction must be recorded before building permit issuance, not at zoning plan check.
- Basis: Planning Division staff guidance, September 2026
