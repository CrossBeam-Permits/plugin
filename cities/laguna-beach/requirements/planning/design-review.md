**When to file.** The City states the trigger on page 1: "This application includes a comprehensive list of information and documents that are required to submit an application for a project requiring Design Review. Projects requiring Design Review are identified in the Laguna Beach Municipal Code. (See LBMC Section 25.05.040.)" The City also states: "Additional applications are required when an applicant is requesting additional entitlements, such as a Coastal Development Permit."

"Required" items are on every submittal of this application. "Conditional" items apply only when their trigger is true for the project. Decide triggers from the plans, application and site facts.

## APPLICATION AND FEES

- **Design Review Application** (Required) — the completed City application form: project location (address and APN), applicant, applicant representative, and property owner contact information, and a project description that briefly describes the proposed improvements. The City files it through the Public Permit Portal and states "Applications without the required application fees will not be accepted."
- **Advance Deposit and Reimbursement Agreement and Title Report** (Conditional) — At intake, require the agreement and a current title report only when the City will peer-review a geotechnical or coastal-hazards report.

## OTHER SUBMITTAL REQUIREMENTS FOR DESIGN REVIEW

- **Colored Elevations** (Conditional) — trigger stated by the City: "Required for building additions, new development, and when Design Review is triggered for exterior modifications to more than 50% of any street-facing façade of a building in a residential zone." Include all sides of structures and retaining walls over 5 feet in height. Include the maximum allowed building envelope. The City states not to show landscaping on the elevations.
- **Color and Material Selections** (Conditional) — include specifications and pictures of all exterior building finishes and roofing. Identify all proposed exterior colors, including manufacturer code specifications. Color follows scope: Applies only when the project actually proposes new or changed exterior finishes, colors, or roofing.
- **Paint swatches installed at the site** (Conditional) — trigger stated by the City: the Planning Commission is the design review authority [LBMC 25.05.040(F)(1)(b)]. At least one week before the scheduled public hearing, install two-foot-square minimum paint swatches at the project site for review under natural light.
- **3D Color Renderings** (Conditional) — trigger confirmed by staff: new development or a major remodel under the Local Coastal Program's 50-percent major-remodel test. Renderings include landscaping, depictions of people to help evaluate scale, and accurate color. Provide front, rear, and side elevations. The City states a streetscape view showing surrounding development is optional but highly encouraged.
- **Digital Site Photos** (Required) — label file names with the direction of the photo and a description.
  - The direction of the photo
  - A description
- **Title Report** (Conditional) — trigger stated by the City: "Required for projects that require a topographic survey." Dated within one year of the application date. Title insurance or a title policy is a substitute, not the deliverable. Apply filing-context before requesting a replacement.
- **Affidavit and Use and Disclosure of Architectural Drawings** (Required) — the City states: "Affidavit and Use Architectural Drawings – sign and submit Exhibits I and J."

## SUBMITTAL ITEMS DUE BEFORE THE CITY WILL NOTICE A PUBLIC HEARING

- **Noticing** (Conditional) — at least 30 calendar days before the public hearing, provide proof that the property has been posted with notice, and a receipt of payment to an approved Listing Service that prepares the mailing list of property owners within 300 feet and tenants within 100 feet. The Listing Service prints and mails the notices and provides staff an affidavit of mailing.
- **Early Neighbor Communication** (Conditional) — the City states early neighbor communication "for all Design Review projects shall occur prior to the project being scheduled for public hearing" [LBMC 25.05.040(C)(1)]. The applicant notifies property owners within 300 feet through a professional listing service, which certifies the accuracy and completeness of the list and the mailing date and provides a copy of the materials sent. The notice states applicant contact information, the project location, a description of the proposed project, and how to participate in the Design Review process. Exhibit K is the City's best-practices handout and Exhibit L is its outreach report template.

## OTHER SUBMITTAL REQUIREMENTS, AS NEEDED

- **Biological Assessment** (Conditional)
- **Historic Assessment** (Conditional) — Clearly identify the character-defining features and the development that will be removed and retained.
- **Water Quality Management Plan (WQMP) and WQMP Submittal Form (Exhibit M)** (Conditional) — An intake requirement only for a new home or major remodel on a slope greater than 25 percent, established from the survey or site facts. A slope alone does not establish new-home or major-remodel scope. At 25 percent or below, use the LID checklist. When the intake trigger applies, provide the WQMP and applicable submittal form; the same requirement governs its full-sized plan sheet.
- **Low Impact Development Checklist** (Conditional) — trigger: the developed slope is 25 percent or below. Do not demand a LID checklist as a substitute when WQMP is only a later staff determination for another scope over 25 percent.
- **Coastal Hazards Study and Bluff Edge Determination** (Conditional) — trigger as this application states it: "Required for oceanfront or bluff properties." The wave uprush and impact report considers a seasonally eroded beach combined with long-term (75 year) erosion, high tide conditions combined with long-term (75 year) projections for sea level rise, and storm waves from a 100-year event or a storm comparable to the 1982/83 El Niño event, prepared by a licensed civil engineer with expertise in coastal processes.
- **Geological, soils, or geotechnical study** (Conditional) — trigger: vacant and oceanfront sites. This application folds the bluff-edge content into this item: coastal bluff lots identify the bluff edge location (Land Use Element Definitions 101 and 102), any geologic hazards, any necessary mitigation measures, and statements that the site is suitable and the development will be safe from geologic hazard for its economic life. For development on oceanfront bluffs the report includes slope stability analyses and estimates of the long-term average bluff retreat or erosion rate over the expected life of the development. A licensed professional Engineering Geologist or Geotechnical Engineer prepares and signs it.
- **Hydraulic Report** (Conditional) — A parcel-level floodplain overlap alone does not trigger it. The later report is a professional engineer’s No-Rise / No-Impact certification supported by hydrologic and hydraulic analyses under LBMC Chapter 25.38.
- **Traffic Study** (Conditional) — trigger: subdivisions, new commercial developments, and major commercial renovations. The City states a traffic study may also be required to comply with the California Environmental Quality Act.
- **Parking Study** (Conditional) — trigger: the proposed project does not meet the minimum parking requirements, or LBMC Chapter 25.52 specifies one. This application states a parking study "may be required"; the Variance application states "will be required" for the same condition.
