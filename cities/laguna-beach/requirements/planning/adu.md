**When to file.** The City states the trigger on page 1: "This application includes a comprehensive list of information and documents that are required to submit an application for an accessory dwelling unit ('ADU') and/or junior accessory dwelling unit ('JADU')." The City also states: "Additional applications are required when an applicant is requesting additional entitlements, such as a Coastal Development Permit or a permit for Design Review," and gives its own examples of Design Review triggers on an ADU project: "design review is required for grading in exceed of 20 cubic yards outside of the building footprint, new vehicular access, retaining walls in excess of 5 feet, decks more than 3 feet above adjacent existing legal grade, and certain fences. (see LBMC Section 25.05.040.)" The City's development standards are at LBMC Chapter 25.17; the application notes that as of March 2025 Chapter 25.17 is replaced by Urgency Ordinance No. 1713.

"Required" items are on every submittal of this application. "Conditional" items apply only when their trigger is true for the project. Decide triggers from the plans, application and site facts.

## APPLICATION AND FEES

- **ADU & JADU Application** (Required) — the completed City application form. The City files it through the Public Permit Portal and states "Applications without the required application fees will not be accepted."
- **Advance Deposit and Reimbursement Agreement and Title Report** (Conditional) — At intake, require the agreement and a current title report only when the City will peer-review a geotechnical or coastal-hazards report.

## COMPANION COASTAL DEVELOPMENT PERMIT

- **Coastal Development Permit Application** (Conditional) — trigger stated by the City: a CDP is required by LBMC Chapters 25.17 and 25.07, as further explained in Exhibit B of this application. The City states: "If required, the Coastal Development Permit Application must be processed concurrently with this ADU/JADU Application." The City also quotes Government Code Section 66329: the local government approves or denies the CDP within 60 days of a completed application, and processes it concurrently with the ADU application; section 66329(a) does not require a public hearing on it. After C1 establishes that the City/local government is the CDP decision maker and the CDP covers only the ADU, Government Code section 66329(c) also makes that local-government decision not subject to appeal under Public Resources Code section 30603, even if the parcel is ordinarily in a Chapter 25.07 appeal category. A CDP that also covers other development follows the appeal area. Do not apply this local procedure to a Coastal Commission decision or unresolved authority. For a conversion, as-built floor plans control the trigger: existing habitable space needs only the ADU & JADU Application; non-habitable space or new floor area also needs the CDP Application.

## CONSTRUCTION PLANS AND DRAWINGS

- **Construction Plans and Drawings** (Required) — the City states: "When preparing construction plans, the drawings need to clearly communicate design, structural integrity, and code compliance." The City's own footnote states the consequence: "The City will continue to process your application without the construction plans and drawings, but the application will not be deemed complete under the law without the construction plans and drawings."

## OTHER SUBMITTAL REQUIREMENTS

- **Rent Reporting** (Required) — the form carries a write-in line: "Estimate of the projected annualized rent that will be charged for the ADU or JADU if the applicant intends to rent the unit." The City treats this line as voluntary: it never gates intake.
- **Percolation Test** (Conditional) — trigger stated by the City: "If the ADU or JADU will connect to an onsite wastewater - treatment system, the owner must include with the application a percolation test completed within the last five years or, if the percolation test has been recertified, within the last 10 years."

- **Title Report** (Conditional) — dated within one year of the application date. This application states no trigger condition on the title report; the Design Review, Variance, and Coastal Development Permit applications do. The lane trigger governs every copy of this item. Title insurance or a title policy is a substitute, not the deliverable. Apply filing-context before requesting a replacement.
- **Affidavit and Use and Disclosure of Architectural Drawings** (Required) — the City states: "Affidavit and Use Architectural Drawings – sign and submit Exhibits I & J."

## OTHER SUBMITTAL REQUIREMENTS, AS NEEDED

- **Historic Assessment** (Conditional) — Clearly identify the character-defining features and the development that will be removed and retained. The City retains an outside consultant to review it, and when the Director requires the assessment the applicant provides the required deposit and the Advance Deposit and Reimbursement Agreement (Exhibit E).
- **Water Quality Management Plan (WQMP) and WQMP Submittal Form (Exhibit K)** (Conditional) — An intake requirement only for a new home or major remodel on a slope greater than 25 percent, established from the survey or site facts. A slope alone does not establish new-home or major-remodel scope. At 25 percent or below, use the LID checklist. When the intake trigger applies, provide the WQMP and applicable submittal form; the same requirement governs its full-sized plan sheet.
- **Low Impact Development Checklist** (Conditional) — trigger: the developed slope is 25 percent or below. Do not demand a LID checklist as a substitute when WQMP is only a later staff determination for another scope over 25 percent.
- **Hydraulic Report** (Conditional) — A parcel-level floodplain overlap alone does not trigger it. The later report is a professional engineer’s No-Rise / No-Impact certification supported by hydrologic and hydraulic analyses under LBMC Chapter 25.38.
