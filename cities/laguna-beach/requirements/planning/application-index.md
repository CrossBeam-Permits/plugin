# Laguna Beach Planning: which application to file

_Generated by the plugin publisher from the maintained Laguna Beach requirements source. Do not edit by hand; changes are overwritten._

On 2026-07-22 the City retired its universal Planning Application. A project files the application that matches the entitlement it needs, and may need more than one. When no discretionary entitlement is needed, it files a Zone Clearance Application. The City states each trigger below.

## Applications

- **The application matching the project's entitlement path** (Required) — every project files at least one of the applications named below. The application, signed by the property owner, is a baseline deliverable. Signature rule, stated by the City on the application forms themselves: an acceptable signature is a wet signature (a scan is acceptable) or an authenticated electronic signature such as DocuSign; a typed name alone is not sufficient. Where a form carries more than one owner signature line, every line must be signed.
- **Zone Clearance Application** (Conditional) — trigger as the City states it: "for projects that DO NOT require a discretionary planning entitlement, such as Design Review or a Coastal Development Permit." Governing section: LBMC 25.05.045.
- **Design Review Application** (Conditional) — trigger as the City states it: "for a project requiring Design Review. Projects requiring Design Review are identified in the Laguna Beach Municipal Code. (See LBMC Section 25.05.040.)"
- **Coastal Development Permit Application** (Conditional) — trigger as the City states it: the Coastal Act and the City's certified Local Coastal Program require a CDP "for all development within the coastal zone, unless specifically exempted or excluded (see Laguna Beach Municipal Code Section 25.07.008 and 25.07.010, respectively)."
- **Variance Application** (Conditional) — trigger as the City states it: the variance procedure "is designed to permit adjustments to the zoning regulations when there are special or extraordinary circumstances applying to a parcel of land or building which prevent the property from being used to the extent intended by the zoning regulations." The City adds: "A variance is intended to be the rare exception, not the rule." Procedures: LBMC 25.05.025.
- **Conditional Use Permit Application** (Conditional) — trigger as the City states it: "Some uses are allowed in a zoning district but may not fit every location because of their size, intensity, or other characteristics. These uses require a CUP because they could have moderate to significant impacts on the site or nearby properties." Covers a CUP amendment as well. Regulations: LBMC 25.05.030.
- **ADU & JADU Application** (Conditional) — the City's Applications & Handouts page lists this form as the "ADU Application"; the form itself is headed "ADU & JADU Application". Trigger as the City states it: "an application for an accessory dwelling unit ('ADU') and/or junior accessory dwelling unit ('JADU')." Standards: LBMC Chapter 25.17, which the application notes is replaced by Urgency Ordinance No. 1713 as of March 2025.
- **SB 9 Application** (Conditional) — trigger as the City states it: an application "for a SB9 Lot Split or Two-Unit development (see Government Code Sections 65852.21 and 66411.7)". The City's form states: "A Coastal Development Permit is required for SB9 Lot Split or SB9 Two-Unit development. The SB9 application(s) will not be deemed complete until a copy of a legally approved and final Coastal Development Permit for the SB9 application(s) has been submitted." Both of the form's checklists repeat this: Lot Split item I ("Approved Coastal Development Permit") and Two-Unit section H ("Copy of legally approved and final coastal development permit"). A pending CDP application, or one filed at the same time, does not meet this requirement. The SB 9 application stays incomplete until the submittal includes a copy of the approved, final CDP.
- trigger as the City states it: "This form serves as the preliminary application for housing development projects seeking vesting rights pursuant to SB 330, the Housing Crisis Act of 2019." The City states the applicant has 180 days after submitting it to file a full application. The City publishes no submittal checklist with this form: it is a preliminary application form with numbered information fields.
- **Lot Line Adjustment Application** (Conditional) — trigger as the City states it: "an application for Lot Line Adjustment ('LLA')." Regulated by LBMC 21.08.030.
- **Tentative Parcel and Tract Map Application** (Conditional) — trigger as the City states it: "an application to subdivide land requiring the approval of a Tentative Parcel Map or Tentative Tract Map," and the requirements for condominium conversions per LBMC Chapter 25.47. The City states a Tentative Parcel Map is required for a subdivision creating four or fewer parcels and a Tentative Tract Map for five or more. Filing is governed by LBMC 21.08.090.
- **Revocable Encroachment Permit Application** (Conditional) — trigger as the City states it: a REP "may allow minor construction in an otherwise unimproved, unused public right-of-way," limited to minor structures such as low fences and retaining walls, walkways, stairs, and construction incidental to landscape activities. The City states the REP is a discretionary approval by the Community Development Director following a noticed public hearing, and that the application itself requires a Design Review Permit Application.
- **Sign Permit Application** (Conditional) — trigger as the City states it: a Sign Permit is required when "constructing, moving, altering, establishing, painting or creating a temporary or permanent sign in any area of the City, including on windows"; when placing signs in interior areas positioned primarily to be read or visible from the exterior; and when placing luminous tube signs inside a building if visible through a window.
- **Reasonable Accommodation Application** (Conditional) — trigger as the City states it: a request "for consideration for reasonable accommodation in accordance with LBMC 25.05.080," meaning flexibility in the application of land use, zoning and building regulations, or waiver of certain requirements, when necessary to eliminate barriers to fair housing opportunities.
- **Floodplain Development Permit Application for Residential Development** (Conditional) — the City's form states no trigger sentence and cites no Municipal Code section; it names its scope only in its title and its "Development Proposed" check-box block. The form carries no tailored submittal checklist.
- **Historic Register Placement Application** (Conditional) — an owner-initiated letter to the Heritage Committee requesting placement of a historic building on the City's Historic Register, so the owner is eligible for historic preservation incentives. The owner commits to recording the City's Historic Preservation Agreement on title before obtaining benefits. Ordinance: LBMC Chapter 25.45. The form carries no tailored submittal checklist.
- **Wireless Facility Permit Application** (Conditional) — trigger as the City states it: "A WFP is required for any new, collocated, or modified wireless facility located within the public right-of-way, and may take the form of a Planning Commission Wireless Facility Permit or an Administrative Wireless Facility Permit (Director approval)." Terms are defined in LBMC Chapter 11.06. This form predates the July 2026 revision; it is stamped "Updated April 2026".
- **Artist Occupancy Permit Application** (Conditional) — the City requires certification as a working artist to qualify for an Artist Work/Live space in the M-1A and M-1B, C-N, LBP, downtown specific plan, R-2, and R-3 districts. The application is submitted to the Cultural Arts Department and turns on a body of work, formal arts training, exhibition history, and letters of recommendation. The applicant files it directly with the City.
- **Short-Term Lodging — Administrative Use Permit Application** (Conditional) — the City's link to this form serves an account login page rather than the PDF, and its Applications & Handouts page carries only the link title, so no trigger text of the City's own is available to state here. The applicant files it directly with the City.
- **Temporary Use Permit Application** (Conditional) — trigger as the City states it: temporary uses are governed by LBMC Chapter 25.05.035 "and may be permitted when such activities are desirable for the community and are consistent with the health, safety and general welfare of persons residing and working in the community." The City states temporary uses of five days or less may be processed administratively and those longer than five days go to the Planning Commission. The applicant files it directly with the City.
