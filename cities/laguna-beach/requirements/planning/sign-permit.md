"Required" items are on every submittal of this application. "Conditional" items apply only when their trigger is true for the project. Decide triggers from the plans, application and site facts.

## APPLICATION AND FEES

- **Sign Permit Application** (Required) — cover and §4: completed application for the sign, with the signed owner affidavit and Use and Disclosure of Architectural Drawings (Exhibits A and B). A typed name alone is not an authenticated signature.
- **Sign Permit fee status** (Required) — §1: inspect available City fee status using the lane's fee policy.

## SIGN PLANS

- **Scaled sign drawings** (Required) — §2, first bullet: drawings of all proposed signs, with dimensions, area computation, location, height above ground, wording/graphics, letter size/font, materials, colors and lighting elements, if any. For a new or amended Master Sign Program include existing signs. Record evidence on the actual sign drawing, rather than substituting a building floor plan.
- **Sign site plan** (Required) — §2, second bullet: a scaled site plan locating the sign and building/suite and identifying its street frontage length. This source does not require a separately stamped topographic survey for every sign.
- **Building or suite elevation drawing for signage** (Required) — — §2, second bullet: a scaled elevation showing the building or suite and frontage. A freestanding sign elevation alone does not establish that the building/suite elevation exists.
- **Color photographs of elevations and existing signs** (Required) — — §2, third bullet: current site photographs covering those subjects. A proposed-sign rendering alone is not a photograph of existing conditions. If there are no existing signs, establish that fact rather than demanding photos of nonexistent signs.
- **Sign lighting specifications** (Conditional) — §2, fourth bullet: when illumination is proposed, provide lighting specifications identifying the fixtures or system.

## MASTER SIGN PROGRAM

- **Coordinated master sign standards** (Conditional) — §3, first bullet: common standards for lettering, sizes, style, colors, types, placement, lighting, quantity and materials when the program trigger is established.
- **Master sign program plot plan** (Conditional) — §3, second bullet: locations and sizes of existing/proposed signs relative to buildings, parking, driveways, streets and landscape. The sign site plan can supply both artifacts if its content does so; cite the same evidence without duplicate applicant requests.

## SOURCE CONFLICTS AND LATER STAGES

- **Confirm additional Sign Guide materials** (Conditional) — The guide adds an 8½ × 11 inch material/color sample board, broader existing-sign drawings and electrical/wiring/fixture-brochure detail. It says lighting comes from above, whereas §2 also permits qualifying halo illumination.
