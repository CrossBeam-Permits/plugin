## FACILITY PLANS AND STUDIES

- **Facility description and purpose** (Required) — A.3: written description of the facility and why it is proposed. §V also asks for equipment scope and whether associated cabling is underground or aerial.
- **Detailed engineering plans and report** (Required) — A.4: engineered drawings/report addressing minimum technological height/diameter, visible component photos/model names, power output/frequency, antenna/equipment capacity and support-structure suitability. Recognize the artifact before assessing design adequacy.
- **Alternative-site and configuration justification** (Required) — — A.5: comparative study explaining alternatives considered and why they are not viable or are more intrusive. A generic project description is not this study.
- **Scaled facility architectural plans** (Required) — A.6: site, elevations and equipment details, including the visible-component finish note. Evaluate these as facility plans; do not rename pole/footing sections building floor plans.
- **Visual impact analysis** (Required) — A.7: existing/proposed photo simulations from three viewing angles with adequate surroundings. The source specifies equal-sized before/after images, two per page, one angle per page. A raw site photograph alone does not establish a simulation. Keep actual visual impacts for plan check.
- **RF categorical exclusion checklist** (Required) — A.8: record the checklist and its determination. Do not infer exclusion from a small antenna, solar power or a technological-height report.
- **RF exposure compliance report** (Conditional) — A.9: required unless A.8 establishes categorical exclusion. The source asks for projected RF modeling, an executive summary and exposure at ground level/nearby buildings within 30 feet. A height-justification report is not an exposure report.
- **FAA documentation** (Required) — A.10: document the FAA disposition. The table gives no additional explanation.
- **Noise study** (Conditional) — A.11: facilities with passive cooling (no fans) are exempt.
- **Traffic control plan** (Required) — A.12: the listed plan for work in the public right-of-way. A.1 permits explained N/A; inspect that evidence. Do not confuse the plan with the later construction work permit.
- **Landscape plan for facility screening** (Conditional) — A.13: only when landscaping mitigates the facility's visual impact. Include plant identity, mature and maintained dimensions, installation size, mature-size symbols, irrigation/control type and maintenance responsibility. For this application use A.13, not additional generic landscape demands.
- **Facility service area information** (Required) — A.14: locate the description/map of the facility's service area; do not infer it from the site address.
- **Right to access public right-of-way** (Required) — A.15: inspect the access-right evidence. It is distinct from the authorization to submit in A.2; identify a shared instrument only when its text establishes both. Apply public-owner uncertainty guidance where the required mechanism is unresolved.
- **Facility mock-up** (Conditional) — A.16: applies to a Planning Commission WFP on a new support structure, except a one-for-one replacement within five feet of the proposed project. A photograph or rendering does not prove a physical mock-up.
- **Other public agency application requirements** (Required) — — A.17: identify the applicable agencies/requirements, or an explained N/A under A.1. This identification is not proof that every later agency permit must already be issued.
