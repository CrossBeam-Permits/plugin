# Laguna Beach Planning: what a landscape plan shows

_Generated by the plugin publisher from the maintained Laguna Beach requirements source. Do not edit by hand; changes are overwritten._

Source: City of Laguna Beach Landscape Plan Submittal Checklist (revised 4/4/2021), printed as Exhibit G of the Zone Clearance Application (July 2026, https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/home/showpublisheddocument/26718/639216211418770000) and Exhibit H of the Design Review Application Package (August 2026, https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/home/showpublisheddocument/26714/639216204685500000)

Use this list when a project needs a landscape plan. Whether it does is in the plan-sheet matrix, the application checklist and `counter-practice.md`.

1. North arrow and scale (minimum 1/8 inch).
2. Dimensioned property boundaries.
3. Footprints of structures and existing/proposed improvements, including signs, utilities, walls, fences, pools and decks where present.
4. Grades: slopes 2:1 or greater, property-corner grades, berms and landforms.
5. Existing-to-remain and proposed plant legend: botanical/common names, mature dimensions, and maintained dimensions if different.
6. Proposed installation container size; the printed minimum tree size is 15 gallons.
7. Plants shown with scaled symbols at mature size.
8. Irrigation/control type and a clear irrigated/nonirrigated-area design intent, especially for fuel-modification, bluff and preserved areas. New controllers have weather sensitivity. Reuse of existing irrigation does not by itself document type, control or area. Those facts may be supplied in notes on the landscape sheets; the exhibit does not say a separately titled irrigation sheet is invariably required.
9. Where lighting is proposed, fixture types/locations, details or manufacturer information, count, wattage and lumens; summary-table inclusion where applicable.
10. Applicable fuel-modification zones, watercourses/setbacks, habitat, heritage trees, water-quality features and other sensitive site features.
11. The appended fuel-modification maintenance/design standards concern substantive review when applicable.
