Pass only if the response routes the equipment independently from the structure and
enumerates all six tests effective 2026-08-20: ground/wall-at-ground mounting; outside
the required front yard setback (the side yard bar comes from §25.50.004(F)(2), not this
test); at least five feet from all property lines; manufacturer dB specification, 60/50
dB property-line limits, and two maintained attenuation techniques;
City sound-form calculation under ARI 275; and a three-foot path around every structure.
It must say all six yes means Zone Clearance, any no means Administrative Design Review,
and any unknown leaves the equipment route undetermined. Fail a response that makes the
side yard part of the six-part test or routes side-yard equipment to Administrative
Design Review on that basis.
