# Laguna Beach parcel facts

_Generated by the plugin publisher from the maintained Laguna Beach parcel-facts script. Do not edit by hand; changes are overwritten._

`parcel_facts.py` looks up one City of Laguna Beach parcel in the City's public GIS layers and prints what they say about it as JSON: the parcel record and, for each mapped overlay, whether it touches the parcel. It only fetches and reports. The agent decides what each fact means for the project, using the skills.

Python 3.8 or later, standard library only. Nothing to install, no key, no account.

```
python3 scripts/parcel-facts/parcel_facts.py --address "<number> <street>, Laguna Beach, CA"
python3 scripts/parcel-facts/parcel_facts.py --apn <APN>
python3 scripts/parcel-facts/parcel_facts.py --lat <latitude> --lon <longitude>
python3 scripts/parcel-facts/parcel_facts.py --address "<street address>" --out permit-work/parcel-facts.json
```

On Windows use `py` or `python` in place of `python3`.

## Matching the parcel

The address is matched against the City parcel layer. Street types (St, Dr, Ave), unit numbers and a missing city are tolerated. The output's `match.status` says what happened:

| `match.status` | Meaning | Exit code |
|---|---|---|
| `matched` | One parcel. `match.method` says how (`exact_address`, `unit_number`, `unit_base`, `unit_suffix`, `street_type`, `apn`, `point`). Any method other than `exact_address`, `apn` or `point` comes with a note to confirm the address. | 0 |
| `needs_choice` | Several parcels could be meant; or the City layer has no row for the address and the candidates are the parcels near where the US Census geocoder places it; or no parcel contains the given coordinates and the candidates are within 60 ft. Nothing is picked. Show `match.candidates` to the user and re-run with `--apn`. | 2 |
| `not_found` | No City parcel. The parcel may be outside the City (unincorporated Emerald Bay uses Laguna Beach mailing addresses). | 2 |
| `error` | The City parcel layer could not be reached. | 1 |

## Overlays (`overlays`)

One entry per layer. `hit` is `true`, `false` or `"unknown"`. A layer that could not be read is `"unknown"` and listed in `errors`; it is never reported as `false`. Each entry gives `label`, `basis` (how the answer was reached), `share_of_parcel` or `distance_ft`, `skill_fact` (the name the skills use) and `source_url`.

Area layers count as a hit when they cover at least 2% of the parcel or its interior point; a smaller touch is reported as `edge_sliver` and not counted. Fuel modification counts any contact. Line layers (blue-line stream, ridgeline) count when the parcel is within the stated `buffer_ft`.

| Key | Layer | How the skills read it |
|---|---|---|
| `zoning` | Zoning map | The zoning map row for this APN. ZONING1 is the base zone; ZONING2 and ZONING3 carry a second zone or an overlay code. Compare with parcel.zone. |
| `specific_plan` | Specific plans | A specific plan whose standards apply in addition to, or instead of, the base zone. Compare with parcel.specific_plan. |
| `coastal_appeal` | Coastal Commission appeal jurisdiction | Inside the Coastal Commission appeal jurisdiction, a City coastal development permit can be appealed to the Commission. Compare with parcel.coastal_appeal_or_deferred. |
| `coastal_deferred` | Coastal area of deferred certification | Inside an area of deferred certification the Coastal Commission, not the City, issues the coastal development permit. |
| `cat_exclusion` | Coastal categorical exclusion areas | Every City parcel has a row; the CatExclusion column (Yes/No) is the answer. A categorical exclusion does not reach development that stays appealable to the Coastal Commission, so read it together with coastal_appeal. |
| `historic` | Historic register and inventory parcels | Only listed parcels have a row. HISTREG_ST = Yes means on the City Historic Register; HISTRATE is the inventory rating; MILLSACT marks a Mills Act contract. |
| `fuel_modification` | Fuel modification zones | Any contact between the parcel and a mapped fuel modification area counts. Three Fire Department layers are read. |
| `open_space_preserve` | Open space preserve | The skills also count the parcel as open space preserve when parcel.environmental_constraints lists OSP. |
| `landslide` | Seismic hazard landslide areas (City map) | A screening map. The skills treat a parcel as a mapped major landslide only when this layer touches it AND parcel.environmental_constraints carries a landslide flag. |
| `watercourse_buffer` | Drainage course 25-foot buffer | The City's 25-foot buffer around mapped drainage courses. |
| `blueline_stream` | Significant drainage course (blue-line stream) centerline | Hit when the parcel is within 25 ft of a mapped drainage course centerline. |
| `ridgeline` | Ridgelines | Hit when the parcel is within the screening distance of a mapped ridgeline. |
| `habitat_vhvh` | Very high value habitat | Very high value habitat (an environmentally sensitive area). Coastal sage scrub has no separate City layer; it is part of the habitat layers. |
| `habitat_hvh` | High value habitat | High value habitat (an environmentally sensitive area). |
| `oceanfront` | Oceanfront / coastal screening polygon | One coast-wide screening polygon, not a bluff edge. The skills treat the parcel as oceanfront only when this polygon covers it AND parcel.environmental_constraints lists Coastal AND parcel.flood_zone has no V or VE zone. A bluff edge determination decides the rest. |
| `wqesa` | Water Quality Environmentally Sensitive Area | Water Quality Environmentally Sensitive Area: adds water quality requirements for development. |

## Not available from public layers (`not_available`)

| Fact | Why | Ask the applicant for |
|---|---|---|
| `coastal_zone` | The coastal zone boundary is a California Coastal Commission map, not a City layer this script reads. | The skills say nearly all of Laguna Beach is in the coastal zone (the certified Land Use Element excludes only the 522-acre Sycamore Hills area). Confirm the parcel with the City or the Coastal Commission's coastal zone boundary map. |
| `fire_hazard_severity_zone` | The adopted 2025 LRA Fire Hazard Severity Zone map is a CAL FIRE map, not read here. | Check the parcel on the map the City adopted: https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/government/departments/fire/fire-prevention/new-lra-fhsz-maps-for-public-comments |
| `slope` | No public City layer gives parcel slope or grades. | A topographic survey by a licensed land surveyor or civil engineer. |
| `bluff_edge` | The oceanfront layer is a screening polygon, not a bluff edge line. | A bluff edge determination (and geotechnical report) by a qualified professional. |
| `heritage_trees` | The City's heritage tree list is not a public layer. A CHTree flag in parcel.environmental_constraints marks a candidate, not a designation. | An arborist report, or ask the City whether a designated heritage tree is on or next to the parcel. |
| `state_geologic_hazard_zones` | Alquist-Priolo fault zones and state seismic hazard zones (liquefaction, landslide) are state maps, not read here. The landslide entry is the City's own map. | A geotechnical report, or the California Geological Survey maps. |
| `fema_flood_map` | parcel.flood_zone is the FEMA zone the City records on the parcel, not a live read of the FEMA flood map. | The current FEMA FIRM panel for the parcel, or an elevation certificate. |
| `draft_historic_register` | Properties proposed in the City's draft historic register update are not in the public historic layer. | Ask Planning whether the property is on the draft register or needs a historic resource assessment. |
| `transit_distance` | Distance to transit stops (ADU and parking rules) is not computed. | Measure the walking distance to the nearest major transit stop on a public transit map. |
| `zoning_standards` | The script reports the zone code only, not setbacks, height or coverage. | Read the zone's standards in the plugin's zoning references. |

## Privacy

The script asks the City layers for a fixed list of property fields. It never requests or prints owner names, owner mailing addresses, sale prices, assessed values or taxes, even though the City parcel layer holds them. Results stay on the user's machine; nothing is sent anywhere except the GIS queries below.
