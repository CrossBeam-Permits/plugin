# Step 7: Write the report

Write in the order of `templates/report.md`. The sections are the same for every project type; the profile decides which question cards go in section 5 and which tiles lead. For a PDF, fill `templates/report.html` and print it (a host that can run a browser or a PDF tool prints it; otherwise give the person the HTML file to open and print). Keep it to about six pages.

## 1. Header and title

- Left: the builder's name, license line and website as they gave them (`00-connect.md` section 4).
- Right: "Prepared <date> · Site visit <date> (<who>)" and "Research: CrossBeam + <other sources> · City not yet consulted" (or "City consulted <date>" when it was).
- Kicker: "<CITY> · <PROJECT TYPE> FEASIBILITY".
- Title: the street address.
- Line under it: client name if the person wants it shown, APN, and the program in one line.

## 2. The verdict

One bold sentence in a shaded box, starting with the verdict word from `06-synthesize.md` section 7, then the reason with the numbers that decide it. Example shape: "Feasible by right: a <size>, <beds>/<baths> <type> fits under <city>'s <cap> cap and gets ministerial, no-hearing review."

Then the summary, three short paragraphs:

1. **Summary:** what fits and the limits that govern it, with section numbers.
2. **Recommended path:** how to file (custom plan or a city pre-approved plan, which permit) and why.
3. **Biggest constraints:** the two or three items most likely to change the design or the cost.

If the address needed checking (`02-site.md` section 2), add a red "Address check" box here.

## 3. The fit table

Right after the key-number tiles, the fit table from `06-synthesize.md` section 4. It shows "fits" with numbers. Keep it to the limits that govern this project (usually 4 to 10 rows).

## 4. Site facts and risks

- The site-facts table (`02-site.md`), with the aerial image beside it when site facts returned one, and a "Permit history" row: earlier permits, open permits or cases, or "CrossBeam has no permit records for <city>".
- **Risks**, ranked (`06-synthesize.md` section 5): a short numbered list, highest first, each with what could happen, how much it moves cost or schedule, and what settles it.

## 5. The project questions

One card per question in the profile's **Cards** list, plus any the client asked. Number them. Each card:

- **Heading:** the question, with the field note in brackets if the visit raised it, e.g. "Separation from the main house (field note: 6+ ft)".
- **Answer line:** one bold sentence that answers it.
- **Bullets:** each with its tag (`tags.md`), the rule or fact, and its citation.

Drop a card that does not apply, and say nothing about rules you did not check.

## 6. Field conditions

Only with field notes. A table: item / observed / what it means. Tag the "what it means" column when it states a rule or a reading. Photos go in a grid below it with one-line captions; never caption a photo with more than it shows.

## 7. What would fit

Only when something does not fit or needs a discretionary approval (`06-synthesize.md` section 6): the nearest by-right version, in two or three sentences.

## 8. Recommended path and timeline

A numbered list of what to do next, in order: design limits to hold, reports to order first, calculations to run early, timing around law changes, and how to file. Then one line on the review: which departments review it (Planning, Building, Fire, Health, Public Works), whether there is a hearing, and the decision clock if you read one in the law. Never estimate a review time you did not read.

## 9. Ask the City first

The open questions as a ready-to-send list, grouped under the department that answers each (Planning, Building, Fire, Public Works, Health): every `INFERRED` item the verdict depends on, every Ask `could_not_confirm` item, every fee question, and every gap (missing code or schedule). Add a contact line only if the person gave you one or the city's published page lists it; never invent a name, email or phone number.

## 10. Intake checklist

A table: item / status or note. What the permit filing will need for this program: the profile's **Intake checklist additions**, plus anything `crossbeam_get_review_requirements` lists for this city and permit type (cite it). The City's own list wins over the profile's.

## 11. Sources and disclaimer

- **Disclaimer** (bold, above the sources): "Preliminary feasibility only. Scope of work and final design are not guaranteed and remain pending City review and full plan submittal." Add the builder's own exclusions if they gave any.
- **Sources:** every source with its date: municipal code sections (with publisher and codified-through date), state sections (with edition), CrossBeam site facts (with the APN), CrossBeam permit records, CrossBeam Ask (with the date and the conversation link if the person wants it shown), CrossBeam fees (with the schedule date), HCD letters, web sources by name, and the site visit. Say what CrossBeam did not have (city code, fee schedule, permit records) so the reader knows where the report leaves CrossBeam.

## 12. Before you hand it over

Check each of these, and fix what fails:

1. The verdict matches the fit table and the cards; no card contradicts it.
2. Every bullet has one tag; every `CODE` bullet names a section you read.
3. Every `INFERRED` item the verdict rests on is in "Ask the City first".
4. No future law is applied as if in force.
5. No number appears that is not in a source.
6. Address, APN and lot size agree everywhere.
7. Every "To confirm" fit row and every ranked risk has a City question or a next step.

Then give the person the report, and ask before you email, upload or share it.
